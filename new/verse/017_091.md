## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:91

> **or until you have a garden of palm trees and vineyards, and cause rivers to flow abundantly in it,**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Garden Of Palm Trees And Vineyards And Rivers Abundantly**

Aw takūna laka jannatun min nakhīlin wa-ʿinabin fa-tufajjira al-anhāra khilālahā tafjīran — or you have garden from palm trees and grapevines and you cause rivers to gush abundantly within it. Aw, or, alternative demand. Takūna from k-w-n, to be, you have, there is. Laka, for you, to you, O Prophet, you have. Jannatun from j-n-n, garden, paradise, from jannah, garden. Min nakhīlin from n-kh-l, palm trees, from nakhl, palm trees, palm. Wa-ʿinabin from ʿ-n-b, grapevines, grapes, vineyards, from ʿinab, grapes, vineyards. Fa-tufajjira from f-j-r, to cause to gush, make flow abundantly, as tafjura cause to gush, as previous tafjura spring gush, and tufajjira cause rivers to gush. Al-anhāra from n-h-r, rivers, plural of nahr, river, as rivers, as rivers flow abundantly. Khilālahā from kh-l-l, within it, through it, among it, within garden. Tafjīran from f-j-r, abundantly, with emphasis, gushing abundantly, as tafjīran abundantly, intensive. Al-Ṭabarī explains that or until you have garden of palm trees and vineyards, palm trees and grapevines, and cause rivers to flow, gush abundantly within it, through it, as demanded sign, miracle, as they demand garden of palm and vines and rivers abundantly, as sign, as proof, as they challenge, we will never believe until spring gush, garden, rivers, etc., as demands, as they persist in disbelief, refuse except disbelief, as most people persist in disbelief, as every kind of lesson set forth yet most persist in disbelief.

**Why Garden And Rivers As Demanded Sign**

Why garden of palm trees and vineyards and rivers abundantly as demanded sign? Because they, disbelievers, challenge Prophet, demand signs, miracles, as proof, as they say we will never believe until spring gush from earth for us, or garden of palm trees and vineyards and rivers abundantly, etc., as demands, as they demand garden, etc., as sign, as they think Prophet should have garden, wealth, etc., as they are affluent, mutraf, etc., and as they demand garden, rivers, etc., as sign, but nothing keeps Us from sending demanded signs except they were denied by earlier peoples, etc., We only send signs as warning, etc., as previous verse 59 says, and as Quran inimitable, cannot produce equal, every kind of lesson set forth yet most persist in disbelief, challenge, demand signs, as they persist in disbelief.

**Connection To Spring Gush And Sky Fall And Allah Angels Face To Face And House Gold Ascend Heaven Book**

Verses 90-93 list demands: spring gush from earth for us, garden of palm trees and vineyards cause rivers abundantly, cause sky to fall in pieces as claimed, or bring Allah and angels before us face to face, house of gold, ascend into heaven and even then not believe in ascension until you bring down book that we can read, say Glory be to my Lord am I not only human messenger, showing challenges, demands for signs, miracles, but Quran inimitable, cannot produce equal, every kind of lesson set forth yet most persist in disbelief, challenge, demand signs, as they persist in disbelief, refuse except disbelief, as disbelief, ungratefulness, as human ever ungrateful, etc., and as nothing keeps Us from sending demanded signs except denied by earlier, etc., We only send signs as warning, etc.

**Link To Human Messenger And Angelic Messenger And Nothing Prevented Believing Except Protest Human Messenger**

Verse 93 ends say Glory be to my Lord am I not only human messenger, An Angelic Messenger heading, and verse 94 nothing has prevented people from believing when guidance comes except protest Has Allah sent human as messenger, and verse 95 had there been angels walking earth well settled We would have sent down for them angel from heaven as messenger, showing that Prophet is human messenger, not angel, not having garden, house of gold, etc., as human messenger, as they demand garden, etc., but he is only human messenger, as human, as humans and jinn cannot produce equal Quran, etc., and as nothing prevented believing except protest human messenger, etc.

**Linguistic Note On Jannat And Nakhil And Inab And Tufajjira And Anhar And Tafjiran**

Takūna from k-w-n, to be, have. Jannat from j-n-n, garden, from jannah. Nakhīl from n-kh-l, palm trees, from nakhl. ʿInab from ʿ-n-b, grapes, vineyards, from ʿinab. Tufajjira from f-j-r, to cause to gush, make flow, from fajjara. Anhār from n-h-r, rivers, plural of nahr. Khilāl from kh-l-l, within, through, among, from khilāl. Tafjīr from f-j-r, gushing abundantly, from fajjara, intensive. The verse's structure — or you have garden from palm trees and grapevines and you cause rivers to gush abundantly within it — with garden of palm and vines and rivers abundantly within it, shows demand for garden, rivers, as sign, miracle, as proof, as they challenge, demand garden, etc., as sign, but Quran inimitable, cannot produce equal, every kind of lesson set forth yet most persist in disbelief, challenge, demand signs.

**How This Verse Teaches Challenge Demanding Garden And Rivers**

This verse teaches challenge demanding garden of palm trees and vineyards and rivers abundantly as sign: they challenge Prophet, say or until you have garden of palm trees and vineyards and cause rivers to flow abundantly in it, within garden, as demanded sign, miracle, as proof, as they persist in disbelief, refuse except disbelief, as most people persist in disbelief, as verse 89 says, and they challenge, demand signs, miracles, as proof, as garden, rivers, etc., as demands, challenges, as they demand signs, but nothing keeps Us from sending demanded signs except they were denied by earlier peoples, etc., We only send signs as warning, etc., and as Quran inimitable, cannot produce equal, every kind of lesson set forth, yet most persist in disbelief, challenge, demand signs, as they persist in disbelief. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should not challenge Prophet demanding garden of palm trees and vineyards and rivers abundantly, etc., as disbelievers do, who persist in disbelief, refuse except disbelief, but believe, follow most upright guidance, wisdom, that Qur'an guides to, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, healing mercy, etc., while disbelievers challenge, demand signs, persist in disbelief, painful punishment prepared, etc.

**A Reflection On Garden And Rivers And Human Messenger**

Jannatun min nakhīlin wa-ʿinabin — garden from palm trees and vineyards, palm trees and grapevines — and rivers flow abundantly within it, tafjīran, abundantly, gushing, as demanded sign, miracle, garden, rivers, as they demand garden, rivers, etc., as sign, as proof, as they think Prophet should have garden, wealth, etc., as they are affluent, mutraf, etc., but Prophet is only human messenger, as verse 93 says Glory be to my Lord am I not only human messenger, An Angelic Messenger, as human messenger, not angel, not having garden, house of gold, etc., as human messenger, as humans and jinn cannot produce equal Quran, etc., and as nothing prevented believing except protest human messenger, etc., had there been angels walking earth well settled We would have sent angel as messenger, etc., as human messenger for humans, angel for angels, etc., so believe, not demand garden, rivers, etc., as disbelievers do, who persist in disbelief, painful punishment, etc.
