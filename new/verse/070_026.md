## Sūrah al-Maʿārij 70:26

> and who ˹firmly˺ believe in the Day of Judgment;

**Expanded Commentary**

**Belief Directs Biography**

Yuṣaddiqūna bi-yawmi-d-dīn — "affirm the Day of Recompense as true." Taṣdīq means holding-true, affirming, crediting — belief as life's compass. Bi-yawm (in the Day) stakes biography on eschatology: conduct calibrated to coming audit. Such affirming reorders priorities — Hereafter first, world second. The righteous live backward from Judgment: choices weighed on its scales preemptively. Readers should test their taṣdīq behaviorally: does the Day direct your diary? Affirmation unfelt in scheduling is un-affirmed. What the Day governs, destiny guards. Believe forward; live backward. Dīn believed shapes days lived.

Yawm al-dīn — Day of Recompense, Judgment, Settlement. Dīn spans religion, judgment, debt-settling — accounts closed, balances paid. "Day of Dīn" is audit-day: ledgers read, wages rendered. Such naming clarifies belief's content — not vague afterlife but precise reckoning. Believers affirm settlement specifically: every deed weighed, every soul paid. Readers should hear dīn's financial flavor: debts (sins) and credits (deeds) netted. What dīn settles, denial cannot dodge. Believe settlement; settle early — repentance prepays debts. The Day nets all; the wise prepay. Dīn believed is dīn prepared.

Affirmation means anticipation enacted. The righteous live the Day pre-dawn: auditing selves nightly, weighing words pre-speech, settling debts pre-demand. Such pre-living mirrors 69:20's certainty ("I surely knew I'd face reckoning") — knowledge worn as conduct. Anticipation is obedience's engine: feared audit disciplines present. Readers should pre-live daily: morning resolves Judgment-weighed, evening reviews Judgment-ready. What dawn will stage, dusk should rehearse. Live the Day early; meet it smiling. Previewed audit proves gentle. Pre-living is believing's proof.

**Third Duty Crowns Giving**

Prayer (verses 22-23), wealth (verses 24-25), now creed — belief placed third. Order intrigues: practice precedes profession. The sūrah lists worship, charity, then faith — orthopraxy cradling orthodoxy. Such sequencing corrects creed-only religion: belief unpracticed is unproven. Yet belief placed centrally anchors all — prayer and charity flow from Day-affirmed. Readers should hold both: practice proves, belief powers. What hands do (pray, give), hearts must credit (Day believed). Third position crowns: duties performed find meaning in destiny affirmed. Practice; profess; persist.

Taṣdīq fuels the duty-list. Prayer persists because Day believed; charity flows because audit affirmed; chastity holds because reckoning feared. Belief is every duty's engine — remove Day, duties stall. Such causality explains the list's order: duties displayed, then their motor named. Readers should fuel duties with Day-meditation: flagging prayer revived by audit-thought; grudging charity loosened by scales-imagined. What belief powers, unbelief paralyzes. Keep the engine affirmed; duties drive themselves. Taṣdīq is fuel; the list is vehicle. Refuel daily.

Contrast clarifies: the damned denied Dīn. Thamūd and ʿĀd denied the Striking Disaster (69:4); the scoffer denies Recompense (paraphrased from 107:1). Denial's signature is Dīn-denied — audit un-believed, settlement scorned. The righteous invert: Dīn affirmed, lives aligned. Such mirroring sorts humanity by one doctrine — Judgment believed or denied. Readers should take sides consciously: affirm with the praying, never deny with the damned. What Dīn-denial damns, Dīn-affirmation delivers. The line runs through eschatology. Stand affirmed.

Mawāzīn — scales — await every deed. Heavy scales inherit pleasant life (paraphrased from 101:6-7); even mustard-seeds get weighed (paraphrased from 21:47). Readiness rebukes delay: scales calibrated, weights certified. Nothing exceeds sensitivity — atoms counted, intentions factored. Such readiness should hasten preparation: weigh selves before weighing. Readers should pre-weigh nightly: today's deeds, scales-imagined. What balances hoped, conduct must load. Scales ready; souls — ready? Load good deeds; lighten sins via repentance. Weigh-in comes; weigh ready.

**Judgment Believed Transforms Transactions**

Audit-faith honest-izes markets. Scales believed coming straighten scales used daily: weights true, measures full, wages prompt. Transactions eschatologized — every deal Judgment-previewed. Cheating steals twice: from counterpart now, from self then. Believers trade transparently: defects disclosed, prices fair, promises kept. Such commerce witnesses: honesty advertises Hereafter. Readers should audit dealings: would this transaction survive scales? What Dīn-belief governs, greed cannot corrupt. Trade Judgment-consciously. Markets mirror creeds. Believe settlement; settle fairly.

Denial never delays. The Day denied arrives on schedule — deniers meet it first as shock, believers as appointment. Refusing to believe the audit does not postpone it; it forfeits preparation. Such is denial's folly: hiding from schedules that hunt. The scoffer's calendar omits Judgment; Judgment's calendar includes scoffers. Incurred means unavoidable — the Day reaches deniers and affirmers alike, differing only in reception. Readers should stop negotiating dates: the Day is incurred already, appointed past appeal. What denial dodges verbally, destiny delivers actually. Believe incurred; prepare accordingly. Denial is not deferral — it is forfeiture of readiness.

Quarrels await settlement. Wronged ones will be righted; usurpers stripped; slanderers exposed. Courts err, evidence hides, witnesses lie — the Day corrects all. Ultimate court admits no bribes, loses no files, frees no guilty. Such clarification comforts victims: unheard cries get heard. It warns victimizers: hidden wrongs surface. Readers nursing grievances should entrust them: the Day's docket holds every case. What courts fumbled, scales settle. Forgive where possible (mercy's premium); entrust where owed. Disputes clarified, rights restored. Await the court; live justly meanwhile.

**Eschatology Is Ethics' Backbone**

Remove the Last Day, morals collapse. Accountability grounds honesty: cheats fear scales, liars dread exposure. Secular scaffolds (reputation, law, conscience) buckle privately — unwatched, unpenalized, unmanned. Only certain audit steadies secret conduct. Belief in Dīn thus structures virtue entire: every duty leans on destiny. The sūrah's list proves it — prayer persists for audit, charity flows for scales, chastity holds for reckoning. Readers should trace virtues to verdicts: why honest when unseen? Because Seen. What backbone supports, limbs perform. Strengthen eschatology; ethics stands. Deny the Day; watch duties dissolve. Backbone believed, body behaves.

**Fear Follows Faith Next**

Belief affirmed; wariness follows. Verse 27 will add fear — mushfiqūn, wary of punishment. Creed then caution: Day believed, then dreaded rightly. Readers should carry affirmation toward awe: believed Day, feared Lord. What follows will tremble properly. The next verse adds wariness of the Lord's punishment, fear crowning faith.
