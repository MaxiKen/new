## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:130

> But if they choose to separate, Allah will enrich both of them from His bounties. And Allah is Ever-Bountiful, All-Wise.

### Expanded Commentary

**Separation can be a form of mercy**

If the spouses choose to separate, Allah will enrich both from His bounty. The verse refuses the idea that divorce necessarily leaves one party ruined or that a marriage must be prolonged because poverty makes leaving frightening. God can provide new support, companionship, stability, or spiritual healing.

The promise does not make separation casual. Rights, waiting periods, child welfare, debts, and housing must be handled justly. Nor does it guarantee a particular worldly outcome. It gives courage to leave an arrangement that has become a site of sustained harm and discourages the fear that no future is possible.

Allah is Ever-Bountiful and All-Wise. The first name inspires hope; the second prevents reckless assumptions. Seek counsel, protect the vulnerable, and separate with dignity when reconciliation has genuinely failed.

**A future after separation**

The promise of enrichment protects people from the fear that an unhappy marriage is their only source of survival. Families should help separated spouses with housing, employment, childcare, and emotional support. Trust in divine provision does not excuse society from building the means through which provision arrives.
