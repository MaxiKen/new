## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:61

> **And ˹remember˺ when We said to the angels, “Prostrate before Adam,” so they all did—but not Iblîs, who protested, “Should I prostrate to the one You have created from mud?”**

**Expanded Commentary**

**When We Said To Angels Prostrate Before Adam**

Wa-idh qulnā li-l-malā'ikati usjudū li-Ādama fa-sajadū illā Iblīsa — and when We said to angels prostrate to Adam, so they prostrated except Iblis. Wa-idh, and when, remember when. Qulnā li-l-malā'ikati, We said to angels, from q-w-l and m-l-k, angels. Usjudū from s-j-d, prostrate, bow down, prostrate, from sajada, to prostrate, as masjid place of prostration, Temple. Li-Ādama, to Adam, before Adam, for Adam, first human, father. Fa-sajadū, so they prostrated, did prostrate, from s-j-d. Illā Iblīsa, except Iblis, Satan, Devil, from b-l-s, Iblis, despair, as Iblis despaired of mercy. Al-Ṭabarī explains that remember when We said to angels, prostrate before Adam, as honour, respect, not worship, prostration of honour, so they all did, prostrated, obeyed, except Iblis, who refused, protested, as he was arrogant, as previous verse 37 do not walk arrogantly, and as those who disbelieve have denying hearts and are arrogant, as Devil arrogant. Al-Qurṭubī says usjudū li-Ādama means prostrate before Adam, as honour, respect, as angels prostrated honouring Adam, as Adam favoured, dignified, as children of Adam dignified, as verse 70 says.

**Iblis Protested Should I Prostrate To One Created From Mud**

Qāla a-asjudu li-man khalaqta ṭīnan — he said, should I prostrate to one You created from mud, clay? Qāla from q-w-l, he said, Iblis said, protested. A-asjudu from s-j-d, should I prostrate, interrogative, should I prostrate? Li-man, to one whom, to whom. Khalaqta from kh-l-q, You created, from khalaqa, to create. Ṭīnan from ṭ-y-n, mud, clay, from ṭīn, mud, clay, as Adam created from mud, clay, as previous chapter said created from mud. Al-Ṭabarī explains that Iblis protested, said should I prostrate to one You created from mud, clay, while I created from fire, as he thought fire better than mud, arrogant, as he was arrogant, refused to prostrate, as Devil ever ungrateful, as wasteful brothers to devils, as Satan sworn enemy, as he refused out of arrogance, as previous verse do not walk arrogantly, you cannot crack earth nor reach mountains height, but Iblis arrogant, refused. Al-Qurṭubī says a-asjudu li-man khalaqta ṭīnan indicates arrogance, Iblis thought himself better, created from fire, Adam from mud, so refused, protested, as arrogance leads to disobedience, as Children of Isra'il became extremely arrogant, etc.

**Why Angels Prostrated And Iblis Refused**

Why angels prostrated and Iblis refused? Because angels obey, not arrogant, while Iblis arrogant, ever ungrateful, sworn enemy, refused to prostrate to one created from mud, thinking fire better, as arrogance, as he said do you see this one you honoured above me, if delay my end until Day Judgment I will take hold of descendants except few, as next verse says, vowing to mislead, as Satan seeks to sow discord, incite, etc. The prostration to Adam is honour for Adam, for children of Adam dignified, as verse 70 says We dignified children of Adam, carried them on land and sea, granted good provisions, privileged far above many creatures, so Adam dignified, honoured, angels prostrated, Iblis refused out of arrogance.

**Connection To Arrogance And Walking Arrogantly And Extremely Arrogant**

Verse 4 Children of Isra'il will become extremely arrogant, ʿuluwwan kabīran, great arrogance, verse 37 do not walk arrogantly, maraḥan, arrogantly, you cannot crack earth nor reach mountains height, verse 61 Iblis refused to prostrate to one created from mud, arrogant, protested, as arrogance leads to refusal, disobedience, as Devil arrogant, as those who disbelieve in Hereafter have denying hearts and are arrogant. The connection is arrogance theme: Children of Isra'il extremely arrogant, do not walk arrogantly, Iblis arrogant refused, all arrogance leads to disobedience, corruption, destruction, severe punishment, as divine law, every wicked society destroyed or punished severely before Day written in Record.

**Link To Dignified Children Of Adam And Satan No Authority Over Faithful**

Verse 70 We dignified children of Adam, carried them on land and sea, granted good provisions, privileged far above many creatures, showing honour for Adam, children, as angels prostrated, Iblis refused out of arrogance, but children of Adam dignified, honoured. Verse 65 you will have no authority over My faithful servants, sufficient Lord as Guardian, showing that despite Iblis vow to take hold of descendants except few, he has no authority over faithful servants, Lord Guardian sufficient, as faithful servants protected, as they say best, etc.

**Linguistic Note On Usjudu And Iblis And Tin**

Qulnā from q-w-l, to say. Malā'ikah from m-l-k, angels, plural of malak. Usjudū from s-j-d, prostrate, from sajada. Ādam, Adam, first human, from a-d-m, Adam. Sajadū from s-j-d, they prostrated. Iblīs, Iblis, Satan, from b-l-s, to despair. Qāla from q-w-l, to say. Asjudu from s-j-d, prostrate. Man khalaqta, whom You created, from kh-l-q. Ṭīn from ṭ-y-n, mud, clay, from ṭīn. The verse's structure — when We said to angels prostrate before Adam, so they prostrated except Iblis, who said should I prostrate to one You created from mud — with command to angels prostrate, obedience except Iblis, and protest should I prostrate to one created from mud, shows honour for Adam, obedience of angels, arrogance of Iblis, refusal, protest, as arrogance leads to disobedience, as theme.

**How This Verse Teaches Obedience Vs Arrogance**

This verse teaches obedience vs arrogance: angels obeyed, prostrated before Adam when commanded, Iblis refused out of arrogance, protested should I prostrate to one created from mud, thinking fire better, arrogant, as Devil ever ungrateful, sworn enemy, seeks to sow discord, etc. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should obey, not be arrogant like Iblis, should be humble, not walk arrogantly, lower wing of humility, as most upright guidance, wisdom, part of wisdom revealed, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, while arrogance leads to refusal, disobedience, being cast into Hell blameworthy rejected, as Iblis, as those who set up other god cast into Hell, etc. The practical lesson is to obey Allah, prostrate, humble, not arrogant, not protest should I prostrate to mud, but obey, as angels obeyed, as children of Adam dignified, honoured, privileged far above many creatures.

**A Reflection On Mud And Honour**

Ṭīnan — mud, clay — material from which Adam created, humble, lowly, from earth, as earth, but Adam honoured, dignified, angels prostrated, children of Adam dignified, carried on land and sea, granted good provisions, privileged far above many creatures, showing that honour not from material, fire vs mud, but from Allah's favour, as He favoured some over others, some prophets above others, etc., and Iblis arrogant thought fire better than mud, refused, protested, but was wrong, arrogant, ever ungrateful, sworn enemy, vowed to take hold of descendants, but no authority over faithful servants, sufficient Lord as Guardian, as faithful servants protected, say best, etc., leading to mighty reward.
