## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:145

> Surely the hypocrites will be in the lowest depths of the Fire—and you will never find for them any helper—

### Expanded Commentary

**The lowest depth and the logic of hypocrisy**

The hypocrites are warned of the lowest depths of the Fire, with no helper. Their position is especially severe because hypocrisy corrupts the inside of the community while borrowing the outward protection of faith. It combines denial with betrayal and makes sacred language an instrument of harm.

The absence of a helper answers the worldly strategy of the hypocrite, who gathered patrons on every side. In the final court, no alliance can rescue the person from the truth of his own choices. Yet the verse should not license public accusations based on mood; its final diagnosis belongs to Allah, while communities deal with observable deception through fair procedures.

**The depth of hidden betrayal**

Hypocrisy is placed in the lowest depth because it injures the community from within and makes sincere trust harder for everyone. The warning should motivate honesty in small matters before contradiction becomes a settled identity. A person who recognizes the pattern can still turn through the repentance described next.
