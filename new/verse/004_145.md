## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:145

> Surely the hypocrites will be in the lowest depths of the Fire—and you will never find for them any helper—

**Expanded Commentary**

**Hypocrites Will Be in the Lowest Depths of the Fire**

The verse states *inna al-munāfiqīna fī al-darki al-asfali mina al-nāri* — surely the hypocrites will be, are, in the lowest depth, the bottom layer, the nethermost reach, of the Fire. *Dark* — depth, level, abyss — *asfal* — lowest — was explained as the deepest layer of Hell, below disbelievers gathered together with hypocrites (4:140), below the self-wronger’s Hell as home (4:97), below the defier’s burning (4:115), below the follower of Satan’s Hell without escape (4:121). *Mina al-nāri* — of the Fire — was read as the Fire whose preparation was humiliating for disbelievers where prayer was guarded (4:102), whose truth is contrasted with Gardens true (4:122), whose lowest depth is for those who deceive God while He outwits them (4:142), stand lazy to be seen (4:142), are torn between neither these nor those (4:143), and take disbelievers as allies giving God clear warrant against themselves (4:144). Early reports noted that the Fire has layers — *darakāt* — unlike Paradise’s *darajāt* — elevated ranks (4:96) — the lowest *dark* is for hypocrisy because hypocrisy is lowest in deceit: to invoke female gods besides Him and Satan (4:117) is open, to deceive while claiming belief is hidden and therefore lower. The sūrah has shown that the sitter where signs are mocked is like them (4:140) and will be gathered together; the lowest depth is the belonging of the torn who belonged to none.

**And You Will Never Find for Them Any Helper**

The clause *wa lan tajida lahum naṣīrā* — and you will never find for them any helper, protector, supporter — was explained with *naṣīr* — helper — the same *naṣīr* the oppressed cry for from grace (4:75) and that God alone suffices as (4:45) and that disbelievers as allies were sought for instead of believers (4:144). To find no *naṣīr* for hypocrites was read as that no *walī* and no *naṣīr* remains where they gave Him clear proof against themselves (4:144) and where *“whoever God leaves to stray you will never find a way”* (4:143). The phrase *lan tajida* — you will never find — was addressed to the Prophet ﷺ and to every searcher: no helper will be found for them, as the deceitful’s advocates in this life found no advocate before God on the Day (4:109) and no *wakīl* (4:109). The lowest depth without helper contrasts with Gardens promised to those who believe and do good where rivers flow and promise is true (4:122) and with enrichment from His bounty for those who separate (4:130). Where the emigrant finds many havens (4:100) and many refuges, the hypocrite finds lowest Fire and no helper, because heavens and earth belong to the One they deceived while He was with them at night plotting (4:108).

**A Verse That Follows Clear Warrant Against Selves With Depth Without Helper**

Placed after *do not take disbelievers as allies instead of believers, would you give God clear warrant against yourselves?* (4:144), this verse follows the warning that taking that alliance gives clear *sulṭān* against yourselves with the consequence that follows manifest proof: lowest depth, no helper. The sūrah has built hypocrisy’s proof step by step: oscillation (4:137), announcing painful punishment (4:138), seeking honour among disbelievers (4:139), sitting where signs are mocked as like them (4:140), waiting to claim with winners (4:141), deceiving while being outwitted, standing lazy to be seen remembering little (4:142), torn between neither (4:143), taking allies instead of believers (4:144) — lowest depth is the depth of that accumulation, as Gardens’ *darajāt* are the elevation of accumulated good where charity, kindness, reconciliation were commanded (4:114) and where prayer was guarded and remembered (4:102–103). Where the sūrah says *“do not let desires deviate from justice; if you distort or refuse He is All-Aware”* (4:135), hypocrisy deviates maximally — twisting testimony and refusing it — so depth is maximal and helper none.

**Lowest Depth as Counter to Elevated Ranks**

The *dark asfal* — lowest depth — was read as counter to *darajātin minhu* — ranks from Him (4:96) where strivers are elevated above sitters though both promised good. The sūrah has *darajāt* for strivers (4:96), forgiveness and mercy, and *darakāt* implied here for hypocrites; the lowest *dark* is the opposite pole to elevated ranks, as Hell without *maḥīṣ* (4:121) is opposite to Gardens forever. The sūrah that warns *with Him are both rewards He hears and sees* (4:134) says depth is not unheard or unseen; the All-Hearing, All-Seeing (4:134) and Fully Aware muḥīṭ of everything (4:126) measures as not wronging even atom (4:40) yet depth for hypocrisy is atom-measure exact after many clarities rejected. For the weak this is clarifying: to be torn between (4:143) and to take disbelievers as allies (4:144) is not a safe middle — it is lowest; to stand for justice though against yourselves (4:135) is to be with believers, whose path the next verse makes open by repentance, mending, holding fast, sincerity.

**A Short Verse That Holds the Gate Open One More Verse**

Though only *“hypocrites in lowest depths of the Fire, you will never find helper,”* the verse holds the gate open one more verse: its dash — *—* — in translation signals the exception — *illā alladhīna tābū* — except those who repent, mend, hold fast to God, and are sincere in devotion, they will be with believers and God will grant believers great reward (4:146). The sūrah has shown that whoever commits evil then seeks finds Him All-Forgiving (4:110), that seeking forgiveness is commanded between prohibitions to advocate (4:106), that whoever earns sin bears it upon self yet can seek and find (4:111, 4:110); depth without helper is the wage where none of those is done. The mercy that is Ever-Pardoning, All-Forgiving (4:99) and All-Forgiving, Most Merciful (4:96) is not cancelled by depth — except — so that the lowest depth is not a fate sealed by lineage but by persisting in wavering and alliance, and the gate is sincerity after repentance.
