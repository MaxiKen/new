## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:143

> Torn between belief and disbelief—belonging neither to these ˹believers˺ nor those ˹disbelievers˺. And whoever Allah leaves to stray, you will never find for them a way.

**Expanded Commentary**

**Torn Between — Belonging Neither to Believers Nor Disbelievers**

The verse opens *mudhabdhabīna bayna dhālika lā ilā hāʾulāʾi wa lā ilā hāʾulāʾi* — torn, wavering, suspended between that, between this and that, belonging neither to these — believers — nor to those — disbelievers. *Mudhabdhab* — torn, swayed, fluttered, shaky — was explained as the hypocrites’ oscillation already named — believed then disbelieved then believed then increased (4:137), waiting to see what happens to claim with winners (4:141), deceiving while being outwitted, standing lazy to be seen (4:142). *Bayna dhālika* — between that — was read as between belief and disbelief, between the host with many havens (4:100) and the host without escape (4:121), between Gardens true (4:122) and Hell prepared. *Lā ilā hāʾulāʾi wa lā ilā hāʾulāʾi* — neither to these nor to those — was explained as that their alliance — *awliyāʾ* — taken from disbelievers instead of believers seeking honour (4:139) did not make them of disbelievers, nor did their standing for prayer to be seen (4:142) make them of believers; they remain between. The sūrah has shown two companies — hypocrites and disbelievers gathered together in Hell (4:140) versus those who believe and do good in Gardens forever (4:122) — and said *“do not sit with mockers else you are like them”* (4:140); the torn are those who sat and are like yet not counted fully until gathering makes like permanent. The image was also linked to the double safety seekers who wish safety from both sides (4:91) yet belong to neither where *fitnah* returns.

**And Whoever God Leaves to Stray, You Will Never Find a Way**

The seal *wa man yuḍlili Allāhu fa lan tajida lahū sabīlā* — and whoever God leaves to stray, causes to stray, leaves straying, you will never find for them a way — was explained with *yuḍlil* — to leave to stray, to allow deviation, to not guide — after they turned to what they chose. *Man yuḍlili Allāhu* matches *“do you wish to guide those left by God to stray?”* (4:88) where regressed hypocrites were allowed to regress because of misdeeds, and *“We will let them pursue what they chose”* (4:115) after defying the Messenger and following other than believers’ path. *Fa lan tajida lahū sabīlā* — you will never find for them a way — was explained as *sabīl* — way, path, argument, means — the same *sabīl* the sūrah said He will guide to noble ways (4:26), the path believers follow (4:115), and the way He will never grant disbelievers over believers (4:141). To find no way for the left-to-stray torn is to say no argument rescues, no alias of *ʿizzah* (4:139) provides way, no sitting as like them (4:140) provides way, no waiting as with winners (4:141) provides way, no outwitted deception (4:142) provides way. Early reports noted that the *mudhabdhab* is like sheep between two flocks, or like a lost beast between herders — the image emphasises belonging to none, found by none.

**A Verse That Follows Lazy Prayer With Wavering Belonging**

Placed after *they seek to deceive God but He outwits, they stand lazy to be seen hardly remembering* (4:142), this verse follows lazy prayer with wavering belonging that lazy prayer manifests. The sūrah has shown prayer as witness for Him even against selves (4:135), as remembrance standing, sitting, lying (4:103), as guarded under arms — the hypocrite’s lazy standing to be seen is witness for people, remembrance little, guard for show; the fruit is being *mudhabdhab* — neither these nor those. The sūrah has shown the community divided into two groups about hypocrites (4:88) asking *why are you two groups regarding hypocrites?*; here the hypocrite is himself two groups in one person — neither these nor those. The sūrah that commands to be mindful (4:131), to entrust to Him as *Wakīl* (4:132), to believe without wavering (4:136) here says wavering ends with no *sabīl* to be found, because the One who can remove and bring others (4:133) removes way from the torn.

**The Left to Stray After Many Clarities**

The phrase *wa man yuḍlili Allāhu* was explained as divine leaving after many clarities: association far astray (4:116), female gods invoked as Satan (4:117), Satan cursed taking share (4:118), ordering cutting and altering (4:119), promising only delusion (4:120), two abodes declared true (4:121–122), wishes not by wish (4:123), good with faith not wronged (4:124), best faith submission following Abraham (4:125), ownership and encompassing (4:126), instruction for orphan women (4:127), settlement best though selfishness present (4:128), impossible emotional equality yet do not incline entirely (4:129), enrichment after separation (4:130), universal command to be mindful (4:131), sufficiency as Trustee (4:132) — after all that, to remain *mudhabdhab* is not ignorance but straying left. The sūrah has said *“He wills to make clear, guide to noble ways, turn in mercy”* (4:26–28) — clarity was given; wavering after clarity is the human turning that He lets be. For the weak this is a call to belong: where the sūrah says *“do not take disbelievers as allies instead of believers, would you give God proof against yourselves?”* (4:144) next, the wavering between alliances is the proof to abandon, by choosing believers as like — *mithluhum* — for Gardens.

**A Short Verse That Freezes the Hypocrite Between**

Though only *“torn between neither these nor those, whoever He leaves to stray you will never find a way,”* the verse freezes the hypocrite between. Immediately after: *O believers, do not take disbelievers as allies instead of believers, would you give God proof against yourselves?* (4:144) — choose side. Immediately after: *hypocrites will be in lowest depth of Fire, you will never find helper* (4:145) — except those who repent, mend, hold fast to God and are sincere will be with believers and God will grant believers great reward (4:146). The torn between is therefore not a permanent station offered; it is the station that will be resolved either into lowest depth or into being with believers, according to whether repentance, mending, holding fast and sincerity replace wavering. The verse generalises the particular case of waiting between winners (4:141): waiting between is straying left, for whom no way will be found.
