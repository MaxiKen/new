## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:94

> O believers! When you struggle in the cause of Allah, be sure of who you fight. And do not say to those who offer you ˹greetings of˺ peace, “You are no believer!”—seeking a fleeting worldly gain. Instead, Allah has infinite bounties ˹in store˺. You were initially like them then Allah blessed you ˹with Islam˺. So be sure! Indeed, Allah is All-Aware of what you do.

### Expanded Commentary

**Verify before taking a life**

Believers are told to be sure when they go out in Allah’s cause and not to declare a person who offers peace an unbeliever in order to gain worldly spoils. The command addresses the lethal danger of haste. An outward greeting, declaration, or sign of non-hostility may be the last protection a person has; dismissing it for gain is a grave betrayal.

**A warning from early Muslim history**

The Prophet rebuked a fighter who killed a man after the man pronounced the testimony, suspecting that he said it only to escape. The Prophetic teaching made the principle clear: people are not to be interrogated about hidden faith after offering a protected sign. Allah alone knows the heart.

The verse reminds believers that they were once in a state of vulnerability and that guidance was a gift. The formerly persecuted must not become arrogant persecutors. In contemporary settings, the rule requires identification, due process, civilian protection, and refusal to turn economic gain or political convenience into evidence of guilt.

**Verify a sign of peace**

The instruction is especially important when fear makes every unfamiliar person appear dangerous. Slow down, distinguish a real threat from an assumption, and do not convert another person’s possible faith into a means of gaining property. The believer remembers that guidance was a gift and can be granted to the person he is tempted to dismiss.
