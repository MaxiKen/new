## Sūrah an-Nūr 24:64

> Surely to Allah belongs whatever is in the heavens and the earth. He knows well what you stand for. And ˹on˺ the Day all will be returned to Him, He will inform them of what they did. For Allah has ˹perfect˺ knowledge of all things.

**Expanded Commentary**

**Cosmic Dominion and Universal Sovereignty: a-lā inna lillāhi mā fī al-samāwāt**

Sūrah an-Nūr culminates in a magnificent cosmological crescendo: *a-lā inna lillāhi mā fī al-samāwāti wa-al-arḍ*, surely to Allah belongs whatever is in the heavens and the earth. The opening attention particle *a-lā* (unquestionably / behold!) commands immediate, solemn focus, while the corroborative particle *inna* paired with the fronted preposition *lillāh* establishes absolute, unshared sovereignty.

Throughout the sixty-four verses of this sublime chapter, the revelation has descended into the intricate details of human life—courtroom procedures, marital chastity, domestic privacy, bedroom permissions, table etiquette, and political discipline. Now, in its concluding verse, the discourse ascends back to the cosmic heavens, reminding humanity that every moral rule, legal ordinance, and social etiquette emanates from the supreme Owner and Architect of the entire universe. Earthly existence is not an unregulated wilderness; it is a sacred precinct within God's vast cosmic palace, where every creature exists under divine ownership.

**Divine Cognizance of Human States: qad yaʿlamu mā antum ʿalayh**

The divine gaze narrows from cosmic expanse to the immediate reality of human conscience: *qad yaʿlamu mā antum ʿalayh*, He knows well what you stand for / He certainly knows the state you are in. The particle *qad* combined with the present-tense verb *yaʿlam* communicates absolute, intimate, and continuous knowledge.

The phrase *mā antum ʿalayh* encompasses the totality of human orientation: beliefs, hidden intentions, hypocritical vacillations, secret sins, quiet sacrifices, and moral struggles. God knows whether an individual stands upon authentic faith, humble obedience, and sincere love for truth, or whether they stand upon cynical hypocrisy, deceitful evasion, and self-serving opportunism. Nothing in the human psyche remains hidden. This intimate awareness dismantles the superficial facades constructed by human beings to impress their peers, calling every servant to rigorous internal honesty before the Lord who perceives every pulse of the heart.

**The Terminal Convergence of Resurrection: wa-yawma yurjaʿūna ilayh**

The verse anchors moral reality in the certainty of the eschatological climax: *wa-yawma yurjaʿūna ilayh*, and on the Day all will be returned to Him. The passive verb *yurjaʿūn* (they will be returned) emphasizes the absolute inescapability of the resurrection.

Human beings may enter this world through biological birth and depart through physical death, but the ultimate destination of every soul is an involuntary, sovereign summons back to the divine presence. Kings, beggars, judges, litigants, sincere believers, and scheming hypocrites will all converge upon the vast plains of the Hereafter, stripped of worldly status, wealth, and tribal protection. The temporal drama of mortal life terminates abruptly, and every created soul must stand before the Creator to confront the ultimate reality of their choices.

**The Unveiling of Mortal Records: fa-yunabbiʾuhum bimā ʿamilū**

The consequence of this cosmic return is complete, transparent disclosure: *fa-yunabbiʾuhum bimā ʿamilū*, He will inform them of what they did. On that Day, the secret books will be unrolled, and the cosmic ledger will speak with unerring accuracy.

The verb *yunabbiʾuhum* indicates that God will not merely present a dry catalog of deeds; He will unveil the full reality, deep motives, and moral weight of every action performed in mortal life. The slanders whispered in dark alleys, the modest glances cast downward in obedience, the bedroom doors respected in secret, the keys managed in honest trust, the desert mirages pursued by the arrogant, and the sacrifices made for the community will all be laid bare before the assembled creation. The hypocrite will be confronted with their cowardly evasions, while the sincere believer will see their humble, quiet devotions magnified into radiant gardens of eternal bliss.

**Transcendent Omniscience as the Ultimate Seal: Allāhu bi-kulli shayʾin ʿAlīm**

The sūrah concludes with its final, majestic theological seal: *wa-Allāhu bi-kulli shayʾin ʿalīm*, for Allah has perfect knowledge of all things. The attribute *al-ʿAlīm* encompasses infinite, all-embracing knowledge that spans past, present, future, and all counterfactual realities.

God knows the trajectories of the farthest galaxies, the movement of microscopic droplets within cumulonimbus clouds, the prayers of soaring birds, the whispers of hypocrites plotting behind closed doors, and the tears of the falsely accused woman weeping in her chamber. Connecting the entire sūrah to divine omniscience establishes the ultimate foundation of moral accountability: human laws can be evaded, human judges can be deceived, and human witnesses can be silenced, but the All-Knowing Lord is an ever-present witness to every thought, word, and deed, ensuring that perfect justice will prevail.

**The Architectural Coherence of Sūrah an-Nūr**

Reflecting upon the entire span of Sūrah an-Nūr reveals an architectural masterpiece of divine wisdom. The chapter opened in verse 1 with an uncompromising declaration of legal authority: *Sūratun anzalnāhā wa-faraḍnāhā* (A sūrah which We have revealed and made obligatory). It ends in verse 64 with the cosmic proclamation of divine ownership and omniscience: *inna lillāhi mā fī al-samāwāti wa-al-arḍ*.

At the literal and spiritual center of the chapter burns the sublime Verse of Light (v. 35): *Allāhu nūru al-samāwāti wa-al-arḍ*. The surrounding legislative ordinances—purifying human honor from slander, protecting society from sexual corruption, regulating domestic entry, fostering table fellowship, and commanding political discipline—are not disparate, disconnected rules. They are the practical physical vessels designed to house and protect that divine light within human society. By submitting to these divine statutes, the individual heart becomes a glowing crystal lamp, the domestic home becomes an elevated sanctuary of praise, and the community becomes a radiant commonwealth of peace, illuminating the earth with the uncreated light of God, guiding humanity safely home to eternal salvation.
