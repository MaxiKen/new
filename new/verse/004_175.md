## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:175

> As for those who believe in Allah and hold fast to Him, He will admit them into His mercy and grace and guide them to Himself through the Straight Path.

**Expanded Commentary**

**Those Who Believe and Hold Fast — Admitted Into Mercy and Grace**

The verse states *fa ammā alladhīna āmanū bi Allāhi wa iʿtaṣamū bihī fa sa yudkhiluhum fī raḥmatin minhu wa faḍlin* — as for those who believe in God and hold fast, cling, to Him, He will admit, will enter them, into mercy from Him and grace, bounty. *Āmanū bi Allāhi* — believe in God — was explained with comprehensive faith — *have faith in God, Messenger, Book and Scriptures, angels, Last Day* (4:136) — steadfast, not wavering, not distinguishing (4:150–152) — the faith that accepts all messengers whose stories told and not (4:164) after lineage from Noah through Jesus (4:163). *Wa iʿtaṣamū bihī* — and hold fast, cling, to Him, to it — was read with *iʿtiṣām* — holding fast — the same *iʿtiṣām* that repentant hypocrites must do — *hold fast to God* (4:146) — and that those well-grounded do — establishing prayer (4:162) — and that the emigrant to God has reward secured (4:100). *Iʿtaṣamū bihī* was explained as *bih* — to Him, to it — both: to God and to His rope — the Qur’an — the brilliant light — *nūr mubīn* — evidence — *burhān* (4:174) — and to the Book sent with truth to judge between people (4:105). *Fa sa yudkhiluhum fī raḥmatin minhu wa faḍlin* — He will admit them into mercy from Him and grace — was explained with *raḥmah* — mercy — and *faḍl* — grace, bounty — the two that make Gardens true (4:122) — mercy as forgiveness for faults, grace as increase. The sūrah has said *“why should He punish if you are grateful and faithful? He is ever Appreciative”* (4:147) — admission into mercy and grace is the Appreciative’s response to holding fast. Where *“with Him are both rewards He hears and sees”* (4:134) — mercy and grace are those rewards.

**And Guide Them to Himself Through the Straight Path**

The second promise *wa yahdīhim ilayhi ṣirāṭan mustaqīman* — and He will guide them to Him, toward Him, through, as, the straight — upright, direct — path — was explained with *yahdīhim ilayhi* — guide them to Him, toward Him — not only to Gardens but to Himself, as the emigrant emigrates to God and Messenger (4:100) — reward falls upon Him. *Ṣirāṭ mustaqīm* — straight path — was explained as the sūrah’s path — noble ways He wishes to make clear (4:26), believers’ path not to be left (4:115), the path He guides to through mercy (now), as the sūrah wills to make clear, guide, turn. *Mustaqīm* — straight, upright — was read as the opposite of *ghuluww* — going to extremes (4:171) — Trinity and denial both deviate; straight is middle — Messiah as servant not god, Mary pure not slandered, Moses spoken to directly — true. The sūrah has shown that *“the Messiah would never be too proud to be servant”* (4:172) — servitude is straight; to be too proud is deviant. *Yahdīhim ilayhi* was linked to *“Gardens under rivers forever, promise ever true”* (4:122) — Gardens are mercy and grace guided to Him.

**A Verse That Follows Evidence and Light With Response**

Placed after *O humanity, conclusive evidence has come from your Lord, We have sent down to you a brilliant light* (4:174), this verse follows evidence and light with response: as for those who believe in God and hold fast to Him — to evidence and light — He will admit them into His mercy and grace and guide them to Himself through the straight path. The sūrah has shown that those who disbelieve and hinder far astray (4:167), disbelieve and wrong not forgiven nor guided except Hell’s path forever easy (4:168–169) — opposite of mercy and grace and straight path. The sūrah that warns not to take disbelievers as allies instead of believers (4:144) and describes lowest depth without helper (4:145) makes *iʿtiṣām bihī* — holding fast to Him — the opposite alliance: to take Him as trustee suffices (4:132). For the weak this is steadying: where you were told to refer disputes to God and Messenger (4:59), holding fast is referral; where you were told to verify reports (4:83), evidence and light is verification; where you were told to be sure before fighting (4:94), holding fast is sureness.

**Mercy and Grace Guided Through Straight Path**

The pair *raḥmah minhu wa faḍl* — mercy from Him and grace — was explained as mercy that pardons the helpless hoped to be pardoned (4:99) — *ʿAfuww Ghafūr* — and forgives the mistaken by fasting (4:92) — and grace that gives many havens to the emigrant (4:100) — *murāgham kathīr wa saʿah* — and great reward to the grounded (4:162) — *ajran ʿaẓīmā* — and repays doers of good in full and increases out of grace (4:173). *Yahdīhim ilayhi ṣirāṭan mustaqīman* was linked to *“He wills to make clear, guide to noble ways, turn in mercy”* (4:26–28) — making clear is *burhān* and *nūr* (4:174), guiding to noble ways is *ṣirāṭ mustaqīm* now, turning in mercy is *raḥmah minhu wa faḍl*. To be guided to Him through straight path is to be among those who believe and do good in Gardens where promise is true (4:122) — straight is the sūrah’s law for orphans, women, inheritance, trust, prayer, judgment, charity, Abraham, settlement, enrichment — all are straight.

**A Short Verse That Anchors Holding Fast Before Final Ruling**

Though only *“as for those who believe in God and hold fast to Him, He will admit into His mercy and grace and guide them to Himself through the straight path,”* the verse anchors holding fast before final ruling regarding siblings. The chapter that began with single soul and honouring ties (4:1), showed orphan girls deprived, settlement best, total inclining prohibited, enrichment after separation, universal command to be mindful, ownership and encompassing, demand for a book from heaven as litany, forbidding *ṭayyibāt* for *ẓulm* and hindering, well-grounded to great reward, lineage, messengers, good news and warnings, sufficient witness, calling humanity, correcting People of the Book, servitude of Messiah, evidence and light, now says holding fast to that evidence and light is the act that places into mercy and grace and straight path. The next verse will set the final asking: they ask you for a ruling regarding those who die without children or parents — God gives ruling regarding siblings — male and female shares as prescribed so you do not go astray (4:176).
