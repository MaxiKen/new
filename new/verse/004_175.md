## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:175

> As for those who believe in Allah and hold fast to Him, He will admit them into His mercy and grace and guide them to Himself through the Straight Path.

### Expanded Commentary

**Holding fast to Allah**

Those who believe in Allah and hold fast to Him are admitted into mercy and grace and guided to the Straight Path. Holding fast means reliance, obedience, and attachment to revealed guidance when pressures pull in other directions. It is active steadfastness, not a feeling of spiritual security.

The order moves from belief to divine mercy, then to guidance. The believer does not guide himself by pride; he clings to God and is led. This creates humility in religious learning and courage in practice. A person may stumble without being lost if he continues to return and hold fast.

The Straight Path is the road that unites pure worship, justice, repentance, and good action. The final promise answers the uncertainty of the chapter’s warnings: the same Lord who exposes betrayal offers a clear route to mercy.

**Holding fast through pressure**

Clinging to Allah includes returning after a fall, seeking knowledgeable counsel, and refusing to let a hostile environment define the truth. Guidance is described as a gift received by those who hold fast, so perseverance and humility belong together. The straight path is walked one faithful decision at a time.
