## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:148

> Allah does not like negative thoughts to be voiced—except by those who have been wronged. Allah is All-Hearing, All-Knowing.

### Expanded Commentary

**The exception for the wronged**

Allah does not like negative speech to be voiced, except by one who has been wronged. The rule restrains slander, gossip, public shaming, and the pleasure of narrating another’s faults. The exception protects the victim’s right to complain, seek justice, warn others from harm, and describe the wrong accurately.

The exception is not unlimited permission to insult or exaggerate. A complaint should remain proportionate and directed toward remedy rather than revenge. A victim may tell a judge, counselor, trusted adviser, or relevant community authority what happened; broadcasting details to entertain an audience is a different act.

Allah hears the speech and knows the truth. This protects the silenced victim and restrains the false accuser. It also teaches listeners to distinguish a person seeking safety from a person seeking the pleasure of exposure.

**Complaint and gossip are different**

A victim may name a wrong to obtain safety or justice, while a listener who spreads the story for entertainment commits another harm. The exception should be applied with care: tell enough to protect and remedy, not more than necessary to punish a person socially after the legitimate purpose has ended.
