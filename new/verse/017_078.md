## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:78

> **Observe the prayer from the decline of the sun until the darkness of the night and the dawn prayer, for certainly the dawn prayer is witnessed ˹by angels˺.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Observe Prayer From Decline Of Sun Until Darkness Of Night**

Aqimi al-ṣalāta li-dulūki al-shamsi ilā ghasaqi al-layli — establish prayer from decline of sun until darkness of night. Aqimi from q-w-m, to establish, observe, keep up, from aqāma, to establish, observe, as aqimi establish prayer, observe prayer, as prayer, ṣalāh. Al-ṣalāta from ṣ-l-w, prayer, ṣalāh, formal prayer, as ṣalāh, prayer. Li-dulūki from d-l-k, decline, sinking, from dalaka, to decline, sink, rub, as dulūk, decline, sinking of sun, as sun declines from zenith, as decline of sun, as Dhuhr, Asr, etc., from decline of sun. Al-shamsi from sh-m-s, sun, from shams, sun, as sun, as night and day signs, sun and moon, etc. Ilā ghasaqi from gh-s-q, darkness, gloom, from ghasaq, darkness, gloom, darkness of night, as ghasaq al-layl, darkness of night, gloom of night, as darkness, as night, as layl night. Al-layli from l-y-l, night, as layl night, as night and day signs, etc. Al-Ṭabarī explains that observe prayer from decline of sun until darkness of night, meaning establish, observe prayers from decline, sinking of sun, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha, until darkness of night, gloom of night, as prayers from decline of sun until darkness, as Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha, as time from decline of sun until darkness of night includes those prayers. **And Dawn Prayer Certainly Dawn Prayer Witnessed By Angels**

Wa-qur'āna al-fajri inna qur'āna al-fajri kāna mashhūdan — and recitation of dawn, dawn prayer, indeed recitation of dawn is witnessed.
