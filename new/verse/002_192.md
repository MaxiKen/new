## Sūrah al-Baqarah 2:192

> But if they cease, then surely Allah is All-Forgiving, Most Merciful.

**Expanded Commentary**

**The Condition Stated**

The verse states that if the opposing party ceases, God is forgiving and merciful, and the sentence supplies the condition on which the fighting ends. The Book places the provision immediately after the command to fight, and the community's scholars read the two sentences as a pair.

The wording looks forward to the possibility that the conflict will stop, and the sentence names the attribute that governs the community's response. What the reader receives is the assurance that the passage's commands are bounded by an outcome the community is expected to accept.

**The Meaning of Ceasing**

The commentators explained the term as describing the abandonment of the conduct that occasioned the fighting, whether that conduct was hostility or the persecution that drove the community from its homes.

The Book's use of the same term in the following verse shows that the subject is the ending of the persecution rather than the acceptance of the faith, and the community's scholars read the passage with attention to that distinction. What the sentence establishes is that the cessation is a matter of conduct, and the reader is shown that the conflict's end is not made conditional on the belief of the opposing party — a point the sūrah's later statement that there is no compulsion in religion also carries.

**The Names at the End of the Sentence**

The sentence closes with the description of God as forgiving and merciful, and the community's teachers read the pair as a statement about the treatment of those who stop fighting. The Book states elsewhere that if the opposing party inclines toward peace, the community is to incline toward it and to place its trust in God, and it states that God does not forbid kindness toward those who have not fought the community. The community's teachers gathered those passages with the present verse when they set out the Book's teaching on relations with other groups.
