## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:23

> **For your Lord has decreed that you worship none but Him. And honour your parents. If one or both of them reach old age in your care, never say to them ˹even˺ ‘ugh,’ nor yell at them. Rather, address them respectfully.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Lord Decreed Worship None But Him**

Wa-qaḍā rabbuka allā taʿbudū illā iyyāhu — and your Lord decreed that you worship none but Him. Wa-qaḍā from q-ḍ-y, to decree, judge, ordain, decide, from same root as qaḍā', decree, judgment. Rabbuka, your Lord, from r-b-b. Allā taʿbudū, that you not worship, from ʿ-b-d, to worship, serve, as ʿibād, servants. Illā iyyāhu, except Him, only Him, from a-l-l and pronoun. Al-Ṭabarī explains that Lord decreed, ordained, commanded decisively, that you worship none but Him alone, no other god with Allah, as previous verse prohibited setting up other god. Qaḍā indicates decisive decree, not mere suggestion, as judge decrees. Al-Qurṭubī says qaḍā here means commanded, ordained, made incumbent, that you worship none but Him, which is foundation of most upright guidance, as Qur'an guides to most upright.

**Honour Your Parents**

Wa-bi-l-wālidayni iḥsānan — and with parents good, excellence, honour your parents. Wa-, and. Bi-l-wālidayni, with parents, to parents, from w-l-d, to beget, parents who beget, dual. Iḥsānan from ḥ-s-n, good, excellence, beautiful doing, from same root as aḥsantum, you do good, in verse 7, and muḥsin, doer of good. Al-Ṭabarī says and honour your parents, do good to parents with excellence, as parents are cause of existence after Allah, and honouring them is most upright after worshipping Allah alone. Al-Rāzī says iḥsānan indicates doing good with excellence, not merely not harming, but doing good excellently. This is second command after monotheism in moral code, showing importance of parents.

**If Parents Reach Old Age In Your Care**

Immā yablughanna ʿindaka al-kibara aḥaduhumā aw kilāhumā — if one or both reach old age with you, in your care. Immā, if, either. Yablughanna from b-l-gh, to reach, attain, from balagha, to reach, with emphasis, indeed reach. ʿIndaka, with you, in your care, near you, from ʿ-n-d. Al-kibara from k-b-r, old age, greatness, from kabura, to be great, old, from same root as akbar, greater. Aḥaduhumā, one of them, from a-ḥ-d, one. Aw kilāhumā, or both of them, from k-l-l, both. Al-Ṭabarī explains that if one or both parents reach old age with you, in your care, under your protection, you must honour them especially in old age when they are weak, needy. Old age is mentioned because need greatest then, and because parents in old age may be difficult, so command to be patient.

**Never Say Ugh Nor Yell**

Fa-lā taqul lahumā uffin wa-lā tanharhumā — then do not say to them even ugh, nor yell at them, scold. Fa-lā taqul from q-w-l, to say. Lahumā, to them, dual. Uffin, ugh, expression of annoyance, disgust, from a-f-f, to be annoyed, ugh. Wa-lā tanharhumā from n-h-r, to yell, scold, rebuke harshly, from nahara, to scold, to drive away. Al-Ṭabarī says do not say to them even ugh, smallest expression of annoyance, nor yell at them, scold them harshly. Uff is minimal word of annoyance, so if even that prohibited, greater annoyance more prohibited. Al-Qurṭubī says uff indicates any expression of disrespect, even slight, and tanharhumā indicates yelling, harsh rebuke, both prohibited, showing that parents in old age must be treated with utmost respect, patience.

**Address Them Respectfully**

Wa-qul lahumā qawlan karīman — and say to them noble word, respectful word. Wa-qul from q-w-l, to say. Lahumā, to them. Qawlan karīman, noble word, respectful, generous word, from q-w-l and k-r-m, noble, generous, honourable, from karuma, to be noble. Al-Ṭabarī says say to them noble, respectful, generous word, meaning gentle, kind, honourable speech, not harsh. Al-Rāzī says qawlan karīman includes respectful address, kind words, honouring, not demeaning. This is positive command after negative prohibitions: not only avoid ugh and yelling, but say respectful word.

**Why Parents After Monotheism**

Why parents after monotheism? Because parents are cause of existence after Allah, and gratitude to parents after gratitude to Allah, as Qur'an often pairs worship Allah and honour parents. Also because human is ever hasty and may be annoyed with old parents, so command to be patient and respectful, especially when they reach old age in your care. The sūrah's theme of gratitude appears: grateful servant Noah, gratitude for bounty never withheld, now gratitude to parents who raised when young, as next verse says be merciful to them as they raised me when young.

**Linguistic Note On Qada And Uff And Karim**

Qaḍā from q-ḍ-y, to decree, judge, ordain. Rabb from r-b-b, Lord. Taʿbudū from ʿ-b-d, to worship. Iyyāhu, Him, pronoun. Wālidayn from w-l-d, parents, dual of wālid, begetter. Iḥsān from ḥ-s-n, excellence, good. Yablughanna from b-l-gh, to reach, with emphasis. ʿIndaka from ʿ-n-d, with you. Kibar from k-b-r, old age. Aḥad from a-ḥ-d, one. Kilāhumā from k-l-l, both. Taqul from q-w-l, to say. Uff from a-f-f, ugh, annoyance. Tanhar from n-h-r, to yell, scold. Qawl from q-w-l, word. Karīm from k-r-m, noble, generous, honourable. The verse's structure — Lord decreed worship none but Him and with parents excellence, if one or both reach old age with you do not say ugh nor yell, but say noble word — with decree of monotheism, command of parents excellence, specific case of old age, prohibitions, positive command, shows comprehensive guidance for most upright dealing with parents.

**How This Verse Teaches Honouring Parents**

This verse teaches honouring parents with excellence, especially in old age in your care, never saying even ugh nor yelling, but addressing respectfully. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should honour parents with excellence, do good to them, be patient when they reach old age, avoid even smallest annoyance expression, avoid yelling, and say noble, respectful words. The practical lesson is to be mindful that parents in old age are test, as they raised you when young with mercy, so be merciful as next verse says pray My Lord be merciful to them as they raised me when young. The verse also teaches that monotheism and honouring parents are foundation of most upright, as Qur'an guides to most upright.

**A Reflection On Uff And Noble Word**

Uffin — ugh — smallest word of annoyance, prohibited, showing how sensitive honouring parents is. If even ugh prohibited, greater disrespect more prohibited. Qawlan karīman — noble, respectful word — positive, showing that speech to parents should be noble, generous, honourable, not demeaning. The believer who reflects will avoid ugh and yelling even when parents old and difficult, and will say noble word, as gratitude for raising when young, and as part of striving for Hereafter whose striving appreciated and ranks far greater.
