## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:85

> **They ask you ˹O Prophet˺ about the spirit. Say, “Its nature is known only to my Lord, and you ˹O humanity˺ have been given but little knowledge.”**

**Expanded Commentary**

**They Ask You About Spirit**

Wa-yas'alūnaka ʿani al-rūḥi — and they ask you about spirit, soul. Wa-yas'alūnaka from s-ʾ-l, to ask, question, as yas'alūnaka they ask you, O Prophet, about spirit, as they ask about spirit, as question. ʿAni al-rūḥi from r-w-ḥ, about spirit, soul, spirit, from rūḥ, spirit, soul, as spirit, soul, as rūḥ, spirit, as spirit, soul, as human spirit, etc., as they ask about rūḥ, spirit, what is spirit, etc. Al-Ṭabarī explains that they, disbelievers, polytheists, Jews, etc., ask you, O Prophet, about spirit, soul, what is spirit, nature of spirit, etc., as question, test, as they asked about spirit, as they asked about Dhul-Qarnayn, etc., as questions. Al-Qurṭubī says yas'alūnaka ʿani al-rūḥi means they ask you about spirit, soul, what is spirit, nature, etc., as question about spirit, soul, as human spirit, etc.

**Say Its Nature Known Only To My Lord**

Qul al-rūḥu min amri rabbī — say, spirit is from command, affair of my Lord. Qul from q-w-l, to say, answer, command to Prophet to say, answer. Al-rūḥu, spirit, soul, as rūḥ, spirit, soul. Min amri from a-m-r, from command, affair, matter, from amr, command, affair, matter, as from command, affair of my Lord, as spirit from command of Lord, affair of Lord, matter of Lord, as spirit is from command of Lord, not known except to Lord, as its nature known only to Lord. Rabbī, my Lord, from r-b-b, my Lord, as my Lord, as Lord, as Lord knows best, etc. Al-Ṭabarī explains that say, spirit is from command, affair of my Lord, meaning spirit, soul, is from command, affair, matter of my Lord, from His command, affair, not known except to Him, its nature known only to Lord, as its nature known only to my Lord, as spirit from command of Lord, as affair of Lord, as matter of Lord, as spirit is from amr, command, of Lord, as creation, etc.

**You Have Been Given But Little Knowledge**

Wa-mā ūtītum mina al-ʿilmi illā qalīlan — and you have not been given from knowledge except little, small. Wa-mā, and not, you have not been given. Ūtītum from a-t-y, to be given, given, from ātā, to give, as ūtītum you have been given, you were given, as you have been given from knowledge. Mina al-ʿilmi, from knowledge, from knowledge, as ʿilm knowledge, as knowledge, as sure knowledge, etc., as knowledge, as you have been given but little knowledge, small knowledge, as human knowledge little, small, compared to Lord's knowledge, as Lord knows best, All-Aware All-Seeing, knows best what is within selves, etc., and human knowledge little. Illā qalīlan, except little, only little, small, from q-l-l, little, small, few, as qalīlan little, small, as little knowledge, small knowledge, as you have been given but little knowledge, small knowledge, as human knowledge little, limited, compared to Lord's knowledge, infinite, etc. Al-Ṭabarī explains that you, O humanity, have been given but little knowledge, small knowledge, meaning human knowledge is little, small, limited, compared to Allah's knowledge, which is vast, infinite, as Lord knows best, All-Aware All-Seeing, knows best, etc., and spirit from command of Lord, nature known only to Lord, you have little knowledge, so do not ask about spirit beyond little knowledge.

**Why Spirit From Command Of Lord And Little Knowledge**

Why spirit is from command of Lord and you have been given but little knowledge? Because they asked about spirit, soul, what is spirit, nature, etc., as question, test, as they asked, and answer: spirit is from command, affair of my Lord, its nature known only to my Lord, and you have been given but little knowledge, small knowledge, limited, compared to Lord's knowledge, vast, infinite, as Lord knows best, All-Aware All-Seeing, knows best what is within selves, how they listen, you best, those in heavens and earth, whose way rightly guided, etc., knows best, and spirit from command of Lord, nature known only to Lord, you have little knowledge, so do not go beyond little knowledge into what is not given, as human knowledge little, limited, as everyone acts in own way, Lord knows best rightly guided, etc., and as Qur'an guides to most upright, explains everything in detail, but spirit from command of Lord, little knowledge given, as part of wisdom, etc.

**Connection To Everyone Acts In Own Way And Lord Knows Best Rightly Guided And If We Willed We Could Take Away What We Revealed**

Verse 84 everyone acts in own way, Lord knows best whose way rightly guided. Verse 85 they ask about spirit, say spirit from command of Lord, you have been given but little knowledge, showing that knowledge little, limited, Lord knows best, etc., and everyone acts in own way, Lord knows best rightly guided, as knowledge little, but Lord knows best, etc. Verse 86-87 if We willed We could have taken away what We revealed to you, then you would find none to guarantee its return from Us, had it not been for mercy of your Lord, indeed His favour upon you immense, showing that revelation, Qur'an, is from Lord, favour immense, mercy, not to be taken away, as spirit from command of Lord, little knowledge given, but revelation, Qur'an, favour immense, mercy, etc.

**Link To Quran Healing Mercy And Truth Come Falsehood Vanish And Human And Jinn Cannot Produce Equal**

Verse 82 Quran healing mercy for believers but increases wrongdoers in loss, verse 81 truth come falsehood vanish, indeed falsehood bound to vanish, verse 88 if all humans and jinn came together to produce equivalent of Quran they could not produce equal, no matter how they supported each other, showing Quran's miraculous nature, inimitability, as healing mercy, truth, vs falsehood bound to vanish, and humans and jinn cannot produce equal, even if supported each other, as Quran inimitable, as revelation, favour immense, mercy, etc., and as spirit from command of Lord, little knowledge given, but Quran, revelation, favour immense, etc.

**Linguistic Note On Rūḥ And Amr And Utiītum And Ilm And Qalilan**

Yas'alūnaka from s-ʾ-l, to ask, question, from sa'ala. Rūḥ from r-w-ḥ, spirit, soul, from rūḥ. Amr from a-m-r, command, affair, matter, from amr. Rabb from r-b-b, Lord. Ūtītum from a-t-y, to be given, from ātā. ʿIlm from ʿ-l-m, knowledge, from ʿalima, to know. Qalīl from q-l-l, little, small, few, from qalīl. The verse's structure — they ask you about spirit, say spirit is from command of my Lord, and you have not been given from knowledge except little — with question about spirit and answer spirit from command of Lord and statement you have been given but little knowledge, shows that spirit from command of Lord, nature known only to Lord, human knowledge little, limited, compared to Lord's knowledge vast, infinite, as Lord knows best, All-Aware All-Seeing, etc.

**How This Verse Teaches Spirit From Command Of Lord And Little Knowledge**

This verse teaches spirit from command of Lord and little knowledge given to humanity: they ask you, O Prophet, about spirit, soul, what is spirit, nature, etc., say, spirit is from command, affair, matter of my Lord, its nature known only to my Lord, from His command, affair, and you, O humanity, have been given but little knowledge, small knowledge, limited, compared to Lord's knowledge, vast, infinite, as Lord knows best, All-Aware All-Seeing, knows best what is within selves, how they listen, you best, those in heavens and earth, whose way rightly guided, etc., knows best, and spirit from command of Lord, nature known only to Lord, you have little knowledge, so do not ask beyond little knowledge, as human knowledge little. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should know spirit from command of Lord, nature known only to Lord, and human knowledge little, small, limited, compared to Allah's knowledge, vast, infinite, so be humble, not arrogant, not follow without sure knowledge, hearing, sight, heart accountable, etc., and seek knowledge, but know little knowledge given, as part of wisdom, most upright, that Qur'an guides to, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, healing mercy, etc.

**A Reflection On Little Knowledge And Spirit From Command**

Mā ūtītum mina al-ʿilmi illā qalīlan — you have not been given from knowledge except little, small — indicates human knowledge little, small, limited, compared to Allah's knowledge, vast, infinite, as Lord knows best, All-Aware All-Seeing, knows best what is within selves, etc., and spirit, rūḥ, from command of Lord, amr, command, affair, matter, of Lord, nature known only to Lord, you have little knowledge, so humble, not arrogant, not walk arrogantly, lower wing of humility, etc., and seek knowledge, but know little, as part of most upright guidance, wisdom, that Qur'an guides to, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, healing mercy, vs wrongdoers loss, etc. The believer who knows spirit from command of Lord, you have little knowledge, will be humble, not claim great knowledge, will seek sure knowledge, not follow without knowledge, hearing, sight, heart accountable, will follow most upright guidance, wisdom, that Qur'an guides to, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, healing mercy, etc., while disbelievers have barrier, veils, deafness, aversion, far astray cannot find Right Way, etc., painful punishment prepared.
