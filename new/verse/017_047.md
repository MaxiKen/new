## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:47

> **We know best how they listen to your recitation and what they say privately—when the wrongdoers say, “You would only be following a bewitched man.”**

**Expanded Commentary**

**We Know Best How They Listen And What They Say Privately**

Naḥnu aʿlamu bi-mā yastamiʿūna bihi idh yastamiʿūna ilayka wa-idh hum najwā — We know best with what they listen with it when they listen to you and when they are private conversation. Naḥnu aʿlamu, We know best, more knowing, from ʿ-l-m, to know, as aʿlamu knows best, as verse 25 Lord knows best what is within selves, and verse 54 Lord knows you best, and verse 55 Lord knows best those in heavens and earth. Bi-mā yastamiʿūna bihi, with what they listen, how they listen, with what they listen with it, from s-m-ʿ, to listen, hear, as samʿ hearing. Idh yastamiʿūna ilayka, when they listen to you, O Prophet, reciting Qur'an. Wa-idh hum najwā, and when they are private, private conversation, secret counsel, from n-j-w, private, secret, from najwā, private conversation, secret counsel. Al-Ṭabarī explains that We know best how they listen to your recitation, with what intention, mockery, spying, etc., and what they say privately, secret counsel, when they are in private, what they say secretly, as they have veils, deafness, aversion, barrier, but listen to Qur'an with mockery, spying, etc. Al-Qurṭubī says naḥnu aʿlamu indicates Allah knows best how they listen, with mockery, denial, and what they say privately in secret counsel.

**When Wrongdoers Say You Only Following Bewitched Man**

Idh yaqūlu al-ẓālimūna in tattabiʿūna illā rajulan masḥūran — when wrongdoers say, you follow not except a man bewitched, enchanted. Idh, when, as when they say privately. Yaqūlu al-ẓālimūna from ẓ-l-m, wrongdoers, oppressors, unjust, from ẓalama, to wrong, oppress, from ẓālim, wrongdoer, oppressor. In tattabiʿūna, you follow not, you follow only, from t-b-ʿ, to follow. Illā rajulan, except a man, only a man. Masḥūran from s-ḥ-r, bewitched, enchanted, affected by magic, from saḥara, to bewitch, enchant, from siḥr, magic, bewitchment, from masḥūr, bewitched, enchanted. Al-Ṭabarī explains that wrongdoers, oppressors, polytheists, say privately, in secret counsel, you would only be following a bewitched man, meaning Prophet Muhammad is bewitched, enchanted, affected by magic, so his Qur'an is magic, not revelation, so do not follow, as they say to followers, you follow only bewitched man, to dissuade. Al-Qurṭubī says this is what wrongdoers say privately, in najwā, secret counsel, to discourage, that Prophet is masḥūr, bewitched, so Qur'an is from bewitchment, not from Allah, as they claim.

**Why Wrongdoers Say Bewitched Man**

Why say bewitched man? Because they cannot comprehend Qur'an due to veils over hearts, deafness in ears, hidden barrier, aversion when Lord alone mentioned, so they think Qur'an is magic, bewitchment, as they have no sure knowledge, follow without knowledge, have denying hearts, arrogant. Saying bewitched man is attempt to explain Qur'an's effect, eloquence, varying signs, without believing, as they have no sure knowledge, follow conjecture. The sūrah's theme: those who do not believe in Hereafter have barrier, veils, deafness, aversion, so they say bewitched man.

**Connection To Hidden Barrier, Veils, Deafness, Aversion, Varying Signs**

Verses 45-46 said hidden barrier, veils over hearts unable to comprehend, deafness in ears, turning backs in aversion when Lord alone mentioned. Verse 47 says We know best how they listen and what they say privately, when wrongdoers say you follow only bewitched man. The connection is that despite barrier, veils, deafness, aversion, they still listen to Qur'an, but with mockery, spying, and say privately bewitched man, to explain Qur'an without believing, as they have no sure knowledge, follow without knowledge, have denying hearts. Varying signs in Qur'an should make mindful but drives farther away, as they say bewitched man.

**Link To See How They Call You Names So Astray Cannot Find Way**

Next verse 48 says observe how they label you, O Prophet, so misguided they cannot locate correct path. Verse 47 says they say you follow only bewitched man, which is calling name, saying masḥūr, bewitched, as name, insult. So following verse says observe how they label you, so far astray unable to find way, as they call bewitched, etc., as wrongdoers.

**Linguistic Note On Yastami'una And Najwa And Zalimuna And Mashur**

Aʿlam from ʿ-l-m, knows best. Yastamiʿūna from s-m-ʿ, to listen, from istamaʿa, to listen. Najwā from n-j-w, private conversation, secret counsel, from najā, to be private, secret. Yaqūlu from q-w-l, to say. Ẓālimūn from ẓ-l-m, wrongdoers, oppressors, from ẓalama. Tattabiʿūna from t-b-ʿ, to follow. Rajul from r-j-l, man. Masḥūr from s-ḥ-r, bewitched, enchanted, from saḥara, to bewitch. The verse's structure — We know best how they listen when they listen to you and when they are private, when wrongdoers say you follow only bewitched man — with knowledge of how they listen and private saying, and quotation of wrongdoers saying bewitched man, shows that Allah knows their listening with mockery and private counsel saying bewitched man, to dissuade, as they have veils, deafness, barrier, aversion.

**How This Verse Teaches Allah Knows Listening And Private Counsel**

This verse teaches that Allah knows best how disbelievers listen to Prophet's recitation, with what intention, mockery, spying, etc., and what they say privately in secret counsel, when wrongdoers say you follow only bewitched man, to dissuade, as they cannot comprehend due to veils, deafness, barrier, aversion, and have no sure knowledge. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should know Allah knows how people listen and private counsel, so be sincere in listening, not mock, and should not say Prophet bewitched, as wrongdoers do, but believe Qur'an is revelation, wisdom, most upright, guides to most upright, varies signs so mindful. The practical lesson is to listen to Qur'an with mindfulness, not mockery, to believe, not say bewitched man, as wrongdoers do, who have gone far astray cannot find Right Way.

**A Reflection On Bewitched Man Accusation**

Rajulan masḥūran — man bewitched, enchanted — accusation that Prophet is bewitched, affected by magic, so Qur'an is magic, not revelation, as wrongdoers say privately to dissuade followers, you follow only bewitched man, meaning if you follow Prophet you follow bewitched, not guided. This accusation is similar to previous accusations that Qur'an is magic, poet, etc., as disbelievers have no sure knowledge, follow without knowledge, have veils, deafness, barrier, aversion, denying hearts, arrogant. The believer knows Prophet not bewitched, Qur'an not magic, but revelation, wisdom, most upright, guides to most upright, explains everything in detail, varies signs so mindful, and that Allah knows best how they listen and private counsel, and that wrongdoers say bewitched man to dissuade, but see how they call names so far astray cannot find Right Way, as next verse says.
