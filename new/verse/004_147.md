## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:147

> Why should Allah punish you if you are grateful and faithful? Allah is ever Appreciative, All-Knowing.

### Expanded Commentary

**Why would God punish gratitude?**

The rhetorical question presents gratitude and faith as the conditions opposite to punishment. Allah is Appreciative and All-Knowing: He recognizes the smallest sincere response and knows the reality behind it. Punishment is not an arbitrary divine desire; it follows rejection and ingratitude.

Gratitude is more than saying praise. It acknowledges the Giver, uses blessings responsibly, and does not convert provision into a weapon against others. Faith is more than affiliation; it becomes trust, obedience, and return after error. When these are present, the believer has entered the path of mercy.

The verse consoles people who fear that their small efforts are unseen. Allah appreciates them fully, while His knowledge also sees the hidden obstacle or the quiet act that humans overlook.

**Gratitude is a reason for hope**

Allah’s appreciation means that a servant need not compete with others to be accepted. Notice the blessing, use it responsibly, and remain faithful in difficulty. Punishment is not the divine default toward the grateful person; the verse invites a life in which gratitude becomes the atmosphere of obedience.
