## Sūrah al-Ḥāqqah 69:31

> then burn them in Hell,

**Expanded Commentary**

**Hellfire Named and Stoked**

Thumma al-jaḥīma ṣallūh — "then roast him in the Blaze." Jaḥīm names intense, blazing Hellfire — a proper dread-name among Gehenna's titles. Ṣallūh commands roasting, scorching, exposure to flames — the causative form: make him burn, have him broiled. Thumma, "then," stages the torment: seizure first, scorching after, sequence deliberate. Naming particularizes dread: not fire vaguely but Jaḥīm specifically, chambers assigned, fuel stocked. Such naming personalizes torment the way addresses personalize mail — destination pronounced, delivery certain. The sentenced soul now knows exactly where it goes. Vagueness veiled the threat across a lifetime; naming unveils it in a syllable. Readers should hear Jaḥīm as a posted address and flee it while fleeing remains possible. For living hearers, repentance still averts the named fire. Flee the named Blaze through obedience; seek the named Gardens through devotion. Jaḥīm pronounced is refuge still open — until death closes the door it names.

**Roasting Follows Shackling**

Sequence sentences the soul in stages: seized (taken), fettered (bound), roasted (burned). Each stage mounts deliberately — dread climbing a staircase with no landing. Shackled helplessness precedes burning by design: bound first, then broiled, so no defense is possible, no shielding, no covering the face. Fetters forestall every instinct of self-protection. Such staging prolongs torment psychologically: each phase anticipated while enduring the last, dread compounding. Thumma paces the procedure — "then" separating stages, anticipation torturing between them. Readers should feel escalation as warning: capture was bad, binding worse, burning — the sentence still continues past it. What stages extend in eternity, mercy might have ended in life. Interrupt the sequence now, while interruption remains possible: repentance breaks chains pre-forged and quenches fires pre-stoked. Seized-then-burned awaits only the bound-for-Hell. Unbind through obedience; never roast through rebellion. Stages scheduled are stages avoidable — until death fixes the itinerary.

**The Blaze Prepared Long**

Hell stands readied since long before its inmates: chambers built, fuel stocked, wardens posted, chains measured. "Prepared for the disbelievers" (paraphrased from 3:131) — preparation predates sinners, certainty precedes sentence. Such readiness rebukes the oldest presumption: perhaps Hell is unready, metaphorical, mercifully delayed. No: ready, literal, awaiting. Antiquity of preparation matches certainty of sentence — God does not bluff with furnished furnaces. The fire's patience is not absence; stocked fuel waits for scheduled burning. Readers should weigh readiness as urgency: fire furnished awaits only furnishing-souls, and souls furnish themselves daily. What stands prepared receives the unprepared without surprise. Prepare oppositely and correspondingly: gardens also stand readied, for the righteous, with equal certainty. Two preparations, two populations, one choice. Ready Hell or ready Heaven — conduct chooses, habits enroll. Preparation is complete on both sides; only population remains pending. Choose gardens through obedience.

**Scorching Answers Coldness**

Cold hearts meet hot fire — requital's symmetry. Hearts unmoved by the poor's chill, unwarmed by truth's glow, frozen toward worship and weeping: such frozen sympathies thaw in flames. Inversion instructs precisely: warmth refused becomes warmth imposed, coldness chosen becomes burning endured. Hearts that iced over toward beggars, warners, and dawn prayers melt unwillingly where melting never ends. Such symmetry pervades divine requital: coldness gets scorched, hardness gets crushed, pride gets leveled. The punishment rhymes with the crime because justice is poetic. Readers should warm themselves livingly and voluntarily: glow toward the needy with charity, kindle at truth with study, thaw in worship with tears. What warmth shares now, flames spare then. Frozen hearts can thaw in this life through mercy given and received, or thaw in the next through fire endured. Thaw voluntarily through generosity; never thaw penally through scorching. Coldness now is pre-burning: cool toward God today, scorched by separation tomorrow. Warm now; rest cool then.

**Passive Voice, Active Horror**

Ṣallūh speaks causative: "make him burn, have him roasted." Wardens act; the wretch endures. Agency is stripped absolutely and grammatically: burned, never burning; object, never subject. Such passivity completes verse 29's perished power — the soul cannot act, only suffer; cannot move, only be moved. Grammar itself tortures: object-case eternally, subjecthood revoked. Active horror (flames working, wardens stoking) meets passive victim (soul enduring, flesh broiling). The sentence that began with seizure perfects helplessness with roasting. Readers should seize agency while grammar still permits: act now — repent, give, pray, forgive — while verbs still take you as subject. What agency spends Godward in life, passivity never claims hellward in death. Subjects choose their verbs; objects suffer verbs chosen. Choose subjecthood through obedience: pray actively, give deliberately, repent specifically. Never arrive where verbs happen to you forever. Act now; never be acted-upon then.

**Stages Prolong the Sentence**

Why stages at all? Seize-then-burn-then-chain extends dread deliberately: each phase anticipated during the last, each endured as its own eternity. Prolonging punishes: drawn sentences out-hurt instant ones, anticipation seasoning pain. Thumma...thumma across verses 31-32 paces methodically — "then...then," procedure savored. Such staging displays justice publicly too: sentence witnessed stage by stage, righteousness of wrath evident. The procedure that terrifies the sentenced instructs the spectators — angels witness, the saved observe from afar. Readers should interrupt staging at its earliest phase: repentance cancels scheduled stages before they commence. What thummas stack in sequence, tawbah topples entirely. Break the sequence livingly through return; skip every stage through surrender. Stages awaited are stages avoidable — until death's scheduling fixes them forever. Cancel via conduct now, or endure via sentence then. The stages stand scheduled but not yet started for every living hearer.

**Chains Await After Burning**

Burning ordered; chaining follows. Verse 32 will bind the scorched soul with a seventy-cubit chain — torment layered upon torment. Scorched first, then shackled at length: fire prepares the flesh, iron keeps it. Readers should brace for iron after flames: furnaces stoked, chains measured, lengths coiled. Procedure advances from burning to binding without rest. What follows coils seventy arms of chain around the roasted. The next verse orders chaining with a seventy-cubit chain, torment's third and longest stage.
