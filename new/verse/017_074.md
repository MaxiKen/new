## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:74

> **Had We not made you steadfast, you probably would have inclined to them a little,**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Had We Not Made You Steadfast You Would Have Inclined Little**

Wa-lawlā an thabbatnāka la-qad kidta tarkanu ilayhim shay'an qalīlan — and had We not made you firm, steadfast, you would have almost inclined to them a little. Wa-lawlā, and had not, were it not that, had We not. An thabbatnāka from th-b-t, to make firm, steadfast, stable, from thabbata, to make firm, steadfast, stable, as thabbatnāka We made you firm, steadfast, stable, as Allah made Prophet steadfast, firm, stable, not lured, not inclined. La-qad kidta from k-w-d, you were about to, almost, nearly, as kidta you were about to, almost, as kādū they were about to, almost, as kidta you almost inclined. Tarkanu from r-k-n, to incline, lean, trust, rely, from rakana, to incline, lean, trust, rely, as tarkanu incline to them, lean towards them, trust them, rely on them. Ilayhim, to them, towards them, disbelievers, polytheists, who tried to lure away. Shay'an qalīlan, a little, little thing, small amount, from sh-y-ʾ, thing, and q-l-l, little, small, few, as little, small inclination, a little. Al-Ṭabarī explains that had We not made you steadfast, firm, stable, you, O Prophet, probably would have inclined to them a little, almost inclined to them a little, meaning you would have almost leaned towards them, compromised a little, due to their attempt to lure you away, tempt you away from what We revealed, hoping you would attribute something else falsely and taken as close friend, as previous verse, but Allah made you steadfast, firm, stable, so you did not incline, not even a little, as Prophet protected, steadfast.

**Why Steadfastness From Allah Prevented Little Inclination**

Why steadfastness from Allah prevented little inclination? Because disbelievers were about to lure Prophet away from revelation, tempt him to attribute something else falsely to Allah for close friendship, as previous verse says they were about to lure you away, hoping attribute something else falsely and taken as close friend, and had Allah not made him steadfast, firm, stable, he would have almost inclined to them a little, small inclination, due to human nature, ever hasty, etc., but Allah made steadfast, firm, stable, as Lord encompasses people, knows best, etc., as He made steadfast, as He is Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, but torment fearsome, and as Our way with messengers no change, as messengers made steadfast, not inclined, etc., as divine law, as every wicked society destroyed or punished severely before Day written in Record, etc., and as double punishment would have been if inclined, as next verse says, then dual torment in worldly life and after death, no supporter against Us, as warning, even for Prophet, double punishment if inclined, so steadfastness essential.

**Connection To Lure Away And Attribute Falsely And Double Punishment**

Verse 73 they were about to lure you away from what We revealed hoping attribute something else falsely and taken as close friend. Verse 74 had We not made you steadfast you would have inclined to them a little. Verse 75 then would have inflicted dual torment in worldly life and after death with no supporter against Us, showing sequence: they were about to lure away, hoping attribute falsely, taken as close friend, had not made steadfast would have inclined a little, then dual torment, no supporter, as warning, as Prophet protected, steadfast, as Lord encompasses, knows best, etc., and as Our way with messengers no change, as messengers made steadfast.

**Link To Our Way With Messengers And Intimidate To Drive Out**

Verse 76 they were about to intimidate you to drive you out of land of Mecca, but then they would not have survived after you left except little while. Verse 77 this has been Our way with messengers We sent before you, you will never find any change in Our way, showing that luring away, inclining, intimidating to drive out are ways of disbelievers against messengers, but Our way with messengers is steadfast, no change, as messengers made steadfast, not lured, not inclined, not intimidated, etc., as divine law, as every wicked society destroyed or punished severely before Day written in Record, etc.

**Linguistic Note On Lawla And Thabbatnaka And Kidta And Tarkanu And Shay'an Qalilan**

Lawlā, had not, were it not that, from l-w-l-ā, had not. Thabbatnāka from th-b-t, to make firm, steadfast, stable, from thabbata. Kidta from k-w-d, to be about to, almost, nearly, from kāda. Tarkanu from r-k-n, to incline, lean, trust, rely, from rakana. Ilayhim, to them. Shay' from sh-y-ʾ, thing. Qalīl from q-l-l, little, small, few. The verse's structure — had We not made you firm, you would have almost inclined to them a little — with condition had not made firm and result almost inclined a little, shows that steadfastness from Allah prevented even little inclination to disbelievers who tried to lure away, tempt, hoping attribute falsely, taken as close friend, but Allah made steadfast, firm, stable, so no inclination, not even little, as Prophet protected, steadfast, as Lord encompasses, knows best, etc.

**How This Verse Teaches Steadfastness From Allah Prevents Inclination**

This verse teaches steadfastness from Allah prevents inclination to disbelievers: had We not made you steadfast, firm, stable, you, O Prophet, probably would have inclined to them, disbelievers who tried to lure away from revelation, hoping attribute something else falsely and taken as close friend, a little, small inclination, almost, as kādū, kidta, almost, about to, but Allah made steadfast, firm, stable, so no inclination, not even little, as Prophet protected, steadfast, as Lord encompasses people, knows best, All-Aware All-Seeing, Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, but torment fearsome, and as Our way with messengers no change, as messengers made steadfast, not inclined, etc., as divine law. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should seek steadfastness from Allah, be firm, stable, not incline to disbelievers, not be lured away from revelation, not attribute something else falsely to Allah for close friendship, but be steadfast, follow most upright guidance, wisdom, that Qur'an guides to, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, avoiding double punishment, no helper, as next verse says, dual torment in worldly life and after death, no supporter against Us, if inclined, even little, so seek steadfastness.

**A Reflection On Little Inclination And Steadfastness**

Shay'an qalīlan — a little, little thing, small amount — indicates that even little inclination to disbelievers, small leaning, trust, reliance, towards them, compromising a little, attributing something else falsely a little, would have led to double punishment, no helper, as next verse says, then We would have made you taste dual torment in worldly life and after death, no supporter against Us, as warning, even for Prophet, double punishment if inclined even little, so steadfastness from Allah essential, thabbatnāka, We made you firm, steadfast, stable, so no inclination, not even little, as Prophet protected, steadfast, as Lord encompasses, knows best, etc., and as Our way with messengers no change, as messengers made steadfast. The believer who reflects will seek steadfastness from Allah, be firm, stable, not incline to disbelievers even little, not be lured away from revelation, not attribute falsely, but be steadfast, follow most upright, wisdom, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, avoiding double punishment, no helper, Hell, torment fearsome, but Lord Most Forbearing All-Forgiving to those who constantly turn.
