## Sūrah an-Nūr 24:13

> Why did they not produce four witnesses? Now, since they have failed to produce witnesses, they are ˹truly˺ liars in the sight of Allah.

**Expanded Commentary**

**The Challenge of Evidentiary Burden**

The divine discourse confronts the scandalmongers with an uncompromising procedural challenge: *lawlā jāʾū ʿalayhi bi-arbaʿati shuhadāʾ*. In human societies, slanderers rarely operate through formal legal depositions; they thrive through insinuations, rhetorical questions, knowing glances, and private whispers that evade judicial scrutiny. The Qur'an abruptly drags the rumor out of the shadowy corridors of gossip into the luminous light of the courtroom, demanding to know where the required four eyewitnesses are.

By issuing this direct challenge, the revelation strips away all rhetorical pretense from the accusers. It makes no difference how convincingly the story was told, how emotionally it was delivered, or how many people repeated it in the marketplace. Divine law recognizes only one currency in allegations of sexual misconduct: four upright, independent eyewitnesses who observed the physical act directly. In the absence of those four witnesses, the entire narrative collapses into criminal slander, exposing the accusers to immediate legal liability and moral disgrace.

**Liars in the Sight of God: Judicial Designation versus Factual Reality**

The verse establishes a decisive jurisprudential ruling: *fa-idh lam yaʾtū bi-al-shuhadāʾi fa-ulāʾika ʿinda Allāhi humu al-kādhibūn*. The great theologian and commentator Fakhr al-Dīn al-Rāzī analyzed the critical legal question raised by the phrase *ʿinda Allāh* (in the sight of God). Does this mean the accuser is a liar purely as a matter of judicial classification, or does it apply even if the accuser believed they spoke the truth?

Al-Rāzī and classical jurists explained that under divine law, truthfulness in leveling accusations of indecency is legally synonymous with possessing the requisite four witnesses. If an individual makes a public allegation without four witnesses, God decrees that they are to be judged, classified, and treated as liars, regardless of what they privately believed. The divine judicial order refuses to entertain unprovable claims. In the sight of God, an unproven allegation is an active falsehood that pollutes the social order and incurs divine condemnation. The legal label reflects objective moral reality.

**The Absolute Protection of the Prophet's Household**

This rigid evidentiary barrier provided an impenetrable shield around the household of the Prophet ﷺ. The political enemies of Islam in Madinah, led by the hypocrites, recognized that they could not defeat the Muslim community on the battlefield after the failure of the confederates at the Trench. They therefore sought to assassinate the moral authority of the Messenger ﷺ by striking at his domestic sanctuary.

Had the legal system permitted public inquiries or trials based upon circumstantial evidence, suspicious coincidences, or solitary testimonies, the prophetic household would have been subjected to endless investigative intrusions and public degradation. By enforcing the absolute four-witness rule, the revelation established that the Prophet's ﷺ domestic sanctity was completely untouchable. Malicious conspiracies were halted at the threshold of the law, denied any procedural mechanism to interrogate the Mother of the Believers or cast shadows upon prophetic honor, preserving the integrity of divine leadership.

**The Distinction Between Rumor and Legal Evidence**

The verse articulates a vital principle in the epistemology of speech: repetition does not constitute corroboration. In popular culture, people frequently succumb to the cognitive fallacy that if an allegation is repeated by dozens of individuals across multiple gatherings, it must contain a grain of truth. The volume of noise is mistaken for the weight of evidence.

The Qur'an thoroughly dismantles this fallacy. A thousand voices repeating an unverified rumor possess exactly zero legal weight. Ten thousand gossipers echoing the same suspicion do not satisfy the requirement of even one witness, let alone four. Divine law insists that evidence must be empirical, direct, and individually verified by upright witnesses who stake their personal integrity before the court. Rumor, no matter how widespread, remains legally void, and multiplying an unproven assertion never transforms a lie into the truth, no matter how persistent the whispers appear.

**The Shifting of the Burden of Guilt**

A profound judicial transformation occurs within this verse regarding the burden of proof. In the court of public gossip, the scandalmongers had attempted to place the burden upon ʿĀʾishah and Ṣafwān, demanding that they explain why they were delayed in the desert, why she was riding his camel, and how they encountered one another. The innocent were placed in the exhausting position of defending their purity.

The divine verse forcefully reverses the courtroom dynamics. The accused owe no explanation whatsoever to the public; their established character and presumption of innocence are absolute. The accusers alone are placed on trial and interrogated by the divine voice: Where are your four witnesses? When they fail to produce them, the legal trap snaps shut upon their own necks. They are instantly transformed from self-appointed moral inspectors into convicted slanderers subject to divine wrath and public flogging, forced to face the judicial consequences of their malicious speech before all citizens.

**Institutional Truth as the Foundation of Community**

Reflecting on the broader political philosophy of this verse reveals why divine law enforces such uncompromising standards. A society that permits reputations to be demolished by unproven allegations cannot long remain free or peaceful. Suspicion becomes a weapon deployed by the unscrupulous to destroy rivals, terrorize families, and enforce social conformity through fear.

By declaring that anyone who speaks without four witnesses is a liar before God, the Qur'an creates a sacred perimeter around human dignity. Citizens are free to live their lives without the terror of being defamed by malicious neighbors or voyeuristic busybodies. Communal peace is anchored in verified institutional truth rather than the volatile whims of rumor, ensuring that justice remains an instrument of protection rather than an engine of persecution, preserving the moral sanity of the commonwealth across all generations.
