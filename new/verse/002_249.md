## Sūrah al-Baqarah 2:249

> When Saul marched forth with his army, he cautioned: “Allah will test you with a river. So whoever drinks ˹his fill˺ from it is not with me, and whoever does not taste it—except a sip from the hollow of his hands—is definitely with me.” They all drank ˹their fill˺ except for a few! When he and the ˹remaining˺ faithful with him crossed the river, they said, “Now we are no match for Goliath and his warriors.” But those ˹believers˺ who were certain they would meet Allah reasoned, “How many times has a small force vanquished a mighty army by the Will of Allah! And Allah is ˹always˺ with the steadfast.”

**Expanded Commentary**

**The Test Announced**

The verse reports that Talut set out with the troops and told them that God would test them with a river, and it states that whoever drank from it would not belong to him while whoever did not taste it would, with the exception of one who scooped a handful. The sentence follows the account of the appointment and supplies the trial through which the people were selected.

The Book's concern is the distinction between those who follow the command and those who do not, and the community's teachers read the sentence as the account of a test whose terms were announced before it was administered. Readers are shown that the passage treats the readiness of the people as the condition of the effort they had requested, and the sentence supplies the provision by which they were tried.

**The Test Described**

The sentence states that the people were tried with the water of a river and that the permission extended only to the smallest quantity, and the commentators explained that the test concerned the control of appetite after a march. The wording is precise in the exception it grants, and the community's scholars discussed the details.

The Book's use of a simple provision as the instrument of a trial appears elsewhere in its narratives, and the community's teachers read the passage as the statement about the discipline that the effort requires. Readers are given the account of a command that could be observed without difficulty and that revealed the condition of those who received it.

**The Outcome Recorded**

The sentence states that the people drank from the river except for the few who observed the command, and the community's scholars read the account as the statement that the trial separated the company. The commentators noted that the number of those who passed was small.

The wording places the outcome within the narrative, and the community's teachers drew the lesson that the willingness of the people to declare their readiness was not matched by their conduct when the command was given. **The Answer of the Confident**

The river that the sentence names has been identified by the commentators, and the reports place the crossing on the way to the encounter with the enemy. The terms of the test were announced before the company set out, so that the people knew the provision they were required to observe.

The result that the sentence records has been read as the account of a discipline that the company had not yet acquired.
