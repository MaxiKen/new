## Sūrah al-Maʿārij 70:29

> and those who guard their chastity

**Expanded Commentary**

**Chastity Listed Fifth**

The fifth duty guards private parts. Prayer came first, wealth's due second, belief in the Day third, fear of punishment fourth — now purity of body. Alladhīna hum li-furūjihim ḥāfiẓūn: "those, they who guard their private parts." Ḥifẓ means guarding, custody, vigilant protection. Furūj names the private parts, modesty's seat and desire's gate. The triplet spanning verses 29-31 repeats Sūrah 23 verbatim (23:5-7) — twin witness across two chapters, single unchanging standard. Such doubling stresses non-negotiability: chastity is not cultural option but creational law. Placement fifth teaches sequence: worship, generosity, conviction, and dread must precede purity — fenced bodies need founded souls. Readers should rank purity accordingly: prayed, gave, believed, feared — then guarded. What the list orders fifth, life should practice fifth. Guard chastity; complete the quintet. Purity crowns the God-fearing.

**Guarding Means Governing**

Ḥifẓ governs rather than kills: desire stewarded, channeled, dignified — never slaughtered, never unleashed. Appetite is legitimate territory requiring custody, like orchards needing fences. Guards protect treasures: chastity stands treasured, therefore fenced. Such governing honors desire's Creator: lawful satisfaction affirmed, unlawful trespass refused. The middle path runs between indulgence and demonization — moderation that neither worships appetite nor curses it. Monastic denial and libertine license both fail the verse; governing alone passes. Readers should govern their members actively: direct appetites Godward through marriage, fence them against trespass through vigilance. What wise governing guides toward bliss, anarchy ruins toward fire. Steward desire as trust; never serve it as master. Guardianship dignifies the guarded; license degrades the licensed. Govern well.

**Eyes Guard First**

Looking precedes acting, glances precede falls. Gazes lead loins step by step: glances linger, imaginations kindle, desires demand, bodies follow. The Book orders the sequence explicitly — lower the gaze first, then guard private parts (paraphrased from 24:30-31). Eyes form the first fence: lowered promptly, averted firmly, modest consistently. Such ordering prevents at the cheapest point: battles won at the eyes never reach the body. Glance-guarded souls never fall; glance-indulged souls rarely stand. The gate determines the fortress. Readers should post eye-sentries urgently: lower gazes on first awareness, look away before lingering, flee screens that solicit. What eyes refuse, bodies never crave; what eyes feed, bodies demand. Guard glances and chastity follows naturally. First fence standing, fortress holds. Eyes: the fortress gate.

Chastity's wisdom spans society, not just souls. Clear lineages, stable families, intact trusts — purity's public harvest. Adultery wrecks correspondently: betrayed spouses, confused offspring, feuding clans, diseased bodies. Guarding preserves what license loots: honor kept, homes whole, children secure. Such social fruits motivate beyond personal piety: purity serves everyone. The verse's plural — ḥāfiẓūn, a guarding community — pictures chaste society: trust ambient, suspicion rare, families flourishing. Each guardian's fence joins neighbors' into civilization's wall. Readers should guard for all, not just self: personal purity builds public trust. What chastity protects across generations, license destroys within one. Guard privately in closets; benefit publicly in streets. Purity's wage is ambient trust. Societies of guardians prosper; societies of licensees rot.

**Fear's Fruit in Flesh**

Verses 27-28 feared punishment; verse 29 guards bodies — dread produces discipline. God-feared souls fence their flesh: audit-dread bridles appetite, exposure-fear chains lust. Inward trembling yields outward fencing. Such causality links the list inseparably: fear flowers as chastity, reverence ripens as restraint. Unfeared bodies roam where they please; feared bodies guard where commanded. The sequence cannot reverse: fenced bodies without trembling hearts breed Pharisaism — outward purity, inward rot. Hearts first, members after. Readers should grow fear into fences deliberately: dread the audit genuinely, and guarding follows gladly. What trembling roots in conscience, purity fruits in conduct. Fear first as root; fence after as fruit. Dreaded Lord, guarded body — cause then effect. Cultivate dread; harvest discipline.

God sees closets, screens, and midnight hours: secret chastity stands fully witnessed. Integrity unseen proves integrity indeed — guards posted where only God inspects. Such awareness purifies at the deepest level: private guarded exactly as public. Hypocrisy guards publicly while roaming privately; sincerity guards both identically. Locked doors test true governors: solitude is sincerity's examination hall. Readers should guard the unseen rigorously: darkened rooms, private browsers, solitary travel — God present in all. What privacy tempts, Presence restrains. Never-private known deeply; always guard accordingly. Sincerity's test is solitude passed repeatedly. Pass unseen daily; shine seen effortlessly. The private guarded becomes the public glorified.

**Modesty Dresses the Guardian**

Ḥayāʾ — modesty — clothes chastity outwardly: dress covered, speech clean, conduct reserved. Shuʿayb's daughter walked to Moses "with modesty" (paraphrased from 28:25) — gait itself guarded. Such dress witnesses: modesty advertised deters approach. Clothing preaches before words: covered dignity invites respect. Speech likewise: clean tongues signal fenced bodies. Readers should dress guardedly: cover beautifully, speak purely, act reservedly. What modesty wears, chastity keeps. Garments guard; speech shields. Modesty: chastity's uniform.

Purity and prayer reinforce mutually. Guarded members sharpen devotion: clean consciences concentrate, uncluttered hearts attend. Conversely, neglected chastity deadens worship — guilt distracts, shame distances, habit hardens. Feedback loops run both directions: prayer strengthens restraint, restraint sweetens prayer. Such linkage explains the list's order — prayer first, chastity fifth: worship founds the purity it then requires. Neither thrives alone. The prayerless rarely stay chaste; the unchaste rarely pray well. Readers should repair both together: stumbling in one, shore up the other. Missed prayers weaken fences; mended prayers rebuild them. What worship waters, purity grows. Fortify prayer; chastity follows. Twin disciplines, single soul.

**Exceptions Named Next**

Guarding commanded absolutely; lawful exceptions follow immediately. Verse 30 will name chastity's permitted channels — spouses and rightful possessions. Rule first, then allowance: fence built high, gates placed wisely. Readers should learn the gates precisely: lawful satisfaction honored, unlawful trespass damned. What follows permits purely within bounds. The next verse names chastity's lawful exceptions, spouses and rightful possessions.
