## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:35

> **Give in full when you measure, and weigh with an even balance. That is fairest and best in the end.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Give Full When Measure And Weigh With Even Balance**

Wa-awfū al-kayla idhā kiltum wa-zinū bi-l-qisṭāsi al-mustaqīmi — and fulfill measure when you measure and weigh with straight, even balance. Wa-awfū from w-f-y, to fulfill, complete, give full, as previous verse awfū bi-l-ʿahdi, fulfill pledge. Al-kayla from k-y-l, measure, volume measure, from kāla, to measure. Idhā kiltum from k-y-l, when you measure, you measure for others. Wa-zinū from w-z-n, to weigh, from wazana, to weigh, as wizr burden from w-z-r, different root, but wazn weight. Bi-l-qisṭāsi from q-s-ṭ, balance, scale, even, just, from qisṭās, balance, scale, from q-s-ṭ, justice, evenness, from qist, justice, fairness. Al-mustaqīmi from q-w-m, straight, even, upright, from istaqāma, to be straight, upright, from same root as mustaqīm, straight, as ṣirāṭ mustaqīm, straight path, and qiyāmah standing. Al-Ṭabarī explains that give in full when you measure, fulfill measure, do not cheat, give full volume, and weigh with even, straight, just balance, scale, not crooked, cheating. Al-Qurṭubī says this is command to give full measure and weigh with even balance, just, fair, not cheating, as part of justice in transactions, protecting wealth, as previous verse protected orphan wealth.

**Fairest And Best In End**

Dhālika khayrun wa-aḥsanu ta'wīlan — that is best, fairest, and best in end, interpretation, result. Dhālika, that, that practice, giving full measure and even balance. Khayrun from kh-y-r, good, better, best, fairest, from khayr, good. Wa-aḥsanu from ḥ-s-n, better, best, more excellent, as aḥsanu best, as previous verse aḥsanu best for orphan wealth. Ta'wīlan from a-w-l, interpretation, result, end, outcome, from awwala, to interpret, result, from ta'wīl, interpretation, result, end. Al-Ṭabarī says that is fairest, better, and best in end, result, outcome, meaning giving full measure and even balance is fairest, most just, and best in end, result, outcome, in world and Hereafter, as it leads to trust, blessing, and avoids punishment. Al-Rāzī says khayrun indicates good, fairest in world, and aḥsanu ta'wīlan indicates best in end, Hereafter, result, as justice leads to good outcome.

**Why Measuring And Weighing Justice Commanded**

Why measuring and weighing justice? Because wealth protection requires justice in transactions, as orphan wealth protected, pledges honoured, now measuring and weighing fairly, not cheating, as part of most upright guidance, wisdom, protecting wealth, property, rights. Cheating in measure and weight is common injustice, especially in markets, so Qur'an commands giving full, even balance, as most upright, fairest, best in end. The sūrah's theme of bounty never withheld, provision abundant or limited, favouring some over others, but justice in measuring ensures fair distribution, not cheating.

**Connection To Orphan Wealth And Pledges And Knowledge**

Verses 34-36: do not approach orphan wealth except best until maturity, honour pledges accountable, give full when measure and weigh with even balance fairest best in end, do not follow what you have no sure knowledge of, hearing, sight, intellect accountable. The connection is protection of wealth and commitments and knowledge: orphan wealth protected, pledges honoured, measuring fair, knowledge not followed without sure knowledge, all accountable, all part of most upright, wisdom, that Qur'an guides to. The series shows comprehensive justice: wealth, pledges, measure, knowledge, humility.

**Link To Previous Chapter About Measuring? And Commercial Justice**

The moral code in this sūrah includes commercial justice: give full measure, even balance, as part of wisdom. Previous communities like Shuʿayb's people were punished for cheating in measure and weight, as Qur'an mentions. The command to give full measure and even balance is to avoid punishment that befell previous peoples who cheated, as many generations destroyed after Noah for such sins, as verse 17 said how many peoples destroyed after Noah, sufficient Lord as All-Aware All-Seeing of sins.

**Linguistic Note On Kayl And Qistas And Mustaqim And Tawil**

Awfū from w-f-y, to fulfill, give full. Kayl from k-y-l, measure, volume. Kiltum from k-y-l, you measured. Zinū from w-z-n, to weigh, from wazana. Qisṭās from q-s-ṭ, balance, scale, justice, even, from qist, justice. Mustaqīm from q-w-m, straight, even, upright, from istaqāma, to be straight. Khayr from kh-y-r, good, best. Aḥsan from ḥ-s-n, better, best. Ta'wīl from a-w-l, interpretation, result, end, from awwala. The verse's structure — fulfill measure when you measure and weigh with even balance, that is best and best in end — with command to give full measure and even balance and statement that is best and best in result, shows that justice in measuring is best, fairest, and leads to best outcome.

**How This Verse Teaches Commercial Justice**

This verse teaches commercial justice: give in full when you measure, do not cheat, give full volume, and weigh with even, straight, just balance, scale, not crooked, cheating, as that is fairest, best, and best in end, result, outcome. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should give full measure and even balance, be just, fair, not cheat in transactions, as cheating is injustice, leads to loss of trust, punishment, while justice leads to blessing, trust, best outcome in world and Hereafter. The practical lesson is to be just in measuring and weighing, give full, even balance, as part of most upright guidance, wisdom, protecting wealth, rights, leading to appreciated striving, mighty reward.

**A Reflection On Fairest And Best In End**

Dhālika khayrun wa-aḥsanu ta'wīlan — that is fairest, best, and best in end, result — indicates that justice in measure and weight is not only fairest now but best in end, result, outcome, in world and Hereafter, as it leads to trust, blessing, provision, and avoids punishment, painful punishment, and leads to mighty reward. The believer who reflects will give full when measure and weigh with even balance, as fairest and best in end, as part of most upright guidance, wisdom, protecting wealth, fulfilling pledges, protecting orphan wealth, not killing sacred life, not approaching adultery, not killing children, giving due to relatives, poor, traveller, honouring parents, worshipping Allah alone, so that striving appreciated, ranks far greater in Hereafter.
