## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:38

> **The violation of any of these ˹commandments˺ is detestable to your Lord.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Violation Of These Commandments Detestable To Lord**

Kullu dhālika kāna sayyi'uhu ʿinda rabbika makrūhan — all that, its evil, its sin, violation, is with your Lord detestable, hated, disliked. Kullu dhālika, all that, all of these, all those commandments, prohibitions, instructions, mentioned from verse 22 to 37, monotheism, parents, relatives, poor, traveller, moderation, provision, children, adultery, life sacred, orphan wealth, pledges, measure, knowledge, arrogance, all that. Kāna, is, was, indicates it is. Sayyi'uhu from s-w-ʾ, evil, bad, sin, evil deed, bad deed, from sā'a, to be evil, bad, from sayyi', evil, bad, sin, evil deed. ʿInda rabbika, with your Lord, before your Lord, in sight of your Lord, from ʿ-n-d and r-b-b, Lord, master, sustainer. Makrūhan from k-r-h, detestable, hated, disliked, abhorred, from kariha, to hate, dislike, detest, abhor, from makrūh, detestable, hated, disliked, abhorred. Al-Ṭabarī explains that all that, its evil, sin, violation of these commandments, is with your Lord detestable, hated, disliked, meaning Allah hates, detests, dislikes violation of these commandments, prohibitions, such as setting up other god, dishonouring parents, saying ugh, yelling, not lowering wing of humility, withholding due, wasteful spending, being tight-fisted, openhanded to poverty, killing children for fear of poverty, approaching adultery, killing sacred life except legal right, exceeding in retaliation, approaching orphan wealth except best, breaking pledges, cheating measure, following without knowledge, walking arrogantly, all evil, detestable to Lord.

**Why Detestable To Lord Emphasized**

Why detestable to Lord emphasized? Because these commandments are most upright guidance, wisdom, ḥikmah, that Qur'an guides to, as verse 9 says guides to most upright and verse 39 says this is part of wisdom revealed, and violation is evil, detestable, hated by Lord, not minor, not small, serious, grave. The phrase ʿinda rabbika makrūhan, with your Lord detestable, indicates that even if people may not see violation as detestable, may think not detestable, with Lord, before Lord, it is detestable, hated, disliked, so avoid, as Lord All-Aware All-Seeing of servants, knows sins, sufficient as All-Aware All-Seeing, knows what is within selves, knows best. The sūrah's theme of Lord knows best what is within selves, All-Aware All-Seeing, sufficient as Aware Seeing, etc., indicates that detestable to Lord matters more than detestable to people, criterion is Lord's view, not people's.

**Connection To Wisdom And Most Upright And Detestable**

Verse 39 will say this is part of wisdom which Lord revealed to you, O Prophet, and do not set up other god or cast into Hell blameworthy rejected. Verse 38 says violation of any of these commandments detestable to Lord, indicating that these commandments are wisdom, most upright, and violation evil, detestable, hated. The connection is that moral code from verse 22 to 37 is wisdom, ḥikmah, revealed, most upright, and violation detestable, so follow wisdom, avoid detestable, follow most upright. The most upright guidance includes monotheism, honouring parents, giving due, moderation, protecting life, wealth, pledges, measure, knowledge, humility, all wisdom, and violation detestable, evil, hated. The most upright leads to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, while violation leads to painful punishment, Hell, detestable.

**Link To Previous Chapter About Evil And Detestable And Consequences**

Previous chapter mentioned evil consequences, detestable, evil overtaking, etc., and said to those who disbelieve in Hereafter belongs evil qualities, and mentioned evil. This verse says violation detestable to Lord, indicating that evil, sayyi', is detestable, hated, not loved, and leads to evil consequences, as previous chapter said evil consequences will overtake, etc. The sūrah's moral code is to avoid evil, do good, as verse 7 said if you do good for yourselves, if do wrong against yourselves, and verse 9 Qur'an guides to most upright, gives good news to believers who do good mighty reward, warns those who do not believe painful punishment. Violation of commandments is doing wrong against self, evil, detestable to Lord, leading to painful punishment, Hell.

**Linguistic Note On Sayyi' And Makruh And Ind**

Kull from k-l-l, all, every. Dhālika from dh-l-k, that, that. Sayyi' from s-w-ʾ, evil, bad, sin, evil deed, from sā'a, to be evil, bad. ʿInda from ʿ-n-d, with, before, in sight of. Rabb from r-b-b, Lord. Makrūh from k-r-h, detestable, hated, disliked, from kariha, to hate, dislike, detest, from karh, hate, dislike. The verse's structure — all that its evil is with your Lord detestable — with all that, its evil, with Lord, detestable, shows that violation of these commandments, its evil, sin, is detestable to Lord, hated, disliked, so avoid, follow commandments, most upright, wisdom.

**How This Verse Teaches Avoiding Evil Detestable To Lord**

This verse teaches avoiding evil, violation of commandments, as detestable to Lord, hated, disliked, abhorred. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should avoid violation of any of these commandments, as its evil is detestable to Lord, hated, disliked, even if people may not hate, may not see as detestable, Lord hates, and Lord is All-Aware All-Seeing, knows, sufficient as Aware Seeing, and will punish, as painful punishment prepared for those who do not believe and do evil, as Hell confinement for disbelievers, as cast into Hell blameworthy rejected for shirk. The practical lesson is to follow commandments, most upright, wisdom, avoid violation, evil, detestable to Lord, to achieve mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour in Hereafter, and avoid painful punishment, detestable, Hell.

**A Reflection On Detestable To Lord As Criterion**

Makrūhan ʿinda rabbika — detestable with your Lord, before your Lord — indicates that criterion for detestable, hated, is Lord's view, not people's view, as Lord knows best what is within selves, All-Aware All-Seeing of servants, knows sins, sufficient as All-Aware All-Seeing of sins of servants, knows best what is within. What is detestable to Lord is truly detestable, evil, should be avoided, even if people think not detestable, think good. The commandments from monotheism to humility are wisdom, most upright, violation evil detestable to Lord, so follow wisdom, avoid evil, be righteous, ṣāliḥ, constantly turn, awwāb, All-Forgiving to awwābīn. The believer who reflects will avoid violation of any of these commandments, as evil detestable to Lord, will follow most upright guidance, wisdom, that Qur'an guides to, leading to appreciated striving, mighty reward, far greater rank and favour in Hereafter, and avoiding painful punishment, Hell, detestable.

**Why All That Includes Positive And Negative Commandments**

Kullu dhālika — all that — includes both positive commands, like worship none but Him, honour parents with excellence, give due to relatives, poor, traveller, fulfill pledges, give full measure, and negative prohibitions, like do not set up other god, do not say ugh, do not yell, do not spend wastefully, do not be tight-fisted nor openhanded, do not kill children, do not approach adultery, do not kill sacred life, do not approach orphan wealth except best, do not follow without knowledge, do not walk arrogantly. Its evil, sayyi'uhu, refers to violation, evil of violating, evil aspect, which is detestable to Lord. So all that, its violation evil detestable, meaning all commandments, violating them evil detestable, not following them evil detestable. The verse therefore summarizes moral code: follow all, avoid violation, as violation detestable to Lord, evil, hated, leading to Hell blameworthy rejected if shirk, etc.
