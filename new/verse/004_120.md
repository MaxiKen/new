## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:120

> Satan only makes them ˹false˺ promises and deludes them with ˹empty˺ hopes. Truly Satan promises them nothing but delusion.

**Expanded Commentary**

**Satan Promises and Deludes With Empty Hopes**

The verse states *yaʿiduhum wa yummanīhim* — he promises them and deludes them with hopes, with *umniyah*, wishing. *Yaʿiduhum* — to promise, to hold out — was explained as Satan’s currency after being cursed and taking an appointed share (4:118) and ordering misleading, slitting ears, altering creation (4:119). The promises were explained as the hopes already named — *ummaniyahum* — empty hopes: that association is nearness, that invoking females brings intercession (4:117), that cutting cattle is devotion, that altering creation is honour, that throwing sin on the innocent cleans the thrower (4:112), that advocacy in this life will have advocacy on the Day (4:109). The sūrah has shown that those who wish you deviate wish it greatly (4:27), that the lagger wishes to share gain without sharing faith (4:73), that the regressed wish you alike in disbelief (4:89); Satan’s promising is the source wish that generates those. *Wa yummanīhim* — and he deludes with hopes — was read as keeping them in wishing — *tamannī* — so that the wish never becomes turning, as the sūrah warns that repentance delayed until death says “now I repent” is not accepted (4:18).

**Truly He Promises Nothing But Delusion**

The qualification *wa mā yaʿiduhumu al-shayṭānu illā ghurūrā* — Satan promises them nothing but delusion, deception, vanity — was explained with *ghurūr* — delusion, deceptive vanity, gloss that vanishes. *Ghurūr* was read as promise that looks like benefit but is harm, as goods neglected for a sweeping assault where weapons are unguarded (4:102) looked safe for a moment. The sūrah elsewhere says *“do not let the deceiver deceive you about God”* (31:33; 35:5) with *gharūr* as Satan’s name, showing that delusion is his title. To say he promises *only* delusion — *illā ghurūra* — was explained as exclusivity: there is no true promise in his portfolio, as the next verses contrast his delusion with Hell without escape (4:121) and with Gardens where God’s promise is always true (4:122). Early notes linked *ghurūr* to the world’s fleeting enjoyment — *matāʿ qalīl* — the little enjoyment the sūrah said is little versus the Hereafter better for the mindful (4:77), and the fleeting gain sought by denying peace (4:94); Satan promises that little as if great. The sūrah has shown that good and evil are both destined while evil from the self as means (4:79); Satan’s delusion is to make the means — altering creation, slitting ears — seem as if it changes destiny toward good.

**A Verse That Follows Altering Creation With Its True Price**

Placed after *he will order slitting ears and altering creation and whoever takes him as guardian suffers tremendous loss* (4:119), this verse follows the order with its true price: the order is paid in delusion. The sūrah that warns there is no good in most secret talks except charity, kindness, reconciliation (4:114) here unmasks the secret talks that command altering: their payment is *ghurūr*. The sūrah that commands to verify reports (4:83) and to be sure before fighting (4:94) shows verification failing where hope is empty: the deluded do not verify Satan’s promise because it is a hope, not a proof. The sūrah has shown that God’s promise — *“We will admit them into Gardens under which rivers flow forever; God’s promise is always true, whose word is more truthful than God’s?”* (4:122) — is tied to *“God does not wrong even an atom”* (4:40); Satan’s promise is tied to loss manifest. The verse was read as the sūrah’s ledger for transaction with Satan: he promises and deludes, you alter, you take him as guardian, you bear clear loss, and his promise, when examined, is nothing but vanity.

**The Economy of Empty Hope**

The concept *umnīyah* — empty hope, wish — was explained linguistically as wishing for what will not be attained by wishing, as the People of the Book and your wishes wish grace by wish alone — *neither by your wishes nor theirs* (4:123). Satan’s economy was described as selling hope without means: he commands slitting ears and altering, which cost the cutter, and pays with hope, which costs him nothing. The sūrah has shown that the believer’s hope is *“you hope from God what they never hope for”* (4:104) — a hope tied to ranks and mercy (4:96) and many havens (4:100) — while Satan’s hope is tied to nothing. The contrast was linked to the beginning where God created from a single soul and spread humanity (4:1) intact; to alter that creation on a hope is to damage the known for the imagined. The verse was also linked to the prior deception where advocates hoped to deceive the Prophet ﷺ yet deceived only themselves (4:113); self-deception is the product sold.

**A Short Verse That Anchors the Two Destinations**

Though only *“he promises and deludes, he promises nothing but delusion,”* the verse anchors the two destinations that follow. Immediately: *they will have Hell as home and find no escape* (4:121) — the delusion’s end near; and then: *those who believe and do good, We will admit into Gardens forever, God’s promise always true* (4:122) — the true promise’s end. The sūrah that let the defier be turned to what he turned to (4:115) and declared association far straying (4:116) here names the engine of that turning: a promise that is nothing but gloss. For the weak for whom burdens were lightened, the verse is a lightening: you need not be deluded, because the promise that would delude you has been labelled — *illā ghurūrā* — nothing but, so that verification — *tabayyanū* (4:94) — can dismiss it and choose the promise that is ever true.
