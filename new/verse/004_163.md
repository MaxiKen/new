## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:163

> Indeed, We have sent revelation to you ˹O Prophet˺ as We sent revelation to Noah and the prophets after him. We also sent revelation to Abraham, Ishmael, Isaac, Jacob, and his descendants, ˹as well as˺ Jesus, Job, Jonah, Aaron, and Solomon. And to David We gave the Psalms.

**Expanded Commentary**

**We Have Sent Revelation to You as We Sent to Noah and Those After Him**

The verse opens *innā awḥaynā ilayka kamā awḥaynā ilā Nūḥin wa al-nabiyyīna min baʿdih* — indeed We have inspired, revealed, to you as We inspired to Noah and the prophets after him. *Awḥaynā* — to inspire, to reveal, to convey — was explained as divine communication to heart, as the Book was sent with truth to judge between people by what He shows (4:105) and as *“O Prophet, have faith in God, Messenger, Book He revealed to His Messenger and Scriptures before”* (4:136) — revelation is one chain. *Kamā awḥaynā ilā Nūḥin* — as We revealed to Noah — was read as that Nūḥ is first of the chain of law-giving messengers after Adam, as the sūrah earlier says *“to Him belongs whatever is in heavens and earth”* (4:126) — Noah’s ark upon water that belongs to Him is the first rescue of the single soul spread (4:1). *Wa al-nabiyyīna min baʿdih* — and prophets after him — was explained as that the revelation to you is not isolate but continuation, as Abraham pure — *ḥanīf* — was taken as friend (4:125) and his way is best faith. The verse generalises the sūrah’s lineage: from Noah through Abraham to you is one *waḥy* — revelation — whose truth was made clear where Mount was raised (4:154) and whose distinction not made is true belief (4:152).

**Also to Abraham, Ishmael, Isaac, Jacob, Descendants, Jesus, Job, Jonah, Aaron, Solomon — And Psalms to David**

The enumeration *wa awḥaynā ilā Ibrāhīma wa Ismāʿīla wa Isḥāqa wa Yaʿqūba wa al-asbāṭi wa ʿĪsā wa Ayyūba wa Yūnusa wa Hārūna wa Sulaymāna wa ātaynā Dāwūda zabūrā* — and We revealed to Abraham, Ishmael, Isaac, Jacob, and the tribes — the *asbāṭ* — descendants — and Jesus, Job, Jonah, Aaron, Solomon; and to David We gave the Psalms — *Zabūr* — was explained as proof of universality. *Ibrāhīma … al-asbāṭ* — Abraham’s line — Ishmael, Isaac, Jacob, tribes — was read as the *millata Ibrāhīma* — Way of Abraham upright (4:125) — made concrete: revelation to each branch, not only to the asking People of the Book who demanded a book from heaven (4:153) — the book has already come to many. *ʿĪsā* — Jesus — was read as the Messiah raised to Him (4:158), son of Mary slandered then son slain falsely — now placed among prophets revealed to, not god. *Ayyūba, Yūnusa* — Job, Jonah — were read as the tested: Job with patience, Jonah with repentance after abandonment — both *“We sent revelation”* to them as to you, showing that revelation includes trials. *Hārūna, Sulaymāna* — Aaron, Solomon — were read as brother-helper and king-prophet; Aaron with Moses where the Mount was raised, Solomon with kingdom where *“give them speck on date stone”* (4:53) — even kingdom with prophethood. *Wa ātaynā Dāwūda zabūrā* — and to David We gave Psalms — was explained as book singled: Psalms as *zabūr* — written, inscribed — among books before (4:136), showing that books before are many, not one book from heaven demanded. Where the sūrah says *“We have sent revelation to you as We sent to Noah”* — *kamā* — likeness is in source — all from Owner of heavens and earth (4:126) — and in purpose — to judge with truth.

**A Verse That Follows Demand for a Book From Heaven With Revelation Lineage**

Placed after the history — demand for a book from heaven, greater demand visible God, thunderbolt, calf yet forgiven, Mount raised, firm covenant, breaking, rejecting, killing, claiming *ghulf* sealed, denying, slander against Mary, boasting killing Messiah not killed but made to appear, raised, belief before death, forbidding *ṭayyibāt* for *ẓulm* and hindering, taking interest though forbidden, consuming unjustly, painful punishment for disbelievers among them, but grounded in knowledge among them believe in what was revealed to you and before, especially establishing prayer and alms, to them great reward (4:153–162), this verse follows demand with lineage: *We have sent revelation to you as We sent to Noah and prophets after him*. Demand for a book from heaven (4:153) is answered with revelation has already been sent to many, including Psalms to David. The sūrah has shown that those who distinguish between God and messengers desire a way between (4:150) — to distinguish is to claim revelation lineage broken; this verse says lineage is one. The sūrah that warns not to go to extremes regarding faith (4:171) — do not say Trinity, Messiah was but messenger, Word through Mary and spirit from Him — here places Jesus among prophets revealed to, not as god, so that extremity is not needed to honour him.

**Revelation to Many Makes Faith Without Distinction Possible**

The phrase *kamā awḥaynā* — as We revealed — was explained to make *“accepting all, rejecting none”* (4:152) possible: because revelation is one source — *to Him belongs whatever is in heavens and earth* (4:126) — to accept all is to accept that Owner’s many givings. Where the hypocrites are *mudhabdhab* between neither (4:143) and desire a way between distinguishing (4:150) — wavering — the lineage from Noah to David to you is the firm path — *sabili al-muʾminīn* (4:115) — not wavering. For the weak this is an opening: where you feared settlement is best (4:128) and settlement involves waiver, revelation lineage shows no waiver of any prophet; faith is not settlement that waives some messengers, but *ḥanīf* submission that accepts all. The next verses will extend that lineage: there are messengers whose stories We told you already and others We did not, and to Moses He spoke directly (4:164).
