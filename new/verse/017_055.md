## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:55

> **Your Lord knows best all those in the heavens and the earth. And We have surely favoured some prophets above others, and to David We gave the Psalms. Other Gods Besides Allah?**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Lord Knows Best All Those In Heavens And Earth**

Wa-rabbuka aʿlamu bi-man fī al-samāwāti wa-al-arḍi — and your Lord knows best who is in heavens and earth. Wa-rabbuka, and your Lord, your Lord, from r-b-b. Aʿlamu, knows best, more knowing, from ʿ-l-m, as previous aʿlamu knows best. Bi-man fī al-samāwāti wa-al-arḍi, with who is in heavens and earth, about those in heavens and earth, all those in heavens and earth, from m-n, who, and s-m-w, heavens, and a-r-ḍ, earth, as seven heavens, earth, all those in them glorify Him, as verse 44 says. Al-Ṭabarī explains that your Lord knows best all those in heavens and earth, who is in them, angels, humans, jinn, etc., knows their states, who deserves mercy, punishment, favouring, etc., as All-Aware All-Seeing, knows best what is within selves, how they listen, you best, those in heavens and earth, all. Al-Qurṭubī says rabbuka aʿlamu bi-man fī al-samāwāti wa-al-arḍi means Lord knows best all those in heavens and earth, knows their inner, outer, who is righteous, who deserves favouring, etc.

**We Favoured Some Prophets Above Others, To David Gave Psalms**

Wa-la-qad faḍḍalnā baʿḍa al-nabiyyīna ʿalā baʿḍin wa-ātaynā Dāwūda zabūran — and We have surely favoured some prophets above others and We gave David Psalms. Wa-la-qad, and surely, indeed, certainly, emphasis. Faḍḍalnā from f-ḍ-l, to favour, prefer, give superiority, as faḍḍalnā some over others in world, and akbaru tafḍīlan greater in favour in Hereafter, and faḍḍalnā some prophets above others, favouring prophets. Baʿḍa al-nabiyyīna, some prophets, some of prophets, from n-b-ʾ, prophets, plural of nabī, prophet, from naba', news. ʿAlā baʿḍin, over others, above others, some above others, in rank, favour, as some prophets favoured above others, as verse 21 some favoured over others in this life but Hereafter far greater in rank and favour, and prophets favoured some above others in rank, favour, as Muhammad favoured, etc. Wa-ātaynā from a-t-y, to give, bring, as ātā give, We gave. Dāwūda, David, prophet David, Dāwūd, from d-w-d, David. Zabūran from z-b-r, Psalms, Scripture, from zabūr, Psalms, Scripture given to David, as Zabūr, Psalms, as Qur'an mentions. Al-Ṭabarī explains that We favoured some prophets above others, some above others in rank, favour, as some prophets given more, and to David We gave Psalms, Zabūr, as example of favouring, as David favoured with Psalms, Scripture, as well as kingdom, etc., as favouring. Al-Qurṭubī says faḍḍalnā baʿḍa al-nabiyyīna ʿalā baʿḍin indicates some prophets favoured above others, as Muhammad favoured above all, etc., and to David gave Psalms as example, as David favoured with Zabūr.

**Why Lord Knows Best Those In Heavens Earth And Favoured Prophets**

Why Lord knows best those in heavens and earth and favoured some prophets above others, to David gave Psalms? Because previous verses said Lord knows you best, may have mercy or punish if wills, not sent you as keeper, and now Lord knows best all those in heavens and earth, favoured some prophets above others, to David gave Psalms, indicating that favouring some over others, including prophets, is by knowledge, will, wisdom, as He knows best those in heavens and earth, who deserves favouring, as He favoured some prophets above others, some over others in world, Hereafter far greater in rank and favour, so favouring prophets is part of favouring some over others, by knowledge, will.

**Connection To See How We Favoured Some Over Others And Hereafter Greater**

Verse 21 said see how We favoured some over others in this life but Hereafter far greater in rank and favour. Verse 55 says Lord knows best those in heavens and earth, We favoured some prophets above others, to David gave Psalms, showing that favouring some over others includes prophets, some prophets favoured above others in rank, favour, as part of favouring, and Hereafter far greater in rank and favour, so prophets favoured in world and Hereafter greater. The connection is favouring theme: some favoured over others in world, prophets favoured, Hereafter greater.

**Link To David Psalms And Wisdom And Other Gods**

To David We gave Psalms, Zabūr, as Scripture, wisdom, as part of wisdom revealed, as verse 39 said this is part of wisdom revealed to you, O Prophet, and David given Psalms as wisdom, Scripture, as example of favouring prophets, and next section Other Gods Besides Allah? indicates invoking those claimed besides Him, they have no power, etc., refuting other gods, as monotheism theme.

**Linguistic Note On A'lamu Bi-man And Faddalna And Nabiyyin And Zaburan**

Rabb from r-b-b, Lord. Aʿlam from ʿ-l-m, knows best. Man fī, who is in, those in. Samāwāt from s-m-w, heavens. Arḍ from a-r-ḍ, earth. Faḍḍalnā from f-ḍ-l, to favour, prefer, from faḍala. Baʿḍ from b-ʿ-ḍ, some. Nabiyyīn from n-b-ʾ, prophets, plural of nabī. Ātaynā from a-t-y, to give. Dāwūd from d-w-d, David. Zabūr from z-b-r, Psalms, Scripture, from zabara, to write. The verse's structure — your Lord knows best those in heavens and earth, and We favoured some prophets above others and gave David Psalms — with knowledge best about those in heavens earth and favouring some prophets above others and giving David Psalms as example, shows that Lord knows best, favours some over others by knowledge, including prophets, as wisdom, favouring.

**How This Verse Teaches Lord Knows Best And Favoured Prophets**

This verse teaches that Lord knows best all those in heavens and earth, knows their states, who deserves mercy, punishment, favouring, etc., All-Aware All-Seeing, and that We favoured some prophets above others, some above others in rank, favour, as part of favouring some over others in world, but Hereafter far greater in rank and favour, and to David We gave Psalms, Zabūr, as example of favouring, Scripture, wisdom. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should know Lord knows best those in heavens and earth, favours some prophets above others by knowledge, wisdom, and to David gave Psalms, as favouring, and should not claim other gods besides Allah, as next section says, as other gods have no power, etc. The practical lesson is to believe Lord knows best, favours some over others, prophets favoured, Hereafter greater, and to follow favoured Prophet Muhammad, given Qur'an, wisdom, most upright, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving.

**A Reflection On Favoured Prophets And Psalms**

Faḍḍalnā baʿḍa al-nabiyyīna ʿalā baʿḍin — We favoured some prophets above others — indicates that prophets not equal in rank, favour, some favoured above others, as Muhammad favoured above all, as Seal, etc., as part of favouring some over others, by knowledge, will, wisdom, as Lord knows best those in heavens and earth, who deserves favouring. Wa-ātaynā Dāwūda zabūran — We gave David Psalms — as example of favouring, as David given Zabūr, Psalms, Scripture, kingdom, wisdom, as favoured, as well as other prophets given Scriptures, as Moses given Scripture guide for Children of Isra'il, etc., all favouring. The believer who reflects will know Lord knows best, favours some prophets above others, to David gave Psalms, and will follow most favoured Prophet, Muhammad, given Qur'an, wisdom, most upright, guides to most upright, explains everything in detail, varies signs so mindful, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour in Hereafter, and will not invoke other gods besides Allah, as they have no power, etc., as next verses say.
