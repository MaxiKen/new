## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:151

> they are indeed the true disbelievers. And We have prepared for the disbelievers a humiliating punishment.

### Expanded Commentary

**The humiliation of selective belief**

The verse calls those who divide the messengers true disbelievers and warns of humiliating punishment. The humiliation answers their attempt to preserve religious prestige while refusing the command that challenges them. Public identity cannot conceal the fact that revelation has been treated as a menu.

The wording should not be used to deny the complex histories of communities or to make ordinary people targets of abuse. It addresses a theological posture of deliberate rejection, and final judgment belongs to Allah. For Muslims, it is a warning not to honour some prophets in speech while dismissing the obligations carried by the final Messenger.

The verse invites intellectual integrity: if a person claims to follow God, he must let God define the boundaries of that claim.

**A warning against religious selectivity**

The humiliating punishment described here answers the desire to look devout without submitting to the whole revealed claim. It should make Muslims cautious about treating scripture as a source of quotations rather than a source of correction. Honour for prophets is shown through obedience to the God who sent them.
