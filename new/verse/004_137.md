## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:137

> Indeed, those who believed then disbelieved, then believed and again disbelieved—˹only˺ increasing in disbelief—Allah will neither forgive them nor guide them to the ˹Right˺ Way.

**Expanded Commentary**

**Those Who Believed Then Disbelieved — Then Increased**

The verse states *inna alladhīna āmanū thumma kafarū thumma āmanū thumma kafarū thumma izdādū kufrā* — indeed those who believed then disbelieved, then believed then disbelieved — then only increasing in disbelief, adding disbelief. *Āmanū thumma kafarū* — believed then disbelieved — repeated twice was explained as oscillation, not mere lapse: belief then denial, then return to belief then again denial, yet not as repentance accepted soon after error (4:17) but as performance, as hypocrisy’s rhythm. *Thumma izdādū kufrā* — then increased, added, in disbelief — was read as that the second disbelief is not return to first level but increase: associating after far straying (4:116), taking Satan as guardian instead of Him (4:119), invoking female gods (4:117) — each step adds. The sūrah has shown that whoever defies the Messenger after guidance is clear and follows other than believers’ path is left to what he chose (4:115) and whoever associates has gone far astray (4:116); to oscillate between belief and denial after clarity is to defy after clarity repeatedly, making guidance not firm. Early reports linked the verse to Jews who believed in Moses then disbelieved in Jesus then believed? The generality was stressed: whoever trades belief for disbelief and returns only to trade again, increasing, is meant — the hypocrite described as *“torn between belief and disbelief, belonging neither to these nor those”* (4:143) and who seek to deceive God while He outwits them (4:142).

**Allah Will Neither Forgive Nor Guide to Right Way**

The seal *lam yakuni Allāhu li yaghfira lahum wa lā li yahdiyuhum sabīlā* — Allah will neither forgive them nor guide them to a way — the Right Way — was explained with two negations: no forgiveness and no guidance. *Li yaghfira lahum* — to forgive them — was read as that *“whoever commits evil then seeks forgiveness finds Him All-Forgiving”* (4:110) has a condition — seeking after evil before death’s saying *now I repent* (4:18); these increase in disbelief instead of seeking, so forgiveness is not. *Wa lā li yahdiyuhum sabīlā* — nor guide them to a way, to path — was read as *sabīla* — way — the same *sabīl* the sūrah wills to make clear as noble ways (4:26), Satan wishes the deviant far path (4:60), believers’ path is not to be left (4:115); those who oscillate and increase have closed the path by repeatedly turning. The phrase *lam yakun li yaghfira* — He will not forgive — was linked to *“He does not forgive associating, forgives what is less”* (4:48, 4:116): increase in disbelief is shown to settle into the unforgiven associate-level where pardon is not, unless the oscillation is broken by firm belief. The sūrah has said *“whoever is left to stray you will never find a way”* (4:88, 4:143) — *lan tajida lahū sabīlā* — here the way not found is forgiveness and guidance, because they left it.

**A Verse That Follows Firm Belief With Description of Unfirm**

Placed after *O believers, have faith in God, Messenger, Book He revealed to His Messenger and Scriptures He revealed before, whoever denies God, angels, Books, messengers, Last Day has gone far astray* (4:136), this verse follows firm belief commanded with description of unfirm belief that oscillates. The sūrah has commanded *qawwāmīn bi al-qisṭ* — ever standing for justice (4:135) — and said both rewards are with Him (4:134) and He can remove humanity and bring others (4:133); oscillation is the opposite of standing: to stand is to remain, to oscillate is to be removed. The sūrah has shown that *“had it not been for grace they would have deceived you yet they deceive only themselves”* (4:113) — oscillation deceives only self, increasing its disbelief, not harming the Prophet. Where the sūrah will give good news of painful punishment to hypocrites who take disbelievers as allies seeking honour (4:138–139) and will declare not to sit with those who deny or ridicule revelations else you are like them (4:140), this verse is the theological root: belief then disbelief then belief then increase is the hypocrite’s creed.

**Increase in Disbelief After Light Was Clear**

The phrase *thumma izdādū kufrā* — then only increasing in disbelief — was explained as that disbelief has degrees, as the sūrah’s *ḍalālan baʿīdan* — far straying — has degrees: to associate is far (4:116), to defy the Messenger after clarity and follow other than believers’ path is left to stray (4:115), to invoke Satan instead of Him is tremendous loss (4:119). Increase was linked to making distinction between God and messengers, believing in some and disbelieving in others desiring compromise (4:150), which is itself increase where the compromise is after guidance clear. The sūrah’s arithmetic of good — multiplied many times over (4:40) — has counterpart in evil’s increase: persistence after oscillation does not stay level but adds, as *šuḥḥ* present with souls (4:128) adds when not overcome by graciousness. The seal *lan yaghfira wa lā yahdiyuhum* was therefore read as consequence of addition: where repentance soon after error is pardon (4:17), increase after oscillation is the opposite direction, so forgiveness and guidance are not.

**A Short Verse That Anchors Hypocrisy Before Its Portrait**

Though only *“those who believed then disbelieved then believed again then increased, He will neither forgive nor guide to Right Way,”* the verse anchors hypocrisy before its portrait. Immediately after: *“give good news of painful punishment to hypocrites who take disbelievers as allies instead of believers, seeking honour”* (4:138–139) — portraits of oscillation made alliance. Immediately after: *“when you hear revelations denied or ridiculed do not sit in that company else you are like them, He will gather hypocrites and disbelievers together in Hell”* (4:140) — company of oscillation. The sūrah that warns not to deviate by following desires (4:27) and not to let desires deviate from justice (4:135) here warns that desire to keep options open — belief then disbelief — ends with neither forgiveness nor way, because the One who is Self-Sufficient (4:131) and whose promise is ever true (4:122) does not guide the one who increased after being shown. For the weak this is clarifying: belief is not a wish by which grace is claimed (4:123) nor a wavering between sides like those who wait to see what happens (4:141), but firm faith that finds Him All-Forgiving when it seeks after evil (4:110).
