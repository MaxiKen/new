## Sūrah al-Maʿārij 70:32

> ˹The faithful are˺ also those who are true to their trusts and covenants;

**Expanded Commentary**

**Sixth Duty: Fidelity**

The sixth duty guards trusts and covenant. Li-amānātihim wa-ʿahdihim rāʿūn: "to their trusts and their covenant, shepherding." Amānāt (plural) covers trusts, deposits — everything entrusted in every form. ʿAhd (singular) names covenant — THE pact, with God, embracing all promises. Rāʿūn means observing, shepherding, tending actively. The twin verse in Sūrah 23 confirms identically (23:8). Such pairing covers fidelity completely: many trusts guarded, one covenant kept. Readers should shepherd both diligently: deposits returned intact, pact honored wholly. What the list places sixth, life should practice sixth. Guard trusts actively; keep covenant absolutely. Fidelity: faith's sixth pillar here.

Amānāt span vertical and horizontal dimensions. Godward: the pretemporal covenant when humanity testified (7:172), plus prayers, fasts, vows owed heaven. Manward: deposits held, secrets kept, offices administered, wages paid. Al-Jalālayn summarizes religion and worldly affairs together — both directions, one duty. Such breadth universalizes fidelity: every responsibility qualifies as trust. Prayer is trust from God; a neighbor's deposit is trust from neighbor; public office is trust from community. Readers should inventory trusts comprehensively: prayers rendered? deposits intact? secrets sealed? posts served? What breadth covers universally, diligence must keep totally. Guard Godward duties; guard manward deposits. Trusts total; keeping must be total. Inventory all.

Rāʿūn — shepherding. The root r-ʿ-y means pasturing, tending, watching protectively. Trusts get pastured: watched over, fed, grown, defended — never merely shelved. Shepherds risk comfort for flocks: vigilance by night, sacrifice by day. Such agency exceeds passive storing: trusts develop under shepherds, stagnate under hoarders. Deposits merely kept gather dust; shepherded responsibilities thrive and multiply. The participle implies profession: shepherds by identity, not occasion. Readers should shepherd actively and proudly: competencies grown, charges developed, flocks multiplied. What shepherding tends with sacrifice, mere keeping holds with indifference. Tend trusts tenderly; never shelve them coldly. Pasture responsibilities daily; harvest fidelity eternally. Shepherds, not shelves.

**The Prophet Warned Betrayers Sternly**

Betrayal defines hypocrisy in prophetic warning. Abū Hurayrah reports the Prophet ﷺ said: the signs of the hypocrite are three — when he speaks he lies, when he promises he breaks it, when he is entrusted he betrays (Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 33; Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 59). All three signs orbit fidelity: truthful speech, kept promises, honored trusts — verse 32's very territory. Such Hadith elevates trust-keeping from manners to faith's criterion: betrayal evidences hypocrisy. The warning's severity matches trust's sanctity. Readers should tremble productively: each broken promise flirts with hypocrisy's signs. What the Prophet warned, the wise heed. Speak truthfully, promise carefully, guard deposits jealously. Signs avoided, faith secured. Checked against sunnah.com; wording and numbers verified.

ʿAhd in the singular crowns the verse: THE covenant — with God, embracing and authorizing all human promises. Covenant-keeping crowns character: promise sacred, oath binding, word as bond. Fulfilling vows proves faith's spine and integrity's core. Such crowning centralizes fidelity: one supreme Covenant ordering many lesser ones. Human promises echo the divine: kept words mirror kept faith. The singular also recalls the primordial testimony — humanity's "yes" to Lordship. Readers should vow carefully and keep absolutely: promise rarely, perform always. What covenant crowns with honor, breach dethrones with disgrace. Crown kept through fidelity; character crowned through constancy. Promise little; perform completely. Covenants: character's crown.

**Fidelity Proves Faith Publicly**

Amīn — the trustworthy — titled prophets before proof: Muḥammad was al-Amīn, the Trusted One, among Meccans who deposited goods with him years before revelation. Trustworthiness witnessed before words preached: character convinced where claims merely asserted. Such titling proves fidelity evangelizes: the trusted get heard. The verse's bracketed subject — "the faithful" — merges with the trustworthy: belief believed because behavior believed. Faith without fidelity persuades none; fidelity without proclamation still preaches. Readers should earn trusted reputations deliberately: deposits safe with you, secrets sealed by you, promises performed through you. What fidelity proves publicly, words cannot prove privately. Be trusted consistently; belief follows credibly. Trustworthiness testifies when tongues tire.

**Small Trusts Train for Great**

Punctuality, secrets, borrowed items — small trusts school great fidelity. Faithfulness in little predicts faithfulness in much; minutes kept precede millions guarded. Training ascends gradually: appointments honored, books returned, confidences sealed — amānah's elementary curriculum. Such pedagogy practicalizes lofty duty: start now, start small. Borrowed pens returned, whispered secrets kept, promised calls made — drills of dependability. Readers should drill daily and flawlessly: small trusts kept perfectly. What littles train thoroughly, larges inherit naturally. Master minutes and books; inherit treasuries and offices. Small kept faithfully; great entrusted confidently. Training: small, daily, perfect.

The primordial pact frames every promise: humanity testified to Lordship before time (paraphrased from 7:172). Every oath echoes that Yes; every vow renews it. Covenant-keeping honors origins: promise-keepers reenact testimony. Such framing dignifies humble pledges: appointments kept mirror cosmic vow. Breach, conversely, reenacts denial. Readers should promise covenant-consciously: each word a miniature mīthāq. What frames all demands all. Keep words; honor Origins. Covenant remembered, promises kept.

**Deposits Test Daily**

Workplaces, secrets, entrusted goods — daily amānah audits. Employers' time: spent or stolen? Confided secrets: sealed or spilled? Borrowed items: returned or retained? Small tests, constant. Such daily trials train: fidelity proved in trifles graduates to treasuries. Most betrayals begin petty — minutes padded, supplies pocketed, gossip traded. Readers should audit evenings: today's trusts — kept all? What days test, character answers. Pass dailies; inherit larges. Fidelity's gym opens every morning. Work out; grow trusted.

**Testimony Follows Trust Next**

Trusts guarded; testimony follows. Verse 33 will stand witnesses upright — truth testified firmly, even against kin. Fidelity extends from deeds to words: kept trusts, then spoken truth. The list progresses outward: hands faithful, then tongues truthful. Readers should carry keeping toward testifying: guarded deposits, honest testimony. What follows uprights witness. The next verse commands standing firm in testimony, truth voicing fidelity.
