## Sūrah al-Naḥl 16:45

> **Do those who devise evil plots feel secure that Allah will not cause the earth to swallow them? Or that the torment will not come upon them in ways they cannot comprehend?**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Feeling Secure While Plotting Evil**

A-fa-amina al-ladhīna makarū al-sayyi'āti an yakhsifa Allāhu bihim al-arḍa — do those who plot evil feel secure that Allah will not cause earth to swallow them? A-fa-amina, do they feel secure, safe, from a-m-n, security, safety. Al-ladhīna makarū al-sayyi'āt, those who plot evil, devise bad plots, from m-k-r, plot, and s-y-ʾ, evil. An yakhsifa Allāhu bihim al-arḍa, that Allah will cause earth to swallow them, from kh-s-f, to swallow, sink, eclipse. Al-Ṭabarī explains that this refers to those who plot against messengers and believers, like Makkans who plotted against Prophet ﷺ in Dār al-Nadwah, feeling secure that no punishment will come. The question a-fa-amina is rhetorical, expecting no, they should not feel secure. The punishment of earth swallowing is mentioned as example of worldly punishment that came to previous nations, like Qārūn who was swallowed, as 28:81 says *So We caused earth to swallow him and his house* (28:81). The verse warns that feeling secure while plotting evil is delusion.

**Torment From Ways They Cannot Comprehend**

Aw ya'tiyahum al-ʿadhābu min ḥaythu lā yashʿurūn — or that torment will not come to them from where they do not perceive. This repeats phrase from verse 26: torment came upon them from where they did not expect. Al-Ṭabarī says torment can come from unexpected direction, manner, time, as building collapse from foundations and roof from above in verse 26. The phrase min ḥaythu lā yashʿurūn, from where they do not perceive, emphasizes that plotters guard some directions but cannot guard all. Their plotting is based on perceived security, but security is only with Allah. The verse lists two possibilities: earth swallowing from below, and torment from unexpected direction, covering below and surrounding.

**Why Earth Swallowing Is Mentioned**

Earth swallowing is powerful image because earth is seen as stable, firm, thanks to mountains that prevent shaking, as verse 15 said. If earth that is stable can be made to swallow, then no stability is independent of Allah. Al-Rāzī notes that earth swallowing is fitting punishment for those who plot evil because they plot on earth, using earth as base for building and plotting, so earth itself becomes instrument of punishment. Qārūn was swallowed because he was proud of earthly treasures. The Makkans who felt secure in Makkah, with its firm mountains and sanctuary, are warned that earth beneath them can swallow if Allah wills. The mention of earth swallowing also connects to mountains as stabilizers: mountains prevent earth from shaking with you, but if Allah wills, earth can still swallow despite mountains, showing that ultimate stability is by Allah's will, not by mountains alone.

**Feeling Secure As Disease**

Amina, to feel secure, safe, is praised when it is security in Allah, but condemned when it is security from Allah's punishment while persisting in evil. The Qur'an says *Do they feel secure from Allah's plan? None feels secure from Allah's plan except losing people* (7:99). Feeling secure while plotting evil is sign of heedlessness and arrogance. It indicates that person thinks his plotting is clever enough to avoid consequence, or that Allah does not see, or that punishment is distant. The verse asks rhetorical question to break that false security: do you feel secure that earth will not swallow you? The expected answer is no, you should not feel secure. Al-Qurṭubī says feeling secure from Allah's punishment while persisting in sin is major sin, because it shows lack of fear of Allah.

**Connection To Previous Plotting Verses**

Verses 26-27 described those before plotted and building collapsed and they were humiliated. Verses 45-47 continue theme of plotters feeling secure, asking if they feel secure from earth swallowing, from torment from unexpected, from being seized while going about, from gradual destruction. The four possibilities cover different modes of punishment: sudden from below, sudden from unexpected, sudden while active, gradual. The list shows that punishment can come in many ways, so feeling secure from one mode does not mean secure from others. The plotters who guard against one may be caught by another. The sūrah thus systematically dismantles false security.

**Linguistic Note On Makara And Khasafa**

Makarū from m-k-r means to plot, scheme with subtlety. Al-sayyi'āt, evil, from s-w-ʾ, evil, here meaning evil plots. Amina from a-m-n means to feel secure, safe, from same root as īmān, faith, which is security. An yakhsifa, that He causes to swallow, from kh-s-f, to sink, swallow, eclipse, used for earth swallowing and for eclipse. Bihim al-arḍa, with them the earth, meaning earth swallows them. Aw ya'tiyahum al-ʿadhābu, or torment comes to them, from a-t-y, to come. Min ḥaythu lā yashʿurūn, from where they do not perceive, from sh-ʿ-r, to perceive. The verse's structure — question, do they feel secure that earth will not swallow them or torment will not come from where they do not perceive — with two possibilities, emphasizes that security is false and punishment can come from below or from unexpected, leaving no direction safe for plotter except repentance.


**A Lesson That Connects To Gratitude And Reflection**

This verse, like all verses in Sūrat al-Niʿam, ultimately calls to gratitude and reflection. Whether it describes emigration, patience, trust, human messengers, clear proofs, earth swallowing, or angels fearing, the purpose is to make reader recognize blessings and respond with thanks and reflection. Al-Qurṭubī notes that every warning in this sūrah is also a blessing because it warns before punishment, giving chance to change. Every promise is also a blessing because it encourages good. The sūrah weaves warning and promise, blessing and accountability, to cultivate heart that is mindful, humble, grateful. The reader who reflects on this verse with that mindset will find both knowledge and motivation to be among those who say goodness when asked what Lord revealed, and who are greeted with peace at death and enter Gardens.

**How This Verse Fits The Sūrah's Argument**

The sūrah moves from blessings to argument against shirk to warnings to examples to guidance. This verse fits in that flow by showing either reward for patience and trust, or principle of human messengers, or modes of punishment, or universal submission. Each verse adds layer to argument that Allah alone creates, sustains, knows, and deserves worship. The verse's placement after previous warnings and before next commands is deliberate: it shows consequence of previous attitude and prepares for next command. The reader is invited to see connection and to respond with gratitude, patience, trust, and asking knowledgeable, rather than with denial, arrogance, and plotting.
