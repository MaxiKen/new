## Sūrah al-Maʿārij 70:27

> and those who fear the punishment of their Lord—

**Expanded Commentary**

**Wariness as Worship**

Mushfiqūn — "wary, anxiously fearful." Ishfāq is tender dread — fear mixed with love, caution with care. Such fear worships: God dreaded rightly, punishment heeded duly. The Book crowns fearers — forgiveness and great reward for those fearing their Lord unseen (paraphrased from 67:12); a cluster commands dread (13:21; 21:49; 39:23; 98:8). Fear is thus duty, not mood — commanded caution. Readers should cultivate ishfāq deliberately: meditate audit, tremble duly. What wariness worships, heedlessness forfeits. Fear commanded is fear sanctified. Be wary; be welcomed.

Min ʿadhābi rabbihim — "of their Lord's punishment." Object specified: punishment, not Lord-as-enemy. Believers fear consequence while loving Commander — dread and devotion wed. Such precision prevents twin errors: fearing God as tyrant (despair) or loving Him as indulgent (presumption). Punishment feared rightly motivates; Lord loved rightly attracts. Readers should aim fear accurately: dread the blaze, adore the Merciful. What object focused, worship balanced. Fear punishment; love Lord. Both, always.

Fear scales with knowing. The learned dread most — only the knowledgeable truly fear (paraphrased from 35:28). Knowledge reveals majesty and audit; revelation understood trembles. Such proportion dignifies study: learning increases ishfāq rightly. Ignorance fears phantoms; knowledge fears facts. Readers should grow fear by growing knowledge: study audit-verses, dread deepens duly. What knowing reveals, wariness respects. Learn more; tremble better. Knowledge-fueled fear never misfires.

**Fourth Duty or Third's Shadow**

Counting questions: fourth duty, or belief's extension? Belief (verse 26) affirmed the Day; fear (verse 27) dreads it — credally close. Some number separately (fourth responsibility); others merge (faith's fruit). Grounds for separate: distinct verb (mushfiqūn), distinct verse. Grounds for merged: fear flows from Day-believed. Strong view: numbered yet rooted — duty listed, belief grounded. Both edify: fear commanded (list it), faith sourced (root it). Readers should practice both countings: fear as duty-done, faith as fear-fed. What belief affirms, wariness enacts. Affirm; dread; do.

Mushfiq treads carefully. Wariness walks — scrupulous steps, weighed words, audited hours. Waraʿ (scrupulousness) is ishfāq's gait: doubtful avoided, duties hastened. Such walking witnesses: the wary glow cautiously. Conduct altered: tongues bridled (audit feared), hands opened (scales hoped), nights prayed (mercy sought). Readers should walk warily: audit-aware always. What wariness walks, heedlessness stumbles. Tread audited-paths; arrive approved. Walk wary; end welcomed.

One fear displaces many. God-feared, all else shrinks — poverty, princes, futures, phantoms. Such economy liberates: single dread dissolving scattered dreads. The fearless-toward-God fear everything; the fearful-toward-Him fear nothing else. Readers should consolidate anxieties: pour all frets into ishfāq. What one fear holds, hundred fears vacate. Halūʿ's panic (verses 19-21) meets its master — mushfiq's dread. Fear God; fear nothing. Dread centralized liberates peripheries.

Rajāʾ (hope) wings with khawf (fear): dread flies mercyward. Hope's role — preventing despair, fueling return. The wary hope pardon while dreading punishment: punishment possible, pardon promised-to-penitents. Such balance sustains striving: fear warns, hope beckons. Cancel either, flight fails — fear-only grounds, hope-only drifts. Readers should pair deliberately: dread sins, trust mercy. What hope holds (pardon), fear guards (from presuming). Fly double-winged. Hope mercy; dread wrath. Balanced birds reach gardens.

**Fear Guards the Tongue First**

Speech faces audit first: words counted, backbiting weighed, lies exposed. The wary weigh pre-speech — "will this pass scales?" Tongues bridled: gossip choked, slander starved, truth told. Such guarding prioritizes rightly: speech sins multiply fastest. One tongue wounds multitudes; one silence saves. Readers should muzzle mornings: resolve weighed-words daily. What tongues utter, books record. Guard speech; halve sins. Fear's first fruit is quiet. Speak weighed; stay wary.

Ishfāq weeps. The wary cry — night vigils wet, audit-verses moistening eyes. Tears of dread worship: grief flowing Godward, fear liquefied. Such weeping differs from despair's — hope floats these tears. The wary weep and continue: tears shed, duties done, dawn faced. Weeping softens what warnings strike — hearts irrigated absorb. Dry-eyed dread hardens; tearful dread heals. Readers should welcome holy tears: weep at audit-thought, then work. What eyes release, hearts resolve. Tears unwept accumulate as hardness; tears wept become light. Cry wary tears. The Day dries all righteous weeping — then, laughter.

Feeling no fear signals danger. Hardened hearts fear nothing — calamity's prelude. Complacency congratulates: "safe, secure, sound." Such self-assurance precedes falls invariably. The fearless should fear their fearlessness: numbness to audit is audit's symptom. Wake-up calls sound — verses like this one. Readers unfrightened should frighten themselves deliberately: picture scales, imagine exposure. What numbness hides, meditation uncovers. Fear your calm; disturb your peace. The trembling are safer than the tranquil-in-sin. Feel fear; find safety.

**Dread Deepens Devotion**

Fear sweetens worship. Prayers prayed trembling taste majesty; recitation voiced awed hears depths. Awe concentrates: wandering minds anchor at dread. Such deepening transforms routine — motions hallowed, syllables weighted. The fearless pray thinly; the fearful, thickly. Majesty tasted in worship then flavors conduct — awe-soaked souls sin reluctantly. Readers should invite dread pre-prayer: ponder standing (ʿarḍ), then stand. What awe begins, love completes — dread's doorway opens to delight's chamber. Worship warned, worship warmed. Tremble entering; glow exiting. Dread is devotion's depth-charge.

**Watchfulness Wears Down Worry**

Vigilance calms what it watches. Shepherds watch flocks peacefully — attention without panic. The wary watch souls similarly: noticing slips, correcting courses, resting between. Such watching differs from worrying — observation versus agitation. Worry churns helplessly; watchfulness acts promptly. The mushfiq observes triggers, avoids occasions, repairs lapses — worry's energy converted to vigilance's work. Readers should trade worry for watch: list triggers, plan avoidances, schedule reviews. What watching manages, worrying multiplies. Shepherd your soul; sleep soundly. Vigilant days make restful nights. Watch well; worry less.

**Security Denied Next**

Wariness stated; insecurity sealed. Verse 28 will void all safety — none secure from punishment. Fear commanded, then complacency killed. Readers should carry dread toward its guard: wary, never safe-feeling. What follows will bar false peace. The next verse denies all security from the Lord's punishment, sealing wariness with warning.
