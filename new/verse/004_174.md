## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:174

> O humanity! There has come to you conclusive evidence from your Lord. And We have sent down to you a brilliant light.

### Expanded Commentary

**Evidence and light**

A conclusive proof has come from the Lord, along with a brilliant light. The proof is the Messenger and the revealed evidence he brings; the light is commonly understood as the Qur’an, which illuminates belief, law, and the hidden causes of human corruption.

Light does not remove the need to walk. A person can possess a lamp and refuse to follow its direction. The verse therefore invites engagement: read, understand, and let the revelation expose both public injustice and private self-deception.

The address to humanity keeps the invitation universal. The light is not the property of a tribe or clerical class. Its benefits are shared by whoever believes and follows it.

**Light must be followed**

A brilliant light is useful only when a person allows it to expose the path ahead. Read the Qur’an not simply to collect quotations but to identify the next duty, repair, or belief it illuminates. The evidence is offered to humanity, so no class may monopolize access to its guidance.
