## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:174

> O humanity! There has come to you conclusive evidence from your Lord. And We have sent down to you a brilliant light.

**Expanded Commentary**

**O Humanity — Conclusive Evidence Has Come From Your Lord**

The verse states *yā ayyuhā al-nāsu qad jāʾakum burhānun min rabbikum* — O humanity, there has certainly come to you a proof, evidence, demonstration, from your Lord. *Yā ayyuhā al-nāsu* — O humanity — was explained as third great humanity address after *“be mindful of your Lord who created you from single soul”* (4:1) and *“if He wills He can remove you and bring others”* (4:133) and *“the Messenger has come with truth from your Lord, so believe for your own good”* (4:170) — now *“conclusive evidence has come.”* *Qad jāʾakum* — has certainly come to you — was read with emphasis: the evidence has arrived, not pending, as lineage from Noah through Jesus to you (4:163) was already sent as *mubashshirīn wa mundhirīn* so no excuse remains (4:165) — now evidence. *Burhānun min rabbikum* — proof from your Lord — was explained with *burhān* — conclusive proof, demonstration — the same root *brh* as clarity where *He wills to make clear* (4:26) and *taught you what you did not know* (4:113) — *burhān* is the Prophet ﷺ himself with miracles and the Qur’an as proof. Early reports linked *burhān* to the Prophet, while *nūr* — light — after is Qur’an, showing two proofs: messenger and book. The sūrah has shown that *“to Him belongs whatever is in heavens and earth, He is Fully Aware”* (4:126) — proof from your Lord who owns and encompasses is the most near. The phrase was linked to *“Gardens for those who believe and do good”* (4:122) — proof’s purpose is Gardens, as *“O humanity, the Messenger has come with truth, so believe for your own good”* (4:170) — better for you.

**And We Have Sent Down to You a Brilliant Light**

The second clause *wa anzalnā ilaykum nūran mubīnā* — and We have sent down to you a clear, brilliant, manifest light — was explained with *nūr* — light — *mubīn* — clear, manifest — the same *nūr* and *mubīn* as *sulṭān mubīn* — compelling proof — Moses was given (4:153) and as clear light where association is not forgiven (4:116) — light shows far straying. *Anzalnā ilaykum* — We sent down to you — was read as the Qur’an sent with truth — *bi al-ḥaqq* — to judge between people by what He shows (4:105) — light shows judgment, as the Mount raised (4:154) was physical light of proof. *Nūr mubīn* was explained as Qur’an that makes clear, as *“He wills to lighten burdens”* (4:28) — light lightens; the sūrah that taught prayer at fixed time (4:103) as *kitāban mawqūtā* — light times. Early reports added that the *burhān* and *nūr* are the two witnesses: God bears witness with His knowledge and angels bear witness (4:166) — His knowledge is light, angels’ witness is proof. Where the sūrah says *“do not go to extremes regarding faith, say nothing but truth”* (4:171) — do not say Trinity — truth is the light that makes extremes visible as extremes, as Satan’s promises only delusion (4:120) are darkness.

**A Verse That Follows Humanity’s Call With Complement**

Placed after *O humanity, the Messenger has come with truth, so believe for your own good* (4:170), *O People of the Book, do not go to extremes, Messiah was but messenger, Word through Mary and spirit from Him, do not say Trinity* (4:171), *Messiah would never be too proud to be servant, nor angels nearest, those too proud will be gathered then repaid in full and increased or punished painful without protector* (4:172–173), this verse follows humanity’s call and messiah’s correction with its complement: conclusive evidence has come from your Lord — messenger — and brilliant light sent down — Qur’an. The sūrah has shown that those who disbelieve and hinder far astray (4:167), disbelieve and wrong not forgiven nor guided except Hell’s path forever easy (4:168–169) — light is the opposite hindering: to send light is to unhinder. The sūrah that warns *most secret talks have no good except charity, kindness, reconciliation* (4:114) — light is the *najwā* made good: the Book’s light as charity. For the weak this is an opening: where you feared rising or turning away and settlement is best (4:128), light shows settlement; where helpless hoped to be pardoned (4:99), light shows pardon hoped is light; where emigrant finds many havens (4:100), light shows havens.

**The Brilliant Light That Makes Disproof Visible**

The phrase *nūran mubīnā* — brilliant, clear light — was explained to include future guidance: light manifest makes Trinity’s *thalāthah* — three — visible as excess, makes *buhtān ʿaẓīm* against Mary visible as slander, makes boasting killing Messiah not killed visible as assumption, makes *ribā* though forbidden visible as unjust consumption. The sūrah has shown that *“why should He punish if you are grateful and faithful? He is ever Appreciative”* (4:147) — light is gratitude’s tool: to be grateful you see what He gave, light shows. The verse generalises the sūrah’s *“He wills to make clear”* (4:26) — *burhān* and *nūr* are that making clear. The next verse will describe believers who hold fast being admitted into mercy and guided (4:175).

**A Short Verse That Anchors Evidence Before Guidance**

Though only *“O humanity, conclusive evidence has come from your Lord, We have sent down to you a brilliant light,”* the verse anchors evidence before guidance to be given. The chapter that began *“be mindful of your Lord who created you from single soul”* (4:1), showed ownership to Him of heavens and earth, sufficiency as Trustee, capability to replace, both rewards with Him Hearing and Seeing, justice though against yourselves, comprehensive faith, oscillation without forgiveness, hypocrite portrait, lowest depth without helper except those who repent, discipline of speech, distinction between God and messengers, demand for a book from heaven as litany, forbidding *ṭayyibāt* for *ẓulm* and hindering, well-grounded to great reward, lineage, messengers told and not, direct speech, good news and warnings, sufficient witness, far straying, Hell’s path easy, humanity’s call, correction of People of the Book, servitude of Messiah and angels, repaying, here says evidence and light have come — so that excuse is cut where light is. The next verses will address the final asking: they ask you for a ruling regarding those who die without children or parents — God gives ruling regarding inheritance of siblings (4:176).
