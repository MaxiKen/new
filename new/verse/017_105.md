**Linguistic Note On Ḥaqq And Anzalnāhu And Nazala And Arsalnāka And Mubashshir And Nadhīr**

Ḥaqq from ḥ-q-q, truth, right, from ḥaqqa. Anzalnā from n-z-l, to send down, reveal, from anzala. Nazala from n-z-l, to come down, descend, from nazala. Arsalnā from r-s-l, to send, dispatch, from arsala. Mubashshir from b-sh-r, bearer of good news, deliverer of good news, from bashshara. Nadhīr from n-dh-r, warner, from nadhara.
