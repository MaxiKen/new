## Sūrah an-Nūr 24:40

> Or ˹their deeds are˺ like the darkness in a deep sea, covered by waves upon waves, topped by ˹dark˺ clouds. Darkness upon darkness! If one stretches out their hand, they can hardly see it. And whoever Allah does not bless with light will have no light!

**Expanded Commentary**

**The Abyssal Sea: Stratification of Profound Darkness**

The revelation presents an alternative, even more harrowing cosmic allegory: *aw ka-ẓulumātin fī baḥrin lujjiyyin yaghshāhu mawjun min fawqihi mawjun min fawqihi saḥāb*. While verse 39 depicted the active, deceptive works of the disbeliever who mistakenly anticipates reward (the desert mirage), verse 40 portrays the inner epistemic state of the disbeliever who is completely submerged in existential despair, skepticism, and nihilism.

The text plunges the reader into a *baḥr lujjiyy*—a fathomless, abyssal ocean depths where sunlight never penetrates. Modern oceanography confirms that solar light diminishes rapidly with depth; below two hundred meters, the ocean enters twilight, and below one thousand meters, it descends into absolute pitch darkness. The Qur'an uses this terrifying oceanic abyss to represent the soul that has severed its connection to divine revelation, trapped in a cold, suffocating abyss of existential alienation and profound spiritual blindness that extinguishes hope.

**Atmospheric and Oceanic Turbulence: mawjun min fawqihi mawj**

The darkness of this oceanic trench is compounded by multiple physical and meteorological barriers: *yaghshāhu mawjun min fawqihi mawjun min fawqihi saḥāb*, covered by waves upon waves, topped by dark clouds. This astonishing imagery describes an intricate three-tiered architecture of occlusion.

First, deep within the sea are internal waves that undulate along boundaries of differing water densities; second, upon the surface crash towering ocean waves driven by gales; third, above the surface hang thick, light-blocking storm clouds (*saḥāb*). Each layer progressively absorbs, scatters, and extinguishes the remaining photons of celestial light. Metaphorically, these tiers represent the multiple covers suffocating the disbelieving soul: the internal darkness of corrupted character, the turbulent emotional waves of uncontrolled worldly desires, and the overarching cloud of cultural pride and philosophical arrogance that completely blocks the light of divine truth, leaving the inner eye starved of vision.

**Compounded Blindness: ẓulumātun baʿḍuhā fawqa baʿḍ**

The culmination of these obscuring layers is captured in an emphatic exclamation: *ẓulumātun baʿḍuhā fawqa baʿḍ*, darknesses, one above the other! This phrase stands in direct, breathtaking antithesis to the proclamation in verse 35: *nūrun ʿalā nūr* (Light upon light!).

Just as divine illumination compounds upon itself through pure human intuition, sacred scripture, and righteous deeds, spiritual blindness compounds upon itself through doubt, transgression, and willful obstinacy. The classical companion Ubayy ibn Kaʿb commented that the disbeliever is trapped in five darknesses: his speech is dark, his actions are dark, his entrance into the grave is dark, his resurrection is dark, and his ultimate destiny in the Fire is dark. It is a total, suffocating occlusion of spiritual perception that leaves no room for truth to enter, transforming the soul into a frozen landscape of negation.

**The Vanishing Extremity: idhā akhraja yadahu lam yakad yarāhā**

The psychological horror of this absolute darkness is made visceral through human physical action: *idhā akhraja yadahu lam yakad yarāhā*, when one puts out his hand, he can hardly see it. A person's hand is the closest, most familiar part of their own physical anatomy; if a person cannot even perceive their own hand held directly before their eyes, all navigational orientation has ceased to exist.

This image symbolizes the total loss of moral and existential orientation. The disbeliever who rejects divine revelation cannot even understand his own human nature, his purpose, or his ultimate destination. He stumbles through life in complete moral disorientation, unable to perceive the consequences of his actions or recognize the evil taking shape in his own hands. His self-proclaimed philosophical sophistication is exposed as pathetic, helpless blindness that cannot guide a single footstep across the dark terrain of mortality.

**The Anatomy of Compound Ignorance: al-jahl al-murakkab**

Classical Islamic theologians and epistemologists analyzed this verse as the definitive Qur'anic diagnosis of compound ignorance (*al-jahl al-murakkab*). Simple ignorance (*al-jahl al-basīṭ*) occurs when an individual does not know a truth but is consciously aware of their lack of knowledge, leaving them receptive to instruction.

In contrast, compound ignorance is a catastrophic spiritual disease: the person does not know the truth, does not know that they do not know, and fervently believes that they possess superior wisdom! Like the submerged swimmer beneath layers of storm clouds and oceanic waves who imagines their darkness is the entire universe, the arrogant skeptic ridicules faith while residing in profound intellectual decay. They are blind to their own blindness, trapped in a self-enclosed prison of philosophical confusion from which no human logic alone can liberate them, mistaking shadows for enlightenment.

**The Sovereign Dispenser of Spiritual Vision: man lam yajʿali Allāhu lahū nūrā**

The verse culminates in an absolute, unyielding metaphysical verdict: *wa-man lam yajʿali Allāhu lahū nūran fa-mā lahū min nūr*, and whoever Allah does not bless with light will have no light! This divine sentence settles the ultimate source of truth, insight, and salvation.

Light is not an autonomous human achievement that can be manufactured through secular philosophy, intellectual brilliance, or technological mastery. God is the sole Creator and Sovereign Bestower of light (*al-Muʿṭī lil-nūr*). If a soul rejects God's guidance, cuts itself off from His messengers, and chooses the dark depths of ego and skepticism, no power in the cosmos can provide it with illumination. It is doomed to stumble through the darkness of mortality into the eternal shadows of the afterlife. True vision is a divine grace, granted to the humble heart that turns toward the Light of the heavens and the earth, finding eternal guidance, serenity, and deliverance in sacred revelation across all realms.
