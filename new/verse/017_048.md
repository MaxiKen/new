## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:48

> **See how they call you names ˹O Prophet˺! So they have gone so ˹far˺ astray that they cannot find the ˹Right˺ Way.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**See How They Call You Names O Prophet**

Unẓur kayfa ḍarabū laka al-amthāla — see how they coined, struck for you examples, parables, names. Unẓur from n-ẓ-r, to see, look, consider, as verse 21 unẓur kayfa faḍḍalnā, see how We favoured. Kayfa, how. Ḍarabū from ḍ-r-b, to coin, strike, give, set forth, from ḍaraba, to strike, coin, give example, as ḍaraba mathal, to coin example, parable. Laka, for you, to you, O Prophet. Al-amthāla from m-th-l, examples, parables, likenesses, names, from mathal, example, parable, likeness. Al-Ṭabarī explains that see how they call you names, O Prophet, how they coined for you examples, parables, names, like bewitched man, poet, magician, madman, etc., as they say you follow only bewitched man, as previous verse, and other names. Al-Qurṭubī says ḍarabū laka al-amthāla means they coined for you examples, parables, names, calling you bewitched, poet, magician, etc., to insult, to dissuade, as wrongdoers do.

**So Far Astray Cannot Find Right Way**

Fa-ḍallū fa-lā yastaṭīʿūna sabīlan — so they went astray, so they cannot find way, cannot find Right Way. Fa-ḍallū from ḍ-l-l, to go astray, stray, be misguided, from ḍalla, to stray, as ḍalla astray. Fa-lā yastaṭīʿūna from ṭ-w-ʿ, to be able, can, from istaṭāʿa, to be able, can, as lā yastaṭīʿūna cannot. Sabīlan from s-b-l, way, path, Right Way, way to guidance, as sabīl way, as ibn al-sabīl son of way. Al-Ṭabarī says so they have gone so far astray, strayed, misguided, that they cannot find Right Way, way to guidance, truth, because they coined examples, names, like bewitched man, poet, etc., which are false, astray, so they cannot find Right Way, cannot find guidance, as they have veils over hearts, deafness in ears, hidden barrier, aversion, denying hearts, arrogant, barrier, so astray cannot find way.

**Why Calling Names Indicates Going Far Astray**

Why calling names indicates going far astray? Because calling Prophet bewitched, poet, magician, etc., shows they have no sure knowledge, follow without knowledge, have veils, deafness, barrier, aversion, denying hearts, arrogant, so they coin false examples, names, to insult, dissuade, which are astray, not Right Way, so they cannot find Right Way, cannot find guidance, as they are far astray. The phrase fa-ḍallū fa-lā yastaṭīʿūna sabīlan indicates consequence: because they coined false examples, names, they went astray, so cannot find way, cannot find Right Way, guidance, truth.

**Connection To Bewitched Man And Private Counsel And Veils**

Verse 47 said We know best how they listen and what they say privately, when wrongdoers say you follow only bewitched man. Verse 48 says see how they call you names, O Prophet, so far astray cannot find Right Way. The connection is that saying bewitched man is example of calling names, coining examples, parables, names, like bewitched, poet, magician, etc., which shows going far astray, cannot find Right Way, as they have veils, deafness, barrier, aversion, denying hearts, arrogant, no sure knowledge.

**Link To Varying Signs And Aversion And Hidden Barrier**

Verse 41 varied signs so they may be mindful but drives farther away, nufūran, aversion. Verses 45-46 hidden barrier, veils, deafness, turning backs in aversion when Lord alone mentioned. Verse 47-48 listening with mockery, saying bewitched man, calling names, so far astray cannot find Right Way. The link is that varying signs should make mindful, but disbelievers with barrier, veils, deafness, aversion, listening with mockery, saying bewitched man, calling names, go far astray, cannot find Right Way, increase in aversion, driving farther away.

**Linguistic Note On Darabu And Amthal And Dallu And Sabil**

Unẓur from n-ẓ-r, to see, look, consider. Kayfa from k-y-f, how. Ḍarabū from ḍ-r-b, to coin, strike, give example, from ḍaraba. Amthāl from m-th-l, examples, parables, likenesses, names, plural of mathal. Ḍallū from ḍ-l-l, to go astray, from ḍalla. Yastaṭīʿūna from ṭ-w-ʿ, to be able, can, from istaṭāʿa. Sabīl from s-b-l, way, path, Right Way. The verse's structure — see how they coined for you examples, so they went astray so they cannot find way — with command to see how they coined examples and consequence they went astray cannot find way, shows that coining false examples, names, like bewitched man, leads to going far astray, cannot find Right Way, guidance.

**How This Verse Teaches Consequence Of Calling Names**

This verse teaches consequence of calling Prophet names, coining false examples, parables: see how they call you names, O Prophet, how they coined for you examples, like bewitched man, poet, magician, etc., so they have gone so far astray that they cannot find Right Way, cannot find guidance, truth, way to truth, as they have veils, deafness, barrier, aversion, denying hearts, arrogant, no sure knowledge, follow without knowledge. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should not call Prophet names, not coin false examples, should see how disbelievers call names and go far astray cannot find Right Way, and should avoid, believe, follow most upright guidance, wisdom, that Qur'an guides to. The practical lesson is to not coin false examples, names, for Prophet, not say bewitched, poet, etc., but believe he is Prophet, revelation, wisdom, most upright, and to follow Right Way, not go astray, cannot find way.

**A Reflection On Far Astray Cannot Find Way**

Fa-ḍallū fa-lā yastaṭīʿūna sabīlan — so they went astray so they cannot find way — indicates that calling names, coining false examples, leads to going far astray, misguided, so far that they cannot find Right Way, cannot find guidance, cannot find path to truth, as they have barrier, veils, deafness, aversion, denying hearts, arrogant. The believer who believes in Hereafter, has no barrier, no veils, no deafness, no aversion, mentions Lord alone, loves monotheism, listens to Qur'an with mindfulness, not mockery, does not say bewitched man, does not call names, does not go far astray, can find Right Way, follows most upright guidance, wisdom, that Qur'an guides to, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, while disbelievers far astray cannot find Right Way, have painful punishment prepared.
