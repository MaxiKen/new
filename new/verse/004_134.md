## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:134

> Whoever desires the reward of this world, then ˹let them know that˺ with Allah are the rewards of this world and the Hereafter. And Allah is All-Hearing, All-Seeing.

**Expanded Commentary**

**Whoever Desires the Reward of This World — With Him Are Both**

The verse states *man kāna yurīdu thawāba al-dunyā fa ʿinda Allāhi thawābu al-dunyā wa al-ākhirati* — whoever desires, wants, seeks the reward, wage, of this nearer world, then let them know, then with God — at His disposal — are the rewards of this world and the Hereafter, together. *Thawāb* — reward, return, recompense — was explained as wage for deed, as the sūrah has promised *thawāb* — fine reward — to both sitter with excuse and striver yet greater for striver (4:95–96), great reward for seeking His pleasure via charity and reconciliation (4:114), tremendous loss for taking Satan as guardian instead of Him (4:119), and *yujza* — recompensed — for evil accordingly (4:123). *Yurīdu thawāba al-dunyā* — desires the world’s reward — was read as the shortcut: to act for world alone, as the lagger desired gain without sharing faith (4:73), as the denier of peace sought fleeting gain (4:94), as the wishers thought grace by wish (4:123). *Fa ʿinda Allāhi thawābu al-dunyā wa al-ākhirati* — with Him are both — was explained as exclusive possession: both rewards are *ʿinda Allāh* — with Him, at His side, from His bounty — as *to Him belongs whatever is in heavens and earth* (4:126, 4:131) — so to desire world alone from other than Him is to seek little from the One who has much. *Wa* — and — joins the two: He has not divided them except where you divide desire; to ask Him is to obtain both, as the emigrant found many havens and abundance in earth (4:100) and the separator was enriched from His spaciousness (4:130) — world — while the believer hoping from Him finds forgiveness and mercy (4:96) — Hereafter.

**And God Is All-Hearing, All-Seeing**

The seal *wa kāna Allāhu Samīʿan Baṣīrā* — And God is All-Hearing, All-Seeing, was ever — was explained with *Samīʿ* — hearing every wish, every secret talk, every advocacy in this life (4:109), every dispute judged by what He shows (4:105) — and *Baṣīr* — seeing every act, every inclination not made equal between wives (4:129), every throwing sin on innocent (4:112), every charity meant for His pleasure (4:114). *Samīʿ Baṣīr* has sealed the command to return trusts and judge fairly — *noble command from the All-Hearing, All-Seeing* (4:58) — and now seals desire for reward: He hears the desire for world, sees the act for it, and measures accordingly without wronging even an atom but multiplying good many times (4:40). To say *with Him are both rewards* and He is All-Hearing, All-Seeing is to say seeking from Him is not unheard or unseen; the One who hears the wife fearing *nushūz* (4:128) and sees the *shuḥḥ* present with souls hears the desirer’s *yurīd* and sees whether desire is for Him or for little.

**A Verse That Follows Capability to Replace With Choice of Reward**

Placed after *if He wills He can remove you O humanity and bring others, He is Most Capable* (4:133), this verse follows capability to replace with choice of reward that determines whether you are removed or remain. The sūrah has warned *O humanity be mindful of your Lord who created you from single soul* (4:1) and later *O humanity He can remove you* (4:133) — between those two *humanity* addresses lies law for orphans, women, inheritance, trust, prayer, judgment, charity, Abraham, settlement, and now desire: to desire world’s reward alone from other than Him is to choose little where great is with Him. The sūrah has shown that those who took Satan as guardian instead of Him suffered tremendous loss seeking what he promised as delusion (4:119–121) while those who believed and did good found Gardens ever true (4:122); to desire world alone is to take the deluder’s little. The sūrah has said *all honour and power belongs to Him* (4:139) where hypocrites seek honour from disbelievers as allies; here *both rewards are with Him* is the same ownership: honour, as reward, is with Him alone. For the weak this is an opening: you need not envy what God gave some over others (4:32), but ask Him — with Him are both, and He hears and sees the asking.

**Both Rewards With Him Makes Single Desire Small**

The phrase *ʿinda Allāhi thawābu al-dunyā wa al-ākhirati* was explained as making single desire small. If you desire world alone, you ask the Capable who can remove and bring (4:133) for what perishes; had you asked Him you would have both — world as *murāgham* and *saʿah* (4:100), and Hereafter as Gardens forever true (4:122). The sūrah has shown that the Hereafter is better for the mindful than little enjoyment (4:77), that prayer at fixed writ (4:103) secures both, that standing firm for justice though against yourselves (4:135) secures both though self-interest seems to favour world alone. *Samīʿ Baṣīr* as seal was linked to *do not let desires cause you to deviate from justice* (4:135): desire for world’s reward deviates where hearing and seeing are not recalled. To remember He hears and sees is to recall that *“whoever commits evil will be rewarded accordingly and find no protector besides Him”* (4:123) yet *“whoever does good male or female with faith will enter Paradise not wronged a speck”* (4:124) — both measured by the All-Hearing, All-Seeing.

**A Short Verse That Anchors Asking**

Though only *“whoever desires world’s reward, with Him are both rewards of this world and Hereafter, He is All-Hearing, All-Seeing,”* the verse anchors asking. The chapter has commanded to refer disputes to God and Messenger (4:59), to verify reports (4:83), to be sure before fighting (4:94), to judge by what He shows (4:105), to command charity, kindness, reconciliation (4:114); all are acts whose reward the desirer might limit to world. The verse says ask the Owner who hears and sees for both, and you will find — as emigrant found havens, as separator found enrichment, as seeker found forgiveness. The next verse will apply that hearing and seeing to justice: stand firm for justice as witnesses for God even against yourselves, parents, kin; rich or poor, God ensures interests; do not let desires deviate, if you distort or refuse, He is All-Aware of what you do (4:135).
