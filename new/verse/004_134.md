## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:134

> Whoever desires the reward of this world, then ˹let them know that˺ with Allah are the rewards of this world and the Hereafter. And Allah is All-Hearing, All-Seeing.

### Expanded Commentary

**Two kinds of reward are with Allah**

Whoever desires the reward of this world should know that Allah possesses both worldly and Hereafter reward. The verse does not condemn lawful worldly benefit. It corrects the narrowness of wanting only immediate return. Provision, success, health, and honour are gifts within God’s power, but they are not the final measure of a good life.

A person may perform religion for status and still fail to seek the Hereafter. Another may work for lawful livelihood while intending to support family and serve society, turning worldly effort into worship. Allah hears the intention and sees the deed, so outward similarity does not mean equal value.

The verse encourages a wider request: ask for good in this world and the next, without making either a rival to God. The believer uses worldly means but keeps the lasting reward as the governing horizon.

**Ask for both worlds wisely**

The verse does not require a false choice between livelihood and the Hereafter. A person can seek work, health, and family stability while intending that these goods support worship and service. The danger is allowing immediate reward to become the only reward one can imagine, because that narrows both prayer and moral judgment.
