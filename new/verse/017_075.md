## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:75

> **and then We truly would have made you taste double ˹punishment˺ both in this life and after death, and you would have found no helper against Us.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Then We Would Have Made You Taste Double Punishment In This Life And After Death**

Idhan la-adhaqnāka ḍiʿfa al-ḥayāti wa-ḍiʿfa al-mamāti — then We would have made you taste double of life and double of death, double punishment in this life and after death. Idhan, then, in that case, then, if you had inclined to them a little, had We not made you steadfast. La-adhaqnāka from dh-w-q, to make taste, make experience, from adhaqa, to make taste, make experience, as adhaqnāka We would have made you taste, experience, as taste punishment, experience punishment. Ḍiʿfa from ḍ-ʿ-f, double, twofold, double, from ḍiʿf, double, twofold, as double, twofold, as double punishment. Al-ḥayāti from ḥ-y-y, life, this life, worldly life, from ḥayāh, life. Wa-ḍiʿfa al-mamāti, and double of death, double of after death, double punishment after death, from m-w-t, death, after death, Hereafter, as mamāt, death, after death, Hereafter. Al-Ṭabarī explains that then We truly would have made you taste double punishment both in this life and after death, meaning if you, O Prophet, had inclined to them, disbelievers who tried to lure you away from revelation, hoping attribute something else falsely and taken as close friend, a little, had We not made you steadfast, then We would have made you taste double punishment in this life, worldly, and double punishment after death, Hereafter, double, twofold, severe, as warning, even for Prophet, double punishment if inclined, even little, as Prophet protected, steadfast, as Lord encompasses, knows best, etc.

**You Would Have Found No Helper Against Us**

Thumma lā tajidu laka ʿalaynā naṣīran — then you would not find for you against Us helper, supporter. Thumma, then, after double punishment. Lā tajidu from w-j-d, to find, you would not find, as lā tajidū you will not find guardian, avenger, etc. Laka, for you, for yourself. ʿAlaynā, against Us, upon Us, against Allah, Us. Naṣīran from n-ṣ-r, helper, supporter, from naṣara, to help, support, as naṣīr, helper, supporter, as manṣūr supported, and naṣīran helper, supporter, as no helper against Us, against Allah, if He punishes double in life and death, no helper, supporter, to protect, avenge, as only Allah has power, as those claimed besides Him have no power, as verse 56 says, no power, so no helper against Us.

**Why Double Punishment And No Helper If Inclined**

Why double punishment both in this life and after death and no helper against Us if inclined a little? Because luring away from revelation, attributing something else falsely to Allah for close friendship with disbelievers, inclining to them even little, is grave, serious, especially for Prophet, who is favoured, given revelation, wisdom, most upright, Night Journey, etc., so if he inclined, even little, double punishment, twofold, severe, in this life and after death, as warning, as even Prophet would have double punishment if inclined, so steadfastness essential, thabbatnāka, We made you firm, steadfast, so no inclination, not even little, as Prophet protected, steadfast, as Lord encompasses, knows best, All-Aware All-Seeing, Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, but torment fearsome, and no helper against Us, no supporter, if He punishes, as only He has power.

**Connection To Lure Away And Steadfast And Intimidate And Our Way No Change**

Verse 73 they were about to lure you away from what We revealed hoping attribute something else falsely and taken as close friend. Verse 74 had We not made you steadfast you would have inclined to them a little. Verse 75 then We would have made you taste double punishment in this life and after death and you would have found no helper against Us, showing sequence: luring away, hoping attribute falsely, taken as close friend, had not made steadfast would have inclined little, then double punishment in life and death, no helper against Us, as warning, as Prophet protected, steadfast, as Lord encompasses, knows best, etc., and as Our way with messengers no change, as messengers made steadfast, not inclined, etc., as divine law, as every wicked society destroyed or punished severely before Day written in Record, etc.

**Link To Our Way With Messengers And Intimidate To Drive Out Of Land**

Verse 76 they were about to intimidate you to drive you out of land of Mecca, but then they would not have survived after you left except little while. Verse 77 this has been Our way with messengers We sent before you, you will never find any change in Our way, showing that luring away, inclining, double punishment, no helper, intimidating to drive out, are ways of disbelievers against messengers, but Our way with messengers is steadfast, no change, as messengers made steadfast, not lured, not inclined, not intimidated, etc., as divine law, as every wicked society destroyed or punished severely before Day written in Record, etc., and as double punishment would have been if inclined, but Prophet made steadfast, so no double punishment, no helper needed.

**Linguistic Note On Adhaqnaka And Di'f And Hayah And Mamat And Nasiran**

Idhan, then, in that case. Adhaqnāka from dh-w-q, to make taste, experience, from adhaqa. Ḍiʿf from ḍ-ʿ-f, double, twofold, from ḍiʿf. Ḥayāh from ḥ-y-y, life, from ḥayāh. Mamāt from m-w-t, death, after death, from māta, to die, from mamāt, death. Tajidu from w-j-d, to find. Naṣīr from n-ṣ-r, helper, supporter, from naṣara, to help, support. The verse's structure — then We would have made you taste double of life and double of death, then you would not find for you against Us helper — with double punishment in life and death and no helper against Us, shows severity if Prophet had inclined even little to disbelievers who tried to lure away, tempt, hoping attribute falsely, taken as close friend, but Allah made steadfast, so no double punishment, no helper needed, as Prophet protected, steadfast.

**How This Verse Teaches Double Punishment And No Helper If Inclined**

This verse teaches double punishment and no helper if inclined to disbelievers: then We truly would have made you taste double punishment both in this life, worldly, and after death, Hereafter, double, twofold, severe, and you would have found no helper, supporter, against Us, against Allah, if He punishes double, no helper, supporter, to protect, avenge, as only Allah has power, as those claimed besides Him have no power, as verse 56 says, no power, so be steadfast, not incline even little to disbelievers who try to lure away from revelation, hoping attribute something else falsely and taken as close friend, but be steadfast, firm, stable, as Allah made steadfast, thabbatnāka, We made you firm, so no inclination, not even little, as Prophet protected, steadfast, as Lord encompasses, knows best, etc., and as Our way with messengers no change, as messengers made steadfast. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should not incline to disbelievers even little, not be lured away from revelation, not attribute falsely, but be steadfast, follow most upright guidance, wisdom, that Qur'an guides to, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, avoiding double punishment in life and death, no helper against Us, Hell, torment fearsome, but Lord Most Forbearing All-Forgiving to those who constantly turn.

**A Reflection On Double And No Helper**

Ḍiʿfa al-ḥayāti wa-ḍiʿfa al-mamāti — double of life and double of death, double punishment in this life and after death — indicates severity, twofold, double, severe, both in worldly life and after death, Hereafter, double punishment, as warning, even for Prophet, double punishment if inclined even little, so steadfastness essential, thabbatnāka, We made you firm, steadfast, stable, so no double punishment. Lā tajidu laka ʿalaynā naṣīran — you would not find for you against Us helper, supporter — indicates no helper, supporter, against Allah if He punishes double, no guardian, no avenger, no helper, as only Allah has power, as those claimed besides Him have no power, as verse 56 says, no power to remove harm or transfer, so no helper against Us, so be steadfast, not incline, follow most upright, wisdom, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, avoiding double punishment, no helper, Hell, as torment fearsome, but Lord Most Forbearing All-Forgiving.
