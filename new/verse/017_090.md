## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:90

> **They challenge ˹the Prophet˺, “We will never believe in you until you cause a spring to gush forth from the earth for us,**

**Expanded Commentary**

**We Will Never Believe Until You Cause Spring To Gush From Earth For Us**

Wa-qālū lan nu'mina laka ḥattā tafjura lanā mina al-arḍi yanbūʿan — and they said, we will never believe in you until you cause to gush for us from earth spring. Wa-qālū from q-w-l, to say, they said, they challenge Prophet, disbelievers, polytheists, who persist in disbelief, etc., challenge, demand signs. Lan nu'mina from a-m-n, to believe, we will never believe, will never believe, emphatic never, lan nu'mina, we will never believe, will not believe, as lan never, will never, emphatic never, as they challenge, we will never believe in you until... Laka, in you, to you, O Prophet, in you, to you, as believe in you, O Prophet, as believe in you, as Prophet, messenger, as human messenger, etc. Ḥattā, until, until you do... Tafjura from f-j-r, to cause to gush, to make flow, to split, to cause spring to gush, from fajara, to cause to gush, to split, to make flow, as tafjura cause to gush, make flow, as spring gush forth from earth. Lanā, for us, to us, for us. Mina al-arḍi, from earth, from earth, from arḍ land, earth, as from earth, as spring from earth, as earth, as land. Yanbūʿan from n-b-ʿ, spring, gushing spring, source, from yanbūʿ, spring, gushing spring, source, that gushes forth from earth, as spring, gushing spring, as they challenge, we will never believe until you cause spring to gush from earth for us, as demanded sign, miracle, as they demand spring gush from earth, as sign, as they persist in disbelief, challenge, demand signs, as every kind of lesson set forth in Quran yet most persist in disbelief, as they challenge, demand spring gush, etc., as signs, but nothing keeps Us from sending demanded signs except they were denied by earlier peoples, etc., We only send signs as warning, etc., as previous verse 59 says, etc.

**Why Spring Gush From Earth As Demanded Sign**

Why spring gush forth from earth for us as demanded sign, challenge? Because they, disbelievers, persist in disbelief, refuse except disbelief, as most people persist in disbelief, as verse 89 says, and they challenge Prophet, demand signs, miracles, as proof, as they say we will never believe in you until you cause spring to gush from earth for us, as demanded sign, as spring gush from earth, as miracle, as they demand spring, etc., as well as garden of palm trees and vineyards cause rivers to flow abundantly, or cause sky to fall in pieces as claimed, or bring Allah and angels before us face to face, or house of gold, or ascend into heaven and even then not believe in ascension until you bring down book that we can read, say Glory be to my Lord, am I not only human messenger, as verses 90-93 list demands, challenges, as they demand signs, miracles, but Quran inimitable, cannot produce equal, every kind of lesson set forth, yet most persist in disbelief, challenge, demand signs, as they persist in disbelief, refuse except disbelief, as disbelief, ungratefulness, etc.

**Connection To Every Kind Of Lesson Set Forth Yet Most Persist In Disbelief And Challenges**

Verse 89 We set forth every kind of lesson for humanity in this Quran, yet most people persist in disbelief, refuse except disbelief. Verse 90-93 they challenge Prophet, we will never believe until you cause spring to gush from earth for us, or garden of palm trees and vineyards cause rivers to flow abundantly, or cause sky to fall in pieces as claimed, or bring Allah and angels before us face to face, or house of gold, or ascend into heaven and even then not believe in ascension until you bring down book that we can read, say Glory be to my Lord, am I not only human messenger, showing challenges, demands for signs, miracles, but every kind of lesson set forth, yet most persist in disbelief, challenge, demand signs, as they persist in disbelief, refuse except disbelief, as disbelief, ungratefulness, as human ever ungrateful, etc., and as nothing keeps Us from sending demanded signs except they were denied by earlier peoples, etc., We only send signs as warning, etc., as previous verse 59 says, etc., and as Quran inimitable, cannot produce equal, even if humans and jinn gathered, supported, etc., yet they demand spring gush, etc., as challenges, demands.

**Link To Nothing Keeps Us From Sending Demanded Signs Except Denied By Earlier And We Only Send Signs As Warning**

Verse 59 nothing keeps Us from sending demanded signs except they had already been denied by earlier peoples, We gave Thamud shecamel as clear sign but they wrongfully rejected, We only send signs as warning, showing that demanded signs, if sent and denied, lead to destruction, severe punishment, as earlier peoples denied and destroyed, as every wicked society destroyed or punished severely before Day written in Record, as many generations destroyed after Noah, etc., and as Thamud shecamel example, etc., so We only send signs as warning, not as demanded entertainment, mocking, but as warning, to frighten, so they may be mindful, etc., and now they challenge, demand spring gush, garden, rivers, sky fall, Allah and angels face to face, house of gold, ascend into heaven and bring down book, etc., as demanded signs, but nothing keeps Us from sending demanded signs except denied by earlier, etc., We only send signs as warning, etc., and as Quran inimitable, cannot produce equal, every kind of lesson set forth, yet most persist in disbelief, challenge, demand signs, as they persist in disbelief.

**Linguistic Note On Lan Nu'mina And Tafjura And Yanbu'an**

Qālū from q-w-l, to say. Lan nu'mina from a-m-n, to believe, we will never believe, from amina, to believe, with lan emphatic never, will never. Laka, in you, to you. Ḥattā, until. Tafjura from f-j-r, to cause to gush, make flow, split, from fajara, to cause to gush, split. Lanā, for us. Arḍ from a-r-ḍ, earth, land, from arḍ. Yanbūʿ from n-b-ʿ, spring, gushing spring, source, from nabaʿa, to gush forth, spring forth, from yanbūʿ, spring. The verse's structure — they said we will never believe in you until you cause to gush for us from earth spring — with challenge we will never believe until you cause spring to gush from earth for us, shows demand for sign, miracle, spring gush from earth, as proof, as they persist in disbelief, challenge, demand signs, as every kind of lesson set forth yet most persist in disbelief, etc.

**How This Verse Teaches Challenge Demanding Spring Gush**

This verse teaches challenge, demanding spring gush from earth as sign: they, disbelievers, challenge Prophet, say we will never believe in you, O Prophet, until you cause spring, gushing spring, source, to gush forth from earth for us, from earth, as demanded sign, miracle, as proof, as they persist in disbelief, refuse except disbelief, as most people persist in disbelief, as verse 89 says, and they challenge, demand signs, miracles, as proof, as they say we will never believe until you cause spring to gush from earth for us, or garden, rivers, sky fall, Allah and angels face to face, house of gold, ascend into heaven and bring down book, etc., as demands, challenges, as they demand signs, but nothing keeps Us from sending demanded signs except they were denied by earlier peoples, etc., We only send signs as warning, etc., and as Quran inimitable, cannot produce equal, every kind of lesson set forth, yet most persist in disbelief, challenge, demand signs, as they persist in disbelief. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should not challenge Prophet demanding spring gush from earth, etc., as disbelievers do, who persist in disbelief, refuse except disbelief, but believe, follow most upright guidance, wisdom, that Qur'an guides to, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, healing mercy, etc., while disbelievers challenge, demand signs, persist in disbelief, painful punishment prepared, etc.
