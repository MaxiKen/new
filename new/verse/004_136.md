## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:136

> O believers! Have faith in Allah, His Messenger, the Book He has revealed to His Messenger, and the Scriptures He revealed before. Indeed, whoever denies Allah, His angels, His Books, His messengers, and the Last Day has clearly gone far astray.

### Expanded Commentary

**Faith is an integrated whole**

Believers are commanded to have faith in Allah, the Messenger, the Qur’an, and the scriptures revealed before it. The call is not addressed only to outsiders; people who already say they believe are reminded to make faith complete and active.

The verse lists Allah, angels, Books, messengers, and the Last Day. Denying one essential element fragments the revealed worldview. A person cannot claim respect for God while rejecting His messengers, or claim loyalty to Muhammad while dismissing the earlier prophets whom the Qur’an confirms.

The warning against far straying is not a demand for superficial uniformity. It requires informed commitment, honest questions, and acceptance of what God has revealed. The believer studies the tradition as a connected chain rather than selecting only the parts that serve a prior ideology.

**Faith and intellectual wholeness**

The list of articles of faith keeps believers from reducing religion to ethics without revelation or doctrine without ethics. Angels, Books, messengers, and the Last Day make human life part of a larger unseen order. Denial of one essential element can distort how a person understands responsibility, history, and hope.
