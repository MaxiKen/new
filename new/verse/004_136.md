## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:136

> O believers! Have faith in Allah, His Messenger, the Book He has revealed to His Messenger, and the Scriptures He revealed before. Indeed, whoever denies Allah, His angels, His Books, His messengers, and the Last Day has clearly gone far astray.

**Expanded Commentary**

**O Believers — Have Faith in God, Messenger, and Books**

The verse opens *yā ayyuhā alladhīna āmanū āminū bi Allāhi wa rasūlihī wa al-kitābi alladhī nazzala ʿalā rasūlihī wa al-kitābi alladhī anzala min qablu* — O believers, have faith — believe, continue believing — in God and His Messenger and the Book He has sent down upon His Messenger and the Scriptures He sent down before. *Āminū* to *āmanū* was explained not as contradiction but as command to firmness, renewal, and comprehensiveness: those who already believe are told to remain believers, to increase belief, to believe without distinction, as the sūrah has shown that those who believe and do good are admitted to Gardens (4:57, 4:122) yet belief must be in the full articles. *Bi Allāhi wa rasūlihī* — in God and Messenger — was read as the two inseparably taught where obeying the Messenger is obeying God (4:80), messengers are only sent to be obeyed by His leave (4:64), and the Messenger is taken as judge without resistance (4:65). *Al-kitābi alladhī nazzala ʿalā rasūlihī* — the Book He revealed to His Messenger — was explained as the Qur’an sent with truth to judge between people by what He shows (4:105) whose word none is more truthful than (4:122). *Wa al-kitābi alladhī anzala min qablu* — and the Scriptures He revealed before — was read as the Books given before — Torah, Gospel — before distortion, matching *“Worship God alone, associate none”* (4:36) and *“We commanded those given Scripture before you and you to be mindful”* (4:131) — the command is one. The sūrah has shown that the People of the Book claim wishes (4:123) and distort words (4:46); to believe in Books before is to believe in them as revealed before distorting hands altered them, not to follow altered claim.

**And Whoever Denies — Has Gone Far Astray**

The threat *wa man yakfur bi Allāhi wa malāʾikatihī wa kutubihī wa rusulihī wa al-yawmi al-ākhiri fa qad ḍalla ḍalālan baʿīdā* — indeed whoever denies — covers, disbelieves in — God, His angels, His Books, His messengers, and the Last Day has clearly gone far astray, wandered far — was explained with five articles. *Yakfur* — to deny, to cover — was read as the denial that makes association not forgiven (4:116) and that makes following other than believers’ path burning (4:115). The five — God, angels, Books, messengers, Last Day — were explained as the Book’s summary of faith, matching *“whoever associates has gone far astray”* (4:116) where *ḍalālan baʿīdan* — far straying — also sealed; here the same seal for denying any of the five. Angels were read as the recorders of night schemes (4:81), the takers of souls asked *what were you doing?* (4:97), the supporters of prayer guard (4:102); to deny angels is to deny those realities. Books and messengers were read as the conveyors of the Book in truth and wisdom taught (4:113); Last Day as the Day about which there is no doubt where all will be gathered (4:87) and gathered together hypocrites and disbelievers in Hell (4:140). *Ḍalla ḍalālan baʿīdā* — gone far astray — was linked to going far after guidance became clear (4:115); denial of these articles after clarity is the farthest distance, because it denies the very witnesses who prove clarity.

**A Verse That Follows Justice With Creed**

Placed after *stand firm for justice as witnesses for God though against yourselves rich or poor, do not follow desire, if you twist or turn away He is All-Aware* (4:135), this verse follows justice with creed that secures it. The sūrah has judged between people by Book, warned no good in most secret talks except charity, kindness, reconciliation (4:114), warned defying the Messenger and following other than believers’ path (4:115), named association far straying (4:116), female gods and Satan’s share (4:117–118), Satan’s orders and delusion (4:119–120), two abodes true (4:121–122), wishes not by wish (4:123), good with faith not wronged (4:124), best faith submission following Abraham whom God took as friend (4:125), ownership and encompassing (4:126), instruction for orphan women and helpless children (4:127), settlement best though selves brought with *shuḥḥ* (4:128), impossible emotional equality yet do not incline entirely (4:129), enrichment after separation (4:130), universal command to be mindful though He is Self-Sufficient (4:131), sufficiency as Trustee (4:132), capability to replace humanity (4:133), both rewards with Him Hearing and Seeing (4:134); now faith in the full articles is the root that makes justice — *qawwāmīn bi al-qisṭ* — possible without twisting. The sūrah that warns not to deviate by desire (4:27) and not to let desires deviate from justice (4:135) here names the content that desire most attacks — God, angels, Books, messengers, Last Day — after which far straying is the fruit.

**Belief Commanded to Believers — Renewal Not Repetition**

The command *yā ayyuhā alladhīna āmanū āminū* was explained as renewal, firmness, and widening: to believers who believed in part — in God but not Messenger, in this Book but not those before, in God and Books but not angels or Last Day — the command is to believe entirely, without making distinction between God and messengers saying *we believe in some and disbelieve in others desiring a compromise* (4:150). The sūrah has shown that making distinction is the compromise of those who deny — they make distinction and desire to forge compromise (4:150) — here the command closes that compromise: have faith in God, Messenger, the Book He revealed to His Messenger, and Scriptures He revealed before, and in angels and Last Day implied — no selecting. Where the sūrah later says *those who believed then disbelieved then believed again then increased in disbelief — He will neither forgive nor guide* (4:137), redoubled belief is shown as fragile where articles are not firm; *āminū* here is the firming before that warning.

**A Short Verse That Anchors Law in Last Day**

Though only *“believe in God, Messenger, Book He revealed to His Messenger and Scriptures before, whoever denies God, angels, Books, messengers, Last Day has gone far astray,”* the verse anchors law in Last Day. The justice just commanded — standing though against selves and parents, not distorting — has no motive without Last Day where advocates in this life will find no advocate before God (4:109) and where those gathered together in Hell will be hypocrites and disbelievers (4:140). To deny Last Day after guidance clear is to make the *yujza* — recompensed accordingly — for evil (4:123) unintelligible, and to deny Books and messengers is to make judging by what He shows (4:105) unintelligible, and to deny angels is to make recording of night schemes (4:81, 4:108) unintelligible. For the weak this is the lightest anchoring: to have faith — renewed, entire — in the Hearing, Seeing (4:134) who owns heavens and earth (4:132) and who will not wrong a speck (4:124) is to entrust justice to the *Wakīl* who suffices, the Self-Sufficient who does not need.
