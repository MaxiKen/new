## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:50

> **Say, ˹O Prophet,˺ “˹Yes, even if˺ you become stones, or iron,**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Say Even If You Become Stones Or Iron**

Qul kūnū ḥijāratan aw ḥadīdan — say, be stones or iron. Qul from q-w-l, to say, command to Prophet to answer disbelievers who mock resurrection, bones and ashes new creation. Kūnū from k-w-n, to be, become, imperative, be, become, plural, be, become, etc. Ḥijāratan from ḥ-j-r, stones, rocks, plural of ḥajar, stone, rock, as ḥijārah stones, rocks, etc. Aw ḥadīdan from ḥ-d-d, iron, from ḥadīd, iron, hard metal, etc. Al-Ṭabarī explains that say, O Prophet, to disbelievers who say when we are bones and ashes will we be raised new creation, mocking, yes even if you become stones or iron, even if you become stones, rocks, or iron, hard, you will be raised, recreated, as Allah Who created first time can recreate even if you become stones or iron, harder than bones and ashes, etc. Al-Qurṭubī says kūnū ḥijāratan aw ḥadīdan means even if you become stones or iron, harder, more resistant to decay, you will be raised, as Allah able to recreate stones, iron, as He created first time, as He is All-Powerful, All-Aware All-Seeing, etc., as Allah able to recreate stones, iron, etc., as He created first time, etc., as All-Powerful, etc.

**Why Stones Or Iron And Harder Than Bones And Ashes**

Why stones or iron? Because disbelievers said when we are bones and ashes, rufāt, crumbled dust, will we be raised new creation, thinking bones and ashes cannot be recreated, as bones and ashes weak, crumbled, but stones and iron are harder, more solid, more resistant, so even if you become stones or iron, harder than bones and ashes, you will be raised, as Allah able to recreate even harder, as argument from harder to easier: if Allah can recreate stones, iron, harder, then can recreate bones and ashes, easier, etc., as argument from harder to easier, etc. The phrase kūnū ḥijāratan aw ḥadīdan is hypothetical, even if you become stones or iron, you will be raised, as Allah's power not limited by hardness, as He created heavens and earth, seven heavens, earth, all things glorify Him, etc., as Allah's power not limited by hardness, material, as He created heavens and earth, etc., as He created first time, etc., as All-Powerful, etc. Stones and iron are examples of hard materials, harder than bones and ashes, rufāt, crumbled dust, indicating that even if you become hardest, stones or iron, you will be raised, recreated, as Allah's power not limited by hardness, material, etc., as He created heavens and earth, etc., as He created first time, etc., as All-Powerful, etc., as argument, etc.

**Connection To Bones And Ashes And New Creation And Whatever Harder And One Who Created First Time Will Bring Back**

Verse 49 said they say mockingly when we are bones and ashes will we be raised new creation, denying resurrection, etc., as bones and ashes new creation, etc., as denial, etc. Verse 50 says say, O Prophet, yes even if you become stones or iron, and verse 51 says or whatever you think is harder to bring to life, then they will ask who will bring us back, say One Who created you first time, they will shake heads and ask when will that be, say perhaps soon, etc., as answer, etc. The connection is that bones and ashes denial answered by even if stones or iron or whatever harder, you will be raised, One Who created first time will bring back, perhaps soon, as resurrection is soon, not far, as Day He will call you respond praising Him thinking you remained only little while, as verse 52 says, etc., as Day call, etc., as resurrection, etc. So bones and ashes denial answered by stones or iron or whatever harder, One Who created first time will bring back, perhaps soon, Day call, etc., as resurrection, etc., as answer, etc., as bones and ashes new creation, etc., as stones or iron, etc., as whatever harder, etc., as One Who created first time will bring back, etc., as perhaps soon, etc., as Day call, etc.

**Link To Allah Created First Time And Perhaps Soon And Day Call And Power To Recreate**

Verse 51-52 will say One Who created first time will bring back, perhaps soon, Day He will call you respond praising Him thinking remained only little while, etc., as One Who created first time will bring back, etc., as perhaps soon, etc., as Day call, etc. Verse 50 says become stones or iron, indicating even if harder, you will be raised, as Allah Who created first time able to recreate, as He created heavens and earth, seven heavens, earth, all things, etc., and resurrection soon, Day call, etc., so believe, not mock, etc., as Allah created first time able to recreate, etc., as He created heavens and earth, etc., as resurrection soon, etc., as Day call, etc., as believe, not mock, etc., as Allah created first time able to recreate, etc., as He created heavens and earth, etc., as resurrection soon, etc., as Day call, etc., as believe, not mock, etc.

**Linguistic Note On Kunu And Hijarah And Hadid And Harder**

Qul from q-w-l, to say. Kūnū from k-w-n, to be, become, imperative plural, be, become. Ḥijārah from ḥ-j-r, stones, rocks, plural of ḥajar, from ḥajara, to be stone. Ḥadīd from ḥ-d-d, iron, from ḥadda, to be sharp, iron sharp. The verse's structure — say be stones or iron — with command to say and hypothetical be stones or iron, indicates even if you become stones or iron, harder than bones and ashes, you will be raised, recreated, as Allah able, as argument from harder to easier, showing power to recreate any, even hardest, etc., as say be stones or iron, etc., as hypothetical, etc., as even if you become stones or iron, etc., as harder than bones and ashes, etc., as you will be raised, etc., as Allah able, etc., as argument from harder to easier, etc., as power to recreate any, even hardest, etc.

**How This Verse Teaches Power To Recreate Even Stones Iron And Whatever Harder**

This verse teaches power of Allah to recreate even if you become stones or iron, harder than bones and ashes, as answer to mocking denial of resurrection when bones and ashes new creation, etc., as answer, etc. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should believe Allah Who created first time can recreate even if become stones or iron, harder, as He is All-Powerful, All-Aware All-Seeing, Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, but also severe, and resurrection perhaps soon, Day He will call you respond praising Him thinking remained only little while, etc., as answer, etc., as resurrection, etc. The practical lesson is to believe in resurrection, that Allah can recreate even stones, iron, whatever harder, as He created first time, not mock, not deny, as disbelievers do, who have barrier, veils, deafness, aversion, far astray cannot find Right Way, painful punishment prepared, etc., as disbelievers have barrier, veils, deafness, aversion, far astray cannot find Right Way, painful punishment prepared, etc., as believer believes in resurrection, etc., as Allah can recreate even stones, iron, whatever harder, as He created first time, etc., as He is All-Powerful, All-Aware All-Seeing, Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, etc.
