## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:142

> Surely the hypocrites seek to deceive Allah, but He outwits them. When they stand up for prayer, they do it half-heartedly only to be seen by people—hardly remembering Allah at all.

**Expanded Commentary**

**The Hypocrites Seek to Deceive God — But He Outwits Them**

The verse opens *inna al-munāfiqīna yukhādiʿūna Allāha wa huwa khādiʿuhum* — surely the hypocrites seek to deceive, to trick, God, but He is outwitting, deceiving them, repaying their deceit. *Yukhādiʿūna* — to seek to deceive, to pretend, to show other than what is — was explained as hypocrisy’s essence: belief then disbelief then increased (4:137), taking disbelievers as allies seeking honour (4:139), sitting where revelations are mocked (4:140), waiting between winners to claim *were we not with you?* (4:141). To deceive God was explained as showing Islam before believers while hiding alliance with disbelievers, as plotting by night what displeases Him while He is with them (4:108) and hiding from people but not from the Encompassing (4:108). *Wa huwa khādiʿuhum* — but He outwits them, He is their deceiver in return — was read as that His outwitting is recompense: He lets them pursue what they chose (4:115), gathers them with disbelievers together in Hell (4:140), will judge between all on the Day (4:141), and leaves to stray whom He leaves (4:143). The sūrah has said that God is Fully Aware of what they do (4:108) and knows what is in hearts (4:63); deception cannot reach Him, it returns. Early notes linked *khādiʿuhum* to *makr* — planning — where *“they plot and God plots, God is best of plotters”* (3:54); the outwitting is His plan that gathering together is the outcome of sitting together.

**When They Stand for Prayer — Half-Heartedly, To Be Seen**

The sign *wa idhā qāmū ilā al-ṣalāti qāmū kusālā yurāʾūna al-nāsa* — and when they stand up for prayer, head to prayer, they stand lazy, sluggish, half-heartedly, showing off to people, to be seen — was explained with *qāmū ilā al-ṣalāti* — standing to prayer — the same standing commanded as *qawwāmīn bi al-qisṭ* — ever standing for justice (4:135) — and *qūmū li al-yatāmā bi al-qisṭ* (4:127) — here the hypocrites stand to prayer as witnesses not for Him but for people. *Kusālā* — lazy, heavy, sluggish — was explained as heaviness where the sūrah earlier said prayer at appointed writ (4:103) and guarded prayer under arms (4:102) requires vigilance; their laziness is the opposite of vigilance and of pursuit without faltering (4:104). *Yurāʾūna al-nāsa* — they show off to people, make seen — *riyāʾ* — was read as the same showing off condemned where they spend wealth to show off and do not believe in God or Last Day (4:38) — *spending to be seen* — here *standing to be seen*. The sūrah has shown that there is no good in most secret talks except charity, kindness, reconciliation seeking His pleasure (4:114); to stand to be seen is to seek people’s pleasure, the *ʿizzah* sought among disbelievers (4:139), not His.

**Hardly Remembering God at All**

The remainder *wa lā yadhkurūna Allāha illā qalīlā* — and they hardly remember, mention, invoke God at all, except a little — was explained with *yadhkurūna* — to remember, to mention — the same *adhkurū Allāha* commanded standing, sitting, lying down when prayer is finished (4:103). The sūrah has shown that remembrance in all postures is prayer’s extension; hypocrites remember a little — *qalīlan* — the minimum to be seen, not the standing, sitting, lying remembrance. *Illā qalīlan* — except a little — was explained as little in quantity and little in sincerity, as the sūrah says they stand lazily to be seen — their few remembrances are for seen-ness. Classical notes added that the *qalīl* matches the world’s little enjoyment — *matāʿ qalīl* (4:77) — that hypocrisy trades Gardens forever (4:122) for little seen-ness. The seal contrasts with *“whose word more truthful than God’s?”* (4:122) and *“God is sufficient as Trustee”* (4:132): to remember little is to trust little.

**A Verse That Follows Waiting With Deception and Lazy Prayer**

Placed after those who wait to see what happens and claim belonging to winners (4:141), this verse follows waiting with the prayer that reveals waiting’s creed: to deceive God, to stand lazy to be seen, to remember little. The sūrah has shown prayer shortened and guarded (4:101–102) and remembered in all postures (4:103) as the believer’s discipline that does not falter in pursuit for hope (4:104); the hypocrite’s prayer deforms each — standing yet lazy, guarded yet to be seen, remembering but little. The sūrah that commands *stand firm for justice as witnesses for God even against yourselves* (4:135) shows the hypocrite’s witness is for people to be seen; the sūrah that says *with Him are both rewards, He is All-Hearing, All-Seeing* (4:134) shows He hears lazy standing and sees showing off. The verse was read as the hypocrite’s litmus after the announcement of painful punishment (4:138) and honour sought elsewhere (4:139): painful punishment is for those whose prayer is for sight, not for Him.

**Deception Outwitted Becomes Being Gathered Together**

The phrase *wa huwa khādiʿuhum* — He outwits them — was linked to *“He will gather hypocrites and disbelievers together in Hell”* (4:140): outwitting is gathering. The hypocrite deceives by sitting where signs are mocked as if not like them, yet the verse says *then you are like them* (4:140); on the Day the like-ness becomes gathering together. The sūrah has shown that whoever takes Satan as guardian instead of Him suffers tremendous loss (4:119) and that Satan promises only delusion (4:120); to stand for prayer to be seen is to take people as guardians instead of Him, whose delusion is *ʿizzah* seen. For the weak this is diagnostic: where you stand for prayer after fear, stand vigilant not lazy, remember standing, sitting, lying — much — not little, and seek His pleasure not people’s sight, for He who can remove humanity if He wills (4:133) outwits the deceiver by the very gathering the deceiver thought to avoid by waiting.
