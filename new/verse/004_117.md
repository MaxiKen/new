## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:117

> Instead of Allah, they only invoke female gods and they ˹actually˺ invoke none but a rebellious Satan—

### Expanded Commentary

**False gods and the rebellious impulse**

The verse says that those who invoke female deities in place of Allah are actually invoking Satan. The immediate reference is to pagan idols associated with goddesses, but the principle is broader: a false object of worship is part of a rebellious system that redirects devotion from the Creator.

The description of Satan as rebellious emphasizes choice. Evil is not an equal cosmic power competing with God; it is a created will in revolt. People who follow it are not discovering freedom but entering a pattern of dependency on false promises and appetites.

The verse should not be used to insult people individually or to erase the complexity of their beliefs. It calls believers to examine their own invocations: whose approval do they seek as ultimate, whose command do they treat as unquestionable, and what desire has become an idol?

**False objects of devotion**

Modern idols may be institutions, personalities, markets, or ideologies that demand unquestioned allegiance. The verse teaches believers to examine what receives their deepest fear and hope. If a created system can command a wrong and still be obeyed, it has begun to occupy the place that belongs to God.
