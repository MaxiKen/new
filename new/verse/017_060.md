## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:60

> **And ˹remember, O Prophet˺ when We told you, “Certainly your Lord encompasses the people.” And We have made what We brought you to see as well as the cursed tree ˹mentioned˺ in the Quran only as a test for the people. We keep warning them, but it only increases them greatly in defiance.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Remember When We Told You Lord Encompasses People**

Laka, to you, O Prophet. Inna rabbaka, indeed your Lord, your Lord, from r-b-b. Aḥāṭa from ḥ-w-ṭ, to encompass, surround, enclose, from aḥāṭa, to encompass, surround, enclose, as encompasses, surrounds, knows, controls, as Lord encompasses people, surrounds, knows, controls, as He knows best those in heavens and earth, etc. Bi-l-nāsi, with people, about people, people, from n-w-s, people, as nās people, humankind. Al-Qurṭubī says aḥāṭa bi-l-nāsi means encompasses people with knowledge, power, protection, surrounds, encloses, so Prophet protected, as Lord encompasses.
