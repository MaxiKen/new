## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:65

> **˹Allah added,˺ “You will truly have no authority over My ˹faithful˺ servants.” And sufficient is your Lord as a Guardian.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**You Will Have No Authority Over My Faithful Servants**

Inna ʿibādī laysa laka ʿalayhim sulṭānun — indeed My servants, faithful servants, not for you over them authority, power. Inna, indeed, surely. ʿIbādī, My servants, My faithful servants, servants who are faithful, sincere, from ʿ-b-d, servants, as ʿibād, servants, as ʿibādī My servants, faithful, as in verse 53 My believing servants say best, etc., and verse 65 faithful servants. Laysa laka, not for you, not for you, Satan, Iblis. ʿAlayhim, over them, upon them, over My faithful servants. Sulṭānun from s-l-ṭ, authority, power, control, proof, from sulṭān, authority, power, as previous sulṭānan authority given to heir of unjustly killed, etc., and sulṭān authority, power, as Satan has no authority over faithful servants. Al-Ṭabarī explains that you, Satan, Iblis, will have no authority, power, control over My faithful servants, sincere servants, who are faithful, who say best, follow most upright guidance, wisdom, etc., as they are protected, as Lord Guardian sufficient, as they are faithful, not among those taken hold of except few, as few faithful, qalīlan, except few, as faithful servants protected. Al-Qurṭubī says ʿibādī here means faithful servants, sincere, believers, who are faithful, not all servants, but faithful, sincere, who have no authority for Satan over them.

**Sufficient Lord As Guardian**

Wa-kafā bi-rabbika wakīlan — and sufficient is your Lord as Guardian, Keeper, Trustee. Wa-kafā from k-f-y, to suffice, be enough, as kafā sufficient, as verse 14 kafā bi-nafsika sufficient yourself as reckoner, and verse 17 kafā bi-rabbika sufficient your Lord as All-Aware All-Seeing, and verse 65 sufficient Lord as Guardian. Bi-rabbika, with your Lord, by your Lord, your Lord, from r-b-b. Wakīlan from w-k-l, Guardian, Keeper, Trustee, Disposer, as wakīl, keeper, guardian, trustee, as verse 2 Trustee of Affairs, and verse 54 not sent you as keeper, wakīlan, over them, and verse 65 sufficient Lord as Guardian, wakīlan. Al-Ṭabarī explains that sufficient is your Lord as Guardian, Keeper, Trustee, Protector, for faithful servants, meaning Lord is enough as Guardian, Keeper, to protect faithful servants from Satan, Iblis, who has no authority over them, as Lord Guardian sufficient, protects, as He encompasses people, knows best, etc. Al-Rāzī says kafā bi-rabbika wakīlan indicates sufficiency of Lord as Guardian, Keeper, for faithful servants, no need for other guardian, as Lord sufficient.

**Why No Authority Over Faithful And Sufficient Guardian**

Why no authority over faithful servants and sufficient Lord as Guardian? Because Iblis vowed to take hold of descendants except few, if delayed until Day, Allah said be gone, followers Hell ample reward, incite whoever you can with voice, cavalry, infantry, manipulate wealth and children, make promises, but promises nothing but delusion, and now you will have no authority over My faithful servants, sufficient Lord as Guardian, showing that despite vow and methods, voice, cavalry, infantry, wealth, children, promises delusion, Satan has no authority, power, control over faithful servants, sincere, who say best, follow most upright guidance, wisdom, etc., as Lord Guardian sufficient, protects, as He encompasses people, knows best, etc., and faithful servants are few, qalīlan, except few, but protected, as Lord Guardian sufficient.

**Connection To Vow And Be Gone And Incite And Delusion**

Verse 62 vow to take hold of descendants except few, verse 63 be gone, followers Hell ample reward, verse 64 incite whoever you can with voice, cavalry, infantry, manipulate wealth and children, make promises, but promises nothing but delusion, verse 65 you will have no authority over My faithful servants, sufficient Lord as Guardian, showing sequence: vow, dismissal, methods delusion, no authority over faithful, sufficient Guardian, as faithful protected, as few, but sufficient Guardian.

**Link To Wasteful Brothers To Devils And Satan Sworn Enemy And Seeks Discord**

Verse 27 wasteful brothers to devils, Devil ever ungrateful, verse 53 Satan seeks to sow discord among them, Satan sworn enemy to humankind, clear enemy, verse 65 no authority over faithful servants, sufficient Lord as Guardian, showing that wasteful brothers to devils follow Satan, but faithful servants who say best, most excellent, have no authority for Satan over them, Lord Guardian sufficient, as faithful protected, say best to prevent discord, as Satan sworn enemy, clear enemy, seeks discord, but no authority over faithful.

**Linguistic Note On Ibadī And Sultan And Kafa And Wakilan**

ʿIbād from ʿ-b-d, servants, faithful servants, from ʿabada, to worship, serve. Laysa, not, is not. Laka, for you, to you. ʿAlayhim, over them, upon them. Sulṭān from s-l-ṭ, authority, power, control, from sulṭān. Kafā from k-f-y, to suffice, be enough, from kafā. Rabb from r-b-b, Lord. Wakīl from w-k-l, Guardian, Keeper, Trustee, Disposer, from wakala, to entrust. The verse's structure — indeed My servants not for you over them authority, and sufficient is your Lord as Guardian — with statement no authority over faithful servants and sufficiency of Lord as Guardian, shows protection of faithful servants from Satan, no authority, Lord sufficient as Guardian, Keeper, Protector.

**How This Verse Teaches Protection Of Faithful Servants**

This verse teaches protection of faithful servants: you, Satan, Iblis, will have no authority, power, control over My faithful servants, sincere servants, who say best, follow most upright guidance, wisdom, etc., and sufficient is your Lord as Guardian, Keeper, Trustee, Protector, for faithful servants, meaning Lord enough as Guardian to protect from Satan, who has no authority over them, as Lord encompasses people, knows best, All-Aware All-Seeing, Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, but torment fearsome, and faithful servants protected, as few, qalīlan, except few, but sufficient Guardian, as Lord sufficient. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should be faithful servant, sincere, say best, follow most upright guidance, wisdom, that Qur'an guides to, so that Satan has no authority over him, Lord Guardian sufficient, protects, as faithful protected, as few, but sufficient, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, avoiding Hell ample reward for followers of Satan.

**A Reflection On No Authority And Sufficient Guardian**

Laysa laka ʿalayhim sulṭānun — not for you over them authority — indicates Satan, Iblis, has no authority, power, control, proof over faithful servants, sincere, faithful, who are My servants, as they are protected, as they say best, follow most upright, wisdom, etc., as they are few, qalīlan, except few, but protected. Wa-kafā bi-rabbika wakīlan — sufficient is your Lord as Guardian — indicates sufficiency of Lord as Guardian, Keeper, Trustee, Protector, for faithful servants, no need for other guardian, as Lord sufficient, encompasses people, knows best, All-Aware All-Seeing, Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, but torment fearsome, and faithful servants protected, as Lord Guardian sufficient, as He steers ships through sea so you may seek bounty, ever Merciful, as next verse says, etc., leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, while followers of Satan Hell ample reward, ample, as recompense, as justice, as Lord knows best, may have mercy if wills or punish if wills, as will, wisdom.
