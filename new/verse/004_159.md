## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:159

> Every one of the People of the Book will definitely believe in him before his death. And on the Day of Judgment Jesus will be a witness against them.

### Expanded Commentary

**The future recognition of Jesus**

Every person from the People of the Book will believe in Jesus before his death, and Jesus will witness against them on the Day of Judgment. Classical exegetes differed over whether “his death” refers to the death of an individual from the People of the Book or to Jesus’s death after his return. The dominant traditional reading connects it with Jesus’s future return, while the grammar and scope of the verse have been discussed in detail.

The essential point is that Jesus’s true status will not remain hidden. He will be recognized as the messenger and servant of Allah, and his testimony will expose how people responded to his message. No community can permanently redefine him according to its own desires.

The verse should produce reverence for Jesus and caution against certainty about details not settled by explicit revelation. It also reminds believers that prophets are witnesses to conduct, not ornaments for identity politics.

**Jesus as witness**

A prophet’s testimony is not a token for communal pride. It is evidence against those who changed his message or used his name for power. Believers should ask whether their speech about Jesus, Muhammad, and the other messengers resembles the truth they claim to defend.
