## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:73

> **They definitely ˹thought they˺ were about to lure you away from what We have revealed to you ˹O Prophet˺, hoping that you would attribute something else to Us falsely—and then they would have certainly taken you as a close friend.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**They Were About To Lure You Away From What We Revealed**

Wa-in kādū la-yaftinūnaka ʿani alladhī awḥaynā ilayka — and indeed they were about to lure, tempt you away from what We revealed to you. Wa-in, and indeed, indeed, surely, emphasis, they were about to. Kādū from k-w-d, to be about to, almost, nearly, from kāda, to be about to, almost. La-yaftinūnaka from f-t-n, to lure, tempt, test, mislead, from fatana, to lure, tempt, test, mislead, as fitnah test, trial, temptation, as yaftinūnaka lure you, tempt you away. ʿAni alladhī, from what, away from what. Awḥaynā from w-ḥ-y, We revealed, inspired, as awḥā revealed, as awḥaynā We revealed to you, O Prophet, as revelation, Qur'an, wisdom, most upright, as this is part of wisdom revealed to you, as verse 39 says. Ilayka, to you, O Prophet. Al-Ṭabarī explains that they, disbelievers, polytheists, definitely thought they were about to lure you away, tempt you away from what We revealed to you, Qur'an, wisdom, most upright, hoping you would attribute something else to Us falsely, compromise, etc., as they tried to lure Prophet away from revelation, to compromise, as they wanted him to attribute something else to Allah falsely, to accept their gods, etc. Al-Qurṭubī says kādū la-yaftinūnaka means they were about to lure you, tempt you away from what We revealed to you, as they tried to make Prophet compromise, attribute something else to Allah falsely.

**Hoping You Would Attribute Something Else To Us Falsely And Then Taken As Close Friend**

Li-taftariya ʿalaynā ghayrahu wa-idhan la-ittakhadhūka khalīlan — so you would invent, attribute something else to Us falsely, other than it, and then they would have taken you as close friend. Li-taftariya from f-r-y, to invent, fabricate, attribute falsely, lie, from iftarā, to invent, fabricate, attribute falsely, as taftariya you would invent, attribute falsely, as attribute something else to Us falsely, other than what We revealed. ʿAlaynā, upon Us, against Us, to Us, to Allah, Us. Ghayrahu, other than it, something else, other than what We revealed, from gh-y-r, other, else. Wa-idhan, and then, in that case, then. La-ittakhadhūka from a-kh-dh, to take, adopt, take as, from ittakhadha, to take, adopt, as ittakhadhūka they would have taken you. Khalīlan from kh-l-l, close friend, intimate friend, from khalīl, close friend, intimate friend, as friend, close friend. Al-Ṭabarī explains that hoping that you would attribute something else to Us falsely, other than what We revealed, invent, fabricate, lie against Us, and then they would have certainly taken you as close friend, intimate friend, as they would have taken you as friend if you compromised, attributed something else falsely, accepted their gods, etc., as they wanted close friendship via compromise.

**Why Lure Away From Revelation And Attribute Falsely And Close Friend**

Why lure away from what We revealed, attribute something else falsely, taken as close friend? Because disbelievers, polytheists, wanted Prophet to compromise, to attribute something else to Allah falsely, to accept their gods, idols, etc., to invent, fabricate, lie against Allah, other than revelation, Qur'an, wisdom, most upright, and if he did, they would take him as close friend, intimate friend, as they wanted friendship via compromise, as they thought they were about to lure him away, tempt him away from revelation, as they tried, as they were about to, almost, as kādū, almost, about to, but Allah made him steadfast, as next verse says had We not made you steadfast you would have inclined to them a little, and then double punishment, etc.

**Connection To Made You Steadfast Would Have Inclined Little And Double Punishment And No Helper**

Verse 73 they were about to lure you away from what We revealed hoping you would attribute something else falsely and then taken as close friend. Verse 74 had We not made you steadfast you would have inclined to them a little. Verse 75 then We would have made you taste double punishment in this life and after death and you would have found no helper against Us, showing sequence: they were about to lure away, tempt, hoping attribute falsely, taken as close friend, but Allah made Prophet steadfast, so he did not incline, otherwise double punishment, no helper, as warning, as Prophet protected, steadfast, as Lord encompasses people, knows best, etc., and as Our way with messengers sent before you, no change in Our way, as verse 77 says.

**Link To Our Way With Messengers And No Change And Intimidate To Drive Out Of Land**

Verse 76 they were about to intimidate you to drive you out of land of Mecca, but then they would not have survived after you left except little while. Verse 77 this has been Our way with messengers We sent before you, you will never find any change in Our way, showing that luring away, intimidating to drive out, are ways of disbelievers against messengers, but Our way with messengers is steadfast, no change, as messengers made steadfast, not lured, not intimidated, etc., as divine law, as every wicked society destroyed or punished severely before Day written in Record, etc.

**Linguistic Note On Kadu And Yaftinunaka And Awḥayna And Taftariya And Khalilan**

Kādū from k-w-d, to be about to, almost, from kāda. Yaftinūnaka from f-t-n, to lure, tempt, test, mislead, from fatana. Alladhī awḥaynā, what We revealed, from w-ḥ-y, to reveal, inspire, from awḥā. Taftariya from f-r-y, to invent, fabricate, attribute falsely, from iftarā. Ghayr from gh-y-r, other, else. Ittakhadhūka from a-kh-dh, to take, adopt, from ittakhadha. Khalīl from kh-l-l, close friend, intimate friend, from khalīl. The verse's structure — they were about to lure you away from what We revealed to you so you would attribute something else to Us falsely and then they would have taken you as close friend — with about to lure away from revelation and hoping attribute falsely and then taken as close friend, shows attempt to make Prophet compromise, attribute falsely, to gain friendship, but Allah made steadfast, not inclined, otherwise double punishment, no helper.

**How This Verse Teaches Attempt To Lure Prophet And Compromise**

This verse teaches attempt to lure Prophet away from revelation and compromise: they, disbelievers, definitely thought they were about to lure you away, tempt you away from what We revealed to you, O Prophet, Qur'an, wisdom, most upright, hoping that you would attribute something else to Us falsely, invent, fabricate, other than revelation, and then they would have certainly taken you as close friend, intimate friend, as they wanted friendship via compromise, accepting their gods, etc., as they tried to lure, tempt, as they were about to, almost, as kādū, almost, about to, but Allah made steadfast, as next verse says, had We not made you steadfast you would have inclined a little, double punishment, no helper, as warning, as Prophet protected, steadfast. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should not be lured away from what Allah revealed, not attribute something else falsely to Allah, not compromise for close friendship with disbelievers, but be steadfast, as Prophet made steadfast, follow most upright guidance, wisdom, that Qur'an guides to, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, avoiding double punishment, no helper.

**A Reflection On Close Friend Via Compromise**

Khalīlan — close friend, intimate friend — they would have taken you as close friend if you attributed something else falsely to Us, compromised, accepted their gods, etc., showing that close friendship with disbelievers via compromise, attributing falsely to Allah, is not allowed, as it would lead to double punishment, no helper, as next verse says, and as Our way with messengers no change, as messengers made steadfast, not compromised, not taken as close friend via false attribution, but steadfast, as Lord encompasses people, knows best, etc., and as Prophet not keeper over them, etc., but warner, deliverer, steadfast, not lured, not inclined, otherwise double punishment. The believer who reflects will not be lured away from revelation, not attribute something else falsely to Allah for close friendship with disbelievers, but be steadfast, follow most upright guidance, wisdom, that Qur'an guides to, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, avoiding double punishment, no helper, Hell, as torment fearsome, but Lord Most Forbearing All-Forgiving to those who constantly turn, etc.
