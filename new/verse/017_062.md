## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:62

> **Adding, “Do you see this one you honoured above me? If you delay my end until the Day of Judgment, I will certainly take hold of his descendants, except for a few.”**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Do You See This One You Honoured Above Me**

Qāla a-ra'ayta hādhā alladhī karramta ʿalayya — he said, do you see this one you honoured above me, you favoured above me? Qāla from q-w-l, he said, Iblis adding, continuing protest. A-ra'ayta from r-ʾ-y, to see, do you see, have you seen, as ra'ayta you see. Hādhā, this one, this, Adam, this one, pointing. Alladhī karramta, whom You honoured, favoured, from k-r-m, to honour, favour, make noble, from karuma, to be noble, from karramta You honoured, as karramnā We dignified children of Adam, dignified, honoured. ʿAlayya, above me, over me, above me, over me, meaning honoured above me, favoured above me, as Iblis thought himself better, honoured above Adam? Actually Allah honoured Adam above Iblis, so Iblis says do you see this one you honoured above me, questioning, protesting. Al-Ṭabarī explains that Iblis said, do you see this one, Adam, you honoured above me, favoured above me, made noble above me, while I am better, created from fire, etc., protesting, arrogant, as he refused to prostrate to one created from mud, thinking fire better, arrogant.

**If You Delay My End Until Day Of Judgment I Will Take Hold Of Descendants Except Few**

La-in akhkhartanī ilā yawmi al-qiyāmati la-aḥtanikanna dhurriyyatahu illā qalīlan — if You delay me until Day of Resurrection, I will certainly take hold of his descendants, except few. La-in from l- and in, if, indeed if, conditional with emphasis, if You delay. Akhkhartanī from a-kh-r, to delay, postpone, give respite, from akhkharat, to delay, postpone, as delay my end, give me respite until Day. Ilā yawmi al-qiyāmati, until Day of Resurrection, Day of Standing, from y-w-m and q-w-m. La-aḥtanikanna from ḥ-n-k, to take hold of, to control, to put bridle, to dominate, to take hold, from iḥtanaka, to take hold, control, dominate, put bridle, as aḥtanaka to take hold, control, dominate, as bridle in mouth, to control, as Iblis vows to take hold of descendants, control, dominate, mislead, as Satan seeks to sow discord, incite, etc. Dhurriyyatahu from dh-r-r, descendants, offspring, progeny, from dhurriyyah, descendants, offspring, progeny, as children of Adam, descendants. Illā qalīlan, except few, small number, from q-l-l, few, little, as qalīlan few, except few, meaning except few faithful, as faithful servants no authority for Satan over them, sufficient Lord as Guardian, as verse 65 says, except few faithful, awwābīn, etc. Al-Ṭabarī explains that Iblis said, if You delay me, give me respite until Day of Judgment, I will certainly take hold of his descendants, control, dominate, mislead, except for few, small number, meaning except few faithful, sincere, who will not follow, as Satan has no authority over faithful servants, sufficient Lord as Guardian.

**Why Iblis Vows To Take Hold Of Descendants**

Why Iblis vows to take hold of descendants except few? Because arrogant, ever ungrateful, sworn enemy, refused to prostrate to Adam created from mud, protested, thought himself better, honoured above? Actually Adam honoured above him, so he envied, vowed to take revenge by misleading descendants, controlling, dominating, except few faithful, as Satan seeks to sow discord, incite with voice, cavalry, infantry, manipulate wealth and children, promise delusion, as verses 64-65 will say, but no authority over faithful servants, sufficient Lord as Guardian, as faithful protected.

**Connection To No Authority Over Faithful Servants And Sufficient Guardian And Incite With Voice Cavalry Infantry**

Verse 62 Iblis vows if delay until Day Judgment will take hold of descendants except few. Verse 63 Allah responded be gone, whoever follows you Hell ample reward. Verse 64 incite whoever you can with voice, mobilize cavalry and infantry, manipulate wealth and children, make promises, but Satan promises nothing but delusion. Verse 65 you will have no authority over My faithful servants, sufficient Lord as Guardian, showing that despite vow to take hold of descendants except few, Satan has no authority over faithful servants, Lord Guardian sufficient, as faithful servants protected, say best, etc., and incitement with voice, cavalry, infantry, wealth, children, promises delusion, but no authority over faithful.

**Link To Children Of Adam Dignified And Carried On Land And Sea**

Verse 70 We dignified children of Adam, carried them on land and sea, granted good provisions, privileged far above many creatures, showing honour for Adam, descendants, despite Iblis vow to take hold, control, dominate, except few, but children of Adam dignified, honoured, privileged, and faithful among them no authority for Satan, Lord Guardian sufficient, as faithful protected.

**Linguistic Note On Karramta And Akhkhartani And Ahtanikanna And Dhurriyyah And Qalilan**

Qāla from q-w-l, to say. Ra'ayta from r-ʾ-y, to see. Hādhā, this. Alladhī karramta, whom You honoured, from k-r-m, to honour, make noble, from karuma. ʿAlayya, above me, over me. Akhkhartanī from a-kh-r, to delay, postpone, from akhkharat. Yawm from y-w-m, Day. Qiyāmah from q-w-m, Resurrection. Aḥtanikanna from ḥ-n-k, to take hold, control, dominate, put bridle, from iḥtanaka, intensive with nun emphasis, certainly take hold. Dhurriyyah from dh-r-r, descendants, offspring, from dharra, to scatter, progeny. Qalīl from q-l-l, few, little. The verse's structure — do you see this one you honoured above me, if You delay me until Day of Resurrection I will certainly take hold of his descendants except few — with questioning do you see this one honoured above me and vow if delay until Day will take hold of descendants except few, shows arrogance, envy, vow to mislead, control, dominate, except few faithful, as Satan's vow, as sworn enemy.

**How This Verse Teaches Satan's Vow And Envy**

This verse teaches Satan's, Iblis's vow and envy: do you see this one you honoured above me, Adam honoured above me, while I better, created from fire, etc., if You delay me, give respite until Day of Judgment, I will certainly take hold of his descendants, control, dominate, mislead, put bridle, except for few, small number, faithful, as Satan seeks to sow discord, incite, etc., but no authority over faithful servants, sufficient Lord as Guardian, as faithful protected. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should know Iblis vowed to take hold of descendants except few, out of arrogance, envy, as sworn enemy, clear enemy, seeks to mislead, control, dominate, but faithful servants no authority for Satan over them, Lord Guardian sufficient, so be faithful servant, say best, follow most upright guidance, wisdom, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, avoiding being among those taken hold of by Satan, except few faithful protected.

**A Reflection On Taking Hold And Few Faithful**

La-aḥtanikanna dhurriyyatahu illā qalīlan — I will certainly take hold of his descendants except few — indicates Satan's plan to control, dominate, mislead descendants, put bridle, as bridle controls horse, as Satan controls via voice, cavalry, infantry, wealth, children, promises delusion, but except few, qalīlan, few, faithful, sincere, awwābīn, constantly turning, righteous, etc., who will not follow, as faithful servants no authority for Satan, Lord Guardian sufficient. The believer who reflects will be among few, qalīlan, faithful, not among many taken hold of, will be faithful servant, say best, follow most upright, wisdom, that Qur'an guides to, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, avoiding Hell ample reward for followers of Satan, as verse 63 says whoever follows you Hell ample reward.
