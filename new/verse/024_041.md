## Sūrah an-Nūr 24:41

> Do you not see that Allah is glorified by all those in the heavens and the earth, even the birds as they soar? Each ˹instinctively˺ knows their manner of prayer and glorification. And Allah has ˹perfect˺ knowledge of all they do.

**Expanded Commentary**

**Cosmic Liturgy: yusabbiḥu lahū man fī al-samāwāt**

The divine speech opens with an awakening rhetorical question: *a-lam tara anna Allāha yusabbiḥu lahū man fī al-samāwāti wa-al-arḍ*, do you not see that Allah is glorified by all those in the heavens and the earth? The visual verb *tara* (do you not see) invites both sensory observation of the natural cosmos and deep intellectual reflection upon its underlying order and harmony.

The entire universe is presented not as a cold, inert mechanism of mute matter, but as an ecstatic, living sanctuary vibrating with sacred praise. Angels in celestial spheres, stars in galactic orbits, oceans, mountains, trees, and microscopic organisms all participate in continuous glorification (*tasbīḥ*). This universal chorus affirms the absolute transcendence, perfection, and purity of the Creator, declaring Him free from every partner, deficiency, or mortal weakness. Every created atom exists in an ontological posture of praise before its Maker, glorifying the Sovereign of all realms whose wisdom sustains every corner of existence and whose beauty fills the horizons.

**Aerial Hymns: wa-al-ṭayru ṣāffāt**

The verse highlights a particularly breathtaking manifestation of terrestrial worship: *wa-al-ṭayru ṣāffāt*, even the birds as they soar with outspread wings. The word *ṣāffāt* depicts birds gliding high in the sky, extending their wings in effortless aerial suspension without frantic flapping, sustained entirely by divine aerodynamic decrees that govern the atmosphere.

Classical commentators, including al-Qurṭubī, al-Ṭabarī, and al-Rāzī, noted that the outstretched wings of soaring birds mirror the outspread arms and bowed postures of human worshippers in prayer. As birds glide through the atmosphere, their rhythmic ascent and aerial maneuvers constitute their own distinctive liturgy of adoration. Their flight is an open sign of complete dependence upon the Sustainer (*al-Qayyūm*), who holds them aloft in the thin air, transforming the skies into a soaring temple of praise where every flight path testifies to divine balance and providence, inspiring awe in human onlookers.

**Distinction Between Prayer and Glorification: kullun qad ʿalima ṣalātahu wa-tasbīḥah**

The text establishes a profound semantic and operational distinction: *kullun qad ʿalima ṣalātahu wa-tasbīḥah*, each instinctively knows its manner of prayer and glorification. While *tasbīḥ* (glorification) is the declaration of divine transcendence and freedom from flaw, *ṣalāh* (prayer) denotes supplication, dynamic posture, and active communion with the Maker.

Early exegete al-Ṭabarī noted that while some scholars understood *ṣalāh* to refer specifically to human and angelic liturgy and *tasbīḥ* to non-human creation, the syntax affirms that each distinct species possesses both its own specific mode of prayer and its own method of praise. The soaring bird has its *ṣalāh* in the beating and gliding of its wings, and its *tasbīḥ* in its dawn melodies and instinctive calls. Creation does not offer a chaotic cacophony; it performs an orderly, differentiated cosmic symphony wherein every species faithfully executes its ordained liturgical assignment, weaving a grand harmony of adoration.

**Instinctive Knowledge and Primordial Endowments**

The phrase *kullun qad ʿalima* indicates that this worship does not arise from external compulsion or mechanical programming; it is rooted in an innate divine knowledge granted to every creature. God has inspired every animal, bird, and celestial body with the knowledge of how to orient its existence toward its Creator.

This intuitive orientation (*al-ilhām al-fiṭrī*) bypasses human linguistic formulations. A bird gliding effortlessly across the thermal currents does not compose theological treatises, yet its very flight pattern, biological design, and joyful calls express total, harmonious alignment with divine will. Every creature recognizes its Maker and responds with appropriate reverence according to the cognitive and physical faculties bestowed upon it, displaying a pristine integrity that puts human heedlessness to shame, functioning as a silent sermon directed toward mortal hearts, calling them back to natural submission.

**The Tragic Dissonance of Human Heedlessness**

Contemplating this universal chorus of praise exposes the tragic anomaly of human and jinn disbelief. In a cosmos where galaxies bow, mountains glorify, and soaring birds sing anthems to their Creator, the disbelieving human being stands out as a jarring, discordant note.

Endowed with intellect, free will, and moral agency, humanity was created to be the conscious crown of terrestrial worship. Yet, many human beings descend into arrogant denial, trivial distraction, or insolent rebellion, refusing to bow the head that God elevated. While birds greet the dawn with hymns of gratitude, heedless mortals slumber past prayer or awaken to pursue petty worldly greed. The verse gently rebukes this spiritual deafness, inviting human beings to abandon their unnatural estrangement and harmonize their hearts with the vast cosmic choir that ceaselessly honors God, reclaiming their true station as humble guardians of creation who live in conscious gratitude.

**Divine Surveillance of Cosmic Devotion: Allāhu ʿalīmun bimā yafʿalūn**

The verse concludes with a comprehensive declaration of divine omniscience: *wa-Allāhu ʿalīmun bimā yafʿalūn*, and Allah has perfect knowledge of all they do. The verb *yafʿalūn* (they do) encompasses both the voluntary and instinctive actions of all creatures.

God does not merely hear the audible prayers of humans; He knows the silent flutter of the sparrow's wing, the migratory flight path of the swallow, and the secret stirrings of devotion within every created heart. Nothing in this expansive cosmic sanctuary escapes His attention or goes unrecorded. For the righteous, this divine knowledge provides profound comfort, assuring them that their quiet prayers in elevated houses are seen and cherished by the same Lord who orchestrates the praise of heavens, earth, and sky, giving them patience to persevere in virtue until the day they meet Him, welcomed into everlasting peace.
