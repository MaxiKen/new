## Sūrah al-Naḥl 16:56

> **And they ˹even˺ assign to those ˹idols˺—who know nothing—a share of what We have provided for them. By Allah! You will certainly be questioned about whatever ˹lies˺ you used to fabricate ˹against Allah˺. Allah’s Daughters?**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Assigning Share Of Provision To Idols**

Wa-yajʿalūna li-mā lā yaʿlamūna naṣīban mimmā razaqnāhum — and they assign to what they do not know, to those who know nothing, a share of what We provided them. Yajʿalūna from j-ʿ-l means to make, assign, set. Li-mā lā yaʿlamūna, to what does not know, to those who know nothing, meaning idols that have no knowledge, no perception, as verse 21 said dead not knowing when they will be resurrected. Naṣīban, share, portion, from n-ṣ-b, share. Mimmā razaqnāhum, from what We provided them, from r-z-q, provision. Al-Ṭabarī explains that polytheists assigned share of their provision, like crops and cattle, to idols, saying this is for Allah and this is for our idols, as 6:136 says *They assign to Allah from what He created of crops and cattle a share and say this is for Allah by their claim and this is for our idols* (6:136). They would give part of harvest and cattle to idols, as offering, while provision was from Allah alone. This is ingratitude: provision from Allah, share given to idols who know nothing.

**Who Know Nothing**

Li-mā lā yaʿlamūna, to those who know nothing, emphasizes idols' ignorance. Idols do not know, do not create, are dead, as previous verses said. To assign share of provision to those who know nothing is irrational: you give provision to those who cannot know you gave, cannot benefit, cannot reward. Al-Rāzī notes that lā yaʿlamūna also indicates that idols do not know they are being worshipped, do not know provision assigned, do not know when worshippers will be raised. Assigning share to ignorant is like giving food to stone. The phrase also indicates that those who assign share do not know what they do, as next verses about daughters show they attribute to Allah what they hate.

**You Will Be Questioned About Lies Fabricated**

Ta-Allāhi la-tus'alunna ʿammā kuntum taftarūn — by Allah, you will certainly be questioned about what you used to fabricate. Ta-Allāhi, by Allah, oath. La-tus'alunna, you will certainly be questioned, from s-ʾ-l, to ask, question, with la- and nun for emphasis, surely. ʿAmmā kuntum taftarūn, about what you used to fabricate, invent lies, from f-r-y, to fabricate, invent. Al-Ṭabarī says you will be questioned on Day about lies you fabricated against Allah: that idols deserve share of provision, that Allah has daughters, that certain cattle are prohibited, etc. The oath ta-Allāhi emphasizes certainty of questioning. Al-Qurṭubī notes that questioning here is for reproach and punishment, not for information, because Allah knows. The lies include assigning share to idols, attributing daughters to Allah, and prohibiting what Allah made lawful.

**Why Assigning Share Is Ingratitude**

Assigning share of provision to idols is ingratitude because provision is from Allah alone, as verse 53 said whatever blessings you have are from Allah. To give share to idols is to return favour with ingratitude, as verse 55 said. It is also shirk in gratitude: gratitude should be to Provider alone, but they give share to non-provider. Al-Zamakhsharī explains that naṣīban mimmā razaqnāhum, share from what We provided, indicates that provision is from Allah, yet share given to idols, showing misplacement of gratitude. The verse also shows economic dimension of shirk: idols had custodians who benefited from shares assigned, so shirk had financial incentive.

**Connection To Next Verses About Daughters**

The translation adds Allah's Daughters? as heading for next verses about attributing daughters to Allah. The present verse about assigning share to idols who know nothing prepares for next about attributing daughters to Allah while hating daughters for selves. Both are forms of fabrication against Allah: assigning share to idols and attributing daughters to Allah. Both show irrationality: you give best provision to idols who know nothing, and you attribute to Allah what you hate for yourselves, daughters, while desiring sons for yourselves. The sūrah exposes inconsistency and ingratitude in both.

**Linguistic Note On Naṣīb And Iftirā'**

Yajʿalūna from j-ʿ-l, to make, assign. Li-mā lā yaʿlamūna, to what does not know, from ʿ-l-m, to know. Naṣīban from n-ṣ-b, share, portion, from same root as nuṣub, idols, and naṣab, toil. Mimmā razaqnāhum, from what We provided them, from r-z-q. Ta-Allāhi, by Allah, oath with ta- for oath. La-tus'alunna, you will be questioned, from s-ʾ-l, with emphatic la- and nun. ʿAmmā kuntum taftarūn, about what you fabricated, from f-r-y, to fabricate, invent lie. The verse's structure — they assign to those who know nothing a share of what We provided, by Allah you will be questioned about what you fabricated — moves from deed to oath of questioning, warning that assigning share to idols is fabrication that will be questioned.

**A Lesson On Provision And Gratitude**

Provision, rizq, is from Allah, as verse 53 said. To assign share to idols is to deny that. True gratitude is to attribute provision to Allah alone and to use provision in obedience to Him, not to give share to idols. Al-Rāzī says rizq includes both material provision and guidance, and assigning share to idols includes both giving material share and giving devotion share. Both are ingratitude. The verse therefore calls to attribute provision to Allah alone and to be grateful, not to fabricate lies about provision.

**How This Verse Fits Sūrah's Argument**

Sūrah al-Naḥl argues from blessings to Creator to oneness. This verse shows opposite: blessings from Allah, yet share given to idols who know nothing, which is ingratitude and shirk. It is example of how blessings are misused. The sūrah lists blessings as signs for those who reflect, understand, are mindful, but some use blessings to assign share to idols, showing they did not reflect. The oath you will be questioned about lies fabricated warns that misuse of blessings has consequence and will be questioned. The reader is invited to be among those who attribute blessings to Allah alone and are grateful, not among those who assign share to idols and fabricate lies.
