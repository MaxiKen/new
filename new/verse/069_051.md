## Sūrah al-Ḥāqqah 69:51

**Expanded Commentary**

**Ḥaqq al-Yaqīn: Truth of Certainty**

Wa-innahu la-ḥaqqu al-yaqīn certifies. Ḥaqq al-yaqīn: truth's certainty. Highest grade: experienced truth. Certainty tasted: reality lived. Not reported: witnessed. Not described: entered. Such certifying climaxes. Sūrah opened: Ḥāqqah announced. Truth promised: reality coming. Sūrah closes: certainty certified. Promise fulfilled textually. Reality guaranteed actually. Readers receive assurance. Doubts dissolved. Convictions cemented. Faith anchored. Hearts settled. Minds rested. Souls assured. Worship grounded. Sacrifice rationalized. Death faced. Eternity awaited. What certifying climaxes, faith rests. Certified certainty; rested hearts. Rested hearts worship. Worshiped Lord pleased. Pleased Lord rewards. Rewarded servants rejoice. Rejoicing spreads invitation. Invited multitudes come. Coming seekers guided. Guided communities form. Formed congregations worship. Worshiped together: unity. United fronts strong. Strong holds firm. Firm stands witness. Witnessing truth prevails. Prevailing light spreads. Spread guidance covers. Covered earth testifies. Testifying creation glorifies. Glorified Creator praised. Praised universally: worship. Worshiped eternally: bliss. Bliss shared: saints. Saints praising: forever.
