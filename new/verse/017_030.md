## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:30

> **Surely your Lord gives abundant or limited provisions to whoever He wills. He is certainly All-Aware, AllSeeing of His servants.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Lord Gives Abundant Or Limited Provisions**

Inna rabbaka yabsuṭu al-rizqa li-man yashā'u wa-yaqdiru — indeed your Lord extends provision for whom He wills and restricts, measures. Inna, indeed, surely. Rabbaka, your Lord, from r-b-b. Yabsuṭu from b-s-ṭ, to extend, expand, give abundant, from basaṭa, to extend, as verse 29 tabsuṭhā, extend hand. Al-rizqa from r-z-q, provision, sustenance, from razaqa, to provide. Li-man yashā'u, for whom He wills, to whoever He wills, from sh-y-ʾ, to will. Wa-yaqdiru from q-d-r, to restrict, measure, give limited, from qadara, to measure, decree, restrict, from same root as qadar, measure, decree, and qadir, able. Al-Ṭabarī explains that Lord extends provision, gives abundant, plentiful, for whom He wills, and restricts, gives limited, measured, for whom He wills, according to wisdom, knowledge, not random. Al-Qurṭubī says yabsuṭu al-rizqa means gives abundant provision, wa-yaqdiru means restricts, gives limited, measured, as test, as previous chapter said if Allah were to extend provision for servants they would transgress but He sends down in due measure what He wills.

**All-Aware All-Seeing Of Servants**

Innahu kāna bi-ʿibādihi khabīran baṣīran — indeed He is with His servants All-Aware, All-Seeing. Innahu, indeed He. Kāna, is, was. Bi-ʿibādihi, with His servants, about His servants, from ʿ-b-d, servants. Khabīran from kh-b-r, All-Aware, Aware, informed, from same root as khabar, news, and khabīr, Aware, as verse 17 sufficient Lord as All-Aware All-Seeing of sins. Baṣīran from b-ṣ-r, All-Seeing, Seeing, from baṣar, sight, as verse 12 mubṣiratan bright and verse 17 Baṣīran. Al-Ṭabarī says He is All-Aware, All-Seeing of His servants, knows their states, needs, what is best for them, whether abundant or limited provision is better, so He gives accordingly with knowledge and sight. Al-Rāzī says khabīran baṣīran indicates comprehensive knowledge of servants, inner and outer, so provision abundant or limited based on complete knowledge, wisdom, not arbitrary, as test.

**Why This Verse Follows Moderation Command**

Verses 26-29 commanded giving due to relatives, poor, traveller, not spending wastefully, wasteful brothers to devils, Devil ever ungrateful, if must turn down due to lack while hoping for bounty give kind word, do not be tight-fisted nor openhanded. Verse 30 says Lord gives abundant or limited provisions to whoever He wills, All-Aware All-Seeing of servants. The connection is that moderation command is based on reality that provision abundant or limited by will, with knowledge, so whether you have abundant or limited, be moderate, give due, avoid waste, avoid tight-fisted, avoid openhanded, give kind word when lack, hope for bounty. The verse explains why some have abundant and some limited: by will, with awareness and sight, as test, not as sign of pleasure or displeasure alone.

**Link To Bounty Never Withheld And Favoured Some Over Others**

Verse 20 said bounty never withheld, We provide both former and latter from bounty. Verse 21 said see how We favoured some over others in this life but Hereafter far greater in rank and favour. Verse 30 says Lord extends provision for whom He wills and restricts, All-Aware All-Seeing. The link is that bounty never withheld in general, but distribution abundant or limited by will, favouring some over others in worldly provision, but Hereafter greater. The provision abundant or limited is part of favouring some over others, and is based on knowledge and sight of servants, what is best for them, as test.

**Connection To Previous Chapter About Provision In Due Measure**

Previous chapter said if Allah were to extend provision for His servants they would transgress in earth but He sends down in due measure what He wills, He is All-Aware All-Seeing of His servants. This verse says similar: Lord extends provision for whom He wills and restricts, He is All-Aware All-Seeing of servants. The theme is consistent: provision measured by will, with awareness and sight, to prevent transgression, as test, not random. The sūrah's moral code about spending is based on this: provision measured, so be moderate.

**Linguistic Note On Yabsutu And Yaqdiru And Khabir Basir**

Inna from a-n-n, indeed. Rabb from r-b-b, Lord. Yabsuṭu from b-s-ṭ, to extend, expand, from basaṭa. Rizq from r-z-q, provision. Man from m-n, whom. Yashā'u from sh-y-ʾ, He wills. Yaqdiru from q-d-r, to restrict, measure, from qadara. Innahu from a-n-n, indeed He. Kāna from k-w-n, to be. ʿIbād from ʿ-b-d, servants. Khabīr from kh-b-r, All-Aware. Baṣīr from b-ṣ-r, All-Seeing. The verse's structure — indeed your Lord extends provision for whom He wills and restricts, indeed He is with His servants All-Aware All-Seeing — with statement of extending and restricting by will and statement of awareness and sight, shows that provision distribution based on knowledge and sight, wisdom, not arbitrary.

**How This Verse Teaches Trust And Moderation**

This verse teaches trust and moderation: Lord gives abundant or limited provisions to whoever He wills, All-Aware All-Seeing of servants, so trust His distribution, be moderate in spending, give due, avoid waste, avoid tight-fisted, avoid openhanded, give kind word when lack, hope for bounty. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should trust that provision abundant or limited is by will with awareness and sight, what is best, so should not envy, should not be arrogant when abundant, should not despair when limited, but be moderate, grateful, patient. The practical lesson is to see provision as test, from All-Aware All-Seeing, and to use it moderately, most upright, to achieve greater ranks in Hereafter.

**A Reflection On All-Aware All-Seeing**

Khabīran baṣīran — All-Aware, All-Seeing — as in verse 17 sufficient Lord as All-Aware All-Seeing of sins, and here All-Aware All-Seeing of servants regarding provision. This indicates comprehensive knowledge: Aware of inner, Seeing outer, knows what is best for each servant, whether abundant or limited provision better to prevent transgression, to test gratitude and patience. The believer who knows Lord is All-Aware All-Seeing of servants will trust provision distribution, be grateful when abundant, patient when limited, moderate in spending, give due to relatives, poor, traveller, avoid wasteful brotherhood to devils, avoid tight-fisted blameworthy and openhanded depleted, give kind word when lack while hoping for bounty, as part of most upright guidance that leads to appreciated striving and mighty reward.
