## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:176

> They ask you ˹for a ruling, O Prophet˺. Say, “Allah gives you a ruling regarding those who die without children or parents.” If a man dies childless and leaves behind a sister, she will inherit one-half of his estate, whereas her brother will inherit all of her estate if she dies childless. If this person leaves behind two sisters, they together will inherit two-thirds of the estate. But if the deceased leaves male and female siblings, a male’s share will be equal to that of two females. Allah makes ˹this˺ clear to you so you do not go astray. And Allah has ˹perfect˺ knowledge of all things.

**Expanded Commentary**

**They Ask You for a Ruling Regarding One Without Children or Parents**

The verse opens *yastaftūnaka quli Allāhu yuftīkum fī al-kalālati* — they ask you, seek your ruling, your *fatwā* — say, God gives you ruling regarding the *kalālah* — the one who dies without children or parents, without direct ascendant or descendant. *Yastaftūnaka* — they ask you — was explained as second asking in the sūrah after *“they ask you regarding women, God instructs you, instruction already recited concerning orphan women deprived yet wished to marry, helpless children, orphans’ rights, whatever good you do is well known to Him”* (4:127) — now they ask regarding *kalālah*. *Quli Allāhu yuftīkum* — say, God instructs you, gives ruling — was read as that *“to Him belongs whatever is in heavens and earth, He is Fully Aware”* (4:126) — so He instructs, as evidence and light have come from your Lord (4:174) — instruction is light. *Fī al-kalālati* — regarding *kalālah* — was explained with *kalālah* — one who leaves neither parent nor child, the one whose line is cut at both ends, whose estate is therefore to siblings, as the sūrah earlier said *“you will inherit half of what your wives leave if childless, but if they have children then one-fourth… And if a man or woman leaves neither parents nor children but only a sibling then…”* (4:12) where *kalālah* rule began for maternal siblings — here it is extended to paternal/full siblings. Early reports noted this verse was among the last revealed, answering Jābir ibn ʿAbd Allāh’s illness where he was *kalālah* without parent or child, asking about his sisters’ shares — the sūrah’s closing is personal.

**If a Man Dies Childless Leaving a Sister — She Inherits One-Half, Brother Inherits All**

The details *in imruʾun halaka laysa lahū waladun wa lahū ukhtun fa lahā niṣfu mā taraka wa huwa yarithuhā in lam yakun lahā walad* — if a man dies, perishes, is destroyed — *halaka* — leaves no child, and has a sister, then for her is half what he leaves, and he — her brother — inherits her — all of her — if she dies childless — were explained with two mirrors. *Imruʾun halaka* — a man perishes — *laysa lahū waladun* — he has no child — was read as the *kalālah* male; *wa lahū ukhtun* — and has a sister — one sister alone; *fa lahā niṣfu mā taraka* — for her half what he leaves — after bequest and debt, as obligatory shares (4:7: little or much, obligatory) — half for one female, as earlier one female alone inherits one-half (4:11) where daughter alone half — here sister half. *Wa huwa yarithuhā in lam yakun lahā walad* — and he inherits her — her brother inherits all of her estate if she dies childless without parent — was explained as that brother as sole sibling inherits entirety where sister alone half — the imbalance was read as not unfair because sibling inheritance here is for full/paternal siblings where male’s share will be equal to two females where together (next), and where no child the brother assumes clan protection, as the single soul spread into humanity (4:1) — brother inherits all to sustain ties. The sūrah has shown that inheritance is *farīḍah* — obligation (4:11) — and that limits set by God admit to Gardens (4:13) and exceeding casts into Hell (4:14) — *kalālah* shares are limits.

**If Two Sisters — They Inherit Two-Thirds**

The case *fa in kānatā ithnatayni fa lahumā al-thuluthāni mimmā taraka* — but if the two — sisters — are two, then for them two-thirds of what he leaves — was explained with *ithnatayni* — two sisters together — as the female pair, as earlier *if you leave only two or more females, their share is two-thirds* (4:11) where daughters two-plus two-thirds — here sisters two two-thirds. *Mimmā taraka* — of what he leaves — after bequest and debt, as *“after fulfilment of bequests and debts without harm”* (4:12) — harm not to heirs. The sūrah has shown that shares for men and women whether little or much are obligatory (4:7), that orphan women deprived yet wished to marry are protected (4:127), that helpless children are stood for with justice (4:127): sisters as *kalālah* heirs are that justice applied at death without child or parent. Early notes added that two sisters as *kalālah* share two-thirds leaving one-third to others where present; the sūrah’s light — *We have sent down brilliant light* (4:174) — makes that clear so you do not go astray.

**But If Siblings Male and Female — Male Equal to Two Females**

The general *wa in kānū ikhwatan rijālan wa nisāʾan fa li al-dhakari mithlu ḥaẓẓi al-unthayayn* — but if they — the deceased’s siblings — are brothers and sisters — male and female — brothers and sisters together — then for the male the like of the share of two females, male’s share equal to that of two females — was explained with *ikhwatan rijālan wa nisāʾan* — brothers and sisters male and female — the mixed group. *Fa li al-dhakari mithlu ḥaẓẓi al-unthayayn* — for the male the like of two females — was read as the sūrah’s arithmetic where *share of male is twice that of female* where children are heirs (4:11) — here siblings share after *kalālah* as children share — principle one: male twice female where survival depends on male’s provision, as *men are maintainers of women as they have been provisioned over women and tasked with supporting them* (4:34) — so inheritance corresponds to expenditure. The sūrah has shown that both do good male or female with faith will enter Paradise not wronged a speck (4:124) — inheritance not Paradise — there male and female both enter; inheritance as world’s division reflects world’s expenditure division. The rule was linked to *“Gardens under rivers forever, promise ever true”* (4:122) — world’s shares are *khayran lakum* — better for you — if you accept, as Light shows.

**God Makes This Clear So You Do Not Go Astray — He Knows All**

The seal *yubayyinu Allāhu lakum an taḍillū wa Allāhu bi kulli shayʾin ʿalīm* — God makes clear to you, explains, lest you go astray, so you do not stray, and God has perfect knowledge of all things, of every thing — was explained with *yubayyinu lakum* — He makes clear to you — the same *yubayyin* as *“He wills to make clear to you, guide to noble ways, turn in mercy”* (4:26) and *burhān* and *nūr mubīn* — evidence and brilliant light — have come (4:174) — now *kalālah* clear. *An taḍillū* — lest you go astray, so you do not stray — was read as that inheritance without clarity would be *ẓulm* — wrongdoing — and *ṣadd* — hindering many from God’s way — the same wrong for which *ṭayyibāt* were forbidden (4:160); to make clear is to prevent straying. *Wa Allāhu bi kulli shayʾin ʿalīm* — and God has perfect knowledge of every thing, of all things — was explained with *bi kulli shayʾ* — of every thing — the same *muḥīṭ* — encompassing everything (4:126) — knowledge that counts every *walad* — child — and every *ukht* — sister — and every *akh* — brother — and every bequest and debt without harm, as He is Fully Aware of everything (4:126) and All-Aware of what you do (4:128, 4:135) — the One who owns heavens and earth (4:126, 4:131) knows *kalālah* shares. The sūrah that began with single soul spread into humanity (4:1) and commanded honouring ties, ends with ties’ shares at death where single soul’s line is cut at both ends — *kalālah* — yet ties remain as siblings, and God makes clear so you do not stray, as He is *ʿAlīm* of all.

**A Verse That Follows Evidence and Light With Inheritance Clear**

Placed after *O humanity, conclusive evidence has come from your Lord, We have sent down to you a brilliant light* (4:174), *as for those who believe in God and hold fast to Him, He will admit into His mercy and grace and guide them to Himself through the straight path* (4:175), this verse follows evidence and light with inheritance clear: they ask you for a ruling regarding *kalālah*, God gives ruling: one sister half, brother inherits all, two sisters two-thirds, mixed male twice female, He makes clear so you do not go astray, He knows all. The sūrah has shown that *“those who believe in God and hold fast”* are admitted into mercy and guided through straight path (4:175) — straight is inheritance shares made clear. The sūrah that began with limits set by God for orphans, women, inheritance, trust, prayer, judgment, charity, Abraham, settlement, enrichment, universal command to be mindful, ownership, capability, both rewards, justice though against yourselves, comprehensive faith, oscillation without forgiveness, hypocrite portrait, lowest depth, speech discipline, distinction, demand for a book from heaven as litany, forbidding *ṭayyibāt*, well-grounded to great reward, lineage, messengers, good news and warnings, sufficient witness, far straying, Hell’s path easy, humanity’s call, correction of People of the Book, servitude of Messiah, evidence and light — now ends where it began: family law — so you do not go astray. For the weak this is an opening: where helpless children were stood for with justice (4:127) and sisters deprived yet wished to marry were protected (4:127), *kalālah* sisters now share as half or two-thirds or with brothers male twice female — all made clear lest you stray, by the One whose earth is spacious and whose promise is true, who is ever Appreciative, All-Knowing, Ever-Pardoning, Most Capable, All-Hearing, All-Seeing, Fully Aware encompassing everything.
