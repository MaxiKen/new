## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:33

> **Do not take a ˹human˺ life—made sacred by Allah—except with ˹legal˺ right. If anyone is killed unjustly, We have given their heirs the authority, but do not let them exceed limits in retaliation, for they are already supported ˹by law˺.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Do Not Take Human Life Made Sacred Except With Legal Right**

Wa-lā taqtulū al-nafsa allatī ḥarrama Allāhu illā bi-l-ḥaqqi — and do not kill soul which Allah made sacred except with right, legal right. Wa-lā, and not, prohibition. Taqtulū from q-t-l, to kill, as verse 31 taqtulū awlādakum, kill children. Al-nafsa from n-f-s, soul, person, life, human life, from nafs. Allatī ḥarrama Allāhu, which Allah made sacred, forbade killing, from ḥ-r-m, sacred, forbidden, from ḥarrama, to make sacred, forbid. Illā bi-l-ḥaqqi, except with right, legal right, truth, justice, from ḥ-q-q, right, truth, legal right, as capital punishment for murder, etc., with justice. Al-Ṭabarī explains that do not kill human life made sacred by Allah except with legal right, meaning do not kill innocent person whose life Allah made sacred, forbidden to kill, except with legal right, such as qisas, retaliation for murder, apostasy, adultery of married, etc., with justice, legal. Al-Qurṭubī says nafs made sacred includes Muslim, dhimmī, muʿāhad, etc., whose life sacred, not to be killed except with legal right, justice.

**If Anyone Killed Unjustly, Heirs Given Authority**

Wa-man qutila maẓlūman fa-qad jaʿalnā li-waliyyihi sulṭānan — and whoever is killed unjustly, We gave his heir authority. Wa-man, and whoever, who. Qutila from q-t-l, killed, passive, was killed. Maẓlūman from ẓ-l-m, unjustly, wronged, oppressed, from ẓalama, to wrong, oppress, from ẓulm, injustice, wrong. Fa-qad jaʿalnā from j-ʿ-l, to make, give, as verse 12 jaʿalnā signs and verse 18 jaʿalnā Hell. Li-waliyyihi, for his heir, guardian, protector, from w-l-y, to be near, guardian, heir, protector, from walī, guardian, heir, close. Sulṭānan from s-l-ṭ, authority, power, proof, from sulṭān, authority, power. Al-Ṭabarī says if anyone killed unjustly, wrongfully, not with legal right, We gave his heir, guardian, family, authority, power, to demand justice, qisas or compensation, via law. Al-Rāzī says sulṭān here means authority to demand retaliation or pardon, via ruler, law, not personal vengeance without law.

**Do Not Exceed Limits In Retaliation, Already Supported By Law**

Fa-lā yusrif fī al-qatli innahu kāna manṣūran — so do not exceed in killing, indeed he is supported. Fa-lā yusrif from s-r-f, to exceed, transgress, waste, from asrafa, to exceed, transgress, waste, from same root as isrāf, excess, waste. Fī al-qatli, in killing, in retaliation, from q-t-l. Innahu kāna manṣūran, indeed he is supported, helped, from n-ṣ-r, to support, help, from naṣara, to help, support, and manṣūr, supported, helped. Al-Ṭabarī explains that do not exceed limits in retaliation, killing, meaning heir should not exceed legal limits, not kill more than killer, not mutilate, not kill other than killer, etc., for he is already supported by law, by Allah, by ruler, so no need to exceed. Al-Qurṭubī says lā yusrif fī al-qatli means do not transgress in killing, retaliation, by killing more than one for one, or killing innocent, etc., because heir already supported by law, by Allah, so justice will be done without excess.

**Why Prohibition Of Killing Sacred Life**

Why prohibition? Because life made sacred by Allah, not to be taken except with legal right, justice, as part of most upright guidance protecting life. Previous verses protected children from killing for fear of poverty, protected chastity from approaching adultery, now protects all sacred life from killing except legal right. The series shows comprehensive protection: children, lineage, life, orphan wealth, etc., all wisdom, most upright. Killing sacred life unjustly is heinous sin, like killing children, as both are killing innocent.

**Connection To Killing Children And Provision**

Verse 31 said do not kill children for fear of poverty, We provide for them and you, killing heinous sin. Verse 33 says do not kill sacred life except with legal right, if killed unjustly heir given authority but do not exceed limits for already supported. The connection is that killing children is example of killing sacred life unjustly, heinous sin, and general prohibition of killing sacred life includes children, all innocent. The provision reminder that We provide for them and you also applies: do not kill for fear of poverty or other reason, except legal right, justice.

**Link To Authority And Support By Law**

The phrase We gave his heir authority but do not exceed limits for already supported by law indicates that Islam gives authority to heir to demand justice but limits excess, as supported by law, by Allah, by ruler, so no need for personal vengeance beyond law. This is most upright: justice, not excess, support by law, not anarchy. The sūrah's theme of justice appears: no bearer bears burden of another, record laid open, sufficient self as reckoner, now justice in retaliation, not exceeding.

**Linguistic Note On Nafs And Mazluman And Sultan And Israf**

Taqtulū from q-t-l, to kill. Nafs from n-f-s, soul, life, person. Ḥarrama from ḥ-r-m, to make sacred, forbid, from ḥaruma. Ḥaqq from ḥ-q-q, right, truth, legal right. Qutila from q-t-l, killed. Maẓlūman from ẓ-l-m, unjustly, wronged, from ẓalama, to wrong. Jaʿalnā from j-ʿ-l, to make, give. Walī from w-l-y, heir, guardian, from waliya. Sulṭān from s-l-ṭ, authority, power. Yusrif from s-r-f, to exceed, transgress, waste, from asrafa. Qatl from q-t-l, killing. Manṣūr from n-ṣ-r, supported, helped, from naṣara. The verse's structure — do not kill sacred soul except with right, whoever killed unjustly We gave his heir authority, so do not exceed in killing, indeed he is supported — with prohibition, exception, authority given, limit not to exceed, reason supported, shows balanced justice: protection of life, legal right exception, authority for heir, limit not to exceed, support by law.

**How This Verse Teaches Protection Of Life And Justice**

This verse teaches protection of life made sacred by Allah, prohibition of killing except with legal right, justice, and teaches justice in retaliation: heir given authority but not to exceed limits, for already supported by law. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should not kill sacred life unjustly, should know life sacred, not to be taken except with legal right, justice, via law, and if someone killed unjustly, heir has authority to demand qisas or pardon, but should not exceed limits, transgress, as supported by law, by Allah, so justice will be done without excess, vengeance. The practical lesson is to protect life, avoid killing unjustly, seek justice via law, not personal excess, as most upright guidance, wisdom.

**A Reflection On Sacred Life And Support**

Al-nafs allatī ḥarrama Allāhu — soul which Allah made sacred — indicates sanctity of human life, made sacred by Allah, not to be violated except with right, justice, legal. This sanctity is foundation of most upright, wisdom. Manṣūran — supported — indicates that heir of unjustly killed is supported by law, by Allah, by ruler, so no need to exceed, transgress, take law into own hands beyond limits. The believer who reflects will protect sacred life, not kill except with legal right, will seek justice via authority given, not exceed, trusting support by law, as part of most upright guidance that leads to mighty reward and avoids painful punishment and Hell.
