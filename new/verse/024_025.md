## Sūrah an-Nūr 24:25

> On that Day, Allah will give them their just penalty in full, and they will ˹come to˺ know that Allah ˹alone˺ is the Ultimate Truth.

**Expanded Commentary**

**The Exact Settlement of the Divine Account: yuwaffīhim Allāhu dīnahumu al-ḥaqq**

The eschatological trial reaches its final judicial resolution: *yawmaʾidhin yuwaffīhim Allāhu dīnahumu al-ḥaqq*. The verb *yuwaffī* denotes paying in full, settling a balance without the slightest deficit, omission, or injustice. The noun *dīn* in classical Arabic lexicography carries the primordial meaning of recompense, debt, and judicial reckoning—the precise return for actions performed in this mortal life.

God does not administer punishment out of arbitrary wrath or vindictive fury. The divine Judge settles the moral debt that the slanderer accumulated through their own agency. The retribution rendered on that Day is characterized as *al-ḥaqq*—absolute, unadulterated justice. Every particle of emotional anguish inflicted upon an innocent soul, every drop of tears shed in domestic privacy, and every fracture caused in the social fabric is weighed with microscopic precision, ensuring that the ledger of existence is balanced to absolute perfection, leaving no injustice unaddressed, no sorrow unavenged, and no injury uncompensated.

**The Unmasking of the Ultimate Truth: Allāha huwa al-Ḥaqqu al-Mubīn**

The verse culminates in one of the most magnificent theological proclamations in the entire Qur'an: *wa-yaʿlamūna anna Allāha huwa al-Ḥaqqu al-Mubīn*, and they will know that Allah is the Manifest Truth. In this fleeting worldly life, the hypocrites, cynics, and scandalmongers lived within a thick fog of delusion. They convinced themselves that truth is merely a social construct, that morality is whatever the powerful can enforce, and that God's warnings were idle rhetoric.

On that Day, the veil of mortal illusion is incinerated by the brilliance of cosmic reality. They will confront the undeniable ontological fact that God is *al-Ḥaqq*—the Supreme Reality, the Source of all existence, whose promises are absolute—and *al-Mubīn*, the Manifest Truth who clarifies all obscurities and exposes all falsehoods. The reality of the divine sovereignty will stand before their eyes with terrifying, unavoidable clarity, reducing every earthly pretense to dust and vindicating the divine governance of creation across the cosmos for all eternity.

**The Epistemological Shock of the Deniers**

The transformation of knowledge is underscored by the verb *wa-yaʿlamūn*—and they will know. In this world, the scandalmongers operated upon arrogant assumptions, weaponized rumors, and cynical calculations, mistaking their ability to manipulate public opinion for genuine insight. They laughed at the warnings of the Prophet ﷺ and dismissed the pain of the innocent.

On the Day of Judgment, their skepticism is permanently shattered by the overwhelming sensory presence of divine judgment. This realization, however, brings no spiritual redemption or salvation. It is an agonizing, inescapable cognitive shock that arrives after the closing of the probationary window of repentance. It is the tragic knowledge of the damned, who recognize the truth of God only when their opportunity to embrace it with faith and virtue has been forever lost, leaving them in eternal regret, crushing humiliation, and irreversible despair.

**The Vindicated Majesty of the Accused**

Reflecting upon the emotional arc of this passage reveals the profound solace this verse provided to the victims of slander. When the Mother of the Believers ʿĀʾishah lay weeping in her chamber, surrounded by the venomous gossip of Madinah, the world felt hopelessly hostile, cruel, and unfair. The liars walked through the marketplace boasting of their influence, while the innocent were submerged in grief.

Verse 25 descends as an eternal divine guarantee that justice will prevail. It promises every slandered soul across human history that their tears are recorded in the divine register, that their honor is fiercely guarded by the Lord of the worlds, and that the architects of character assassination will not escape divine retribution. God Himself guarantees that the truth of their innocence will shine brightly before the assembled creation, while their persecutors face absolute humiliation, establishing the triumph of the pure over the wicked throughout eternity, bringing peace to wounded hearts.

**The Semantic Unity of dīn as Religion, Law, and Recompense**

A rich theological insight emerges from the multidimensional semantic range of the Arabic root *d-y-n*. In Qur'anic discourse, the word *dīn* encompasses the religion revealed by God, the legal statutes commanding human obedience, and the final eschatological recompense administered on the Day of Judgment (*Yawm al-Dīn*).

These three dimensions are fundamentally inseparable facets of one cosmic reality. The religion is the truth that guides; the law is the justice that governs; and the recompense is the reality that settles all accounts. Those who mocked the legal *dīn* by slandering chaste women and violating divine boundaries will inevitably collide with the retributive *dīn* when God settles their account in full. To live in defiance of divine law is to guarantee an inescapable collision with divine justice, verifying that the universe is governed by absolute moral order, perfect balance, and divine majesty.

**The Triumphant Conclusion to the Crisis of al-Ifk**

This verse represents the majestic theological summit and conclusion of the entire passage addressing the Grand Slander, which commenced in verse 11. Over fifteen intense verses, the Qur'an dissected the psychology of gossip, exposed the conspiracies of hypocrites, rebuked the passive credulity of believers, established procedural safeguards for courts, and healed communal trauma through divine mercy.

Verse 25 brings this extraordinary journey to its eternal anchor. Human falsehoods, no matter how loudly proclaimed or widely circulated, are ephemeral shadows that vanish before the dawn. God is the Ultimate, Manifest Truth, whose light penetrates every darkness and whose justice outlasts every empire of deceit. The passage concludes by anchoring the peace and dignity of the faithful in the eternal reality of the Living, Omnipotent Creator, pointing toward the uncreated light of God that fills the heavens and the earth, illuminating all creation.
