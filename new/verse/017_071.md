## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:71

> **˹Beware of˺ the Day We will summon every people with their leader. So whoever will be given their record in their right hand will read it ˹happily˺ and will not be wronged ˹even by the width of˺ the thread of a date stone.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Day We Will Summon Every People With Their Leader**

Yawma nadʿū kulla unāsin bi-imāmihim — Day We will call, summon every people with their leader, imam. Yawma from y-w-m, Day, Day of Resurrection, Beware of Day, Day We will summon. Nadʿū from d-ʿ-w, to call, summon, as yadʿūkum He will call you, Day He will call you respond praising Him, and nadʿū We will call, summon. Kulla unāsin from a-n-s, every people, every group, every community, from unās, people, plural of insān, human, as every people, every community. Bi-imāmihim from a-m-m, with their leader, imam, guide, from imām, leader, guide, imam, as leader, guide, book, record, etc., as every people summoned with their leader, imam, who led them, Prophet, Book, etc. Al-Ṭabarī explains that Beware of Day We will summon every people with their leader, imam, meaning Day of Judgment We will call every people, every community, with their leader, imam, who led them, Prophet, or Book, or leader, to be judged, as each people summoned with leader. Al-Qurṭubī says bi-imāmihim means with their leader, imam, guide, Prophet, or Book, or record, etc., as every people summoned with leader.

**Whoever Given Record In Right Hand Will Read Happily And Not Wronged Even Thread Of Date Stone**

Fa-man ūtiya kitābahu bi-yamīnihi fa-ulā'ika yaqra'ūna kitābahum wa-lā yuẓlamūna fatīlan — then whoever is given his book in his right hand, those will read their book and will not be wronged even thread of date stone. Fa-man, then whoever, who. Ūtiya from a-t-y, to be given, given, from ātā, to give, as ūtiya given, passive, given his book. Kitābahu from k-t-b, his book, record, as kitāb Book, Record, as record laid open, bound destiny to neck, record laid open, read your book, sufficient self as reckoner, etc. Bi-yamīnihi from y-m-n, in his right hand, right, from yamīn, right hand, right, as right hand, as right hand given book indicates success, as opposed to left, behind back. Fa-ulā'ika, then those, so those. Yaqra'ūna from q-r-ʾ, to read, recite, as iqra' read, read your book, etc., they will read their book, happily, as happily, as success. Kitābahum, their book, record, from k-t-b. Wa-lā yuẓlamūna from ẓ-l-m, to be wronged, oppressed, injustice, from ẓalama, to wrong, oppress, as yuẓlamūna will not be wronged, not oppressed. Fatīlan from f-t-l, thread of date stone, thin thread, small amount, from fatīl, thread, thin thread in groove of date stone, as even width of thread of date stone, smallest amount, as not wronged even by width of thread of date stone, smallest injustice, as justice, no injustice, as Lord does not wrong. Al-Ṭabarī explains that whoever given record in right hand, book in right hand, will read it happily, joyfully, and will not be wronged even by width of thread of date stone, smallest amount, meaning no injustice, full justice, reward, as mighty reward, appreciated striving, etc. Al-Qurṭubī says fatīlan means thread of date stone, thin thread in groove, smallest amount, indicating no injustice even smallest.

**Why Summon Every People With Leader And Right Hand Reading Happily No Wrong Even Thread**

Why summon every people with leader and right hand reading happily no wrong even thread? Because Day of Judgment, Day He will call you respond praising Him thinking remained only little while, and Day We will summon every people with their leader, to be judged, as each people summoned with leader, imam, Prophet, Book, etc., and whoever given record in right hand will read happily and not wronged even thread of date stone, showing justice, no injustice, mighty reward for believers who do good, appreciated striving, as Qur'an guides to most upright, gives good news to believers who do good mighty reward, warns those who do not believe painful punishment, and record laid open, bound destiny to neck, read book, sufficient self as reckoner, no bearer bears burden, etc., and now right hand reading happily, no wrong even thread, showing success, justice, as opposed to blind in this world blind in Hereafter far more astray, as next verse says.

**Connection To Record Laid Open And Read Book Sufficient Self And No Bearer Bears Burden**

Verse 13 bound destiny to neck, record laid open, verse 14 read your book sufficient self as reckoner, verse 15 no bearer bears burden, etc., verse 71 summon every people with leader, whoever given record in right hand will read happily and not wronged even thread, showing record theme: destiny bound to neck, record laid open, read book, sufficient self, no bearer bears burden, now summon every people with leader, given record right hand read happily, no wrong even thread, showing justice, success, as record laid open, reading, etc., and accountability.

**Link To Dignified Children Of Adam And Privileged And Day Call**

Verse 70 dignified children of Adam, carried on land and sea, granted good provisions, privileged far above many creatures, showing honour for children of Adam, and verse 71 Day summon every people with leader, whoever given record right hand read happily no wrong even thread, showing that dignified children of Adam will be summoned, judged, some given right hand reading happily, no wrong, as success, as mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, while others blind in this world blind in Hereafter far more astray, as next verse.

**Linguistic Note On Nad'u And Unas And Imam And Utiya Kitabahu Bi-yaminihi And Fatilan**

Yawm from y-w-m, Day. Nadʿū from d-ʿ-w, to call, summon, from daʿā. Kull from k-l-l, every. Unās from a-n-s, people, plural of insān, human. Imām from a-m-m, leader, guide, from amma, to lead, from imām. Ūtiya from a-t-y, to be given, from ātā. Kitāb from k-t-b, book, record. Yamīn from y-m-n, right hand, right, from yamīn. Yaqra'ūna from q-r-ʾ, to read, recite. Yuẓlamūna from ẓ-l-m, to be wronged, oppressed, from ẓalama. Fatīl from f-t-l, thread of date stone, thin thread, from fatala, to twist, thread. The verse's structure — Day We will summon every people with their leader, then whoever given his book in right hand those will read their book and will not be wronged even thread — with Day summon every people with leader and whoever given right hand read happily no wrong even thread, shows Day of Judgment summoning with leader, success for right hand, justice no wrong even smallest thread, as mighty reward, appreciated striving.

**How This Verse Teaches Summoning With Leader And Right Hand Success No Injustice**

This verse teaches summoning every people with leader on Day and right hand success no injustice: Beware of Day We will summon every people with their leader, imam, guide, Prophet, Book, etc., to be judged, and whoever will be given their record, book, in right hand, right, will read it happily, joyfully, and will not be wronged even by width of thread of date stone, smallest amount, no injustice, full justice, mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should prepare for Day summon every people with leader, be among those given record in right hand, reading happily, not wronged even thread, by believing, doing good, following most upright guidance, wisdom, that Qur'an guides to, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, while blind in this world blind in Hereafter far more astray, as next verse says, etc.

**A Reflection On Right Hand And No Wrong Even Thread**

Bi-yamīnihi — in his right hand — indicates success, honour, as right hand given book indicates success, as opposed to left hand, behind back, indicating failure, as Qur'an says. Yaqra'ūna kitābahum — they will read their book, happily — indicates joy, success, reading record laid open happily, as record laid open, read your book, sufficient self as reckoner, etc., now reading happily, as success, mighty reward, appreciated striving. Wa-lā yuẓlamūna fatīlan — not wronged even thread of date stone, width of thread — indicates justice, no injustice even smallest, smallest amount, thread of date stone, thin thread in groove, smallest, as Lord does not wrong, as He is All-Aware All-Seeing, knows best, Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, but torment fearsome, and justice, no wrong even thread, as mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, while blind in this world blind in Hereafter far more astray, as next verse, etc.
