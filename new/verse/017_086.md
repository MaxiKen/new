## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:86

> **If We willed, We could have certainly taken away what We have revealed to you ˹O Prophet˺—then you would find none to guarantee its return from Us—**

**Expanded Commentary**

**If We Willed We Could Have Taken Away What We Revealed To You**

Wa-la-in shi'nā la-nadhhabanna bi-alladhī awḥaynā ilayka — and if We willed, We would certainly take away, go away with what We revealed to you. Wa-la-in, and if, and indeed if, if We willed, conditional with emphasis, if We willed. Shi'nā from sh-y-ʾ, to will, wish, if We willed, wished, from shā'a, to will. La-nadhhabanna from dh-h-b, to take away, go away with, remove, from adhhaba, to take away, remove, go away with, with emphasis, certainly take away, with nun emphasis, la-nadhhabanna certainly take away, remove. Bi-alladhī, with what, what, that which. Awḥaynā from w-ḥ-y, We revealed, inspired, as awḥā revealed, as awḥaynā We revealed to you, O Prophet, as revelation, Qur'an, wisdom, most upright, as this is part of wisdom revealed to you, as verse 39 says, and as spirit from command of Lord, etc., and as what We revealed to you, Qur'an, revelation, as favour immense, mercy, etc. Ilayka, to you, O Prophet. Al-Ṭabarī explains that if We willed, We could have certainly taken away, removed, gone away with what We revealed to you, O Prophet, Qur'an, revelation, wisdom, most upright, meaning Allah able to take away revelation, Qur'an, if He willed, remove it, take it away, as He is All-Powerful, All-Aware All-Seeing, etc., as He gives abundant or limited provisions, etc., and as He is Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, but torment fearsome, and as He could take away revelation if willed, as warning, as favour, mercy, not to be taken for granted.

**Then You Would Find None To Guarantee Its Return From Us**

Thumma lā tajidu laka bihi ʿalaynā wakīlan — then you would not find for you with it, about it, against Us, guardian, guarantor, keeper. Thumma, then, after taking away. Lā tajidu from w-j-d, to find, you would not find, you would find not. Laka, for you, for yourself. Bihi, with it, about it, concerning it, revelation, Qur'an. ʿAlaynā, against Us, upon Us, against Allah, Us. Wakīlan from w-k-l, guardian, guarantor, keeper, trustee, disposer, as wakīl, keeper, guardian, trustee, as verse 2 Trustee of Affairs, verse 54 not sent you as keeper over them, verse 65 sufficient Lord as Guardian, wakīlan, verse 68 no guardian to protect you, wakīlan, and verse 86 no guarantor to guarantee return from Us, wakīlan, as no guardian, guarantor, keeper, to guarantee its return, to bring back revelation, if Allah took it away, as only Allah has power, as those claimed besides Him have no power, as verse 56 says, no power, so if He took away revelation, you would find none to guarantee its return from Us, no guardian, guarantor, keeper, to bring back.

**Why If Willed Could Take Away Revelation And No Guarantor**

Why if We willed We could have taken away what We revealed to you and you would find none to guarantee its return from Us? Because revelation, Qur'an, is favour immense, mercy, from Lord, as next verse says had it not been for mercy of your Lord, indeed His favour upon you immense, as mercy, favour immense, not to be taken for granted, as Allah able to take away if willed, remove, as He is All-Powerful, All-Aware All-Seeing, etc., and if taken away, no guarantor, keeper, guardian, to guarantee return from Us, no one to bring back, as only Allah has power, as those claimed besides Him have no power, as verse 56 says, no power to remove harm or transfer, etc., so revelation is favour, mercy, immense, not to be taken for granted, as He could take away if willed, but mercy prevents, favour immense.

**Connection To Spirit From Command And Little Knowledge And Mercy Favour Immense**

Verse 85 they ask about spirit, say spirit from command of Lord, you have been given but little knowledge. Verse 86 if We willed We could have taken away what We revealed to you, then you would find none to guarantee its return from Us. Verse 87 had it not been for mercy of your Lord, indeed His favour upon you immense, showing that spirit from command of Lord, little knowledge given, but revelation, Qur'an, is from Lord, favour immense, mercy, not to be taken away, as spirit from command, little knowledge, but revelation favour immense, mercy, etc., and if willed could take away, no guarantor, but mercy prevents, favour immense, as mercy, favour immense, not to be taken for granted.

**Link To Quran Healing Mercy And Truth Come Falsehood Vanish And Human Jinn Cannot Produce Equal**

Verse 82 Quran healing mercy for believers but increases wrongdoers in loss, verse 81 truth come falsehood vanish, indeed falsehood bound to vanish, verse 88 if all humans and jinn came together to produce equivalent of Quran they could not produce equal, no matter how they supported each other, showing Quran's miraculous nature, inimitability, as healing mercy, truth, vs falsehood bound to vanish, and humans and jinn cannot produce equal, even if supported, as Quran inimitable, as revelation, favour immense, mercy, etc., and as if willed could take away revelation, no guarantor, but mercy, favour immense, prevents, as Quran healing mercy, truth, inimitable, favour immense.

**Linguistic Note On Shi'nā And Nadhhabanna And Awḥayna And Wakilan**

La-in, if, indeed if, conditional with emphasis. Shi'nā from sh-y-ʾ, to will, wish, from shā'a. Nadhhabanna from dh-h-b, to take away, go away with, remove, from adhhaba, to take away, with nun emphasis, certainly take away. Alladhī awḥaynā, what We revealed, from w-ḥ-y, to reveal, inspire, from awḥā. Tajidu from w-j-d, to find. Wakīl from w-k-l, guardian, guarantor, keeper, trustee, from wakala. The verse's structure — if We willed We would certainly take away with what We revealed to you, then you would not find for you with it against Us guardian, guarantor — with condition if willed would take away revelation and result no guardian to guarantee return from Us, shows power to take away revelation, no guarantor to bring back, as only Allah has power, as favour, mercy, immense, not to be taken for granted.

**How This Verse Teaches Power To Take Away Revelation And No Guarantor**

This verse teaches power to take away revelation and no guarantor to guarantee return: if We willed, We could have certainly taken away, removed, gone away with what We revealed to you, O Prophet, Qur'an, revelation, wisdom, most upright, then you would find none, no guardian, guarantor, keeper, trustee, to guarantee its return from Us, to bring back revelation, if Allah took it away, as only Allah has power, as those claimed besides Him have no power, as verse 56 says, no power, so revelation is favour immense, mercy, from Lord, as next verse says had it not been for mercy of your Lord, indeed His favour upon you immense, as mercy, favour immense, not to be taken for granted, as He could take away if willed, but mercy prevents, favour immense. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should know Allah able to take away revelation, Qur'an, if He willed, remove it, and you would find no guarantor to guarantee return from Us, no guardian, keeper, to bring back, as only Allah has power, so revelation is favour immense, mercy, immense, not to be taken for granted, should be grateful, follow most upright guidance, wisdom, that Qur'an guides to, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, healing mercy, etc., while wrongdoers increase in loss, etc.

**A Reflection On Taking Away Revelation And No Guarantor**

La-nadhhabanna bi-alladhī awḥaynā ilayka — We would certainly take away with what We revealed to you — indicates power to take away revelation, Qur'an, if willed, remove, take away, as Allah All-Powerful, All-Aware All-Seeing, etc., able to take away revelation, as He gives abundant or limited provisions, etc., and as He is Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, but torment fearsome, and as He could take away revelation if willed, as warning, as favour, mercy, not to be taken for granted. Thumma lā tajidu laka bihi ʿalaynā wakīlan — then you would not find for you with it against Us guardian, guarantor — indicates no guardian, guarantor, keeper, to guarantee return, bring back revelation, if Allah took it away, as only Allah has power, as those claimed besides Him have no power to remove harm or transfer, as verse 56 says, no power, so revelation is favour immense, mercy, from Lord, as next verse says had it not been for mercy of your Lord, indeed His favour upon you immense, as mercy, favour immense, not to be taken for granted, should be grateful, follow most upright, wisdom, leading to mighty reward, etc.
