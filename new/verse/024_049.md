## Sūrah an-Nūr 24:49

> But if the truth is in their favour, they come to him, fully submitting.

**Expanded Commentary**

**The Psychology of Opportunistic Adjudication: wa-in yakun lahumu al-ḥaqq**

The Qur'an delivers a penetrating psychological exposure of hypocritical opportunism: *wa-in yakun lahumu al-ḥaqqu yaʾtū ilayhi mudhʿinīn*. Having exposed how the hypocrites reflexively turned away from prophetic judgment when they feared their fraudulent claims would be dismissed (v. 48), the text now reveals their radically opposite behavior when circumstances reversed.

If they possess a clear, legally solid title to property, an outstanding debt, or an undeniable entitlement (*in yakun lahumu al-ḥaqq*), their hesitation instantly vanishes. They run to the prophetic court with eager speed, demanding that the dispute be arbitrated immediately according to Islamic law! Their commitment is not to the transcendent principle of divine justice, but to the selfish utilization of the judicial apparatus whenever it guarantees their personal enrichment. They love the court only when the court serves as an instrument for their profit, revealing complete moral bankruptcy, bad faith, and utter indifference to spiritual truth.

**The Theatrics of Submissive Posturing: yaʾtū ilayhi mudhʿinīn**

The verse captures their performative compliance with surgical precision: *yaʾtū ilayhi mudhʿinīn*, they come to him, fully submitting. The active participle *mudhʿinīn* derives from *adhʿana*, denoting swift, humble, yielding, and submissive obedience.

When the law favors them, their demeanor undergoes a theatrical transformation. The very individuals who previously sneered at prophetic authority, fabricated excuses, and plotted with tribal conspirators now arrive at the mosque with bowed heads, modest gazes, and voices dripping with feigned piety. They present themselves as model citizens and paragons of Islamic obedience, loudly praising the flawless justice of the Prophet ﷺ and declaring their unyielding fidelity to the Qur'an. This theatrical humility exposes the grotesque elasticity of their character, demonstrating that pride and humility are mere costumes donned or discarded according to financial calculation and personal advantage.

**Instrumentalizing Religion for Personal Gain**

This verse diagnoses a spiritual disease that plagues human societies across all eras: the instrumentalization of sacred law for private self-interest. To the opportunist, divine revelation, ethical principles, and religious courts are not sacred realities that demand human surrender; they are utilitarian tools to be exploited for personal advantage.

When religious rules protect their assets, validate their contracts, or enforce their claims against others, they become militant defenders of religious orthodoxy, quoting scripture with ferocity and demanding literal compliance. But the moment religious rules demand that they forgive a debt, surrender illegally seized land, pay fair wages, or treat a spouse with equitable kindness, they immediately search for secular loopholes, dispute jurisdictions, or denounce the law as excessively burdensome. This selective adherence reduces sacred truth to an opportunistic weapon, profaning holy values in the service of base greed and unbridled egoism.

**The Antithesis of Authentic Faith**

The behavior depicted in verse 49 stands in absolute, diametrical opposition to the authentic character of faith (*al-īmān*). True faith is defined by unconditional, non-negotiable surrender (*taslīm*) to the divine will, regardless of personal consequence or material loss.

The authentic believer approaches divine arbitration with the spirit articulated in 4:65: they find within themselves no resistance whatsoever against prophetic judgment, submitting with complete, wholehearted acceptance (*wa-yusallimū taslīmā*). If the court rules against the true believer, they accept the verdict with dignified humility, grateful that an injustice was prevented in this world rather than punished in the next. The hypocrite, conversely, experiences religion as a zero-sum commercial negotiation. They worship their own ego (*al-hawā*); God and His Messenger are acknowledged only insofar as they serve as useful advocates for the ego's insatiable desires, creating an idolatry of self that poisons the spirit.

Furthermore, this opportunistic posture reveals a total absence of the fear of God (*taqwā*). A person who genuinely believes in the Day of Judgment understands that acquiring property or wealth through a biased or fraudulent legal judgment in this world is akin to swallowing burning coals of hellfire into their belly. The Prophet ﷺ famously cautioned litigants in his court: I am only a human being, and you bring your disputes to me; perhaps one of you is more eloquent in his argument than the other, and I rule in his favor based on what I hear. Whoever I grant a right that belongs to his brother, let him not take it, for I am only granting him a piece of the Fire. The hypocrite ignores this terrifying prophetic warning because they care nothing for the Hereafter, obsessed exclusively with immediate material acquisition.

**Sociological Manifestations of Selective Justice**

In the broader social sphere, the dynamic exposed in verse 49 corrodes the foundations of civic order and public morality. When powerful individuals or privileged factions embrace the law only when it benefits them, the legal system inevitably degenerates into an instrument of class oppression.

In such corrupted societies, the law becomes a net that catches small fish while letting sharks swim through. The wealthy enforce contracts ruthlessly against the poor, using police and courts to extract every penny, but use their political leverage, expensive lawyers, and jurisdictional maneuvers to evade accountability when their own transgressions are challenged. The Qur'an's merciless exposure of *yaʾtū ilayhi mudhʿinīn* shames this structural hypocrisy, demanding a society where the rule of law is applied with absolute, blind impartiality across all citizens regardless of wealth, lineage, or prestige, safeguarding communal health.

**The Inviolable Sovereignty of Truth**

The phrase *al-ḥaqq* (the truth / the right) carries deep ontological significance in the Qur'an. *Al-Ḥaqq* is one of the supreme names of God Himself, signifying absolute, eternal, and objective reality.

Because truth belongs to God, it is sovereign, immutable, and incapable of being co-opted by human hypocrisy. The hypocrites imagined that by selectively appealing to *al-ḥaqq*, they were outsmarting the system and manipulating the Prophet ﷺ. In reality, they were merely condemning themselves. By approaching the court when they were in the right, they publicly validated the legitimacy and authority of prophetic adjudication, leaving themselves with zero legitimate excuse when they turned away from that very same court in other disputes. Their own opportunistic compliance served as an unshakeable legal testimony against them before the divine throne on Judgment Day, ensuring their eternal exposure.
