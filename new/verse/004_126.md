## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:126

> To Allah ˹alone˺ belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth. And Allah is Fully Aware of everything.

### Expanded Commentary

**God’s ownership encompasses every realm**

Everything in the heavens and earth belongs to Allah. The statement follows the praise of Abraham and prevents any lineage from becoming a rival source of authority. Prophets, scholars, families, and rulers are honoured only as servants within the divine kingdom.

Allah is fully aware of everything, including what ownership conceals: the origin of wealth, the use of authority, and the motive behind a charitable act. A person may own property legally while remaining morally accountable for how it was acquired and spent. A ruler may govern temporarily while being answerable for the people placed under his care.

The verse’s universality also creates a foundation for environmental and social responsibility. Creation is not an ownerless warehouse. Humans hold it under God’s knowledge and must not treat other creatures, land, or people as disposable.

**Stewardship under ownership**

Human ownership is real in law but dependent in theology. The owner must ask how property was acquired, whether a right was hidden, and what obligations accompany possession. Seeing everything as belonging to Allah makes stewardship more careful without making lawful work or personal responsibility meaningless.
