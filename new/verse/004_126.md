## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:126

> To Allah ˹alone˺ belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth. And Allah is Fully Aware of everything.

**Expanded Commentary**

**To God Alone Belongs Whatever Is in Heavens and Earth**

The verse states *wa li Allāhi mā fī al-samāwāti wa mā fī al-arḍ* — and to God, for God, belongs whatever, all that, is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth. *Mā fī al-samāwāti wa mā fī al-arḍ* — whatever is in heavens and on earth — was explained as absolute ownership: angels nearest to Him who would never be too proud to be servants (4:172), the Messiah a servant not too proud (4:172), the single soul spread into humanity (4:1), the orphans’ wealth held as trust (4:2, 4:10), the women whose dowries are gracious (4:4), the inheritance shares appointed (4:11–12), the earth made spacious for emigration (4:100), the prayer shortened on travel (4:101), and the promise true whose abode is Gardens or Hell (4:122, 4:121). *Li Allāhi* — to Him, for Him — was read as exclusive belonging, as the sūrah repeats *to Him belongs whatever is in heavens and earth* (4:131, 4:132, 4:170) to bracket the passage on women, reconciliation, and trusteeship. The phrase was explained as the reply to invoking female gods besides Him (4:117) — they belong to Him, not beside Him — and to taking Satan as guardian instead of Him (4:119) — the guardian taken belongs to Him, cursed (4:118). The sūrah has said that the People of the Book claim wishes yet grace is not by wishes (4:123), that Abraham was taken as close friend (4:125) — friendship with the Owner of all is the highest honour — and that whoever desires world’s reward, with Him are both worlds’ rewards (4:134); ownership here is the ground of those promises.

**And God Is Fully Aware of Everything**

The seal *wa kāna Allāhu bi kulli shayʾin muḥīṭā* — And God is Fully Aware, Encompassing, of everything, of every thing — was explained as *muḥīṭ* — encompassing, encircling, comprehending — the same name that sealed night plotting in His presence — *He is Fully Aware of what they do* (4:108) — now universalised to *bi kulli shayʾ* — of everything. *Bi kulli shayʾin* — of every thing — was read as covering the heavens and earth just said to belong to Him, the charity and kindness and reconciliation commanded (4:114), the dispute judged by what He shows (4:105), the good male or female with faith (4:124), and the speck not wronging (*naqīrā* — 4:124) and the atom not wronging (4:40). The sūrah has sealed knowledge diversely — All-Knowing, All-Wise where sin is earned (4:111), All-Hearing, All-Seeing where trusts are judged (4:58), All-Aware of what you do where verification was commanded (4:94), *Muḥīṭ* where night hides (4:108) — here *Muḥīṭ* of everything gathers them: nothing escapes perimeter because everything belongs. Early notes linked *muḥīṭ* to spaciousness: earth was said to be spacious (4:97, 4:100), yet His awareness is more spacious, encompassing the spacious.

**A Verse That Follows Best Faith With Ownership**

Placed after *who is better than those who submit, do good, follow Abraham ḥanīf, God took Abraham as friend* (4:125), this verse follows best faith with ownership that makes friendship intelligible. Abraham was taken as *khalīl* by the One to whom belongs whatever is in heavens and earth; his *millah ḥanīf* was submission to the Owner, not to the female gods who themselves belong to Him and are invoked only as Satan’s share (4:117–118). The sūrah has said *God suffices as Guardian and Helper* (4:45), *God is sufficient as Trustee* (4:81, 4:132), *God is sufficient as Witness* (4:79); to say *to Him belongs whatever is in heavens and earth* is to say guardianship, help, trusteeship, and witnessing are His by right of ownership. Where the sūrah taught that the Book was sent with truth to judge between people by what He shows (4:105) and that most secret talks have no good except charity, kindness, reconciliation seeking His pleasure (4:114), ownership as *muḥīṭ* explains why judging by what He shows and seeking His pleasure succeeds: the Show-er and the Pleasure-owner owns and encompasses.

**The Belonging That Makes Oaths and Treaties Possible**

The sūrah repeats *to Him belongs whatever is in heavens and earth* as oath-ground for contracts the chapter has detailed: the firm commitment of marriage — *mīthāq ghalīẓ* — taken from women (4:21), the treaty — *mīthāq* — with covenanted peoples whose blood-money is delivered even when mistaken (4:92) and whose allies are excepted from hostility (4:90), and the single soul’s tie that makes family honour a due (4:1). To say all belongs to Him is to say oaths sworn by Him have owner as witness — *God is sufficient as Witness* (4:79) — and trustee — *God is sufficient as Trustee* (4:132). The sūrah has shown that those who trade a portion for misguidance and who believe in *jibt* (4:51) think oaths belong to other lords; this verse returns every *mā* — whatever — to the One whose earth is spacious and whose promise is ever true, so that settlement between spouses (4:128) and reconciliation between people (4:114) hold because the Holder holds all.

**Ownership That Makes Wishes Void and Promises True**

The pairing — *to Him belongs* and *He encompasses everything* — was explained as making *“neither by your wishes”* (4:123) void and *“God’s promise ever true, whose word more truthful?”* (4:122) true. Wishes do not move ownership; promise from the Owner does. The sūrah has moved from two abodes — Hell without escape (4:121) and Gardens forever true (4:122) — through the law of wishes, through the entry of doers of good with faith not wronged a speck (4:124), through best faith as submission to Owner and following Abraham whom Owner took as friend — to the Owner’s declaration that all belongs and all is encompassed. For the weak for whom burdens were lightened, the declaration is lightening: the One to whom your weak *ḥīlah* and *sabīl* were unknown to others (4:98) is the One who owns heavens and earth and encompasses, so your helplessness hoped to be pardoned (4:99) and your emigration where you find many havens (4:100) and your prayer shortened (4:101) are under the same Ownership. The next passage will use that ownership to teach about women: they ask regarding women, say He instructs you regarding them, instruction already revealed concerning orphan women deprived yet wished to marry, helpless children, orphans’ rights, and whatever good you do is well known to Him (4:127).
