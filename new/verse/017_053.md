## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:53

> **Tell My ˹believing˺ servants to say only what is best. Satan certainly seeks to sow discord among them. Satan is indeed a sworn enemy to humankind.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Tell My Believing Servants To Say Only What Is Best**

Wa-qul li-ʿibādī yaqūlū allatī hiya aḥsanu — and say to My servants they say what is best, most excellent. Wa-qul from q-w-l, to say, command to Prophet. Li-ʿibādī, to My servants, My believing servants, from ʿ-b-d, servants, slaves, worshippers, as ʿibādihi His servants. Yaqūlū, they say, they should say. Allatī hiya aḥsanu, what is best, most excellent, best, from ḥ-s-n, good, best, as aḥsanu best, as previous aḥsanu best, and ḥasan good, and aḥsantum do good, and iḥsānan excellence. Al-Ṭabarī explains that tell My believing servants to say only what is best, most excellent, best speech, kind, gentle, as qawlan karīman noble word for parents, qawlan maysūran kind word when lack means, and now what is best, most excellent, as best speech, to avoid discord, as Satan seeks to sow discord among them. Al-Qurṭubī says allatī hiya aḥsanu means what is best, most excellent, best speech, kind, gentle, truthful, as best speech prevents discord, Satan seeks discord, so say best.

**Satan Seeks To Sow Discord Among Them**

Inna al-shayṭāna yanzaghu baynahum — indeed Satan sows discord, incites, seeks to sow discord among them, between them. Inna, indeed, surely. Al-shayṭāna from sh-ṭ-n, Satan, Devil, from shaṭana, to be far. Yanzaghu from n-z-gh, to sow discord, incite, provoke, cause discord, from nazagha, to sow discord, incite, provoke, from nazgh, discord, incitement, provocation, as Satan's incitement. Baynahum, between them, among them, among servants, believers. Al-Ṭabarī explains that Satan seeks to sow discord among them, among believing servants, incite, provoke, cause enmity, discord, via evil speech, harsh words, etc., so tell servants to say best to prevent Satan's discord. Al-Rāzī says yanzaghu baynahum indicates Satan seeks to sow discord, incite, provoke, cause enmity, via speech, so saying best prevents his discord.

**Satan Sworn Enemy To Humankind**

Inna al-shayṭāna kāna li-l-insāni ʿaduwwan mubīnan — indeed Satan is to human enemy clear, sworn, manifest enemy. Inna, indeed. Al-shayṭāna, Satan. Kāna, is, was, indicates he is. Li-l-insāni, to human, for human, to humankind, from a-n-s, human, as insān human, ever hasty. ʿAduwwan from ʿ-d-w, enemy, foe, from ʿadā, to be enemy, from ʿaduww, enemy. Mubīnan from b-y-n, clear, manifest, sworn, evident, from bāna, to be clear, manifest, from mubīn, clear, manifest. Al-Ṭabarī explains that Satan is indeed sworn enemy to humankind, clear, manifest enemy, not hidden, as he refused to prostrate to Adam, vowed to take hold of descendants except few, as verse 62-64 will say, and seeks to sow discord, incite, as sworn enemy. Al-Qurṭubī says ʿaduwwan mubīnan means clear, manifest, sworn enemy, evident enmity, not hidden, so beware, say best to prevent his discord.

**Why Say Best To Prevent Satan Discord**

Why say best to prevent Satan discord? Because Satan, Devil, ever ungrateful to Lord, kafūran, ever ungrateful, as verse 27 says, and is sworn enemy to humankind, clear enemy, seeks to sow discord among servants, believers, via evil speech, harsh words, so saying best, most excellent, prevents his discord, as best speech, kind, gentle, truthful, prevents enmity, discord. The sūrah's moral code included honouring parents with noble word, kind word when lack means, now best speech among servants, to prevent Satan discord, as Satan seeks discord.

**Connection To Wasteful Brothers To Devils And No Authority Over Faithful**

Verse 27 said wasteful brothers to devils, Devil ever ungrateful. Verse 65 will say you will have no authority over My faithful servants, sufficient Lord as Guardian. Verse 53 says tell believing servants to say best, Satan seeks to sow discord among them, Satan sworn enemy to humankind. The connection is that wasteful brothers to devils follow Devil, but faithful servants who say best, most excellent, have no authority for Satan over them, Lord Guardian, sufficient, as Satan seeks discord but saying best prevents discord, as faithful servants protected.

**Link To Lower Wing Of Humility And Noble Word And Kind Word**

Verse 23-24 noble word for parents, lower wing of humility out of mercy, verse 28 kind word when lack means, verse 53 best speech among servants to prevent Satan discord, all about good speech: noble word, kind word, best speech, as part of most upright guidance, wisdom, that Qur'an guides to, as Qur'an guides to most upright, including speech, most upright speech.

**Linguistic Note On Ahsanu And Yanzaghu And Aduwwan Mubinan**

Qul from q-w-l, to say. ʿIbād from ʿ-b-d, servants. Yaqūlū from q-w-l, to say. Aḥsan from ḥ-s-n, best, most excellent, from ḥasuna, to be good. Shayṭān from sh-ṭ-n, Satan, Devil. Yanzaghu from n-z-gh, to sow discord, incite, provoke, from nazagha. Bayn from b-y-n, between. Insān from a-n-s, human. ʿAduww from ʿ-d-w, enemy, from ʿadā. Mubīn from b-y-n, clear, manifest, from bāna. The verse's structure — say to My servants they say what is best, indeed Satan sows discord among them, indeed Satan is to human enemy clear — with command to say best, reason Satan sows discord, and statement Satan sworn enemy, shows that saying best prevents Satan discord, as Satan sworn enemy seeks discord, so say best, most excellent, to prevent.

**How This Verse Teaches Best Speech To Prevent Satan Discord**

This verse teaches best speech to prevent Satan discord: tell My believing servants to say only what is best, most excellent, best speech, kind, gentle, truthful, as Satan seeks to sow discord among them, incite, provoke, cause enmity, and Satan is indeed sworn enemy to humankind, clear, manifest enemy, not hidden, so beware, say best to prevent his discord. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should say best, most excellent, best speech, kind, gentle, truthful, to prevent Satan discord, enmity, as Satan seeks to sow discord among servants, believers, via evil speech, harsh words, so saying best, most excellent, prevents, as part of most upright guidance, wisdom, that Qur'an guides to. The practical lesson is to say best, noble word, kind word, best speech, to parents, relatives, poor, traveller, servants, all, to prevent Satan discord, as Satan sworn enemy, clear enemy, seeks discord, but faithful servants who say best have no authority for Satan over them, Lord Guardian sufficient.

**A Reflection On Satan Sworn Enemy**

ʿAduwwan mubīnan — enemy clear, manifest, sworn, evident — indicates Satan's enmity to humankind is clear, manifest, not hidden, sworn, evident, as he refused to prostrate to Adam, said should I prostrate to one created from mud, vowed to take hold of descendants except few, if delayed until Day Judgment, as verse 62 says, and Allah said be gone, whoever follows you Hell ample reward, etc., as verses 63-65 will say, and Satan incites with voice, cavalry, infantry, manipulates wealth and children, promises delusion, but no authority over faithful servants, sufficient Lord as Guardian. The believer who knows Satan sworn enemy, clear enemy, will say best, most excellent, to prevent his discord, will seek protection, will be faithful servant, no authority for Satan, Lord Guardian sufficient, as most upright guidance, wisdom, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving.
