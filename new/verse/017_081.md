## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:81

> **And declare, “The truth has come and falsehood has vanished. Indeed, falsehood is bound to vanish.”**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Truth Has Come And Falsehood Vanished**

Wa-qul jā'a al-ḥaqqu wa-zahaqa al-bāṭilu — and say, truth has come and falsehood has vanished, perished. Wa-qul from q-w-l, to say, declare, command to Prophet to declare. Jā'a from j-y-ʾ, to come, arrive, as jā'a truth has come. Al-ḥaqqu from ḥ-q-q, truth, true, right, reality, from ḥaqq, truth, as ḥaqq truth, as previous ḥaqqa decree becomes true, justified, etc., and ḥaqq truth. Wa-zahaqa from z-h-q, to vanish, perish, disappear, from zahaqa, to vanish, perish, disappear, as zahaqa falsehood vanished, perished. Al-bāṭilu from b-ṭ-l, falsehood, vanity, false, from bāṭil, falsehood, vanity, false, opposite of ḥaqq truth. Al-Ṭabarī explains that declare, truth has come and falsehood has vanished, perished, meaning truth, Islam, Qur'an, revelation, has come, arrived, and falsehood, disbelief, shirk, idolatry, has vanished, perished, disappeared, as truth came, falsehood vanished, as when Prophet entered Mecca, truth came, falsehood vanished, as idols fell, etc. Al-Qurṭubī says jā'a al-ḥaqqu wa-zahaqa al-bāṭilu means truth has come, Islam, Qur'an, and falsehood has vanished, perished, as truth came, falsehood vanished, as divine law, as truth vs falsehood.

**Indeed Falsehood Bound To Vanish**

Inna al-bāṭila kāna zahūqan — indeed falsehood is bound to vanish, ever vanishing, perishing. Inna, indeed, surely. Al-bāṭila, falsehood, vanity, false, from b-ṭ-l. Kāna, is, was, indicates it is. Zahūqan from z-h-q, bound to vanish, ever vanishing, perishing, vanishing, from zahaqa, to vanish, perish, from zahūq, bound to vanish, ever vanishing, perishing, as falsehood bound to vanish, ever vanishing, perishing, not stable, not lasting, as falsehood bound to vanish, as truth stable, lasting. Al-Ṭabarī explains that indeed falsehood is bound to vanish, ever vanishing, perishing, meaning falsehood is bound to vanish, ever vanishing, perishing, not stable, not lasting, as it vanishes, perishes, as truth remains, as divine law, as truth has come and falsehood vanished, indeed falsehood bound to vanish, as falsehood ever vanishing, as truth ever remaining.

**Why Truth Come Falsehood Vanish And Bound To Vanish**

Why truth has come and falsehood vanished, indeed falsehood bound to vanish? Because honourable entrance and exit and supporting authority from Yourself, as previous verse supplication for honourable entrance and exit and supporting authority, as Hijrah, entrance to Medina, exit from Mecca, with truth, sincerity, honourable, and supporting authority from Allah, leads to truth coming, falsehood vanishing, as truth, Islam, Qur'an, revelation, has come, arrived, and falsehood, disbelief, shirk, idolatry, has vanished, perished, disappeared, as when Prophet entered Mecca, truth came, falsehood vanished, idols fell, etc., and indeed falsehood bound to vanish, ever vanishing, perishing, not stable, not lasting, as falsehood bound to vanish, as truth stable, lasting, as divine law, as Our way with messengers no change, you will never find any change in Our way, as truth come falsehood vanish is part of Our way, divine law, no change, as truth has come and falsehood vanished, indeed falsehood bound to vanish, as truth vs falsehood.

**Connection To Honourable Entrance Exit Supporting Authority And Quran Healing Mercy**

Verse 80 say My Lord grant me honourable entrance and honourable exit and give me supporting authority from Yourself, supplication for Hijrah, entrance to Medina, exit from Mecca, with truth, sincerity, honourable, supporting authority from Allah. Verse 81 declare truth has come and falsehood has vanished, indeed falsehood bound to vanish, showing that honourable entrance and exit and supporting authority lead to truth coming, falsehood vanishing, as truth, Islam, Qur'an, has come, falsehood vanished, as when Prophet entered Mecca, truth came, falsehood vanished, idols fell, etc., and indeed falsehood bound to vanish, ever vanishing, as truth stable, lasting. Verse 82 We send down Quran as healing and mercy for believers but it only increases wrongdoers in loss, showing that truth, Qur'an, is healing and mercy for believers, but increases wrongdoers in loss, as truth vs falsehood, healing mercy vs loss.

**Link To Quran Healing Mercy And When Grant Favours Turn Away Arrogantly Lose Hope**

Next verses 82-83 We send down Quran as healing and mercy for believers but only increases wrongdoers in loss, when We grant people favours they turn away acting arrogantly, but when touched with evil they lose all hope, showing that truth, Qur'an, healing mercy for believers, but wrongdoers increase in loss, and when favours granted they turn away arrogantly, but when evil touched lose hope, as human nature, ever ungrateful, etc., and as truth come falsehood vanish, indeed falsehood bound to vanish, as truth, Qur'an, healing mercy, vs falsehood, loss, arrogance, despair.

**Linguistic Note On Jā'a And Haqq And Zahaqa And Batil And Zahuqan**

Qul from q-w-l, to say, declare. Jā'a from j-y-ʾ, to come, arrive, from jā'a. Ḥaqq from ḥ-q-q, truth, true, right, reality, from ḥaqqa, to be true. Zahaqa from z-h-q, to vanish, perish, disappear, from zahaqa. Bāṭil from b-ṭ-l, falsehood, vanity, false, from baṭala, to be false, vain. Zahūq from z-h-q, bound to vanish, ever vanishing, perishing, from zahaqa, intensive form zahūq, bound to vanish. The verse's structure — say truth has come and falsehood has vanished, indeed falsehood is bound to vanish — with declaration truth has come and falsehood vanished and statement falsehood bound to vanish, shows truth vs falsehood, truth coming, falsehood vanishing, falsehood bound to vanish, ever vanishing, as divine law, as truth stable, falsehood unstable, as part of Our way no change.

This verse teaches truth has come and falsehood has vanished, indeed falsehood bound to vanish, ever vanishing, perishing, not stable, not lasting, as truth, Islam, Qur'an, revelation, has come, arrived, and falsehood, disbelief, shirk, idolatry, has vanished, perished, disappeared, as when Prophet entered Mecca, truth came, falsehood vanished, idols fell, etc., and indeed falsehood bound to vanish, ever vanishing, perishing, as falsehood bound to vanish, as truth stable, lasting, as divine law, as Our way with messengers no change, you will never find any change in Our way, as truth come falsehood vanish is part of Our way, divine law, no change, as truth vs falsehood. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should declare truth has come and falsehood has vanished, indeed falsehood bound to vanish, ever vanishing, as truth, Islam, Qur'an, has come, falsehood vanished, and indeed falsehood bound to vanish, ever vanishing, as falsehood unstable, not lasting, as truth stable, lasting, so follow truth, avoid falsehood, as most upright guidance, wisdom, that Qur'an guides to, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, healing mercy, vs wrongdoers loss, etc.
