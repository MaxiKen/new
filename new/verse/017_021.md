## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:21

> **See how We have favoured some over others ˹in this life˺, but the Hereafter is certainly far greater in rank and in favour.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**See How We Favoured Some Over Others In This Life**

Unẓur kayfa faḍḍalnā baʿḍahum ʿalā baʿḍin — see how We favoured some over others. Unẓur from n-ẓ-r, to see, look, consider, observe, as previous chapter said see how they call you names, and as Qur'an often says unẓur, look, consider. Kayfa, how, manner. Faḍḍalnā from f-ḍ-l, to favour, prefer, give bounty, superiority, from same root as faḍl, bounty, favour, grace, as verse 12 seeking bounty of Lord and verse 20 bounty never withheld and verse 21 favour. Baʿḍahum ʿalā baʿḍin, some over others, some above others, in provision, wealth, offspring, numbers, strength, knowledge, as verse 6 aided with wealth and offspring and made more numerous, and verse 20 We provide both former and latter from bounty, former desiring fleeting and latter desiring Hereafter. Al-Ṭabarī explains that see how We favoured some over others in this life, in provision, wealth, children, etc., some have abundant, some limited, as verse 30 says Lord gives abundant or limited provisions to whoever He wills, All-Aware All-Seeing. The difference in worldly favour is visible, observable, test, not hidden, and should be seen with reflection.

**Hereafter Far Greater In Rank And Favour**

Wa-la-l-ākhiratu akbaru darajātin wa-akbaru tafḍīlan — and Hereafter is certainly greater in rank and greater in favour. Wa-la-, and surely, and indeed, emphasis, la for emphasis. Al-ākhiratu, Hereafter, from ā-kh-r, last, latter, opposite of al-ūlā, first, world. Akbaru, greater, bigger, from k-b-r, great, big, from kabura. Darajātin from d-r-j, ranks, degrees, levels, steps, from same root as darajah, rank, degree, step, as Paradise has darajāt, ranks, levels, as hadith says Paradise has one hundred levels. Wa-akbaru tafḍīlan, and greater in favour, preference, superiority, from f-ḍ-l, favour, and tafḍīl, preferring, favouring, superiority, from faḍḍala. Al-Ṭabarī says Hereafter is far greater in rank, degrees, levels, and in favour, preference, meaning difference in Hereafter between people is far greater than difference in worldly life, ranks in Hereafter far higher, more significant, more lasting, than worldly ranks. Al-Rāzī says darajāt refers to ranks in Paradise, levels of reward, and tafḍīlan refers to favour, bounty, reward, superiority, and Hereafter greater in both than worldly differences, worldly difference small compared to Hereafter difference which is immense, eternal.

**Why This Verse Follows Provision And Bounty**

Verse 20 said We provide both former and latter from bounty of Lord, bounty never withheld, both groups provided, former desiring fleeting and latter desiring Hereafter. Verse 21 says see how We favoured some over others in this life, but Hereafter far greater in rank and favour. The connection is that provision from bounty is not equal: some favoured over others in worldly provision, as test, some abundant, some limited, but Hereafter difference far greater, so do not be deceived by worldly favouring, do not envy worldly favouring, focus on Hereafter where ranks and favour far greater, more important. Verse 18-19 contrasted fleeting desirer who gets hastened limited worldly then Hell condemned rejected vs Hereafter desirer whose striving appreciated, mighty reward. Verse 21 explains that worldly favouring is minor, temporary, Hereafter favouring major, lasting, so desire Hereafter, strive accordingly.

**Link To Abundant Or Limited Provision And Wisdom**

Verse 30 will say Lord gives abundant or limited provisions to whoever He wills, All-Aware All-Seeing of servants. Verse 21 previews that: We favoured some over others in this life, some abundant, some limited, but Hereafter greater in rank and favour. The difference in provision is by will and wisdom, not random, and is test, as previous chapter said if Allah were to extend provision for servants they would transgress but He sends down in due measure what He wills, All-Aware All-Seeing. The person who is favoured in world with abundant provision should be grateful, not arrogant like mutraf elite who rebelled when commanded, and who is not favoured, limited provision, should be patient, not despair, both should desire Hereafter where ranks far greater. The favouring some over others in world is part of divine wisdom to test gratitude and patience.

**Linguistic Note On Faḍḍala And Darajat And Tafdil**

Unẓur from n-ẓ-r, to see, look, consider, from naẓara. Kayfa from k-y-f, how. Faḍḍalnā from f-ḍ-l, to favour, prefer, from faḍala, to be superior, to favour, from faḍl, bounty, favour, superiority. Baʿḍ from b-ʿ-ḍ, some, part. Ākhirah from ā-kh-r, Hereafter, last, latter. Akbar from k-b-r, greater, from kabura, to be great. Darajāt from d-r-j, ranks, degrees, levels, from darajah, step, rank, from daraja, to step, to rank. Tafḍīlan from f-ḍ-l, favouring, preference, superiority, from faḍḍala, to prefer, favour. The verse's structure — see how We favoured some over others in this life, but Hereafter greater in rank and greater in favour — with command to see worldly difference and statement that Hereafter greater in two aspects, rank and favour, shows that worldly difference visible but minor compared to Hereafter which is far greater in both rank and favour, eternal.

**How This Verse Teaches Perspective And Not Envy**

This verse teaches perspective and avoidance of envy: see worldly favouring, some over others, but remember Hereafter far greater in rank and favour, so do not envy worldly favouring, do not be arrogant if favoured, do not despair if not favoured. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should not envy worldly favouring, because Hereafter ranks far greater, and should strive for Hereafter ranks, not only worldly, should use worldly favouring, whether abundant or limited, to desire Hereafter and strive accordingly with faith, so that striving appreciated and ranks in Hereafter far greater, mighty reward. The practical lesson is to use worldly favouring as test, to be grateful if abundant, patient if limited, and to focus on Hereafter where rank and favour far greater, eternal.

**A Reflection On Ranks And Favour In Hereafter**

Akbaru darajātin wa-akbaru tafḍīlan — greater in rank and greater in favour — indicates that Hereafter has ranks, degrees, levels, as Paradise has many levels, and favour, bounty, reward, superiority, far greater than worldly, which is limited, temporary. Worldly favouring some over others is limited, temporary, test, while Hereafter favouring is lasting, far greater, based on desire for Hereafter and striving with faith, appreciated striving. The person who desires fleeting alone may be favoured in worldly provision hastened, but Hereafter rank low, Hell condemned rejected; who desires Hereafter and strives has high rank in Hereafter, appreciated striving, mighty reward, far greater rank and favour. The believer who reflects will not be deceived by worldly favouring, will seek Hereafter where rank and favour far greater, and will follow most upright guidance to achieve it.

**Connection To Seeking Bounty And Using It**

Verse 12 said seek bounty of Lord and know number years and calculation, and verse 20 bounty never withheld, but some favoured over others in bounty, provision. The verse reminds that seeking bounty is allowed, even commanded, to seek bounty of Lord, but Hereafter far greater, so seek bounty as means to Hereafter, not as ultimate goal alone. The person who seeks bounty with gratitude and uses it to honor parents, give relatives due, poor, traveller, avoid waste, moderate, will have greater rank in Hereafter, far greater favour. The person who seeks bounty only for fleeting alone, without Hereafter, will have hastened limited worldly then Hell condemned rejected, losing far greater rank and favour in Hereafter.
