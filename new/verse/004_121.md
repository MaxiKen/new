## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:121

> It is they who will have Hell as their home, and they will find no escape from it!

### Expanded Commentary

**The end of delusion**

Those who follow Satan have Hell as their home and no escape. The language answers the false promises of the preceding verses. Satan offered a path that appeared open and liberating, but it ends in confinement. His “guardian” cannot rescue the people who trusted him because he never possessed the authority he pretended to offer.

The warning is not a reason to imagine that every sinner is already condemned. It describes the final home of those who persist in the satanic orientation and die without returning. The living reader is addressed before the door closes: abandon the false promise, repair what can be repaired, and seek the One whose mercy is real.

**The false guardian’s end**

Satan’s promises are exposed by their destination. A person who has followed every appetite may discover that the freedom promised by rebellion has become captivity. The warning is directed to the living, who can still replace a false guardian with worship, truthful companionship, and repentance.
