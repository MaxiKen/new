## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:44

> **The seven heavens, the earth, and all those in them glorify Him. There is not a single thing that does not glorify His praises—but you ˹simply˺ cannot comprehend their glorification. He is indeed Most Forbearing, All-Forgiving.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Seven Heavens, Earth, All Those In Them Glorify Him**

Tusabbiḥu lahu al-samāwātu al-sabʿu wa-al-arḍu wa-man fīhinna — glorify Him the seven heavens and earth and whoever is in them. Tusabbiḥu from s-b-ḥ, to glorify, praise, from sabbaḥa, to glorify, as subḥāna glorified, subḥānahu glorified. Lahu, Him, for Him, to Him. Al-samāwātu al-sabʿu, seven heavens, from s-m-w, heavens, sky, and sabʿ, seven. Wa-al-arḍu, and earth, from a-r-ḍ. Wa-man fīhinna, and whoever is in them, all those in them, from m-n, who, and fī, in. Al-Ṭabarī explains that seven heavens, earth, and all those in them, all creation, angels, humans, jinn, animals, etc., glorify Him, declare His glory, free from imperfection, exalted above claims. Al-Qurṭubī says tusabbiḥu indicates glorification, tasbīh, by all creation, seven heavens, earth, and whoever in them, all glorify Him, as all creation glorifies, praises.

**Not A Single Thing That Does Not Glorify His Praises**

Wa-in min shay'in illā yusabbiḥu bi-ḥamdihi — and there is not a thing except glorifies with His praise. Wa-in, and not, there is not. Min shay'in, a thing, any thing, from sh-y-ʾ, thing. Illā, except. Yusabbiḥu from s-b-ḥ, glorifies, praises, as tusabbiḥu. Bi-ḥamdihi, with His praise, with His hamd, praise, from ḥ-m-d, praise, hamd. Al-Ṭabarī says there is not a single thing, any thing, that does not glorify His praises, meaning every thing, all creation, glorifies Him with His praise, declares glory and praise, even if you do not comprehend. Al-Rāzī says this indicates universal glorification: everything glorifies Him with praise, not only seven heavens, earth, and those in them, but every thing, all creation, glorifies.

**You Cannot Comprehend Their Glorification**

Wa-lākin lā tafqahūna tasbīḥahum — but you do not comprehend, understand their glorification. Wa-lākin, but, however. Lā tafqahūna from f-q-h, to comprehend, understand, have fiqh, from faqiha, to comprehend, understand. Tasbīḥahum from s-b-ḥ, their glorification, tasbīh, from sabbaḥa. Al-Ṭabarī explains that you, humans, do not comprehend, understand their glorification, how they glorify, because their glorification is in ways you do not understand, languages you do not comprehend, as all things glorify but you cannot comprehend. Al-Qurṭubī says lā tafqahūna tasbīḥahum means you do not understand their tasbīh, glorification, because it is not in human language, or because you are heedless, as seven heavens, earth, all things glorify but humans often heedless.

**He Is Most Forbearing All-Forgiving**

Innahu kāna ḥalīman ghafūran — indeed He is Most Forbearing, All-Forgiving. Innahu, indeed He, Allah. Kāna, is, was. Ḥalīman from ḥ-l-m, Most Forbearing, forbearing, clement, from ḥaluma, to be forbearing, from ḥilm, forbearance, clemency. Ghafūran from gh-f-r, All-Forgiving, forgiving, from ghafara, to forgive, as Ghafūr All-Forgiving. Al-Ṭabarī says He is Most Forbearing, All-Forgiving, forbearing, not hastening punishment despite claims, outrageous claims that He has daughters, other gods, etc., and forgiving to those who repent, turn, as awwābīn, constantly turning. Al-Rāzī says ḥalīman ghafūran indicates forbearance and forgiveness, as Allah forbears despite polytheist claims, does not hasten punishment, and forgives those who repent.

**Why Universal Glorification After Glorified Exalted Above Claims**

Verse 43 said Glorified Highly Exalted is He above what they claim, with great exaltation. Verse 44 says seven heavens, earth, all those in them glorify Him, not a thing that does not glorify His praises but you cannot comprehend their glorification, He is Most Forbearing All-Forgiving. The connection is that after stating Allah Glorified Exalted above claims of other gods, daughters, etc., Qur'an says all creation, seven heavens, earth, all those in them, and every thing, glorifies Him with praise, showing that true glorification is by all creation, not that He has daughters, sons, partners, but that He is Glorified by all, exalted above claims. The fact that you cannot comprehend their glorification indicates that glorification is universal but human comprehension limited.

**Link To Night Journey And Signs And Bounty**

Verse 1 opened with Glory be to One Who took servant by night, glorification for Night Journey. Verse 44 says seven heavens, earth, all those in them glorify Him, universal glorification, showing that Night Journey through heavens is part of universal glorification, and that all things glorify, including those seen in Night Journey. The sūrah's theme of signs: night and day signs, shadows, etc., all signs that glorify, as seven heavens, earth, all things glorify Him, sign of monotheism.

**Linguistic Note On Tusabbihu And Hamd And Tafqahuna And Halim Ghafur**

Tusabbiḥu from s-b-ḥ, to glorify, from sabbaḥa. Samāwāt from s-m-w, heavens. Sabʿ from s-b-ʿ, seven. Arḍ from a-r-ḍ, earth. Man fīhinna, whoever in them. Shay' from sh-y-ʾ, thing. Yusabbiḥu from s-b-ḥ, glorifies. Ḥamd from ḥ-m-d, praise. Tafqahūna from f-q-h, to comprehend, understand, from faqiha. Tasbīḥ from s-b-ḥ, glorification. Ḥalīm from ḥ-l-m, Forbearing, from ḥaluma. Ghafūr from gh-f-r, Forgiving, from ghafara. The verse's structure — seven heavens, earth, all those in them glorify Him, not a thing except glorifies with His praise, but you do not comprehend their glorification, indeed He is Forbearing Forgiving — with universal glorification by heavens, earth, all in them, every thing, but human incomprehension, and divine forbearance forgiveness, shows that all creation glorifies, human often fails to comprehend, but Allah forbearing forgiving.

**How This Verse Teaches Universal Glorification And Forbearance**

This verse teaches universal glorification: seven heavens, earth, all those in them, and every thing glorifies Him with praise, but you cannot comprehend their glorification, and He is Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, forbearing despite claims, forgiving to those who repent. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should glorify Allah, join universal glorification, say subḥān Allāh, Glorified is Allah, as all things glorify, and should know that all things glorify but you cannot comprehend how, so be humble, not arrogant, and know Allah Forbearing Forgiving, forbears despite polytheist claims, forgives those who turn. The practical lesson is to glorify Allah often, as all creation does, to be mindful that all things glorify, and to seek forgiveness, as He is Forbearing Forgiving.

**A Reflection On Forbearing Forgiving After Claims**

Ḥalīman ghafūran — Most Forbearing, All-Forgiving — after universal glorification and incomprehension, indicates that despite polytheist claims that Allah has daughters, other gods, etc., which are outrageous, tremendous, Allah is Forbearing, does not hasten punishment, forbears, and Forgiving to those who repent, turn, as awwābīn. The fact that seven heavens, earth, all things glorify Him, but polytheists claim daughters, sons, other gods, shows their claims false, against universal glorification, but Allah forbears, does not hasten, forgives if they repent. The believer who reflects will join universal glorification, glorify Him with praise, comprehend that all things glorify, even if he cannot comprehend how, and will seek forbearance and forgiveness, as He is Most Forbearing All-Forgiving.
