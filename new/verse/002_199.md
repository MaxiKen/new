## Sūrah al-Baqarah 2:199

> Then go forth with the rest of the pilgrims. And seek Allah’s forgiveness. Surely Allah is All-Forgiving, Most Merciful.

**Expanded Commentary**

**The Command to Join the Crowd**

The verse instructs the community to surge onward from the place where the people surge onward, and it adds the command to seek forgiveness. The sentence addresses a group that had held itself apart from the general movement of the pilgrims.

The Book's instruction concerns the outward conduct of the pilgrimage and the attitude that produced the separation. What the reader receives is a command about joining the body of the pilgrims, and the sentence's second clause supplies the disposition with which the journey is to be completed.

**The Group Addressed**

The commentators identified the group as the Ḥums, a confederation of tribes that included the Quraysh and that claimed a particular devotion to the sanctuary and its surroundings. Members of the group would not stand with the other pilgrims at ʿArafāt, which lies outside the sacred precinct, and they would remain at the site within the precinct instead.

The Book's command corrects the practice by instructing them to move with the crowd. What the reader is shown is a distinction that the community had drawn for itself and that the Book removed, and the sentence's wording treats the pilgrims as a single body rather than as groups with different standings.
