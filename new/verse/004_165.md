## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:165

> ˹All were˺ messengers delivering good news and warnings so humanity should have no excuse before Allah after ˹the coming of˺ the messengers. And Allah is Almighty, All-Wise.

**Expanded Commentary**

**Messengers Delivering Good News and Warnings**

The verse states *rusulan mubashshirīna wa mundhirīna* — messengers, apostles, as bearers of good news and warners, givers of glad tidings and warnings. *Mubashshirīn* — bearing good news — and *mundhirīn* — warning — were explained as the two faces of revelation already named: Gardens forever for those who believe and do good where promise is true (4:122) — good news — and Hell without escape for followers of Satan’s delusion (4:121), painful punishment for hypocrites (4:138), humiliating punishment for true disbelievers (4:151), painful punishment for disbelievers among the Book’s People for *ẓulm* and *ribā* (4:161) — warnings. *Rusulan* — messengers — includes those whose stories We told you before and those We did not narrate, and to Moses He spoke directly (4:164), and those given Psalms to David (4:163), and the chain from Noah through Abraham to Jesus (4:163) — all were *mubashshirīn wa mundhirīn*. The sūrah has shown that those who believe accepting all, rejecting none, will be given rewards (4:152), and that those well-grounded believe in what was revealed to you and before (4:162) — good news is for them. The sūrah has shown that *“whoever commits evil will be rewarded accordingly and find no protector”* (4:123) — warning is for him.

**So Humanity Should Have No Excuse Before God After Messengers**

The purpose *li allā yakūna li al-nāsi ʿalā Allāhi ḥujjatun baʿda al-rusuli* — so that there be not for humanity against God an argument, excuse, plea, after the messengers — was explained with *ḥujjah* — argument, proof, excuse — the same *sulṭān mubīn* — clear warrant — given against hostile turners (4:91) and that you give against yourselves if you take disbelievers as allies (4:144) and compelling proof Moses was given (4:153) — here humanity’s *ḥujjah* against Him is removed. *Baʿda al-rusuli* — after the messengers — was read as after the lineage from Noah to you — *“We have sent revelation to you as We sent to Noah”* (4:163) — there is no *“we did not know”* left, because He can remove humanity and bring others (4:133) yet He sent messengers first. Early reports linked this to the sūrah’s *“He wills to make clear, guide to noble ways, turn in mercy”* (4:26) — clarity is via messengers, so excuse is cut. The phrase was linked to *“on Resurrection Day the Messiah will be witness against them”* (4:159) — witnesses are *rusul* against their peoples, as *“We will bring witness from every community and you as witness against yours”* (4:41) — the witness is the *ḥujjah*. Where the hypocrites sought to deceive God while He outwits them (4:142) and argued *we believe in some and disbelieve in some desiring a way between* (4:150) — they claimed a *ḥujjah* of compromise; the verse says none remains.

**And God Is Almighty, All-Wise**

The seal *wa kāna Allāhu ʿAzīzan Ḥakīmā* — And God is Almighty, All-Powerful, All-Wise, was ever — was explained with *ʿAzīz* — Almighty, Mighty — the One who raises Jesus to Himself (4:158) and can remove humanity (4:133) yet sends warners before removing — and *Ḥakīm* — All-Wise — who sends good news and warnings in wisdom, not need. *ʿAzīz Ḥakīm* seals the sending as not weak: the messengers’ *basharah* — good news — and *indhār* — warning — are backed by Almighty who can do as He warns, and Wise who warns where wise to warn, as He is *Qadīr* to pardon though capable (4:149) and *Ghafūr Raḥīm* to reward — good news is mercy from Wise, warning is justice from Mighty. The sūrah has shown that *“He is Self-Sufficient, Praiseworthy”* though you disobey (4:131) — He needs no *ḥujjah* from you, He removes your *ḥujjah*.

**A Verse That Follows Lineage With Purpose**

Placed after *We have sent revelation to you as We sent to Noah… Psalms to David* (4:163), *messengers whose stories We told you and others We did not, and to Moses He spoke directly* (4:164), this verse follows lineage with purpose: all were messengers delivering good news and warnings so that humanity have no excuse after messengers, He is Almighty, All-Wise. The sūrah has traced the demand for a book from heaven and demand greater — visible God — thunderbolt, calf yet forgiven, Mount raised, firm covenant, breaking, rejecting, killing, claiming *ghulf* sealed, denying, slander against Mary, boasting killing Messiah not killed but made to appear, raised, belief before death, forbidding *ṭayyibāt* for *ẓulm* and hindering, taking interest though forbidden, consuming unjustly, painful punishment for disbelievers among them, but grounded among them to great reward — now the *why* of all that sending: so no excuse. The sūrah that asks *“was not God’s earth spacious enough to emigrate?”* (4:97) and promises many havens (4:100) says messengers are the spaciousness of excuse removed, as earth is the spaciousness of refuge opened. For the weak this is clarifying: where you feared rising or turning away and settlement is best (4:128), good news is that settlement pleasing to Him is great reward (4:114), warning is that inclining entirely leaving hanging is Hamm; both were told before excuse remains.

**The Excuse Removed Is the Alliance Sought**

The *ḥujjah* removed — *no excuse before God after messengers* — was linked to *“would you give God clear warrant against yourselves?”* (4:144) — *sulṭān mubīn* — by taking disbelievers as allies instead of believers seeking honour (4:139) — that warrant is the excuse that remains for the hypocrite. Messengers sent as *mubashshirīn wa mundhirīn* remove that warrant’s claim to ignorance: the honour sought among disbelievers was warned against by the very messengers whose revelation lineage includes Moses directly spoken to. The next verse will set the witness that makes that *ḥujjah* stand: yet if you are denied, God bears witness to what He sent down to you — He sent it with His knowledge — angels too bear witness — and God alone suffices as Witness (4:166).
