## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:165

> ˹All were˺ messengers delivering good news and warnings so humanity should have no excuse before Allah after ˹the coming of˺ the messengers. And Allah is Almighty, All-Wise.

### Expanded Commentary

**Messengers remove the excuse**

Messengers bring good news and warnings so humanity has no excuse before Allah after their coming. The verse establishes the moral purpose of revelation: it makes the path intelligible, offers hope for obedience, and warns about the consequence of rejection.

The statement does not erase questions of access, distortion, or capacity. Allah’s justice accounts for what a person actually received and understood. It does mean that once a message is clearly conveyed, indifference is not neutrality. People are responsible for their response.

Allah is Almighty and Wise. His power ensures that messengers can fulfil their task; His wisdom ensures that judgment considers circumstances. Believers should imitate this balance when teaching: convey clearly, warn honestly, and leave final judgment to God.

**The fairness of warning**

The messenger’s arrival makes the moral path visible, but divine judgment still considers access, understanding, and capacity. This guards against both excuses after clear truth and harsh verdicts made by people who know little about another’s circumstances. Convey the message and trust the Wise Judge.
