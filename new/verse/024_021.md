## Sūrah an-Nūr 24:21

> O believers! Do not follow the footsteps of Satan. Whoever follows Satan’s footsteps, then ˹let them know that˺ he surely bids ˹all to˺ immorality and wickedness. Had it not been for Allah’s grace and mercy upon you, none of you would have ever been purified. But Allah purifies whoever He wills. And Allah is All-Hearing, All-Knowing.

**Expanded Commentary**

**The Incremental Strategy of the Adversary: khuṭuwāti al-shayṭān**

The divine warning opens with an urgent address to the faithful: *yā ayyuhā alladhīna āmanū lā tattabiʿū khuṭuwāti al-shayṭān*. The command targets *khuṭuwāt*—the gradual, stealthy footsteps of the demonic enemy. Satan does not invite an upright believer to monstrous crimes in a single, overt leap; he operates through subtle, imperceptible increments. A fleeting glance leads to an unwholesome curiosity, an idle conversation leads to an ear lent to malicious gossip, and a listening ear leads to the enthusiastic dissemination of character assassination.

By warning against the footsteps rather than the ultimate destination, the revelation diagnoses the progressive psychology of spiritual decay. Transgression is an escalator that individuals board through small compromises. The believers who participated in the slander of ʿĀʾishah never intended to wage war against the Prophet's ﷺ household; they merely took one careless step behind the whisperer by repeating a rumor heard in passing. The verse instructs the believer that moral defense must begin at the threshold of the first step, arresting evil before it gathers momentum.

**The Command to Immorality and Wickedness: al-faḥshāʾ wa-al-munkar**

The nature of demonic promptings is exposed without ambiguity: *wa-man yattabiʿ khuṭuwāti al-shayṭāni fa-innahū yaʾmuru bi-al-faḥshāʾi wa-al-munkar*. The text unmasks the true character of the enemy's whispers. In classical Arabic semantics, *al-faḥshāʾ* denotes extreme, shameless transgressions that exceed all moral boundaries, particularly in matters of sexual impropriety and reputational violence. The term *al-munkar* signifies that which sound human nature, uncorrupted intellect, and divine legislation universally reject as repugnant.

Satan cloaks these destructive vices beneath seductive rationalizations. He convinces the gossiper that repeating a rumor is an act of civic concern, an exercise of free inquiry, or a harmless sharing of information. The Qur'an strips away this rhetorical disguise, declaring that every step inspired by the adversary terminates in shamelessness and moral corruption. To follow his guidance is to actively labor for the debasement of the human spirit and the fracture of communal solidarity.

**The Impossibility of Self-Purification: mā zakā minkum min aḥadin abadā**

The verse articulates a humbling theological reality: *wa-lawlā faḍlu Allāhi ʿalaykum wa-raḥmatuhū mā zakā minkum min aḥadin abadā*. The negation is absolute and sweeping. Were it not for divine grace and mercy, not a single human being would ever achieve moral purity or spiritual salvation. Human beings are inherently vulnerable to cognitive delusion, emotional instability, and the whisperings of egoism and desire.

Left entirely to their own devices, human societies inevitably descend into ethical barbarism and mutual exploitation. Purity of heart, verbal discipline, and social justice are not self-generated human accomplishments; they are gifts bestowed through divine revelation, prophetic education, and continuous spiritual grace. By reminding the community of this foundational truth, the verse shatters spiritual pride, ensuring that even the most righteous believers recognize their absolute dependence upon divine assistance for every measure of virtue they possess.

**Divine Selection in Spiritual Cleansing: Allāha yuzakkī man yashāʾ**

The text clarifies the ultimate source of spiritual elevation: *wa-lākinna Allāha yuzakkī man yashāʾ*, but Allah purifies whom He wills. Purification (*tazkiyah*) is an act of divine sovereignty. While human beings are commanded to exert moral effort, exercise vigilance, and seek forgiveness, the actual transformation of the human heart is a miracle wrought by the Creator.

God cleanses those who prepare their souls to receive divine light, turning their contrition into lasting righteousness. In the immediate historical context, this divine purification was vividly manifested in the redemption of the sincere believers who had stumbled during the crisis of the slander. When they repented, endured their statutory discipline, and sought divine pardon, God cleansed their records and restored their spiritual dignity. Divine purification demonstrates that no human error is beyond the restorative power of divine grace.

**The Witness of Divine Omnipresence: Allāhu Samīʿun ʿAlīm**

The verse concludes with two profound divine names: *wa-Allāhu samīʿun ʿalīm*, and Allah is All-Hearing, All-Knowing. God is *Samīʿ*—All-Hearing of every whispered word, every passing rumor, every subtle inflection of mockery, and every private sigh of the wounded innocent. He is *ʿAlīm*—All-Knowing of every secret motivation, every subconscious jealousy, and every demonic suggestion that crosses the human mind.

Connecting the warning against Satan to these divine attributes introduces an inescapable spiritual awareness into the daily life of the believer. When an individual realizes that their private conversations and internal thoughts are directly observed by the All-Hearing, All-Knowing Lord, careless speech becomes terrifying. This spiritual consciousness acts as a continuous shield, empowering the servant to reject the footsteps of the adversary long before an unwholesome thought manifests upon the tongue.

**The Contextual Pivot from Scandal to Spiritual Hygiene**

Positioned at the conclusion of the direct historical discourse on the Grand Slander, verse 21 serves as a profound architectural transition. It elevates a specific historical scandal into an eternal manual of spiritual hygiene for the entire human race. The crisis of ʿĀʾishah was not an accident; it was a demonic campaign designed to fracture the early community through informational warfare.

By analyzing the mechanics of that failure, the verse equips believers in every era with the cognitive tools required to identify and resist similar traps. Whether confronting domestic whispers, political propaganda, or cultural decadence, the believer is taught to trace the subtle footsteps back to their demonic origin. Moral integrity requires relentless vigilance at the very beginning of the path, ensuring that the community walks securely in the radiant light of divine guidance.
