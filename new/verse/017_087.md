## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:87

> **had it not been for the mercy of your Lord. Indeed, His favour upon you is immense.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Had It Not Been For Mercy Of Your Lord**

Illā raḥmatan min rabbika — except mercy from your Lord, as mercy from your Lord, except as mercy from your Lord. Illā, except, but, as exception, except mercy, but as mercy. Raḥmatan from r-ḥ-m, mercy, as raḥmah mercy, as mercy, as ever Merciful to you, as He is ever Merciful to you, as verse 66 says, and as Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, etc., and as mercy from your Lord, as mercy, as exception, except mercy from your Lord, meaning revelation not taken away except as mercy? Actually previous verse if We willed We could have taken away what We revealed to you, then you would find none to guarantee return from Us, had it not been for mercy of your Lord, meaning it was not taken away except as mercy? Actually illā raḥmatan min rabbika means except as mercy from your Lord, but as mercy from your Lord, meaning revelation remains, not taken away, as mercy from Lord, as favour immense, as mercy, as exception, except mercy, meaning it was not taken away, but remains as mercy from Lord. Min rabbika, from your Lord, from your Lord, from r-b-b, Lord, as from your Lord, as mercy from your Lord. Al-Ṭabarī explains that had it not been for mercy of your Lord, meaning revelation, Qur'an, remains, not taken away, as mercy from your Lord, as favour immense, as mercy, as exception, except mercy from your Lord, meaning it remains as mercy, not taken away, as mercy, as favour immense.

**Indeed His Favour Upon You Immense**

Inna faḍlahu kāna ʿalayka kabīran — indeed His favour, bounty, grace upon you is great, immense, tremendous. Inna, indeed, surely. Faḍlahu from f-ḍ-l, favour, bounty, grace, bounty, favour, as faḍl bounty, favour, as faḍl, bounty, as bounty never withheld, some favoured over others, provision, etc., and faḍlahu His favour, bounty, grace upon you, O Prophet. Kāna, is, was, indicates it is. ʿAlayka, upon you, over you, to you, O Prophet. Kabīran from k-b-r, great, immense, tremendous, big, from kabura, to be great, as kabīran great, immense, tremendous, as His favour upon you immense, great, tremendous, as favour immense, great, tremendous, as revelation, Qur'an, wisdom, most upright, Night Journey, etc., all favour immense, great, tremendous, from Lord, as mercy, etc. Al-Ṭabarī explains that indeed His favour, bounty, grace upon you, O Prophet, is immense, great, tremendous, meaning Allah's favour, bounty, grace upon Prophet Muhammad is immense, great, tremendous, as revelation, Qur'an, wisdom, most upright, Night Journey, station of praise, honourable entrance and exit, supporting authority, truth come falsehood vanished, healing mercy, etc., all favour immense, great, tremendous, as mercy, etc.

**Why Mercy Of Lord And Favour Immense After Taking Away Revelation Warning**

Why had it not been for mercy of your Lord, indeed His favour upon you immense, after if We willed We could have taken away what We revealed to you, then you would find none to guarantee return from Us? Because revelation, Qur'an, is favour immense, mercy, from Lord, not to be taken for granted, as Allah able to take away if willed, remove, as He is All-Powerful, All-Aware All-Seeing, etc., and if taken away, no guarantor, keeper, guardian, to guarantee return from Us, no one to bring back, as only Allah has power, as those claimed besides Him have no power, as verse 56 says, no power, so revelation remains, not taken away, as mercy from Lord, as exception, except mercy from your Lord, meaning it remains as mercy, not taken away, as mercy, as favour immense, great, tremendous, indeed His favour upon you immense, great, tremendous, as revelation, Qur'an, wisdom, most upright, Night Journey, station of praise, etc., all favour immense, great, tremendous, as mercy, etc., not to be taken for granted, should be grateful, follow most upright guidance, wisdom, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, etc.

**Connection To Spirit From Command And Little Knowledge And If Willed Could Take Away And Human Jinn Cannot Produce Equal**

Verse 85 spirit from command of Lord, you have been given but little knowledge, little knowledge, limited, Lord knows best, etc. Verse 86 if We willed We could have taken away what We revealed to you, then you would find none to guarantee return from Us, power to take away revelation, no guarantor. Verse 87 had it not been for mercy of your Lord, indeed His favour upon you immense, mercy, favour immense, revelation remains as mercy, favour immense, not taken away, as mercy, favour immense, and verse 88 if all humans and jinn came together to produce equivalent of Quran they could not produce equal, no matter how they supported each other, showing Quran's inimitability, miraculous, as healing mercy, truth, vs falsehood bound to vanish, and humans and jinn cannot produce equal, even if supported, as Quran inimitable, as revelation, favour immense, mercy, etc., and as spirit from command, little knowledge, but revelation favour immense, etc.

**Link To Quran Healing Mercy And Truth Come Falsehood Vanish And Favour And Mercy**

Verse 82 Quran healing mercy for believers but increases wrongdoers in loss, verse 81 truth come falsehood vanish, indeed falsehood bound to vanish, verse 87 mercy of Lord, favour immense, showing Quran healing mercy, truth, mercy, favour immense, as healing mercy, truth, mercy, favour immense, vs falsehood bound to vanish, loss, etc., and as mercy, favour immense, not to be taken for granted, should be grateful, follow most upright, wisdom, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, etc.

**Linguistic Note On Rahmatan And Fadl And Kabiran**

Raḥmah from r-ḥ-m, mercy, from raḥima, to have mercy. Rabb from r-b-b, Lord. Faḍl from f-ḍ-l, favour, bounty, grace, from faḍala, to favour, be superior. Kabīr from k-b-r, great, immense, tremendous, from kabura, to be great. The verse's structure — except mercy from your Lord, indeed His favour upon you is great — with exception mercy from Lord and statement His favour upon you immense, shows that revelation remains, not taken away, as mercy from Lord, as favour immense, great, tremendous, not to be taken for granted, as mercy, favour immense.

**How This Verse Teaches Mercy And Favour Immense**

This verse teaches mercy of Lord and favour immense: had it not been for mercy of your Lord, indeed His favour, bounty, grace upon you, O Prophet, is immense, great, tremendous, meaning revelation, Qur'an, remains, not taken away, as mercy from Lord, as favour immense, great, tremendous, as revelation, Qur'an, wisdom, most upright, Night Journey, station of praise, honourable entrance and exit, supporting authority, truth come falsehood vanished, healing mercy, etc., all favour immense, great, tremendous, as mercy, etc., not to be taken for granted, should be grateful, follow most upright guidance, wisdom, that Qur'an guides to, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, healing mercy, etc., while wrongdoers increase in loss, etc. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should know revelation, Qur'an, is mercy from Lord, favour immense, great, tremendous, not taken away, remains as mercy, favour immense, should be grateful, follow most upright, wisdom, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, etc., while wrongdoers increase in loss, etc., as truth vs falsehood, healing mercy vs loss, etc.
