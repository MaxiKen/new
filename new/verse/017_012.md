## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:12

> **We made the day and night as two signs. So We made the sign of the night devoid of light, and We made the sign of the day ˹perfectly˺ bright, so that you may seek the bounty of your Lord and know the number of years and calculation ˹of time˺. And We have explained everything in detail.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Day And Night As Two Signs**

Wa-jaʿalnā al-layla wa-l-nahāra āyatayni — and We made night and day as two signs. Jaʿalnā from j-ʿ-l, to make. Al-layla, night, and al-nahāra, day, as two signs, āyatayn, dual of āyah, sign, proof. Al-Ṭabarī explains that night and day are two signs of Allah's power, creation, and wisdom, as previous chapter said He subjected for your benefit night and day, sun and moon, stars by His command, signs for those who understand. Night and day as signs indicate they point to Creator, show His power to alternate, and serve human needs. The dual āyatayni emphasizes they are two distinct signs that work together.

**Sign Of Night Devoid Of Light, Sign Of Day Bright**

Fa-maḥawnā āyata al-layli wa-jaʿalnā āyata al-nahāri mubṣiratan — so We made sign of night devoid of light, erased, and made sign of day bright, giving sight. Maḥawnā from m-ḥ-w, to erase, blot out, make devoid, from same root as maḥw, erasure. Āyata al-layl, sign of night. Mubṣiratan, bright, giving sight, making visible, from b-ṣ-r, to see, to be bright. Al-Ṭabarī says We made night dark, devoid of light, so that you rest, and day bright so that you see and seek bounty. The erasure of night's light and making day bright are deliberate acts that show power and wisdom: night for rest and concealment, day for work and seeking bounty. Al-Rāzī explains that maḥw of night indicates that night originally could have been bright but was made dark as sign, and day made bright as sign, showing that both darkness and light are created and controlled.

**Seeking Bounty And Knowing Years And Calculation**

Li-tabtaghū faḍlan min rabbikum wa-li-taʿlamū ʿadada al-sinīna wa-l-ḥisāb — so that you may seek bounty from Lord and know number of years and calculation. Li-tabtaghū from b-gh-y, to seek, desire. Faḍlan, bounty, provision, from f-ḍ-l, bounty. Min rabbikum, from Lord. Wa-li-taʿlamū, and to know, from ʿ-l-m. ʿAdada al-sinīna, number of years, from ʿ-d-d and s-n-w, years. Wa-l-ḥisāb, and calculation, reckoning, from ḥ-s-b. Al-Ṭabarī says day bright so that you may seek bounty via work, trade, travel, and night and day alternation allows you to know number of years and calculation of time, months, seasons, etc. Al-Qurṭubī notes that knowing years and calculation is essential for religious and worldly affairs: fasting, pilgrimage, contracts, etc., all depend on knowing time, which depends on night and day and sun and moon. The signs therefore serve practical purposes: seeking bounty and knowing time.

**We Explained Everything In Detail**

Wa-kulla shay'in faṣṣalnāhu tafṣīlan — and everything We explained in detail, with detailed explanation. Kulla shay'in, everything, all things. Faṣṣalnāhu from f-ṣ-l, to explain in detail, separate, clarify, from same root as tafṣīl, detailed explanation. Tafṣīlan, in detail, with emphasis, detailed explanation. Al-Ṭabarī says We explained everything in detail, meaning We made clear in Qur'an everything needed for guidance, including signs of night and day, rulings, stories, etc. Al-Rāzī says faṣṣalnāhu tafṣīlan indicates that Qur'an explains everything in detail, not vaguely, so that people can seek bounty and know years and calculation and follow most upright way. The phrase appears in other verses to indicate Qur'an's detailed explanation, as in 6:126 and 17:12.

**Why Night And Day Are Signs**

Night and day are signs because they show power, wisdom, and mercy: power to create and alternate, wisdom to make one dark for rest and one bright for work, mercy to allow seeking bounty and knowing time. Previous chapter said night and day subjected for your benefit, signs for those who understand. This verse adds that they are two signs, one erased, one bright, for seeking bounty and knowing calculation, and everything explained in detail. The sūrah's theme of signs appears: shadows, living creatures, angels, night and day, all signs that should lead to submission and gratitude.

**Connection To Haste**

Previous verse said human prays for evil as for good, is ever hasty. This verse says We made night and day as two signs, made night devoid and day bright so you may seek bounty and know years and calculation, and explained everything in detail. The connection is that haste leads to praying for evil and to not considering signs; reflection on night and day as signs with detailed explanation cures haste by showing that time is measured, bounty is to be sought in day, rest at night, and everything explained, so there is no need to hasten. The person who knows number of years and calculation understands that everything has appointed term, as previous chapter said He delays for appointed term and when time arrives cannot be delayed or advanced, so he will not hasten.

**Linguistic Note On Maḥw And Mubṣir**

Jaʿalnā from j-ʿ-l, to make. Layl from l-y-l, night. Nahār from n-h-r, day. Āyatayn dual of āyah, sign. Maḥawnā from m-ḥ-w, to erase, blot out. Āyata al-layl, sign of night. Mubṣiratan from b-ṣ-r, to see, bright, giving sight, meaning day makes you see, or day is visible. Li-tabtaghū from b-gh-y, to seek. Faḍlan from f-ḍ-l, bounty. ʿAdada from ʿ-d-d, number. Sinīna from s-n-w, years. Ḥisāb from ḥ-s-b, calculation. Kulla shay'in, everything, from k-l-l and sh-y-ʾ. Faṣṣalnāhu from f-ṣ-l, to detail. Tafṣīlan, in detail, from same root. The verse's structure — We made night and day two signs, We erased night sign and made day sign bright so you may seek bounty and know years and calculation, everything We explained in detail — with creation, purpose, and explanation, shows that signs are made for human benefit and are explained in detail to guide to most upright.

**How This Verse Teaches Gratitude And Time Management**

This verse teaches gratitude for night and day as signs and for detailed explanation, and teaches time management: night for rest, day for seeking bounty, and knowing years and calculation for organizing affairs. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should use night for rest and worship, day for seeking bounty with gratitude, and should know times for religious duties via calculation. The verse also teaches that everything explained in detail in Qur'an, so follow its guidance to most upright to receive mighty reward and avoid painful punishment. The practical lesson is to reflect on night and day as signs, to seek bounty in day with trust, to know calculation of time for duties, and to follow detailed explanation that guides to most upright.
