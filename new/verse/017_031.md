## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:31

> **Do not kill your children for fear of poverty. We provide for them and for you. Surely killing them is a heinous sin.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Do Not Kill Children For Fear Of Poverty**

Wa-lā taqtulū awlādakum khashyata imlāqin — and do not kill your children fearing poverty, destitution. Wa-lā, and not, prohibition, strong. Taqtulū from q-t-l, to kill, slay, from qatala, to kill. Awlādakum from w-l-d, children, offspring, plural of walad, child, from walada, to beget, to bear. Khashyata from kh-sh-y, fear, apprehension, dread, from khashiya, to fear, dread. Imlāqin from m-l-q, poverty, destitution, need, from amlaqa, to be poor, destitute, from imlāq, poverty, destitution. Al-Ṭabarī explains that do not kill your children for fear of poverty, as Arabs in jāhiliyyah killed children, especially daughters, for fear of poverty, destitution, not being able to provide, as they buried daughters alive, killed sons fearing they would eat with them. The prohibition includes killing children for fear of poverty, whether already born or unborn, male or female, via infanticide, abortion, neglect, as fear of poverty leads to killing, which is heinous sin, great sin. Al-Qurṭubī says this includes abortion for fear of poverty, infanticide, as Arabs killed children fearing poverty, and Qur'an prohibits, as provision from Allah, not from parents alone.

**We Provide For Them And For You**

Naḥnu narzuquhum wa-iyyākum — We provide for them and for you. Naḥnu, We, Allah, majestic We. Narzuquhum from r-z-q, to provide, provision, sustenance, to give rizq, from razaqa, to provide, as verse 30 yabsuṭu al-rizqa, extends provision, gives abundant or limited. Hum, them, children, offspring. Wa-iyyākum, and you, parents, you, from iyyā, you, emphatic you. Al-Ṭabarī says We provide for them and for you, meaning Allah provides for children and for parents, provision not from parents alone but from Allah who gives abundant or limited as He wills, All-Aware All-Seeing, bounty never withheld, some favoured over others, but provision from Allah. Therefore fear of poverty is unfounded, as provision from Allah, not from parents, and Allah provides for children and parents, both, independent. Al-Rāzī says naḥnu narzuquhum wa-iyyākum indicates that provision for children is from Allah, not parents, so do not kill them fearing poverty, as Allah provides for them and for you, both, and fearing poverty indicates lack of trust in Allah's provision, lack of tawakkul, distrust in bounty never withheld, that is from bounty of Lord.

**Surely Killing Them Is Heinous Sin**

Inna qatlahum kāna khiṭ'an kabīran — indeed killing them is great sin, heinous sin, grave error, tremendous sin. Inna, indeed, surely, emphasis. Qatlahum from q-t-l, killing them, from qatala, killing. Kāna, is, was, indicates it is. Khiṭ'an from kh-ṭ-ʾ, sin, error, grave sin, great error, from khaṭi'a, to sin, err, from khiṭ', sin, error, grave, from same root as khaṭa', sin, error. Kabīran from k-b-r, great, big, heinous, grave, tremendous, from kabura, to be great, as akbar greater, far greater. Al-Ṭabarī says killing children is heinous sin, great sin, grave error, not small, minor, but great, heinous, because it is killing innocent life made sacred by Allah except with legal right, and because it is due to fear of poverty, distrust in Allah's provision, and because child has right to life, innocent. Al-Qurṭubī says khiṭ'an kabīran means great sin, heinous, grave, tremendous, as killing children is among greatest sins, major sins, as hadith says greatest sins include shirk, killing child for fear he will eat with you, disobeying parents, etc., indicating gravity.

**Why Fear Of Poverty Leads To Killing And Distrust**

Why fear of poverty leads to killing? Because provision abundant or limited by will, and some favoured over others in this life, and bounty never withheld, but human ever hasty, ʿajūlan, ever hasty, prays for evil as for good, desires fleeting, fears poverty, so kills children to avoid poverty, to avoid burden, to avoid sharing provision. The prohibition addresses root cause: fear of poverty, imlāq, which is due to lack of trust in Allah who provides for them and for you, who gives abundant or limited with awareness, All-Aware All-Seeing, who provides both former and latter from bounty, bounty never withheld. The verse corrects by saying We provide for them and for you, so do not fear poverty, trust provision, do not kill, be grateful, not ungrateful like Devil ever ungrateful. Fear of poverty is from Satan who promises poverty, as Qur'an says Satan promises you poverty and commands shameful deeds.

**Connection To Moderation And Provision And Giving Due**

Verses 26-30 taught giving due to relatives, poor, traveller, not spending wastefully, wasteful brothers to devils, Devil ever ungrateful, if must turn down due to lack while hoping for bounty give kind word, do not be tight-fisted nor openhanded, Lord gives abundant or limited provisions, All-Aware All-Seeing. Verse 31 says do not kill children for fear of poverty, We provide for them and for you, killing is heinous sin. The connection is that fear of poverty that leads to withholding due, tight-fisted, miserly, or to killing children, is addressed by reminding that provision from Allah, abundant or limited by will, with awareness and sight, and that He provides for children and parents, so do not kill for fear of poverty, do not be tight-fisted, give due, be moderate, trust bounty never withheld. The verse links provision to protection of children.

**Link To Protecting Life And Orphan Wealth And Measuring**

Next verses will say do not kill human life made sacred except with legal right, if killed unjustly heir given authority but do not exceed limits for already supported, and do not come near wealth of orphan unless intending to enhance it until they attain maturity, honour pledges accountable, give full when measure and weigh with even balance fairest best in end, do not follow without sure knowledge hearing sight intellect accountable, do not walk arrogantly you cannot crack earth nor reach mountains height, violation detestable to Lord, this is part of wisdom revealed, do not set up other god or cast into Hell blameworthy rejected. Verse 31 about not killing children is part of series of prohibitions protecting life and wealth: children, adultery, life sacred, orphan wealth, measuring, etc., all most upright, wisdom, detestable if violated.

**Linguistic Note On Imlaq And Khit' And Rizq**

Taqtulū from q-t-l, to kill. Awlād from w-l-d, children. Khashyah from kh-sh-y, fear. Imlāq from m-l-q, poverty, destitution, from amlaqa, to be poor. Narzuqu from r-z-q, to provide, from razaqa. Qatlahum from q-t-l, killing them. Khiṭ' from kh-ṭ-ʾ, sin, error, from khaṭi'a, to sin. Kabīr from k-b-r, great, heinous, grave. The verse's structure — do not kill children fearing poverty, We provide for them and for you, indeed killing them is great sin — with prohibition, reason of provision for them and you, and statement of gravity great sin, shows that killing children for fear of poverty is prohibited, provision from Allah for both, and sin great, heinous, grave, not minor, so avoid.

**How This Verse Teaches Trust And Protection**

This verse teaches trust in Allah's provision and protection of children: do not kill children for fear of poverty, because We provide for them and for you, provision from Allah who gives abundant or limited with awareness, All-Aware All-Seeing, bounty never withheld, some favoured over others but Hereafter far greater, so trust, do not fear poverty to point of killing, withholding, being tight-fisted. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should trust Allah provides for children and parents, should not kill children for fear of poverty, whether via infanticide, abortion, neglect, etc., and should know killing them is heinous sin, great sin, grave error, major sin. The practical lesson is to provide for children, trust provision, give due to relatives, poor, traveller, be moderate, not tight-fisted nor openhanded, and protect children's life, as most upright guidance, wisdom, part of appreciated striving, mighty reward, avoiding painful punishment.
