## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:139

> who choose disbelievers as allies instead of the believers. Do they seek honour and power through that company? Surely all honour and power belongs to Allah.

**Expanded Commentary**

**Who Choose Disbelievers as Allies Instead of Believers**

The verse states *alladhīna yattakhidhūna al-kāfirīna awliyāʾa min dūni al-muʾminīna* — those who take disbelievers as allies, guardians, close protectors instead, apart from, the believers. *Yattakhidhūna awliyāʾ* — to take as allies — was explained with *walī* — guardian, protector, helper — the same *walī* God alone suffices as (4:45), the help the oppressed cry for from grace (4:75), the trustee who suffices (4:132). *Min dūni al-muʾminīn* — instead of believers, apart from believers, short of believers — was read as choosing the other company while believers are available, as earlier *“do not take them as allies unless they emigrate”* (4:89) where alliance was conditioned on emigration, and *“do not take disbelievers as allies instead of believers, would you give God proof against yourselves?”* (4:144) where alliance is warning. The sūrah has traced *awliyāʾ* from the lagger who had no bond — *ka an lam takun baynakum wa baynahu mawaddah* — as if no affection between you (4:73) — to the double safety seekers (4:91), to the treaty people excepted (4:90), to Satan taken as guardian instead of Him (4:119); here the *awliyāʾ* taken are disbelievers instead of believers, for the hypocrites who oscillate (4:137, 4:143) the natural alliance is the side that promises *ʿizzah* without *qisṭ*.

**Do They Seek Honour and Power Through That Company?**

The question *ayabtaghūna ʿindahumu al-ʿizzata* — do they seek, desire, pursue honour, might, power, *ʿizzah*, at their side, with them, through that company? — was explained with *ʿizzah* — honour, might, power, invincibility — the same *ʿizzah* Satan rejected when cursed (4:118) and claimed as share. *Abtaghā ʿindahum* — to seek at their side, with them — was read as seeking honour where honour is not: with those who disbelieve, whose schemes are weak (4:76) and whose abode is Hell without escape (4:121) versus Gardens true (4:122). The sūrah has shown that honour was claimed by *jibt* and *ṭāghūt* believers who declared disbelievers better guided (4:51) and who wish you deviate (4:44); to seek *ʿizzah* among disbelievers instead of believers is to repeat that evaluative reversal — to call weakness honour. Early reports linked the verse to hypocrites who cultivated Quraysh for protection and status in Madinah while claiming belief; the question was read as exposing the transaction: alliance for honour where alliance for God was available as Garden and as self-sufficient ownership (4:131).

**Surely All Honour and Power Belongs to God**

The answer *fa inna al-ʿizzata li Allāhi jamīʿā* — then surely all, entirely, honour and power belongs to God — was explained with *jamīʿan* — entirely, all together — the same totality as *to Him belongs whatever is in heavens and earth* (4:126, 4:131) repeated thrice, and as *with Him are both rewards* (4:134). *Al-ʿizzata li Allāhi* — honour belongs to Him — was read as that *ʿizzah* is not transferred by taking allies; it is His to give to whom He wills — *“you, the best *dīn* submits face and follows Abraham whom He took as friend”* (4:125) — friendship with the Owner of honour is honour. The sūrah has said that God suffices as Guardian and Helper (4:45) and suffices as Trustee (4:132) and is Self-Sufficient, Praiseworthy (4:131); to seek *ʿizzah* beside Him is to seek the encompassed (4:126) among the encompassed. The phrase was linked to *“do they seek honour through that company? Surely all honour belongs to Him”* as the sūrah’s ownership applied to honour: as all in heavens and earth belong to Him, all honour belongs to Him, so the seeker who takes disbelievers as allies seeks at the side of those who own nothing.

**A Verse That Names the Transaction Behind Oscillation**

Placed after *give good news of painful punishment to hypocrites* (4:138), this verse names the transaction behind oscillation: they believed then disbelieved then believed then increased (4:137), torn between neither believers nor disbelievers (4:143) because they sought *ʿizzah* where alliance seemed to give it. The sūrah has shown that whoever defies the Messenger after guidance and follows other than believers’ path is left to what he chose (4:115) — choosing disbelievers as allies is that following — and that associating is far straying (4:116) — seeking *ʿizzah* among associators is that straying’s economy. Where the sūrah says *stand firm for justice as witnesses for God though against yourselves* (4:135) not letting desires deviate, seeking honour from wrong company is the desire that deviates: to witness for disbelievers to gain *ʿizzah* instead of witnessing for God though against yourselves is the precise inversion. For the weak this is steadying: honour is not with the company that wishes you deviate (4:27), nor with Satan’s delusion promise (4:120), but with Him whose earth is spacious (4:100) and whose promise ever true (4:122).

**Honour That Is Entirely His Leaves No Honour With Them**

The word *jamīʿan* — entirely, all together — was explained to leave no remainder with those chosen as allies: if all honour is His, none is at their side to give. The sūrah has shown that the emigrant finds many havens from Him (4:100), the doer of good male or female enters Paradise not wronged a speck (4:124) because grace is His, the best faith is submission to Him (4:125) — honour is that friendship. To choose disbelievers as *awliyāʾ* instead of believers is therefore not a neutral social choice but a theological claim that honour is elsewhere, which the ownership verses deny: *to Him belongs whatever is in heavens and earth* — honour included. The next verse will show the company that seeks such honour in sitting: when you hear revelations denied or ridiculed do not sit in that company unless they speak other talk, else you are like them, and He will gather hypocrites and disbelievers together in Hell (4:140).
