## Sūrah an-Nūr 24:24

> on the Day their tongues, hands, and feet will testify against them for what they used to do.

**Expanded Commentary**

**The Eschatological Exposure of Bodily Organs**

The revelation unveils a terrifying scene of eschatological accountability: *yawma tashhadu ʿalayhim alsinatuhum wa-aydīhim wa-arjuluhum bimā kānū yaʿmalūn*. In this temporary worldly life, the human ego exercises executive control over the physical faculties, deploying them to achieve selfish desires, advance social ambitions, and formulate deceptive words. The sinner uses the tongue to lie, the hands to write malicious gossip, and the feet to walk toward conspiratorial assemblies.

On the Day of Resurrection, that executive illusion is violently shattered. The physical organs of the body are emancipated from the conscious control of the sinner and endowed with independent speech by the divine command. The very faculties that served as the instruments of sin transform into uncompromising prosecution witnesses, testifying against their owner before the supreme judicial assembly of the Creator, laying bare every hidden crime before the entire cosmos with absolute precision, total disclosure, and undeniable clarity, leaving no room for excuses.

**The Paradox of the Testifying Tongue**

A profound and terrifying paradox defines the first witness: *tashhadu ʿalayhim alsinatuhum*, their own tongues will testify against them. In this world, the tongue is the principal weapon of the slanderer, an agile muscle capable of formulating eloquent excuses, feigning injured innocence, and weaving intricate webs of deception to deflect blame onto others.

On the Day of Judgment, all rhetorical agency is stripped away from the ego. The tongue is compelled by divine power to articulate the naked truth of its crimes. It can no longer equivocate, flatter, or conceal. The tongue recounts every slanderous whisper, every malicious dinner conversation, every fabricated rumor, and every cruel laugh it ever uttered in the private chambers of the world. The slanderer stands helplessly trapped within their own body, forced to listen as their own voice proclaims their eternal damnation, converting their former weapon into their executioner before the court of God, witnessing against itself.

**The Witness of the Hands and Feet**

The testimony expands to encompass the non-vocal limbs: *wa-aydīhim wa-arjuluhum*, and their hands and their feet. The hands testify to the physical actions they performed in the service of defamation: the defamatory letters they penned, the financial transactions they executed to fund slander campaigns, the mocking physical gestures they made to humiliate the innocent, and the doors they knocked upon to spread scandal.

The feet testify to every journey undertaken in the service of falsehood: the steps taken toward gatherings of gossip, the paths trodden to spy upon private domestic sanctuaries, and the physical presence maintained in assemblies where the honor of the righteous was shredded. Every cell, muscle, and nerve of the human body bears an indelible empirical record of moral agency, preserved by the angels and unlocked by the All-Hearing, All-Seeing Creator on the Day of Truth, leaving no room for denial, evasion, or obfuscation.

**Parallel Qur'anic Accounts of Bodily Testimony**

This striking imagery of physical testimony recurs across several pivotal passages of the Qur'an, illustrating a unified eschatological doctrine. In Sūrah Yā-Sīn, God describes the sealing of speech: *“On this Day We will seal their mouths, their hands will speak to Us, and their feet will testify to what they used to earn”* (36:65). In Sūrah Fuṣṣilat, the resurrected confront their own bodies in sheer astonishment: *“‘Why did you testify against us?’ They will say, ‘Allah made us speak—the One Who makes everything speak’”* (41:21).

These parallel revelations demonstrate that material creation is neither inert nor indifferent to moral law. Matter possesses an intrinsic consciousness granted by its Creator, quietly recording every touch, sound, and movement. When the veil of worldly illusion is lifted, physical reality naturally aligns itself with transcendent justice, repudiating the corrupt soul that forced it into the service of wickedness, establishing cosmic harmony beneath divine sovereignty throughout the universe and testifying to divine truth.

**The Dissolution of Pretense and Perjury**

Reflecting upon the psychological terror of this moment reveals why the Qur'an confronts humanity with this eschatological reality. In earthly courtrooms, corrupt individuals frequently manipulate procedural rules, produce bribed witnesses, or exploit the evidentiary thresholds of human law to escape conviction, taking pride in their legal impunity.

On the Day of Resurrection, all earthly procedural loopholes completely evaporate. The human being is stripped naked of all rhetoric, prestige, and legal defense. Perjury becomes physically impossible because the witness box is occupied by one's own flesh and blood. The individual cannot hire an advocate, intimidate a witness, or invent an alibi; their own hands and feet speak directly to God, establishing an unshakeable evidentiary record that leaves the transgressor utterly speechless, utterly helpless, and righteously condemned before all creation, with nowhere to flee and no shelter to claim.

**The Moral Economy of Physical Agency**

The deeper theological lesson of this verse concerns the sacred nature of the human body. Our limbs are not our sovereign property to do with as we please; they are sacred trusts (*amānāt*) bestowed upon us by the Almighty for a brief probationary interval. God granted us tongues to praise Him and speak truth, hands to build and aid the weak, and feet to walk toward justice and righteousness.

When a human being uses these divine gifts to slander chaste women, spread indecency, and terrorize innocent households, they commit a monstrous betrayal of their cosmic trust. On the Day of Judgment, the betrayed limbs demand justice from their oppressive owner, testifying against the ego that forced them into unholy rebellion. True spiritual wisdom demands that a believer honor their bodily trust today, disciplining every limb before the arrival of that inescapable Day of exposure, turning physical existence into an offering of obedience, pure devotion, and constant reverence.
