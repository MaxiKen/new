## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:32

> **Do not go near adultery. It is truly a shameful deed and an evil way.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Do Not Go Near Adultery**

Wa-lā taqrabū al-zinā — and do not approach, go near adultery, fornication, unlawful sexual intercourse. Wa-lā, and not, prohibition, strong. Taqrabū from q-r-b, to approach, go near, come close, be near, from qaruba, to be near, close, as qurbā closeness, kinship, and taqrabū approaching. Al-zinā from z-n-y, adultery, fornication, unlawful sexual intercourse, from zanā, to commit adultery, fornication, from zinā, adultery. Al-Ṭabarī explains that do not go near adultery, not only do not commit adultery, but do not approach, go near, come close to it, meaning avoid all means, steps, causes, that lead to adultery, as approaching, going near leads to committing, falling into. The prohibition of approaching is stronger, more comprehensive than prohibition of committing, as it blocks means, prevents, as wisdom, most upright, to protect lineage, honour, family, children. Al-Qurṭubī says this includes avoiding looking with desire, touching, seclusion with non-mahram, flirting, etc., that lead to zinā, as all are approaching, going near, steps to adultery, and should be avoided.

**Shameful Deed And Evil Way**

Innahu kāna fāḥishatan wa-sā'a sabīlan — indeed it is shameful deed and evil way, bad path. Innahu, indeed it, adultery, zinā. Kāna, is, was, indicates it is. Fāḥishatan from f-ḥ-sh, shameful, obscene, indecent, lewd, from faḥusha, to be shameful, obscene, indecent, from fāḥishah, shameful deed, obscenity, indecency, lewdness. Wa-sā'a from s-w-ʾ, evil, bad, worse, from sā'a, to be evil, bad, from sū', evil, bad. Sabīlan from s-b-l, way, path, road, method, from sabīl, way, path, as ibn al-sabīl son of way, traveller. Al-Ṭabarī says adultery is shameful deed, fāḥishah, obscene, indecent, shameful, lewd, and evil way, bad path, evil way to follow, evil method, leading to evil consequences, as it leads to mixing lineage, confusion of parentage, harm to children, family, society, etc., and leads to Hell, not most upright. Al-Rāzī says fāḥishatan indicates indecency, shamefulness, obscenity in itself, and sā'a sabīlan indicates evil way, path, method, leading to further evil, not most upright, not wisdom, but evil.

**Why Approaching Prohibited Not Only Committing**

Why approaching prohibited, not only committing? Because human is ever hasty, ʿajūlan, ever hasty, prays for evil as for good, desires fleeting world, so approaching adultery, going near, leads to committing, as steps lead, as hasty desire leads. The most upright guidance blocks means, not only act, to protect, as wisdom, part of wisdom revealed, as verse 39 says this is part of wisdom revealed. The sūrah's moral code includes protecting lineage, honour, family, children, as previous verse protected children from killing for fear of poverty, provision for them and you, killing heinous sin, now protects from adultery which harms children, lineage, family, honour, and leads to killing children for fear of poverty or shame, as unwanted children killed, as Arabs killed children. So prohibition of adultery prevents killing children, protects lineage.

**Connection To Killing Children And Killing Life Sacred And Orphan Wealth**

Verse 31 said do not kill children for fear of poverty, We provide for them and for you, killing heinous sin, great sin. Verse 32 says do not go near adultery, shameful deed evil way. Verse 33 says do not kill human life made sacred except with legal right, if killed unjustly heir given authority but do not exceed limits for already supported. Verse 34 says do not approach orphan wealth except best until maturity, honour pledges accountable. The connection is that killing children, adultery, killing sacred life, consuming orphan wealth are all prohibitions protecting life and lineage and wealth and honour, all most upright, wisdom, most upright guidance. Adultery often leads to killing children for fear of poverty or shame, as unwanted children killed, abortion, infanticide, so prohibition of adultery prevents killing children, protects children. The series shows comprehensive protection: children, chastity, life sacred, orphan wealth, pledges, measuring, knowledge, humility, all wisdom.

**Link To Moral Code And Most Upright And Wisdom**

The moral code in this sūrah from verse 22 to 39 is detailed: monotheism, parents, relatives, poor, traveller, moderation, provision, children, adultery, life sacred, orphan wealth, pledges, measuring, knowledge, arrogance, all wisdom, most upright, that Qur'an guides to, as verse 9 says Qur'an guides to what is most upright. Adultery is included as shameful deed and evil way, not most upright, opposite of most upright guidance that Qur'an guides to, opposite of wisdom, detestable to Lord as verse 38 says violation detestable. The verse teaches that most upright includes chastity, avoiding approaching adultery, protecting lineage, honour, family, children.

**Linguistic Note On Taqrabu And Fahishah And Sabila**

Taqrabū from q-r-b, to approach, go near, come close, from qaruba, to be near, close. Zinā from z-n-y, adultery, fornication, from zanā, to commit adultery. Fāḥishah from f-ḥ-sh, shameful, obscene, indecent, from faḥusha, to be shameful, obscene. Sā'a from s-w-ʾ, evil, bad, from sā'a, to be evil. Sabīl from s-b-l, way, path, road, method. The verse's structure — do not approach adultery, indeed it is shameful deed and evil way — with prohibition of approaching, not only committing, and two descriptions, shameful deed and evil way, shows gravity: not only act shameful, obscene, but way evil, path bad, method evil, leading to further evil, not most upright, not wisdom, detestable.

**How This Verse Teaches Chastity And Avoiding Means To Adultery**

This verse teaches chastity and avoiding means to adultery: do not go near adultery, not only do not commit, but avoid approaching, means, steps, causes, that lead to it, because it is shameful deed, fāḥishah, obscene, indecent, and evil way, bad path, evil method. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should avoid adultery and avoid approaching it, such as looking with desire, touching, seclusion with non-mahram, flirting, chatting that leads, etc., that lead to zinā, as approaching leads to committing, as steps. The practical lesson is to guard chastity, lower gaze, avoid seclusion, avoid means, follow most upright guidance that protects lineage, honour, family, children, and avoids shameful deed and evil way that leads to painful punishment and Hell confinement, as Qur'an guides to most upright.

**A Reflection On Shameful Deed And Evil Way And Most Upright**

Fāḥishatan wa-sā'a sabīlan — shameful deed and evil way — two descriptions: shameful, obscene, indecent in itself, fāḥishah, and evil way, bad path, method, sā'a sabīlan, leading to further evil, not most upright, not wisdom, but evil, detestable to Lord. Adultery is shameful deed, fāḥishah, indecency, obscenity, and evil way, path that leads to mixing lineage, confusion, harming children, family, society, leading to killing children, to Hell, not most upright. The most upright guidance that Qur'an guides to, wisdom revealed, is chastity, avoiding approaching adultery, protecting children, life, honour, family, as wisdom, most upright. The believer who reflects will avoid approaching adultery, will see it as shameful deed and evil way, will follow most upright guidance of chastity, protecting lineage, family, children, honour, and will seek mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour in Hereafter, and avoid painful punishment, Hell, detestable violation.

**Additional Note On Protection Of Lineage**

Protection of lineage, nasab, is one of five essentials in Islamic law: religion, life, lineage, intellect, wealth. Adultery violates lineage, so prohibition of approaching adultery protects lineage, as most upright, wisdom. The sūrah's moral code protects all five: religion via monotheism, life via not killing children and sacred life, lineage via not approaching adultery, intellect via not following without knowledge, wealth via orphan wealth, measuring, giving due, moderation, etc., comprehensive most upright guidance, wisdom.
