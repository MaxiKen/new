## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:158

> Rather, Allah raised him up to Himself. And Allah is Almighty, AllWise.

### Expanded Commentary

**Raising Jesus and divine power**

Allah raised Jesus to Himself, and He is Almighty and Wise. The statement honours Jesus without making him divine. Raising a messenger is an act of power and protection, while wisdom places the event within a divine plan that human opponents could not defeat.

The verse answers the boast of the previous passage: the people who claimed victory did not control the end of Jesus’s mission. It also corrects two opposite errors—denying Jesus’s prophetic status and elevating him beyond servanthood. The Qur’anic Jesus is a sign, messenger, and word from God, not a partner in Godhead.

The Muslim should discuss the verse with clarity and respect. Its doctrinal claim is firm, but mocking Christians or treating a mystery as an excuse for hostility contradicts the Qur’an’s own manner of addressing People of the Book.

**Honouring Jesus through servanthood**

The Qur’anic correction is not a diminishing of Jesus but a protection of his prophetic dignity. A messenger raised by God is honoured without being worshipped. Muslims should explain this distinction with gratitude for the shared prophetic figure and with language that does not turn doctrinal disagreement into contempt.
