## Sūrah al-Maʿārij 70:38

> Does every one of them expect to be admitted into a Garden of Bliss?

**Expanded Commentary**

**Greedy Hopes Rebuked**

Ayaṭmaʿu kullu imriʾin minhum an yudkhala jannata naʿīm interrogates each mocker individually. Ṭamaʿ denotes covetous hoping: craving rewards without rendering service. Such greed draws rebuke because expectation must follow effort, not replace it. Kullu imriʾ individualizes the indictment: each one separately presumed. Gardens of bliss stood expected by souls who mocked bliss's Messenger. The interrogative form exposes without informing, for questions indict where statements merely describe. Hoping itself stands questioned: what deeds back these dreams? Readers should examine their own hopes for ṭamaʿ's taint, checking whether effort precedes expectation. What questions expose in presumption, deeds must correct in practice. Greedy hopes reveal graceless hearts. Rebuked ṭamaʿ must yield to required deeds.

Rajaa hopes through deeds: working first, then trusting. Ṭamaʿ presumes without them: expecting arrival while parked. Such distinction separates salvation from self-deception. Amānī — wishful fancies — deceive multitudes: "wishful thinking" (paraphrased from 2:111). Presumption paralyzes because expected arrival removes travel's motive. Hope mobilizes because trusted mercy rewards journeying. Mockers presumed gardens while ignoring duties; believers hope gardens while performing them. The difference is labor: hoped-for mercy crowns effort, presumed mercy excuses idleness. Readers should hope actively: work earnestly by day, trust humbly by night. What distinguishes in practice, destiny honors in outcome. Hope working arrives; presumption wishing stalls. Act hopefully always; never presume idly.

Jannatu naʿīm names bliss's garden, delight's permanent home. Such bliss unfolds elsewhere: rivers flowing, fruits dangling, thrones facing, peace chorused. Naʿīm signifies soft, refined delight — pleasure without poison, joy without fatigue. Bliss totals across senses and soul: bodies sated, hearts satisfied, eyes cooled. Mockers coveted comforts while scorning commands. Coveting without qualifying defines presumption's essence precisely. Gardens admit workers through mercy, never idlers through wishing. Readers should qualify diligently: duties performed earnestly, mercy hoped humbly. What naʿīm offers in delight, duties purchase through grace. Bliss described invites; qualification required admits. Delight's home houses workers' rest.

**Admission Has Conditions**

Verses 22-35 listed admission conditions plainly: eight duties spanning worship and ethics. Such listing tickets entry transparently: no duties, no gardens. Conditions specify prayer guarded, charity paid, Day believed, punishment feared, chastity fenced, trusts kept, testimony stood. Gatecrashers face rejection because presumption bounces at guarded gates. Mockers skipped the entire list yet expected seating. Admission offices here verify strictly: audit checks every condition. Readers should meet conditions systematically, treating the eight as checklist. What conditions require in conduct, mercy crowns in reward. Conditions met open gardens; conditions scorned close them. Ticketed entry only; no crashing permitted. Permitted entrants celebrate: gates opened. Opened gates welcome: angels greeting. Greeting angels congratulate: efforts crowned. Crowned efforts rest: bliss begins. Begun bliss continues: eternity unfolds. Unfolded eternity satisfies: desires fulfilled. Fulfilled desires expand: delights renewed. Renewed delights humble: gratitude overflows. Overflowing gratitude praises: Giver glorified. Glorified Giver bestows: cycles endless. Endless cycles bless: gardens eternal. Eternal gardens await: workers welcomed. Welcomed workers rest: labors ended. Ended labors rewarded: bliss begun. Begun bliss deepens: joys multiplied.

**Mockers Want Gardens**

Irony peaks unbearably: scoffers expect bliss. Such incoherence convicts through contradiction: mocking message while expecting mercy. Mouths mocking and hearts hoping cannot both stand. Message scorned yet reward craved violates elementary logic. Either revelation speaks true — then obey it — or false — then abandon gardens. Mockers demand both: scorn plus bliss, rebellion plus reward. Incoherence itself testifies: suppressed belief leaks through hope. Readers should resolve coherence firmly: believe the message, then hope its reward. What incoherence wants impossibly, logic refuses necessarily. Mockers' gardens shimmer as mirage. Believe coherently; hope consistently.

"Whoever hopes to meet his Lord, let him work righteousness" (paraphrased from 18:110). Such pairing prescribes hope's formula permanently: meeting hoped through working. Hoped meeting requires righteous labor as its vehicle. Righteousness specifies belief plus action, creed plus conduct. Formula stays fixed across ages: liqāʾ through ʿamal. Mockers hoped meetinglessly, offering no deeds with dreams. Believers work hopefully, pairing sweat with trust. Readers should pair always: morning hope voiced, daily deeds performed. What pairing prescribes in sequence, presumption skips in laziness. Hope deedfully; work hopefully. Paired salvation succeeds; skipped presumption fails.

**Earnest Seekers Welcomed**

Earnest seekers welcomed warmly: gates open. Such welcome contrasts: workers embraced. Seeking defined: asking, striving, knocking. Askers receive: doors opened. Strivers guided: paths shown. Knockers admitted: persistence rewarded. Earnestness evidences: effort visible. Welcome assured: mercy promised. Readers should seek earnestly: ask daily. Strive consistently: worship regularly. Knock persistently: supplicate repeatedly. What welcome awaits (seekers), earnestness earns. Seek earnestly; welcomed warmly. Askers embraced; strivers guided.

Wishful congregations warned: mosques fill with presumers. Such warning sobers: attendance ≠ admission. Prayers performed: hearts absent. Charity given: motives mixed. Presumption infects worshipers: rituals presumed sufficient. Sincerity required: hearts weighed. Hypocrites prayed: mosques attended. Gates rejected: tickets forged. Readers worshiping should audit: sincerity checked. Purify motives: worship for Allah. What warning sobers (presumers), sincerity saves. Worship sincerely; never presume ritually. Congregations warned; sincere welcomed.

**Tickets Checked Strictly**

Gates check tickets strictly: claims verified, deeds weighed. Such strictness warns presumers: no entry unqualified. Ticket components: faith stamped, duties punched. Forged tickets detected: hypocrisy exposed. Borrowed tickets invalid: no proxies. Strictness comforts workers: earned entry honored. Laxity would insult: equal outcomes unjust. Readers should secure tickets early: faith renewed, duties performed. Verify holdings nightly: tickets valid? What strictness guards (justice), workers welcome. Checked strictly; admitted justly. Secure tickets; pass gates.

**Created Lowly Next**

Hopes rebuked, origins recalled next. Verse 39 will humble presumers by naming their lowly creation. Such sequence deflates systematically: greedy hopes punctured by fluid facts. Readers should brace for humbling as origins expose pretension. What follows creates lowly from known substance. The next verse recalls creation from fluid, origins humbling hopes.
