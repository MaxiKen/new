## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:36

> **Do not follow what you have no ˹sure˺ knowledge of. Indeed, all will be called to account for ˹their˺ hearing, sight, and intellect.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Do Not Follow What You Have No Sure Knowledge Of**

Wa-lā taqfu mā laysa laka bihi ʿilmun — and do not follow, pursue, track what you have no knowledge of, no sure knowledge. Wa-lā, and not, prohibition, strong. Taqfu from q-f-w, to follow, pursue, track, to go behind, from qafā, to follow, track, to go behind, from same root as qafā, back, nape, following behind. Mā laysa laka bihi ʿilmun, what you have no knowledge of, what you have no sure knowledge, no certain knowledge, from l-y-s, not, is not, and ʿ-l-m, knowledge, sure knowledge, certain knowledge, from ʿalima, to know. Al-Ṭabarī explains that do not follow, pursue, say, do, what you have no sure knowledge of, no knowledge, meaning do not speak without knowledge, do not accuse without knowledge, do not testify without knowledge, do not follow conjecture, zann, without sure knowledge, as following without knowledge leads to falsehood, injustice, sin, as many follow conjecture, but conjecture avails not against truth. Al-Qurṭubī says this includes prohibitions of slander, false testimony, speaking about Allah without knowledge, following conjecture, guessing, etc., all prohibited, as knowledge required, sure knowledge, not conjecture, as Qur'an guides to most upright with detailed explanation, everything explained in detail, so follow sure knowledge.

**Hearing, Sight, Intellect Accountable**

Inna al-samʿa wa-al-baṣara wa-al-fu'āda kullu ulā'ika kāna ʿanhu mas'ūlan — indeed hearing and sight and heart, intellect, all those will be questioned, accountable, responsible. Inna, indeed, surely, emphasis. Al-samʿa from s-m-ʿ, hearing, ear, sense of hearing, faculty of hearing, from samiʿa, to hear. Wa-al-baṣara from b-ṣ-r, sight, eye, seeing, faculty of sight, from baṣara, to see, as mubṣiratan bright, giving sight, and Baṣīr All-Seeing, All-Aware All-Seeing. Wa-al-fu'āda from f-ʾ-d, heart, intellect, mind, inner heart, from fu'ād, heart, core heart, intellect, from fa'ada, to be heart, to have heart. Kullu ulā'ika, all those, all these, all of them, hearing, sight, heart. Kāna ʿanhu mas'ūlan, will be questioned about it, accountable for it, responsible, from s-ʾ-l, to question, ask, as verse 34 pledge accountable mas'ūlan, and verse 36 accountable. Al-Ṭabarī says hearing, sight, heart, intellect will be questioned, accountable, meaning you will be asked about what you heard, what you saw, what you thought, intended, what you followed, whether you followed without knowledge, used hearing, sight, heart properly to gain sure knowledge, or misused. Al-Rāzī says samʿ, baṣar, fu'ād are faculties of knowledge, means to gain knowledge, hearing, sight, intellect, and they will be accountable, questioned, so do not follow without sure knowledge, use them to gain sure knowledge, as they are trusts.

**Why Following Without Knowledge Prohibited**

Why prohibited? Because following without sure knowledge leads to falsehood, injustice, shameful deed, evil way, killing sacred life, consuming orphan wealth, breaking pledges, cheating measure, walking arrogantly, etc., all evils rooted in following without knowledge, conjecture. The most upright guidance requires sure knowledge, not conjecture, as Qur'an guides to most upright with detailed explanation, everything explained in detail, faṣṣalnāhu tafṣīlan, so follow sure knowledge, not conjecture, not follow what you have no sure knowledge of. The sūrah's theme of knowledge appears: Qur'an explains everything in detail, guides to most upright, night and day signs to know number years and calculation, etc., so follow sure knowledge, use hearing, sight, intellect properly.

**Connection To Measuring, Pledges, Orphan Wealth, Life, Humility**

Verses 34-37: orphan wealth protected except best until maturity, pledges honoured accountable, give full measure even balance fairest best in end, do not follow without sure knowledge hearing sight intellect accountable, do not walk arrogantly on earth you cannot crack earth nor reach mountains height. The connection is accountability and justice and humility: orphan wealth protected accountable, pledges accountable, measure fair best in end, knowledge accountable, humility required, all accountable, all part of most upright, wisdom, detestable if violated. Hearing, sight, heart will be questioned, as pledges questioned, as record laid open, sufficient self as reckoner, no bearer bears burden, messenger sent, etc., theme of accountability consistent across sūrah.

**Link To Previous Chapter About Knowledge And Hearing Sight And Denying Hearts**

Previous chapter said those who do not believe in Hereafter have denying hearts, munkir, and are arrogant, and mentioned hearing, sight, etc., as faculties. This verse says hearing, sight, heart accountable, will be questioned, so use them for sure knowledge, not follow without knowledge, not deny Hereafter without knowledge, not be arrogant. The moral code includes not following without sure knowledge, which is foundation for justice in all previous prohibitions: do not kill children without knowledge of provision, do not approach adultery without knowledge, do not kill sacred life, protect orphan wealth, fulfill pledges, give full measure, all require sure knowledge, not conjecture, not following without knowledge.

**Linguistic Note On Taqfu And Samʿ And Fu'ad And Mas'ul**

Taqfu from q-f-w, to follow, pursue, track, from qafā, to follow, to track, to go behind. Mā laysa, what not, what is not, is not. Laka, for you, to you. Bihi, with it, about it, concerning it. ʿIlm from ʿ-l-m, knowledge, sure knowledge, certain knowledge, from ʿalima, to know. Samʿ from s-m-ʿ, hearing, from samiʿa, to hear. Baṣar from b-ṣ-r, sight, from baṣara, to see. Fu'ād from f-ʾ-d, heart, intellect, from fa'ada. Kull from k-l-l, all. Ulā'ika, those, these. Mas'ūl from s-ʾ-l, questioned, accountable, from sa'ala, to ask, question. The verse's structure — do not follow what you have no knowledge of, indeed hearing and sight and heart all those will be questioned about it — with prohibition of following without knowledge and reason that faculties hearing, sight, heart accountable, questioned, shows that following without knowledge misuses faculties that will be questioned, so avoid, use faculties for sure knowledge, most upright.

**How This Verse Teaches Sure Knowledge And Accountability**

This verse teaches sure knowledge and accountability of faculties: do not follow, pursue, say, do, what you have no sure knowledge of, no knowledge, conjecture, guess, as it leads to falsehood, injustice, sin, and hearing, sight, intellect will be accountable, questioned about what you heard, saw, thought, followed, intended. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should not speak without knowledge, not accuse without knowledge, not testify without knowledge, not follow conjecture, should use hearing, sight, heart to gain sure knowledge, as they will be questioned, accountable, record laid open will show what you followed, heard, saw, thought. The practical lesson is to verify, gain sure knowledge, not follow without knowledge, use hearing, sight, intellect properly, as most upright guidance, wisdom, part of appreciated striving, mighty reward, avoiding painful punishment.

**A Reflection On Hearing Sight Heart Accountable And Most Upright**

Al-samʿa wa-al-baṣara wa-al-fu'āda kullu ulā'ika kāna ʿanhu mas'ūlan — hearing and sight and heart all will be questioned about it, accountable — indicates that faculties of knowledge, hearing, sight, intellect, are trusts, amanah, not to be misused by following without sure knowledge, and they will be questioned, accountable, on Day when record laid open, sufficient self as reckoner, no bearer bears burden, messenger sent, detailed explanation given. The believer who reflects will not follow what he has no sure knowledge of, will use hearing, sight, heart to seek sure knowledge, detailed explanation that Qur'an provides, most upright guidance, wisdom, and will be accountable, so will be careful, as part of most upright, wisdom, leading to appreciated striving, mighty reward, far greater rank and favour, avoiding detestable violation, painful punishment.
