## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:6

> **Then ˹after your repentance˺ We would give you the upper hand over them and aid you with wealth and offspring, causing you to outnumber them.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Restoration After First Punishment**

Thumma radadnā lakum al-karrata ʿalayhim — then We gave you return, upper hand, over them. Thumma, then, after first punishment and after repentance. Radadnā from r-d-d, to return, give back. Lakum al-karrata, for you the return, upper hand, turn to prevail, from k-r-r, to return, to attack again. ʿAlayhim, over them, over enemies who ravaged homes. Al-Ṭabarī explains that after Israelites were punished by servants of great might who ravaged homes, and after they repented, Allah gave them return, upper hand over enemies, allowing them to return to Holy Land, rebuild Temple, and prevail. The phrase radadnā lakum al-karrata ʿalayhim indicates that victory and dominance were returned to Israelites after being taken away, showing that restoration is possible after repentance.

**Aid With Wealth And Offspring**

Wa-amdadnākum bi-amwālin wa-banīn — and We aided you with wealth and sons, offspring. Amdadnākum from m-d-d, to aid, extend, supply, from same root as madd, extension. Bi-amwālin, with wealth, money, property. Wa-banīn, and sons, offspring, children. Al-Ṭabarī says Allah aided Israelites after restoration with wealth and offspring, increasing their numbers and provision, as part of returning upper hand. Wealth and offspring are two main sources of strength and honor in ancient society: wealth for economy, sons for manpower and defense. Al-Rāzī notes that amwāl and banīn are mentioned together often in Qur'an as adornment of worldly life, as 18:46 says *Wealth and children are adornment of worldly life* (18:46). Their increase after punishment shows that repentance brings restoration of both material and human resources.

**Causing You To Outnumber Them**

Wa-jaʿalnākum akthara nafīran — and We made you more numerous, greater in manpower, outnumbering. Jaʿalnākum from j-ʿ-l, to make. Akthara, more, greater, from k-th-r, many. Nafīran from n-f-r, to go forth, to rally, manpower, number of people who go forth for war or support. Al-Ṭabarī explains akthara nafīran means more numerous in manpower, soldiers, supporters, outnumbering enemies. Al-Qurṭubī says nafīr means those who go forth to help, so more nafīr means more helpers, larger army, greater numbers. After being enslaved and reduced, Israelites were made more numerous, showing that Allah can increase numbers after decrease, as He can give upper hand after defeat.

**Why Restoration Follows Repentance**

Thumma radadnā lakum al-karrata ʿalayhim — then We gave you return over them — indicates that restoration came after repentance, as brackets ˹after your repentance˺ clarify. Al-Ṭabarī says Israelites repented after first punishment, returned to obedience, so Allah restored them. The pattern is: corruption, punishment, repentance, restoration with wealth and offspring and numbers. This pattern appears throughout Qur'an for many communities, showing that mercy follows repentance. The same pattern applies to Makkans: if they repent after warning, they will be given upper hand, as happened to some who became Muslims and were aided. The verse therefore offers hope: even after punishment, repentance leads to restoration.

**The Cycle Of History**

This verse describes part of cycle: first corruption punished, then restoration. Next verses will say if you do good it is for yourselves, if you do wrong it is against yourselves, then second corruption punished with disgrace and Temple entry and destruction, then perhaps mercy if you repent, but if you return to sin, punishment returns. The cycle shows that history is not random but governed by law: corruption leads to punishment, repentance leads to restoration, doing good benefits self, doing wrong harms self. Al-Rāzī explains that this cycle is to teach that divine law is consistent and that human choice determines where in cycle they are.

**Connection To Blessings And Gratitude**

Wealth and offspring as aid after restoration are blessings, as previous chapter listed wealth and offspring as blessings and as adornment of worldly life. The verse says We aided you with wealth and offspring, showing that restoration includes blessings, material and human, both needed for strength. The appropriate response is gratitude, not arrogance and second corruption, not using wealth and offspring to transgress. The Israelites were given upper hand, wealth, offspring, numbers after first punishment, but they later corrupted again, using blessings to become extremely arrogant, leading to second punishment with disgrace and Temple entry and utter destruction. The lesson is that blessings after restoration should lead to gratitude, shukr, and obedience, not to second corruption and arrogance. The sūrah's theme of gratitude appears: blessings are from Allah, should be thanked, not used for corruption, and grateful servant Noah was example.

**Why Numbers Matter**

Akthara nafīran — more numerous — shows that numbers matter after restoration, as community strength depends on manpower. After being reduced by punishment, being made more numerous indicates demographic recovery, which is mercy. The Israelites after Babylonian exile returned and multiplied, as history shows. The phrase akthara nafīran also indicates that upper hand requires numbers, and Allah made them more numerous to give them upper hand. This is part of restoration: not only return to land but increase in numbers so that they outnumber enemies who had ravaged homes.

**Linguistic Note On Karrat And Nafīr**

Karrat from k-r-r means return, attack, turn, from same root as karr, to return, attack again. In war context, karr means attack after retreat, so al-karrata ʿalayhim means return of attack, upper hand over them. Amdadnākum from m-d-d, to aid, extend, supply. Amwāl from m-w-l, wealth, property. Banīn from b-n-y, sons, offspring, from same root as ibn, son. Nafīr from n-f-r, to go forth, rally, from same root as nafara, to go forth, and nafr, manpower. Akthara nafīran, more numerous in manpower, indicates greater numbers. The verse's structure — then We gave you return over them and aided you with wealth and offspring and made you more numerous — with three aids, shows comprehensive restoration: dominance, wealth, offspring, numbers, all from Allah.

**How This Verse Teaches About Restoration**

This verse teaches that restoration after punishment is possible via repentance and that restoration includes upper hand, wealth, offspring, and numbers. It shows that Allah's mercy includes giving second chance after first punishment. The practical lesson is to repent after sin and to use restoration with gratitude, not to return to corruption. For Makkans who were being warned, it offers hope: if you repent and follow Qur'an that guides to most upright, you will be given upper hand and aid, as believers were after emigration given good home in world. The verse also warns that restoration is not guarantee against second corruption; if you corrupt again, second punishment will come, more severe, with disgrace and Temple entry.
