## Sūrah al-Baqarah 2:240

> Those of you who die leaving widows should bequeath for them a year’s maintenance without forcing them out. But if they choose to leave, you are not accountable for what they reasonably decide for themselves. And Allah is Almighty, All-Wise.

The verse instructs those who are about to die and leave wives behind to make a bequest for them, covering a year's maintenance and residence without their being turned out, and it states that if they leave the house themselves there is no blame in what they do honourably. The sentence treats the position of the widow and the arrangements that follow a death.

The Book's concern is the provision for the woman who loses her husband, and the community's teachers read the sentence as the statement of the obligation that accompanies the marriage. Readers are shown that the legislation attends to the household beyond the death of the one who provided for it.
