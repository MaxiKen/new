## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:118

> cursed by Allah—who said, “I will surely take hold of a certain number of Your servants.

### Expanded Commentary

**Satan’s declared strategy**

Satan says he will take a portion of Allah’s servants. The language presents temptation as selective and organized, not as an irresistible force imposed on everyone equally. He seeks willing participation, targeting weakness, pride, fear, and the desire for easy certainty.

The statement is also a divine exposure of the enemy’s method. Believers are told the plan so that secrecy loses its power. A temptation named can be resisted: ask whose interest a suggestion serves, what it makes you forget, and whether it turns a lawful gift into an object of worship.

The verse’s “portion” warns communities not to assume that religious numbers guarantee purity. Satan’s influence can enter through respected people, institutions, and apparently pious habits. Vigilance begins with humility.

**Temptation seeks a portion**

No one should assume that a community’s majority protects every individual from temptation. Watch for the places where status, anger, or fear are rewarded, because those are the doors through which rebellion becomes attractive. Naming the strategy makes vigilance a shared practice rather than a private panic.
