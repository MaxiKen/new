## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:118

> cursed by Allah—who said, “I will surely take hold of a certain number of Your servants.

**Expanded Commentary**

**Cursed by God**

The verse opens *laʿanahu Allāhu* — cursed by God, expelled from mercy, removed from favour, condemned. *Laʿanahu* — He cursed him — was explained as God’s removal of the rebellious Satan — *shayṭān marīd* — invoked besides Him as female gods (4:117) — from mercy, as the sūrah earlier said God is angry, curses, and prepares mighty punishment for the intentional killer (4:93) and as it said the deceitful are not loved (4:107). The curse here was read as the original curse upon Iblīs after refusing to prostrate to Adam and after requesting respite until the appointed Day (7:12–15; 15:34–35; 38:77–78), where *“cursed”* — *malʿūn*, *rajīm* — is named. The sūrah has said that the Devil’s schemes are weak (4:76) yet must be curbed, and that whoever takes Satan as guardian instead of God suffers tremendous loss (4:119); the curse is the foundation of that loss: the guardian taken is already expelled. Early reports linked the curse to the declaration *“upon you is curse until the Day of Recompense”* (38:78) after he said *“I will surely take a share.”* The sūrah’s seal *“God does not wrong even an atom”* (4:40) frames the curse: it is not arbitrary; it is the measure for rebellion — *marīd* — after guidance was clear and prostration refused.

**Who Said — I Will Surely Take a Share of Your Servants**

The vow *wa qāla la attakhidhanna min ʿibādika naṣīban mafrūḍā* — and he said, I will surely take hold, seize for myself, from Your servants a certain, appointed, share, portion — was explained as Satan’s declared program after being cursed. *La attakhidhanna* — surely I will take — emphatic with oath and *nūn* of certainty, as the hostile who wish you to disbelieve as they have (4:89) and who plot by night (4:108) also speak with emphasis, but Satan’s oath here is before God Himself. *Min ʿibādika* — from Your servants — was read as from humanity created from a single soul (4:1) and to whom the earth was made spacious (4:97, 4:100). *Naṣīban mafrūḍan* — a share appointed, a portion fixed, due — was explained as a portion he will claim as if due, a fixed share of those who follow him besides God to call females (4:117), to believe in *jibt* and *ṭāghūt* (4:51), to desire deviation greatly (4:27). The phrase *mafrūḍ* — appointed — was linked to inheritance shares appointed as *farīḍah* (4:11–12) where God’s apportioning is just; Satan’s *mafrūḍ* is the counterfeit apportioning where he claims servants as if inheritance. The sūrah has shown that those given a portion trade it for misguidance (4:44); here Satan claims a portion for misguidance.

**A Verse That Places Curse Before Program**

Placed between *they invoke only rebellious Satan* (4:117) and *I will mislead, delude, order slitting ears and altering creation* (4:119), this verse places curse before program. He is not cursed because he takes a share; he takes a share because he is cursed — expelled, then vowing. The order was read as the sūrah’s causal chain after association’s far straying (4:116): association is the human side of accepting the share Satan claimed, and Hell without escape (4:121) is the share’s end. The sūrah has shown that whoever defies the Messenger after guidance and follows other than believers’ path is left to what he chose (4:115); to accept Satan’s share is that defiance in worship. Where the emigrant’s reward is said to have fallen upon God secure (4:100) and the striver’s wages are ranks from Him (4:96), the Satanic share is the inverse inheritance — a portion that fell upon the cursed, not upon God. The verse generalises the theft case’s throwing sin on the innocent (4:112): Satan throws his curse-burden onto his share, but the burden stays with the thrower and is borne as slander — here as *buhtān* upon creation.

**A Share Claimed With Oath — Not With Right**

The oath *la attakhidhanna* was explained to show that Satan does not claim by right but by oath, by force of vow. The sūrah has said God suffices as Guardian and Helper (4:45) and that whoever takes Satan as guardian instead of God suffers tremendous loss (4:119); the guardian taken by oath cannot protect because the oath was made from curse, not from authority. The phrase *naṣīban mafrūḍan* was linked to the fixed shares of kingdom the sūrah said they do not control — *would not give a speck* (4:53) — while Satan claims a share of humanity as if controlling kingdom; the claim is shown as false apportioning. The sūrah elsewhere says the Devil promises only delusion (4:120) and has no warrant; his *mafrūḍ* is therefore not a writ — *kitāb mawqūt* (4:103) — but a boast whose writ is Hell without escape. For the weak for whom burdens were lightened, the verse is a warning that the share Satan claims is not the strong only; his program targets every servant, but his share is only those who accept invocation besides God, female or otherwise, as worship.

**From Female Idols to Appointed Share**

Though only *“cursed by God who said, I will surely take an appointed share of Your servants,”* the verse moves from the single act — calling females besides Him (4:117) — to the system — taking a share. The sūrah that began from a single soul spread into countless men and women (4:1) now sees that spread claimed portionwise by the cursed. The sūrah has shown that those who were oppressed and remained while earth is wide wronged themselves (4:97) while the helpless hoped for pardon (4:99); Satan’s appointed share is those who remain in invocation besides Him where earth’s havens were available. The next verse will detail his program upon that share: mislead, delude with empty hopes, order slitting ears of cattle and altering God’s creation, where whoever takes him as guardian suffers tremendous loss (4:119).
