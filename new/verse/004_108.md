## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:108

> They try to hide ˹their deception˺ from people, but they can never hide it from Allah—in Whose presence they plot by night what is displeasing to Him. And Allah is Fully Aware of what they do.

**Expanded Commentary**

**They Hide From People but Cannot Hide From God**

The verse states *yastakhfūna mina al-nāsi wa lā yastakhfūna mina Allāhi* — they try to hide, seek to be hidden, from people, but they can never hide from God. *Yastakhfūn* — to seek concealment, to hide, to make hidden — was explained as the deceitful’s attempt after theft to move the armour, to place it with a Jew, to argue by night a story that would read clean by day. *Mina al-nāsi* — from people — was read as from the eyes of the community, from the Prophet ﷺ as judge before evidence was shown (4:105), from the kin whose advocacy they sought (4:107). *Wa lā yastakhfūna mina Allāhi* — but from God they cannot be hidden — was explained with the sūrah’s repeated knowledge: God knows what is in hearts (4:63), knows best who enemies are (4:45), is Ever-Pardoning All-Forgiving yet also All-Hearing All-Seeing who commands return of trusts (4:58), and is All-Aware of what you do (4:94). The contrast was read as the sūrah’s exposure of night plotting: what can be hidden from people for a night cannot be hidden from the One in whose presence — *maʿahum* — they plot.

**In Whose Presence They Plot by Night What Displeases Him**

The circumstance *wa huwa maʿahum idh yubayyitūna mā lā yarḍā mina al-qawli* — and He is with them when they plot, harbour by night what is displeasing, not approved, of speech — was explained as divine presence not as indwelling but as knowledge and witnessing. *Yubayyitūn* — to harbour by night, to plot by night, to contrive in darkness — was read as the same root *b-y-t* seen where a group spends the night contradicting what they said before you and God records their schemes (4:81): *yubayyitūna* is their night speech that does not match day speech. *Mā lā yarḍā mina al-qawli* — what He does not approve of speech, what is displeasing Him — was explained as the false defence, the projection onto the innocent (4:112), the counsel to shift guilt. The sūrah has said that God records schemes (4:81) and that most secret talks have no good except charity, kindness, reconciliation (4:114); night plotting that shifts theft onto a Jew is the evil secret talk. That He is *maʿahum* — with them — at that hour was read as terrifying gentleness: the plotting is not done in absence, but in presence, as the sūrah elsewhere says *“He is with you wherever you are”* (57:4) by knowledge, and here adds displeasure — *lā yarḍā* — at the speech. Early reports noted that the plotters met by night to agree to accuse the Jew and to coach the defence, assuming darkness would cover counsel; the verse declares darkness itself is presence.

**And God Is Fully Aware of What They Do**

The seal *wa kāna Allāhu bi mā yaʿmalūna muḥīṭā* — And God is Fully Aware, Encompassing, of what they do — was explained as *muḥīṭ* — encompassing, surrounding, comprehending — not merely aware but encircling. *Muḥīṭ* appears where the sūrah says God encompasses all things in knowledge (4:126), and where He is sufficient as Trustee (4:132). To be *muḥīṭ* of what they do means no night speech escapes perimeter: the theft, the moving of goods, the coaching, the advocacy sought (4:107), the advocacy given (4:109), and the advocacy withheld when the Book judges (4:105). The sūrah has sealed knowledge names diversely — All-Knowing, All-Wise where mistake law and pursuit meet (4:92, 4:104), All-Hearing, All-Seeing where trusts are judged (4:58) — here *Muḥīṭ* fits night, whose darkness seems to create enclosure; the verse says the night is itself enclosed. The attribute was also linked to *“God does not wrong even an atom”* (4:40): His encompassing does not add weight; it measures the atom exact, including the atom of night speech.

**A Verse That Turns Night Into Presence**

Placed after the two prohibitions to advocates (4:105, 4:107) and the insertion to seek forgiveness (4:106), this verse turns night into presence. The sūrah has shown that those who spend the night contradicting what they said are recorded (4:81) and that reflection on the Qur’an would reveal many inconsistencies had it been from other than God (4:82); inconsistency between day claim and night plot is the deceitful’s equivalent. The sūrah that warns not to take disbelievers as allies for honour (4:139) and that declares hypocrites will be in lowest depth (4:145) here shows the advocate’s risk: to plead by day for what was plotted by night in His presence is to plead for speech He displeases while He encompasses. For the weak for whom burdens were lightened, the verse is not a burden but a clearing: where you were asked to hide a kinsman’s theft by day after he plotted by night, you need not hide, because hiding from people is not hiding from the Encompassing, and the honest word that *“whoever wrongs themselves then seeks forgiveness finds Him”* (4:110) remains open to the plotter if he leaves night plotting.

**From People’s Eyes to God’s Encompassing**

The triad — *hide from people, not from God, God encompasses* — was read as the sūrah’s inversion of the human instinct to seek cover. The believer’s cover is forgiveness sought (4:106) and found (4:110); the deceitful’s cover is night. The sūrah has taught that those given a portion trade guidance for misguidance and wish you deviate (4:44), and that Satan promises and deludes with empty hopes and orders altering creation (4:119–20); night plotting that is displeasing is Satan’s side of speech. The Book in truth sent to judge by what He shows (4:105) is the daylight that night cannot extinguish because the Night itself is with Him and by Him encompassed. The next verse will draw the advocate into that daylight: you advocate for them in this life, but who will advocate for them before God on Resurrection Day, or who will be their defender? (4:109).
