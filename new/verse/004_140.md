## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:140

> He has already revealed to you in the Book that when you hear Allah’s revelations being denied or ridiculed, then do not sit in that company unless they engage in a different topic, or else you will be like them. Surely Allah will gather the hypocrites and disbelievers all together in Hell.

### Expanded Commentary

**Do not normalize ridicule of revelation**

The verse commands believers not to sit with people who deny or ridicule Allah’s revelations unless they change the subject. Remaining in such a gathering without protest can communicate approval and gradually dull the heart. Association becomes morally relevant when it helps falsehood continue.

**Exit and conversation**

The instruction does not ban every encounter with a critic or prevent respectful discussion. It distinguishes dialogue aimed at understanding from a setting in which sacred things are mocked for entertainment and believers are expected to laugh along. If the topic changes, the situation changes; boundaries need not become permanent hostility.

The warning that one could become like them concerns participation and acceptance, not accidental proximity. The believer protects the tongue and heart while remembering that people may still repent. The final gathering of hypocrites and disbelievers in Hell shows the seriousness of choosing companionship that normalizes contempt.

**Boundaries around ridicule**

A believer may leave a conversation without hating everyone present. The boundary protects the heart from normalizing contempt while leaving open the possibility of a serious, respectful discussion later. The verse thus combines moral clarity with social composure rather than demanding constant confrontation.
