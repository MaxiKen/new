## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:140

> He has already revealed to you in the Book that when you hear Allah’s revelations being denied or ridiculed, then do not sit in that company unless they engage in a different topic, or else you will be like them. Surely Allah will gather the hypocrites and disbelievers all together in Hell.

**Expanded Commentary**

**When You Hear Revelations Denied or Ridiculed — Do Not Sit**

The verse states *wa qad nazzala ʿalaykum fī al-kitābi an idhā samiʿtum āyāti Allāhi yukfaru bihā wa yustahzaʾu bihā fa lā taqʿudū maʿahum ḥattā yakhūḍū fī ḥadīthin ghayrih* — He has already revealed to you in the Book that when you hear God’s signs, revelations, being denied, covered, or mocked, ridiculed, then do not sit with them until they engage in another discourse, other topic. *Nazzala ʿalaykum fī al-kitābi* — He has revealed to you in the Book — was explained as the earlier revelation referenced at 6:68 — *when you see those who engage in Our signs then turn away until they engage in other talk* — the sūrah’s cross-reference where sitting with mockers is prohibited, recited again here for hypocrites who take disbelievers as allies seeking honour (4:139). *Āyāti Allāhi yukfaru bihā wa yustahzaʾu bihā* — signs denied or ridiculed — was explained as the denial that makes association far straying (4:116) and the ridicule that is the mocker’s weapon, as the sūrah has shown that those given a portion distort words and say *“we hear and disobey”* (4:46) and that Satan promises only delusion (4:120). *Fa lā taqʿudū maʿahum* — do not sit with them — was read as prohibition of company, not merely speech, matching *no good in most secret talks except charity, kindness, reconciliation* (4:114) — sitting where signs are ridiculed is the secret talk without those goods. *Ḥattā yakhūḍū fī ḥadīthin ghayrih* — until they engage in another topic, plunge into other talk — was read as the limit: leaving until the subject changes, as *“do not sit in that company unless they engage in a different topic”* is the translation’s phrasing.

**Or Else You Will Be Like Them**

The consequence *innakum idhan mithluhum* — surely you, then, are like them, similar — was explained as moral assimilation: sitting where signs are denied or mocked without protest or leaving makes the sitter like the denier and mocker, even where he claims belief. *Mithluhum* — like them — was read with the sūrah’s assimilation language where *“when you hear revelations denied do not sit in that company”* elsewhere makes the sitter like them because silence endorses. The sūrah has shown that the sitter with treaty people is excepted from hostility (4:90), that the constructor of ambiguity is warned *“do not take them as allies”* (4:89), and that witnesses for God must be against even selves (4:135); to sit is the sitter’s testimony that the denied sign is not worthy of standing. Early notes linked the verse to hypocrites who kept company with Quraysh mockers for honour (4:139): to seek honour among mockers while revelations are ridiculed is to be like the mocker where honour was sought. The phrase was also linked to *whoever distorts testimony or refuses* (4:135) — sitting is refusing testimony where signs need testimony as witnesses for Him.

**He Will Gather Hypocrites and Disbelievers Together in Hell**

The gathering *inna Allāha jāmiʿu al-munāfiqīna wa al-kāfirīna fī Jahannama jamīʿā* — surely God will gather the hypocrites and disbelievers all together, entirely, in Hell — was explained with *jāmiʿ* — gatherer — the same gatherer who will gather you on the Day about which there is no doubt (4:87) and who gathered revelation as Book with truth (4:105). *Jamīʿan* — all together, entirely — was read as that sitting together in mockery in this life — *taqʿudū maʿahum* — ends as being gathered together in Hell: company chosen becomes company assigned. The sūrah has shown that the hypocrite oscillates between neither believers nor disbelievers (4:143) yet *innakum idhan mithluhum* counts the sitter as like the disbeliever; gathering together makes that likeness abiding — *khālidīn* — without escape (4:121) as that gathering is *“what an evil destination”* for self-wrongers (4:97). The phrase was linked to *“Gardens under rivers forever, God’s promise ever true”* (4:122) versus *“Hell as home no escape”* (4:121): the sitting that was for *ʿizzah* (4:139) ends in gathering for humiliation, as the guarded prayer’s preparation was humiliating punishment for disbelievers (4:102). For the weak this is the lightest of warnings: to rise from mocking company is not enmity but survival, as not sitting is the Book’s instruction.

**A Verse That Follows Honour Sought With Company Prohibited**

Placed after *who choose disbelievers as allies seeking honour, surely all honour belongs to God* (4:139), this verse follows honour sought with company prohibited. The hypocrites sought honour through company with disbelievers (4:139); the verse says the company where signs are denied or mocked is precisely where you may not sit lest you be like them — so honour sought there is gathered as humiliation. The sūrah has shown that trusteeship suffices (4:132), that He can remove humanity and bring others (4:133), that both rewards are with Him (4:134); to sit with mockers for worldly *ʿizzah* is to desire world’s reward from beside Him where both rewards are with Him. The sūrah has also shown that standing for justice as witnesses for Him though against yourselves (4:135) requires leaving where justice is mocked; to remain seated where signs are denied is to distort testimony. The verse generalises the sūrah’s procedure: where report is heard of security or fear verify through Messenger and authority (4:83), and where a bearer of peace offers *salām* be sure (4:94), here where a sitter hears signs denied verify by leaving until other talk.

**Gathering Together as the Company’s End**

The verb *jāmiʿ* — gatherer — was explained to show that company in this life where secret talks had no good except charity, kindness, reconciliation (4:114) was rejected, while company where signs are mocked was chosen; the other world repays company with company: hypocrites and disbelievers together in Hell, believers and doers of good together in Gardens (4:122). The sūrah that warned *do not be advocate for deceitful* (4:105) and *do not advocate for self-wrongers* (4:107) and asked *who will advocate before God on the Day?* (4:109) here says advocates in this life who sat with mockers will be gathered with the mocked-for — not as advocates but as like — *mithluhum*. The next verse will show their waiting: those who wait to see what happens to you, saying to winners on each side *were we not on your side?* (4:141) — waiting is the posture of sitting extended into battlefield.
