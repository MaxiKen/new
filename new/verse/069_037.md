## Sūrah al-Ḥāqqah 69:37

> which none will eat except the evildoers.”

**Expanded Commentary**

**Eaters Identified: The Khāṭiʾūn**

Lā yaʾkuluhu illā al-khāṭiʾūn — "none eats it except the sinners." Khāṭiʾūn comes from kh-ṭ-ʾ: erring, sinning, missing the mark. Such naming identifies ghislīn's diners precisely: eaters match cuisine. Classical scholars distinguish khāṭiʾ (deliberate sinner) from mukhṭiʾ (honest errer). The khāṭiʾ sins knowingly, persists willfully, repents never. Ghislīn's guest-list admits deliberates only: accidental errers eat elsewhere. This precision consoles strugglers: stumbling differs from settling. Mistakes repented never seat sinners at foul tables. Persistence unrepented reserves seats permanently. Readers should examine erring honestly: accidental or deliberate? Stumbles confess quickly; settlements persist defiantly. Never settle in sin, for settlers dine on discharge. Rise per fall, repent per slip. Khāṭiʾūn persist; believers return. Distinguish erring; choose returning. Seats reserved for persisters only.

Illā excludes doubly: only sinners eat, and sinners eat only this. Such exclusivity mercies believers: ghislīn never touches righteous lips. Exclusion zones protect: gardens fenced from filth, diners separated eternally. The same illā that dooms sinners secures saints. Exclusivity also warns presumers: no righteous accidents, no mistaken seatings. Audit sorts perfectly: diners matched to diets. Believers need never fear foul food: faith exempts. Sinners cannot hope for better: persistence fixes menus. Readers should read illā as refuge: belong to believers, escape ghislīn. Exclusivity comforts the faithful while terrifying the defiant. Two exclusions, two populations, one sorting. Secured saints, sentenced sinners. Illā divides; conduct chooses sides. Choose saints' side livingly.

**Sin as Diet**

Deeds diet souls: spiritual food shapes eternal menus. Lawful deeds nourish — prayer feeds, charity fattens, dhikr sweetens. Foul deeds poison — backbiting sickens, cheating sours, arrogance bloats. Such nutrition inverts at audit: souls eat what they fed. Ghislīn-diners consumed filth spiritually for decades: gossip chewed, bribes swallowed, pride devoured. Bodies now receive souls' diet made visible. The menu externalizes the internal: hidden rot served openly. Readers should diet spiritually now: consume revelation, digest worship, metabolize charity. Starve vices: gossip fasts, anger diets, envy cleanses. What souls eat now (deeds), bodies eat then (requital). Diet purely; dine joyfully. Spiritual nutrition decides eternal cuisine. Eat good deeds; never eat ghislīn.

**Deliberate Defiance, Deliberate Menu**

Khāṭiʾ deliberates: sins chosen, warnings weighed, defiance preferred. Such deliberation deserves deliberate menu: ghislīn served intentionally. Fit matches: chosen filth returns as served filth. Deliberation distinguishes diners: ignorant errers excused, knowing rebels seated. Knowledge increases liability: instructed sinners sin deliberately. The khāṭiʾ knew revelation, heard warnings, watched funerals — then chose. Deliberate defiance hardens deliberately: each chosen sin calcifies conscience. Readers possessing knowledge should tremble productively: instructed, therefore liable. Act on knowledge promptly, for acted knowledge excuses. Unacted knowledge accuses at audit. Deliberate obedience now prevents deliberate menu then. Choose deliberately Godward; never persist deliberately denialward. Deliberation: diners' defining trait.

Mukhṭiʾ errs then corrects: mistakes admitted, amends made, lessons learned. Khāṭiʾ errs then persists: mistakes defended, repeated, celebrated. Such divergence decides diners: correctors dine in gardens, persisters in ghislīn. Correction mechanics: confess quickly, repair fully, resolve firmly. Persistence mechanics: deny reflexively, repeat compulsively, boast eventually. Every error forks: correction-path or persistence-path. Readers erring should fork correctly instantly: admit before sunset, repair before sleeping. Never defend errors, never repeat deliberately. Corrected errors become wisdom; persisted errors become cuisine. Fork correctionward always; never persist toward ghislīn. Correctors forgiven, persisters seated. Error universal; persistence optional. Choose correction.

**Appetites Reveal Allegiances**

Cravings confess masters: lawful appetites serve God, foul appetites serve lusts. Such revelation runs constant: hunger for revelation signals faith, hunger for gossip signals disease. Ghislīn-diners craved foulness for years: scandal savored, obscenity relished, cruelty enjoyed. Appetites trained toward filth inherit filth served. Lawful appetites likewise train: Qur'an craved, charity relished, worship savored. Readers should audit appetites: what is craved? Screen histories confess; meal choices testify. Retrain foul cravings: fast from filth, feast on revelation. Starve gossip-hunger; feed Qur'an-hunger. What appetites crave (masters), audit serves (menus). Crave purely; dine nobly. Appetites: allegiance's appetite-trail. Follow lawful hunger.

Company cures persistence: correctors befriend correctors. Such fellowship interrupts: righteous friends rebuke gently. Sinners persisting need pattern-breakers: companions who confront. Hiding sins from friends enables: secrecy sustains. Confessing struggles to trusted righteous invites: accountability breaks. Correctors' circles practice: mutual counsel, shared worship, honest friendship. Seek mentors specifically: scholars consulted, elders heeded. Avoid enablers firmly: mocking company corrupts. Readers persisting should recruit correctors today: confess one struggle. Join study circles: remembrance rewires. Befriend repented sinners: veterans guide. What company cures (persistence), isolation feeds. Correct together; never persist alone. Company: cure or curse.

**Persistence Patterns Exposed**

Persistence follows patterns: sins scheduled, warnings muted, repentance postponed. Such patterns expose khāṭiʾūn before audit does. Scheduled sins recur: weekly indulgences, nightly habits, seasonal lapses. Muted warnings accumulate: sermons unheard, verses unpondered, funerals unattended-in-heart. Postponed repentance calcifies: tomorrow's tawbah never dawns. Patterns persist because comfort rewards: sin soothes temporarily. Breaking requires pattern-interruption: schedules rewritten, warnings amplified, repentance immediate. Readers should map personal patterns honestly: which sins recur? Interdict triggers: routes changed, screens filtered, company switched. Amplify warnings: funerals attended, graves visited, verses pondered. Repent this hour: delayed tawbah is denied tawbah. What patterns persist (toward ghislīn), interruption breaks. Expose patterns; shatter schedules. Persisters patterned; correctors interrupt. Interruption works daily: today's break weakens tomorrow's pull. Momentum reverses: correction streaks replace sin streaks. Start interrupting this hour, for delayed breaks never come. Breaks compound: each interruption weakens habit's grip. Grip loosened daily: freedom approaches steadily. Approach freedom hourly; arrive finally. Arrived freedom celebrated: eternity secured gratefully forever and ever.

**Oaths Sworn in Creation**

Ghislīn's diners named; the passage pivots. Verses 38-39 will swear cosmic oaths — by seen and unseen. Such pivoting defends revelation: Hell's horrors certified by creation's witness. Readers should brace for oaths: diners sentenced, Scripture defended. What follows swears doubly. The next verses swear by visible and invisible, creation witnessing revelation.
