## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:138

> Give good news of a painful punishment to hypocrites,

### Expanded Commentary

**A painful “good news”**

The command to give hypocrites good news of painful punishment is an ironic reversal. They used pleasant words and religious appearance to conceal betrayal; revelation announces the consequence beneath the appearance. Irony reveals that worldly comfort was never the true news.

Hypocrisy in the chapter is a public danger because it exploits the trust reserved for believers. Yet the verse should not become a weapon for ordinary people to label opponents. The warning concerns a settled pattern of contradiction and treachery known in its full reality to Allah. Communities should address observable acts and leave ultimate hearts to God.

The phrase also comforts those who suffered from manipulation. Hidden betrayal is not hidden from the final judge, and the person who performed sincerity as a costume cannot rely on the costume forever.

**Irony exposes false comfort**

The “good news” of punishment overturns the hypocrite’s expectation that outward respectability will always protect him. It also reminds victims that a polished public image is not the final account. Communities should resist both gullibility and obsession with exposing every person; truthful evidence and fair procedure remain necessary.
