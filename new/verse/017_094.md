## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:94

> **And nothing has prevented people from believing when guidance comes to them except their protest: “Has Allah sent a human as a messenger?”**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Nothing Prevented People From Believing When Guidance Comes Except Protest Has Allah Sent Human As Messenger**

Wa-mā manaʿa al-nāsa an yu'minū idh jā'ahumu al-hudā illā an qālū a-baʿatha Allāhu basharan rasūlan — and nothing prevented people from believing when guidance came to them except that they said, has Allah sent human as messenger? Wa-mā manaʿa, and nothing prevented, kept, withheld, from m-n-ʿ, to prevent, keep, withhold, as manaʿa prevented, as previous mā manaʿanā nothing prevented Us from sending signs, etc., and mā manaʿa al-nāsa nothing prevented people from believing. Al-nāsa from n-w-s, people, mankind, as nās people. An yu'minū from a-m-n, to believe, that they believe, from amina, to believe. Idh jā'ahumu al-hudā, when guidance came to them, when guidance came, arrived to them, from j-y-ʾ, to come, and h-d-y, guidance, as hudā guidance, as guidance, as Qur'an guides to most upright, as guidance, as guidance came to them, as revelation, etc. Illā an qālū, except that they said, except their saying, protest, from q-w-l, to say, as qālū they said. A-baʿatha Allāhu basharan rasūlan, has Allah sent human as messenger, interrogative, has Allah sent human as messenger, protest, from b-ʿ-th, to send, raise, dispatch, as baʿatha sent, as Allah sent human as messenger, as basharan rasūlan human messenger, human as messenger, as human messenger, as Prophet Muhammad human messenger, as only human messenger, as previous verse Glory be to my Lord am I not only human messenger, An Angelic Messenger, as human messenger, etc., and as they protest, has Allah sent human as messenger, as disbelief, as they protest, has Allah sent human as messenger, as nothing prevented believing except protest human messenger, as they think messenger should be angel, not human, as they demand angelic messenger, etc. Al-Ṭabarī explains that nothing has prevented people from believing when guidance comes to them, when guidance, Qur'an, revelation, came, arrived to them, except their protest, saying, has Allah sent human as messenger, as they protest, has Allah sent human as messenger, as they think messenger should be angel, not human, as they demand angelic messenger, as they say has Allah sent human as messenger, as disbelief, as protest, as nothing prevented believing except protest human messenger, as they persist in disbelief, refuse except disbelief, etc.

**Why Protest Human Messenger Prevents Believing**

Why protest, has Allah sent human as messenger, prevents believing when guidance comes? Because they, disbelievers, polytheists, think messenger should be angel, not human, as they demand angelic messenger, as they say has Allah sent human as messenger, as protest, as disbelief, as they think human cannot be messenger, as they demand angel, as they have no sure knowledge, follow without knowledge, have barrier, veils, deafness, aversion, far astray cannot find Right Way, deny resurrection, challenge demanding spring gush, garden, rivers, sky fall, Allah and angels face to face, house gold, ascend heaven bring down book, etc., and now protest human messenger, as nothing prevented believing when guidance comes except protest human messenger, as they protest, has Allah sent human as messenger, as disbelief, as they think messenger should be angel, not human.

**Connection To Human Messenger And Angelic Messenger And Had There Been Angels Walking Earth**

Verse 93 say Glory be to my Lord am I not only human messenger, An Angelic Messenger heading, and verse 94 nothing has prevented people from believing when guidance comes except protest Has Allah sent human as messenger, and verse 95 had there been angels walking earth well settled We would have surely sent down for them angel from heaven as messenger, showing that Prophet is human messenger, not angel, as human messenger for humans, as only human messenger, as Glory be to my Lord am I not only human messenger, as human messenger, etc., and as nothing prevented believing except protest human messenger, as they protest, has Allah sent human as messenger, as disbelief, and had there been angels walking earth well settled We would have sent angel as messenger, as angel messenger for angels, human messenger for humans, as wisdom, most upright, as Our way with messengers no change, etc.

**Link To Nothing Keeps Us From Sending Demanded Signs And We Only Send Signs As Warning And Human Jinn Cannot Produce Equal**

Verse 59 nothing keeps Us from sending demanded signs except they were denied by earlier peoples, We gave Thamud shecamel as clear sign but they wrongfully rejected, We only send signs as warning, showing that demanded signs, if sent and denied, lead to destruction, severe punishment, as earlier peoples denied and destroyed, as every wicked society destroyed or punished severely before Day written in Record, etc., and as Thamud shecamel example, etc., so We only send signs as warning, not as demanded entertainment, etc., and verse 88 humans and jinn cannot produce equal Quran, even if supported, inimitability, challenge, iʿjāz, and verse 94 nothing prevented believing when guidance comes except protest human messenger, showing that despite inimitability, every kind of lesson set forth, yet most persist in disbelief, protest human messenger, as disbelief, as nothing prevented believing except protest human messenger, as they protest, has Allah sent human as messenger, as disbelief.

**Linguistic Note On Mana'a And Hudā And Ba'atha And Bashar Rasul**

Manaʿa from m-n-ʿ, to prevent, keep, withhold, from manaʿa. Nās from n-w-s, people, from nās. Yu'minū from a-m-n, to believe, from amina. Jā'a from j-y-ʾ, to come, arrive, from jā'a. Hudā from h-d-y, guidance, from hadā, to guide. Qālū from q-w-l, to say. Baʿatha from b-ʿ-th, to send, raise, dispatch, from baʿatha. Bashar from b-sh-r, human, mankind, from bashar. Rasūl from r-s-l, messenger, from rasūl. The verse's structure — nothing prevented people from believing when guidance came to them except that they said has Allah sent human as messenger — with nothing prevented believing when guidance came and exception they said has Allah sent human as messenger, protest, shows that disbelievers' protest, has Allah sent human as messenger, prevents believing when guidance comes, as they think messenger should be angel, not human, as disbelief, as they persist in disbelief, refuse except disbelief, etc.
