## Sūrah an-Nūr 24:50

> Is there a sickness in their hearts? Or are they in doubt? Or do they fear that Allah and His Messenger will be unjust to them? In fact, it is they who are the ˹true˺ wrongdoers.

**Expanded Commentary**

**The Tripartite Diagnostic: Disease, Skepticism, or Fear of Injustice**

The revelation culminates its exposure of hypocritical evasion with a devastating pedagogical interrogation: *a-fī qulūbihim maraḍun am irtābū am yakhāfūna an yaḥīfa Allāhu ʿalayhim wa-rasūluh*. The divine rhetoric employs three interrogative alternatives introduced by *am* (or / rather).

This inquiry is not formulated because God is uncertain of their internal condition; rather, it serves as a relentless clinical cross-examination designed to force the hypocrites—and the community observing them—to confront the ugly root causes of their aversion to divine arbitration. The three queries chart the deepening spiral of spiritual decay: it begins with an internal moral disease (*maraḍ*), degenerates into intellectual and religious skepticism (*rayb*), and culminates in the monstrous theological blasphemy of imagining that God and His Messenger could commit legal injustice (*ḥayf*) against them.

**The Pathology of the Diseased Heart: a-fī qulūbihim maraḍ**

The first diagnostic query targets the interior state of their spiritual center: *a-fī qulūbihim maraḍ*, is there a disease in their hearts? In Qur'anic cardiology, the heart (*al-qalb*) is the seat of perception, moral intuition, and spiritual vision.

A healthy heart is soft, transparent, responsive to truth, and filled with the love of God. A diseased heart, however, has suffered infection by spiritual pathologies: pride, envy, malice, love of illicit wealth, and addiction to worldly status. When a heart is sick, its moral immune system collapses completely. It perceives wholesome divine medicine as toxic poison and craves harmful worldly indulgences that accelerate its destruction. The hypocrites' refusal to submit their disputes to prophetic adjudication was an undeniable symptom of this cardiovascular infection; their hearts were too corrupted by selfish desires to tolerate the sterile scalpel of divine justice, recoiling from the cure.

**Epistemic Paralysis: am irtābū**

The second diagnostic query exposes their intellectual and existential instability: *am irtābū*, or are they in doubt? The root *r-y-b* denotes corrosive, paralyzing skepticism that robs the mind of conviction and serenity.

Did they lack certainty regarding the divine origin of the Prophet's mission? Did they harbor lingering doubts about the existence of God, the truth of the afterlife, or the reality of eschatological accountability? A person who genuinely doubts the truth of revelation will inevitably vacillate in their practical obedience. When faith is reduced to a hesitant hypothesis rather than an unshakeable certainty, the soul refuses to make significant worldly sacrifices for its sake. Their refusal to accept prophetic arbitration unmasked the fragile, rotting foundations of their supposed faith, showing that beneath their loud verbal confessions lay an abyss of unresolved religious skepticism.

**Projecting Tyranny onto Transcendent Justice: am yakhāfūn**

The third diagnostic query reaches the outer limits of moral absurdity: *am yakhāfūna an yaḥīfa Allāhu ʿalayhim wa-rasūluh*, or do they fear that Allah and His Messenger will be unjust to them? The verb *yaḥīf* denotes judicial bias, partiality, tipping the scales of justice, and defrauding a litigant of their rightful entitlement.

To even contemplate that the Creator of the universe—the Sovereign Lord who created the heavens with mathematical balance and commands universal justice—could act unjustly toward a mortal creature is an outrageous theological impossibility and grave sin. Similarly, to suspect the Prophet Muḥammad ﷺ—who was renowned even before revelation as *al-Amīn* (the Trustworthy) and sent as a mercy to all worlds—of defrauding a citizen in a property dispute is grotesque slander. By hesitating to enter the prophetic courtroom, the hypocrites committed the ultimate psychological projection: being unjust themselves, they projected their own corrupt instincts onto God and His Messenger, exposing their own dark inclinations.

**The Rhetorical Reversal: bal ulāʾika humu al-ẓālimūn**

The divine discourse shatters all three pretexts with a definitive, explosive refutation: *bal ulāʾika humu al-ẓālimūn*, in fact, it is they who are the true wrongdoers! The disjunctive particle *bal* (nay / rather / in fact) abruptly terminates the interrogation, sweeping aside their excuses and delivering the ultimate verdict.

The problem does not lie in the judiciary of Madinah; it lies in the criminality of the litigants. The demonstrative pronoun *ulāʾik* (those very ones) combined with the isolating pronoun *hum* establishes exclusivity: they, and they alone, are the true perpetrators of injustice (*al-ẓālimūn*). They do not fear injustice from the Prophet; they fear justice from the Prophet! They know with absolute certainty that if an impartial, incorruptible judge examines the facts of their case, their fraudulent deeds, stolen lands, and extortionate profits will be ripped from their hands and restored to their rightful owners. Their aversion to the court is not a defense against oppression, but a desperate flight from righteousness, proving their guilt.

**The Inversion of Guilt and the Psychology of Slander**

Verse 50 unmasks a timeless tactic of corrupt political and social actors: the aggressive inversion of guilt. Throughout human history, oppressors and fraudsters routinely deflect attention from their own crimes by accusing the judicial system of political bias, corruption, or procedural unfairness, hoping to confuse the public.

When caught in the act of theft or treachery, the wrongdoer postures as an innocent victim of institutional persecution. They slander the judge, attack the court, and undermine public confidence in the rule of law to protect their ill-gotten gains. Sūrah an-Nūr cuts through this manipulative smoke screen with laser precision. By declaring *bal ulāʾika humu al-ẓālimūn*, the Qur'an establishes a foundational legal and moral standard: whenever a party evades an impartial court that rules by the truth, society must recognize that evasion not as a legitimate grievance, but as conclusive evidence of wrongdoing, bad faith, and moral failure.
