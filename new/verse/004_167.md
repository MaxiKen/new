## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:167

> Those who disbelieve and hinder ˹others˺ from the Way of Allah have certainly strayed far away.

### Expanded Commentary

**Straying and making others stray**

Those who disbelieve and hinder others from Allah’s way have strayed far away. The verse gives special seriousness to obstruction because it adds social harm to personal rejection. A person can disbelieve privately, but blocking others through persecution, propaganda, intimidation, or corruption spreads the loss.

“Far away” describes both direction and consequence. The person moves away from truth while attempting to move others with him. Yet the warning also encourages believers to remove barriers to guidance through clear teaching, good character, and justice rather than through coercion. A community that behaves unjustly can become an obstacle even while speaking religiously.

The verse therefore asks whether our conduct opens or closes the path. Knowledge, welcome, and truthful representation are forms of calling; hypocrisy and oppression are forms of hindering.

**Do not be an obstacle**

A person can hinder guidance by mocking, persecuting, misrepresenting, or behaving so unjustly that people associate the faith with harm. Calling others to God therefore includes removing barriers created by one’s own character. Truthful speech and good conduct are part of the evidence a community offers.
