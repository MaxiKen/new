## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:167

> Those who disbelieve and hinder ˹others˺ from the Way of Allah have certainly strayed far away.

**Expanded Commentary**

**Those Who Disbelieve and Hinder From God’s Way — Far Astray**

The verse states *inna alladhīna kafarū wa ṣaddū ʿan sabīli Allāhi qad ḍallū ḍalālan baʿīdā* — surely those who disbelieve and hinder, bar, turn others from God’s way, path, have certainly gone far, strayed far away. *Kafarū* — to disbelieve, to cover — was explained as denial after messengers as good news and warnings removed excuse (4:165) and after sufficient witness bears witness with His knowledge and angels (4:166). *Wa ṣaddū ʿan sabīli Allāhi* — and hinder from God’s way — was explained with *ṣadd* — to block, to hinder, to turn away — the same hindering as *We forbade Jews certain good foods for wrongdoing and hindering many from God’s way* (4:160) — there *bi ṣaddihim ʿan sabīli Allāhi kathīran* — hindering many — here *ṣaddū* — hindering — as act of those who disbelieve. *Sabīli Allāhi* — God’s way, path — was read as the noble ways He wishes to make clear (4:26), the believers’ path not to be left (4:115), the straight path He guides to through mercy (4:175), the Book sent with truth to judge between people by what He shows (4:105) — all are God’s way. *Qad ḍallū ḍalālan baʿīdā* — have certainly gone far astray, strayed far away — was explained as *ḍalāl baʿīd* — far straying — the same seal as *whoever associates has gone far astray* (4:116) and *whoever denies God, angels, Books, messengers, Last Day has gone far astray* (4:136) — to disbelieve and hinder is that far. Early reports linked *ṣaddū* to Jews who concealed description of the Prophet ﷺ and to hypocrites who take disbelievers as allies seeking honour (4:139) and to those who deny revelation being ridiculed yet sit with mockers (4:140) — hindering is both doctrinal concealment and social sitting.

**A Verse That Follows No Excuse and Sufficient Witness With Far Straying**

Placed after *messengers as good news and warnings so humanity have no excuse after messengers, He is Almighty, All-Wise* (4:165), *yet if you are denied, God bears witness to what He sent with His knowledge, angels too bear witness, He alone suffices as Witness* (4:166), this verse follows no excuse and sufficient witness with far straying for those who disbelieve and hinder from God’s way. The sequence — lineage from Noah through Jesus to you (4:163), messengers told and not told, Moses spoken to directly (4:164), purpose so no excuse (4:165), sufficient witness (4:166) — now the verdict for deniers and hinderers: they have gone far. Scripture notes that *“neither by your wishes nor those of People of the Book”* (4:123) — grace not by wish — and *“with Him are both rewards He hears and sees”* (4:134) — to hinder from His way is to hinder from both rewards where both are with Him whose ownership is heavens and earth (4:126). Elsewhere the principle that *“whoever earns sin earns only upon himself”* (4:111) is qualified here: hindering adds others’ misguidance to earnings — therefore *baʿīd* — far.

**Disbelief Alone and Disbelief Plus Hindering**

The pairing — *kafarū* — disbelieved — and *ṣaddū* — hindered — was explained to show two degrees: disbelief alone is straying, disbelief plus hindering is far straying. The principle that *“whoever does evil will be rewarded accordingly”* (4:123) shows that disbelief alone will be *yujza bihī* — rewarded accordingly; hindering adds to it, as throwing sin on innocent adds slander to original sin (4:112). Forbidding *ṭayyibāt* for *ẓulm* plus *ṣadd kathīr* — hindering many — (4:160) shows that *ṭayyibāt* removal corresponds to double cause; here *ḍalālan baʿīdā* is for *kufr* plus *ṣadd*. Where the Book says *“do not incline entirely leaving the other suspended”* (4:129) — hindering the suspended from right is the household *ṣadd* — and *“settlement best”* (4:128) — settlement is opposite of hindering. For the weak this is protective: where you are hindered — the helpless who cannot find way (4:98) — the hinderer’s far straying is declared, not yours.

**The Hinderer’s Far Straying as Warning for the Community**

The *ṣaddū* — they hinder — was described as not only blocking others by force but by example: to disbelieve and claim hearts are *ghulf*, to slander Mary, to boast killing Messiah, to take *ribā* though forbidden, all hinder many who observe. The sūrah has taught that those who give charity, kindness, reconciliation seeking His pleasure earn great reward (4:114), the opposite of hindering; the hinderer’s work undoes that good by making God’s way seem blocked. Early commentators noted that *ṣadd* after sufficient witness (4:166) is worse than *ṣadd* before, because the witness — God with knowledge and angels — has already borne testimony that the way is true. That is why the seal is *baʿīd* — far — the distance from truth after truth was made near by messengers as good news and warnings.

**Far Astray After Knowledge Made Clear**

The *baʿīd* — far — was explained as after knowledge made clear where *He wills to make clear, guide to noble ways, turn in mercy, He is All-Knowing, All-Wise* (4:26–28) — clarity was via messengers, so straying is far because clarity was great. That *“the Messiah will never be too proud to be servant, nor angels nearest”* (4:172) shows that hindering from God’s way includes calling to worship females besides Him (4:117) and to take Satan as guardian instead of Him (4:119) — far from servanthood. The verse generalises the sūrah’s warning *do not go to extremes regarding faith* (4:171) — Trinity as hindering from His way — far straying is the extreme’s end. The next verse will seal that far straying: those who disbelieve and wrong themselves — surely He will neither forgive nor guide them to any path (4:168) — except that of Hell to stay forever, and that is easy for Him (4:169).
