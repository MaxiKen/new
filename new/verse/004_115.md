## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:115

> And whoever defies the Messenger after guidance has become clear to them and follows a path other than that of the believers, We will let them pursue what they have chosen, then burn them in Hell—what an evil end!

### Expanded Commentary

**The path of the believers**

The verse warns whoever opposes the Messenger after guidance has become clear and follows a path other than that of the believers. The issue is not an honest question or a person who has not yet received clarity. It is defiance after evidence and deliberate departure from the community of faith.

**Consensus and its limits**

The phrase “path of the believers” supports the authority of the established community and the importance of scholarly agreement. It does not mean that every majority opinion is automatically revelation or that a ruler may silence criticism by claiming consensus. The believers’ path is measured by the Qur’an, authentic Sunnah, justice, and the inherited work of qualified scholarship.

Allah lets the defiant pursue what they have chosen, then warns of Hell. This is a form of abandonment: the person is not physically prevented from error, but his choice becomes his road. The verse calls for humility before clear guidance and careful discernment before declaring a matter settled.

**The believers’ path is accountable**

Following the community does not mean surrendering conscience to a crowd. The believers’ path is a path of evidence, worship, justice, and correction. A person who claims consensus should be able to show the scholarship behind the claim and accept that honest disagreement can exist before a matter becomes clear.
