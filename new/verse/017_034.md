## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:34

> **Do not come near the wealth of the orphan—unless intending to enhance it—until they attain maturity. And honour ˹your˺ pledges, for you will surely be accountable for them.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Do Not Come Near Wealth Of Orphan Unless Enhancing**

Wa-lā taqrabū māla al-yatīmi illā bi-allatī hiya aḥsanu ḥattā yablugha ashuddahu — and do not approach wealth of orphan except with what is best until he reaches maturity. Wa-lā, and not, prohibition. Taqrabū from q-r-b, to approach, go near, as verse 32 lā taqrabū al-zinā, do not approach adultery. Māla from m-w-l, wealth, property, from māl. Al-yatīmi from y-t-m, orphan, one whose father died while young, from yatama, to be orphan. Illā bi-allatī hiya aḥsanu, except with what is best, better, most excellent, from ḥ-s-n, good, best, as aḥsantum do good, iḥsānan excellence, aḥsanu best. Ḥattā, until. Yablugha from b-l-gh, to reach, attain, as verse 23 yablughanna old age. Ashuddahu from sh-d-d, maturity, strength, full strength, from shadda, to be strong, from ashudd, strength, maturity, full strength, adulthood. Al-Ṭabarī explains that do not come near wealth of orphan, do not touch, use, except with what is best, intending to enhance it, improve it, invest, protect, until he attains maturity, full strength, adulthood, able to manage wealth. Al-Qurṭubī says approaching wealth of orphan prohibited except with best intention, enhancing, protecting, not consuming, until maturity, when orphan can manage, then return wealth.

**Honour Pledges Accountable**

Wa-awfū bi-l-ʿahdi inna al-ʿahda kāna mas'ūlan — and fulfill pledge, covenant, indeed pledge is questioned, accountable. Wa-awfū from w-f-y, to fulfill, complete, keep, from wafā, to fulfill, keep promise. Bi-l-ʿahdi from ʿ-h-d, pledge, covenant, promise, commitment, from ʿahida, to pledge. Inna al-ʿahda, indeed pledge, covenant. Kāna mas'ūlan from s-ʾ-l, questioned, asked about, accountable, responsible, from sa'ala, to ask, question, and mas'ūl, questioned, accountable. Al-Ṭabarī says honour pledges, fulfill covenants, promises, commitments, for you will be accountable for them, questioned about them on Day, record laid open, sufficient self as reckoner. Al-Rāzī says ʿahd includes covenants with Allah, with people, promises, oaths, all must be fulfilled, and you will be accountable, questioned, as part of justice, no bearer bears burden of another but each accountable for own pledges.

**Why Orphan Wealth Protected**

Why orphan wealth protected? Because orphan is weak, fatherless, vulnerable, as parents honoured, children protected from killing, now orphan wealth protected, as part of most upright guidance protecting weak, vulnerable. The sūrah's moral code protects weak: parents old, children, orphan, poor, traveller, etc. Orphan wealth is trust, not to be approached except to enhance, improve, protect, until maturity, when orphan can manage. This is justice, not consuming orphan wealth unjustly, which is heinous sin, as Qur'an says those who consume orphan wealth unjustly consume fire in bellies.

**Connection To Killing Children And Life Sacred And Measuring**

Verses 31-33 protected life: children, chastity, sacred life. Verse 34 protects wealth of vulnerable orphan and pledges, accountability. Verse 35 will say give full when measure and weigh with even balance, fairest best in end. The connection is protection of wealth: orphan wealth not to be approached except enhancing, and measuring and weighing fairly, not cheating, both protecting wealth, property, justice in financial dealings, most upright. The series shows comprehensive protection: life, lineage, wealth, pledges, measure, knowledge, humility.

**Link To Accountability And Record Laid Open**

Honour pledges for you will be accountable for them, mas'ūlan, questioned, accountable, links to record laid open, read your book, sufficient yourself as reckoner, no bearer bears burden, etc. Pledges will be in record laid open, you will be questioned, accountable. The sūrah's theme of accountability appears: destiny bound to neck, record laid open, read book, sufficient self as reckoner, no bearing other's burden, messenger sent, etc., now pledges accountable.

**Linguistic Note On Yatim And Ashudd And Ahd And Mas'ul**

Taqrabū from q-r-b, to approach. Māl from m-w-l, wealth. Yatīm from y-t-m, orphan, from yatama. Aḥsan from ḥ-s-n, best, better, from ḥasuna, to be good. Yablugha from b-l-gh, to reach. Ashudd from sh-d-d, maturity, strength, from shadda, to be strong. Awfū from w-f-y, to fulfill, from wafā. ʿAhd from ʿ-h-d, pledge, covenant. Mas'ūl from s-ʾ-l, questioned, accountable, from sa'ala. The verse's structure — do not approach wealth of orphan except with best until he reaches maturity, and fulfill pledge, indeed pledge is accountable — with prohibition of approaching orphan wealth except best until maturity and command to fulfill pledge with reason accountable, shows protection of vulnerable wealth and importance of fulfilling commitments, both accountable.

**How This Verse Teaches Protection Of Orphan Wealth And Fulfilling Pledges**

This verse teaches protection of orphan wealth and fulfilling pledges: do not come near wealth of orphan unless intending to enhance it, improve, protect, invest, until they attain maturity, full strength, adulthood, able to manage, then return, and honour pledges, fulfill covenants, promises, for you will be accountable for them, questioned on Day, record laid open. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should not consume orphan wealth unjustly, should protect, enhance, until maturity, and should fulfill pledges, covenants, promises, with Allah and people, as accountable, questioned. The practical lesson is to protect orphan wealth as trust, enhance, not consume, and to fulfill pledges, as most upright guidance, wisdom, part of appreciated striving, mighty reward.

**A Reflection On Best And Maturity And Accountability**

Bi-allatī hiya aḥsanu — with what is best, better — indicates that approaching orphan wealth allowed only if intention best, enhancing, improving, protecting, not consuming, not harming. Ḥattā yablugha ashuddahu — until he attains maturity, strength — indicates limit: until orphan mature, strong, able to manage wealth, then return. Awfū bi-l-ʿahdi — fulfill pledge — indicates importance of keeping commitments. Inna al-ʿahda kāna mas'ūlan — pledge is accountable, questioned — indicates you will be questioned about pledges, accountable, record laid open will show. The believer who reflects will protect orphan wealth with best intention until maturity, fulfill pledges, knowing accountable, as part of most upright guidance, wisdom, that Qur'an guides to, leading to appreciated striving, mighty reward, and avoiding painful punishment.
