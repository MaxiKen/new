## Sūrah al-Shuʿarāʾ 26:216

> But if they disobey you, say, “I am certainly free of what you do.”

**Expanded Commentary**

**The Messenger Is Not Responsible for Their Disobedience**

The command gives the Prophet words for a painful situation: if people disobey, he is free of what they do. This is not permission to stop caring about them. It is a declaration that their refusal does not become his sin. He has conveyed the warning and cannot carry a person's chosen worship or corruption as though the choice belonged to him.

The statement also protects the purity of the message. The Prophet does not compromise with disobedience to preserve a relationship. He can love relatives and remain gracious to believers while refusing to approve the conduct of those who reject Allah's command. Affection cannot turn false worship or injustice into something for which the messenger accepts blame.

**Disavowal Concerns the Deed, Not a License for Cruelty**

To be free of what they do means to reject the wrongdoing and decline responsibility for it. It does not require the Prophet to act with personal hatred or to deny the humanity of those who disobey. Quran 10:41 gives a parallel distinction between each side's deeds and each side's responsibility.

This distinction matters in moral correction. A person can say that fraud, idolatry, or oppression is unacceptable without claiming that he knows the full hidden account of every wrongdoer. He can protect victims, oppose the act, and still hope that the one who committed it will repent. Separation from wrongdoing is not the same as delight in another person's ruin.

**Warning Has a Boundary**

The messenger's duty is to warn and convey, not to control the inward response of the audience. Once the warning has been made clear, the hearer bears a responsibility that cannot be transferred back to the speaker. This boundary keeps the Prophet from believing that every rejection proves a defect in his delivery or that every acceptance proves his personal power.

It also keeps followers from making a human leader the owner of their faith. They cannot say that they disobeyed because the messenger failed to force them, and the messenger cannot say that he is accountable for choices he opposed. Allah gives guidance, the messenger conveys it, and the individual answers for what he does with it.

**Freedom From Wrong Protects the Community**

A community needs people who can refuse participation in its corruption. If every relationship requires silent approval, wrongdoing becomes stronger through family loyalty and social fear. The Prophet's declaration teaches that belonging has a limit: no one may demand that a believer endorse disobedience in order to remain connected.

The words are therefore firm but not despairing. They leave open the possibility that the disobedient will change, while making clear that the messenger will not carry the old conduct as his own. Truthful separation can be an act of mercy to the wrongdoer and protection for the faithful. It tells everyone where responsibility lies before Allah.

To be free of another person's deeds does not mean becoming indifferent to the person. A believer can refuse to join a corrupt practice, warn against it, and still hope that the wrongdoer will repent. The separation is from the act and its moral approval. It is not a command to abandon justice, family duty, or the possibility of reconciliation when the person leaves the wrong.

Quran 10:41 gives a parallel form of this boundary by distinguishing the Prophet's deeds from the deeds of those who reject him. The principle protects both sides. The messenger cannot be blamed for a refusal he opposed, and the opponent cannot borrow the messenger's obedience as a substitute for his own. Every person meets Allah with a record that belongs to that person.

This is especially important for followers who place too much weight on a leader's personality. No teacher can obey on behalf of the student, and no student can transfer his disobedience to the teacher who warned him. Religious guidance creates responsibility rather than removing it. The messenger's disavowal makes that responsibility visible and prevents the community from hiding behind the claim that someone else should have saved it.

The statement also teaches how to leave a harmful group without inventing superiority. A person can say that he is free of a practice because Allah forbids it, while admitting that he too depends on Allah's mercy. He does not need to call himself pure or to predict the final state of everyone who remains. He simply refuses participation and keeps the moral line clear.

When this boundary is maintained, warning remains honest. The Prophet can be gracious to believers and firm toward disobedience without confusing the two. He can invite the wrongdoer back while refusing to carry the wrong as his own. Allah knows the difference between a servant who warned and a person who chose to disobey, and His justice assigns each responsibility correctly.

This boundary is also a protection against inherited guilt. A child is not automatically responsible for a parent's disbelief, and a follower is not automatically innocent because a leader approved the act. People influence one another, but influence does not erase choice. The messenger's disavowal places the deed back with the person who insists on doing it.

At the same time, a person who has influence cannot hide behind this verse and claim that his conduct affects no one. The Prophet is told to warn because guidance and example matter. The disobedient answer for their own deeds, while the person who encouraged or enabled them answers for his own influence. Freedom from another's act is not freedom from one's duty to avoid leading others into it.

The verse leaves a narrow but hopeful path. Reject the wrongdoing now, repair the harm where possible, and do not demand that the messenger or community carry your refusal. If you return to Allah, the earlier separation from the act need not become a declaration that you are beyond mercy. It is a line drawn around the wrong so that the person can step back from it.
