## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:124

> But those who do good— whether male or female—and have faith will enter Paradise and will never be wronged ˹even as much as˺ the speck on a date stone.

### Expanded Commentary

**No gendered exclusion from reward**

The verse places male and female believers side by side: whoever does good and has faith enters Paradise. In a chapter dealing with women’s legal rights, this affirmation is especially important. Spiritual reward does not depend on being male, and women’s good deeds are not merely counted through the men of their families.

The promise that no one is wronged even by the speck of a date stone echoes the chapter’s insistence on precise justice. A quiet act of worship, care, courage, or patience is fully known. The believer should not measure worth by public recognition or by the scale of worldly power.

The verse also prevents a false opposition between faith and good work. Faith gives the action its orientation; good action gives faith public and practical form. Both men and women are addressed as responsible servants.

**Equal accountability and reward**

The parallel mention of men and women also guards against measuring a woman’s piety only through a male relative. Her faith and good work stand before Allah directly. The smallness of an action in public eyes is no reason to neglect it when the divine scale does not omit even a particle.
