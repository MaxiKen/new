## Sūrah al-Maʿārij 70:31

> but whoever seeks beyond that are the transgressors.

**Expanded Commentary**

**Beyond Means Transgression**

Fa-man ibtaghā warāʾa dhālika fa-ulāʾika hum al-ʿādūn — "whoever seeks beyond that: those, they are the transgressors." Warāʾa dhālika means beyond that — past spouses and lawful possessions. ʿĀdūn names transgressors, boundary-crossers — from ʿ-d-w, exceeding limits, hostility through excess. Beyond stands defined with legal precision: outside verse 30's gates. Transgression stands named with moral clarity: crossing is ʿudwān, aggression against limits. Such clarity creates binary: inside blameless, outside transgressing — no man's land between. Readers should map bounds exactly: spouses inside covenant; everything else beyond. What beyond beckons sweetly, transgression brands bitterly. Stay inside deliberately; never cross casually. Beyond: transgression's territory.

Ibtaghā — seeks, pursues, desires actively. Intention itself stands criminalized: seeking convicts before doing completes. Pursuit proves transgression pre-act: hunting beyond bounds, fantasies fed, occasions engineered. Such interiority guards at the earliest point: crush seeking and doing never follows. Thought-patterns indicted include fed fantasies, Hunted occasions, planned trespasses. The verse targets pursuit's machinery, not just arrival. Readers should police pursuits ruthlessly: starve fantasies by redirecting thought, flee occasions by changing routes, confess temptations early. What seeking starts in mind, doing completes in flesh. Kill seeking promptly; spare doing permanently. Pursuit monitored preserves purity. Hunt holiness instead.

Binary stands absolute: inside blameless per verse 30, outside transgressing per this verse. No third zone exists: gray claimed proves black on inspection. Loopholes imagined — modern customs, mutual consent, strict privacy, harmless discretion — all voided: warāʾa (beyond) covers every beyond. Such closure simplifies radically: two categories, total coverage. Conscience cannot negotiate; culture cannot amend. Readers should abandon loophole-hunting permanently: beyond is beyond regardless of era. What the verse binaries absolutely, conscience must honor simply. No exceptions qualify the exception; no footnotes soften warāʾa. Inside or transgressing — choose inside daily. Loopholes: closed eternally.

**Transgression Against Three**

Three victims suffer each trespass: God (limits trespassed, Lawgiver defied), self (dignity squandered, health risked, conscience stained), others (spouses betrayed, families fractured, societies infected). ʿUdwān wounds triply with single acts. Against God: defiance of explicit bounds. Against self: honor pawned cheaply, peace forfeited. Against others: trusts shattered, innocents stricken. Such triple-harm magnifies "private" sin into public wreckage: nothing stays private past consequences. Readers should count victims before acting: three harmed per trespass, minimum. What bounds protect simultaneously (Lord's right, soul's health, neighbor's trust), crossing wounds simultaneously. Spare all three; stay inside. Victims: none when fenced. Count thrice; cross never.

Fa-ulāʾika hum — "those, they are." Double pronouns thunder emphasis: THOSE are THE transgressors, unmistakably. Arabic doubling stresses identification: verdict certain, category closed. Such grammar appears at the Book's weightiest verdicts — "they are the losers," "they are the guided" — emphasis reserved for destiny. No ambiguity survives doubling: transgressors, undeniably, emphatically. Readers should hear grammatical thunder as mercy: clarity this absolute prevents accidental crossing. What grammar doubles for stress, conscience must single for obedience. Emphasis received gratefully; obedience due promptly. They-are warned explicitly; be never-they deliberately. Pronouns: doubled, destiny sealed.

**Boundaries Preach the Lawgiver**

Limits imply Limiter necessarily. Moral lines presuppose Moral Author: bounds bespeak Commander, fences reveal Fencer. Chastity-legislation theologizes by existing: purity commanded proves Commander cares about purity. Such inference evangelizes quietly: law points past itself to Lawgiver. Deny the Author and lines erase; affirm the Author and lines bind. Every fence testifies: Someone fenced, Someone cares, Someone watches. Readers should read bounds upward to their Source: fences reveal Fencer's wisdom, mercy, holiness. What limits legislate in conduct, Lordship authors in reality. Honor fences practically; know Fencer personally. Boundaries are theology made fence. Trace lines to Lord. Trace lines further: every fence-line runs back to mercy's drafting table. Study limits till the Limiter's love appears — protection, never punishment. What lines legislate for conduct, love authored for creatures. Follow fences to their Fountain; meet Law within Love. Traced fully, bounds spell mercy. Read fences; find the Fencer's heart.

**Repentance Reopens the Gate**

Fallen? Return. Tawbah reopens what transgression barred: remorse felt, sin quit, resolve fixed. The gate stands open till death — no fall final while breath lasts. Such mercy encourages strugglers: stumbling differs from settling. Rise per fall; never camp in sin. Shame should drive Godward, not away — mercy awaits returners. Readers fallen should rise instantly: repent now, resolve firmly, relapse never deliberately. What repentance reopens, persistence keeps. Fallen yet returning beats standing yet proud. Rise; return; remain.

Moral fashions cycle; divine bounds abide. Every age normalizes some trespass — past or present, East or West. Revelation's fences ignore fashion: warāʾa dhālika outlasts all eras. Such permanence anchors: believers navigate shifting seas by fixed stars. Chastity's bounds span all revelations — Torah, Gospel, Qur'an agree. Universality proves transcendence: no culture invented this fence; heaven revealed it. Readers pressed by permissive peers should stand on permanence: fashions fade, bounds remain. What cultures permit temporarily, audit condemns eternally. Stand fixed; outlast fashions. Bounds: heaven's constants.

**Resolve Fortified Daily**

Morning resolve, evening review: vigilance routinized. Dawn vows chastity — "today, guarded." Dusk audits — "kept or breached?" Such rhythm prevents drift: daily reset. Relapse patterns get mapped: triggers identified, avoidances planned. Resolve fortified by routine outlasts resolve by mood. Accountability aids: mentors consulted, friends enlisted. Readers should ritualize resolve: fixed morning words, fixed evening reckoning. What routine guards, spontaneity loses. Fortify daily; stand permanently. Resolved mornings, reviewed evenings, guarded life.

**Trusts Follow Chastity Next**

Purity section complete across verses 29-31; fidelity section follows at verse 32: trusts guarded, covenants kept. Body fenced, then word bound: chastity yields to honesty. The list progresses outward naturally: members governed, then promises kept. Readers should carry purity toward fidelity: guarded bodies, kept words, matching integrity. What follows shepherds trusts. The next verse commands guarding trusts and covenants, fidelity extending purity outward.
