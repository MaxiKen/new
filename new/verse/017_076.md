## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:76

> **They were about to intimidate you to drive you out of the land ˹of Mecca˺, but then they would not have survived after you ˹had left˺ except for a little while.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**They Were About To Intimidate You To Drive You Out Of Land**

Wa-in kādū la-yastafizzūnaka mina al-arḍi li-yukhrijūka minhā — and indeed they were about to intimidate, provoke you to drive you out from land. Wa-in, and indeed, indeed, surely, they were about to. Kādū from k-w-d, to be about to, almost, nearly, as kādū they were about to, almost, as previous kādū lure away, and kādū intimidate. La-yastafizzūnaka from f-z-z, to intimidate, provoke, make to slip, cause to leave, from istafazza, to intimidate, provoke, make to tremble, to cause to leave, as yastafizzūnaka intimidate you, provoke you, to drive you out, as Satan incite whoever you can with voice, istafiz, incite, entice, and yastafizzūnaka intimidate you, provoke you, to drive out. Mina al-arḍi, from land, from earth, from land of Mecca, as arḍ land, earth, as land of Mecca, as Mecca, as land. Li-yukhrijūka from kh-r-j, to drive out, expel, make exit, from akhraja, to drive out, expel, make exit, as yukhrijūka drive you out, expel you, from land, as they wanted to drive Prophet out of Mecca, intimidate to drive out. Minhā, from it, from land. Al-Ṭabarī explains that they, disbelievers, polytheists, were about to intimidate you, provoke you, to drive you out of land of Mecca, as they tried to intimidate Prophet to drive him out, expel him, as they intimidated, provoked, as they wanted to expel, as they did later, as Hijrah, etc., but Allah protected, as Lord encompasses people, knows best, etc., and as Our way with messengers no change, as messengers intimidated to drive out, etc.

**But Then They Would Not Have Survived After You Left Except Little While**

Wa-idhan lā yalbathūna khilāfaka illā qalīlan — and then they would not remain after you, behind you, except little while, short time. Wa-idhan, and then, in that case, then, if they drove you out. Lā yalbathūna from l-b-th, to remain, stay, tarry, as labitha, to remain, stay, as yalbathūna remain, stay, as labithtum remained, as remained only little while, as Day call thinking remained only little while. Khilāfaka from kh-l-f, after you, behind you, after you left, behind you, from khilāf, after, behind, after you, behind you, as after you left, behind you. Illā qalīlan, except little, only little while, short time, from q-l-l, little, small, few, as qalīlan little while, short time, as little while, as worldly life little while, as remained only little while. Al-Ṭabarī explains that then they would not have survived, remained after you left, behind you, except for little while, short time, meaning if they drove you out of land of Mecca, intimidated to drive out, they would not remain, survive after you left, behind you, except little while, short time, then would be destroyed, punished severely, as divine law, as every wicked society destroyed or punished severely before Day written in Record, many generations destroyed after Noah, etc., and as Our way with messengers, when messengers driven out, people destroyed after little while, as history, as Ad, Thamud, etc.

**Why Intimidate To Drive Out And Not Survive Except Little While**

Why intimidate to drive out of land and not survive except little while? Because disbelievers, polytheists, tried to intimidate Prophet to drive him out of Mecca, as they wanted to expel, as they intimidated, provoked, as they were about to, almost, kādū, almost, about to, as they tried to lure away, tempt, hoping attribute falsely, taken as close friend, and intimidate to drive out, as ways against messengers, but Our way with messengers, divine law, is that when messengers driven out, intimidated to drive out, people do not remain, survive after them, behind them, except little while, short time, then destroyed, punished severely, as every wicked society destroyed or punished severely before Day written in Record, as many generations destroyed after Noah, etc., as history, as Ad, Thamud, etc., destroyed after messengers driven out, etc., and as Lord encompasses people, knows best, etc., and as Prophet not keeper over them, etc., but warner, deliverer, steadfast, not lured, not inclined, not intimidated, etc.

**Connection To Lure Away And Steadfast And Double Punishment And Our Way No Change**

Verses 73-75 they were about to lure you away from what We revealed hoping attribute something else falsely and taken as close friend, had We not made you steadfast you would have inclined a little, then double punishment in life and death, no helper against Us. Verse 76 they were about to intimidate you to drive you out of land of Mecca, but then they would not have survived after you left except little while. Verse 77 this has been Our way with messengers We sent before you, you will never find any change in Our way, showing sequence: luring away, steadfast, double punishment warning, intimidating to drive out, not surviving except little while, Our way with messengers no change, as divine law, as every wicked society destroyed or punished severely before Day written in Record, etc., and as messengers made steadfast, not lured, not inclined, not intimidated, etc.

**Link To Our Way With Messengers And No Change And Prayer And Night Prayer**

Verse 77 Our way with messengers no change, you will never find any change in Our way, indicating divine law, sunnah, way with messengers, consistent, no change, as messengers sent before, disbelievers tried to lure away, intimidate to drive out, but Our way, divine law, is steadfast, no change, as messengers protected, people destroyed after little while if drove out, etc., and next verses 78-80 about prayer, dawn prayer witnessed, night prayer additional, station of praise, honourable entrance and exit, supporting authority, etc., showing Prophet's way, prayer, etc., as part of Our way with messengers, no change, as prayer, etc.

**Linguistic Note On Kadu And Yastafizzunaka And Arḍ And Yukhrijuka And Yalbathuna And Khilafaka And Qalilan**

Kādū from k-w-d, to be about to, almost, nearly, from kāda. Yastafizzūnaka from f-z-z, to intimidate, provoke, make to slip, cause to leave, from istafazza. Arḍ from a-r-ḍ, land, earth, from arḍ. Yukhrijūka from kh-r-j, to drive out, expel, from akhraja. Yalbathūna from l-b-th, to remain, stay, tarry, from labitha. Khilāf from kh-l-f, after, behind, from khalafa, to be after, behind. Qalīl from q-l-l, little, small, few, from qalīl. The verse's structure — they were about to intimidate you to drive you out from land, and then they would not remain after you except little — with about to intimidate to drive out from land and then not remain after you except little, shows attempt to intimidate to drive out and consequence not remaining after you except little while, short time, then destroyed, as divine law, Our way with messengers no change.

**How This Verse Teaches Intimidation To Drive Out And Short Survival**

This verse teaches intimidation to drive out and short survival after: they, disbelievers, were about to intimidate you, provoke you, to drive you out of land of Mecca, from land, to expel you, as they tried, as they were about to, almost, kādū, almost, about to, as they tried to lure away, tempt, etc., but then they would not have survived, remained after you left, behind you, except for little while, short time, then would be destroyed, punished severely, as divine law, as every wicked society destroyed or punished severely before Day written in Record, many generations destroyed after Noah, etc., and as Our way with messengers, when messengers driven out, people destroyed after little while, as history, as Ad, Thamud, etc., destroyed. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should know disbelievers tried to intimidate Prophet to drive him out of Mecca, but if they had driven out, they would not remain after him except little while, short time, then destroyed, as divine law, Our way with messengers no change, as messengers protected, people destroyed after little while, so do not intimidate messengers to drive out, follow most upright guidance, wisdom, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, avoiding destruction, severe punishment, painful punishment, Hell, torment fearsome.

**A Reflection On Little While After Driving Out**

Illā qalīlan — except little, only little while, short time — indicates that if disbelievers drove Prophet out of land of Mecca, intimidated to drive out, they would not remain, survive after him, behind him, except little while, short time, then destroyed, punished severely, as divine law, as every wicked society destroyed or punished severely before Day written in Record, as many generations destroyed after Noah, etc., and as Our way with messengers no change, you will never find any change in Our way, as messengers sent before, disbelievers tried to drive out, but people destroyed after little while, as history, as Ad, Thamud, etc. The believer who reflects will not intimidate Prophet, messenger, to drive out of land, but follow, believe, be faithful servant, no authority for Satan, Lord Guardian sufficient, say best, follow most upright, wisdom, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, avoiding destruction after little while, severe punishment, as divine law, Our way no change.
