## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:8

> **Perhaps your Lord will have mercy on you ˹if you repent˺, but if you return ˹to sin˺, We will return ˹to punishment˺. And We have made Hell a ˹permanent˺ confinement for the disbelievers.”**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Perhaps Your Lord Will Have Mercy If You Repent**

ʿAsā rabbukum an yarḥamakum — perhaps your Lord will have mercy on you. ʿAsā from ʿ-s-y means perhaps, maybe, indicating hope and possibility. Rabbukum, your Lord, your Nurturer. An yarḥamakum, that He will have mercy on you, from r-ḥ-m, mercy. Al-Ṭabarī explains that after describing two corruptions and punishments, Allah says perhaps your Lord will have mercy on you if you repent, return to obedience, abandon corruption and arrogance. The phrase ʿasā indicates that mercy is hoped for after repentance, not guaranteed without repentance, but with repentance it is expected because Allah is Merciful. Al-Qurṭubī says this was fulfilled when Israelites repented and were restored, and that it remains open: if they repent, mercy will come. The brackets ˹if you repent˺ clarify condition: mercy if you repent.

**If You Return To Sin, We Will Return To Punishment**

Wa-in ʿudttum ʿudnā — but if you return to sin, We will return to punishment. In ʿudttum, if you return, from ʿ-w-d, to return. ʿUdnā, We will return, from same root, indicating reciprocal return: if you return to corruption, We will return to punishment. Al-Ṭabarī says if you return to sin, corruption, arrogance, We will return to punishment, as happened second time and will happen again if you return. The phrase shows divine law of reciprocity: your return to sin leads to Our return to punishment. It is both warning and justice: you control whether mercy continues or punishment returns by whether you return to sin or not.

**Hell As Permanent Confinement For Disbelievers**

Wa-jaʿalnā jahannama li-l-kāfirīna ḥaṣīran — and We made Hell for disbelievers a confinement, prison, mat. Jaʿalnā from j-ʿ-l, to make. Jahannam, Hell. Li-l-kāfirīna, for disbelievers, those who cover truth. Ḥaṣīran from ḥ-ṣ-r, to confine, surround, from same root as ḥaṣīr, mat, prison, because mat confines and surrounds, or prison that confines. Al-Ṭabarī explains ḥaṣīran means confinement, prison, place that confines and surrounds disbelievers, from which they cannot escape. Al-Qurṭubī says ḥaṣīran means prison, mat that confines, or bed, indicating Hell is confinement, permanent prison for disbelievers. The brackets ˹permanent˺ clarify that confinement is permanent, not temporary.

**Why Mercy And Return Are Paired**

ʿAsā rabbukum an yarḥamakum, perhaps Lord will have mercy, and wa-in ʿudttum ʿudnā, if you return We will return, are paired to show that mercy and punishment both depend on human return: return to repentance leads to mercy, return to sin leads to punishment. Al-Rāzī explains that this pairing shows divine justice and mercy: mercy is offered after punishment if you repent, but if you return to sin, punishment returns, showing that you control outcome by your choice. The verse therefore offers hope and warning together: hope of mercy if you repent, warning of return to punishment if you return to sin.

**Hell As Confinement**

Jaʿalnā jahannama li-l-kāfirīna ḥaṣīran — We made Hell for disbelievers confinement. Ḥaṣīr originally means mat, but also prison, confinement, because it surrounds and confines. Al-Zamakhsharī says ḥaṣīran means prison, confinement that surrounds, indicating Hell surrounds disbelievers from all sides, confining them, as 29:54 says Hell will encompass disbelievers. The image of Hell as confinement contrasts with Gardens of Eternity that are spacious with rivers flowing. Confinement is fitting for those who were arrogant and sought to be high; they will be confined low. The phrase li-l-kāfirīna, for disbelievers, indicates that Hell as confinement is specifically for disbelievers who persist in disbelief and corruption.

**Connection To Two Corruptions History**

This verse concludes history of two corruptions: first corruption punished by servants of great might ravaging homes, then restoration with upper hand, wealth, offspring, numbers, principle if you do good for yourselves if you do wrong against yourselves, second corruption punished with disgracing faces and Temple entry and utter destruction, then perhaps mercy if you repent, if you return to sin We return to punishment, and Hell made confinement for disbelievers. The history shows cycle and ends with possibility of mercy and warning of return and final confinement. The lesson for Makkans is similar: you have been warned, you have been given Qur'an that guides to most upright, if you repent and follow, mercy, if you return to sin and corruption and arrogance, punishment returns, and Hell is confinement for disbelievers.

**Linguistic Note On ʿAsā And Ḥaṣīr**

ʿAsā from ʿ-s-y, perhaps, maybe, indicating hope. Rabbukum from r-b-b, Lord. An yarḥamakum from r-ḥ-m, mercy. Wa-in ʿudttum from ʿ-w-d, to return. ʿUdnā from same root, We return. Jaʿalnā from j-ʿ-l, to make. Jahannam, Hell. Li-l-kāfirīna, for disbelievers, from k-f-r. Ḥaṣīran from ḥ-ṣ-r, to confine, surround, from same root as ḥaṣīr, mat, prison. The verse's structure — perhaps Lord will have mercy if you repent, if you return to sin We return to punishment, We made Hell confinement for disbelievers — with hope, reciprocal warning, and final confinement, creates conclusion to Israelite history that is both hopeful and warning, guiding listener to choose repentance and mercy over return to sin and confinement.

**How This Verse Teaches About Mercy And Justice**

This verse teaches that mercy is possible after punishment if you repent, but justice requires that if you return to sin, punishment returns. It shows that divine mercy does not mean ignoring sin; it means giving chance to repent. If chance is used to return to sin, punishment returns, and final confinement is Hell for disbelievers. The practical lesson is to repent after sin, not to return to sin after being given mercy and restoration, and to know that Hell is confinement for those who persist in disbelief. For Makkans who were being warned, it says perhaps Lord will have mercy if you repent and follow Qur'an that guides to most upright, but if you return to corruption and arrogance and plotting, punishment returns, and Hell is permanent confinement.
