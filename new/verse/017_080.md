## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:80

> **And say, “My Lord! Grant me an honourable entrance and an honourable exit and give me a supporting authority from Yourself.”**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Say My Lord Grant Me Honourable Entrance And Honourable Exit**

Wa-qul rabbi adkhilnī mudkhala ṣidqin wa-akhrijnī mukhraja ṣidqin — and say, My Lord, make me enter entrance of truth, sincerity, honourable entrance, and make me exit exit of truth, honourable exit. Wa-qul from q-w-l, to say, command to Prophet to say, supplicate. Rabbi from r-b-b, My Lord, My Lord, as rabbi My Lord. Adkhilnī from d-kh-l, to make enter, cause to enter, from adkhala, to make enter, cause to enter, as adkhilnī make me enter, grant me entrance. Mudkhala from d-kh-l, entrance, place of entrance, manner of entrance, from mudkhal, entrance, place of entrance, manner of entrance, as mudkhala ṣidqin entrance of truth, sincerity, honourable entrance, as entrance with truth, sincerity, honourable. Ṣidqin from ṣ-d-q, truth, sincerity, honesty, from ṣidq, truth, sincerity, as ṣidq, truth, sincerity, honourable, as entrance of truth, sincere, honourable. Wa-akhrijnī from kh-r-j, to make exit, cause to exit, from akhraja, to make exit, cause to exit, as akhrijnī make me exit, grant me exit. Mukhraja from kh-r-j, exit, place of exit, manner of exit, from mukhraj, exit, place of exit, manner of exit, as mukhraja ṣidqin exit of truth, sincerity, honourable exit, as exit with truth, sincerity, honourable. Ṣidqin, truth, sincerity, as truth, sincerity, honourable. Al-Ṭabarī explains that say, My Lord, grant me honourable entrance and honourable exit, meaning make me enter entrance of truth, sincerity, honourable entrance, and make me exit exit of truth, honourable exit, as supplication for honourable entrance and exit, truthful, sincere, honourable, as entrance and exit with truth, sincerity, honourable, as Prophet supplicates for honourable entrance and exit, as Hijrah, entrance to Medina, exit from Mecca, etc., as honourable entrance and exit, with truth, sincerity.

**Give Me Supporting Authority From Yourself**

Wa-ijʿal lī min ladunka sulṭānan naṣīran — and make for me from Yourself, from Your presence, authority, supporting, helper. Wa-ijʿal from j-ʿ-l, to make, grant, give, as jaʿalnā We made, etc., and ijʿal make, grant. Lī, for me, to me. Min ladunka, from Yourself, from Your presence, from near You, from ladun, presence, near, from ladunka from Yourself, from Your presence. Sulṭānan from s-l-ṭ, authority, power, proof, from sulṭān, authority, power, as sulṭānan authority, power, as supporting authority, authority that supports, helps. Naṣīran from n-ṣ-r, supporting, helper, helping, from naṣara, to help, support, as naṣīr helper, supporter, and naṣīran supporting, helper, helping, as supporting authority, authority that supports, helps, helper, supporting. Al-Ṭabarī explains that and give me, grant me from Yourself, from Your presence, supporting authority, authority, power, that supports, helps, helper, supporting, as supplication for supporting authority from Allah, from His presence, authority that supports, helps, as Prophet supplicates for supporting authority from Allah, to support, help, as he was about to be driven out of land, intimidated to drive out, etc., and needed supporting authority, as Our way with messengers no change, etc., and as prayer, night prayer, station of praise, honourable entrance and exit, supporting authority, etc.

**Why Honourable Entrance And Exit And Supporting Authority**

Why honourable entrance and honourable exit and supporting authority from Yourself? Because Prophet was about to be intimidated to drive out of land of Mecca, as verse 76 says they were about to intimidate you to drive you out of land, but then they would not survive after you left except little while, and Our way with messengers no change, you will never find any change in Our way, as verse 77 says, and prayer from decline until darkness and dawn witnessed and night prayer additional station of praise, as verses 78-79 say, as part of Our way, divine law, no change, and now supplication for honourable entrance and exit and supporting authority from Yourself, as Prophet supplicates for honourable entrance, entrance of truth, sincere, honourable, and honourable exit, exit of truth, sincere, honourable, and supporting authority from Allah, from His presence, to support, help, as he was about to be driven out, needed honourable entrance to Medina, honourable exit from Mecca, and supporting authority from Allah, to support, help, as Hijrah, etc., and as truth come falsehood vanished, falsehood bound to vanish, as verse 81 says, etc.

**Connection To Intimidate To Drive Out And Our Way No Change And Prayer And Station Of Praise**

Verse 76 they were about to intimidate you to drive you out of land of Mecca, but then they would not survive after you left except little while. Verse 77 Our way with messengers no change, you will never find any change in Our way. Verse 78 observe prayer from decline until darkness and dawn prayer witnessed. Verse 79 rise at last part of night offering additional prayers so Lord may raise you to station of praise. Verse 80 say My Lord grant me honourable entrance and honourable exit and give me supporting authority from Yourself, showing sequence: intimidate to drive out, not survive except little while, Our way no change, prayer, night prayer station of praise, honourable entrance and exit and supporting authority, as supplication for Hijrah, entrance to Medina, exit from Mecca, supporting authority from Allah, as part of Our way, divine law, no change, as prayer, etc., and as truth come falsehood vanished, as next verse says.

**Link To Truth Come Falsehood Vanished And Quran Healing Mercy And When Grant Favours Turn Away**

Next verses 81-83 and declare truth has come and falsehood has vanished, indeed falsehood bound to vanish, We send down Quran as healing and mercy for believers but it only increases wrongdoers in loss, when We grant people favours they turn away acting arrogantly, but when touched with evil they lose all hope, showing that honourable entrance and exit and supporting authority lead to truth coming, falsehood vanishing, Quran healing mercy, etc., as part of Our way, divine law, no change.
