## Sūrah an-Nūr 24:43

> Do you not see that Allah gently drives the clouds, then joins them together, piling them up into masses, from which you see raindrops come forth? And He sends down from the sky mountains ˹of clouds˺ loaded with hail, pouring it on whoever He wills and averting it from whoever He wills. The flash of the clouds’ lightning nearly takes away eyesight.

**Expanded Commentary**

**Dynamic Meteorology: yuzjī saḥāban**

The Qur'an directs human contemplation toward the dynamic, awe-inspiring processes of atmospheric physics: *a-lam tara anna Allāha yuzjī saḥāban thumma yuʾallifu baynahū thumma yajʿaluhū rukāmā*. The verb *yuzjī* depicts gentle, gradual driving; winds are steered by divine decree to push isolated, scattered fragments of water vapor across vast atmospheric expanses.

Modern meteorological science reveals that cloud formation begins precisely with this gentle movement: wind currents herd cumulus clouds into convergence zones across great oceanic and continental expanses. The subsequent phase, *thumma yuʾallifu baynah* (then He joins them together), describes how micro-clouds coalesce, combining their thermal updrafts and moisture reserves into unified structures. Divine providence operates through precise physical mechanisms, choreographing invisible air masses, temperatures, barometric pressures, and moisture levels to initiate the complex cycle of terrestrial hydration that sustains life across continents, displaying unmatched creative power and engineering perfection in every gentle breeze.

**Cumulonimbus Architecture: yajʿaluhu rukāman**

The text describes the third stage of cloud development with striking accuracy: *thumma yajʿaluhū rukāmā*, then piling them up into dense masses. The noun *rukām* signifies vertical stacking, layering, and condensation into towering heaps.

In modern atmospheric science, this corresponds to the development of massive cumulonimbus clouds. When smaller cumulus clouds merge, powerful vertical updrafts carry moist air thousands of meters upward into the colder reaches of the troposphere. The cloud structure expands vertically, piling mass upon mass until it forms a gigantic, towering anvil-shaped mountain of condensed moisture. This vertical piling is essential for cloud dynamics, enabling the condensation, collision, and coalescence of billions of microscopic water droplets into heavy raindrops capable of falling through turbulent updrafts to reach the thirsty ground below, nourishing vegetation, sustaining wildlife, and replenishing vital springs that feed entire human communities.

**Emergence of Life-Giving Rain: yakhruju min khilālih**

The verse traces the miraculous consequence of this atmospheric architecture: *fa-tarā al-wadqa yakhruju min khilālih*, from which you see raindrops come forth. The term *al-wadq* specifically designates gentle, penetrating raindrops emerging from the interior recesses (*khilāl*) of the cloud mass.

Rain does not fall as a catastrophic, crushing solid sheet of water that would destroy vegetation, scour topsoil, and crush human dwellings. Instead, divine wisdom engineers atmospheric resistance, air friction, and surface tension so that moisture falls as billions of measured, distinct droplets. Emerging from the very heart of dark, towering storm clouds, this measured rain revives parched landscapes, fills underground aquifers, feeds roaring rivers, and germinates sleeping seeds, demonstrating how divine mercy emerges out of seeming darkness to sustain life across the globe, transforming barren wilderness into lush gardens of abundant provision, beauty, and renewal.

**Atmospheric Mountains and Catastrophic Hail: jibālin fīhā min barad**

The discourse ascends to the violent, frigid heights of the storm cloud: *wa-yunazzilu mina al-samāʾi min jibālin fīhā min barad*, and He sends down from the sky mountains loaded with hail. Classical exegetes noted two profound interpretations of *jibāl* (mountains).

First, looking upward at cumulonimbus clouds, or looking down upon them from aircraft, they present the literal visual appearance of immense, snow-capped mountain ranges rising miles into the sky. Second, inside these atmospheric "mountains," supercooled water droplets circulate through violent vertical currents, freezing onto ice pellets layer by layer to form destructive hail (*barad*). When these frozen missiles plummet to earth, they possess formidable destructive force, capable of flattening crops, shattering roofs, injuring livestock, and devastating agricultural economies in minutes, revealing the dual nature of weather as both mercy and trial, warning human hearts of latent power and reminding them of divine majesty.

**Divine Sovereignty over Blessings and Afflictions: yuṣību bihi man yashāʾ**

The distribution of weather phenomena is governed by precise divine sovereignty: *fa-yuṣību bihī man yashāʾu wa-yaṣrifuhū ʿan man yashāʾ*, pouring it on whoever He wills and averting it from whoever He wills. In a single valley, hail may obliterate the orchards of one village while completely sparing neighboring fields just meters away.

Meteorologists recognize the extreme localized nature of hailstorms, which often travel in narrow, erratic swaths determined by micro-climatic turbulence. The Qur'an elevates this physical reality to spiritual awareness: weather is never an indifferent, mechanical accident. God employs atmospheric phenomena as instruments of both mercy and testing. Rain brings fertility and wealth to one community, while localized hail brings financial hardship or divine warning to another, reminding humanity of its absolute vulnerability and total dependence upon divine grace for daily survival, food security, and economic stability.

**The Blinding Glare of Cosmic Electricity: yakādu sanā barqih**

The verse culminates in an intense sensory climax: *yakādu sanā barqihi yadhhabu bi-al-abṣār*, the flash of the clouds' lightning nearly takes away eyesight. Within the towering cumulonimbus mountain, violent collisions between ice crystals and supercooled water generate massive electrical charges.

When these colossal charges discharge as lightning bolts, they unleash blinding flashes of light (*sanā barqih*) that illuminate the dark heavens with terrifying brilliance and thunderous power. The overwhelming flash temporarily overpowers human retinal receptors, threatening total blindness and shattering night darkness. Symbolically, this lightning flash mirrors the terrifying, incandescent majesty of divine revelation when it strikes the darkness of the world. Divine truth is so bright, so pure, and so intense that those unprepared for its brilliance are overwhelmed, while those with guided vision see by its flash the path leading to eternal salvation, moving through life with awe, profound humility, and unshakeable certainty beneath the shelter of divine grace.
