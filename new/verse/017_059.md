## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:59

> **Nothing keeps Us from sending the ˹demanded˺ signs except that they had ˹already˺ been denied by earlier peoples. And We gave Thamûd the shecamel as a clear sign, but they wrongfully rejected it. We only send the signs as a warning.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Nothing Keeps Us From Sending Demanded Signs Except They Were Denied By Earlier**

Wa-mā manaʿanā an nursila bi-l-āyāti illā an kadhdhaba bihā al-awwalūna — and nothing prevented Us that We send with signs except that they were denied by earlier, first peoples. Wa-mā, and not, nothing. Manaʿanā from m-n-ʿ, to prevent, keep, withhold, from manaʿa, to prevent, keep. An nursila from r-s-l, to send, dispatch, as rasūl messenger, We send, as nursila We send with signs. Bi-l-āyāti from a-y-y, signs, proofs, miracles, from āyah, sign, as āyatayni two signs night and day, etc. Illā an kadhdhaba, except that they denied, disbelieved, from k-dh-b, to deny, lie, disbelieve, as kadhdhaba denied. Bihā, with them, about them, signs. Al-awwalūna from a-w-l, earlier, first peoples, earlier generations, from awwal, first, earlier. Al-Ṭabarī explains that nothing keeps Us, prevents Us from sending demanded signs, miracles, that disbelievers demand, except that they, signs, had already been denied by earlier peoples, first generations, who demanded signs then denied them, so were destroyed, as divine law, as many generations destroyed after Noah, etc., and as every wicked society destroyed or punished severely before Day written in Record, so if We send demanded signs and they deny, they will be destroyed, punished severely, as earlier peoples, so We do not send demanded signs to avoid hastening destruction, as forbearance, as He is Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, but torment fearsome.

**We Gave Thamud Shecamel As Clear Sign But They Wrongfully Rejected**

Wa-ātaynā Thamūda al-nāqata mubṣiratan fa-ẓalamū bihā — and We gave Thamud shecamel as clear sign, bright, giving sight, but they wronged with it, wrongfully rejected it. Wa-ātaynā from a-t-y, to give, bring, as ātaynā We gave, as ātaynā David Psalms, etc. Thamūda, Thamud, people of Thamud, tribe of Thamud, who denied prophet Ṣāliḥ. Al-nāqata from n-w-q, shecamel, female camel, as nāqah, shecamel, as sign for Thamud, shecamel as clear sign, miracle. Mubṣiratan from b-ṣ-r, clear, bright, giving sight, making visible, as mubṣiratan bright sign of day, giving sight, as clear sign, visible, clear, as mubṣiratan clear sign. Fa-ẓalamū bihā from ẓ-l-m, to wrong, oppress, do injustice, wrongfully reject, from ẓalama, to wrong, oppress, as ẓālimūn wrongdoers. Bihā, with it, about it, shecamel. Al-Ṭabarī explains that We gave Thamud shecamel as clear sign, bright, visible, clear miracle, as sign, but they wronged with it, wrongfully rejected it, oppressed, did injustice, by hamstringing shecamel, killing it, denying sign, so were destroyed, punished severely, as example of earlier peoples who denied signs, demanded signs then denied, destroyed.

**We Only Send Signs As Warning**

Wa-mā nursilu bi-l-āyāti illā takhwīfan — and We do not send with signs except as warning, fear, to frighten. Wa-mā nursilu, and We do not send, We send not. Bi-l-āyāti, with signs, with miracles, signs. Illā takhwīfan from kh-w-f, warning, fear, to frighten, make fear, from khawwafa, to frighten, warn, from takhwīf, warning, frightening, making fear. Al-Ṭabarī explains that We only send signs, miracles, as warning, to frighten, warn, make fear, so that people may be mindful, remember, take heed, fear punishment, as signs are warning, not for entertainment, not to be demanded mockingly, but as warning, to frighten, so that they may believe, repent, as varying signs so they may be mindful, but drives farther away for disbelievers.

**Why No Demanded Signs Because Earlier Denied And Thamud Shecamel Example**

Why nothing keeps Us from sending demanded signs except they were denied by earlier peoples, and Thamud shecamel clear sign wrongfully rejected, We only send signs as warning? Because divine law: if demanded signs sent and denied, destruction, severe punishment follows, as earlier peoples denied signs, destroyed, as many generations destroyed after Noah, every wicked society destroyed or punished severely before Day written in Record, so to avoid hastening destruction, forbearance, Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, We do not send demanded signs that disbelievers demand mockingly, as they would deny and be destroyed, as Thamud given shecamel clear sign but wrongfully rejected, hamstrung, destroyed, as example. We only send signs as warning, to frighten, not as demanded entertainment, mocking, but as warning, so they may be mindful, fear.

**Connection To Varying Signs So They May Be Mindful But Drives Farther Away**

Verse 41 varied signs in Qur'an so they may be mindful but drives farther away, nufūran, aversion. Verse 59 nothing keeps Us from sending demanded signs except they were denied by earlier peoples, We gave Thamud shecamel clear sign but they wrongfully rejected, We only send signs as warning, showing that varying signs in Qur'an is warning, not demanded signs that would lead to destruction if denied, as earlier peoples denied and destroyed, as Thamud shecamel example, so Qur'an signs are warning, not demanded miracles that would hasten destruction, as forbearance.

**Link To Seven Heavens Earth Glorify And Hidden Barrier And Veils**

Verse 44 seven heavens, earth, all those in them glorify Him, not a thing except glorifies with praise, but you cannot comprehend, He is Forbearing Forgiving. Verse 45-46 hidden barrier, veils, deafness, aversion when Lord alone mentioned. Verse 59 signs as warning, not demanded, as earlier denied, Thamud shecamel, showing that signs are warning, forbearance, Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, but torment fearsome, so warning, not demanded signs that would lead to destruction if denied, as forbearance.

**Linguistic Note On Mana'ana And Ayat And Kadhdhaba And Naqah And Mubshirah And Zalamu And Takhwif**

Manaʿa from m-n-ʿ, to prevent, keep, withhold, from manaʿa. Nursila from r-s-l, to send. Āyāt from a-y-y, signs, miracles, plural of āyah. Kadhdhaba from k-dh-b, to deny, disbelieve, from kadhdhaba. Awwalūn from a-w-l, earlier, first peoples, plural of awwal. Ātaynā from a-t-y, to give. Thamūd, Thamud, people name. Nāqah from n-w-q, shecamel, from nāqah. Mubṣirah from b-ṣ-r, clear, bright, giving sight, from abṣara, to make see. Ẓalamū from ẓ-l-m, to wrong, oppress, from ẓalama. Takhwīf from kh-w-f, warning, frightening, from khawwafa. The verse's structure — nothing prevented Us that We send with signs except that they were denied by earlier, We gave Thamud shecamel clear sign but they wronged with it, We send not with signs except warning — with reason no demanded signs because earlier denied, example Thamud shecamel clear sign wrongfully rejected, and purpose signs as warning, shows divine law, forbearance, warning, not demanded entertainment.

**How This Verse Teaches Signs As Warning Not Demanded Entertainment**

This verse teaches that nothing keeps Allah from sending demanded signs, miracles, that disbelievers demand mockingly, except that they, signs, had already been denied by earlier peoples, first generations, who demanded then denied, destroyed, punished severely, as many generations destroyed after Noah, every wicked society destroyed or punished severely before Day written in Record, so to avoid hastening destruction, forbearance, Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, He does not send demanded signs that would lead to destruction if denied, as Thamud given shecamel clear sign, bright, but they wrongfully rejected, wronged with it, hamstrung, destroyed, as example, and We only send signs as warning, to frighten, warn, make fear, so they may be mindful, remember, take heed, not as demanded entertainment, mocking, but as warning. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should know signs, miracles, are warning, not demanded entertainment, should be mindful, fear, believe, not demand signs mockingly, not deny as earlier peoples, Thamud, etc., who denied and destroyed.

**A Reflection On Thamud Shecamel And Warning**

Al-nāqata mubṣiratan — shecamel clear sign, bright, giving sight, visible, clear miracle — for Thamud, people of Ṣāliḥ, as clear sign, miracle, shecamel from rock, etc., but they wronged with it, ẓalamū bihā, wrongfully rejected, oppressed, hamstrung, killed, denied, so destroyed, punished severely, as example of earlier peoples who denied signs, demanded signs then denied, destroyed, as divine law, every wicked society destroyed or punished severely before Day written in Record. Wa-mā nursilu bi-l-āyāti illā takhwīfan — We only send signs as warning, to frighten, warn — indicates purpose of signs, miracles, is warning, frightening, not entertainment, mocking, demanded, but warning, so they may be mindful, fear, believe, repent, as varying signs in Qur'an so they may be mindful, but drives farther away for disbelievers with barrier, veils, deafness, aversion, far astray cannot find Right Way. The believer who reflects will see signs as warning, be mindful, fear, believe, follow most upright guidance, wisdom, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, avoiding destruction, severe punishment, painful punishment, Hell, torment fearsome.
