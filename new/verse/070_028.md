## Sūrah al-Maʿārij 70:28

> ˹knowing that˺ none should feel secure from their Lord’s punishment—

**Expanded Commentary**

**No Immunity Exists**

Inna ʿadhāba rabbihim ghayru maʾmūn — "their Lord's punishment is unsecured-against." Maʾmūn (secure-from) negated absolutely: no immunity, no exemption, no safe-conduct. Inescapability elsewhere confirmed — its punishment clings inseparably (paraphrased from 25:65). Such absoluteness bars exceptions: rank exempts none, ritual none, lineage none. Security's door sealed universally. Readers should abandon immunity-hopes: piety protects, never insures. What none escapes presumptuously, all may escape penitently. No immunity; ample mercy. Feel unsafe; stay repentant.

Feeling-safe-while-sinful sedates. Amn (security) as complacency — "God forgives; continue" — tranquilizes conscience. Such false peace precedes ruin: sedated souls skip repentance. Gradual luring (istidrāj) operates thus — blessings continued, warnings muted, doom matured. Readers should distrust peaceful sinning: comfort in disobedience signals sedation. What security soothes, audit shatters. True peace flows from obedience, never despite it. Feel safe only when surrendered. Sedation is not salvation.

Verse 28 guards verse 27: fearers must never graduate to safety-feeling. Mushfiq (wary) plus ghayr maʾmūn (unsecure) — dread without endpoint. Even obedience never earns complacency: duties done, vigilance continues. Such guarding prevents Pharisaism — "I fear, therefore safe." Fear's fruit is humility, not assurance. Readers should fear and keep fearing: no stage exempts. What wariness begins, unsecurity sustains. Pray, give, tremble — then tremble still. Assurance belongs to heaven, not earth. Stay wary; stay unsecure.

**Presumption Versus Despair**

Two cliffs flank the path: presumption (safe-feeling in sin) and despair (mercy-despaired in guilt). Verse 28 fences presumption; mercy's verses fence despair. The middle path walks hope-fear: dreading punishment, hoping pardon. Wings both required — fear alone grounds (despair), hope alone (presumption). Readers should check balance: fearing without hoping? Read mercy. Hoping without fearing? Read this verse. What wings both lift, cliffs neither claim. Fly balanced. Fear and hope, dread and trust.

No rank exempts. Prophets feared; saints trembled; scholars paled. If the nearest stayed unsecure, the distant dare not presume. Such closure democratizes dread — all souls equally exposed, equally warned. Loopholes imagined (lineage, learning, liberality) all voided: ghayr maʾmūn admits none. Readers should close personal loopholes: "my prayers exempt"? No. "My charity insures"? No. What verse universalizes, conscience must personalize. No exemptions; only repentance. Close loopholes; open tears.

Paradox crowns: the never-secure get secured. Fear now purchases safety then — dread invested, deliverance harvested. Those presuming safety forfeit it; those refusing it receive. Such reversal rewards vigilance: unsecurity's wage is security. The Day secures the trembling, exposes the tranquil-in-sin. Readers should invest in holy insecurity: tremble now, rest then. What dread deposits, mercy multiplies. Feel unsafe penitently; dwell secure eternally. Insecurity now is security's seed.

Scales weigh sultans and street-sweepers identically. Power unweighed: thrones splinter on scales. Wealth unweighed: vaults vaporize. Lineage unweighed: pedigrees perish. Equality absolute — deeds sole currency. Such impartiality terrifies grandees, comforts lowly: justice blind to rank, keen to conduct. Readers should leverage equality: lowly? Excel in deeds. Lofty? Descend in humility. What audit ignores (status), mercy rewards (piety). Fear no man's rank; fear only record. Audit impartial; prepare accordingly.

**Repentance Replaces Security**

Tawbah answers unsecurity rightly. No immunity? Then perpetual return: sins confessed, resolves renewed, mercy sought. Repentance is insecurity's fruit — trembling that turns. Daily istighfār institutionalizes return: mornings open with seeking, nights close with it. Such rhythm replaces resting: never arrived, always returning. Readers should schedule repentance: fixed times, honest inventories. What return renews, complacency ruins. Turn daily; rest never. Security unfelt plus mercy sought equals safety granted. Repent; repeat; remain.

ʿĀd felt secure — valleys flourishing, warnings shrugged — then wind. Thamūd felt secure — mountains carved, camel hamstrung — then blast. Pharaoh felt secure — Nile commanded, Moses mocked — then sea. Security-feeling preludes ruin invariably: presumption's anesthesia before wrath's surgery. Verse 28 vaccinates against this civilizational disease: none secure, ever. Readers should distrust collective calm: prosperity preaching safety lies. What secure-feeling civilizations shared (ruin), secure-feeling souls inherit. History's refrain warns each generation. Feel trembling, not tranquil, amid blessings. Complacency is pre-ruin's symptom. Diagnose early; repent urgently.

Hope strives; presumption slumbers. Hoping pardon while pursuing sin insults mercy — promises conditioned on turning. True hope obeys anxiously: duties done, sins fled, mercy begged. Such hope honors: striving proves sincerity. Presumption claims without striving — "forgiven" presumed, unchanged. Readers should test hopes: striving attached? Then hope well. Slumbering attached? Then presume never. What hope holds rightly (pardon for penitents), presumption grabs wrongly. Hope with works; never without. Mercy meets strivers, not slumberers.

**Vigilance Is the Saints' Signature**

Saints never relaxed. Biographies agree: the righteous kept watch — nights prayed, days audited, tongues guarded. Vigilance signatures every testimony: "he feared his Lord" crowns eulogies. Such watchfulness is not anxiety-disorder but audit-awareness — calm vigilance, not cowering. Saints slept peacefully because they watched diligently. Rest follows vigilance; vigilance never rests. Readers should copy signatures: schedule watches — dawn resolve, evening review. What saints sustained, strivers can start. Begin vigilance; mature toward signature. Never-relaxed inherits rest eternal. Watch now; rest then.

**Unease Is the Believer's Climate**

Holy disquiet marks healthy faith. Believers rest contentedly yet never securely — peace without presumption. Such climate feels paradoxical: calm surface, vigilant depths. Restlessness about audit healths; restfulness in sin sickens. The believer's unease is not despair — hope buoys — but refusal of sedation. Comfort with holy discomfort matures saints. Readers should normalize trembling: unease with unrepented sins proves life. What disquiet signals (conscience alive), quiet may betray (conscience sedated). Welcome holy unease; fear false peace. Tremble awake; sleep forgiven.

**Chastity Commanded Next**

Fear-duties complete (verses 27-28); purity-duties follow (verses 29-31: guarding chastity). Dread then discipline — inward fear, outward purity. The sūrah's order links: God-feared souls guard bodies. Readers should carry trembling toward chastity: feared Lord, guarded limbs. What follows will fence desire. The next verses command guarding private parts, purity screening the God-fearing.
