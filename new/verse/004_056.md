## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:56

> Surely those who reject Our signs, We will cast them into the Fire. Whenever their skin is burnt completely, We will replace it so they will ˹constantly˺ taste the punishment. Indeed, Allah is Almighty, All-Wise.

### Expanded Commentary

**The Fire and the continuity of sensation**

The verse depicts the punishment of those who reject Allah’s signs: the skin is replaced whenever it is burned so that the punishment continues to be tasted. The image is bodily because rejection is not merely an abstract opinion; it is enacted through a person’s faculties, choices, and treatment of others. The Qur’an makes the consequence perceptible to the imagination without asking the reader to reduce the unseen to a laboratory model.

**Divine power and wisdom together**

The conclusion names Allah as Almighty and Wise. His power makes resurrection and punishment possible; His wisdom ensures that judgment is not arbitrary. The verse is not saying that pain is inflicted without moral meaning. It addresses persistent rejection after signs, placing consequence within a just account of response.

**A warning against desensitization**

The repeated replacement of skin also warns against the false security that wrongdoing becomes harmless through repetition. In worldly life the conscience can become numb; a person can hear a warning so often that he feels nothing. The Hereafter reverses that numbness. The reality hidden by habit becomes continuously evident.

The proper use of the verse is moral awakening, not fascination with torment. It should make a believer take signs seriously, repent from denial and oppression, and refuse to use divine punishment as a tool for threatening people personally. Judgment belongs to Allah; responsibility belongs to us.

**The unseen consequence of denial**

The description of punishment is meant to make the hidden seriousness of rejection perceptible. It should lead to repentance and moral attention rather than to voyeurism about pain. Believers who recite the verse should ask what signs they themselves ignore in daily conduct and relationships.
