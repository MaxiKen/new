## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:63

> **Allah responded, “Be gone! Whoever of them follows you, Hell will surely be the reward for all of you—an ample reward.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Be Gone Whoever Follows You Hell Reward Ample**

Qāla idhhab fa-man tabiʿaka minhum fa-inna jahannama jazā'ukum jazā'an mawfūran — He said, go, be gone, then whoever follows you from them, then indeed Hell is your reward, reward ample. Qāla from q-w-l, He said, Allah said, responded to Iblis vow. Idhhab from dh-h-b, to go, be gone, go away, from dhahaba, to go, imperative, be gone, go. Fa-man tabiʿaka from t-b-ʿ, to follow, whoever follows you, from tabiʿa, to follow, as tabiʿaka follows you. Minhum, from them, from descendants, children of Adam. Fa-inna jahannama, then indeed Hell, Gehenna, Hell, from j-h-n-m. Jazā'ukum from j-z-y, reward, recompense, from jazā', reward, recompense, as reward for following. Jazā'an mawfūran from w-f-r, ample, abundant, complete, from wafura, to be ample, abundant, complete, from mawfūr, ample, abundant, complete, as ample reward, abundant reward, complete recompense, as Hell ample reward for followers of Satan, as punishment ample, abundant, complete. Al-Ṭabarī explains that Allah responded to Iblis vow to take hold of descendants except few, saying be gone, go, whoever of them, descendants, follows you, follows Satan, Iblis, then indeed Hell will be reward for all of you, for you and followers, ample reward, abundant, complete recompense, as punishment, as Hell, as ample reward, as recompense for following Satan, as Satan promises delusion, but reward Hell ample.

**Why Be Gone And Hell Ample Reward**

Why be gone and Hell ample reward? Because Iblis arrogant, refused to prostrate to Adam created from mud, protested should I prostrate to mud, said do you see this one you honoured above me, if delay until Day Judgment will take hold of descendants except few, vowing to mislead, control, dominate, out of envy, arrogance, as sworn enemy, clear enemy, ever ungrateful, wasteful brothers to devils, seeks to sow discord, etc., so Allah responded be gone, go away, whoever follows you from descendants, Hell will be reward for all of you, you and followers, ample reward, abundant, complete, as punishment, as Hell, as ample recompense, as justice, as Lord knows best, may have mercy if wills or punish if wills, as will, wisdom, as faithful servants no authority for Satan over them, sufficient Lord as Guardian, as faithful protected, but followers of Satan Hell ample reward.

**Connection To No Authority Over Faithful And Incite With Voice Cavalry Infantry**

Verse 62 Iblis vow to take hold of descendants except few if delayed until Day. Verse 63 Allah responded be gone, whoever follows you Hell ample reward. Verse 64 incite whoever you can with voice, mobilize cavalry and infantry, manipulate wealth and children, make promises, but Satan promises nothing but delusion. Verse 65 you will have no authority over My faithful servants, sufficient Lord as Guardian, showing that despite vow, Allah says be gone, followers Hell ample reward, but faithful servants no authority, Lord Guardian sufficient, and Satan can incite with voice, cavalry, infantry, wealth, children, promises delusion, but no authority over faithful, as faithful protected, say best, etc.

**Link To Hell For Fleeting Desirer Condemned Rejected And Blameworthy Rejected**

Verse 18 fleeting desirer hastened worldly then Hell condemned rejected, madhmūman madḥūran, condemned rejected. Verse 39 do not set up other god or cast into Hell blameworthy rejected, malūman madḥūran, blameworthy rejected. Verse 63 whoever follows you Hell ample reward, jazā'an mawfūran, ample reward, showing Hell as reward for followers of Satan, fleeting desirers, polytheists, etc., ample, abundant, complete, as recompense, as justice, as Lord knows best, etc.

**Linguistic Note On Idhhab And Tabi'aka And Jazā' And Mawfuran**

Qāla from q-w-l, to say. Idhhab from dh-h-b, to go, be gone, from dhahaba. Man tabiʿa from t-b-ʿ, to follow, whoever follows. Minhum, from them. Jahannam from j-h-n-m, Hell. Jazā' from j-z-y, reward, recompense, from jazā. Mawfūr from w-f-r, ample, abundant, complete, from wafura, to be ample. The verse's structure — He said be gone, then whoever follows you from them, then indeed Hell is your reward, reward ample — with command be gone and condition whoever follows you and result Hell reward ample, shows dismissal of Iblis, be gone, and warning that followers Hell ample reward, abundant, complete, as punishment, as justice, as Lord knows best, may have mercy or punish, as will.

**How This Verse Teaches Dismissal And Hell For Followers**

This verse teaches dismissal of Satan and Hell ample reward for followers: Allah responded to Iblis vow, be gone, go away, whoever of them, descendants, follows you, follows Satan, then indeed Hell will be reward for all of you, you and followers, ample reward, abundant, complete recompense, as punishment, as Hell, as ample, as justice, as Lord knows best, may have mercy if wills or punish if wills, as will, wisdom, as faithful servants no authority for Satan over them, sufficient Lord as Guardian, as faithful protected, but followers Hell ample reward. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should not follow Satan, Iblis, who vowed to take hold of descendants except few, who incites with voice, cavalry, infantry, manipulates wealth and children, promises delusion, but promises nothing but delusion, and followers Hell ample reward, abundant, complete, as punishment, as Hell, as ample recompense, so be faithful servant, no authority for Satan, Lord Guardian sufficient, say best, follow most upright guidance, wisdom, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, avoiding Hell ample reward.

**A Reflection On Ample Reward Hell**

Jazā'an mawfūran — reward ample, abundant, complete — indicates Hell as ample reward, abundant, complete recompense for followers of Satan, as punishment ample, abundant, complete, not small, but ample, as justice, as Lord knows best, All-Aware All-Seeing, Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, but torment fearsome, and Hell ample reward for followers, as ample, abundant, complete, as recompense for following Satan, who promises delusion, but promises nothing but delusion, and incites with voice, cavalry, infantry, wealth, children, promises, but no authority over faithful servants, sufficient Lord as Guardian, as faithful protected, say best, etc., leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, while followers Hell ample reward, abundant, complete, as punishment, as Hell, as ample, as justice.
