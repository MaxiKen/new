## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:84

> **Say, ˹O Prophet,˺ “Everyone acts in their own way. But your Lord knows best whose way is rightly guided.”**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Everyone Acts In Own Way**

Qul kullun yaʿmalu ʿalā shākilatihi — say, everyone acts, works according to his way, likeness, nature. Qul from q-w-l, to say, command to Prophet. Kullun, everyone, each one, each person, from k-l-l, all, every, everyone. Yaʿmalu from ʿ-m-l, to act, do, work, from ʿamila, to act, do, work, as yaʿmalu acts, works, does, as everyone acts, works. ʿAlā shākilatihi from sh-k-l, way, likeness, nature, manner, form, from shākilah, way, likeness, nature, manner, form, as shākilatihi his way, his likeness, his nature, his manner, his form, as everyone acts according to his own way, nature, manner, as human nature, when favoured turns away arrogantly, when evil touched despairs, etc., as everyone acts in own way, according to own nature, manner, as disbelievers act in own way, believers act in own way, etc. Al-Ṭabarī explains that say, everyone acts in their own way, according to his way, likeness, nature, manner, meaning everyone acts, works according to his own way, nature, manner, as human acts according to own nature, as disbelievers act according to disbelief, arrogance, ungratefulness, etc., believers act according to belief, gratitude, patience, etc., as everyone acts in own way.

**Lord Knows Best Whose Way Rightly Guided**

Fa-rabbukum aʿlamu bi-man huwa ahdā sabīlan — then your Lord knows best who is more guided way, rightly guided. Fa-rabbukum, then your Lord, your Lord, from r-b-b. Aʿlamu, knows best, more knowing, from ʿ-l-m, to know, as aʿlamu knows best, as previous Lord knows best what is within selves, how they listen, you best, those in heavens and earth, etc., and aʿlamu knows best. Bi-man huwa ahdā, with who is more guided, who is rightly guided, more rightly guided, from h-d-y, to guide, be guided, from hadā, to guide, and ahdā, more guided, rightly guided, more rightly guided. Sabīlan from s-b-l, way, path, as sabīl way, path, Right Way, as rightly guided way, Right Way. Al-Ṭabarī explains that your Lord knows best whose way is rightly guided, more rightly guided, meaning Lord knows best who is more guided, rightly guided, whose way, shākilah, is rightly guided, more rightly guided, as He knows best what is within selves, knows best those in heavens and earth, etc., knows best whose way rightly guided, as He is All-Aware All-Seeing, knows best.

**Why Everyone Acts In Own Way And Lord Knows Best Rightly Guided**

Why everyone acts in own way and Lord knows best whose way rightly guided? Because when grant favours human turns away arrogantly, goes far with side, when evil touched despairs, loses hope, as human nature, ever ungrateful, ever hasty, etc., and everyone acts in own way, according to own nature, manner, as disbelievers act in own way, arrogance, ungratefulness, despair, etc., believers act in own way, gratitude, patience, etc., and Lord knows best whose way, shākilah, is rightly guided, more rightly guided, as He knows best what is within selves, knows best those in heavens and earth, etc., knows best whose way rightly guided, as He is All-Aware All-Seeing, knows best, as He knows best how they listen, etc., and as Qur'an guides to most upright, gives good news to believers mighty reward, warns disbelievers painful punishment, as two ways, etc., and as truth come falsehood vanished, healing mercy vs loss, etc., and as everyone acts in own way, Lord knows best rightly guided.

**Connection To When Grant Favours Turn Away Arrogantly Despair When Evil Touched And Quran Healing Mercy**

Verse 83 when grant favours human turns away acting arrogantly, goes far with side, when evil touched despairs, loses hope, ya'ūsan, human nature, ever ungrateful, ever hasty. Verse 84 everyone acts in own way, Lord knows best whose way rightly guided, showing that turning away arrogantly when favoured and despairing when evil touched are ways, shākilah, some act in that way, own way, but Lord knows best whose way rightly guided, more rightly guided, as He knows best, All-Aware All-Seeing, etc., and as Qur'an healing mercy for believers but increases wrongdoers in loss, as two ways, healing mercy vs loss, etc., and as truth come falsehood vanished, indeed falsehood bound to vanish, as truth vs falsehood, etc., and as everyone acts in own way, Lord knows best rightly guided, as He knows best.

**Link To Lord Knows Best What Is Within Selves And Those In Heavens Earth And How They Listen**

Verse 25 Lord knows best what is within selves, if righteous All-Forgiving to constantly turning. Verse 47 We know best how they listen and what they say privately, when wrongdoers say you follow only bewitched man. Verse 54 Lord knows you best, may have mercy or punish if wills, not sent you as keeper. Verse 55 Lord knows best those in heavens and earth, favoured some prophets above others, to David gave Psalms. Verse 84 Lord knows best whose way rightly guided, showing knowledge theme: Lord knows best what is within selves, how they listen, you best, those in heavens and earth, whose way rightly guided, as He knows best, All-Aware All-Seeing, knows best, as He knows best, etc., and as everyone acts in own way, Lord knows best rightly guided.

**Linguistic Note On Kullun And Ya'malu And Shakilatihi And A'lamu And Ahda Sabilan**

Kull from k-l-l, everyone, each, all, from kull. Yaʿmalu from ʿ-m-l, to act, do, work, from ʿamila. Shākilah from sh-k-l, way, likeness, nature, manner, form, from shakala, to be similar, to form, as shākilah way, likeness, nature. Rabb from r-b-b, Lord. Aʿlam from ʿ-l-m, knows best, more knowing. Man huwa, who he is, who is. Ahdā from h-d-y, more guided, rightly guided, from hadā, to guide. Sabīl from s-b-l, way, path, Right Way. The verse's structure — say everyone acts according to his way, then your Lord knows best who is more guided way — with everyone acts in own way and Lord knows best whose way rightly guided, shows that everyone acts according to own nature, manner, but Lord knows best who is rightly guided, more guided, as He knows best, All-Aware All-Seeing, as He knows best what is within selves, etc.

**How This Verse Teaches Everyone Acts In Own Way And Lord Knows Best Rightly Guided**

This verse teaches everyone acts in own way, according to own nature, manner, likeness, and Lord knows best whose way is rightly guided, more rightly guided, more guided way, Right Way. Al-Qurṭubī says everyone acts, works according to his own way, shākilah, nature, manner, likeness, as disbelievers act in own way, arrogance, ungratefulness, despair, etc., believers act in own way, gratitude, patience, etc., and Lord knows best whose way, shākilah, is rightly guided, more rightly guided, as He knows best what is within selves, knows best those in heavens and earth, etc., knows best whose way rightly guided, as He is All-Aware All-Seeing, knows best. The practical lesson is to act in rightly guided way, most upright way, that Qur'an guides to, wisdom, that is part of wisdom revealed, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, healing mercy, etc., and know Lord knows best whose way rightly guided, so seek rightly guided way, most upright, not own way that is arrogance, despair, ungratefulness, etc., but rightly guided way, most upright, that Lord knows best.

**A Reflection On Way And Rightly Guided**

Shākilatihi — his way, likeness, nature, manner, form — indicates everyone has own way, nature, manner, according to which he acts, works, as human nature, when favoured turns away arrogantly, when evil touched despairs, etc., as everyone acts in own way, own nature. Ahdā sabīlan — more guided way, rightly guided way, more rightly guided way — indicates that among ways, some more guided, rightly guided, Right Way, more rightly guided, as Lord knows best who is more guided way, rightly guided, as He knows best, All-Aware All-Seeing, knows best what is within selves, etc., and as Qur'an guides to most upright, most upright way is rightly guided, more guided, as truth come falsehood vanished, indeed falsehood bound to vanish, as truth, most upright, rightly guided, vs falsehood, astray, far astray cannot find Right Way, blind in world blind in Hereafter far more astray, etc. The believer who acts in rightly guided way, most upright way, that Qur'an guides to, wisdom, will be among those Lord knows best as rightly guided, more guided way, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, healing mercy, station of praise, honourable entrance and exit, supporting authority, truth come falsehood vanished, etc., while disbeliever acts in own way that is arrogance, despair, ungratefulness, far astray cannot find Right Way, blind, etc., painful punishment, loss, etc.
