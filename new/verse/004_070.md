## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:70

> This is Allah’s favour, and Allah fully knows ˹who deserves it˺.

### Expanded Commentary

**The whole gift belongs to Allah**

“This is Allah’s favour” refers to the honour of obedience and its company. The verse prevents the obedient from turning the promise into a basis for pride. Faith, opportunity, perseverance, and reward all depend on divine favour, even though the servant must choose and strive.

**Knowledge of deserving**

Allah fully knows who deserves the reward. Human beings see public acts and cannot measure intention, obstacles, sincerity, or concealed repentance. This is a comfort to the unknown servant and a warning to the celebrated one. Recognition by people is neither proof of acceptance nor evidence of rejection.

The verse also guards against despair. A person may think that a long history of failure disqualifies him from noble company; Allah knows the truth of a final turning and the effort hidden from others. The correct response is to seek favour through obedience without claiming ownership of the outcome.

**Hidden deserving is known to God**

The promise is safe in divine knowledge even when people misjudge it. Someone whose work is unseen may be closer to the honoured than a celebrated figure, while a public reputation may hide serious betrayal. This should produce gratitude rather than anxiety: strive sincerely and leave the ranking to Allah.
