## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:152

> As for those who believe in Allah and His messengers—accepting all; rejecting none—He will surely give them their rewards. And Allah is All-Forgiving, Most Merciful.

**Expanded Commentary**

**Those Who Believe in God and Messengers — Accepting All, Rejecting None**

The verse states *wa alladhīna āmanū bi Allāhi wa rusulihī wa lam yufarriqū bayna aḥadin minhum* — and those who believe, have faith, in God and His messengers and did not make distinction between any of them, any one among them. *Āmanū bi Allāhi wa rusulihī* was explained as the comprehensive faith just commanded — *have faith in God, His Messenger, Book He revealed to His Messenger and Scriptures He revealed before* (4:136) — now as description of its people. *Wa lam yufarriqū bayna aḥadin minhum* — and did not make distinction between any one of them — was read as the opposite of *wishing to make distinction between God and messengers saying we believe in some and disbelieve in some desiring a way between* (4:150) where verdict was *true disbelievers, humiliating preparation* (4:151). To not distinguish between any messenger was explained as to accept all — *“We have sent revelation to you as We sent to Noah and prophets after him, Abraham, Ishmael, Isaac, Jacob, descendants, Jesus, Job, Jonah, Aaron, Solomon, David We gave Psalms”* (4:163) — all are God’s, none rejected. The sūrah has shown that the People of the Book demanded a book from heaven yet demanded greater — *show God visible* from Moses (4:153) — and then took calf; distinguishing among messengers is that demand’s theology: to accept Moses but not Jesus or Muhammad ﷺ is modern calf after clear signs. To not distinguish was also linked to *“Gardens for those who believe and do good”* (4:122) — comprehensive belief is part of that good, as *millata Ibrāhīma ḥanīfā* (4:125) included all prophets in Abraham’s way.

**He Will Surely Give Them Their Rewards**

The promise *ulāʾika sawfa yuʾtīhim ujūrahum* — then to those — the comprehensive believers — He will surely, will soon, give them their rewards, their wages — was explained with *sawfa* — will, soon — certainty as the emigrant’s secured wage (4:100) and great reward for commanding charity (4:114) and great reward for being with believers after repentance (4:146). *Ujūrahum* — their rewards, wages — was read as *ajr* — wage — the same *ajr* that hypocrites sought as *ʿizzah* among disbelievers (4:139) but belong to Him entirely (4:139) and are both rewards with Him (4:134) — the believers’ wage is from Him, not from company sought. The verse generalises the promise where *“whoever does good male or female with faith will enter Paradise not wronged a speck”* (4:124) — the comprehensive believer is that doer; and *“who is better than those who submit face doing good following Abraham?”* (4:125) — submission includes accepting all messengers, so reward is friendship as Abraham was taken *khalīl* (4:125). Early notes linked *ujūrahum* to *“God wills to make clear, guide to noble ways, turn in mercy”* (4:26): wages are the mercy guided to.

**And God Is All-Forgiving, Most Merciful**

The seal *wa kāna Allāhu Ghafūran Raḥīmā* — And God is All-Forgiving, Most Merciful, was ever — was explained with *Ghafūr* — much forgiving, covering — and *Raḥīm* — ever merciful — the pair that seals grateful faith not punished (4:147), seeking forgiveness found (4:110), and universal pardon for less than association (4:116). To seal comprehensive belief with *Ghafūr Raḥīm* was read as that those who believe in all messengers still have faults needing cover, as the sūrah has shown that strivers need forgiveness among ranks (4:96) and emigrants find havens though weakness remains (4:100). *Ghafūr Raḥīm* here contrasts with *ʿadhāban muhīnā* — humiliating punishment prepared for true disbelievers (4:151) — showing that distinction’s wage is humiliation, comprehensiveness’s wage is forgiveness and mercy besides reward. Where the sūrah says *“He does not wrong even an atom”* (4:40) and *“you will never be wronged even speck on date stone”* (4:124), the comprehensive believer’s reward is the atom not wronged yet multiplied many times; the Seal says the Multiplier is Forgiving, Merciful.

**A Verse That Follows True Disbelief With True Belief**

Placed after *they are indeed true disbelievers, We have prepared for disbelievers humiliating punishment* (4:151), this verse follows true disbelief with true belief and its opposite preparation: not humiliating punishment prepared but rewards given and forgiveness, mercy. The sūrah has moved from the passage on women, settlement, enrichment (4:127–130) through universal command to be mindful (4:131), trusteeship, capability, both rewards (4:131–134), standing for justice (4:135) with five articles of faith (4:136), oscillation without forgiveness or way (4:137), announcement of painful to hypocrites (4:138), alliance for honour (4:139), sitting where mocked as like them (4:140), waiting between winners (4:141), deceiving while being outwitted, lazy prayer (4:142), torn between (4:143), alliance giving clear proof (4:144), lowest depth without helper (4:145) except four — repent, mend, hold fast, be sincere (4:146) — the principle *why punish if grateful and faithful?* (4:147) — the discipline of loud evil except by wronged (4:148) and pardoning though capable (4:149) — distinguishing between God and messengers desiring a way between (4:150); here the opposite distinction — not distinguishing between any messenger — is rewarded. The sūrah that warns not to deviate by following desires (4:27) and not to let desires deviate from justice (4:135) names comprehensive acceptance as the just faith.

**The Comprehensive Faith That Mirrors Comprehensiveness of Revelation**

The phrase *lam yufarriqū bayna aḥadin minhum* was explained as mirroring *“We have sent revelation to you as We sent to Noah and prophets after him”* (4:163): revelation is comprehensive, so faith is comprehensive. To accept all and reject none is to say the single soul spread into humanity (4:1) received guidance spread through prophets one after another, all from the Owner to whom belongs whatever is in heavens and earth (4:126). The sūrah has shown that the People of the Book demanded a single book from heaven as sign yet had demanded visible God from Moses (4:153); to accept all prophets is to accept that the Book’s descent is with His knowledge and angels bear witness (4:166), and God alone suffices as Witness, so that no demand for visible proof suspends belief in any messenger. For the weak this is the lightest completeness: where settlement between spouses is best (4:128) and where enrichment after separation is from His spaciousness (4:130), the comprehensive believer’s settlement with revelation is to take all, not to slit and alter (4:119), and his enrichment is rewards, forgiveness, mercy.
