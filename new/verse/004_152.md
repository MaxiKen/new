## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:152

> As for those who believe in Allah and His messengers—accepting all; rejecting none—He will surely give them their rewards. And Allah is All-Forgiving, Most Merciful.

### Expanded Commentary

**One faith receives all messengers**

Those who believe in Allah and His messengers, rejecting none, receive their rewards. The verse establishes the Qur’anic unity of revelation. Believing in Muhammad does not require insulting Moses, Jesus, or any prophet; it requires affirming the truth of all messengers and following the guidance finally revealed through the last of them.

Allah is All-Forgiving and Merciful. The promise includes people who come from traditions of confusion and sincerely accept guidance, as well as believers who repent from disrespectful speech about earlier prophets. Faith is not a contest in which honouring one messenger requires degrading another.

The chapter’s social and theological legislation meet here: a community built on one God and one chain of messengers has no warrant for ethnic superiority or religious mockery.

**Respect across the prophetic family**

Belief in all messengers gives Muslims a basis for religious respect without pretending that all theological claims are identical. Moses, Jesus, and Muhammad are honoured as messengers, and their communities can be addressed with truth and courtesy. Forgiveness and mercy remain open to those who sincerely abandon rejection.
