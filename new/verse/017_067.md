## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:67

> **When you are touched with hardship at sea, you ˹totally˺ forget all ˹the gods˺ you ˹normally˺ invoke, except Him. But when He delivers you ˹safely˺ to shore, you turn away. Humankind is ever ungrateful.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**When Touched With Hardship At Sea You Forget All Gods Except Him**

Wa-idhā massakumu al-ḍurru fī al-baḥri ḍalla man tadʿūna illā iyyāhu — and when hardship touches you in sea, those you call besides Him go astray, you forget. Wa-idhā, and when. Massakum from m-s-s, to touch, afflict, befall, from massa, to touch, as massakumu touches you, afflicts you. Al-ḍurru from ḍ-r-r, hardship, adversity, harm, affliction, from ḍurr, harm, hardship, as ḍurr hardship at sea, storm, etc. Fī al-baḥri, in sea, at sea, as baḥr sea, as ships through sea. Ḍalla from ḍ-l-l, to go astray, be lost, forget, disappear, from ḍalla, to go astray, be lost, as ḍalla man tadʿūna, those you invoke go astray, you forget, disappear, as you forget all gods you normally invoke. Man tadʿūna from d-ʿ-w, those you call, invoke, you normally invoke, besides Him, as you invoke besides Him, idols, gods, etc., but at sea hardship you forget them. Illā iyyāhu, except Him, only Him, Allah alone, from iyyā, Him, only Him. Al-Ṭabarī explains that when hardship, adversity, harm touches you at sea, storm, drowning, etc., you totally forget all gods you normally invoke besides Him, idols, etc., except Him, Allah alone, meaning you invoke only Him, forget others, as hardship at sea makes you remember only Allah, as only He can remove harm, as those claimed besides Him have no power to remove harm or transfer, as verse 56 says, so at sea you forget them, invoke only Him.

**When Delivers You To Shore You Turn Away, Humankind Ever Ungrateful**

Fa-lammā najjākum ilā al-barri aʿraḍtum wa-kāna al-insānu kafūran — then when He delivers you to land, shore, you turn away, and human is ever ungrateful. Fa-lammā, then when, so when. Najjākum from n-j-w, to deliver, save, rescue, from najjā, to deliver, save, rescue, as najjākum delivers you, saves you, rescues you, from sea hardship to shore, land. Ilā al-barri from b-r-r, to land, shore, land, from barr, land, shore, opposite of baḥr sea, as land. Aʿraḍtum from ʿ-r-ḍ, to turn away, avoid, from aʿraḍa, to turn away, avoid, as turn away from Allah, from gratitude, after being delivered. Wa-kāna al-insānu kafūran, and human is ever ungrateful, very ungrateful, from k-f-r, ungrateful, disbelieving, very ungrateful, as kafūran ever ungrateful, very ungrateful, as Devil ever ungrateful to Lord, kafūran, and humankind ever ungrateful, kafūran, as human ever hasty, ever ungrateful, as insān human, as in verse 11 human ever hasty, etc. Al-Ṭabarī explains that when He delivers you safely to shore, land, from sea hardship, you turn away, avoid, turn away from gratitude, from invoking Him alone, return to invoking other gods, etc., and humankind is ever ungrateful, very ungrateful, ungrateful to Lord, as they forget, turn away after being delivered, as ungratefulness.

**Why Forget Gods At Sea Except Him And Turn Away At Shore And Ever Ungrateful**

Why forget all gods except Him at sea hardship and turn away at shore and ever ungrateful? Because at sea hardship, storm, drowning, you realize only Allah can remove harm, as those claimed besides Him have no power to remove harm or transfer, as verse 56 says, they have no power, no ownership, so you forget them, invoke only Him, Allah alone, as only He can save, deliver, as He steers ships for you through sea to seek bounty, ever Merciful, as verse 66 says, and He delivers you to shore, land, saves you, rescues you, but when delivered safely to shore, you turn away, avoid, return to shirk, invoking other gods, etc., as humankind ever ungrateful, kafūran, very ungrateful, as Devil ever ungrateful, kafūran, and human ever hasty, ever ungrateful, as insān, as human prays for evil as for good, ever hasty, etc.

**Connection To Lord Steers Ships To Seek Bounty Ever Merciful And No Power To Remove Harm**

Verse 66 Lord steers ships for you through sea so you may seek bounty, ever Merciful to you. Verse 67 when hardship touches you at sea you forget all gods you normally invoke except Him, when He delivers you to shore you turn away, humankind ever ungrateful, showing that ships through sea to seek bounty is mercy, ever Merciful, but at sea hardship you forget other gods, invoke only Him, as only He has power to remove harm, as those claimed besides Him have no power to remove harm or transfer, as verse 56 says, no power, so at sea you realize, forget others, invoke only Him, but at shore you turn away, ungrateful, as humankind ever ungrateful, kafūran, as Devil ever ungrateful, kafūran, and human ever hasty.

**Link To Do You Feel Secure Land Swallow Storm Stones And Send Back To Sea Violent Storm**

Next verses 68-69 do you feel secure that He will not cause land to swallow you up or unleash storm of stones, then find none to protect you, or do you feel secure that He will not send you back to sea once again and send violent storm drowning you for denial, then find none to avenge you against Us, showing that after being delivered to shore, you turn away, ungrateful, but do you feel secure that land will not swallow you, storm of stones, or sea violent storm drowning, etc., as warning, as He is ever Merciful but also torment fearsome, as Lord's torment fearsome, maḥdhūran, to be feared.

**Linguistic Note On Massakum And Durr And Dalla And Najjakum And Barr And A'radtum And Kafuran**

Massakum from m-s-s, to touch, afflict, from massa. Ḍurr from ḍ-r-r, hardship, harm, adversity, from ḍarra. Baḥr from b-ḥ-r, sea. Ḍalla from ḍ-l-l, to go astray, be lost, forget, disappear, from ḍalla. Tadʿūna from d-ʿ-w, to call, invoke. Iyyāhu, Him, only Him. Najjā from n-j-w, to deliver, save, rescue, from najjā. Barr from b-r-r, land, shore, from barr. Aʿraḍtum from ʿ-r-ḍ, to turn away, avoid, from aʿraḍa. Insān from a-n-s, human, from insān. Kafūr from k-f-r, ever ungrateful, very ungrateful, from kafara, to be ungrateful, intensive kafūr. The verse's structure — when hardship touches you in sea, those you invoke besides Him go astray, you forget, except Him, then when He delivers you to land you turn away, and human is ever ungrateful — with hardship at sea forgetting other gods except Him and delivering to land turning away and human ever ungrateful, shows human nature at sea hardship invokes only Allah, but at shore turns away, ungrateful, ever ungrateful, as Devil ever ungrateful.

**How This Verse Teaches Human Nature At Sea And Ungratefulness**

This verse teaches human nature at sea hardship and ungratefulness: when hardship touches you at sea, storm, drowning, you totally forget all gods you normally invoke besides Him, idols, etc., except Him, Allah alone, invoke only Him, as only He can remove harm, as those claimed besides Him have no power, as verse 56 says, but when He delivers you safely to shore, land, you turn away, avoid, return to shirk, invoking other gods, etc., and humankind is ever ungrateful, very ungrateful, ungrateful to Lord, as Devil ever ungrateful, kafūran, and human ever hasty, etc. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should be grateful, not ungrateful, should invoke Allah alone at sea and at shore, not forget, not turn away, should be grateful for being delivered, as Lord steers ships for you to seek bounty, ever Merciful, as He dignified children of Adam, carried on land and sea, granted good provisions, privileged far above many creatures, as mercy, ever Merciful, so be grateful, not ever ungrateful, as humankind ever ungrateful.

**A Reflection On Ever Ungrateful**

Kafūran — ever ungrateful, very ungrateful — as Devil ever ungrateful to Lord, kafūran, verse 27, and humankind ever ungrateful, kafūran, verse 67, showing similarity between Devil and humankind in ungratefulness, as Devil ever ungrateful, human ever ungrateful, both kafūran, very ungrateful, as wasteful brothers to devils, brothers, companions, share ungratefulness, as human ever hasty, prays for evil as for good, ever hasty, ever ungrateful, as insān, human, ever hasty, ever ungrateful, so be grateful, not ungrateful, invoke Allah alone, not other gods, as only He has power to remove harm, transfer, steer ships, seek bounty, ever Merciful, Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, but torment fearsome, so be grateful, not ever ungrateful, follow most upright guidance, wisdom, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, avoiding painful punishment, Hell, as torment fearsome.
