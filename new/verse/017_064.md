## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:64

> **And incite whoever you can of them with your voice, mobilize against them all your cavalry and infantry, manipulate them in their wealth and children, and make them promises.” But Satan promises them nothing but delusion.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Incite Whoever You Can With Voice, Mobilize Cavalry Infantry, Manipulate Wealth Children, Make Promises**

Wa-istafziz man istaṭaʿta minhum bi-ṣawtika wa-ajlib ʿalayhim bi-khaylika wa-rajilika wa-shārikhum fī al-amwāli wa-al-awlādi wa-ʿidhum — and incite, entice whoever you can of them with your voice and mobilize against them with your cavalry and infantry and share with them in wealth and children and promise them. Wa-istafziz from f-z-z, to incite, entice, provoke, make to slip, from istafazza, to incite, entice, provoke, to make to tremble, to incite. Man istaṭaʿta, whoever you can, whoever you are able, from ṭ-w-ʿ, to be able, can, as istaṭaʿta you can, you are able. Minhum, from them, from descendants. Bi-ṣawtika from ṣ-w-t, with your voice, voice, from ṣawt, voice, as Satan's voice, incitement, as Satan seeks to sow discord, incites, etc. Wa-ajlib from j-l-b, to mobilize, bring, gather, from ajlaba, to mobilize, bring, gather, as mobilize against them. ʿAlayhim, against them. Bi-khaylika from kh-y-l, with your cavalry, horses, from khayl, cavalry, horses, plural of khayl, horses, cavalry. Wa-rajilika from r-j-l, and your infantry, foot soldiers, from rajil, infantry, foot, from rajila, foot. Wa-shārikhum from sh-r-k, to share, associate, manipulate, from shāraka, to share, associate, as share with them, manipulate, associate in wealth and children, as Satan shares, manipulates wealth and children, as via unlawful wealth, children, etc. Fī al-amwāli wa-al-awlādi, in wealth and children, in wealth and offspring, as amwāl wealth, awlād children, as wealth and children adornment, but Satan manipulates. Wa-ʿidhum from w-ʿ-d, to promise, make promises, from waʿada, to promise, as promise them, make promises. Al-Ṭabarī explains that Allah says to Satan, incite, entice whoever you can of them, descendants, with your voice, with incitement, deception, and mobilize against them with your cavalry and infantry, all your forces, horses and foot, and share with them, manipulate them in wealth and children, via unlawful wealth, unlawful children, etc., and make them promises, promise them, delude them, as Satan promises delusion, but promises nothing but delusion.

**But Satan Promises Nothing But Delusion**

Wa-mā yaʿiduhum al-shayṭānu illā ghurūran — and Satan promises them not except delusion, deception. Wa-mā, and not, but. Yaʿiduhum from w-ʿ-d, to promise, as ʿidhum promise them. Al-shayṭānu, Satan, Devil, Iblis. Illā ghurūran from gh-r-r, delusion, deception, vanity, from gharra, to deceive, delude, from ghurūr, delusion, deception, vanity, as delusion, deception, false promise. Al-Ṭabarī explains that Satan promises them nothing but delusion, deception, vanity, false promises, as he promises wealth, children, etc., but promises delusion, deception, not truth, as he has no power to fulfill, as he has no authority over faithful servants, sufficient Lord as Guardian, as faithful protected, but promises delusion to followers, as Hell ample reward.

**Why Incite With Voice Cavalry Infantry Wealth Children Promises Delusion**

Why incite with voice, cavalry, infantry, wealth, children, promises delusion? Because Satan, Iblis, vowed to take hold of descendants except few, if delayed until Day, and Allah said be gone, whoever follows you Hell ample reward, and now Allah says incite whoever you can with voice, mobilize cavalry and infantry, manipulate wealth and children, make promises, but promises nothing but delusion, showing Satan's methods: voice, incitement, deception, cavalry, infantry, forces, wealth, children, promises, all delusion, deception, not truth, as he has no authority over faithful servants, sufficient Lord as Guardian, as faithful protected, say best, etc., but followers deluded, Hell ample reward.

**Connection To No Authority Over Faithful And Sufficient Guardian And Voice And Wealth Children**

Verse 62 vow to take hold of descendants except few, verse 63 be gone, followers Hell ample reward, verse 64 incite whoever you can with voice, cavalry, infantry, manipulate wealth and children, make promises, but promises nothing but delusion, verse 65 you will have no authority over My faithful servants, sufficient Lord as Guardian, showing Satan's methods, voice, cavalry, infantry, wealth, children, promises delusion, but no authority over faithful servants, Lord Guardian sufficient, as faithful protected, say best, etc., and incitement is delusion, not truth.

**Link To Wasteful Brothers To Devils And Satan Sworn Enemy And Seeks Discord**

Verse 27 wasteful brothers to devils, Devil ever ungrateful, verse 53 Satan seeks to sow discord among them, Satan sworn enemy to humankind, clear enemy, verse 64 incite with voice, cavalry, infantry, wealth, children, promises delusion, showing Satan's methods to sow discord, incite, as sworn enemy, clear enemy, ever ungrateful, wasteful brothers to devils follow him, but faithful servants no authority, Lord Guardian sufficient, say best to prevent discord.

**Linguistic Note On Istafziz And Sawt And Ajlib And Khayl And Rajil And Sharik And Ghurur**

Istafziz from f-z-z, to incite, entice, provoke, from istafazza. Man istaṭaʿta, whoever you can, from ṭ-w-ʿ, to be able. Ṣawt from ṣ-w-t, voice, from ṣawt. Ajlib from j-l-b, to mobilize, bring, gather, from ajlaba. Khayl from kh-y-l, cavalry, horses, from khayl. Rajil from r-j-l, infantry, foot, from rajil. Shārik from sh-r-k, to share, associate, manipulate, from shāraka. Amwāl from m-w-l, wealth, plural of māl. Awlād from w-l-d, children, plural of walad. ʿIdh from w-ʿ-d, to promise, from waʿada. Yaʿidu from w-ʿ-d, to promise. Ghurūr from gh-r-r, delusion, deception, vanity, from gharra, to deceive. The verse's structure — incite whoever you can with voice and mobilize against them with cavalry and infantry and share with them in wealth and children and promise them, but Satan promises them nothing but delusion — with methods voice, cavalry, infantry, wealth, children, promises, and statement promises nothing but delusion, shows Satan's methods are delusion, deception, not truth, no authority over faithful, Lord Guardian sufficient.

**How This Verse Teaches Satan's Methods Are Delusion**

This verse teaches Satan's methods are delusion, deception, not truth: incite whoever you can with voice, with incitement, deception, mobilize against them with cavalry and infantry, all forces, share, manipulate them in wealth and children, via unlawful wealth, unlawful children, etc., and make promises, promise them, but Satan promises them nothing but delusion, deception, vanity, false promises, not truth, as he has no power, no authority over faithful servants, sufficient Lord as Guardian, as faithful protected, but followers deluded, Hell ample reward, ample. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should know Satan's methods, voice, cavalry, infantry, wealth, children, promises delusion, and should be faithful servant, no authority for Satan, Lord Guardian sufficient, say best, follow most upright guidance, wisdom, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, avoiding delusion, Hell ample reward.

**A Reflection On Voice Cavalry Infantry Wealth Children Delusion**

Bi-ṣawtika wa-khaylika wa-rajilika — with your voice and cavalry and infantry — indicates Satan's forces, voice, incitement, deception, cavalry, horses, infantry, foot, all forces, mobilized against descendants, to mislead, control, dominate, except few faithful, as vow to take hold, but no authority over faithful servants, sufficient Lord as Guardian, as faithful protected, say best, etc. Wa-shārikhum fī al-amwāli wa-al-awlādi — share with them in wealth and children, manipulate wealth and children — indicates Satan manipulates wealth and children, via unlawful wealth, usury, theft, etc., and unlawful children, adultery, etc., as wealth and children adornment but manipulated by Satan leads to evil, as previous verses about wealth, children, etc. Wa-ʿidhum — promise them — but promises nothing but delusion, ghurūran, delusion, deception, vanity, false, not truth, as Satan's promises delusion, as he promised delusion to followers, but Hell ample reward, ample, as recompense, as justice. The believer who reflects will not be incited by voice, cavalry, infantry, wealth, children, promises delusion, but be faithful servant, no authority for Satan, Lord Guardian sufficient, say best, follow most upright, wisdom, leading to mighty reward.
