## Sūrah an-Nūr 24:9

> and a fifth oath that Allah may be displeased with her if he is telling the truth.

**Expanded Commentary**

**The Culminating Imprecation of the Wife**

The procedural sequence of marital defense reaches its terrifying climax in the fifth declaration: *wa-al-khāmisata anna ghaḍaba Allāhi ʿalayhā in kāna mina al-ṣādiqīn*. In the preceding verse, the woman pronounced four distinct oaths swearing that her husband was lying, thereby establishing an evidentiary counterweight to his charges. In this fifth step, she shifts from factual denial to direct self-imprecation. She explicitly calls down the divine wrath upon her own soul if her husband has spoken the truth.

This symmetrical culmination completes the judicial architecture of *liʿān*. Just as the husband was required to seal his testimony by invoking the divine curse, the wife must seal her defense by invoking divine wrath. Classical jurists noted that this fifth declaration transforms her testimony into an existential wager. She does not merely assert innocence before a human magistrate; she surrenders herself entirely to the righteous anger of the Almighty if she is guilty of the betrayal, leaving her eternal fate in divine hands.

**The Theological Distinction Between laʿnah and ghaḍab**

Classical exegetes, including al-Zamakhsharī and Fakhr al-Dīn al-Rāzī, observed a profound linguistic and theological distinction in the divine choice of words. In verse 7, the husband invokes *laʿnah* (the curse of God), whereas in verse 9, the wife invokes *ghaḍab* (the wrath of God). While both terms denote severe divine displeasure, *ghaḍab* represents an even more intense and immediate manifestation of divine retribution than *laʿnah*.

A curse signifies expulsion and banishment from divine mercy, whereas wrath denotes active, consuming divine anger. Commentators explained that if a woman commits adultery and subsequently stands in the house of God to swear false oaths, her offense compounds marital betrayal with sacrilegious perjury. By invoking *ghaḍab*, the revelation emphasizes the catastrophic spiritual peril awaiting any woman who would dare use the divine name to conceal private corruption and deceive the community, facing the raw fury of heaven.

**The Prophetic Interruption and the Moment of Trembling**

The Sunnah preserves a moving record of the psychological gravity that attended this fifth oath. In authoritative traditions, including the report in Sunan Abī Dāwūd 2256 and related accounts in Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī, the Prophet ﷺ commanded the presiding authority to pause the woman before she uttered the final formula. The judge warned her: Fear God, for this fifth oath brings the inescapable consequence of divine retribution, and the punishment of the Hereafter is far more severe than worldly disgrace.

In one historical instance, when warned of the finality of this fifth oath, the woman hesitated and drew back, causing the spectators to believe she was about to confess. However, she resolved herself and proclaimed: I will not disgrace my family for the rest of time, and proceeded to complete the oath. This prophetic intervention illustrates the deep pastoral concern embedded in Islamic jurisprudence, which actively provides litigants every opportunity to step back from eternal ruin, prioritizing genuine salvation over hasty procedural closure.

**The Complete Termination of the Spousal Relationship**

The formal completion of the wife's fifth oath repels the statutory penalty of adultery from her person, but it marks the immediate and irreversible termination of the marriage. The husband and wife are instantly separated by operation of law. Classical jurists unanimously agreed that a household cannot endure when spouses have traded public imprecations of divine curses and divine wrath.

Regarding financial rights, the Prophet ﷺ ruled that the woman retains her full deferred dowry (*mahr*) if the marriage was consummated. When the accusing husband inquired about the recovery of his wealth, the Prophet ﷺ declared: The dowry is hers in exchange for what you enjoyed of her intimacy; if you spoke the truth, you have no claim to it, and if you lied, it is even further from your reach. The law scrupulously protects the woman's financial entitlements even amidst domestic devastation, preventing the husband from using defamation as a financial weapon.

**The Lineage and Custody of the Child**

When the process of *liʿān* involves a husband disowning a child born of the marriage, the completion of the oaths establishes definitive legal rulings regarding lineage and custody. The Prophet ﷺ decreed that the child is attributed exclusively to the mother: *al-walad li-ummih*. The child inherits from the mother and her heirs, and the mother inherits from the child, while all legal ties to the former husband are severed.

Crucially, the law erects a formidable shield around the mother and child against future slander. Anyone who dares cast aspersions on the child's legitimacy or defames the mother based upon the severed marriage is subjected to the full penalty of eighty lashes for *qadhf*. Divine law ensures that the child is not rendered an outcast or subjected to social stigmatization, preserving their dignity through legitimate maternal kinship, guaranteeing that no innocent newborn bears the stigma of parental conflict.

**The Divine Horizon Beyond Human Courts**

The conclusion of the *liʿān* procedure reveals the philosophical boundary where human jurisprudence surrenders to divine sovereignty. Both husband and wife have made their solemn representations under oath; both have staked their souls upon their veracity before the assembled community. Earthly judges possess no metaphysical instrument to verify what transpired behind closed doors.

The court declares the proceedings closed, imposing neither lashes nor stoning, and dissolves the marriage. The two litigants depart into society, each knowing that while earthly institutions have finished their work, divine justice remains perpetually active. The liar carries an inescapable spiritual burden, while the truthful party rests secure in the knowledge that God is the ultimate witness who never allows truth to be eclipsed. In the divine presence, no secret remains hidden and no deceit escapes ultimate reckoning.
