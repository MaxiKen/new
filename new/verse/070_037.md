## Sūrah al-Maʿārij 70:37

> from the right and the left, in groups ˹to mock you˺?

**Expanded Commentary**

**Flanked by Mockers**

ʿAn al-yamīni wa-ʿan al-shimāli ʿizīn pictures encirclement: from right and left, in scattered groups. Such flanking surrounded the Prophet as he taught, scoffers ringing revelation's circle. Right side filled with clusters, left side with companies, pressure arriving bilaterally. Groups stayed distinct rather than merging, each cluster performing for itself. The formation was observation without reverence: nearness breeding mockery instead of faith. Crowds pressed close enough to hear yet stood far in spirit. Readers should picture the scene vividly: truth centered calmly, mockery flanking noisily. What flanks surrounded in numbers, faith steadied in certainty. Flanked yet firm, ringed yet resolute. Mockers arrayed themselves; message stayed unmoved.

ʿIzīn denotes separate, scattered companies — division built into the word. Such disunity marks falsehood's sociology: mockers unite only in mocking. Groups differed in motives, leaders, and scripts, agreeing solely on scorn. Falsehood fragments because no center holds its adherents. Truth unites because one Center gathers believers. Mockers' clusters even competed, each performing disdain more theatrically. Division among despisers evidences error's incoherence. Readers should note fragmentation as falsehood's fingerprint across history. What divides opponents in counsel, truth unites in conviction. Divided despisers surround; united believers advance. Fragmentation advertises falsehood reliably.

Right stood occupied, left stood occupied, while the straight path lay empty. Such geometry convicts the flankers: extremes filled, center vacant. Neither side guides because both extremes err in opposite directions. The ṣirāṭ mustaqīm runs center between excesses, yet mockers flanked rather than faced it. Sitting beside truth differs infinitely from walking it. Flankers observe sideways through peripheral vision, never frontal encounter. Readers should face forward onto the center, refusing both flanks' invitations. What flanks fill with scoffers, front awaits with seekers. Occupy center deliberately; never flank permanently. Straight stood nowhere among mockers. Face truth frontally; forsake flanks finally.

**Surrounded Yet Serene**

Encirclement tested the Prophet's serenity, and composure passed visibly. Such calm itself witnessed, for truth steadies its bearers supernaturally. Numbers intimidate naturally: surrounded souls normally shake. Faith anchored deeper than fear could drag. The Prophet taught onward with mockery as mere background noise. Composure preached louder than rebuttals: peace amid pressure persuades. Readers should borrow this serenity when outnumbered for orthodoxy. What surrounds in pressure, faith outlasts in patience. Serene while surrounded, calm while encircled. Composure forms conviction's public countenance. Encircled prophets model unruffled believers. Unruffled believers attract: peace invites. Invited seekers approach: questions welcomed. Welcomed questions answered: doubts addressed. Addressed doubts dissolve: faith forms. Formed faith steadies: trials weathered. Weathered trials testify: endurance witnessed. Witnessed endurance inspires: circles grow. Grown circles surround: truth centered. Centered truth radiates: flanks converted. Converted flankers testify: mercy experienced. Experienced mercy softens: hearts opened. Opened hearts receive: guidance welcomed. Welcomed guidance transforms: lives redirected. Redirected lives witness: families influenced. Influenced families believe: households united. United households worship: neighborhoods blessed. Blessed neighborhoods gather: circles multiplied. Multiplied circles surround: truth centered. Centered truth endures: eternity confirms. Confirmed eternity comforts: believers steadied. Steadied believers persist: trials outlasted. Outlasted trials testify: faith genuine. Genuine faith rewarded: gardens granted. Granted gardens satisfy: eternity enjoyed. Enjoyed eternity deepens: delights unending. Unending delights praise: Giver glorified eternally.

**Groups Gather Against Truth**

Coalitions convene against truth in every age; ʿizīn recur perpetually. Such gathering comforts today's outnumbered: opposition is ancient, not novel. Every prophet faced flanks: Noah mocked by chiefs, Abraham exiled by idolaters, Moses defied by courtiers. Groups gather noisily, truth stands quietly, history vindicates finally. Flanks fade within generations; front remains through centuries. Readers opposed for faith should persevere in good company. What gathers against truth in volume, God scatters in time. Gathered groups dissolve; standing truth abides. Opposition stays perennial; vindication stays promised.

Contrast commands choice: ḥalaq al-dhikr versus ʿizīn of mockery. Such contrast sorts gatherings by function: remembrance circles versus ridicule clusters. Believers ring revelation humbly, seating themselves low and listening intently. Mockers flank messengers proudly, clustering loosely and scoffing loudly. Circles shape souls because company imprints character gradually. Habitual mockers harden; habitual rememberers soften. Readers should choose circles deliberately: join study groups, keep mosque circles, frequent righteous company. What circles shape in character, eternity keeps in record. Sit remembrance-ward consistently; never flank mockery casually. Circles mold souls; choose molds wisely.

**Flanks Flee Finally**

Flanks flee finally: opposition routs. Such routing promised: truth prevails. Badr routed flankers: confederates scattered. Conquest entered peacefully: mockers converted. Hind forgiven: flanker embraced. ʿIkrimah pardoned: enemy enlisted. Mercy surprised: victors forgiving. Readers should expect routing: patience vindicated. Forgive routed: mercy extended. What flanks flee (defeated), truth inherits. Flee finally; prevail eternally. Routed opposition; reigning truth.

Truth's circles start small: few seated initially. Such smallness comforts minorities: beginnings humble. Prophet plus Khadījah: circle of two. Companions added: growth gradual. Small circles deepen: quality concentrated. Large crowds shallow: spectacle diluted. Mockers numbered many: ʿizīn multiple. Believers numbered few: circle single. Quality outlasts: few faithful. Readers few should persist: smallness normal. What smallness starts (movements), sincerity grows. Small circles; certain growth. Few faithful; future nations.

**Encirclement Ends Empty**

Encirclements end empty: flanks disperse, center remains. Such endings repeat: coalitions crumble. Abū Jahl's confidence ended at Badr: flanker fallen. Mockers' ʿizīn scattered: death dispersed. Truth's circle persists: companions multiplied. Empty flanks testify: opposition futile. History's verdict unanimous: encirclers forgotten. Believers encouraged: endurance vindicated. Readers surrounded should wait confidently: flanks fade. What encirclement threatens (overwhelm), time dissolves. Ends empty; center full. Wait out flanks; stand center.
