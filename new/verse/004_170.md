## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:170

> O humanity! The Messenger has certainly come to you with the truth from your Lord, so believe for your own good. But if you disbelieve, then ˹know that˺ to Allah belongs whatever is in the heavens and the earth. And Allah is All-Knowing, All-Wise.

**Expanded Commentary**

**O Humanity — The Messenger Has Come With Truth From Your Lord**

The verse opens *yā ayyuhā al-nāsu qad jāʾakumu al-rasūlu bi al-ḥaqqi min rabbikum* — O humanity, the Messenger — *al-rasūl* — the Prophet Muḥammad ﷺ — has certainly come to you with the truth, the true, from your Lord. *Yā ayyuhā al-nāsu* — O humanity — was explained as second great humanity address after *“O humanity, be mindful of your Lord who created you from a single soul”* (4:1) and *“O humanity, He can remove you and bring others if He wills, He is Most Capable”* (4:133) — now *“O humanity, the Messenger has come with truth.”* *Qad jāʾakumu al-rasūlu* — has certainly come to you the Messenger — was read as fulfilment of the chain — *We have sent revelation to you as We sent to Noah… Psalms to David* (4:163), messengers told and not, Moses spoken to directly (4:164), messengers as good news and warnings so no excuse after messengers, He is Almighty, All-Wise (4:165), sufficient witness to what He sent with His knowledge and angels witness (4:166) — the Messenger is the told messenger. *Bi al-ḥaqqi* — with truth, as truth — was explained as that He came with true content and true descent, as the Book was sent with truth — *bi al-ḥaqq* — to judge between people by what He shows (4:105) and as God’s promise is true, whose word more truthful? (4:122). *Min rabbikum* — from your Lord — was read as from the Owner to whom belongs whatever is in heavens and earth (4:126) who is Lord who created from single soul (4:1) — truth from Him.

**So Believe For Your Own Good**

The call *fa āminū khayran lakum* — so believe, have faith, it is better for you, good for you, as good — was explained with *khayran lakum* — better for you, good for you — not for Him, for the addressee, as He is Self-Sufficient, Praiseworthy (4:131) though you disobey — to Him still belongs all. *Āminū* — have faith, believe — was read as comprehensive faith — *have faith in God, His Messenger, Book He revealed to His Messenger and Scriptures before* (4:136) — steadfast, not wavering, not distinguishing between God and messengers desiring a way between (4:150) — to be among those who accept all, rejecting none, to be given rewards (4:152). *Khayran lakum* was linked to *“Gardens forever, promise ever true”* (4:122) — belief’s *khayr* is Gardens and great reward promised to the grounded (4:162) and to those who hold fast to Him guided through straight path (4:175); to believe is to obtain the havens promised to the emigrant (4:100) and enrichment after separation (4:130) — *khayran lakum* is both world and Hereafter. Early reports added that the *fa* — so — makes belief the response after the Messenger has come with truth and after excuse removed (4:165) and witness sufficient (4:166) — belief is not speculation but response.

**But If You Disbelieve — To Him Belongs Whatever Is in Heavens and Earth**

The warning *wa in takfurū fa inna li Allāhi mā fī al-samāwāti wa mā fī al-arḍ* — but if you disbelieve, cover, then indeed to Him belongs whatever is in heavens and whatever on earth — was explained as that disbelief does not harm the Owner: as *We have commanded those before you and you to be mindful, if you disbelieve to Him still belongs all, He is Self-Sufficient, Praiseworthy* (4:131) — here *if you disbelieve* after the Messenger has come with truth, the same ownership remains. *Li Allāhi mā fī al-samāwāti wa mā fī al-arḍ* — to Him belongs whatever is in heavens and earth — was read as that disbelief after the Messenger’s truth is like the earlier demand greater than a book — *show God visible* — answered with thunderbolt and Mount raised (4:153–154) — to demand while ownership is His is disbelief against Owner. The sūrah has shown that *“why should He punish if you are grateful and faithful? He is ever Appreciative”* (4:147) — punishment is not His need; ownership after disbelief says the same — disbelief impoverishes the disbeliever, not the Owner. The clause was also linked to *“except that of Hell to stay forever, and that is easy for Him”* (4:169) — easy where ownership is all — Hell’s path easy is within that belonging.

**And God Is All-Knowing, All-Wise**

The seal *wa kāna Allāhu ʿAlīman Ḥakīmā* — And God is All-Knowing, All-Wise, was ever — was explained with *ʿAlīm* — All-Knowing — hearing disbelief and belief, knowing whether *khayran lakum* — better for you — is chosen — *He is All-Aware of what you do* (4:135) — and *Ḥakīm* — All-Wise — who sent messengers as good news and warnings so no excuse (4:165) and took Mount for covenant and sent Book with truth to judge (4:105) — wisdom is that the Messenger came with *ḥaqq* from Lord. *ʿAlīm Ḥakīm* was also the seal for *“whoever earns sin earns only upon himself, God is All-Knowing, All-Wise”* (4:111) — His knowing and wisdom guarantee that belief and disbelief are measured as not wronging an atom (4:40) and not wronging a speck (*naqīrā* — 4:124). To call to humanity — *yā ayyuhā al-nāsu* — to believe for your good, yet He is Self-Sufficient, All-Knowing, All-Wise, shows belief is medicine not debt.

**A Verse That Follows Hell’s Path Easy With Humanity’s Call**

Placed after the history — demand for a book from heaven and greater demand visible God, thunderbolt, calf yet forgiven, Mount raised, firm covenant, breaking, rejecting signs, killing prophets, claiming *ghulf* sealed, denying, slander against Mary, boasting killing Messiah not killed but made to appear, raised, belief before death, forbidding *ṭayyibāt* for *ẓulm* and hindering, taking interest though forbidden, consuming unjustly, painful punishment for disbelievers among them, but grounded among them to great reward, then lineage, messengers told and not, directly spoken, good news and warnings so no excuse, sufficient witness, disbelief and hindering far astray, disbelief and wrong not forgiven nor guided except Hell’s path forever easy — now the sūrah turns from third-person litigants to second-person humanity: O humanity, the Messenger has come with truth, so believe for your good, if you disbelieve to Him still belongs all, He is All-Knowing, All-Wise. The sūrah that began with single soul now ends its warning segment with humanity’s choice where ownership and knowledge and wisdom guarantee that believing is better for you, not for Him. For the weak this is an opening: where you were told to refer disputes to God and Messenger (4:59), the Messenger has come with truth — refer now — where you were told to verify reports (4:83), verify truth has come. The next passage will address People of the Book directly: do not go to extremes regarding faith, say nothing about God except truth, the Messiah was but messenger, Word through Mary and spirit from Him, believe in God and His messengers, do not say Trinity, stop — for your own good — He is only One God, far above having a son, to Him belongs whatever is in heavens and earth, He suffices as Trustee (4:171).
