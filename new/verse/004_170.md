## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:170

> O humanity! The Messenger has certainly come to you with the truth from your Lord, so believe for your own good. But if you disbelieve, then ˹know that˺ to Allah belongs whatever is in the heavens and the earth. And Allah is All-Knowing, All-Wise.

### Expanded Commentary

**A universal invitation to believe**

The Messenger has come to all humanity with truth from the Lord. The address widens beyond the People of the Book and beyond the first community. Faith is offered for the hearer’s own good; God does not need human belief to complete His kingdom.

If people disbelieve, everything still belongs to Allah. The consequence returns to them, not to a deficiency in God. This is why the invitation combines mercy and independence: believe because truth will benefit you, not because God is dependent on your assent.

Allah is All-Knowing and Wise. He knows who receives the message, how it is understood, and how each person responds. The believer carries the invitation with confidence but without coercion, pride, or despair.

**Faith is for the hearer’s benefit**

God’s independence means the invitation is free from divine need. The believer should therefore call others with sincerity, not manipulate them by pretending that God’s honour depends on winning an argument. Truth benefits the person who accepts it by ordering life toward knowledge, mercy, and accountability.
