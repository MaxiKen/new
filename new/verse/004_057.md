## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:57

> As for those who believe and do good, We will admit them into Gardens under which rivers flow, to stay there for ever and ever. There they will have pure spouses, and We will place them under a vast shade.

### Expanded Commentary

**Gardens as the opposite of denial**

The verse sets the fate of believers who do good against the punishment just described. Faith is paired with action because a claim without conduct does not express submission. The promised gardens, flowing water, lasting residence, pure spouses, and expansive shade translate security into images of abundance and rest for people who lived under hardship, conflict, and exposure.

**Purity without the world’s injuries**

The spouses of Paradise are described as pure. The purity includes freedom from betrayal, illness, resentment, and the social harms that can corrupt earthly relationships. The Qur’anic promise does not reduce the reward to physical pleasure; it presents companionship within a purified environment where no one’s dignity is threatened.

**Shade after exposure**

The “vast shade” answers the fatigue and heat of earthly struggle. It is also a symbol of divine protection. The believer who protected orphans, spouses, neighbours, and travellers under the sun of worldly responsibility is promised shelter under a greater mercy. The images are concrete because the body matters, but their moral atmosphere is deeper than comfort.

The verse’s condition remains “believe and do good.” Good deeds do not purchase God’s grace as though Paradise were a commercial wage, yet they are the evidence of living faith. The final reward is a gift that fulfills, rather than cancels, the believer’s effort.

**Reward as protected life**

The garden imagery gathers the needs exposed throughout the chapter: safety after danger, shade after exposure, companionship without betrayal, and abundance without exploitation. The reward does not detach the body from faith; it promises that bodily and relational goods will finally be free from the injustices that corrupt them here.
