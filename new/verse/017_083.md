## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:83

> **When We grant people Our favours, they turn away, acting arrogantly. But when touched with evil, they lose all hope.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**When Grant People Favours They Turn Away Arrogantly**

Wa-idhā anʿamnā ʿalā al-insāni aʿraḍa wa-na'ā bi-jānibihi — and when We grant favours, bestow blessings upon human, he turns away and goes far, with side, acting arrogantly. Wa-idhā, and when. Anʿamnā from n-ʿ-m, to grant favours, bestow blessings, favour, from anʿama, to grant favours, bestow blessings, as anʿamnā We grant favours, bestow blessings, as favours, blessings, as bounty never withheld, some favoured over others, provision abundant or limited, etc., and anʿamnā grant favours. ʿAlā al-insāni, upon human, to human, as insān human, ever hasty, ever ungrateful, etc., as human, as insān. Aʿraḍa from ʿ-r-ḍ, to turn away, avoid, as aʿraḍtum you turned away, as turn away, avoid, as when delivered to shore you turned away, etc., and aʿraḍa turns away, avoids, as turning away from gratitude, from Allah, etc. Wa-na'ā from n-ʾ-y, to go far, keep far, distance, from na'ā, to go far, keep far, to be distant, as na'ā bi-jānibihi, goes far with side, with side, acting arrogantly, turning side, as arrogance, as going far with side, side, as arrogant, as turning away acting arrogantly, going far with side, side, as arrogance, as previous do not walk arrogantly, maraḥan, arrogantly, and become extremely arrogant, ʿuluwwan kabīran, great arrogance, and na'ā bi-jānibihi acting arrogantly, going far with side, as arrogance. Al-Ṭabarī explains that when We grant people, human, Our favours, blessings, bounty, provision, etc., they turn away, avoid, turn away from gratitude, from Allah, and go far with side, acting arrogantly, turning side, going far, distant, arrogantly, as human nature, ever ungrateful, ever hasty, etc., as human turns away arrogantly when favoured, as ungratefulness, as Devil ever ungrateful, etc.

**When Touched With Evil They Lose All Hope**

Wa-idhā massahu al-sharru kāna ya'ūsan — and when evil touches him, he is despairing, loses all hope. Wa-idhā, and when. Massahu from m-s-s, to touch, afflict, befall, as massakumu touches you, hardship touches you at sea, etc., and massahu evil touches him, afflicts him. Al-sharru from sh-r-r, evil, bad, adversity, harm, as sharr evil, as opposite of khayr good, as evil, bad, adversity, harm, as evil touches, afflicts, as hardship, etc. Kāna ya'ūsan from y-ʾ-s, despairing, losing hope, despondent, from ya'isa, to despair, lose hope, from ya'ūs, despairing, losing hope, despondent, as ya'ūsan despairing, losing all hope, despondent, as when evil touches, he despairs, loses hope, as human nature, ever ungrateful, ever hasty, etc., as when touched with evil loses hope, as human despairs, as previous when grant favours turn away arrogantly, when evil touched lose hope, as two extremes, arrogance when favoured, despair when evil touched, as human nature.

**Why Turn Away Arrogantly When Favoured And Despair When Evil Touched**

Why turn away arrogantly when favoured and despair when evil touched? Because human is ever hasty, ʿajūlan, ever hasty, prays for evil as for good, ever hasty, and ever ungrateful, kafūran, ever ungrateful, as humankind ever ungrateful, kafūran, as Devil ever ungrateful, kafūran, and human acts in own way, everyone acts in own way, Lord knows best whose way rightly guided, as verse 84 says, and human nature, when favoured, turns away arrogantly, goes far with side, acting arrogantly, as arrogance, as extremely arrogant, as do not walk arrogantly, etc., and when evil touched, hardship, adversity, despairs, loses all hope, despondent, as human despairs, as previous chapter said when grant favours turn away, when evil touched despair, etc., as human nature, ever hasty, ever ungrateful, etc., and as Qur'an healing mercy for believers but increases wrongdoers in loss, as truth vs falsehood, etc., and as truth come falsehood vanished, indeed falsehood bound to vanish, etc.

**Connection To Truth Come Falsehood Vanish And Quran Healing Mercy And Everyone Acts In Own Way**

Verse 81 truth has come and falsehood vanished, indeed falsehood bound to vanish, truth vs falsehood. Verse 82 We send down Quran as healing and mercy for believers but only increases wrongdoers in loss, healing mercy vs loss, truth vs falsehood. Verse 83 when We grant people favours they turn away acting arrogantly, but when touched with evil they lose all hope, showing human nature, ever ungrateful, turning away arrogantly when favoured, losing hope when evil touched, as human nature, ever hasty, ever ungrateful, as insān, and as truth come falsehood vanish, healing mercy vs loss, etc., and verse 84 everyone acts in own way, Lord knows best whose way rightly guided, showing that human acts in own way, some turn away arrogantly when favoured, despair when evil touched, some grateful, patient, etc., and Lord knows best whose way rightly guided, as He knows best what is within selves, etc.

**Link To Lord Steers Ships Ever Merciful And Forget Gods At Sea Turn Away At Shore Ever Ungrateful And Warnings**

Verse 66 Lord steers ships for you through sea to seek bounty, ever Merciful. Verse 67 hardship at sea forget all gods except Him, delivers to shore turn away, humankind ever ungrateful, kafūran. **Linguistic Note On An'amna And A'rada And Na'a Bi-janibihi And Massahu And Sharr And Ya'usan**

Anʿamnā from n-ʿ-m, to grant favours, bestow blessings, from anʿama. Insān from a-n-s, human, from insān. Aʿraḍa from ʿ-r-ḍ, to turn away, avoid, from aʿraḍa. Na'ā from n-ʾ-y, to go far, keep far, distance, from na'ā. Jānib from j-n-b, side, from jānib. Massahu from m-s-s, to touch, afflict, from massa. Sharr from sh-r-r, evil, bad, adversity, harm, from sharr. Ya'ūs from y-ʾ-s, despairing, losing hope, from ya'isa, to despair. The verse's structure — when We grant favours upon human he turns away and goes far with side, and when evil touches him he is despairing — with favours granted turning away arrogantly going far with side and evil touched despairing losing hope, shows human nature, ever ungrateful, turning away arrogantly when favoured, despairing when evil touched, two extremes, arrogance when favoured, despair when evil, as human nature, ever hasty, ever ungrateful.

**How This Verse Teaches Human Nature Turning Away Arrogantly And Despairing**

This verse teaches human nature, turning away arrogantly when favoured and despairing when evil touched: when We grant people, human, Our favours, blessings, bounty, provision, etc., they turn away, avoid, turn away from gratitude, from Allah, and go far with side, acting arrogantly, turning side, going far, distant, arrogantly, as arrogance, as extremely arrogant, as do not walk arrogantly, etc., but when touched with evil, hardship, adversity, harm, they lose all hope, despair, despondent, ya'ūsan, despairing, losing hope, as human nature, ever ungrateful, ever hasty, etc., as human turns away arrogantly when favoured, despairs when evil touched, two extremes, not moderate, not grateful, patient, as most upright guidance teaches gratitude when favoured, patience when evil touched, not arrogance, despair. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should be grateful when favoured, not turn away arrogantly, go far with side, and patient when evil touched, not despair, lose hope, but hope, trust, as most upright, wisdom, that Qur'an guides to, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, healing mercy, vs wrongdoers loss, etc.

**A Reflection On Arrogance And Despair As Extremes**

Aʿraḍa wa-na'ā bi-jānibihi — turns away and goes far with side, acting arrogantly — indicates arrogance when favoured, turning away from gratitude, going far with side, side, as arrogant, as extremely arrogant, as do not walk arrogantly, maraḥan, arrogantly, you cannot crack earth nor reach mountains height, but human arrogant when favoured, turns away, goes far with side. Ya'ūsan — despairing, losing all hope, despondent — indicates despair when evil touched, losing hope, despondent, as human despairs when evil touched, loses hope, as human ever ungrateful, ever hasty, etc. Both arrogance when favoured and despair when evil touched are extremes, not most upright, not wisdom, most upright is gratitude when favoured, patience when evil touched, moderate, as most upright guidance teaches, wisdom, that Qur'an guides to, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, healing mercy, vs wrongdoers loss, arrogance, despair, as human nature, but believer avoids extremes, follows most upright.
