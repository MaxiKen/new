## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:58

> **There is not a ˹wicked˺ society that We will not destroy or punish with a severe torment before the Day of Judgment. That is written in the Record.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Not A Wicked Society That We Will Not Destroy Or Punish Before Day Of Judgment**

Wa-in min qaryatin illā naḥnu muhlikūhā qabla yawmi al-qiyāmati aw muʿadhdhibūhā ʿadhāban shadīdan — and there is not a town, society, except We will destroy it or punish it with severe torment before Day of Resurrection. Wa-in, and not, there is not. Min qaryatin, a town, society, settlement, from q-r-y, town, as qaryah town, society, as previous qaryah when intend to destroy society, command elite but they rebel, decree justified, destroy utterly, and how many generations destroyed after Noah, etc. Illā, except. Naḥnu, We, Allah. Muhlikūhā from h-l-k, destroyers, will destroy, from ahlaka, to destroy, as nuhlika destroy, ahlaknā destroyed, muhlikūhā destroyers, will destroy. Qabla yawmi al-qiyāmati, before Day of Resurrection, Day of Standing, from q-b-l, before, and y-w-m, Day, and q-w-m, Resurrection. Aw muʿadhdhibūhā, or punish it, torment it, from ʿ-dh-b, to punish, torment, as muʿadhdhibīn punishers, muʿadhdhibūhā punishers, will punish. ʿAdhāban shadīdan, punishment severe, torment severe, from ʿ-dh-b and sh-d-d, severe, strong, from shadīdan severe. Al-Ṭabarī explains that there is not a wicked society, town, that We will not destroy or punish with severe torment before Day of Judgment, meaning every society, town, that is wicked, disbelieving, will be destroyed or punished with severe torment before Day of Judgment, either destruction or severe punishment, as divine law, as many generations destroyed after Noah, etc., as qaryah when intend to destroy, etc. Al-Qurṭubī says qaryah here means society, town, settlement, wicked, disbelieving, that will be destroyed or punished severely before Day of Judgment, as either destruction utterly or severe torment, as test, punishment, as history shows.

**That Is Written In Record**

Kāna dhālika fī al-kitābi masṭūran — that is in Book, Record, written, inscribed. Kāna, is, was, indicates it is. Dhālika, that, that decree, destruction or severe punishment before Day. Fī al-kitābi, in Book, Record, in Record, Preserved Tablet, Book, as kitāb Book, Record, as verse 13 record laid open, and verse 58 Record, Book. Masṭūran from s-ṭ-r, written, inscribed, lined, from saṭara, to write, inscribe, line, from masṭūr, written, inscribed, lined, as Record written. Al-Ṭabarī explains that that, destruction or severe punishment of wicked societies before Day of Judgment, is written in Record, Book, Preserved Tablet, inscribed, decreed, as divine decree, written, as everything explained in detail, as everything in Record, as destiny bound to neck, etc. Al-Rāzī says kāna dhālika fī al-kitābi masṭūran indicates that destruction or severe punishment before Day is written in Record, Book, Preserved Tablet, as divine decree, preordained, as part of divine knowledge, will, as Lord knows best those in heavens and earth, etc.

**Why Every Wicked Society Destroyed Or Punished Severely Before Day**

Why every wicked society destroyed or punished severely before Day? Because divine law: when intention to destroy society, command elite but they rebel, decree justified, destroy utterly, as verse 16 says, and how many generations destroyed after Noah, as verse 17 says, and Lord All-Aware All-Seeing of sins, sufficient, etc., and provision abundant or limited, etc., but wicked societies that rebel, corrupt, become extremely arrogant, as Children of Isra'il twice, etc., will be destroyed or punished severely before Day, as either destruction utterly or severe torment, as either via servants of great might, or via other punishments, as history shows, as warning.

**Connection To Destroying Society When Elite Rebel And How Many Destroyed After Noah**

Verse 16 whenever We intend to destroy society We command its elite but they rebel so decree justified destroy utterly, verse 17 how many peoples destroyed after Noah, sufficient Lord as All-Aware All-Seeing of sins, verse 58 there is not wicked society that We will not destroy or punish severely before Day, that is written in Record. The connection is that destruction or severe punishment of wicked societies before Day is divine law, illustrated by many generations destroyed after Noah, and by principle when intend to destroy society command elite but they rebel, decree justified, destroy utterly, and is written in Record, Book, as decree, preordained, as part of divine knowledge, will.

**Link To Record Laid Open And Record Written And Detailed Explanation**

Verse 13 record laid open, bound destiny to neck, record wide open, verse 14 read your book, sufficient self as reckoner, verse 58 that is written in Record, masṭūran, written, inscribed, as Record, Book, Preserved Tablet, where destruction or severe punishment written, as everything explained in detail, as verse 12 everything explained in detail, and as destiny bound to neck, etc., and as Record, Book, will be laid open on Day, read, etc., so destruction or severe punishment written in Record as part of detailed explanation.

**Linguistic Note On Qaryah And Muhlikuhā And Mu'adhdhibuhā And Kitab And Masturan**

Qaryah from q-r-y, town, society, settlement, from qarā, to gather. Muhlikūhā from h-l-k, destroyers, will destroy, from ahlaka, to destroy, from halaka, to perish. Qabla from q-b-l, before. Yawm from y-w-m, Day. Qiyāmah from q-w-m, Resurrection, Standing. Muʿadhdhibūhā from ʿ-dh-b, punishers, will punish, from ʿadhdhaba, to punish. ʿAdhāb from ʿ-dh-b, punishment. Shadīd from sh-d-d, severe, strong, from shadda, to be strong, severe. Kitāb from k-t-b, Book, Record, from kataba, to write. Masṭūr from s-ṭ-r, written, inscribed, from saṭara, to write, inscribe. The verse's structure — there is not a town except We will destroy it or punish it severely before Day of Resurrection, that is in Book written — with universal statement no town except destroyed or punished severely before Day and statement that is written in Record, shows divine law of destruction or severe punishment for wicked societies before Day, written in Record, preordained, as part of divine knowledge, will.

**How This Verse Teaches Divine Law Of Destruction Or Severe Punishment**

This verse teaches divine law: there is not a wicked society, town, that We will not destroy or punish with severe torment before Day of Judgment, that is written in Record, Book, inscribed, as decree, preordained, as part of divine knowledge, will, as Lord knows best those in heavens and earth, All-Aware All-Seeing of sins, sufficient, etc., and as many generations destroyed after Noah, etc., and as when intend to destroy society command elite but they rebel, decree justified, destroy utterly. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should know every wicked society will be destroyed or punished severely before Day, as divine law, written in Record, so avoid wickedness, corruption, arrogance, follow most upright guidance, wisdom, that Qur'an guides to, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, avoiding destruction, severe punishment, painful punishment, Hell. The practical lesson is to avoid being wicked society, corrupt, arrogant, follow most upright, be righteous, awwāb, constantly turning, as Lord Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, but torment fearsome, and destruction or severe punishment written in Record for wicked.

**A Reflection On Written In Record**

Masṭūran fī al-kitābi — written in Record, Book, inscribed — indicates that destruction or severe punishment of wicked societies before Day is not random, not arbitrary, but written, inscribed, in Record, Book, Preserved Tablet, as divine decree, preordained, as part of divine knowledge, will, as Lord knows best, All-Aware All-Seeing, knows sins, sufficient, and as everything explained in detail, detailed explanation, and as destiny bound to neck, record laid open, read book, sufficient self as reckoner, etc., and as Record will be laid open on Day, read, etc., so destruction or severe punishment written in Record as part of detailed explanation, divine law, warning, so avoid wickedness, follow most upright, wisdom, that Qur'an guides to, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, avoiding destruction, severe punishment, painful punishment, Hell, as torment fearsome, but Lord Most Forbearing All-Forgiving to those who constantly turn.
