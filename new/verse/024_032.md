## Sūrah an-Nūr 24:32

> Marry off the ˹free˺ singles among you, as well as the righteous of your bondmen and bondwomen. If they are poor, Allah will enrich them out of His bounty. For Allah is All-Bountiful, All-Knowing.

**Expanded Commentary**

**The Communal Mandate to Facilitate Marriage: ankiḥū al-ayāmā**

The divine command establishes a collective social obligation: *wa-ankiḥū al-ayāmā minkum*. The imperative form *ankiḥū* (marry off / arrange marriages for) addresses the entire Muslim community, legal guardians, and communal leaders. The term *al-ayāmā* is the plural of *ayyim*, designating any individual who lacks a spouse—whether male or female, never-married, widowed, or divorced.

Marriage in the Qur'anic vision is not an isolated, purely private contract left entirely to individual initiative or romance; it is a foundational pillar of social order. The community carries an active responsibility to facilitate honorable marriages, dismantling financial, bureaucratic, and social obstacles so that adults can satisfy their biological and emotional needs within sacred covenants. Leaving citizens trapped in involuntary celibacy generates social instability, illicit temptations, and communal breakdown. By making marriage facilitation a communal duty, the revelation builds a proactive culture of social care and collective solidarity that safeguards moral dignity across the entire population, establishing a healthy social foundation.

**The Matrimonial Rights of the Enslaved: al-ṣāliḥīna min ʿibādikum**

The divine legislation boldly includes the most vulnerable social strata: *wa-al-ṣāliḥīna min ʿibādikum wa-imāʾikum*, as well as the righteous among your bondmen and bondwomen. In many ancient civilizations and contemporary legal codes of that era, enslaved individuals were classified as subhuman property, entirely stripped of legal personality and matrimonial rights.

The Qur'an radically dismantled this dehumanization, commanding masters to arrange and support the marriages of their enslaved dependents. The qualification *al-ṣāliḥīn* emphasizes moral readiness, upright character, and capability for domestic responsibility. By recognizing their right to establish lawful families, have children, and experience domestic affection, the revelation affirms the universal human dignity of the enslaved, elevating them into autonomous moral subjects before God and establishing that spiritual virtue supersedes legal servitude. The divine law ensures that the yearning for companionship and righteous family life is honored in every servant regardless of social station, affirming universal spiritual equality.

**The Divine Promise of Economic Enrichment: yughnihimu Allāh**

The verse addresses the universal anxiety that frequently delays marriage: financial poverty. The divine promise intervenes with astonishing economic clarity: *in yakūnū fuqarāʾa yughnihimu Allāhu min faḍlih*, if they are poor, Allah will enrich them out of His bounty. Human prudence routinely counsels delay, advising youth to postpone marriage until substantial wealth, property, and career security are accumulated.

The Qur'an boldly challenges this purely materialistic calculation, asserting that marriage is not a financial drain that worsens poverty, but a divine catalyst for material enrichment. Authoritative prophetic traditions reinforce this cosmic principle: Three individuals have an absolute right upon God's assistance, including the one who enters marriage desiring chastity. The eminent Companion Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq urged: Obey God in what He commanded you of marriage, and He will fulfill what He promised you of wealth! The Companion ʿAbd Allāh ibn Masʿūd similarly declared: Seek financial sufficiency through marriage! This divine promise instills courage in the hearts of the faithful, freeing them from disabling financial panic and anchoring trust in God's providence.

**The Psychological and Sociological Dynamic of Matrimonial Prosperity**

Analyzing how marriage attracts enrichment reveals a profound interplay between spiritual blessing (*barakah*) and human psychology. On a spiritual level, God blesses a union contracted to protect chastity and uphold divine boundaries, opening avenues of uncalculated sustenance (*rizq*).

On a psychological and sociological level, marriage radically restructures the human ego. An aimless single individual frequently squanders time, energy, and resources. When united in marriage, the individual acquires deep emotional stability, purpose, and a sense of solemn duty toward a spouse and future children. Shared domestic labor reduces wasteful expenditure, emotional partnership provides psychological resilience during adversity, and the burning desire to provide for loved ones inspires diligent industry and fiscal discipline. Divine bounty operates through both metaphysical grace and the natural socioeconomic incentives of family life, transforming two struggling individuals into an economically resilient household capable of thriving together.

**Dismantling the Cult of Materialistic Obstacles**

This divine statute serves as an enduring critique of the cultural vanities that communities erect around marriage. Across history, families have often sabotaged the marriages of their sons and daughters by demanding exorbitant dowries, lavish banquet halls, expensive jewelry, and pre-existing wealth, creating artificial economic barriers that trap young people in prolonged bachelorhood.

Verse 32 repudiates this materialistic elitism. The revelation commands communities to prioritize religious commitment and character over accumulated gold. If an individual possesses faith, righteousness, and the willingness to work, poverty must never be used as a weapon to reject their proposal. To deny an honorable suitor solely on grounds of material poverty is to foster widespread moral corruption, forcing society to re-anchor its values in divine trust rather than commercial materialism. The true wealth of a marriage lies in virtue, mutual mercy, and divine blessing that enriches the soul.

**The Divine Horizon: Allāhu Wāsiʿun ʿAlīm**

The verse seals the matrimonial charter with two majestic divine names: *wa-Allāhu wāsiʿun ʿalīm*, and Allah is All-Bountiful, All-Knowing. The attribute *al-Wāsiʿ* signifies the One whose capacity, generosity, and treasures are utterly boundless, incapable of being depleted by providing sustenance to every creature in existence.

He is *al-ʿAlīm*—All-Knowing of the secret intentions that animate every marriage proposal. God knows who seeks marriage sincerely desiring chastity, family, and spiritual peace, and He knows who approaches relationships with exploitative, selfish motives. Connecting the facilitation of marriage to these divine attributes provides the ultimate assurance to the impoverished believer: the All-Bountiful Lord possesses infinite resources to enrich the faithful, while His infinite knowledge guarantees that every righteous effort will be rewarded in full, opening doors of provision that surpass all human expectation and calculation.
