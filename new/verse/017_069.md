## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:69

> **Or do you feel secure that He will not send you back to sea once again, and send upon you a violent storm, drowning you for your denial? Then you will find none to avenge you against Us.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Do You Feel Secure He Will Not Send You Back To Sea And Violent Storm Drowning For Denial**

Am a-mintum an yuʿīdakum fīhi tāratan ukhrā fa-yursila ʿalaykum qāṣifan mina al-rīḥi fa-yughriqakum bi-mā kafartum — or do you feel secure that He will not return you in it another time, send upon you violent storm wind, drown you for what you disbelieved, denied. Am, or, or do you feel secure, alternative to previous do you feel secure land swallow or storm stones. A-mintum from a-m-n, to feel secure, safe, as previous a-fa-amintum do you feel secure. An yuʿīdakum from ʿ-w-d, to return, bring back, repeat, as yuʿīdakum return you, bring you back, as yuʿīdunā will bring us back, return us, as resurrection, return. Fīhi, in it, in sea, in baḥr, sea, as ships through sea, hardship at sea, etc. Tāratan ukhrā from t-w-r, another time, once again, another turn, from tārah, time, turn, and ukhrā, other, another, from ā-kh-r, other. Fa-yursila from r-s-l, then send, and send, as yursila send. ʿAlaykum, upon you. Qāṣifan from q-ṣ-f, violent storm, that breaks, shatters, from qaṣafa, to break, shatter, violent, from qāṣif, violent storm, breaking, shattering, as violent storm wind that breaks ships, etc. Mina al-rīḥi from r-w-ḥ, from wind, storm wind, from rīḥ, wind, as rīḥ wind, storm. Fa-yughriqakum from gh-r-q, to drown, make drown, from aghraqa, to drown, make drown, as yughriqakum drown you, make you drown. Bi-mā kafartum from k-f-r, for what you disbelieved, denied, for your disbelief, denial, ungratefulness, as kafartum you disbelieved, denied, were ungrateful, from kafara, to disbelieve, deny, be ungrateful, as kafūran ever ungrateful, and kafartum disbelieved. Al-Ṭabarī explains that or do you feel secure, safe, that He will not send you back to sea once again, another time, after delivering you to shore, you turned away, ungrateful, and send upon you violent storm, qāṣif, wind that breaks, shatters, violent storm, drowning you, making you drown, for your denial, disbelief, ungratefulness, as punishment, as every wicked society destroyed or punished severely before Day written in Record, as many generations destroyed after Noah, etc., as sea dangerous, ships steered, but violent storm drowning for denial, as warning, as He is ever Merciful but torment fearsome.

**Then You Will Find None To Avenge You Against Us**

Thumma lā tajidū lakum ʿalaynā tabīʿan — then you will find not for you against Us follower, avenger, pursuer. Thumma, then, after violent storm drowning for denial. Lā tajidū from w-j-d, to find, you will not find, as previous lā tajidū lakum wakīlan, no guardian. Lakum, for you. ʿAlaynā, against Us, upon Us, against Allah, Us. Tabīʿan from t-b-ʿ, follower, pursuer, avenger, one who follows, pursues, avenges, from tabiʿa, to follow, pursue, from tabīʿ, follower, pursuer, avenger, as one who avenges you against Us, follows to avenge. Al-Ṭabarī explains that then you will find none to avenge you against Us, no follower, pursuer, avenger, who will follow, pursue, avenge you against Us, against Allah, if He drowns you for denial, as only Allah has power, as those claimed besides Him have no power to remove harm or transfer, as verse 56 says, no power, so if He sends back to sea again and sends violent storm drowning you for denial, you will find none to avenge you against Us, no avenger, pursuer, follower, to avenge, as only Allah has power.

**Why Send Back To Sea Violent Storm Drowning For Denial And No Avenger**

Why send back to sea once again and violent storm drowning for denial and no avenger? Because when hardship at sea you forget all gods except Him, invoke only Him, but when delivered to shore, land, you turn away, ungrateful, ever ungrateful, as humankind ever ungrateful, kafūran, as Devil ever ungrateful, kafūran, so do you feel secure that He will not send you back to sea once again, another time, and send violent storm, qāṣif, wind breaking, shattering, drowning you for your denial, disbelief, ungratefulness, as punishment, as every wicked society destroyed or punished severely before Day written in Record, as many generations destroyed after Noah, etc., and then you will find none to avenge you against Us, no follower, pursuer, avenger, to avenge you against Allah, if He drowns you for denial, as only Allah has power, as those claimed besides Him have no power, as verse 56 says, no power, so be grateful, not ungrateful, not turn away, not deny.
