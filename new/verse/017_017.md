## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:17

> **˹Imagine˺ how many peoples We have destroyed after Noah! And sufficient is your Lord as All-Aware and All-Seeing of the sins of His servants. This Life or the Next?**

**Expanded Commentary**

**How Many Peoples Destroyed After Noah**

Wa-kam ahlaknā mina al-qurūni min baʿdi Nūḥin — and how many generations We destroyed after Noah. Kam, how many, how much, interrogative for many, rhetorical to indicate many. Ahlaknā from h-l-k, to destroy, cause to perish, as previous verse nuhlika, destroy, and dammarnā, destroy utterly. Mina al-qurūni from q-r-n, generations, peoples, centuries, from same root as qarn, generation, century, horn, period. Min baʿdi, after, from baʿd. Nūḥin, Noah, prophet Noah, from n-w-ḥ, name. Al-Ṭabarī explains that how many generations, peoples, We destroyed after Noah due to their rebellion after messenger sent and elite rebelled, as example of previous principle that when intention to destroy society and elite rebel, decree justified and destruction utterly. Al-Qurṭubī says after Noah because Noah was first major messenger to people of earth after Adam, and after him many generations destroyed for rejecting messengers, as Qur'an mentions ʿĀd, Thamūd, Pharaoh, etc. The phrase kam ahlaknā indicates many, not few, showing pattern of destruction after warning.

**Sufficient Lord As Aware And Seeing Sins**

Wa-kafā bi-rabbika bi-dhunūbi ʿibādihi khabīran baṣīran — and sufficient is your Lord with sins of His servants as All-Aware and All-Seeing. Kafā from k-f-y, to suffice, be enough, as in verse 14 kafā bi-nafsika sufficient yourself as reckoner. Bi-rabbika, with your Lord, by your Lord, from r-b-b, Lord, master, sustainer. Bi-dhunūbi, with sins, of sins, from dh-n-b, sin, tail, from same root as dhanb, sin, offense. ʿIbādihi, His servants, slaves, from ʿ-b-d, to worship, serve, from ʿabada. Khabīran from kh-b-r, to be aware, informed, from same root as khabar, news, and khabīr, All-Aware, who knows inner. Baṣīran from b-ṣ-r, to see, seeing, from same root as baṣar, sight, and mubṣir, bright, giving sight, as verse 12 made day bright mubṣiratan. Al-Ṭabarī says sufficient is your Lord as All-Aware and All-Seeing of sins of His servants, meaning He knows and sees their sins, no need for other informer, He is enough to know and punish accordingly with justice. Al-Rāzī says khabīran baṣīran indicates complete knowledge: aware of inner sins and seeing outer deeds, nothing hidden.

**Why After Noah Specifically**

Why after Noah? Because Noah's people were first major destroyed after Adam due to rejection of messenger, and after him pattern continued, and because verse 3 said progeny of those carried with Noah, grateful servant, linking to Noah. Now verse says how many generations destroyed after Noah, indicating that being progeny of those carried with Noah does not guarantee safety; many after Noah destroyed for sins despite being progeny of survivors. The Children of Isra'il story also after Noah, as they are progeny of those carried with Noah, and they were warned about corruption twice. The sūrah therefore reminds that history shows many destroyed after Noah when they rebelled, so current people, also progeny of those carried, should not be complacent, should follow most upright guidance.

**Connection To Destroying Society When Elite Rebel**

Verse 16 said whenever We intend to destroy society We command its elite but they rebel so decree justified then We destroy utterly. Verse 17 says how many peoples We have destroyed after Noah, sufficient Lord as All-Aware and All-Seeing sins of servants. The connection is that destruction of many generations after Noah illustrates principle of verse 16: elite rebelled, decree justified, destruction utterly. The phrase kam ahlaknā indicates many examples, not just one, showing consistent divine law. And sufficient Lord as Aware Seeing indicates He knows sins that caused destruction, no injustice, destruction based on knowledge of sins, not arbitrary, and His awareness sufficient to judge.

**Link To Previous Chapter About Destroying Towns And Provision**

Previous chapter also mentioned destroying towns, and that Allah does not punish until messenger sent, and that He gives provision in due measure, Aware Seeing of servants. This verse continues theme of destruction after Noah as warning, and that Lord is Aware Seeing of sins of servants, as previous chapter said He is Aware Seeing of servants regarding provision. The sūrah's opening about Night Journey and Children of Isra'il corruption also about destruction. The verse therefore places current audience in historical context: many before destroyed, so heed warning, follow most upright guidance that gives mighty reward.

**Linguistic Note On Qurun And Khabir Basir**

Kam from k-m, how many. Ahlaknā from h-l-k, to destroy. Qurūn from q-r-n, generations, centuries, from qarn, horn, generation as period, as horn marks period. Baʿd from b-ʿ-d, after. Nūḥ, Noah, prophet name. Kafā from k-f-y, to suffice. Rabb from r-b-b, Lord. Dhunūb from dh-n-b, sins. ʿIbād from ʿ-b-d, servants. Khabīr from kh-b-r, All-Aware, informed, who knows hidden. Baṣīr from b-ṣ-r, All-Seeing. The verse's structure — how many generations We destroyed after Noah, sufficient your Lord with sins of His servants as All-Aware All-Seeing — with rhetorical question about many destroyed and statement of sufficiency of Lord's awareness, shows that destruction is based on knowledge of sins, not arbitrary, and that Lord's awareness sufficient to judge justly, no need for other.

**How This Verse Teaches Reflection On History**

This verse teaches reflection on history: how many generations destroyed after Noah for sins, so reflect and avoid their path. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should consider history of past generations destroyed, as Qur'an often says travel in earth and see how was end of those before, to learn lesson, and that many destroyed after Noah shows pattern. The sūrah's mention of Children of Isra'il corruption twice is part of that history, and current Makkan audience also should learn. The practical lesson is to not be among mutraf, elite, who rebel, but to follow command, seek bounty with gratitude, and remember that Lord is All-Aware and All-Seeing of sins, so hidden sins not hidden from Him, and to repent.

**A Lesson On Lord Being Sufficient As Aware Seeing**

Wa-kafā bi-rabbika bi-dhunūbi ʿibādihi khabīran baṣīran — sufficient is your Lord with sins of His servants as All-Aware, All-Seeing. This phrase reassures that Allah knows and sees sins, sufficient as watcher, no need for other witness, and that judgment based on complete knowledge. For believer, it is warning that sins seen, even hidden, and also comfort that Lord is Aware of struggles and sincere efforts. The combination khabīran baṣīran, All-Aware All-Seeing, indicates comprehensive knowledge: khabīr knows inner, hidden, baṣīr sees outer, apparent, as verse 12 said day bright mubṣiratan, giving sight, and here Lord Baṣīr sees all. The believer who knows Lord is All-Aware All-Seeing of sins will be mindful, will repent, will follow most upright guidance that leads to mighty reward and avoids painful punishment, and will not pray for evil out of haste, will not desire fleeting alone.
