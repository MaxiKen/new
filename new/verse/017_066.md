## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:66

> **It is your Lord Who steers the ships for you through the sea, so that you may seek His bounty. Surely He is ever Merciful to you.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Lord Steers Ships Through Sea So You May Seek Bounty**

Rabbukumu alladhī yuzjī lakum al-fulka fī al-baḥri li-tabtaghū min faḍlihi — your Lord is Who drives, steers for you ships in sea so you may seek from His bounty. Rabbukumu, your Lord, your Lord, from r-b-b, Lord, as rabbukum your Lord. Alladhī yuzjī from z-j-y, to drive, steer, push gently, from azjā, to drive, steer, as yuzjī drives, steers ships. Lakum, for you, for your benefit. Al-fulka from f-l-k, ships, ship, from fulk, ship, ships, as fulk ships, as ark, as progeny of those carried with Noah in Ark, fulk, as ships. Fī al-baḥri from b-ḥ-r, in sea, sea, ocean, from baḥr, sea. Li-tabtaghū from b-gh-y, to seek, desire, so you may seek, as tabtaghū seek bounty, as verse 12 li-tabtaghū faḍlan, so you may seek bounty, and verse 20 bounty, etc. Min faḍlihi, from His bounty, from His favour, bounty, grace, from f-ḍ-l, bounty, favour, as faḍl bounty, as faḍlihi His bounty. Al-Ṭabarī explains that your Lord is Who steers, drives ships for you through sea, so that you may seek His bounty, provision, via trade, travel, etc., as ships steered through sea by Lord, as He subjected sea, etc., as He is ever Merciful, as He provides, etc. Al-Qurṭubī says yuzjī lakum al-fulka fī al-baḥri means drives, steers ships for you in sea, so you may seek bounty, provision, via sea travel, trade, etc., as sea subjected for seeking bounty, as previous chapter said He subjected for your benefit night and day, sun and moon, etc., and sea, etc.

**He Is Ever Merciful To You**

Innahu kāna bikum raḥīman — indeed He is with you, to you, ever Merciful. Innahu, indeed He, Allah, your Lord. Kāna, is, was, indicates He is. Bikum, with you, to you, about you, you all. Raḥīman from r-ḥ-m, Merciful, ever Merciful, from raḥima, to have mercy, from raḥmah, mercy, as Raḥīm Merciful, ever Merciful, as He is ever Merciful to you, as He steers ships for you to seek bounty, as mercy, as He is Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, but also Merciful, as He provides, etc. Al-Ṭabarī explains that He is ever Merciful to you, meaning Lord ever Merciful to you, humans, as He steers ships for you to seek bounty, as mercy, as He is Merciful, not hastening punishment, as Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, but torment fearsome, but Merciful to you, as He provides, etc.

**Why Ships Through Sea To Seek Bounty And Merciful**

Why ships through sea to seek bounty and ever Merciful? Because Lord gives abundant or limited provisions, All-Aware All-Seeing, bounty never withheld, some favoured over others, provision, etc., and ships steered through sea so you may seek bounty, provision, via trade, travel, as sea subjected, as He dignified children of Adam, carried them on land and sea, granted good provisions, privileged far above many creatures, as verse 70 says, and ships are means to seek bounty, as mercy, ever Merciful to you, as He provides, etc., as He is Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, but also Merciful, ever Merciful, as He steers ships for you, etc.

**Connection To Dignified Children Of Adam Carried On Land And Sea And Bounty**

Verse 66 Lord steers ships for you through sea so you may seek bounty, ever Merciful to you. Verse 70 We dignified children of Adam, carried them on land and sea, granted good provisions, privileged far above many creatures, showing that carrying on land and sea, ships, is part of dignifying children of Adam, honouring, as Lord steers ships for you to seek bounty, as mercy, ever Merciful, as He dignified, carried, granted provisions, privileged. The connection is ships through sea to seek bounty is part of dignifying, carrying, provisioning, privileging children of Adam, as mercy, ever Merciful.

**Link To Seeking Bounty And Bounty Never Withheld And Provision**

Verse 12 made day bright so you may seek bounty of Lord and know number years and calculation, explained everything in detail. Verse 20 bounty never withheld, We provide both former and latter from bounty. Verse 30 Lord gives abundant or limited provisions, All-Aware All-Seeing. Verse 66 Lord steers ships for you through sea so you may seek bounty, ever Merciful, showing that seeking bounty via ships through sea is from bounty, provision, mercy, ever Merciful, as Lord provides, gives abundant or limited, bounty never withheld, some favoured over others, but Hereafter far greater, so seek bounty as means, not sole goal.

**Linguistic Note On Yuzji And Fulk And Bahr And Tabtaghu And Fadl And Rahiman**

Rabb from r-b-b, Lord. Alladhī yuzjī from z-j-y, to drive, steer, push gently, from azjā. Lakum, for you. Fulk from f-l-k, ships, ship, from fulk. Baḥr from b-ḥ-r, sea, from baḥr. Tabtaghū from b-gh-y, to seek, desire, from ibtaghā. Faḍl from f-ḍ-l, bounty, favour, grace, from faḍl. Raḥīm from r-ḥ-m, Merciful, ever Merciful, from raḥima. The verse's structure — your Lord is Who drives for you ships in sea so you may seek from His bounty, indeed He is with you ever Merciful — with Lord drives ships for you in sea to seek bounty and He is ever Merciful to you, shows that ships through sea to seek bounty is from Lord, mercy, ever Merciful, as He dignified children of Adam, carried on land and sea, granted provisions, privileged.

**How This Verse Teaches Lord Steers Ships To Seek Bounty And Mercy**

This verse teaches that Lord steers ships for you through sea so you may seek His bounty, provision, via trade, travel, etc., and He is ever Merciful to you, as mercy, as He provides, gives abundant or limited, bounty never withheld, some favoured over others, Hereafter far greater, etc., and as He dignified children of Adam, carried them on land and sea, granted good provisions, privileged far above many creatures, as part of dignifying, carrying, provisioning, privileging, as mercy, ever Merciful. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should know Lord steers ships for you through sea to seek bounty, as mercy, ever Merciful, should seek bounty via sea, trade, travel, with gratitude, not ungrateful like humankind ever ungrateful when hardship at sea forget gods except Him but when delivered to shore turn away, as next verse says, etc., should be grateful, not ungrateful, seek bounty with gratitude, as most upright guidance, wisdom, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving.

**A Reflection On Ever Merciful And Ships And Bounty**

Raḥīman — ever Merciful, Merciful — after Lord steers ships for you through sea so you may seek bounty, indicates mercy in steering ships, seeking bounty, as sea dangerous, but Lord steers ships, makes sea subservient, so you may seek bounty, provision, trade, travel, as mercy, ever Merciful to you, humans, as He is ever Merciful, not hastening punishment, Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, but torment fearsome, and ships through sea to seek bounty is mercy, as He dignified children of Adam, carried on land and sea, granted good provisions, privileged far above many creatures, as part of dignifying, honouring, as mercy, ever Merciful, so be grateful, seek bounty with gratitude, not ungrateful, as humankind ever ungrateful when hardship at sea forget gods except Him but when delivered turn away, as next verse says, etc., and be faithful servant, no authority for Satan, Lord Guardian sufficient, say best, follow most upright, wisdom, leading to mighty reward.
