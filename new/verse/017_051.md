## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:51

> **or whatever you think is harder to bring to life!” Then they will ask ˹you˺, “Who will bring us back ˹to life˺?” Say, “The One Who created you the first time.” They will then shake their heads at you and ask, “When will that be?” Say, “Perhaps it is soon!”**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Or Whatever You Think Harder To Bring To Life**

Aw khalqan mimmā yakburu fī ṣudūrikum — or creation from what is greater in your chests, whatever you think harder to bring to life. Aw, or, or even. Khalqan from kh-l-q, creation, creature, from khalaqa, to create. Mimmā, from what, whatever. Yakburu from k-b-r, to be greater, harder, bigger, from kabura, to be great, as akbar greater, kabīran great. Fī ṣudūrikum, in your chests, in your breasts, in your minds, hearts, from ṣ-d-r, chest, breast, as ṣadr, chest. Al-Ṭabarī explains that or whatever you think is harder to bring to life, greater in your chests, harder to recreate, like heavens, earth, mountains, etc., whatever you think greater, harder than stones, iron, bones, ashes, you will be raised, as Allah Who created first time can recreate whatever, even greater, harder. Al-Qurṭubī says mimmā yakburu fī ṣudūrikum means whatever is greater in your chests, harder in your minds, to bring to life, to recreate, like heavens, earth, mountains, etc., even if you become that, you will be raised.

**Who Will Bring Us Back, One Who Created First Time**

Fa-sa-yaqūlūna man yuʿīdunā qul alladhī faṭarakum awwala marratin — then they will say who will bring us back, return us, say One Who created you first time, originated you first time. Fa-sa-yaqūlūna from q-w-l, then they will say, future, they will ask. Man yuʿīdunā from ʿ-w-d, to bring back, return, repeat, from aʿāda, to bring back, return, as yuʿīdunā will bring us back, return us to life. Qul, say, O Prophet, answer. Alladhī faṭarakum, One Who created you, originated you, from f-ṭ-r, to create, originate, split, from faṭara, to create, originate, as faṭara, Creator, Originator, as faṭara heavens and earth. Awwala marratin, first time, first occurrence, from a-w-l, first, and m-r-r, time, occurrence, from marrah, time, occurrence. Al-Ṭabarī explains that then they will ask, who will bring us back to life, return us, after we become bones, ashes, stones, iron, whatever harder? Say, O Prophet, One Who created you first time, originated you first time, will bring you back, return you, as He created first time from nothing, can recreate, bring back, as He is All-Powerful, All-Aware All-Seeing, as He created heavens and earth, seven heavens, earth, all things.

**They Shake Heads And Ask When Will That Be, Perhaps Soon**

Fa-sa-yun-ghiḍūna ilayka ru'ūsahum wa-yaqūlūna matā huwa qul ʿasā an yakūna qarīban — then they will shake, nod to you their heads and say when is it, when will that be, say perhaps it is near, soon. Fa-sa-yun-ghiḍūna from gh-ḍ-ḍ, to shake, nod, move, from anghaḍa, to shake, nod, move, as yun-ghiḍūna shake, nod. Ilayka, to you, towards you. Ru'ūsahum from r-ʾ-s, heads, plural of ra's, head. Wa-yaqūlūna, and they say. Matā huwa, when is it, when will that be, resurrection, bringing back, from m-t-y, when. Qul, say. ʿAsā an yakūna qarīban, perhaps it is near, soon, from ʿ-s-y, perhaps, maybe, and k-w-n, to be, and q-r-b, near, close, soon, from qarīb, near, soon. Al-Ṭabarī explains that they will shake, nod their heads at you, in mockery, denial, disbelief, and ask when will that be, when will resurrection, bringing back be? Say, O Prophet, perhaps it is soon, near, close, not far, as Day He will call you respond praising Him thinking remained only little while, as verse 52 says, and Hour near, command at hand, etc.

**Why Whatever Harder And First Creation And Perhaps Soon**

Why whatever harder, first creation, perhaps soon? Because disbelievers said bones and ashes new creation impossible, mocking, denying resurrection, as they have no sure knowledge, barrier, veils, deafness, aversion, far astray cannot find Right Way, so answer: even if stones, iron, whatever harder you think greater in chests, you will be raised, One Who created first time will bring back, perhaps soon, near, not far, as Day call, etc., so believe, prepare, not mock. The argument: first creation from nothing is harder than recreation from bones, ashes, stones, iron, whatever harder, so if Allah created first time from nothing, He can recreate, bring back, as He is All-Powerful.

**Connection To Bones And Ashes And Stones Iron And Day Call**

Verse 49 bones and ashes new creation mocking denial, verse 50 stones or iron, verse 51 whatever harder, who will bring back, One Who created first time, shake heads when will that be, perhaps soon, verse 52 Day He will call you respond praising Him thinking remained only little while. The connection is sequence of resurrection argument: bones and ashes denial, even stones, iron, whatever harder, you will be raised, One Who created first time will bring back, perhaps soon, Day call, etc., showing resurrection real, near, soon, not far, so believe, prepare.

**Linguistic Note On Yakburu Fi Sudurikum And Yu'iduna And Fatara And Yun-ghiduna And Qariban**

Khalq from kh-l-q, creation. Mimmā, from what, whatever. Yakburu from k-b-r, to be greater, harder, bigger, from kabura. Ṣudūr from ṣ-d-r, chests, breasts, plural of ṣadr. Yuʿīdunā from ʿ-w-d, to bring back, return, from aʿāda. Faṭara from f-ṭ-r, to create, originate, from faṭara. Awwal from a-w-l, first. Marrah from m-r-r, time, occurrence. Yun-ghiḍūna from gh-ḍ-ḍ, to shake, nod, from anghaḍa. Ru'ūs from r-ʾ-s, heads. Matā from m-t-y, when. ʿAsā from ʿ-s-y, perhaps, maybe. Qarīb from q-r-b, near, soon, close. The verse's structure — or creation from what is greater in your chests, then they will say who will bring us back, say One Who created you first time, then they will shake heads and say when is it, say perhaps it is near — with hypothetical whatever harder, question who will bring back, answer One Who created first time, shaking heads mocking, question when, answer perhaps soon, shows resurrection argument, first creation proof, nearness, soon, Day call.

**How This Verse Teaches Resurrection Via First Creation And Nearness**

This verse teaches resurrection via first creation and nearness: or whatever you think harder to bring to life, whatever greater in your chests, you will be raised, One Who created you first time will bring you back, return you, as He created first time from nothing, can recreate, bring back, perhaps it is soon, near, close, not far, as Day He will call you respond praising Him thinking remained only little while. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should believe resurrection, that One Who created first time will bring back, even if become bones, ashes, stones, iron, whatever harder, greater in chests, as first creation harder than recreation, and that resurrection perhaps soon, near, close, Day call, etc., so prepare, believe in Hereafter, not mock, not deny, as disbelievers do, who have barrier, veils, deafness, aversion, far astray cannot find Right Way, painful punishment prepared, hidden barrier.

**A Reflection On First Creation And Perhaps Soon**

Alladhī faṭarakum awwala marratin — One Who created you first time, originated you first time — is proof of resurrection: if He created first time from nothing, no bones, no ashes, no stones, no iron, from nothing, He can recreate, bring back, return, from bones, ashes, stones, iron, whatever harder, as recreation easier than first creation, as Qur'an says He is One Who begins creation then repeats it and it is easier for Him. ʿAsā an yakūna qarīban — perhaps it is soon, near — indicates resurrection, Hour, near, soon, not far, as command at hand, Hour near, Day He will call you soon, thinking remained only little while, as verse 52 says, so prepare, believe, follow most upright guidance, wisdom, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, while disbelievers mock, shake heads, ask when, but perhaps soon, Day call, etc.
