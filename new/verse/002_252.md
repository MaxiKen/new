## Sūrah al-Baqarah 2:252

> These are Allah’s revelations which We recite to you ˹O Prophet˺ in truth. And you are truly one of the messengers.

**Expanded Commentary**

**The Statement About the Verses**

The verse states that these are the verses of God which are recited in truth and that the recipient is among the messengers, and the sentence closes the passage that has treated the community of the earlier prophet. The Book has set out the account and now states what the material is.

The wording identifies the narrative as the revelation that is recited with truth, and the community's teachers read the sentence as the statement that the accounts of the sūrah are given with a purpose. Readers are shown that the passage's history belongs to the guidance that the community has received, and the sentence supplies the statement about its standing.

**The Phrase Recited in Truth**

The commentators explained the phrase as the statement that the account has been given without distortion, and they noted the contrast with the manner in which the people of the Book had handled their own traditions. The community's scholars read the clause as the statement about the reliability of what the sūrah presents.

The sentence states that the recipient is among the messengers, and the community's scholars read the clause as the confirmation of the standing that the passage's earlier addresses had assumed. The Book's practice of addressing its messenger directly appears at several points in the sūrah.

**The Place in the Sūrah**

The sentence stands at the end of the passage about Talut and David and before the verses that treat the messengers and the community's spending, and the community's teachers read it as a transition. The narratives of the earlier communities were read by the community's scholars as material that supports the mission rather than as an independent history.
