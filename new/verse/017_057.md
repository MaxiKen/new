## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:57

> **˹Even˺ the closest ˹to Allah˺ of those invoked would be seeking a way to their Lord, hoping for His mercy, and fearing His punishment. Indeed, your Lord’s torment is fearsome.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Closest Of Those Invoked Seek Way To Lord**

Ulā'ika al-ladhīna yadʿūna yabtaghūna ilā rabbihim al-wasīlata ayyuhum aqrabu — those whom they invoke seek to their Lord means, way, which of them is closest. Ulā'ika, those, those invoked, those claimed besides Him, like angels, prophets, etc., closest to Allah. Al-ladhīna yadʿūna from d-ʿ-w, those whom they invoke, call, as idʿū invoke those you claimed. Yabtaghūna from b-gh-y, to seek, desire, as ibtaghā seeking bounty, seeking way, as previous ibtaghaw sought way to Lord of Throne, and yabtaghūna seek means to Lord. Ilā rabbihim, to their Lord, to their Lord, from r-b-b. Al-wasīlata from w-s-l, means, way, means of nearness, from wasīlah, means, way, means to approach, nearness. Ayyuhum aqrabu, which of them is closest, nearer, from q-r-b, close, near, as aqrab closest. Al-Ṭabarī explains that even closest to Allah of those invoked, those whom polytheists invoke besides Allah, like angels, prophets, etc., would be seeking a way, means, to their Lord, which of them is closest, competing to be closest to Lord, seeking nearness, as they themselves are servants seeking Lord, not gods. Al-Qurṭubī says ulā'ika al-ladhīna yadʿūna yabtaghūna ilā rabbihim al-wasīlata ayyuhum aqrabu means those invoked themselves seek means to their Lord, which is closest, as they are servants, not gods, seeking nearness to Lord, competing.

**Hoping For Mercy And Fearing Punishment**

Wa-yarjūna raḥmatahu wa-yakhāfūna ʿadhābahu — and they hope His mercy and fear His punishment. Wa-yarjūna from r-j-w, to hope, expect, as tarjūhā you hope, hoping for Lord's bounty, mercy. Raḥmatahu from r-ḥ-m, His mercy, from raḥmah, mercy. Wa-yakhāfūna from kh-w-f, to fear, dread, from khāfa, to fear. ʿAdhābahu from ʿ-dh-b, His punishment, torment, from ʿadhāb, punishment. Al-Ṭabarī explains that those invoked, even closest, hope for His mercy, expect mercy, and fear His punishment, dread punishment, as they are servants, not gods, fearing, hoping, as all servants hope mercy, fear punishment, as Lord knows best, may have mercy if wills or punish if wills, as will, wisdom. Al-Rāzī says yarjūna raḥmatahu wa-yakhāfūna ʿadhābahu indicates they themselves are in need of mercy, fearing punishment, so how can they be gods, divine, invoked besides Allah? They themselves need Lord.

**Lord's Torment Fearsome**

Inna ʿadhāba rabbika kāna maḥdhūran — indeed punishment of your Lord is feared, fearsome, to be feared, guarded against. Inna, indeed, surely. ʿAdhāba from ʿ-dh-b, punishment, torment, as ʿadhāban alīman painful punishment, and ʿadhābahu His punishment. Rabbika, your Lord, from r-b-b. Kāna, is, was, indicates it is. Maḥdhūran from ḥ-dh-r, feared, to be feared, guarded against, from ḥadhira, to fear, beware, from maḥdhūr, feared, to be feared, guarded against, as fearsome, to be feared. Al-Ṭabarī explains that indeed punishment, torment of your Lord is fearsome, to be feared, guarded against, feared, as it is severe, painful, as Hell, painful punishment prepared for those who do not believe in Hereafter, as torment fearsome. Al-Qurṭubī says maḥdhūran means feared, to be feared, guarded against, as Lord's torment fearsome, severe, should be feared.

**Why Closest Invoked Seek Way And Hope Mercy Fear Punishment**

Why closest of those invoked seek way to Lord, hope mercy, fear punishment, torment fearsome? Because those invoked, like angels, prophets, etc., even closest to Allah, are servants, not gods, not divine, themselves need Lord, seek means, way, to be closest, hope mercy, fear punishment, as all servants, as they themselves glorify Him, seven heavens, earth, all those in them glorify Him, etc., and not a thing except glorifies with praise, but you cannot comprehend, He is Forbearing Forgiving, but torment fearsome, so they seek mercy, fear punishment, as servants.

**Connection To Invoke Those Claimed Besides Him No Power**

Verse 56 said invoke those you claim besides Him they have no power to remove harm or transfer. Verse 57 says even closest of those invoked would be seeking way to their Lord, hoping mercy, fearing punishment, indeed Lord's torment fearsome, showing that those invoked themselves are servants seeking Lord, not gods, no power, need mercy, fear punishment, so how can they be invoked as gods? They themselves seek Lord, hope mercy, fear punishment, torment fearsome, so invoke Lord alone, not them.

**Link To Lord Knows Best Those In Heavens Earth And Favoured Prophets**

Verse 55 Lord knows best those in heavens and earth, favoured some prophets above others, to David gave Psalms. Verse 57 closest of those invoked seek way to Lord, hoping mercy, fearing punishment, torment fearsome, showing that those in heavens and earth, including closest, like angels, prophets, etc., seek Lord, know best, favoured, but still servants, not gods, need mercy, fear punishment, as Lord knows best those in heavens and earth, favoured some prophets above others, but all servants.

**Linguistic Note On Yabtaghuna And Wasilah And Yarjuna And Yakhaafuna And Mahdhuran**

Ulā'ika, those. Ladhīna yadʿūna, those whom they invoke, from d-ʿ-w. Yabtaghūna from b-gh-y, to seek, desire, from ibtaghā. Rabb from r-b-b, Lord. Wasīlah from w-s-l, means, way, means of nearness, from wasala, to be near. Ayyuhum aqrabu, which of them is closest, from q-r-b, close. Yarjūna from r-j-w, to hope, expect. Raḥmah from r-ḥ-m, mercy. Yakhāfūna from kh-w-f, to fear. ʿAdhāb from ʿ-dh-b, punishment. Maḥdhūr from ḥ-dh-r, feared, to be feared, from ḥadhira. The verse's structure — those whom they invoke seek to their Lord means which is closest and hope His mercy and fear His punishment, indeed punishment of your Lord is fearsome — with seeking means, hoping mercy, fearing punishment, and torment fearsome, shows that those invoked themselves are servants seeking Lord, not gods, need mercy, fear punishment, torment fearsome, so invoke Lord alone.

**How This Verse Teaches Those Invoked Are Servants Not Gods**

This verse teaches that even closest to Allah of those invoked, those claimed besides Him, like angels, prophets, etc., would be seeking way, means, to their Lord, which of them is closest, competing to be closest, hoping for His mercy, expecting mercy, and fearing His punishment, dreading punishment, indeed Lord's torment is fearsome, to be feared, guarded against, so they themselves are servants, not gods, not divine, need Lord, seek nearness, hope mercy, fear punishment, so how can they be invoked as gods besides Allah? They themselves seek Lord. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should know those invoked besides Allah, even closest, like angels, prophets, are servants seeking Lord, hoping mercy, fearing punishment, torment fearsome, so invoke Allah alone, not them, as they have no power to remove harm or transfer, no power, as previous verse, and they themselves seek Lord.

**A Reflection On Seeking Means And Hoping Mercy Fearing Punishment**

Yabtaghūna ilā rabbihim al-wasīlata ayyuhum aqrabu — seek to their Lord means which is closest — indicates competition among closest invoked to be closest to Lord, seeking means, nearness, as servants, not gods, as all servants seek nearness to Lord, compete, as believers should compete for good, for ranks far greater in Hereafter, as Hereafter far greater in rank and favour. Wa-yarjūna raḥmatahu wa-yakhāfūna ʿadhābahu — hope His mercy and fear His punishment — indicates balance of hope and fear, as servants hope mercy, fear punishment, as Lord may have mercy if wills or punish if wills, as will, wisdom, knowledge, as Lord knows best, All-Aware All-Seeing, Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, but torment fearsome, maḥdhūran, to be feared, guarded against, so hope mercy, fear punishment, as servants, not gods, so invoke Lord alone, who has mercy, punishment, torment fearsome, but also Forbearing Forgiving.
