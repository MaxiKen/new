## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:68

> **Do you feel secure that He will not cause the land to swallow you up, or unleash upon you a storm of stones? Then you will find none to protect you.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Do You Feel Secure Land Swallow You Or Storm Of Stones**

A-fa-amintum an yakhsifa bikum jāniba al-barri aw yursila ʿalaykum ḥāṣiban — do you feel secure that He will not cause land to swallow you, side of land, or send upon you storm of stones? A-fa-amintum from a-m-n, to feel secure, safe, from amina, to feel secure, safe, interrogative, do you feel secure, safe, as fa-amintum do you feel secure, as rhetorical, warning, do you feel secure? An yakhsifa from kh-s-f, to cause to swallow, to make sink, to cause land to swallow, from khasafa, to cause to swallow, sink, as yakhsifa cause to swallow, make sink, as earth swallows. Bikum, with you, you, you. Jāniba al-barri, side of land, side of shore, part of land, from j-n-b, side, and b-r-r, land, shore, as side of land, as land, shore, as after being delivered to shore, land, you turn away, ungrateful, do you feel secure that land will not swallow you? Aw yursila ʿalaykum ḥāṣiban, or send upon you storm of stones, from r-s-l, to send, and ḥ-ṣ-b, storm of stones, pebbles, from ḥāṣib, storm of stones, that throws stones, pebbles, as storm of stones, as punishment, as Thamud, etc., as ḥāṣib storm of stones. Al-Ṭabarī explains that do you feel secure, safe, that He will not cause land, side of land, shore, to swallow you up, sink you, after He delivered you to shore, you turned away, ungrateful, do you feel secure that land will not swallow you, as Qarun swallowed, etc., or unleash upon you storm of stones, ḥāṣib, storm that throws stones, pebbles, as punishment, as people of Lut, etc., as warning, as He is ever Merciful but also torment fearsome, as Lord's torment fearsome, maḥdhūran, to be feared, as every wicked society destroyed or punished severely before Day written in Record.

**Then You Will Find None To Protect You**

Thumma lā tajidū lakum wakīlan — then you will find not for you guardian, protector, keeper. Thumma, then, after land swallow or storm stones. Lā tajidū from w-j-d, to find, you will not find. Lakum, for you, for yourselves. Wakīlan from w-k-l, guardian, protector, keeper, trustee, as wakīl, keeper, guardian, as verse 2 Trustee of Affairs, verse 54 not sent you as keeper over them, verse 65 sufficient Lord as Guardian, wakīlan, and verse 68 no guardian, protector, keeper, to protect you from land swallow or storm stones, as only Allah Guardian sufficient, but if He punishes, no guardian to protect, as only He has power to remove harm, transfer, etc., as those claimed besides Him have no power, as verse 56 says, so if He causes land to swallow or sends storm stones, you will find no guardian, protector, to protect you.

**Why Land Swallow And Storm Stones Warning After Turning Away Ungrateful**

Why land swallow and storm stones warning after turning away, ungrateful, when delivered to shore? Because when hardship at sea you forget all gods except Him, invoke only Him, but when delivered to shore, land, you turn away, ungrateful, ever ungrateful, as humankind ever ungrateful, kafūran, as Devil ever ungrateful, kafūran, so warning: do you feel secure, safe, that He will not cause land, side of land, shore, to swallow you up, sink you, after delivering you, or unleash upon you storm of stones, ḥāṣib, storm throwing stones, as punishment, as every wicked society destroyed or punished severely before Day written in Record, as many generations destroyed after Noah, etc., as Qarun swallowed, people of Lut storm stones, etc., as examples, and then you will find no guardian, protector, keeper, to protect you, as only Allah Guardian sufficient, but if He punishes, no guardian, as only He has power.

**Connection To Lord Steers Ships Ever Merciful And Forget Gods At Sea Turn Away At Shore Ever Ungrateful**

Verse 66 Lord steers ships for you through sea so you may seek bounty, ever Merciful. Verse 67 when hardship at sea you forget all gods except Him, when delivers to shore you turn away, humankind ever ungrateful. Verse 68 do you feel secure that He will not cause land to swallow you or unleash storm of stones, then you will find none to protect you, showing that after being delivered to shore, turning away, ungrateful, do you feel secure that land will not swallow you, as Qarun, or storm stones, as Lut, etc., as warning, as He is ever Merciful but also torment fearsome, and no guardian to protect if He punishes, as only He Guardian sufficient, but if He punishes, no guardian.

**Link To Or Send Back To Sea Violent Storm Drowning For Denial No Avenger**

Next verse 69 poses another security question about returning to sea and a fierce gale sinking you for ingratitude, then finding no champion against Us, showing second warning: do you feel safe He will not return you to sea and unleash fierce gale sinking you for denial, as punishment, as sea dangerous, ships steered, but violent gale drowning for denial, as warning, as He is ever Merciful but torment fearsome, and no avenger against Us, as only Allah has power.

**Linguistic Note On Amintum And Yakhsifa And Janib And Barr And Hāsib And Wakilan**

Amintum from a-m-n, to feel secure, safe, from amina. Yakhsifa from kh-s-f, to cause to swallow, sink, from khasafa. Jānib from j-n-b, side, from jānib. Barr from b-r-r, land, shore, from barr. Yursila from r-s-l, to send. Ḥāṣib from ḥ-ṣ-b, storm of stones, from ḥaṣaba, to throw stones, from ḥāṣib, storm throwing stones. Tajidū from w-j-d, to find. Wakīl from w-k-l, guardian, protector, keeper, from wakala. The verse's structure — do you feel secure that He will not cause land to swallow you side of land or send upon you storm of stones, then you will find not for you guardian — with rhetorical question do you feel secure that land will not swallow or storm stones and statement then you will find no guardian, shows warning after turning away ungrateful at shore, do you feel secure that land will not swallow or storm stones, as punishment, as every wicked society destroyed or punished severely before Day written in Record, and no guardian to protect if He punishes.

**How This Verse Teaches Warning After Ungratefulness**

This verse teaches warning after ungratefulness at shore: do you feel secure, safe, that He will not cause land, side of land, shore, to swallow you up, sink you, after delivering you to shore, you turned away, ungrateful, ever ungrateful, or unleash upon you storm of stones, ḥāṣib, storm throwing stones, pebbles, as punishment, as Qarun swallowed, people of Lut storm stones, etc., as every wicked society destroyed or punished severely before Day written in Record, many generations destroyed after Noah, etc., then you will find none to protect you, no guardian, protector, keeper, to protect you from land swallow or storm stones, as only Allah Guardian sufficient, but if He punishes, no guardian, as only He has power to remove harm, transfer, etc., as those claimed besides Him have no power, as verse 56 says, so be grateful, not ungrateful, not turn away after being delivered, as humankind ever ungrateful, kafūran, as Devil ever ungrateful, kafūran, follow most upright guidance, wisdom, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, avoiding destruction, severe punishment, painful punishment, Hell, torment fearsome.

**A Reflection On No Guardian To Protect**

Lā tajidū lakum wakīlan — you will find not for you guardian, protector, keeper — indicates that if Allah causes land to swallow you, side of land, or sends upon you storm of stones, ḥāṣib, as punishment after turning away ungrateful at shore, you will find no guardian, protector, keeper, to protect you, save you, as only Allah Guardian sufficient, wakīlan, sufficient Lord as Guardian, verse 65, but if He punishes, no guardian to protect, as only He has power, as those claimed besides Him have no power to remove harm or transfer, as verse 56 says, no power, so invoke Him alone, be grateful, not ungrateful, not turn away, as humankind ever ungrateful, kafūran, follow most upright, wisdom, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, avoiding land swallow, storm stones, no guardian, destruction, severe punishment.
