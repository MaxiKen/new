## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:80

> Whoever obeys the Messenger has truly obeyed Allah. But whoever turns away, then ˹know that˺ We have not sent you ˹O Prophet˺ as a keeper over them.

### Expanded Commentary

**Obeying the Messenger is obeying Allah**

The verse makes Prophetic obedience a theological consequence of revelation. Muhammad is not an independent source competing with Allah; he conveys and embodies what Allah authorizes. To obey him in his revealed guidance is therefore to obey the One who sent him.

**Turning away and the limits of Prophetic office**

The Messenger is not sent as a keeper over people. He conveys clearly but does not control hearts or force faith. This distinction protects the Prophet from both accusations of failure when people reject him and from later claims that religious leadership means domination of consciences. Accountability belongs to those who hear and choose.

For Muslims, the verse demands careful study of authentic Sunnah rather than reducing obedience to slogans. A report must be established, understood in context, and reconciled with the Qur’an and the body of Prophetic teaching. Following the Messenger includes his justice, mercy, humility, and protection of rights, not only isolated commands quoted without his example.

**Prophetic example interprets command**

Obedience to the Messenger includes learning how he applied revelation: with justice, mercy, consultation, and protection of the weak. An isolated quotation that produces cruelty cannot represent the whole Prophetic guidance. The messenger’s authority is understood through the messenger’s character as well as his words.
