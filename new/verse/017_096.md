Kafā from k-f-y, to be sufficient, enough, from kafā. Allāh, Allah. Shahīd from sh-h-d, witness, from shahida, to witness. Bayn from b-y-n, between, from bayna. ʿIbād from ʿ-b-d, servants, slaves, plural of ʿabd. Khabīr from kh-b-r, All-Knowing, Fully Aware, from khabura. Baṣīr from b-ṣ-r, All-Seeing, from baṣura.
