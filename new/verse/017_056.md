## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:56

> **Say, ˹O Prophet,˺ “Invoke those you claim ˹to be divine˺ besides Him—they do not have the power to undo harm from you or transfer it ˹to someone else˺.”**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Invoke Those You Claim Besides Him**

Qul idʿū al-ladhīna zaʿamtum min dūnihi — say, call, invoke those you claimed besides Him, from besides Him. Qul from q-w-l, to say, command to Prophet. Idʿū from d-ʿ-w, to call, invoke, pray, supplicate, as yadʿu human prays, calls, as yadʿūkum He will call you. Al-ladhīna zaʿamtum, those you claimed, asserted, alleged, from z-ʿ-m, to claim, assert, allege, from zaʿama, to claim. Min dūnihi, from besides Him, besides Allah, other than Him, from d-w-n, besides, other than, besides Him. Al-Ṭabarī explains that say, O Prophet, invoke, call those you claim to be divine besides Him, besides Allah, those you claim are gods, like idols, angels as daughters, etc., invoke them, call them, to see if they can help. Al-Qurṭubī says idʿū al-ladhīna zaʿamtum min dūnihi means invoke those you claim besides Him, besides Allah, to be divine, gods, to see if they have power.

**They Have No Power To Undo Harm Or Transfer It**

Fa-lā yamlikūna kashfa al-ḍurri ʿankum wa-lā taḥwīlan — then they do not possess removal of harm from you nor transfer. Fa-lā, then not, so not. Yamlikūna from m-l-k, to possess, own, have power, control, from malaka, to possess, own, have power, as mālik owner. Kashfa from k-sh-f, removal, uncovering, removing, from kashafa, to remove, uncover, as kashf, removal. Al-ḍurri from ḍ-r-r, harm, adversity, affliction, from ḍarra, to harm, from ḍurr, harm. ʿAnkum, from you, away from you. Wa-lā taḥwīlan from ḥ-w-l, transfer, transformation, changing, from ḥawwala, to transfer, transform, change, from taḥwīl, transfer, transformation. Al-Ṭabarī explains that they, those claimed besides Him, do not have power, do not possess ability to remove harm, adversity, affliction from you, nor to transfer it to someone else, change it, transform it, meaning they have no power to help, no power to remove harm nor transfer, so why invoke them, claim them divine? Al-Rāzī says lā yamlikūna kashfa al-ḍurri ʿankum wa-lā taḥwīlan indicates they have no power, no ownership, no control over harm removal or transfer, so they are not divine, not gods, only Allah has power, as He provides, gives abundant or limited, All-Aware All-Seeing, removes harm, etc.

**Why Invoke Those Claimed Besides Him Have No Power**

Why invoke those claimed besides Him have no power? Because polytheists claimed idols, angels as daughters, other gods besides Allah have power to intercede, remove harm, transfer, etc., but they have no power, no ownership, no control, as only Allah has power, as Lord knows best those in heavens and earth, favoured some prophets above others, to David gave Psalms, etc., and seven heavens, earth, all those in them glorify Him, not a thing except glorifies with praise, etc., and Allah Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, etc., so only Allah has power to remove harm, transfer, etc., not those claimed besides Him. The argument refutes other gods besides Allah, as Other Gods Besides Allah? heading indicates.

**Connection To Other Gods Argument And Glorified Exalted And Universal Glorification**

Verses 42-44 had there been other gods they would have sought way to Lord of Throne, Glorified Highly Exalted above what they claim, seven heavens, earth, all those in them glorify Him, not a thing except glorifies with praise, but you cannot comprehend, He is Forbearing Forgiving. Verse 56 says invoke those you claim besides Him they have no power to remove harm or transfer, showing they have no power, not divine, only Allah glorified, exalted, glorified by all creation, has power, so invoke Him alone, not other gods.

**Link To Closest Of Those Invoked Seek Way To Lord Hoping Mercy Fearing Punishment**

Next verse 57 says even closest of those invoked would be seeking way to their Lord, hoping for mercy, fearing punishment, indeed Lord's torment fearsome, indicating that even those invoked, closest to Allah, like angels, prophets, etc., themselves seek way to Lord, hope mercy, fear punishment, so they are not divine, not gods, but servants seeking Lord, so how can they be invoked as gods? They themselves seek Lord.

**Linguistic Note On Id'u And Za'amtum And Yamlikuna And Kashf And Tahwil**

Qul from q-w-l, to say. Idʿū from d-ʿ-w, to call, invoke. Ladhīna zaʿamtum, those you claimed, from z-ʿ-m, to claim. Min dūnihi, besides Him, from d-w-n. Yamlikūna from m-l-k, to possess, own, have power. Kashf from k-sh-f, removal, uncovering, from kashafa. Ḍurr from ḍ-r-r, harm, adversity. ʿAnkum, from you. Taḥwīl from ḥ-w-l, transfer, transformation, from ḥawwala. The verse's structure — say invoke those you claimed besides Him, then they do not possess removal of harm from you nor transfer — with command to invoke those claimed besides Him and statement they have no power to remove harm or transfer, shows refutation of other gods besides Allah, as they have no power, no ownership, no control, so not divine, only Allah has power.

**How This Verse Teaches Other Gods Have No Power**

This verse teaches that other gods claimed besides Allah have no power to remove harm from you or transfer it to someone else, no ownership, no control, so they are not divine, not gods, only Allah has power, as He provides, gives abundant or limited, All-Aware All-Seeing, removes harm, etc., and seven heavens, earth, all those in them glorify Him, etc., He is Forbearing Forgiving, Lord knows best those in heavens and earth, etc. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should not invoke those claimed besides Him, besides Allah, as they have no power to remove harm or transfer, no ownership, no control, so invoke Allah alone, who has power, who provides, removes harm, transfers, etc., as most upright, monotheism, wisdom, part of wisdom revealed, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving. The practical lesson is to invoke Allah alone, not other gods claimed besides Him, as they have no power, no ownership, to remove harm or transfer, so trust Allah alone, who is Lord, knows best, All-Aware All-Seeing, Most Forbearing All-Forgiving.

**A Reflection On No Power To Remove Harm Or Transfer**

Lā yamlikūna kashfa al-ḍurri ʿankum wa-lā taḥwīlan — they do not possess removal of harm from you nor transfer — indicates complete lack of power, ownership, control: they cannot remove harm, adversity, affliction, from you, nor transfer it to someone else, change it, transform it, so they are powerless, not divine, not gods, only Allah has power, ownership, control, to remove harm, transfer, etc., as He gives abundant or limited provisions, All-Aware All-Seeing, bounty never withheld, etc. The believer who reflects will invoke Allah alone, who has power to remove harm and transfer, not those claimed besides Him who have no power, no ownership, as they are powerless, not divine, as even closest of those invoked seek way to Lord, hope mercy, fear punishment, Lord's torment fearsome, as next verse says, so they themselves are servants, not gods.
