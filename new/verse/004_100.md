## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:100

> Whoever emigrates in the cause of Allah will find many safe havens and bountiful resources throughout the earth. Those who leave their homes and die while emigrating to Allah and His Messenger—their reward has already been secured with Allah. And Allah is All-Forgiving, Most Merciful.

### Expanded Commentary

**Migration opens unexpected provision**

Whoever emigrates for Allah will find many safe places and abundant resources. The promise addresses the fear that leaving familiar property, family, and employment means total ruin. Provision can appear in new lands through safety, work, friendship, and the freedom to worship. It may not appear immediately or in the form expected, but the person who leaves oppression for God is not abandoned.

**The secured reward**

If death overtakes a person during the migration, the reward is already secured with Allah. The value of the act lies in its direction and sincere beginning, not only in reaching the destination. This principle encourages action without pretending that every journey ends comfortably.

Migration can also be inward: leaving a sin, a corrupt alliance, or a habit that has become an idol. Yet the verse’s original social meaning must not be erased. Refugees and displaced people require protection, and those who welcome them participate in the mercy promised here. Allah is Forgiving and Merciful to the one who leaves for Him and to the community that makes room.

**Migration changes the meaning of loss**

Leaving property or status can feel like defeat, but the verse promises that safety and provision may appear in places the traveller did not expect. The welcoming community must remember that the migrant is not a charity object but a person who has made a costly act of faith. Hospitality becomes part of the reward’s social form.
