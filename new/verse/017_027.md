## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:27

> **Surely the wasteful are ˹like˺ brothers to the devils. And the Devil is ever ungrateful to his Lord.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Wasteful Are Brothers To Devils**

Inna al-mubadh-dhirīna kānū ikhwāna al-shayāṭīni — indeed wasteful are brothers of devils. Inna, indeed, surely, emphasis. Al-mubadh-dhirīna from b-dh-r, wasteful, squanderers, those who spend wastefully, extravagantly, from badhdhara, to waste, squander, scatter wastefully, intensive form mubadh-dhir, one who wastes much, squanderer. Kānū, are, were, indicates they are, were, state. Ikhwāna from a-kh-w, brothers, companions, close friends, like brothers, from akh, brother, plural ikhwān, brothers, companions. Al-shayāṭīni from sh-ṭ-n, devils, satans, from shaṭana, to be far, distant, devil far from good, mercy, truth, plural of shayṭān, devil, Satan. Al-Ṭabarī explains that wasteful, those who spend wastefully, squander wealth in disobedience, sin, or extravagance beyond need, without benefit, are like brothers to devils, companions of devils, friends of devils, because devils waste, spend in falsehood, disobey, are ungrateful, and command wasteful spending. Being brothers indicates similarity in character, companionship, following same path, sharing traits. Al-Qurṭubī says ikhwān al-shayāṭīn means they are like brothers to devils, companions, friends, followers, because they obey devils in wasteful spending, as devils command waste, as Satan incites with voice, cavalry, infantry, wealth, children, promises delusion.

**Devil Ever Ungrateful To Lord**

Wa-kāna al-shayṭānu li-rabbihi kafūran — and Devil is to his Lord ever ungrateful, very ungrateful, intensely ungrateful. Wa-kāna, and is, was, indicates state, he is. Al-shayṭānu, Devil, Satan, Iblīs, from sh-ṭ-n, devil. Li-rabbihi, to his Lord, towards his Lord, from r-b-b, Lord, master, sustainer who gave bounty. Kafūran from k-f-r, ungrateful, disbelieving, very ungrateful, intensive, from kafara, to disbelieve, be ungrateful, deny blessings, from same root as kufr, disbelief, ungratefulness, ingratitude, and kāfir, disbeliever, ungrateful, and kafūr, very ungrateful. Al-Ṭabarī says Devil is ever ungrateful to his Lord, very ungrateful, denying blessings, disobeying, refusing to be grateful, as he refused to prostrate to Adam out of arrogance, was ungrateful for honour, for creation, for command. Al-Rāzī says kafūran indicates intensive, ever, very ungrateful, characteristic of Devil, who is ungrateful despite bounty, and wasteful who wastes bounty is also ungrateful, like brother.

**Why Wasteful Brothers To Devils And Ungrateful**

Why brothers? Because both wasteful and devils share characteristics: spending in falsehood, disobedience, sin, extravagance, ungratefulness, leading astray, commanding evil. Wasteful spends bounty never withheld, provision abundant or limited, in disobedience, extravagance, without benefit, which is ungrateful to Lord who gave bounty, who favoured some over others, who gives abundant or limited with awareness. Devil is ever ungrateful to Lord, kafūran, very ungrateful, so wasteful who wastes bounty is like brother to Devil, companion in ungratefulness, sharing trait of ingratitude. The phrase ikhwāna al-shayāṭīn indicates that wasteful are companions, friends, followers of devils, as devils command wasteful spending, incite to waste, as verse 64 will say Satan promises delusion and incites with voice, cavalry, infantry, manipulates wealth and children.

**Connection To Giving Due And Not Spending Wastefully And Moderation**

Verse 26 said give to close relatives their due, poor and needy travellers, and do not spend wastefully, tabdhīr prohibited. Verse 27 says wasteful are like brothers to devils, Devil ever ungrateful to Lord. The connection is that prohibition of wasteful spending is emphasized by stating that wasteful are brothers to devils, companions, and devils ungrateful, so wasteful spending is ungratefulness, characteristic of devils, serious sin, not minor. Giving due to relatives, poor, traveller is grateful use of bounty, not wasteful, gratitude, while wasteful spending is ungrateful, like Devil, ingratitude. The sūrah's theme of gratitude appears: grateful servant Noah, verse 3, gratitude for bounty never withheld, now ungrateful Devil as opposite of grateful servant, showing two paths: grateful vs ungrateful, wasteful brother to devils vs giving due grateful.

**Link To Satan Seeks Discord And No Authority Over Faithful Servants**

Later verses 53 and 65 will say Satan seeks to sow discord among servants, tell believing servants to say only best, Satan sworn enemy, and you will have no authority over My faithful servants, sufficient Lord as Guardian. Wasteful being brothers to devils indicates that wasteful follows Satan who seeks discord, who commands waste, but faithful servants who give due and avoid waste have no authority for Satan over them, Lord is Guardian, sufficient. The verse therefore warns against following Satan via wasteful spending, tabdhīr, which makes one brother to devils, and encourages being faithful servant who gives due, avoids waste, says best, so Satan has no authority.

**Linguistic Note On Mubadhdhir And Ikhwan And Kafur**

Inna from a-n-n, indeed, surely. Mubadh-dhirīn from b-dh-r, wasteful, squanderers, from badhdhara, to waste, squander, intensive form mubadh-dhir, one who wastes much, from tabdhīr, wasteful spending. Kānū from k-w-n, to be. Ikhwān from a-kh-w, brothers, companions, plural of akh, brother, from akhawa, to be brother. Shayāṭīn from sh-ṭ-n, devils, plural of shayṭān, from shaṭana, to be far, distant, far from good. Shayṭān from same root, devil far from mercy. Kafūr from k-f-r, very ungrateful, intensive, from kafara, to be ungrateful, disbelieve, from kufr, ingratitude, disbelief, intensive form kafūr, ever ungrateful. Rabb from r-b-b, Lord. The verse's structure — indeed wasteful are brothers of devils, and Devil is to his Lord ever ungrateful — with statement of brotherhood and reason of ungratefulness, shows that wasteful spending is ungratefulness, characteristic of Devil, serious, so avoid, be grateful.

**How This Verse Teaches Gratitude Vs Waste And Ingratitude**

This verse teaches gratitude vs waste and ingratitude: wasteful are brothers to devils, Devil ever ungrateful to Lord, so wasteful spending is ungratefulness, characteristic of devils, avoid. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should avoid wasteful spending, tabdhīr, which is spending in disobedience, sin, or extravagance beyond need, without benefit, because it makes one like brother to devils, companion, friend of devils, and is ungratefulness to Lord who gave bounty never withheld, who favoured some over others, who gives abundant or limited with awareness, All-Aware All-Seeing. Instead, give due to relatives, poor, traveller, which is grateful use of bounty, gratitude, not waste. The practical lesson is to use bounty with gratitude, not waste, to be moderate, not tight-fisted nor openhanded, as most upright guidance teaches, wisdom, and to remember Devil ever ungrateful, so do not be like him, be grateful like Noah grateful servant.

**A Reflection On Brotherhood To Devils And Ever Ungrateful**

Ikhwāna al-shayāṭīn — brothers to devils — strong phrase indicating close companionship, similarity, following, sharing traits, will be with devils in Hereafter, as companions. Wasteful are like brothers to devils, meaning they follow devils, obey devils, share characteristics of waste, disobedience, ungratefulness, will be with devils. Devil ever ungrateful to Lord, kafūran, intensive, ever, very ungrateful, despite bounty, despite honour, despite command, ungrateful. The believer who reflects will avoid wasteful spending that makes one brother to devils, will be grateful for bounty never withheld, give due to deserving, be moderate, not tight-fisted nor openhanded, give kind word when lack while hoping for bounty, and will seek protection from devils who have no authority over faithful servants, Lord sufficient as Guardian, as most upright guidance.
