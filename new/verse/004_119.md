## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:119

> I will certainly mislead them and delude them with empty hopes. Also, I will order them and they will slit the ears of cattle and alter Allah’s creation.” And whoever takes Satan as a guardian instead of Allah has certainly suffered a tremendous loss.

### Expanded Commentary

**Empty hopes and the alteration of creation**

Satan promises misguidance and fills people with false hopes. He commands practices that mark animals and alters Allah’s creation. Classical exegetes connected the language to pagan customs, while later scholars discussed how it applies to deliberate distortion of the natural order and to harmful manipulation.

The essential point is not that every change to the body or environment is forbidden. Healing, cultivation, clothing, and beneficial technology are part of human stewardship. The warning concerns alteration governed by idolatry, vanity, cruelty, or rejection of the limits God has set. Motive, harm, and purpose matter.

The person who takes Satan as a guardian suffers tremendous loss because he exchanges a real Lord for a deceiver. False hope promises control without responsibility; divine guidance offers responsibility with mercy. The believer tests every cultural command by truth, benefit, and worship rather than by novelty or ancestral habit.

**Creation and responsible change**

The verse does not prohibit beneficial medicine, cultivation, or adaptation. It warns against changes rooted in idolatry, cruelty, vanity, or a desire to reject the Creator’s moral order. Wisdom asks what is being changed, why, who bears the harm, and whether the act expresses stewardship or domination.
