## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:119

> I will certainly mislead them and delude them with empty hopes. Also, I will order them and they will slit the ears of cattle and alter Allah’s creation.” And whoever takes Satan as a guardian instead of Allah has certainly suffered a tremendous loss.

**Expanded Commentary**

**I Will Certainly Mislead and Delude With Empty Hopes**

The vow continues *wa la uḍillannahum wa la umanniyannahum* — I will certainly mislead them and certainly delude them with hopes, with wishing, with *umniyah* — empty promising. *Uḍillannahum* — to lead astray, to cause deviation — was explained as Satan’s first work upon his appointed share (4:118) after curse, the *ḍalāl baʿīd* — far straying — of association (4:116). *Ummaniyahum* — to delude with hopes, to cast wishes, to promise — was read as the mechanism of misguidance: not force but hopes, as the sūrah has shown that Satan promises only delusion (4:120) and that his hopes are empty — *ghurūr* — while God’s promise is true (4:122). The sūrah has shown desires that wish you deviate greatly (4:27), and that the deceitful hide from people but God is with them where they plot by night (4:108); empty hopes delude because they hide consequence. The one who is misled thinks he is given respite as generosity — the sūrah’s *istidrāj* without name but present — while the Book says *“He delays but His plan is flawless”* (68:44 cited in sūrah 7:183). The hopes delude where the emigrant’s true hope is for ranks, forgiveness, mercy (4:96) and many havens (4:100); Satan’s hope is the counterfeit that spending to show off (4:38) or withholding bounty (4:37) is honour preserved.

**I Will Order Them and They Will Slit the Ears of Cattle**

The program *wa la āmurannahum fa la yubattikunna ādhāna al-anʿāmi* — and I will surely order, command them, and they will slit, cut, the ears of cattle — was explained as Satan ordering rites upon livestock. *Āmurannahum* — I will command — was read as Satan issuing command as if lord, in rivalry to God who commands return of trusts (4:58) and to whom the Book’s commands refer disputes (4:59). *Yubattikunna* — to slit, to cut lengthwise, to notch — *ādhāna al-anʿāmi* — ears of cattle — was explained as the Meccan custom of dedicating livestock to idols — *baḥīrah, sāʾibah, waṣīlah, ḥām* (5:103) — by slitting ears as mark, forbidding its use, devoting it to the female gods (4:117). The act was read not as pastoral technique but as worship-mark: to alter the ear is to claim ownership of the animal beyond the Creator’s intact creation. The sūrah has shown that those given a portion believe in *jibt* and *ṭāghūt* (4:51); slitting ears is the ritual of that belief. The verb *la yubattikunna* — they will surely slit — with double emphasis shows obedience: his order is executed.

**And Alter God’s Creation**

The wider command *wa la āmurannahum fa la yughayyirunna khalqa Allāhi* — and I will order them and they will alter, change, God’s creation — was explained as the generalisation of slitting ears: changing what God created intact, wholesome, upon *fiṭrah*. *Khalq Allāh* — God’s creation — was read as the form, the wholeness, the natural disposition, the law inscribed in creation, as the sūrah began from a single soul created and from it its mate (4:1) and later warned not to alter the *fiṭrah* — innate way. Commentators explained altering as physical mutilations dedicating to idols, as tattooing, as castration, as claiming the lawful as forbidden and vice versa, and morally as altering the religion — changing limits set by God (4:13–14) — and altering the self that wrongs itself (4:110). The common element was explained as Satan commanding to claim lordship over creation: to cut what God made whole and to call the cut devotion. Where the sūrah says there is no good in most secret talks except commanding charity, kindness, reconciliation (4:114), this is the evil command — to slit and to alter — whose compliance proves the guardian taken.

**And Whoever Takes Satan as Guardian Instead of God Suffers Tremendous Loss**

The verdict *wa man yattakhidh al-shayṭāna waliyyan min dūni Allāhi fa qad khasira khusrānan mubīnā* — and whoever takes Satan as guardian, protector, ally instead of God has certainly suffered a tremendous, manifest, clear loss — was explained with *walī* — guardian, patron, protector — the same *walī* and *naṣīr* the oppressed cry for from grace (4:75) and that God alone suffices as (4:45). To take Satan as *walī* instead — *min dūni Allāhi* — was read as choosing the cursed (4:118) as guardian whose promise is delusion (4:120). *Khasara khusrānan mubīnā* — suffered tremendous, clear loss — was explained as loss manifest in both worlds: in this world by slitting what was wholesome and altering what was created balanced, in the next by Hell without escape (4:121) versus Gardens forever under true promise (4:122). The sūrah has shown that whoever takes Satan as associate, what an evil associate (4:38), and that the share Satan takes is the share of those who invoke besides Him (4:117–118); to take him as *walī* is to become that share. The loss is *mubīn* — clear — because the alternative — the Book and wisdom taught (4:113), the great reward for charity and reconciliation (4:114), the forgiveness found upon seeking (4:110) — was clear, and the choice of the cutter over the Creator is clear deviation.

**A Verse That Makes Satan’s Program Concrete**

Placed after curse and appointed share (4:118), this verse makes Satan’s program concrete: mislead, delude with hopes, order slitting ears and altering creation. The sūrah that warned not to deviate by following desires (4:27) and not to follow other than believers’ path (4:115) here shows what the other path orders and what its followers execute: cutting creation to mark it for another. The sūrah that began with unity from a single soul (4:1) and that will say to Him belongs whatever is in heavens and earth and He encompasses all (4:126) sees altering creation as assault on that unity. For the weak this is protective: to alter creation is not light craft but tremendous loss, clear, because the Creator who made humanity weak yet wills to lighten (4:28) and who gives many havens (4:100) is replaced by a commander who orders cutting. The next verse will summarise the commander’s economy: he promises and deludes with empty hopes, he promises nothing but delusion (4:120), whose end is Hell without escape (4:121).
