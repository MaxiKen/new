## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:87

> Allah, there is no god ˹worthy of worship˺ except Him. He will certainly gather ˹all of˺ you together on the Day of Judgment—about which there is no doubt. And whose word is more truthful than Allah’s?

### Expanded Commentary

**The certainty of gathering**

Allah alone is worthy of worship, and He will gather every person on the Day about which there is no doubt. Tawḥīd and resurrection are joined: the One who deserves worship is the One before whom every worshipper will stand. No individual disappears into history, and no community escapes the gathering.

**The truest word**

The rhetorical question—whose word is truer than Allah’s?—answers the doubts raised by hypocrites and deniers. Human predictions fail, institutions collapse, and promises are broken; divine promise is not subject to those weaknesses. The certainty of the Day is therefore not a human theory but a declaration from the One whose speech creates and judges.

The verse also places worldly disputes in perspective. A person may win a case through influence or lose it through bias, but the final gathering corrects every incomplete verdict. The wise believer uses this certainty to speak truth and to avoid treating temporary advantage as final success.

**The final gathering dignifies the present**

Because every person will be gathered, no life is disposable and no deed is irrelevant. The belief can motivate care for people whom history overlooks and discourage the powerful from treating temporary victory as a final verdict. Allah’s word makes the moral future certain even when the present is confused.
