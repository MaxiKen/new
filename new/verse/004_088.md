## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:88

> Why are you ˹believers˺ divided into two groups regarding the hypocrites while Allah allowed them to regress ˹to disbelief˺ because of their misdeeds? Do you wish to guide those left by Allah to stray? And whoever Allah leaves to stray, you will never find for them a way.

### Expanded Commentary

**Why believers differ about hypocrites**

The verse challenges the believers who split into two groups regarding the hypocrites. Some wanted to treat them as insiders despite their betrayal; others wanted to respond as though every case were identical. The Qur’an directs attention to their own chosen regression and warns against trying to guide those who persist in turning away.

**Judgment and protection**

The verse does not make ordinary believers judges of hidden hearts. It addresses a visible political-religious crisis in which people claimed Islam while aiding hostile forces. A community may protect itself from demonstrated treachery while leaving final spiritual judgment to Allah. This balance prevents both naïveté and collective paranoia.

The statement that Allah allowed them to regress because of their deeds expresses the relationship between choice and abandonment. Repeated refusal can make a person less receptive to guidance. Yet the verse’s warning is not permission to stop inviting sincere repentance; it is a description of those whose conduct has made their position clear.

**Protect without stereotyping**

A community may respond to demonstrated betrayal while refusing to punish an entire population for the actions of some. Evidence, fair procedure, and an opportunity for repentance are necessary. The verse’s warning about hypocrisy should create vigilance against conduct, not a license for suspicion based on appearance or identity.
