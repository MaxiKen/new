## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:39

> **This is part of the wisdom which your Lord has revealed to you ˹O Prophet˺. And do not set up any other god with Allah ˹O humanity˺, or you will be cast into Hell, blameworthy, rejected.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**This Is Part Of Wisdom Revealed To You**

Dhālika mimmā awḥā ilayka rabbuka mina al-ḥikmati — that is from what your Lord revealed to you of wisdom. Dhālika, that, all that moral code from verse 22 to 38, commandments, most upright guidance. Mimmā, from what, part of what. Awḥā from w-ḥ-y, to reveal, inspire, from waḥā, to reveal, inspire, as Qur'an revealed. Ilayka, to you, O Prophet, Muhammad, from ilā, to. Rabbuka, your Lord, from r-b-b. Mina al-ḥikmati, of wisdom, from ḥ-k-m, wisdom, judgment, from ḥakama, to judge, be wise, from ḥikmah, wisdom, as Qur'an is wisdom, and Prophet given wisdom. Al-Ṭabarī explains that that, all that moral code, most upright guidance, is part of wisdom which your Lord revealed to you, O Prophet, via Qur'an, as wisdom, ḥikmah, which includes monotheism, honouring parents, giving due, moderation, protecting life, wealth, pledges, measure, knowledge, humility, etc., all wisdom, most upright. Al-Qurṭubī says ḥikmah here means wisdom, which is putting things in right place, most upright, and Qur'an guides to most upright, so moral code is wisdom.

**Do Not Set Up Other God Or Cast Into Hell Blameworthy Rejected**

Wa-lā tajʿal maʿa Allāhi ilāhan ākhara fa-tulqā fī jahannama malūman madḥūran — and do not make with Allah other god, then you will be cast into Hell blameworthy rejected. Wa-lā tajʿal from j-ʿ-l, to make, set up, as verse 22 lā tajʿal maʿa Allāhi ilāhan ākhara, do not set up other god, same prohibition framing moral code at beginning and end. Maʿa Allāhi, with Allah. Ilāhan ākhara, other god, from a-l-h and ā-kh-r. Fa-tulqā from l-q-y, to be cast, thrown, from alqā, to cast, throw, passive tulqā, be cast. Fī jahannama, into Hell, Gehenna, from j-h-n-m. Malūman from l-w-m, blameworthy, blamed, as verse 29 malūman blameworthy. Madḥūran from d-ḥ-r, rejected, driven away, banished, as verse 18 madḥūran rejected, condemned rejected for fleeting desirer. Al-Ṭabarī says do not set up other god with Allah, or you will be cast into Hell blameworthy, blamed, and rejected, driven away from mercy, banished, as punishment for shirk, setting up other god, which is greatest violation, detestable to Lord, evil.

**Why Wisdom And Shirk Framing**

Why this verse frames moral code with wisdom and shirk prohibition at both ends? Because moral code from verse 22 to 38 is wisdom, ḥikmah, revealed, most upright, and shirk prohibition at beginning (verse 22 condemned abandoned) and end (verse 39 cast into Hell blameworthy rejected) frames wisdom, showing that monotheism is foundation and conclusion, most important, wisdom's core. The moral code between is wisdom that is built on monotheism and leads to avoiding Hell. The phrase dhālika mimmā awḥā ilayka rabbuka mina al-ḥikmati indicates that all that is part of wisdom revealed, not all wisdom, but part, as Qur'an contains more wisdom, but this part is essential, most upright.

**Connection To Qur'an Guides To Most Upright And Detestable**

Verse 9 said Qur'an guides to what is most upright, gives good news to believers who do good mighty reward, warns those who do not believe painful punishment. Verse 38 said violation of any of these commandments detestable to Lord. Verse 39 says this is part of wisdom revealed to you, do not set up other god or cast into Hell blameworthy rejected. The connection is that Qur'an guides to most upright, which is wisdom, and violation detestable, and setting up other god leads to Hell blameworthy rejected, most detestable, so follow wisdom, most upright, avoid shirk, avoid violation detestable, to get mighty reward, appreciated striving, avoid painful punishment, Hell.

**Link To Night Journey And Wisdom Revealed**

The sūrah opened with Night Journey, Glory be to One Who took His servant by night from Sacred Mosque to Farthest Mosque whose surroundings blessed to show signs, All-Hearing All-Seeing. Then gave Moses Scripture guide, warned Children of Isra'il, etc., then Qur'an guides to most upright. Verse 39 says this is part of wisdom revealed to you, O Prophet, linking Night Journey and revelation of wisdom: Prophet taken to show signs, given Qur'an that guides to most upright, part of wisdom revealed, including moral code, and warning not to set up other god or Hell. The Night Journey was to show signs and to reveal wisdom.

**Linguistic Note On Awha And Hikmah And Tulqa And Malum Madhur**

Dhālika from dh-l-k, that. Mimmā from m-n and m-ā, from what. Awḥā from w-ḥ-y, to reveal, inspire, from waḥā. Ilayka, to you. Rabbuka, your Lord. Ḥikmah from ḥ-k-m, wisdom, from ḥakama, to judge, be wise. Tajʿal from j-ʿ-l, to make, set up. Maʿa, with. Allāh, Allah. Ilāh from a-l-h, god. Ākhar from ā-kh-r, other. Tulqā from l-q-y, to be cast, thrown, from alqā. Jahannam from j-h-n-m, Hell. Malūm from l-w-m, blameworthy, from lāma. Madḥūr from d-ḥ-r, rejected, driven away, from daḥara. The verse's structure — that is from what your Lord revealed to you of wisdom, and do not make with Allah other god then you will be cast into Hell blameworthy rejected — with statement that moral code part of wisdom revealed and prohibition of shirk with consequence cast into Hell blameworthy rejected, frames wisdom with monotheism foundation and warning.

**How This Verse Teaches Wisdom And Warning Against Shirk**

This verse teaches that moral code from monotheism to humility is part of wisdom revealed to Prophet, wisdom, most upright, and warns against setting up other god with Allah, or you will be cast into Hell blameworthy, blamed, and rejected, driven away, banished from mercy. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should follow wisdom revealed, most upright guidance, which includes all commandments, and should avoid shirk, setting up other god, which leads to being cast into Hell blameworthy rejected, as greatest detestable violation, evil. The practical lesson is to follow wisdom, most upright, worship Allah alone, honour parents, give due, moderate, protect life, wealth, pledges, measure, knowledge, humility, as wisdom, and avoid shirk, which leads to Hell blameworthy rejected.

**A Reflection On Wisdom Revealed And Hell Blameworthy Rejected**

Mina al-ḥikmati — of wisdom, part of wisdom — indicates that Qur'an contains wisdom, most upright, and this moral code is part of it, essential, revealed to Prophet. Following wisdom leads to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour in Hereafter. Setting up other god leads to being cast into Hell, tulqā fī jahannama, cast, thrown, not entered willingly, blameworthy, malūman, blamed, and rejected, madḥūran, driven away, banished, as fleeting desirer condemned rejected, but worse: cast into Hell. The believer who reflects will follow wisdom revealed, most upright, worship Allah alone, avoid shirk, so not cast into Hell blameworthy rejected, but have appreciated striving, mighty reward, as Qur'an guides to most upright.
