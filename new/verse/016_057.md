## Sūrah al-Naḥl 16:57

> **And they attribute ˹angels as˺ daughters to Allah—glory be to Him!— the opposite of what they desire for themselves.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Attributing Daughters To Allah**

Wa-yajʿalūna li-Allāhi al-banāt — and they attribute to Allah daughters. Yajʿalūna from j-ʿ-l means to make, assign, attribute. Li-Allāhi, to Allah. Al-banāt, daughters, plural of bint, daughter. Al-Ṭabarī explains that Makkan polytheists attributed angels as daughters to Allah, saying angels are daughters of Allah, while they themselves desired sons and disliked daughters, as next verses describe. This is fabrication against Allah, as verse 56 said you will be questioned about lies fabricated. The attribution of daughters to Allah is lie because Allah has no children, no daughters, no sons, as 112:3 says *He neither begets nor is born* (112:3). Al-Rāzī notes that attributing daughters to Allah is both shirk and insult: shirk because it attributes to Allah what is not His, insult because they attribute to Allah what they themselves hate, daughters, while desiring sons for themselves.

**Glory Be To Him**

Subḥānahu — glory be to Him, exalted, free from defect. Subḥān from s-b-ḥ means to be free, far removed, to swim away from defect. Subḥānahu is tanzīh, declaring Allah free from what they attribute. Al-Ṭabarī says subḥānahu here declares Allah free from having daughters, from having children, from what they attribute. The phrase appears often after false attribution to Allah, as in 16:1 *Glorified and Exalted is He above what they associate* (16:1) and 16:3. It is to remind that Allah is beyond such attribution. Al-Zamakhsharī notes that subḥānahu is placed in middle of verse to interrupt false attribution with declaration of transcendence, showing that false attribution should be immediately followed by glorification.

**The Opposite Of What They Desire For Themselves**

Wa-lahum mā yashtahūn — and for themselves what they desire, meaning sons. Lahum, for them, for themselves. Mā yashtahūn, what they desire, what they wish, meaning sons, because they desired sons and disliked daughters. Al-Ṭabarī explains that they attribute daughters to Allah while for themselves they desire sons, which is opposite of what they attribute to Allah. This shows inconsistency and injustice: you attribute to Allah what you hate for yourselves, daughters, while keeping for yourselves what you love, sons. If daughters are inferior in your view, why attribute them to Allah whom you claim to honor? If sons are superior, why not attribute sons to Allah? Their attribution reveals both lie and insult: they give Allah what they consider inferior and keep superior for themselves.

**Why This Attribution Is Evil**

Attributing daughters to Allah is evil for several reasons. First, it attributes to Allah what He does not have: children. Allah has no children. Second, it attributes to Allah what they themselves hate, showing disrespect. Third, it is based on ignorance: they say angels are daughters of Allah without knowledge, as verse 56 said they assign share to those who know nothing. Fourth, it is fabrication that will be questioned, as verse 56 warned. Al-Qurṭubī notes that this attribution was common among Arabs who worshipped angels as daughters of Allah and sought intercession from them, as 53:27 says *Those who do not believe in Hereafter name angels with female names* (53:27). The verse exposes irrationality: you worship angels as daughters of Allah, yet you dislike daughters for yourselves.

**Connection To Next Verses About Baby Girl**

Verses 58-59 will describe how when one of them is given good news of baby girl, his face grows gloomy and he hides from people, deciding whether to keep her in disgrace or bury her alive. This shows their hatred for daughters, which makes attribution of daughters to Allah even more insulting. They hate daughters for themselves, yet attribute them to Allah. The sūrah thus exposes moral contradiction: you hate daughters for yourself, yet you say Allah has daughters, implying you think Allah deserves what you hate. This is evil judgment, as verse 59 says *Evil indeed is their judgment*.

**Linguistic Note On Banāt And Subḥān**

Yajʿalūna from j-ʿ-l, to make, assign. Li-Allāhi, to Allah. Al-banāt, daughters, plural of bint, from b-n-y, to build, daughter as built? Subḥānahu, glory be to Him, from s-b-ḥ. Wa-lahum mā yashtahūn, and for them what they desire, from sh-h-w, to desire. The verse's structure — they attribute to Allah daughters, glory be to Him, and for them what they desire — with attribution, glorification, and contrast, creates sharp critique of fabrication that attributes to Allah what is hated for self, showing injustice and lie.

**How This Verse Teaches Tawḥīd And Justice**

Tawḥīd requires attributing to Allah only what befits Him, not what you hate. Justice requires giving to others what you would like for yourself, not opposite. This verse shows polytheists fail both: they fail tawḥīd by attributing daughters to Allah, and fail justice by giving Allah what they hate for themselves. Al-Rāzī says this is worst injustice: to give to Allah what you consider inferior. The verse therefore calls to attribute to Allah only finest attributes, as verse 60 will say to Allah belong finest attributes, and to be just in attribution. The believer should attribute to Allah what befits His majesty, not what he himself dislikes.

**A Lesson On Gender And Value**

The verse also touches on gender bias: polytheists desired sons and disliked daughters, yet attributed daughters to Allah. The Qur'an does not endorse bias but exposes it as evil judgment. It says *Evil indeed is their judgment* (16:59) for deciding whether to keep daughter in disgrace or bury alive. The Qur'an honors daughters as blessing, not disgrace, and forbids burying alive, as 81:8-9 says *When baby girl buried alive is asked for what sin she was killed* (81:8-9). The verse therefore not only refutes attribution of daughters to Allah but also critiques devaluation of daughters, showing that attributing to Allah what you hate for yourself is both theological lie and social injustice.
