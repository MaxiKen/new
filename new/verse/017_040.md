## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:40

> **Has your Lord favoured you ˹pagans˺ with sons and taken angels as ˹His˺ daughters? You are truly making an outrageous claim.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Has Lord Favoured You With Sons And Taken Angels As Daughters**

A-fa-aṣfākum rabbukum bi-l-banīna wa-ittakhadha mina al-malā'ikati ināthan — has your Lord favoured you with sons and taken from angels daughters? A-fa-, has, interrogative with fa, so, has, questioning, rebuking. Aṣfākum from ṣ-f-w, to favour, choose, select, from aṣfā, to choose, favour, select, from ṣafā, to be pure, choose. Rabbukum, your Lord, from r-b-b. Bi-l-banīna from b-n-y, sons, offspring, plural of ibn, son, as banīn sons. Wa-ittakhadha from a-kh-dh, to take, adopt, from ittakhadha, to take. Mina al-malā'ikati from m-l-k, angels, plural of malak, angel, from malaka, to possess, messenger. Ināthan from a-n-th, daughters, females, plural of unthā, female, daughter, from anutha, female. Al-Ṭabarī explains that has your Lord favoured you pagans with sons and taken angels as His daughters? This is rebuke to Makkan pagans who claimed angels are daughters of Allah, while they themselves preferred sons and disliked daughters, yet attributed daughters to Allah, contradiction, outrageous claim. The question is rhetorical, rebuking, indicating absurdity: has Lord favoured you with sons, which you like, and taken for Himself daughters, which you dislike? This is unjust attribution, outrageous.

**You Make Outrageous Claim**

Innakum la-taqūlūna qawlan ʿaẓīman — indeed you say tremendous, outrageous, great word, claim. Innakum, indeed you, you pagans. La-taqūlūna from q-w-l, to say, indeed you say, with la emphasis. Qawlan from q-w-l, word, saying, claim. ʿAẓīman from ʿ-ẓ-m, great, tremendous, outrageous, mighty, from ʿaẓuma, to be great, from ʿaẓīm, great, tremendous. Al-Ṭabarī says you are truly making outrageous claim, tremendous word, great lie, saying Allah has daughters, angels as daughters, while you prefer sons, attributing to Allah what you dislike for yourselves, which is outrageous, tremendous claim, great sin. Al-Qurṭubī says ʿaẓīman means great, tremendous, outrageous, as claiming Allah has daughters is great lie, outrageous, as Allah has no offspring, no partner, as verse 111 will say all praise for Allah Who never had offspring nor partner.

**Why This Verse Follows Wisdom And Shirk Warning**

Verse 39 said this is part of wisdom revealed, do not set up other god or cast into Hell blameworthy rejected. Verse 40 says has your Lord favoured you pagans with sons and taken angels as daughters? You make outrageous claim. The connection is that setting up other god includes attributing daughters to Allah, angels as daughters, which is shirk, outrageous claim, part of setting up other god with Allah, as angels claimed as daughters and worshipped. The verse rebukes pagans for this outrageous claim, showing that shirk includes attributing offspring to Allah, which is detestable, evil, as previous verse said violation detestable to Lord. The wisdom revealed includes monotheism, Allah has no offspring, no partner, as verse 111 will say.

**Link To Angels As Daughters And Sons Preference**

Makkan pagans preferred sons, disliked daughters, burying daughters alive for fear of poverty, as verse 31 prohibited killing children for fear of poverty, yet they attributed daughters, which they disliked, to Allah, whom they claimed to honour, contradiction, showing injustice, outrageous claim. The verse highlights contradiction: you favour sons for yourselves, yet attribute daughters to Allah, whom you claim is Lord, which is unjust, outrageous, as you attribute to Allah what you dislike. This shows their claim false, outrageous, tremendous.

**Connection To Allah Never Had Offspring**

Verse 111 will say all praise for Allah Who never had offspring nor does He have partner in kingdom nor is He pathetic needing protector, and revere Him immensely. Verse 40 previews that: Allah has no sons, no daughters, no offspring, claiming angels as daughters outrageous. The sūrah's theme of monotheism appears: do not set up other god, Lord decreed worship none but Him, this is wisdom, Allah never had offspring, so do not attribute daughters, sons.

**Linguistic Note On Asfā And Ināth And Azim**

A-fa- from a-, interrogative, has, and fa-, so. Aṣfā from ṣ-f-w, to favour, choose, select, from ṣafā, to choose, favour. Rabb from r-b-b, Lord. Banīn from b-n-y, sons, plural of ibn. Ittakhadha from a-kh-dh, to take, from ittakhadha. Malā'ikah from m-l-k, angels, plural of malak. Ināth from a-n-th, daughters, females, plural of unthā. Taqūlūna from q-w-l, to say. Qawl from q-w-l, word, saying. ʿAẓīm from ʿ-ẓ-m, great, tremendous, outrageous, from ʿaẓuma. The verse's structure — has your Lord favoured you with sons and taken from angels daughters? Indeed you say tremendous word — with rhetorical question rebuking attribution of daughters to Allah while favouring sons for selves, and statement that you say outrageous claim, shows absurdity and gravity of claim that Allah has daughters.

**How This Verse Teaches Monotheism Without Offspring**

This verse teaches monotheism without offspring: Allah has no sons, no daughters, no offspring, has not taken angels as daughters, claiming so outrageous, tremendous lie, great sin. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should believe Allah has no offspring, no sons, no daughters, no partner, as all praise for Allah Who never had offspring nor partner, and should not attribute daughters, sons, offspring to Allah, as pagans did with angels as daughters, which is outrageous claim, tremendous word, shirk, detestable. The practical lesson is to affirm Allah has no offspring, no partner, worship none but Him, as wisdom, most upright, and avoid outrageous claim that Allah has daughters, sons, offspring, which is shirk, leading to Hell blameworthy rejected.

**A Reflection On Outrageous Claim**

Qawlan ʿaẓīman — tremendous, outrageous, great word, claim — indicates gravity of attributing daughters to Allah, angels as daughters, while preferring sons for selves, contradiction, injustice, outrageous, tremendous lie, great sin, as Allah is far above having offspring, as verse 43 will say Glorified and Highly Exalted is He above what they claim. The believer who reflects will not make outrageous claim, will affirm Allah has no offspring, no daughters, no sons, no partner, will worship none but Him, follow wisdom revealed, most upright guidance, so that striving appreciated, mighty reward, far greater rank and favour, and avoid painful punishment, Hell blameworthy rejected for shirk, outrageous claim.
