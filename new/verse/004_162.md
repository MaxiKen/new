## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:162

> But those of them well-grounded in knowledge, the faithful ˹who˺ believe in what has been revealed to you ˹O Prophet˺ and what was revealed before you—˹especially˺ those who establish prayer— and those who pay alms-tax and believe in Allah and the Last Day, to these ˹people˺ We will grant a great reward.

**Expanded Commentary**

**But Those Well-Grounded in Knowledge Among Them**

The verse states *lākini al-rāsikhūna fī al-ʿilmi minhum* — but those firmly grounded, deeply rooted, in knowledge, among them — among the People of the Book — whose litany was demand, thunderbolt, calf, breaking, rejecting, killing, claiming *ghulf* sealed, slander, boasting murder, forbidding *ṭayyibāt* for *ẓulm*, taking *ribā* though prohibited, consuming wealth unjustly — among them the *rāsikhūn*. *Rāsikhūn fī al-ʿilm* — firmly grounded in knowledge — was explained as those rooted deep so that wind does not move them, as the sūrah has shown that those who distinguish between God and messengers desire a way between (4:150) are not grounded, but these are, as Abraham was *ḥanīf* following the upright (4:125) and as the Book was sent with truth to judge between people by what He shows (4:105). *Minhum* — among them — was read as not all among them: as the painful punishment was prepared for *kāfirīn minhum* — disbelievers among them (4:161) — great reward is for *rāsikhūn minhum* — grounded among them — keeping the sūrah’s balance: few believe except few (4:155) — here the few are named.

**The Faithful Who Believe in What Was Revealed to You and Before You**

The description *wa al-muʾminūna yuʾminūna bi mā unzila ilayka wa mā unzila min qablika* — and the faithful — believers — who believe in what was revealed to you and what was revealed before you, before thee — was explained as comprehensive faith that does not distinguish between any messenger (4:152) and accepts revelation lineage — *“We have sent revelation to you as We sent to Noah and prophets after him”* (4:163) — Abraham, Ishmael, Isaac, Jacob, descendants, Jesus, Job, Jonah, Aaron, Solomon, David given Psalms. To believe in *mā unzila ilayka* — what was revealed to you — and *mā unzila min qablika* — what was revealed before you — was read as that the sūrah commanded *have faith in God, His Messenger, Book He revealed to His Messenger and Scriptures He revealed before* (4:136) — steadfast, not selective; the *rāsikhūn* are those who kept that. The sūrah has shown that *“whoever believes in God and messengers accepting all, rejecting none, He will give rewards”* (4:152) — these are they.

**Especially Those Who Establish Prayer and Pay Alms**

The detail *wa al-muqīmīna al-ṣalāta wa al-muʾtūna al-zakāta* — and especially those who establish, stand firm for, prayer — *muqīmīn* — and those who give, pay, alms — *zakāh* — was explained with two pillars: *iqāmat al-ṣalāh* — establishing prayer — the same *kitāban mawqūtā* — writ at fixed time (4:103) — shortened on travel (4:101), guarded under arms (4:102), remembered standing, sitting, lying (4:103), lazy to be seen by hypocrites (4:142) — the *rāsikhūn* establish it. *Wa al-muʾtūna al-zakāta* — and those who pay alms — was read as the opposite of *akhdh al-ribā* — taking interest though forbidden — and *akl bi al-bāṭil* — consuming wealth unjustly (4:161): to give *zakāh* is to release wealth, to take *ribā* is to increase it wrongly. Early reports noted the grammar — *al-muqīmīna* in accusative as praise, as Arabs single out the praised with accusative — showing special praise for those who establish prayer among the grounded. The sūrah has said *“charge your wealth by mutual consent, do not kill yourselves, He is ever Merciful”* (4:29) — *zakāh* is mutual consent’s alms. The pair was also linked to *“Gardens for those who believe and do good”* (4:122): establishing prayer and giving alms are the *ṣāliḥāt* — righteous deeds.

**And Those Who Believe in God and the Last Day — To Them Great Reward**

The seal *wa al-muʾminīna bi Allāhi wa al-yawmi al-ākhiri ulāʾika sa nuʾtīhim ajran ʿaẓīmā* — and those who believe in God and the Last Day — to these We will surely, will soon, grant a great, mighty reward — was explained with *yuʾminūna bi Allāhi wa al-yawmi al-ākhiri* as the two articles that anchor all — God and Last Day — without which Articles — denying God, angels, Books, messengers, Last Day leads far astray (4:136) — belief incomplete; the *rāsikhūn* affirm them. *Ulāʾika sa nuʾtīhim ajran ʿaẓīmā* — to these We will grant great reward — was explained as the same *ajr ʿaẓīm* promised to those who command charity, kindness, reconciliation seeking His pleasure (4:114) and to the repentant sincere with believers (4:146) and to comprehensive believers (4:152) — the *rāsikhūn* who establish prayer and give alms are inclusive. The sūrah has shown that *“with Him are both rewards He hears and sees”* (4:134) — great reward is both — world as *murāgham* and *saʿah* and Hereafter as Gardens true. *Sa nuʾtīhim* — We will grant — was read as divine assurance that reward secured upon God as emigrant’s reward secured (4:100) — the same certainty.

**A Verse That Follows Disbelief Among Them With Faith Among Them**

Placed after *We forbade Jews certain good foods for wrongdoing and hindering many from God’s way* (4:160), *taking interest though forbidden and consuming wealth unjustly, We have prepared for the disbelievers among them painful punishment* (4:161), this verse follows disbelief among them with faith among them: but the firmly grounded in knowledge among them, the faithful who believe in what was revealed to you and before you, especially those who establish prayer and pay alms and believe in God and Last Day — to them great reward. The sūrah has shown that even after litany — demand greater than a book, thunderbolt, calf after clear signs yet forgiven, Mount raised, firm covenant, breaking, rejecting, killing prophets, claiming *ghulf*, denial, slander against Mary with *buhtān ʿaẓīm*, boasting killing Messiah not killed but made to appear — there remain *rāsikhūn* — firmly grounded — who do not distinguish (4:152) and who will receive great reward. The verse was read as the sūrah’s middle after ownership to Him of heavens and earth and encompassing (4:126) — the grounded are those who accept that ownership and witness with His knowledge (4:166). For the weak this is opening: where interest was taken from the debtor and wealth consumed unjustly, the grounded who give alms are the ones who enrich without stripping, as the separator enriched from His bounty (4:130) — the great reward includes *ṭayyibāt* restored.
