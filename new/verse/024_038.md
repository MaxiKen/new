## Sūrah an-Nūr 24:38

> ˹hoping˺ that Allah may reward them according to the best of their deeds, and increase them out of His grace. And Allah provides for whoever He wills without limit.

**Expanded Commentary**

**Divine Evaluation by the Best Standard: li-yajziyahumu Allāh**

The divine decree unveils the ultimate reward awaiting those noble souls who refused to let worldly commerce distract them from divine worship: *li-yajziyahumu Allāhu aḥsana mā ʿamilū*. The prepositional prefix *lām* in *li-yajziyahum* indicates both purpose and consequence; their disciplined devotion was oriented toward this singular divine reckoning.

The phrase *aḥsana mā ʿamilū* (the best of what they did) reveals an astonishing principle of divine justice and mercy. Classical commentators, including al-Rāzī, al-Ṭabarī, and al-Qurṭubī, explained that God does not evaluate the believer through a harsh, hyper-critical scrutiny that exposes every minor flaw and human imperfection. Instead, He takes the finest, most sincere, and most beautiful deeds performed by the servant and uses them as the standard measure by which the rest of their righteous conduct is judged and elevated. Minor shortcomings are graciously pardoned and overshadowed by the excellence of their highest devotions, filling the believer with profound hope, enduring gratitude, and unshakeable spiritual tranquility that sustains the heart across every trial, inspiring deeper love for God.

**Transcendent Grace Beyond Merit: wa-yazīdahum min faḍlih**

Divine reward is not confined to a strict one-to-one transactional equivalence: *wa-yazīdahum min faḍlih*, and increase them out of His grace. In the theological framework of the Qur'an, human salvation is never purchased merely by the mechanical weight of mortal deeds.

Even the most devout servant, having spent a lifetime in prayer, fasting, and charity, could never balance the scales against the infinite blessings of life, sight, intellect, and guidance bestowed by God. Therefore, while the *jazāʾ* (recompense) corresponds to the excellence of their deeds, the *ziyādah* (increase) flows entirely from unearned divine bounty (*faḍl*). God bestows unimaginable celestial honors, spiritual unveilings, and eternal joys that far surpass anything the servant earned or imagined, proving that paradise is an inheritance of divine grace rather than commercial wages, comforting the soul that knows its finite limitations and yearns for divine generosity to complete its salvation and grant it everlasting peace.

**Limitless Provision: Allāhu yarzuqu man yashāʾu bi-ghayri ḥisāb**

The verse concludes with an awe-inspiring cosmological declaration: *wa-Allāhu yarzuqu man yashāʾu bi-ghayri ḥisāb*, and Allah provides for whoever He wills without limit. The expression *bi-ghayri ḥisāb* (without reckoning) operates on multiple layers of divine generosity and transcendent majesty.

First, it means that God does not maintain a petty, restrictive ledger when showering His bounties upon His chosen servants; His divine treasury is infinite, incapable of diminution or exhaustion. Second, it signifies that the recipient does not have to reckon or calculate how sustenance will arrive; divine provision descends through completely unforeseen channels (*min ḥaythu lā yaḥtasib*), exceeding every material expectation and logical forecast. Third, in the Hereafter, the reward granted to the righteous is boundless in duration and intensity, devoid of any final balance sheet or termination, continuing in ever-increasing ecstasy throughout eternity without depletion, fatigue, or interruption, establishing everlasting bliss.

**Overcoming the Trap of Self-Righteous Calculation**

A profound psychological liberation occurs when the believer internalizes the truth of unreckoned divine provision. Materialistic culture conditions human beings to view life as an exhausting, zero-sum economic calculation where every effort must yield an immediate, measurable return.

This transactional mentality frequently poisons religious life, leading people into self-righteous arrogance if they perform rituals, or deep despair if worldly hardship strikes despite their prayers. Verse 38 frees the soul from this commercial neurosis. The believer realizes that their modest acts of worship are accepted and multiplied by a Lord whose generosity is boundless. They do not approach God as anxious accountants bargaining for wages; they serve Him with joyous, loving gratitude, trusting that His grace will reward them far beyond their humble efforts, sanctifying their service with sincere humility, deep love, profound reverence, and spiritual contentment that no worldly hardship can shatter.

**Cosmic Generosity and the Architecture of Divine Mercy**

Reflecting on the relationship between verse 37 and verse 38 illuminates the supreme coherence of Qur'anic pedagogy. In verse 37, the merchants willingly paused their commercial calculations—buying, selling, profit margins, and inventory—to honor God's remembrance.

In reciprocal divine justice, verse 38 assures these merchants that because they abandoned worldly calculations for the sake of God, God will provide for them without any calculation whatsoever! The finite profit they sacrificed in the physical marketplace is replaced by infinite spiritual and material sustenance descending from the Lord of the heavens and the earth. Giving up temporary, calculated wealth for divine pleasure unlocks the uncalculated, boundless abundance of the Creator, demonstrating the magnificent symmetry of divine mercy across both terrestrial and celestial existence, rewarding faithful restraint with eternal bounty, joy, honor, and spiritual elevation that endures forever.

**The Ultimate Vindication of Detached Devotion**

The promise embedded in this verse provides unwavering solace to every believer who has suffered worldly loss for the sake of religious principle. To turn down an unethical financial transaction, to lose business opportunities due to prayer, or to spend hard-earned capital on zakāh can feel daunting in an aggressive economy.

Verse 38 stands as an unshakeable divine guarantee that no servant will ever be impoverished by putting God first. The All-Bountiful Provider sees every sacrifice made in secret, and He guarantees that the final balance sheet will end in overwhelming victory. In both the provisional realms of this temporary world and the permanent abodes of the afterlife, those who trusted in God's bounty will find themselves enriched with treasures that decay can never destroy, basking in everlasting peace, divine approval, radiant honor, unceasing joy, and eternal fulfillment in the company of the righteous.
