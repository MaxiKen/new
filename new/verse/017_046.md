## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:46

> **We have cast veils over their hearts—leaving them unable to comprehend it—and deafness in their ears. And when you mention your Lord alone in the Quran, they turn their backs in aversion.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Veils Over Hearts Unable To Comprehend And Deafness In Ears**

Wa-jaʿalnā ʿalā qulūbihim akinnatan an yafqahūhu wa-fī ādhānihim waqran — and We made over their hearts veils that they not comprehend it and in their ears deafness. Wa-jaʿalnā from j-ʿ-l, to make, put, as previous jaʿalnā barrier. ʿAlā qulūbihim, over their hearts, upon their hearts, from q-l-b, hearts, plural of qalb, heart. Akinnatan from k-n-n, veils, coverings, that cover, from kanna, to cover, veil, from akinnah, veils, coverings, plural of kinān, veil, covering. An yafqahūhu, that they comprehend it, understand it, Qur'an, from f-q-h, to comprehend, understand, as lā tafqahūna tasbīḥahum, you do not comprehend their glorification, and lā tafqahūna comprehension. Wa-fī ādhānihim, and in their ears, from a-dh-n, ears, plural of udhun, ear. Waqran from w-q-r, deafness, heaviness, from waqara, to be heavy, deaf, from waqr, deafness, heaviness, as deafness. Al-Ṭabarī explains that We cast veils, coverings over their hearts, so they cannot comprehend Qur'an, cannot understand, and deafness, heaviness in their ears, so they cannot hear, benefit, as they disbelieve in Hereafter, have denying hearts, arrogant, barrier hidden, as previous verse. Al-Qurṭubī says akinnatan means veils, coverings, that prevent comprehension, and waqran means deafness, heaviness, preventing hearing, both prevent benefiting from Qur'an.

**When You Mention Lord Alone In Quran They Turn Backs In Aversion**

Wa-idhā dhakarta rabbaka fī al-Qur'āni waḥdahu wallaw ʿalā adbārihim nufūran — and when you mention your Lord alone in Qur'an, they turn on their backs in aversion, fleeing. Wa-idhā, and when. Dhakarta from dh-k-r, to mention, remember, as li-yadh-dhakkarū so they may be mindful, remember. Rabbaka, your Lord, from r-b-b. Fī al-Qur'āni, in Qur'an. Waḥdahu, alone, only Him, alone, from w-ḥ-d, alone, one, as waḥdahu alone, only Allah alone. Wallaw from w-l-y, to turn, turn away, from wallā, to turn, turn away, to turn back. ʿAlā adbārihim, on their backs, upon their backs, from d-b-r, back, rear, from dubur, back. Nufūran from n-f-r, aversion, fleeing, as verse 41 nufūran aversion, driving farther away, and verse 46 nufūran aversion. Al-Ṭabarī explains that when you, O Prophet, mention your Lord alone, only Allah alone, in Qur'an, without mentioning other gods, idols, they turn their backs in aversion, fleeing, disliking, as they dislike monotheism, want other gods mentioned, as they claim other gods, daughters, etc., so when Lord alone mentioned, they turn backs in aversion, flee.

**Why Veils, Deafness, Aversion For Mentioning Lord Alone**

Why veils over hearts, deafness in ears, turning backs in aversion when Lord alone mentioned? Because they disbelieve in Hereafter, have denying hearts, arrogant, as previous chapter said, and have outrageous claim that Allah has daughters, other gods, etc., so when Lord alone mentioned, monotheism, they dislike, turn backs in aversion, flee, as they want other gods, idols, daughters, etc. The veils, deafness, aversion are punishment for disbelief in Hereafter, arrogance, denying hearts, and for outrageous claims, as they have barrier hidden, veils, deafness, aversion, so they cannot comprehend Qur'an, cannot benefit, increase in nufūr, aversion, driving farther away.

**Connection To Hidden Barrier And Varying Signs And Aversion**

Verse 45 said when you recite Qur'an We put hidden barrier between you and those who do not believe in Hereafter. Verse 46 says We cast veils over hearts unable to comprehend and deafness in ears, when you mention Lord alone in Qur'an they turn backs in aversion. Verse 41 said We varied signs in Qur'an so they may be mindful but it only drives them farther away, nufūran. The connection is that hidden barrier, veils, deafness, aversion are reasons why varying signs drives them farther away, increases in aversion, as they cannot comprehend, cannot hear, turn backs in aversion when Lord alone mentioned, as they dislike monotheism, want other gods.

**Link To Hearing Sight Heart Accountable And Not Comprehending**

Verse 36 said hearing, sight, heart accountable, will be questioned. Verse 44 said you cannot comprehend their glorification, all things glorify but you cannot comprehend. Verse 46 says veils over hearts unable to comprehend, deafness in ears, turning backs in aversion, showing that disbelievers cannot comprehend Qur'an due to veils, deafness, as they have denying hearts, arrogant, barrier, and they turn backs in aversion when Lord alone mentioned, as they dislike monotheism, while believers comprehend, benefit, glorify, join universal glorification.

**Linguistic Note On Akinnah And Waqr And Wallaw And Nufur**

Jaʿalnā from j-ʿ-l, to make. Qulūb from q-l-b, hearts, plural of qalb. Akinnah from k-n-n, veils, coverings, plural of kinān, veil, from kanna, to cover. Yafqahū from f-q-h, to comprehend, understand. Ādhān from a-dh-n, ears, plural of udhun. Waqr from w-q-r, deafness, heaviness, from waqara, to be heavy, deaf. Dhakara from dh-k-r, to mention, remember. Rabb from r-b-b, Lord. Waḥdahu from w-ḥ-d, alone, from waḥada. Wallaw from w-l-y, to turn, turn away, from wallā. Adbār from d-b-r, backs, plural of dubur, back. Nufūr from n-f-r, aversion, fleeing, from nafara. The verse's structure — We made over hearts veils that they not comprehend and in ears deafness, and when you mention Lord alone in Qur'an they turn on backs in aversion — with veils preventing comprehension and deafness preventing hearing and turning backs in aversion when Lord alone mentioned, shows that disbelievers in Hereafter have veils, deafness, aversion, preventing benefit from Qur'an, disliking monotheism.

**How This Verse Teaches Veils And Aversion For Disbelievers**

This verse teaches that for those who do not believe in Hereafter, Allah casts veils over hearts leaving them unable to comprehend Qur'an and deafness in ears, and when Lord alone mentioned in Qur'an they turn backs in aversion, fleeing, disliking monotheism, wanting other gods. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should believe in Hereafter, so no veils, no deafness, no aversion, should mention Lord alone in Qur'an and love monotheism, not turn backs in aversion, should be mindful, comprehend, benefit, as Qur'an guides to most upright, varies signs so they may be mindful. The practical lesson is to believe in Hereafter, remove veils, deafness, aversion, by faith, humility, not arrogance, and to mention Lord alone, love monotheism, not turn backs, as most upright.

**A Reflection On Mentioning Lord Alone And Turning Backs**

Wa-idhā dhakarta rabbaka fī al-Qur'āni waḥdahu wallaw ʿalā adbārihim nufūran — when you mention your Lord alone in Qur'an, they turn on backs in aversion — indicates that disbelievers dislike monotheism, Lord alone, want other gods, daughters, sons, partners, etc., so when Lord alone mentioned, without other gods, they turn backs in aversion, fleeing, disliking, as they have denying hearts, arrogant, veils, deafness, barrier, and increase in nufūr, aversion, driving farther away, as varying signs should make mindful but drives farther away. The believer who believes in Hereafter mentions Lord alone and loves, does not turn backs, benefits, is mindful, follows most upright, wisdom, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, while disbeliever turns backs in aversion, has veils, deafness, barrier, painful punishment prepared.
