## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:125

> And who is better in faith than those who ˹fully˺ submit themselves to Allah, do good, and follow the Way of Abraham, the upright? Allah chose Abraham as a close friend.

**Expanded Commentary**

**Who Is Better in Faith Than Those Who Submit and Do Good**

The verse opens *wa man aḥsanu dīnan mimman aslama wajhahū li Allāhi wa huwa muḥsinun wa attabaʿa millata Ibrāhīma ḥanīfā* — and who is better in faith, in religion, than those who fully submit their face, themselves, to God and are doers of good, and follow the Way of Abraham, the upright. *Man aḥsanu dīnan* — who is better in religion, in faith — was explained as rhetorical negation: none is better, as earlier *who is more truthful than God in speech?* (4:87, 4:122). *Aslama wajhahū li Allāhi* — submitted his face to God — was read as full submission, face as the noblest part and the direction of prayer, as the sūrah commands *“submit yourselves to God”* in trust. *Wa huwa muḥsin* — while being doer of good, beautiful in deed — was explained as *iḥsān* — doing good, making good, worship as if seeing — the same *muḥsin* who does charity, kindness, reconciliation seeking God’s pleasure (4:114) and who does good male or female with faith (4:124). *Wa attabaʿa millata Ibrāhīma ḥanīfā* — and follows the way, the creed, the community of Abraham, the upright, the inclining from crooked to straight — was read as the *ḥanīf* — pure monotheist who turns away from associators, as Abraham turned from his people’s female gods (4:117) and obeyed command without altering creation (4:119). The three — submission, goodness, following Abraham pure — were explained as the sūrah’s definition of best *dīn*, against wishes that claimed grace by belonging (4:123) and against taking Satan as guardian instead of God (4:119).

**The Way of Abraham the Upright**

The phrase *millata Ibrāhīma ḥanīfā* was explained with history. *Millah* — way, creed, path — was read as the path God made clear as noble ways of those before you (4:26), which Abraham exemplifies. *Ḥanīf* — upright, inclining, pure — was explained as one who inclines from associating (4:116) to the Single, as Abraham was not of the associators (6:161) and was tested with commands and fulfilled them (2:124). The sūrah has said that the People of the Book claim wishes yet some distort words (4:46, 4:153) and that the Qur’an came confirming your scriptures (4:47); following Abraham *ḥanīf* was read as return to the common monotheism before distortions, where invocation is only of God, not female gods nor Satan (4:117). The Way of Abraham was also linked to *“We have given descendants of Abraham the Book and wisdom and great authority”* (4:54) and to *“He chose Abraham as close friend”* here — Abraham’s way is not tribal claim but submitted face and beautiful deed, the same *islām* that began from a single soul’s creation (4:1) and that remains spacious as earth’s havens (4:100).

**And God Chose Abraham as Close Friend**

The seal *wa attakhadha Allāhu Ibrāhīma khalīlā* — and God chose, took, Abraham as a close friend, intimate — was explained as *khalīl* — close friend, bosom friend, the one whose love interpenetrates — *khullah* — the heart’s core. *Ittakhadha* — took — was read as divine choosing, not human claiming; the sūrah has said that God elevates whomever He wills and knows who deserves favour (4:49, 4:70), and that *to Him belongs whatever is in heavens and earth* (4:126, 4:131–132), so friendship is by His taking, not by wish. The title *khalīl* was explained with reports that Abraham’s hospitality, steadfastness after being thrown into fire, and his plea for forgiveness for his father even after being shown enmity, showed the face submitted; God’s taking him as *khalīl* is therefore not an inherited status but a response to *aslama wajhahū wa huwa muḥsin* — submitted and did good. Classical notes mentioned that the Prophet ﷺ said *“had I taken a *khalīl* among you I would have taken Abū Bakr, but your companion is God’s *khalīl*”* (Bukhārī 466, Muslim 2383, similar), showing the station’s uniqueness, yet the verse invites emulation: whoever submits and does good and follows that *ḥanīf* way is best in *dīn*.

**A Verse That Answers Wishes With Abraham’s Submission**

Placed after *those who do good male or female with faith will enter Paradise not wronged a speck* (4:124) and before *to Him belongs whatever is in heavens and earth and He is Fully Aware* (4:126), this verse answers wishes with Abraham’s submission. The sūrah removed *“neither by your wishes nor those of the People of the Book, whoever commits evil will be rewarded accordingly”* (4:123) and gave Paradise by belief and good without wrong (4:124); now it asks *who is better than the submitter, the doer of good, the follower of Abraham ḥanīf?* — answer: none. The sūrah has shown Satan’s path — defying the Messenger after guidance (4:115), associating far astray (4:116), invoking females/Satan (4:117), cursed taking share (4:118), ordering cutting and altering (4:119), promising only delusion (4:120) — versus God’s path — Book in truth (4:105), wisdom taught (4:113), no good except charity, kindness, reconciliation (4:114); Abraham is the human embodiment of God’s path, who left his people’s idols, submitted face to fire, and was made *khalīl*. For the weak this is an accessible model: best *dīn* is not speculation about wishes but submission of face, beautification of deed, and following the upright way whose owner owns heavens and earth.

**The Close Friend Whose Way Is the Believers’ Path**

The phrase *sabili al-muʾminīn* — path of the believers (4:115) — was linked here to *millata Ibrāhīma*: the believers’ path is Abraham’s *ḥanīf* way where defiance after clarity is refused. The sūrah that warns not to take disbelievers as allies for honour (4:139, 4:144) and declares hypocrites in lowest depth (4:145) makes Abraham the criterion: his alliance was with the Single, his honour was *khalīl* taken by Him. The verse generalises the judgment chapter’s lesson where grace preserved the Prophet from being deceived (4:113): the preservation came via Book and wisdom teaching what he did not know; Abraham’s preservation came via *ḥanīf* submission. Both say: best faith is submitted face, goodness, and upright way, taken as friend by the One to whom all belongs. The next verse will seal that ownership: to Him belongs whatever is in heavens and earth, and He is Fully Aware of everything (4:126).
