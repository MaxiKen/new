## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:166

> Yet ˹if you are denied, O Prophet,˺ Allah bears witness to what He has sent down to you—He has sent it with His knowledge. The angels too bear witness. And Allah ˹alone˺ is sufficient as a Witness.

**Expanded Commentary**

**If You Are Denied, God Bears Witness to What He Sent Down**

The verse opens *lākini Allāhu yashhadu bi mā anzala ilayka* — yet, but, rather, God bears witness, testifies, to what He has sent down, revealed, to you. *Lākini* — yet, but — was explained as reply to *“humanity should have no excuse after messengers”* (4:165) where Almighty, All-Wise sends good news and warnings; if after that they deny you — *yet if you are denied, O Prophet* — God bears witness. *Yashhadu bi mā anzala ilayka* — bears witness to what He sent down to you — was explained as that the sūrah’s lineage — *We have sent revelation to you as We sent to Noah… Psalms to David* (4:163), messengers told and not told, Moses spoken to directly (4:164) — all sent as *mubashshirīn wa mundhirīn* so no excuse (4:165) — is witnessed by Him who sent: *“to Him belongs whatever is in heavens and earth, He is Fully Aware”* (4:126). *Anzala ilayka* — sent down to you — was read as the Qur’an sent with truth to judge between people by what He shows (4:105) and as the instruction regarding orphan women already recited (4:127) — all are *mā anzala ilayka*. The sūrah has warned *whoever disobeys, to Him belongs whatever is in heavens and earth, He is Self-Sufficient, Praiseworthy* (4:131) — to deny the One who bears witness is to deny the Owner to whom all belongs.

**He Sent It With His Knowledge — Angels Too Bear Witness**

The detail *anzalahu bi ʿilmihī* — He sent it down with His knowledge, by His knowledge, through His knowledge — was explained with *bi ʿilmihī* — with His knowledge — as that the Book’s content — limits, trusts, prayer at fixed time, settlement best, comprehensive faith accepting all messengers — is known by Him who knows what is in hearts (4:63) and is Fully Aware of what they do (4:108) and is All-Hearing, All-Seeing (4:134) — revelation is not arbitrary. *Wa al-malāʾikatu yashhadūn* — and the angels too bear witness, testify — was explained as the second witness: angels nearest to Him who would never be too proud to be servants (4:172), who guard, who raise Mount, who take souls asking *what were you doing?* (4:97), who stood witness at *“create you from single soul”* (4:1) — they witness to what He sent. Early reports linked this to Jews who denied the Prophet’s prophethood after demanding a book from heaven (4:153); the verse says angels witness to the Book sent to you as they witnessed the Mount raised (4:154). The sūrah has shown that those whose stories We told and did not tell are many — *“with Him are both rewards He hears and sees”* — angels witness to the told and not told.

**And God Alone Is Sufficient as Witness**

The seal *wa kafā bi Allāhi shahīdā* — and God alone is sufficient, enough, as Witness — was explained with *shahīd* — witness — the same *shahīd* as the Bringing of witness from every community (4:41) and Jesus as witness against them (4:159) — here the Supreme Witness suffices. *Kafā bi Allāhi shahīdā* was read as that where People of the Book demand a book from heaven as sign and hypocrites desire a way between distinguishing (4:150) and where night plotters hide from people but He is with them (4:108) — His witness suffices. The phrase was also the sūrah’s answer to *“with Him are both rewards, He is All-Hearing, All-Seeing”* (4:134): He who hears and sees suffices as Witness because witnessing is hearing and seeing. Where the sūrah says *“He is sufficient as Trustee”* (4:132) and *“sufficient as Guardian and Helper”* (4:45) — to be sufficient as Witness is to be the Trust upon whom judgment by Book (4:105) rests.

**A Verse That Follows No Excuse With Sufficient Witness**

Placed after *messengers as good news and warnings so humanity have no excuse after messengers, He is Almighty, All-Wise* (4:165), this verse follows no excuse with sufficient witness: yet if you are denied, God bears witness to what He sent to you — He sent it with His knowledge — angels too bear witness — He alone suffices as Witness. The sūrah has traced *sulṭān mubīn* — clear warrant — Moses was given (4:153), given over hostile turners (4:91), that you give against yourselves if you take allies instead of believers (4:144); witness is the *sulṭān* that makes warrant true. The sūrah that asks *“who is more truthful than God in speech?”* (4:122) — none — says His *shahādah* — witness — is most truthful. For the weak this is an opening: where you were told to verify reports through Messenger and authority (4:83) and to be sure before fighting (4:94) and to stand for justice though against yourselves (4:135), the witness that your verification stands is His witness and angels’ — sufficient, so that you need not be hanging between neither these nor those (4:143) waiting to see.

**The Knowledge With Which He Sent Explains the Law’s Detail**

The phrase *bi ʿilmihī* — with His knowledge — was explained to keep law’s detail intelligible: inheritance shares — * obligatory shares* (4:11–12), dowry graciously waived (4:4), *muḥsanāt* — chaste — marrying with permission and dowry where *shuḥḥ* is present (4:25), settlement best though selfishness present (4:128), not inclining entirely (4:129), enrichment from His bounty (4:130) — all are *bi ʿilmihī*, not guess. Where the sūrah says *“He wishes to make clear, guide to noble ways, turn in mercy, He is All-Knowing, All-Wise”* (4:26–28) — *ʿilmihī* is that clarity. The next verse will set the opposite witnesses: *those who disbelieve and hinder from God’s way have certainly gone far astray* (4:167) — those who deny the Sufficient Witness and hinder many, their straying is far because witness was sufficient and with knowledge.
