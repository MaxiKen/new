## Sūrah al-Ḥāqqah 69:41

> It is not the prose of a poet ˹as you claim˺, ˹yet˺ you hardly have any faith.

**Expanded Commentary**

**Not a Poet's Prose**

Wa-mā huwa bi-qawli shāʿir denies the poetry charge absolutely. Shāʿir composes from imagination, chasing rhymes wherever fancy wanders. Revelation descends from knowledge, commanding wherever guidance requires. Such denial separates two crafts permanently. Poetry imagines possibilities; revelation declares realities. Poets pursue beauty first, truth incidentally. Qur'an pursues truth first, beauty consequently. Meccan accusers mastered poetry's canons, memorizing odes and judging contests. They recognized instantly this speech breaks every mold. Meters stand unmeasured by Khalīl's scales. Rhymes serve meanings, never merely sounds. Content commands armies, legislates markets, resurrects dead. No qaṣīdah ever abolished usury or freed slaves. Poetry moves emotions temporarily; revelation moves history permanently. Verses toppled Persia and Byzantium within decades. Odes entertain courts; this Book built civilization. Readers must distinguish arts from revelation forever. What poets produce amuses; what prophets convey obligates. Never confuse vessels: poetry decorates life, revelation directs it. Denied poetry categorically; affirmed revelation certainly. Beauty shared, authority differed. Craft human; content divine.
