## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:37

> **And do not walk on the earth arrogantly. Surely you can neither crack the earth nor stretch to the height of the mountains.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Do Not Walk On Earth Arrogantly**

Wa-lā tamshi fī al-arḍi maraḥan — and do not walk on earth with arrogance, exultation, insolence. Wa-lā, and not, prohibition. Tamshi from m-sh-y, to walk, go, from mashā, to walk. Fī al-arḍi, on earth, in earth, from a-r-ḍ, earth. Maraḥan from m-r-ḥ, arrogantly, exultantly, insolently, with pride, boastful, from mariḥa, to be arrogant, exultant, boastful, from maraḥ, arrogance, exultation, insolence, boastfulness. Al-Ṭabarī explains that do not walk on earth arrogantly, exultantly, insolently, with pride, boastful, as arrogant walk, as mutraf elite who rebelled, as those who become extremely arrogant as Children of Isra'il did twice, as previous chapter said those who disbelieve in Hereafter have denying hearts and are arrogant. Al-Qurṭubī says maraḥan means arrogance, exultation, boastfulness, insolence, walking with pride, as if you are great.

**You Can Neither Crack Earth Nor Reach Mountains Height**

Innaka lan takhriqa al-arḍa wa-lan tablugha al-jibāla ṭūlan — indeed you will never crack earth nor reach mountains in height. Innaka, indeed you, you who walk arrogantly. Lan, never, will never, negation for future, emphatic never. Takhriqa from kh-r-q, to crack, pierce, tear, from kharaqa, to crack, pierce, tear, from same root as kharq, crack. Al-arḍa, earth. Wa-lan tablugha, and never reach, attain, from b-l-gh, to reach, attain, as yablugha maturity. Al-jibāla from j-b-l, mountains, plural of jabal, mountain. Ṭūlan from ṭ-w-l, height, tallness, length, from ṭāla, to be tall, long, from ṭūl, height, length. Al-Ṭabarī says you can neither crack earth with your arrogant walking, nor stretch, reach height of mountains, meaning you are weak, small, cannot crack earth nor reach mountains height, so why arrogance? Your arrogant walking cannot affect earth nor mountains, you are insignificant compared to them, so do not be arrogant. Al-Rāzī says this is to humble arrogant walker: you cannot crack earth nor reach mountains height, you are weak, small, so arrogance unfounded, foolish.

**Why Arrogance Prohibited After Knowledge And Measuring**

Verses 34-37: orphan wealth protected, pledges honoured accountable, give full measure even balance fairest best in end, do not follow without sure knowledge hearing sight intellect accountable, do not walk arrogantly on earth you cannot crack earth nor reach mountains height. The connection is humility vs arrogance: knowledge accountability and measuring justice require humility, not arrogance. Arrogance leads to following without knowledge, cheating measure, breaking pledges, consuming orphan wealth, killing sacred life, approaching adultery, killing children, being tight-fisted or wasteful, setting up other god, etc., all evils rooted in arrogance, as Children of Isra'il became extremely arrogant and corrupted twice, and as Devil refused to prostrate out of arrogance, and as those who disbelieve in Hereafter have denying hearts and are arrogant. Prohibition of arrogant walk is to teach humility, most upright.

**Link To Children Of Isra'il Extremely Arrogant And Mutraf Elite**

Verse 4 said Children of Isra'il will cause corruption twice and become extremely arrogant, very arrogant, ʿuluwwan kabīran, great arrogance. Verse 16 said elite, mutraf, affluent, rebelled. Verse 37 says do not walk arrogantly, you cannot crack earth nor reach mountains height. The link is that arrogance leads to corruption, rebellion, as Children of Isra'il extremely arrogant corrupted, elite arrogant rebelled, so do not be arrogant, be humble, as verse 24 lower wing of humility out of mercy for parents. Humility is most upright, arrogance is evil, detestable to Lord as verse 38 will say violation of these commandments detestable.

**Linguistic Note On Tamshi And Marah And Takhriqa And Jibal**

Tamshi from m-sh-y, to walk, from mashā. Arḍ from a-r-ḍ, earth. Maraḥ from m-r-ḥ, arrogance, exultation, insolence, boastfulness, from mariḥa. Innaka from a-n-n, indeed you. Lan, never, will never, emphatic negation. Takhriqa from kh-r-q, to crack, pierce, tear, from kharaqa. Arḍ from a-r-ḍ, earth. Tablugha from b-l-gh, to reach, attain, from balagha. Jibāl from j-b-l, mountains, plural of jabal. Ṭūl from ṭ-w-l, height, tallness, from ṭāla. The verse's structure — do not walk on earth arrogantly, indeed you will never crack earth nor reach mountains height — with prohibition of arrogant walk and reason you cannot crack earth nor reach mountains, shows futility of arrogance, you are weak, small, cannot affect earth nor mountains, so humility required.

**How This Verse Teaches Humility**

This verse teaches humility, not arrogance: do not walk on earth arrogantly, exultantly, insolently, boastfully, because you can neither crack earth nor stretch to height of mountains, you are weak, small, insignificant compared to earth and mountains, so why arrogance? Al-Qurṭubī says believer should be humble, not arrogant, walk humbly, not exultantly, as arrogance leads to corruption, rebellion, as Children of Isra'il extremely arrogant corrupted twice, as mutraf elite rebelled, as Devil arrogant refused to prostrate, as those who disbelieve in Hereafter arrogant. The practical lesson is to walk humbly, lower wing of humility, not only for parents but generally, as most upright guidance, wisdom, part of appreciated striving, mighty reward, avoiding painful punishment and detestable violation.

**A Reflection On Cracking Earth And Reaching Mountains**

Lan takhriqa al-arḍa wa-lan tablugha al-jibāla ṭūlan — you will never crack earth nor reach mountains in height — vivid image to humble arrogant: your arrogant stamping cannot crack earth, your stretching cannot reach mountains height, you are weak, small, earth vast, mountains tall, you cannot affect them, so arrogance foolish, unfounded. The believer who reflects will be humble, not walk arrogantly, will lower wing of humility out of mercy, will not become extremely arrogant like Children of Isra'il, will not be among mutraf elite who rebel, will not be like Devil arrogant, will be humble, as most upright, wisdom, leading to appreciated striving, mighty reward, and avoiding detestable violation that is detestable to Lord.
