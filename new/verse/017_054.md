## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:54

> **Your Lord knows you best. He may have mercy on you if He wills, or punish you if He wills. We have not sent you ˹O Prophet˺ as a keeper over them.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Your Lord Knows You Best**

Rabbukum aʿlamu bikum — your Lord knows you best, more knowing about you. Rabbukum, your Lord, from r-b-b. Aʿlamu, knows best, more knowing, from ʿ-l-m, to know, as aʿlamu knows best, as verse 25 Lord knows best what is within selves, and verse 47 We know best how they listen, and verse 55 Lord knows best those in heavens and earth. Bikum, with you, about you, you all. Al-Ṭabarī explains that your Lord knows you best, knows your states, whether you deserve mercy or punishment, whether you will believe or disbelieve, as He is All-Aware All-Seeing of servants, knows best, as He knows what is within selves, knows best how they listen, etc. Al-Qurṭubī says rabbukum aʿlamu bikum means Lord knows you best, knows your inner, outer, whether you will be guided or astray, whether mercy or punishment appropriate.

**He May Have Mercy If He Wills Or Punish If He Wills**

In yasha' yarḥamkum aw in yasha' yuʿadhdhibkum — if He wills He will have mercy on you or if He wills He will punish you. In, if. Yasha' from sh-y-ʾ, to will, wish, from shā'a, to will. Yarḥamkum from r-ḥ-m, to have mercy, mercy, from raḥima, to have mercy, as raḥmah mercy. Aw, or. In yasha', if He wills. Yuʿadhdhibkum from ʿ-dh-b, to punish, torment, from ʿadhdhaba, to punish, as ʿadhāb punishment, painful punishment. Al-Ṭabarī explains that He may have mercy on you if He wills, if you repent, believe, follow most upright, or punish you if He wills, if you persist in disbelief, disbelief in Hereafter, outrageous claims, etc., as mercy and punishment by will, with wisdom, knowledge, as He knows you best, All-Aware All-Seeing, Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, but also severe. Al-Rāzī says in yasha' yarḥamkum aw in yasha' yuʿadhdhibkum indicates will, not arbitrary, but based on knowledge, wisdom, as He knows best, and mercy and punishment by will, as test.

**We Have Not Sent You As Keeper Over Them**

Wa-mā arsalnāka ʿalayhim wakīlan — and We have not sent you over them as keeper, guardian, disposer. Wa-mā, and not. Arsalnāka from r-s-l, to send, dispatch, as rasūl messenger, We sent you, O Prophet. ʿAlayhim, over them, upon them, over disbelievers, polytheists. Wakīlan from w-k-l, keeper, guardian, disposer, one entrusted, from wakala, to entrust, from wakīl, keeper, guardian, trustee, disposer of affairs, as Trustee of Affairs in verse 2. Al-Ṭabarī explains that We have not sent you, O Prophet, as keeper over them, guardian, to compel them to believe, to be responsible for their guidance, as guidance is from Allah, not from you, you are deliverer of good news and warner, as verse 105 says We sent you only as deliverer of good news and warner, not keeper, guardian. Al-Qurṭubī says wakīlan means keeper, guardian, disposer, one who disposes affairs, responsible, as Prophet not keeper over them to compel, guide, but warner, deliverer.

**Why Lord Knows Best And Mercy Punishment By Will And Not Keeper**

Why Lord knows best, mercy punishment by will, not keeper? Because disbelievers have barrier, veils, deafness, aversion, say bewitched man, call names, far astray cannot find Right Way, deny resurrection bones and ashes new creation, stones, iron, whatever harder, shake heads when, perhaps soon, Day call, etc., and Prophet tells servants to say best to prevent Satan discord, Satan sworn enemy, so Lord knows best, mercy punishment by will, Prophet not keeper over them, not responsible for their guidance, as guidance from Allah, He knows best, may have mercy if wills, punish if wills, as All-Aware All-Seeing, Most Forbearing All-Forgiving.

**Connection To Lord Knows Best What Is Within Selves And All-Aware All-Seeing**

Verse 25 Lord knows best what is within selves, if righteous All-Forgiving to constantly turning, verse 30 Lord gives abundant or limited provisions, All-Aware All-Seeing of servants, verse 17 sufficient Lord as All-Aware All-Seeing of sins, verse 47 We know best how they listen, verse 54 Lord knows you best, may have mercy or punish if wills, not sent you as keeper, verse 55 Lord knows best those in heavens and earth, favoured some prophets above others, to David gave Psalms. The connection is knowledge theme: Lord knows best what is within selves, how they listen, you best, those in heavens and earth, All-Aware All-Seeing, so mercy punishment by will based on knowledge, not arbitrary, and Prophet not keeper, as knowledge and guidance from Allah.

**Link To Prophet As Warmer Not Keeper And Mercy Punishment**

The sūrah's theme of Prophet as warner, deliverer of good news and warner, not keeper, appears in verse 54 and 105, indicating role: deliver good news to believers who do good mighty reward, warn those who do not believe painful punishment, but not keeper, guardian, to compel, as guidance from Allah, Lord knows best, may have mercy or punish if wills, as will, wisdom.

**Linguistic Note On A'lamu Bikum And Yarhamkum And Yu'adhdhibkum And Wakilan**

Rabb from r-b-b, Lord. Aʿlam from ʿ-l-m, knows best, more knowing. Bikum, with you, about you. Yasha' from sh-y-ʾ, to will. Yarḥam from r-ḥ-m, to have mercy. Yuʿadhdhib from ʿ-dh-b, to punish. Arsalnā from r-s-l, to send. Wakīl from w-k-l, keeper, guardian, trustee, disposer, from wakala. The verse's structure — your Lord knows you best, if He wills He will have mercy on you or if He wills He will punish you, and We have not sent you over them as keeper — with knowledge best, mercy punishment by will, and not sent as keeper, shows that Lord knows best, mercy punishment by will based on knowledge, and Prophet not keeper over them to compel, but warner, deliverer.

**How This Verse Teaches Lord Knows Best And Mercy Punishment By Will**

This verse teaches that Lord knows you best, knows your states, inner, outer, whether mercy or punishment appropriate, may have mercy if wills, if you repent, believe, follow most upright, or punish if wills, if persist in disbelief, disbelief in Hereafter, outrageous claims, etc., as will, wisdom, knowledge, All-Aware All-Seeing, Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, but also severe, and Prophet not sent as keeper over them, guardian, to compel, guide, as guidance from Allah, not from Prophet, Prophet is deliverer of good news and warner, not keeper. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should know Lord knows best, may have mercy if wills, punish if wills, so seek mercy via repentance, belief, most upright, and know Prophet not keeper over disbelievers to compel, but warner, deliverer, so follow guidance, not rely on Prophet to compel, as guidance from Allah.

**A Reflection On Knowing Best And Mercy And Punishment**

Rabbukum aʿlamu bikum — your Lord knows you best — is comforting and warning: comforting that Lord knows you best, knows your struggles, intentions, whether righteous, awwāb, constantly turning, and warning that He knows best whether mercy or punishment appropriate, may have mercy if wills, punish if wills, as will, wisdom, knowledge, All-Aware All-Seeing. Wa-mā arsalnāka ʿalayhim wakīlan — We have not sent you as keeper over them — indicates Prophet's role limited to delivering good news and warning, not keeper, guardian, to compel, as guidance from Allah, Lord knows best, may have mercy or punish, as will. The believer who reflects will seek mercy via being righteous, awwāb, constantly turning, following most upright guidance, wisdom, that Qur'an guides to, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, and avoid punishment, painful punishment, Hell, by believing, not disbelieving in Hereafter, not making outrageous claims, not having barrier, veils, deafness, aversion.
