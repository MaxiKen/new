## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:77

> **˹This has been˺ Our way with the messengers We sent before you. And you will never find any change in Our way.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Our Way With Messengers Sent Before You**

Sunnata man qad arsalnā qablaka min rusulinā — way, practice, law of those We sent before you of Our messengers. Sunnata from s-n-n, way, practice, law, tradition, established way, from sunnah, way, practice, law, tradition. Man qad arsalnā, whom We sent, those We sent, from r-s-l, to send, as arsalnā We sent, as rusul messengers, as We sent before you messengers. Qablaka, before you, before you, O Prophet, before you, as before you, as messengers sent before you, O Prophet, as previous messengers. Min rusulinā, from Our messengers, of Our messengers, from r-s-l, messengers, plural of rasūl, messenger, as rusul, messengers, Our messengers, as messengers sent before you, as Our way with messengers. Al-Ṭabarī explains that this has been Our way, practice, law, tradition, with messengers We sent before you, O Prophet, meaning divine law, established way, practice with messengers sent before you, as disbelievers tried to lure away, tempt, hoping attribute falsely, taken as close friend, tried to intimidate to drive out of land, etc., but Our way, practice, law, is steadfast, no change, as messengers made steadfast, not lured, not inclined, not intimidated, etc., and people who drove out messengers destroyed after little while, etc., as divine law, as every wicked society destroyed or punished severely before Day written in Record, etc.

**You Will Never Find Any Change In Our Way**

Wa-lā tajidu li-sunnatinā taḥwīlan — and you will not find for Our way any change, transformation, alteration. Wa-lā, and not, you will never find. Tajidu from w-j-d, to find, you will find, you will never find. Li-sunnatinā, for Our way, for Our practice, law, tradition, from s-n-n, way, practice, law, as sunnatinā Our way, practice, law, tradition, established way. Taḥwīlan from ḥ-w-l, change, transformation, alteration, from ḥawwala, to change, transform, alter, from taḥwīl, change, transformation, as change, transformation, alteration, as no change, no transformation, no alteration in Our way, practice, law, as divine law consistent, no change, as sunnah, way, practice, law, established, consistent, no change. Al-Ṭabarī explains that you will never find any change in Our way, practice, law, meaning Our way, practice, law with messengers is consistent, established, no change, no transformation, no alteration, as divine law, sunnah, way, practice, law, with messengers, as messengers sent before, disbelievers tried to lure away, intimidate to drive out, but Our way is steadfast, people destroyed after little while if drove out, etc., as divine law, no change, you will never find any change, transformation, alteration.

**Why Our Way With Messengers No Change**

Why Our way with messengers sent before you, no change in Our way? Because divine law, sunnah, way, practice, law with messengers is consistent, established, no change, no transformation, alteration, as messengers sent before, disbelievers tried to lure away, tempt, hoping attribute falsely, taken as close friend, tried to intimidate to drive out of land, etc., but Our way, practice, law, is that messengers made steadfast, firm, stable, not lured, not inclined, not intimidated, etc., and people who drove out messengers, intimidated to drive out, do not remain, survive after them, behind them, except little while, short time, then destroyed, punished severely, as every wicked society destroyed or punished severely before Day written in Record, many generations destroyed after Noah, etc., and as double punishment would have been if Prophet inclined, etc., but made steadfast, so no double punishment, no helper needed, as Our way, divine law, consistent, no change, you will never find any change.

**Connection To Lure Away And Steadfast And Double Punishment And Intimidate To Drive Out**

Verses 73-76 they were about to lure you away from what We revealed hoping attribute something else falsely and taken as close friend, had We not made you steadfast you would have inclined a little, then double punishment in life and death, no helper against Us, they were about to intimidate you to drive you out of land of Mecca, but then they would not have survived after you left except little while. Verse 77 this has been Our way with messengers We sent before you, you will never find any change in Our way, showing that luring away, steadfast, double punishment warning, intimidating to drive out, not surviving except little while, are part of Our way with messengers sent before you, divine law, sunnah, way, practice, law, consistent, no change, as messengers sent before, disbelievers tried to lure away, intimidate to drive out, but Our way is steadfast, no change, people destroyed after little while if drove out, etc., as divine law.

**Link To Prayer From Decline Of Sun Until Darkness And Dawn Prayer Witnessed And Night Prayer Additional Station Of Praise**

Next verses 78-79 observe prayer from decline of sun until darkness of night and dawn prayer, certainly dawn prayer witnessed by angels, and rise at last part of night offering additional prayers, so Lord may raise you to station of praise, showing that Our way with messengers includes prayer, etc., as part of way, practice, law, with messengers, no change, as prayer, etc., as messengers observed prayer, etc., as part of Our way, divine law, consistent, no change, you will never find any change in Our way, as prayer, etc.

**Linguistic Note On Sunnata And Arsalna And Tajidu And Tahwilan**

Sunnah from s-n-n, way, practice, law, tradition, established way, from sanna, to establish way, practice. Arsalnā from r-s-l, to send, dispatch, as rasūl messenger, We sent. Qablaka, before you, from q-b-l, before. Rusul from r-s-l, messengers, plural of rasūl. Tajidu from w-j-d, to find. Sunnatinā, Our way, practice, law, from s-n-n. Taḥwīl from ḥ-w-l, change, transformation, alteration, from ḥawwala, to change, transform.
