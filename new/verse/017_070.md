## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:70

> **Indeed, We have dignified the children of Adam, carried them on land and sea, granted them good and lawful provisions, and privileged them far above many of Our creatures.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**We Dignified Children Of Adam**

Wa-la-qad karramnā banī Ādama — and We have surely dignified, honoured children of Adam, sons of Adam. Wa-la-qad, and surely, indeed, certainly, emphasis, as la-qad surely. Karramnā from k-r-m, to dignify, honour, make noble, generous, from karuma, to be noble, generous, from karramnā We dignified, honoured, as karramta You honoured above me, Iblis said do you see this one you honoured above me, and karramnā We dignified children of Adam, showing honour for Adam, children, as angels prostrated, Iblis refused out of arrogance, but children of Adam dignified, honoured, as honour, nobility. Banī Ādama from b-n-y, children, sons, offspring, and Ādam, Adam, first human, as children of Adam, sons of Adam, all humankind, descendants of Adam, as progeny of those carried with Noah, etc., as children of Adam, all humans. Al-Ṭabarī explains that We have surely dignified, honoured children of Adam, sons of Adam, all humankind, made noble, generous, honoured, with many honours, as intellect, speech, etc., as dignified, honoured, above many creatures. Al-Qurṭubī says karramnā banī Ādama means We dignified, honoured children of Adam with many honours: intellect, speech, writing, etc., made noble, honoured, above many creatures, privileged.

**Carried Them On Land And Sea**

Wa-ḥamalnāhum fī al-barri wa-al-baḥri — and We carried them in land and sea, on land and sea. Wa-ḥamalnāhum from ḥ-m-l, to carry, bear, transport, from ḥamala, to carry, bear, as ḥamalnāhum We carried them, transported them, as carrying on land and sea, as ships through sea, as animals on land, etc. Fī al-barri wa-al-baḥri, in land and sea, on land and sea, in land and sea, as barr land, shore, and baḥr sea, as land and sea, as Lord steers ships for you through sea so you may seek bounty, ever Merciful, as verse 66 says, and as We carried them on land and sea, as part of dignifying, honouring, as carrying, transporting, as honour, as privilege, as He subjected for your benefit night and day, sun and moon, etc., and land and sea, etc. Al-Ṭabarī explains that We carried them on land and sea, transported them, on land via animals, vehicles, and on sea via ships, etc., as part of dignifying, honouring, as carrying, as privilege, as He steers ships for you through sea, etc.

**Granted Good And Lawful Provisions**

Wa-razaqnāhum mina al-ṭayyibāti — and We provided them from good, pure, lawful provisions, good things. Wa-razaqnāhum from r-z-q, to provide, provision, sustenance, as narzuquhum We provide for them, as yabsuṭu al-rizqa extends provision, gives abundant or limited, as provision, and razaqnāhum We provided them, granted provisions. Mina al-ṭayyibāti from ṭ-y-b, good, pure, lawful, wholesome, from ṭayyib, good, pure, lawful, wholesome, plural ṭayyibāt, good, pure, lawful things, provisions, as good and lawful provisions, wholesome, pure, lawful, as provision good, pure, lawful, not impure, unlawful, as bounty never withheld, some favoured over others, provision abundant or limited, but good provisions granted. Al-Ṭabarī explains that We granted them good and lawful provisions, good things, pure, lawful, wholesome provisions, as food, drink, etc., good, pure, lawful, as provision, as part of dignifying, honouring, as granting good provisions, as bounty, as mercy, ever Merciful.

**Privileged Far Above Many Creatures**

Wa-faḍḍalnāhum ʿalā kathīrin mimman khalaqnā tafḍīlan — and We privileged them, favoured them over many of those We created with great favour, privilege. Wa-faḍḍalnāhum from f-ḍ-l, to favour, privilege, prefer, give superiority, as faḍḍalnā some over others, favoured some over others, and faḍḍalnāhum We privileged them, favoured them, privileged. ʿAlā kathīrin, over many, above many, from k-th-r, many, much. Mimman khalaqnā, from those We created, of those We created, from m-n and kh-l-q, those We created, creatures, creation, as many creatures. Tafḍīlan from f-ḍ-l, with great favour, privilege, great favouring, privileging, with emphasis, great favour, privilege, from tafḍīl, favouring, privileging, as akbaru tafḍīlan greater in favour, and tafḍīlan great favour, privilege, as privileged far above many creatures with great favour. Al-Ṭabarī explains that We privileged them, favoured them far above many of Our creatures, many of those We created, with great favour, privilege, great favouring, meaning children of Adam privileged, favoured far above many creatures, many of creation, with great favour, privilege, as honour, dignity, as part of dignifying, carrying, provisioning, privileging, as children of Adam privileged far above many creatures, as honour, as He favoured some over others, some prophets above others, etc., but children of Adam privileged far above many.

**Why Dignified, Carried, Provided, Privileged**

Why dignified children of Adam, carried on land and sea, granted good provisions, privileged far above many creatures? Because Adam honoured, angels prostrated, Iblis refused out of arrogance, but children of Adam dignified, honoured, despite Iblis vow to take hold of descendants except few, and despite ungratefulness, ever ungrateful, turning away at shore, etc., and despite warnings land swallow, storm stones, no guardian, sea return violent storm drowning, no avenger, etc., children of Adam dignified, honoured, carried, provided, privileged, as mercy, ever Merciful, as Lord steers ships for you through sea to seek bounty, ever Merciful, as He is Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, but torment fearsome, and as part of favouring some over others, but Hereafter far greater in rank and favour, so dignified in world but Hereafter greater.

**Connection To Lord Steers Ships Ever Merciful And Forget Gods At Sea Turn Away Ungrateful And Warnings**

Verse 66 Lord steers ships for you through sea so you may seek bounty, ever Merciful. Verse 67 hardship at sea forget all gods except Him, delivers to shore turn away, humankind ever ungrateful. Verse 68-69 do you feel secure land swallow or storm stones, no guardian, or sea return violent storm drowning for denial, no avenger, warnings after ungratefulness. Verse 70 We dignified children of Adam, carried them on land and sea, granted good provisions, privileged far above many creatures, showing that despite warnings, ungratefulness, children of Adam dignified, honoured, carried on land and sea, granted good provisions, privileged far above many creatures, as mercy, ever Merciful, as Lord steers ships, etc., as part of dignifying, honouring, privileging, as He favoured some over others, etc., but Hereafter far greater.

**Link To See How We Favoured Some Over Others And Hereafter Greater And Favoured Prophets**

Verse 21 see how We favoured some over others in this life but Hereafter far greater in rank and favour. Verse 55 Lord knows best those in heavens and earth, favoured some prophets above others, to David gave Psalms. Verse 70 We dignified children of Adam, carried on land and sea, granted good provisions, privileged far above many creatures with great favour, showing favouring theme: some favoured over others in world, prophets favoured, children of Adam privileged far above many creatures, Hereafter far greater in rank and favour, so favouring in world minor compared to Hereafter greater.

**Linguistic Note On Karramna And Hamalnahum And Barr And Bahr And Razaqnahum And Tayyibat And Faddalnahum And Tafdilan**

Karramnā from k-r-m, to dignify, honour, make noble, from karuma, to be noble. Banī from b-n-y, children, sons, from ibn, son. Ādam, Adam. Ḥamalnā from ḥ-m-l, to carry, bear, transport, from ḥamala. Barr from b-r-r, land, shore, from barr. Baḥr from b-ḥ-r, sea, from baḥr. Razaqnā from r-z-q, to provide, provision, from razaqa. Ṭayyibāt from ṭ-y-b, good, pure, lawful, wholesome, plural of ṭayyib, from ṭāba, to be good, pure. Faḍḍalnā from f-ḍ-l, to favour, privilege, prefer, from faḍala. Kathīr from k-th-r, many, much. Khalqnā from kh-l-q, We created, from khalaqa. Tafḍīl from f-ḍ-l, favouring, privileging, with great favour, from faḍḍala. The verse's structure — We dignified children of Adam and carried them in land and sea and provided them from good things and privileged them over many of those We created with great favour — with four honours, dignified, carried, provided good, privileged far above many with great favour, shows comprehensive honour for children of Adam, dignified, honoured, privileged, as mercy, ever Merciful, as part of favouring, but Hereafter far greater.

**How This Verse Teaches Dignity And Honour Of Children Of Adam**

This verse teaches dignity and honour of children of Adam: We have surely dignified, honoured children of Adam, all humankind, made noble, generous, honoured, with intellect, speech, etc., carried them on land and sea, transported them, on land via animals, vehicles, on sea via ships, so they may seek bounty, ever Merciful, granted them good and lawful provisions, good, pure, lawful, wholesome provisions, and privileged them far above many of Our creatures, many of those We created, with great favour, privilege, great favouring, as honour, dignity, privilege, as part of favouring some over others, but Hereafter far greater in rank and favour, so dignified in world but Hereafter greater, far greater. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should know children of Adam dignified, honoured, carried on land and sea, granted good provisions, privileged far above many creatures, with great favour, as mercy, ever Merciful, so be grateful, not ever ungrateful, not turn away after being delivered, not feel secure land swallow or storm stones, sea return violent storm, but be grateful, follow most upright guidance, wisdom, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour in Hereafter, which is far greater than worldly dignity.

**A Reflection On Privileged Far Above Many Creatures With Great Favour**

Faḍḍalnāhum ʿalā kathīrin mimman khalaqnā tafḍīlan — privileged them over many of those We created with great favour, great privileging — indicates that children of Adam privileged, favoured far above many creatures, many of creation, with great favour, privilege, tremendous favour, not small, but great, tafḍīlan, great favour, as He favoured some over others in world, some prophets above others, to David gave Psalms, etc., but children of Adam privileged far above many creatures with great favour, as honour, dignity, as part of dignifying, carrying, provisioning, privileging, as mercy, ever Merciful, Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, but torment fearsome for those who are ungrateful, turn away, feel secure land swallow, storm stones, sea return violent storm, etc., and as Hereafter far greater in rank and favour, far greater than worldly privileging, so desire Hereafter, strive accordingly, be believer, appreciated striving, mighty reward, far greater rank and favour.
