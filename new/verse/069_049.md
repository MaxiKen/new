## Sūrah al-Ḥāqqah 69:49

> And We certainly know that some of you will persist in denial,

**Expanded Commentary**

**Divine Foreknowledge**

Wa-innā la-naʿlamu asserts: We know. Knowledge certain: lām emphasizes. Such knowing encompasses. Deniers identified: persons known. Hearts exposed: motives seen. Futures foreseen: persistence predicted. Knowledge complete: nothing hidden. Past recorded fully. Present witnessed totally. Future known certainly. Denial predicted specifically. Persons unnamed publicly. Mercy veils identities. Warnings issued generally. Doors open universally. Knowledge never published: individuals unwarned-by-name. Hope remains: predictions conditional-upon-persistence. Readers must revere knowledge. Hidden exposed eventually. Secrets published finally. Hearts weighed accurately. Motives judged precisely. Privacy respected temporally. Accountability enforced eternally. What knowing encompasses, wisdom veils. Known certainly; veiled mercifully. Veiled identities protect. Exposed sinners despaired. Despair kills repentance. Repentance requires hope. Hope sustained: veils. Veils lifted gradually. Gradual exposure merciful. Death unveils partially. Graves reveal narrowly. Resurrection exposes fully. Full exposure just. Justice satisfied perfectly. Mercy precedes wrath. Wrath follows persistence. Persistence defined: dying denial. Denial lifetime: sealed. Sealed hearts darkened. Darkened eyes blinded. Blinded wander lost. Lost eternally: warned. Warned repeatedly: messengers. Messengers rejected: rebels. Rebels regretting: eventually. Eventually irreversible: doom. Doomed justly: warned.

Harvest gathered: angels. Angels reaping: commanded. Commanded precisely: ordered. Ordered ranks: assembled. Assembled nations: awaiting. Awaiting verdicts: trembling. Trembling multitudes: silent. Silent dread: pervasive. Pervasive awe: humbling. Humbling majesty: unveiled. Unveiled glory: prostrated. Prostrated creation: subdued. Subdued rebels: forced. Forced kneeling: worthless. Worthless late: faith. Faith timely: valued. Valued early: believers. Believers kneeling: gladly. Gladly worshiping: love. Love motivating: service. Service rendered: joyfully. Giving perpetual: Generous. Generous Lord: praised. Praised endlessly: grateful. Grateful returners: devoted. Devoted converts: zealous. Zealous workers: tireless. Tireless striving: rewarded. Rewarded abundantly: gardens. Gardens welcome: returners. Returners feast: eternally. Eternally grateful: praising. Praising return: completed. Completed circles: mercy. Mercy triumphs: judgment.
