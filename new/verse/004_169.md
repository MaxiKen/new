## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:169

> except that of Hell, to stay there for ever and ever. And that is easy for Allah.

### Expanded Commentary

**The path of Hell and divine capability**

The path mentioned is Hell, where the persistent deniers remain, and the verse says that this is easy for Allah. The ease does not imply triviality or caprice. It means that no power can prevent the execution of a judgment grounded in perfect knowledge and justice.

The repeated warning of permanence should awaken urgency. It is not intended to make people despair before death or to encourage believers to play judge. It makes clear that the Hereafter is not a temporary inconvenience that can be negotiated through worldly status.

Allah’s capability also supports the oppressed. If earthly institutions fail to punish a persecutor or restore a stolen right, divine justice is not limited by those failures. The believer seeks justice now while trusting the final court.

**Final justice beyond institutions**

The ease with which Allah can execute judgment comforts those who see injustice go unpunished and restrains those who exploit a weak court. It does not excuse believers from building just institutions. The final account is a guarantee that earthly effort matters even when earthly results are incomplete.
