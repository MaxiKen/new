## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:169

> except that of Hell, to stay there for ever and ever. And that is easy for Allah.

**Expanded Commentary**

**Except That of Hell — To Stay There Forever**

The verse completes *illā ṭarīqa Jahannam khālidīna fīhā abadan* — except the way, the path of Hell, staying, abiding, in it forever and ever. *Illā ṭarīqa Jahannam* — except the path of Hell — was explained as the only *ṭarīq* — way — to which those who disbelieve and wrong — not forgiven, not guided to any path (4:168) — will be guided: Hell’s way. *Ṭarīq* — path, way — was read as *sabīl* — the sūrah’s path language — noble ways He wishes to make clear (4:26), believers’ path not to be left (4:115), associations far straying (4:116), disbelief plus hindering far astray (4:167) — now the *ṭarīq* guided to is Hell’s, as *“whoever defies the Messenger and follows other than believers’ path We will let pursue what he chose then burn, evil destination”* (4:115) — burning is that path. *Khālidīna fīhā abadan* — staying therein forever and ever — was explained with double emphasis — *khālidīn* and *abadan* — the same double for Gardens for those who believe and do good (4:122) — now for Hell’s path where *“they will have Hell as home and find no escape”* (4:121) — no *maḥīṣ*. The sūrah has shown that *“they are true disbelievers, humiliating punishment prepared for disbelievers”* (4:151) — humiliating is Hell’s path; and *“lowest depth of Fire, you will never find helper”* (4:145) — lowest depth is that path’s place. *Abadan* — forever ever — was linked to the sūrah’s promise where Gardens forever (4:122) is for belief and good, here forever for disbelief and wrong — the only *ṭarīq* left is lasting.

**And That Is Easy for God**

The seal *wa kāna dhālika ʿalā Allāhi yasīrā* — and that — guiding to Hell’s path and abiding forever — is easy, light, facile for God, upon Him easy — was explained with *yasīr* — easy, light, not burdensome — the same *yasīr* as *“whoever does this unjustly We will burn in Fire, and that is easy for God”* (4:30) where devouring wealth unjustly and killing yourselves though Merciful was threatened with Fire easy for Him. *ʿAlā Allāhi yasīrā* — easy for God, upon Him easy — was read as that His power to do is not constrained, as He can remove humanity and bring others (4:133) — He is Most Capable to do so — here He can guide to Hell’s path — easy. The sūrah has shown that He is *ʿAzīz Ḥakīm* — Almighty, All-Wise — who raises Jesus (4:158) and sends messengers so no excuse remains (4:165) and bears witness with His knowledge (4:166) — His *ʿizzah* makes Hell’s path easy where He is denied. The phrase was linked to *“Gardens forever, promise ever true”* (4:122) — both Gardens and Hell forever are with Him whose awareness encompasses everything (4:126) — both are easy as promise true and threat true.

**A Verse That Completes Neither Forgiven Nor Guided**

Placed after *those who disbelieve and wrong — He will neither forgive nor guide them to any path* (4:168), this verse completes neither forgiven nor guided with except Hell’s path forever, and that is easy for Him. The litany from demand for a book from heaven (4:153) through thunderbolt, calf, pardon, Mount raised, firm covenant, breaking, rejecting signs, killing prophets, claiming *ghulf* sealed, denying, slander against Mary, boasting killing Messiah not killed but made to appear, raised, belief before death, forbidding *ṭayyibāt* for *ẓulm* and hindering, taking interest though forbidden, consuming unjustly, painful punishment for disbelievers among them, but grounded among them to great reward, then lineage, messengers told and not, directly spoken, good news and warnings so no excuse, sufficient witness, those who disbelieve and hinder far astray, disbelieve and wrong not forgiven nor guided — now except Hell’s path forever easy — shows that excuse removed and witness sufficient leaves Hell’s path as the only remaining for persisters. For the weak this is an anchor: where helpless hoped to be pardoned (4:99) were not among those who disbelieve and wrong but among those who cannot find *ḥīla* or *sabīl* — Hell’s path is not for helpless but for hinderers and wrongers — the easy for Him is not arbitrary but measure.

**The Only *Ṭarīq* Left Is Hell’s**

The word *illā* — except — was explained to keep Hell’s path as the only *ṭarīq* left where any other *ṭarīq* would be mercy and guidance; to be guided to Hell’s path is not guidance as *hidāyah* to mercy but as *tawlīyah* — letting pursue what he chose (4:115) — the sūrah’s leaving to stray (4:88, 4:143). The sūrah has shown that *“with Him are both rewards He hears and sees”* (4:134) — Hell’s path forever is the other reward’s opposite; Gardens true (4:122) is the *hidāyah* path, Hell forever (4:169) is the allowed stray path. The verse generalises the sūrah’s *yasīr* — easy — where *“burn in Fire, that is easy for God”* (4:30) — devouring wealth is cousin to consuming people’s wealth unjustly and taking *ribā* (4:161) — all are *yasīr* to punish as all are *muḥīṭ* to know. The next passage will turn from history’s litigants to humanity: O humanity, the Messenger has come with truth from your Lord, so believe for your own good, if you disbelieve then to Him belongs whatever is in heavens and earth, He is All-Knowing, All-Wise (4:170) — belief’s call where Hell’s path easy is alternative.

**A Short Verse That Anchors Easy Threat After Far Straying**

Though only *“except that of Hell to stay for ever and ever, and that is easy for God,”* the verse anchors easy threat after far straying declared (4:167) and neither forgiven nor guided (4:168). The sūrah that warns *do not go to extremes regarding faith* (4:171) and declares *Trinity, stop* — *God is only One God, far above having a son* — sees the Trinity boast as part of disbelief and hindering far astray whose path is Hell forever easy; the correction follows. The next verses will address humanity and People of the Book directly: the Messenger has come with truth (4:170), do not go to extremes regarding faith (4:171), Messiah not too proud to be servant nor angels nearest (4:172) — all are the alternative path — belief and good, mercy and grace, straight path (4:174–175) — versus Hell’s easy.
