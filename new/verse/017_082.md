## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:82

> **We send down the Quran as a healing and mercy for the believers, but it only increases the wrongdoers in loss.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**We Send Down Quran As Healing And Mercy For Believers**

Wa-nunazzilu mina al-Qur'āni mā huwa shifā'un wa-raḥmatun li-l-mu'minīna — and We send down from Qur'an what is healing and mercy for believers. Wa-nunazzilu from n-z-l, to send down, reveal, gradually, as nunazzilu We send down, reveal, as We send down Quran, as Quran revealed in stages, successive revelations, as verse 106 Quran revealed in stages so you may recite to people at deliberate pace, sent down successive revelations, as nunazzilu send down, reveal. Mina al-Qur'āni, from Qur'an, from Qur'an, part of Qur'an, what is, as from Qur'an, as Qur'an, as healing and mercy. Mā huwa, what it is, what is, that which is. Shifā'un from sh-f-y, healing, cure, from shafā, to heal, cure, from shifā', healing, cure, as healing, cure for hearts, etc. Wa-raḥmatun from r-ḥ-m, mercy, as raḥmah mercy, as healing and mercy, as Qur'an healing and mercy for believers, as mercy, as ever Merciful, as Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, etc. Li-l-mu'minīna from a-m-n, for believers, for those who believe, as mu'minīn believers, as believers, as believers who do good mighty reward, etc. Al-Ṭabarī explains that We send down Quran as healing and mercy for believers, meaning Qur'an, revelation, is healing, cure, for hearts, from ignorance, doubt, etc., and mercy, mercy for believers, as guidance, most upright, wisdom, etc., as healing and mercy for believers, as believers benefit, as Qur'an guides to most upright, gives good news to believers who do good mighty reward, etc., as healing and mercy.

**But It Only Increases Wrongdoers In Loss**

Wa-lā yazīdu al-ẓālimīna illā khasāran — and it does not increase wrongdoers except in loss, ruin. Wa-lā, and not, but. Yazīdu from z-y-d, to increase, add, as yazīdu increases, as previous yazīduhum increases them aversion, defiance, etc., and lā yazīdu does not increase, but increases wrongdoers except loss. Al-ẓālimīna from ẓ-l-m, wrongdoers, oppressors, unjust, from ẓālim, wrongdoer, oppressor, as ẓālimīn wrongdoers, as wrongdoers, as disbelievers, polytheists, etc., as wrongdoers. Illā khasāran from kh-s-r, loss, ruin, perdition, from khasira, to lose, be in loss, from khasār, loss, ruin, as loss, ruin, perdition, as wrongdoers increase in loss, ruin, as Qur'an increases wrongdoers in loss, as they have barrier, veils, deafness, aversion, far astray cannot find Right Way, deny resurrection, etc., and varying signs drives farther away, nufūran, aversion, warning increases greatly in defiance, ṭughyānan kabīran, great defiance, and Qur'an increases wrongdoers in loss, as loss.

**Why Healing Mercy For Believers But Loss For Wrongdoers**

Why Qur'an healing and mercy for believers but only increases wrongdoers in loss? Because Qur'an guides to most upright, explains everything in detail, varies signs so they may be mindful, but it only drives them farther away for disbelievers, nufūran, aversion, as they have hidden barrier, veils over hearts unable to comprehend, deafness in ears, turning backs in aversion when Lord alone mentioned, saying bewitched man, calling names, far astray cannot find Right Way, denying resurrection bones and ashes new creation, etc., and human ever hasty, ever ungrateful, etc., and Satan has no authority over faithful servants, sufficient Lord as Guardian, but wrongdoers follow Satan, etc., and truth has come and falsehood vanished, indeed falsehood bound to vanish, as truth vs falsehood, and Qur'an healing and mercy for believers, as truth, healing, mercy, but wrongdoers increase in loss, as falsehood, loss, as they disbelieve, deny, etc.

**Connection To Truth Come Falsehood Vanish And Human Response To Favours And Hardship**

Verse 81 truth has come and falsehood vanished, indeed falsehood bound to vanish, truth vs falsehood. Verse 82 We send down Quran as healing and mercy for believers but only increases wrongdoers in loss, showing truth, Qur'an, healing mercy for believers, vs falsehood, wrongdoers loss, as truth vs falsehood, healing mercy vs loss, as Qur'an guides to most upright, gives good news to believers mighty reward, warns disbelievers painful punishment, as healing mercy vs loss. The next verse describes human nature when favoured turning away with arrogance and when hardship touches despairing, showing ever ungrateful nature, ever hasty, etc., and as truth come falsehood vanish, healing mercy vs loss, etc.

**Link To Healing And Mercy And Ever Merciful And Most Forbearing All-Forgiving**

Qur'an healing and mercy for believers, as healing, cure for hearts, mercy, as ever Merciful to you, as Lord steers ships for you through sea to seek bounty, ever Merciful, as verse 66 says, and as He is Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, ḥalīman ghafūran, verse 44, and as healing and mercy, as mercy, as ever Merciful, as Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, but torment fearsome, and wrongdoers increase in loss, as loss, ruin, as painful punishment prepared, etc.

**Linguistic Note On Nunazzilu And Shifa' And Rahmah And Yazidu And Zalimina And Khasaran**

Nunazzilu from n-z-l, to send down, reveal, gradually, from nazzala, to send down. Qur'ān from q-r-ʾ, recitation, Qur'an, from qara'a. Shifā' from sh-f-y, healing, cure, from shafā, to heal. Raḥmah from r-ḥ-m, mercy, from raḥima. Mu'minīn from a-m-n, believers, from amina. Yazīdu from z-y-d, to increase, add, from zāda. Ẓālimīn from ẓ-l-m, wrongdoers, oppressors, from ẓalama. Khasār from kh-s-r, loss, ruin, perdition, from khasira, to lose. The verse's structure — We send down from Qur'an what is healing and mercy for believers, but it does not increase wrongdoers except loss — with sending down Qur'an as healing mercy for believers and increasing wrongdoers except loss, shows dual effect: healing mercy for believers, loss for wrongdoers, as truth vs falsehood, as Qur'an guides to most upright, gives good news mighty reward, warns painful punishment, as healing mercy vs loss.

**How This Verse Teaches Quran Healing Mercy For Believers Loss For Wrongdoers**

This verse teaches Quran as healing and mercy for believers but loss for wrongdoers: We send down, reveal Quran as healing, cure, and mercy for believers, for those who believe, as healing for hearts from ignorance, doubt, etc., and mercy, as guidance, most upright, wisdom, etc., as healing and mercy, as believers benefit, follow most upright, mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, but it only increases wrongdoers, disbelievers, polytheists, etc., in loss, ruin, perdition, as they have barrier, veils, deafness, aversion, far astray cannot find Right Way, deny resurrection, etc., and varying signs drives farther away, nufūran, aversion, warning increases greatly in defiance, ṭughyānan kabīran, great defiance, and Qur'an increases wrongdoers in loss, as loss, ruin, as painful punishment prepared, etc. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should see Qur'an as healing and mercy, benefit, follow most upright guidance, wisdom, that Qur'an guides to, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, healing, mercy, while wrongdoers increase in loss, ruin, as they disbelieve, deny, have barrier, etc., so avoid being among wrongdoers in loss, be among believers healing mercy.

**A Reflection On Healing And Mercy Vs Loss**

Shifā'un wa-raḥmatun li-l-mu'minīna — healing and mercy for believers — indicates Qur'an, revelation, truth, is healing, cure, for hearts, from ignorance, doubt, disease, and mercy, mercy for believers, as guidance, most upright, wisdom, etc., as healing and mercy, as ever Merciful to you, as Lord steers ships for you through sea to seek bounty, ever Merciful, as He is Most Forbearing All-Forgiving, but torment fearsome, and healing mercy for believers leads to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, station of praise, honourable entrance and exit, supporting authority, truth come falsehood vanished, indeed falsehood bound to vanish, etc., as truth, healing mercy. Wa-lā yazīdu al-ẓālimīna illā khasāran — does not increase wrongdoers except loss — indicates Qur'an, truth, increases wrongdoers, disbelievers, in loss, ruin, perdition, as they have barrier, veils, deafness, aversion, far astray, deny resurrection, etc., varying signs drives farther away, warning increases defiance, etc., and loss, ruin, as painful punishment, Hell, torment fearsome, etc. The believer who sees Qur'an as healing and mercy, follows most upright, will have healing, mercy, mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, while wrongdoer increases in loss, ruin, painful punishment, Hell, as torment fearsome.
