## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:68

> and guided them to the Straight Path.

### Expanded Commentary

**Guidance grows through obedience**

The reward is followed by guidance to the straight path. This order teaches that guidance is not only information received before action. When a person obeys sincerely, the path becomes clearer and the capacity to continue increases. Conversely, repeated resistance can make obvious truth appear burdensome.

**The straight path as direction**

The path is straight because it joins belief, worship, justice, and social duty without the distortions of excess or neglect. The hypocrites wanted to keep religious language while avoiding the path’s demands. The verse offers an alternative: obedience aligns the inner and outer life.

Guidance does not mean the disappearance of every question. It means a reliable orientation through questions. A believer may make a mistake, seek counsel, and return, but does not surrender the compass to appetite or public pressure. The verse encourages action as a form of learning: begin with the duty known, and ask Allah to open what is not yet clear.

**Action can deepen understanding**

Some guidance becomes clear only when a person begins to obey the part already understood. This is not blind action; it is the practical testing of a truth. Prayer, honest accounting, and fair speech make later questions easier to approach because the heart is no longer negotiating from pure self-interest.
