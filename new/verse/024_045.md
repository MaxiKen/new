## Sūrah an-Nūr 24:45

> And Allah has created from water every living creature. Some of them crawl on their bellies, some walk on two legs, and some walk on four. Allah creates whatever He wills. Surely Allah is Most Capable of everything.

**Expanded Commentary**

**The Primordial Matrix: khalaqa kulla dābbatin min māʾ**

The revelation directs human inquiry into the biological foundation of terrestrial life: *wa-Allāhu khalaqa kulla dābbatin min māʾ*, and Allah has created from water every living creature. The noun *dābbah* designates any sentient, ambulating creature that moves across the earth's surface.

The assertion that all living creatures originate from water articulates a profound biological and chemical truth. Modern cellular biology confirms that water (*H2O*) constitutes between sixty to ninety percent of the cellular cytoplasm in all living organisms, serving as the indispensable universal solvent without which biochemical reactions, nutrient transport, metabolic synthesis, and genetic replication are physically impossible. Furthermore, classical exegetes like al-Rāzī noted that *māʾ* also refers to reproductive fluids that initiate fertilization and embryonic development. Water is both the primordial matrix from which life emerged and the continuous biological fluid that sustains every living cell on earth.

**Diverse Locomotion: Crawlers, Bipeds, and Quadrupeds**

From this uniform chemical foundation, the text highlights the astonishing diversity of animal locomotion: *fa-minhum man yamshī ʿalā baṭnih wa-minhum man yamshī ʿalā rijlayn wa-minhum man yamshī ʿalā arbaʿ*. Despite sharing an identical elemental origin in water, living creatures diverge into radically different anatomical forms and locomotive mechanics.

First, there are creatures that glide or crawl upon their bellies (*man yamshī ʿalā baṭnih*), such as snakes, legless lizards, worms, and mollusks, utilizing rhythmic muscular contractions and specialized scales to navigate subterranean burrows, rocky crevices, and tree canopies. Second, there are bipeds (*man yamshī ʿalā rijlayn*), including humans and birds, balancing their upright weight on two limbs, freeing hands for tool use or transforming forelimbs into aerodynamic wings. Third, there are quadrupeds (*man yamshī ʿalā arbaʿ*), including beasts of burden, predators, and grazing herds, whose four-legged anatomy provides terrestrial speed, load-bearing endurance, and environmental stability across vast landscapes.

**Anatomical Differentiation as Divine Artistry**

The morphological variety of terrestrial fauna presents an insurmountable challenge to purely materialist assumptions of blind chance. If life arose from unguided accidental collisions of matter within a primordial soup, one would expect either uniform, monotonous biological structures or chaotic, unviable monstrosities.

Instead, the animal kingdom exhibits breathtaking, purposeful morphological variety. The skeletal frameworks, musculature, neurological networks, and sensory organs of crawlers, bipeds, and quadrupeds are each exquisitely calibrated for their respective ecological niches. The serpent moves silently through narrow fissures inaccessible to quadrupeds; the bird ascends into aerial freedom; the human walks upright with an elevated gaze toward the cosmos. Uniformity of chemical origin paired with boundless functional diversity points unerringly to an imaginative, conscious, and transcendent Designer who crafts living forms with supreme aesthetic and utilitarian brilliance.

**Sovereign Creative Liberty: yakhluqu Allāhu mā yashāʾ**

The verse explains the philosophical cause behind this biological diversity: *yakhluqu Allāhu mā yashāʾ*, Allah creates whatever He wills. Creation is not constrained by deterministic necessity, mechanical algorithms, or rigid natural laws that bind the Creator.

The laws of physics and biology are themselves creations of God, sustained by His active will and subject to His sovereign discretion. God is not an impersonal first cause who set an automated clockwork universe in motion only to withdraw into cosmic detachment. He is the Living Creator (*al-Khāliq*) who continuously innovates, designs, and actualizes whatever forms He chooses. Mentioning crawling, two-legged, and four-legged creatures serves as a representative taxonomy that hints at still greater wonders—creatures with six legs, eight legs, or centipedes with dozens of limbs, as well as marine creatures and invisible microorganisms that populate every ecological sphere.

**The Teleology of Biological Complexity**

Contemplating the diverse locomotive faculties of animals reveals an integrated ecological teleology that directly serves human civilization. Quadrupeds provide transportation, agricultural traction, meat, wool, and milk, enabling human communities to cultivate soil, transport trade goods across continents, and establish stable civilizations.

Bipeds among birds contribute to pest control, seed dispersal, and pollination, while their soaring flight inspires human aspirations for exploration. Even creatures crawling upon their bellies serve vital ecological roles, aerating soil, decomposing organic matter, and maintaining predator-prey equilibria in delicate biomes. Humanity occupies a uniquely dignified position within this zoological order: created upright on two legs, endowed with rational intellect, moral agency, and spiritual accountability, man is appointed not to exploit or abuse fellow creatures, but to act as a benevolent, responsible steward over the living trust placed into his hands by the Creator.

The diversity of locomotion also demonstrates the limitless range of divine mercy in equipping each creature with the exact physiological apparatus necessary for its survival and sustenance. A snake gliding silently through dry sand is furnished with sensory organs capable of detecting infrared radiation; a camel striding across arid dunes is equipped with specialized footpads that prevent sinking into soft sand and eyelashes that protect against fierce desert dust storms; an eagle soaring above mountain peaks possesses telescopic visual acuity to locate prey from thousands of feet in the air. Every physical feature stands as clear proof of purposeful engineering, destroying any rational basis for attributing the living world to random chance.

**Divine Omnipotence over Natural Law: ʿalā kulli shayʾin Qadīr**

The verse seals its biological exposition with a definitive cosmological formula: *inna Allāha ʿalā kulli shayʾin qadīr*, surely Allah is Most Capable of everything. The divine attribute *al-Qadīr* denotes absolute, untrammeled omnipotence.

He who fashioned living, breathing, intelligent beings out of a humble drop of lifeless water possesses the effortless power to resurrect decayed bones, reconstruct decomposed bodies, and call all mortal beings to account on the Day of Judgment. The skeptics who doubt the physical resurrection are invited to gaze upon their own biological origin: if God initiated life from stagnant dust and fluid water in the first creation, replicating that creation in the afterlife is infinitely effortless for Him. Biological observation thus transitions seamlessly into eschatological certainty, leaving the thinking mind in reverent submission before the Lord of all living forms.
