## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:131

> To Allah ˹alone˺ belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth. Indeed, We have commanded those given the Scripture before you, as well as you, to be mindful of Allah. But if you disobey, then ˹know that˺ to Allah belongs whatever is in the heavens and the earth. And Allah is Self-Sufficient, Praiseworthy.

### Expanded Commentary

**One command across revelations**

Everything in the heavens and earth belongs to Allah, and all communities are commanded to be mindful of Him. The verse places Muslims in continuity with earlier People of the Book: divine ownership and moral accountability are not new inventions of one nation.

**Disobedience does not diminish God**

If people disobey, Allah remains the owner. His sovereignty does not depend on human compliance, and disobedience cannot make the universe ownerless. The statement can free the believer from panic about the success of falsehood while warning against thinking that divine patience means approval.

Allah is Self-Sufficient and Praiseworthy. Worship benefits the servant; God does not need the servant’s obedience. This is why the command is simultaneously serious and merciful: it directs people toward the good that their Lord knows they need.

**Mindfulness across communities**

The command to earlier scripture communities and to Muslims establishes a shared moral vocabulary. Different revelations and legal histories do not make God’s ownership local. Each community should therefore examine whether its claim to divine service produces humility and justice rather than entitlement.
