## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:71

> O believers! Take your precautions and go forth either in groups or together.

### Expanded Commentary

**Precaution is part of reliance**

Believers are told to take precautions and to go forth in groups or together. The command concerns military danger, but its principle is wider: trust in Allah does not require abandoning means. Planning, intelligence, communication, protective equipment, and coordinated movement are forms of responsible reliance.

**Groups and collective discipline**

The options of going in small groups or as a whole respond to changing circumstances. There is no single tactical form detached from context. A commander must consider the threat, the mission, and the protection of people. The verse therefore supports flexibility without chaos.

In contemporary application, precautions include safeguarding civilians, verifying information, protecting aid workers, and refusing impulsive violence. The command is not permission for aggression; it is instruction for a community already facing danger. The believer prepares carefully, then entrusts the result to Allah rather than treating preparation as a guarantee.

**Preparation is not a lack of trust**

The command to take precautions corrects a spirituality that calls planning unbelief. Medical supplies, communication plans, safe routes, and trained leadership all serve the preservation of life. Reliance begins after responsible means have been taken, not before them.
