## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:92

> **or cause the sky to fall upon us in pieces, as you have claimed, or bring Allah and the angels before us, face to face,**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Cause Sky To Fall Upon Us In Pieces As You Claimed**

Aw tusqiṭa al-samā'a kamā zaʿamta ʿalaynā kisafan — or you cause sky to fall upon us as you claimed in pieces. Aw, or, alternative demand. Tusqiṭa from s-q-ṭ, to cause to fall, make fall, drop, from asqaṭa, to cause to fall, make fall, as tusqiṭa cause sky to fall. Al-samā'a from s-m-w, sky, heaven, heavens, as samā', sky, heaven. Kamā zaʿamta, as you claimed, as you asserted, alleged, as you claimed, from z-ʿ-m, to claim, assert, allege, as zaʿamta you claimed, as you claimed, as Prophet claimed sky could fall, etc., as warning, as punishment, as sky fall in pieces, etc. ʿAlaynā, upon us, over us, upon us, as sky fall upon us. Kisafan from k-s-f, pieces, fragments, chunks, from kisaf, pieces, fragments, as pieces, fragments, as sky fall in pieces, fragments, as punishment, as they demand sky fall in pieces as claimed, as punishment, as sign, as they challenge, we will never believe until... cause sky to fall in pieces as you claimed, as demanded sign, etc. Al-Ṭabarī explains that or cause sky to fall upon us in pieces as you have claimed, meaning cause sky, heaven, to fall upon us in pieces, fragments, as you claimed, asserted, that sky could fall, as punishment, as sign, as they demand sky fall in pieces as claimed, as punishment, as sign, as proof, as they challenge, demand signs, etc., as they persist in disbelief, refuse except disbelief, etc.

**Bring Allah And Angels Before Us Face To Face**

Aw ta'tiya bi-Allāhi wa-al-malā'ikati qabīlan — or you bring Allah and angels before us face to face, as group, before us. Aw, or, alternative demand. Ta'tiya from a-t-y, to bring, come with, bring, as ta'tiya you bring, come with, bring Allah and angels. Bi-Allāhi wa-al-malā'ikati, with Allah and angels, Allah and angels, as Allah and angels, as bring Allah and angels before us, as they demand bring Allah and angels face to face, as sign, as proof, as they challenge, we will never believe until... bring Allah and angels before us face to face, as demanded sign, etc. Qabīlan from q-b-l, face to face, before us, as group, before us, face to face, as qabīlan face to face, before us, as group, as face to face, as they demand bring Allah and angels before us face to face, as sign, as proof, as they challenge, demand signs, etc. Al-Ṭabarī explains that or bring Allah and angels before us face to face, as group, before us, face to face, meaning bring Allah and angels before us, face to face, as group, as sign, as proof, as they demand bring Allah and angels before us face to face, as demanded sign, etc., as they persist in disbelief, challenge, demand signs, as they persist in disbelief, refuse except disbelief, etc.

**Why Sky Fall In Pieces And Allah Angels Face To Face As Demanded Signs**

Why cause sky to fall in pieces as claimed and bring Allah and angels before us face to face as demanded signs? Because they, disbelievers, challenge Prophet, demand signs, miracles, as proof, as they say we will never believe until spring gush, garden, rivers, sky fall in pieces as claimed, Allah and angels face to face, house of gold, ascend into heaven and bring down book, etc., as demands, challenges, as they demand signs, but nothing keeps Us from sending demanded signs except they were denied by earlier peoples, etc., We only send signs as warning, etc., as previous verse 59 says, and as Quran inimitable, cannot produce equal, every kind of lesson set forth yet most persist in disbelief, challenge, demand signs, as they persist in disbelief, refuse except disbelief, as disbelief, ungratefulness, as human ever ungrateful, etc., and as sky fall in pieces as claimed and Allah and angels face to face are demands that would be destruction if denied, etc., as warning, as every wicked society destroyed or punished severely before Day written in Record, etc.

**Connection To Spring Gush Garden Rivers House Gold Ascend Heaven Book And Human Messenger**

Verses 90-93 list demands: spring gush from earth for us, garden of palm trees and vineyards cause rivers abundantly, cause sky to fall in pieces as claimed, or bring Allah and angels before us face to face, golden house, climbing to heaven and still disbelieving unless scripture brought down readable, say Glory be to my Lord am I not only human messenger, An Angelic Messenger heading, showing challenges, demands for signs, miracles, but Quran inimitable, cannot produce equal, every kind of lesson set forth yet most persist in disbelief, challenge, demand signs, as they persist in disbelief, refuse except disbelief, as disbelief, ungratefulness, etc., and as nothing keeps Us from sending demanded signs except denied by earlier, etc., We only send signs as warning, etc., and as Prophet is only human messenger, not having power to cause sky fall or bring Allah and angels face to face, etc., as human messenger, as Glory be to my Lord am I not only human messenger, as human messenger, etc.

**Link To Nothing Keeps Us From Sending Demanded Signs And We Only Send Signs As Warning**

Verse 59 nothing keeps Us from sending demanded signs except they had already been denied by earlier peoples, We gave Thamud shecamel as clear sign but they wrongfully rejected, We only send signs as warning, showing that demanded signs, if sent and denied, lead to destruction, severe punishment, as earlier peoples denied and destroyed, as every wicked society destroyed or punished severely before Day written in Record, as many generations destroyed after Noah, etc., and as Thamud shecamel example, etc., so We only send signs as warning, not as demanded entertainment, mocking, but as warning, to frighten, so they may be mindful, etc., and now they demand sky fall in pieces as claimed and Allah and angels face to face, as demanded signs, but nothing keeps Us from sending demanded signs except denied by earlier, etc., We only send signs as warning, etc., and as Quran inimitable, cannot produce equal, every kind of lesson set forth yet most persist in disbelief, challenge, demand signs.

**Linguistic Note On Tusqiṭa And Samā' And Kisafan And Ta'tiya And Qabīlan**

Tusqiṭa from s-q-ṭ, to cause to fall, make fall, from asqaṭa. Samā' from s-m-w, sky, heaven, from samā'. Zaʿamta from z-ʿ-m, to claim, assert, allege, from zaʿama. Kisaf from k-s-f, pieces, fragments, from kasafa, to cut into pieces, from kisaf, pieces. Ta'tiya from a-t-y, to bring, come with, from atā. Allāh, Allah. Malā'ikah from m-l-k, angels, plural of malak. Qabīl from q-b-l, face to face, before us, group, from qabila, to be face to face, from qabīl, face to face, group. The verse's structure — or you cause sky to fall upon us as you claimed pieces, or you bring Allah and angels before us face to face — with demands cause sky to fall in pieces as claimed and bring Allah and angels face to face, shows demands for signs, miracles, sky fall, Allah and angels face to face, as proof, as they challenge, demand signs, as they persist in disbelief, etc.

This verse teaches challenge demanding sky fall in pieces as claimed and bringing Allah and angels before us face to face as signs: they challenge Prophet, say or cause sky to fall upon us in pieces as you have claimed, as you asserted, and or bring Allah and angels before us face to face, as group, before us, face to face, as demanded signs, miracles, as proof, as they persist in disbelief, refuse except disbelief, as most people persist in disbelief, as verse 89 says, and they challenge, demand signs, miracles, as proof, as sky fall, Allah and angels face to face, etc., as demands, challenges, as they demand signs, but nothing keeps Us from sending demanded signs except they were denied by earlier peoples, etc., We only send signs as warning, etc., and as Quran inimitable, cannot produce equal, every kind of lesson set forth, yet most persist in disbelief, challenge, demand signs, as they persist in disbelief. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should not challenge Prophet demanding sky fall in pieces as claimed or Allah and angels face to face, etc., as disbelievers do, who persist in disbelief, refuse except disbelief, but believe, follow most upright guidance, wisdom, that Qur'an guides to, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, healing mercy, etc., while disbelievers challenge, demand signs, persist in disbelief, painful punishment prepared, etc.
