## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:156

> and for their denial and outrageous accusation against Mary,

**Expanded Commentary**

**And for Their Denial and Outrageous Accusation Against Mary**

The verse continues *wa bi kufrihim wa qawlihim ʿalā Maryama buhtānan ʿaẓīmā* — and for their denial, their *kufr*, and their word against Mary, their saying upon Mary as a tremendous, mighty slander — *buhtān ʿaẓīm*. *Bi kufrihim* — for their denial — was explained as continuation of the accumulated causes for condemnation — breaking covenant, rejecting signs, killing prophets unjustly, saying hearts are *ghulf* sealed for disbelief (4:155) — now denial as such and slander against Maryam. *Wa qawlihim ʿalā Maryama buhtānan ʿaẓīmā* — and their saying against Mary as mighty slander — was explained with *buhtān* — slander, calumny, false accusation that bewilders — the same *buhtān* and manifest sin borne by whoever throws sin on the innocent (4:112), now against the innocent Maryam, mother of the Messiah. *ʿAẓīm* — mighty, tremendous, great — was explained as that slander against Maryam is not private insult but defamation of the *ṣiddīqah* chosen, the Word through her — *“the Messiah, Jesus, son of Mary, was but messenger of God and His Word through Mary and spirit from Him”* (4:171), whose mother was *“above women of the worlds”* in reports. Those who consume orphans’ wealth consume fire (4:10) and throwing sin on the innocent bears slander (4:112); to slander Maryam with unchastity — accusing the pure who was given a spirit — is the throw of sin upon the purest, therefore *ʿaẓīm*. Early reports noted that the Jews who rejected Jesus accused Maryam of *zina* — fornication — to deny his messengership, as they denied all messengers where distinguishing is true disbelief (4:151); the verse names the slander as *buhtān ʿaẓīm* because it combines denial of a prophet with defamation of his mother.

**A Verse That Continues Broken Covenant With Mary’s Slander**

Placed after broken covenant, rejecting signs, killing prophets, saying hearts are *ghulf* sealed (4:155), this verse continues the same condemnation with the next instance: denial and mighty slander against Mary. Demand for a book from heaven and the greater demand — *show God visible* — were struck, followed by the calf after clear signs yet forgiven (4:153), and the Mount raised for firm covenant (4:154); slander against Mary is the next breaking: after calf — idol — after rejecting signs, they reject the sign of Mary’s purity and Jesus’ birth without father as Word. The later reminder that *“We have sent revelation to you as We sent to Noah, Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, Jesus, Job, Jonah, Aaron, Solomon, David We gave Psalms”* (4:163) — all accepted without distinction (4:152) — shows that to slander Mary is to reject that chain that includes Jesus. The verse was linked to *“Gardens for those who believe and do good”* (4:122) — Mary believed and did good; to accuse her is to refuse that good. For the weak this is protective: where orphan women deprived yet wished to marry are protected from being married for profit (4:127), Mary the most deprived — alone — is protected by God naming slander against her as *ʿaẓīm*.

**The *Buhtān* That Mirrors Throwing Sin on the Innocent**

The word *buhtān* — mighty slander — was explained to mirror *iḥtamala buhtānan wa ithman mubīnā* — bears slander and manifest sin — where one throws sin on the innocent (4:112). There the innocent was a neighbour Jew accused of theft; here the innocent is Maryam, accused to deny messengership. Earlier passages note that *“there is no good in most secret talks except charity, kindness, reconciliation”* (4:114); slander of Mary is the evil secret talk that has no good. Elsewhere the Book teaches that *“He does not like loud negative speech except by wronged”* (4:148) — accusation against Mary is negative speech not by wronged but by wrongdoer, therefore not allowed, therefore not liked, therefore *ʿaẓīm*. That same teaching was linked to *“He is All-Hearing, All-Knowing”* (4:148): the accusers of Mary voiced *buhtān ʿaẓīm* where they were not wronged but wronging.

**Denial that Precedes Slander**

The pairing *wa bi kufrihim wa qawlihim* — and for their denial and their saying — was explained to show that slander follows denial: denial of Jesus as messenger makes slander of Mary as pretext, as those who wish to make distinction between God and messengers say *we believe in some and disbelieve in some* (4:150) — to disbelieve in Jesus they slander his mother. Association is far straying (4:116) and defying the Messenger after clarity is left to what he chose (4:115); denial and slander here are that defiance applied to the Messiah. Early notes added that Maryam’s chastity was attested by *“We breathed into her Our spirit”* (66:12) and by Jesus speaking in cradle; to deny the son is to need to deny the mother, so *buhtān* becomes necessary. The shortness — *and for their denial and outrageous accusation against Mary* — was read as the sūrah’s compression where the next verse expands denial into boasting *we killed the Messiah* (4:157) — denial moves from slandering mother to boasting murder of son, both false.

**A Short Phrase That Anchors Accusation as Cause of Condemnation**

Though only *“and for their denial and outrageous accusation against Mary — mighty slander,”* the phrase anchors accusation as cause of condemnation beside breaking covenant and killing prophets. The shortness makes the history — demand, thunderbolt, calf, Mount, gate, Sabbath, breaking, rejecting, killing, saying hearts are *ghulf*, denial, slander — a litany of one people’s repeated breaking, yet the sūrah will still note *but those well-grounded in knowledge among them believe* (4:162) — few — so that the *buhtān ʿaẓīm* is not extended to all. The next verse will complete that denial: boasting *we killed the Messiah, son of Mary, messenger of God* — but they killed not nor crucified, it was made to appear, they have no knowledge only assumptions, they certainly did not kill him (4:157).
