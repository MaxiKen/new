## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:156

> and for their denial and outrageous accusation against Mary,

### Expanded Commentary

**The accusation against Mary**

The verse names the outrageous accusation against Mary after mentioning the rejection and killing of prophets. Mary’s honour is defended by revelation because sexual slander attacks both an individual woman and the truth of the divine sign given through her. The Qur’an presents her as chosen, pure, and a model of devotion.

Accusing a woman in order to discredit the child or the messenger born through her is a recurring social pattern. The verse exposes the moral ugliness of using reputation as a weapon against a person who cannot easily answer the accusation. The chapter’s broader protection of chastity and testimony belongs beside this statement.

The warning applies to believers as well. Religious disagreement does not permit sexual insult, and suspicion is not evidence. A community that praises Mary while spreading rumours about living women has not learned the verse’s ethic.

**Slander as a weapon against signs**

The attack on Mary shows how accusations about a woman can be used to discredit a child, a family, or a message. Protecting chastity therefore protects truth and social stability. Believers should not repeat a charge merely because it explains an event neatly; moral neatness is not evidence.
