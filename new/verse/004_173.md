## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:173

> As for those who believe and do good, He will reward them in full and increase them out of His grace. But those who are too proud and arrogant, He will subject them to a painful punishment. And besides Allah they will find no protector or helper.

### Expanded Commentary

**Full reward and additional grace**

Believers who do good will receive their reward in full, with an increase from Allah’s grace. The promise honours effort without reducing reward to a strict wage. God completes what human beings leave unfinished and adds what they could never earn by calculation.

Those who are proud and arrogant receive painful punishment and find no protector. Their arrogance has rejected the very relationship that could save them. The absence of a helper answers the false promise of self-sufficiency: the person who refused dependence on God discovers that created allies cannot replace Him.

The verse joins doctrine and character. Correct belief about Jesus and Allah must produce humility, worship, and good action. Theology that leaves the ego enthroned has not reached the servant’s heart.

**Grace exceeds calculation**

A good act receives its full reward and then more. This should produce generosity rather than a ledger mentality in which a person demands a precise return from God. The proud person, by contrast, loses protection because he has made self-sufficiency his religion. Humility is therefore both a theological truth and a practical safeguard.
