## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:173

> As for those who believe and do good, He will reward them in full and increase them out of His grace. But those who are too proud and arrogant, He will subject them to a painful punishment. And besides Allah they will find no protector or helper.

**Expanded Commentary**

**Those Who Believe and Do Good — Rewarded in Full and Increased Out of Grace**

The verse opens *fa ammā alladhīna āmanū wa ʿamilū al-ṣāliḥāti fa yuwaffīhim ujūrahum wa yazīduhum min faḍlihī* — as for those who believe and do good, do righteous deeds, He will repay, fulfil, their rewards fully and increase them out of His bounty, grace. *Āmanū wa ʿamilū al-ṣāliḥāt* — faith and righteous deeds — was explained as the sūrah’s shorthand for the faithful: opposed to those too proud to worship Him (4:172) and those who disbelieve and hinder far astray (4:167). *Fa yuwaffīhim ujūrahum* — He will fulfil their wages, repay fully — was read with *waffā* — to give in full, to complete — the same root for *“God does not wrong even an atom, if it is good He multiplies”* (4:40) — fulfilling is not wronging even speck (*naqīrā* — 4:124) — the atom not wronged is the wage given full. *Wa yazīduhum min faḍlihī* — and increase them out of His grace, bounty — was explained with *faḍl* — grace, bounty — the same *faḍl* without which a group would have deceived you yet they deceive only themselves (4:113), and *“great is His favour upon you”* (4:113) — increase is from grace, not debt, as Gardens forever where promise is true (4:122) — increase is Rivers under, not cut. Early reports linked increase to *“He will admit them into Gardens … under rivers flow”* and to abundant Havens for emigrant (4:100) — world and Hereafter.

**But Those Too Proud Will Be Subjected to Painful Punishment**

The contrast *wa ammā alladhīna istankafū wa istakbarū fa yuʿadhdhibuhum ʿadhāban alīmā* — but as for those who were too proud, disdained, and were arrogant, magnified themselves — He will punish them with a painful, aching punishment — was explained with *istankafū wa istakbarū* — too proud and arrogant — the same pair as *the Messiah would never be too proud to be servant, nor angels nearest; those too proud and arrogant will be gathered* (4:172) — now gathered to punishment. *Fa yuʿadhdhibuhum ʿadhāban alīmā* — He will punish them with painful punishment — was explained with *ʿadhāb alīm* — painful — the same *alīm* announced to hypocrites as good news of painful punishment (4:138) and prepared for disbelievers among the Book’s People for *ribā* and *akl bi al-bāṭil* (4:161) — painful matches proud: to be too proud to serve (4:172) is to earn pain as servant’s opposite. The sūrah has shown that *“lowest depth of Fire, you will never find helper”* (4:145) — painful is lowest depth; and *“He will neither forgive nor guide them to any path except Hell’s path forever, and that is easy for Him”* (4:168–169) — easy where Almighty — here painful as promised.

**And Besides Him They Will Find No Protector or Helper**

The closure *wa lā yajidūna lahum min dūni Allāhi waliyyan wa lā naṣīrā* — and they will not find for themselves besides Him, apart from Him, any protector, guardian, nor helper — was explained with *walī* and *naṣīr* — the same pair the oppressed cry for from grace (4:75) and that God alone suffices as (4:45) and that hypocrites sought among disbelievers as allies seeking honour (4:139) — the too proud sought *ʿizzah* among disbelievers and among pride itself, yet will find no *walī* and no *naṣīr* besides Him where punishment is. *Min dūni Allāhi* — besides Him, apart from Him — was read as that no female god invoked (4:117) and no Satan as guardian taken instead of Him (4:119) and no disbeliever as ally (4:144) will avail on the Day where all are gathered before Him all together (4:172). The sūrah has said *“with Him are both rewards He hears and sees”* (4:134) — protection besides Him is not needed where both rewards are with Him; to seek it is to find none. Where the sūrah says *“He is sufficient as Trustee”* (4:171) — Messiah’s case — the too proud find no trustee.

**A Verse That Follows Gathering With Repaying and Punishing**

Placed after *the Messiah would never be too proud to be servant, nor angels nearest; those too proud and arrogant will be gathered before Him all together* (4:172), this verse follows gathering with repaying and punishing: as for those who believe and do good — fulfilled wages and increase from grace; but those too proud and arrogant — painful punishment, no protector or helper besides Him. The sūrah has shown that *“whoever does good male or female with faith will enter Paradise not wronged a speck”* (4:124) — fulfilled wages is that entry; and *“whoever commits evil will be rewarded accordingly and find no protector besides Him”* (4:123) — painful where disbelief and wrong are — now applied to pride. The sūrah that warns not to deviate by following desires (4:27) and not to let desires deviate from justice (4:135) sees *istinkāf wa istikbār* — being too proud and arrogant — as the pride that deviates most, because *ʿubūdiyyah* — servitude — is the sūrah’s ethic: to stand for justice though against yourselves (4:135) is servitude, to not incline entirely leaving hanging (4:129) is servitude, to verify before fighting (4:94) is servitude.

**Repaying in Full and Increase as Grace**

The phrase *fa yuwaffīhim ujūrahum wa yazīduhum min faḍlihī* was explained to keep repaying in full as justice — not wronging even atom (4:40) — and increase as grace — *faḍl* — the same grace that taught you what you did not know (4:113) and that makes *“why should He punish if you are grateful and faithful? He is ever Appreciative”* (4:147) true: appreciation is increase from grace. The sūrah has shown that the emigrant finds many havens and abundance (4:100) — increase from grace — and enrichment after separation (4:130) — increase from His bounty. For the weak this is steadying: where you were told settlement is best though selves brought with *shuḥḥ* (4:128), to be among those who believe and do good is to have wages fulfilled and grace increase; where you were too proud to worship, wage is painful and no protector. The next passage will turn from history to humanity: O humanity, there has come to you conclusive evidence from your Lord, We have sent down to you a brilliant light (4:174).
