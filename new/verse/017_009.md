## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:9

> **Surely this Quran guides to what is most upright, and gives good news to the believers—who do good—that they will have a mighty reward.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Qur'an Guides To Most Upright**

Inna hādhā al-qur'āna yahdī li-l-latī hiya aqwamu — indeed this Qur'an guides to what is most upright, most straight, most just. Inna for emphasis, surely. Hādhā al-qur'ān, this Qur'an, which was sent down as Reminder to Prophet to explain to people. Yahdī, guides, from h-d-y, to guide, same root as hudā. Li-l-latī hiya aqwamu, to what is most upright, most straight, from q-w-m, to stand, be upright, straight, just. Aqwam means most upright, most straight, most correct, superlative of qawīm, upright. Al-Ṭabarī explains that Qur'an guides to most upright way, most just, most correct in beliefs, morals, and actions, more upright than previous Scriptures that were also guides but have been altered or are specific to one community. Al-Rāzī says aqwam includes most upright in creed, tawḥīd, not taking trustee besides Allah, not taking two gods, and most upright in law, justice, grace, courtesy to relatives, forbidding indecency, etc., as verses 22-39 will detail as moral code that is most upright.

**Good News To Believers Who Do Good**

Wa-yubashshiru al-mu'minīna al-ladhīna yaʿmalūna al-ṣāliḥāti anna lahum ajran kabīran — and gives good news to believers who do good that they will have mighty reward. Yubashshiru from b-sh-r, to give good news, glad tidings, same root as bushshira for baby girl but here true good news. Al-mu'minīn, believers, those who believe. Al-ladhīna yaʿmalūna al-ṣāliḥāt, who do good deeds, righteous deeds, from ʿ-m-l and ṣ-l-ḥ, good. Anna lahum ajran kabīran, that they will have mighty, great reward, from a-j-r, reward, and k-b-r, great, mighty. Al-Ṭabarī says Qur'an gives good news to believers who do good that they will have mighty reward in Hereafter, Gardens of Eternity with rivers and whatever they desire as previous chapter described, and good home in world. The good news is for believers who do good, not only believe without deeds, showing that faith and deeds together lead to mighty reward.

**Why This Verse Follows Israelite History**

Verses 2-8 described Israelite history: given Torah as guide, commanded not to take trustee besides Allah, warned about two corruptions, punished, restored, principle good for self wrong against self, second punishment with disgrace and Temple entry, perhaps mercy if repent, if return to sin punishment returns, Hell confinement. Verse 9 says surely this Qur'an guides to most upright and gives good news to believers who do good of mighty reward. The connection is that after showing Israelite history of corruption and punishment despite having Torah as guide, the sūrah says this Qur'an guides to most upright, more upright than previous, and gives good news to believers who do good. It implies that Israelites had guide but corrupted; this Qur'an guides to most upright, so follow it to avoid their fate and to receive mighty reward. It also shows that Qur'an is not only warning like previous verses but also good news.

**What Is Most Upright**

Aqwam, most upright, includes most upright in creed, law, and character. In creed, most upright is tawḥīd: your God is One, do not take two gods, to Him belongs whatever in heavens and earth, all blessings from Him, so worship Him alone. In law, most upright is justice, grace, courtesy to relatives, honoring parents, giving relatives due, not spending wastefully, not being tight-fisted nor openhanded excessively, honoring covenant, not breaking oaths, etc., as verses 22-39 will detail. In character, most upright is humility, not walking exultantly, fearing Lord above, doing what commanded like angels, being grateful servant like Noah. Al-Qurṭubī says aqwam means most just, most correct, most balanced, without excess or deficiency, which is characteristic of Qur'anic guidance.

**Mighty Reward For Believers Who Do Good**

Ajran kabīran, mighty reward, is described in previous chapter as Gardens of Eternity with rivers, whatever they desire, good home, peace at death, etc., and in this chapter will be described as Paradise and good life. Al-Rāzī says kabīran, mighty, great, indicates reward is great in quantity, quality, and duration, beyond imagination. The good news is for al-mu'minīna al-ladhīna yaʿmalūna al-ṣāliḥāt, believers who do good deeds, showing that mighty reward is for those who combine faith and deeds, not faith alone without deeds or deeds without faith. The verse therefore encourages both belief and good deeds.

**Linguistic Note On Aqwam And Ajr**

Hādhā al-qur'ān, this Qur'an, from q-r-ʾ, to recite, read. Yahdī from h-d-y, to guide. Li-l-latī hiya aqwamu, to what is most upright, from q-w-m, to stand, upright, with aqwam superlative, most upright. Yubashshiru from b-sh-r, to give good news. Al-mu'minīn from a-m-n, believers. Yaʿmalūna al-ṣāliḥāt, do good deeds, from ʿ-m-l and ṣ-l-ḥ. Ajran from a-j-r, reward. Kabīran from k-b-r, great, mighty. The verse's structure — surely this Qur'an guides to most upright and gives good news to believers who do good of mighty reward — with two functions of Qur'an, guiding and giving good news, creates concise summary of Qur'an's role: guide to most upright way and bearer of good news of mighty reward for those who follow.

**How This Verse Connects To Sūrah's Purpose**

Sūrah al-Isrā' purpose includes showing that Qur'an guides to most upright after showing Israelite history of corruption despite having Torah. It also includes moral code that is most upright, as verses 22-39 will detail. This verse is thesis for moral code: Qur'an guides to most upright, so follow its commands about parents, relatives, spending, etc., which are most upright. It also connects to Night Journey: Prophet taken by night to show signs, then given Qur'an that guides to most upright, so that he may explain to people what was revealed and perhaps they will reflect as previous chapter said. The verse therefore is central to sūrah's message: follow Qur'an that guides to most upright to receive mighty reward and avoid punishment that came to those who corrupted.

**A Lesson On Guidance And Good News**

The verse teaches that Qur'an is both guidance and good news. Guidance to most upright way in beliefs, morals, actions, and good news of mighty reward for believers who do good. Al-Qurṭubī says guidance is for all, but good news is specifically for believers who do good, showing that guidance must be accepted and acted upon to receive good news. The believer who follows most upright way will have mighty reward in Hereafter and good life in world as previous chapter promised. The disbeliever who does not believe in Hereafter, as verse 10 will say, has painful punishment prepared. The verse therefore invites to be among believers who do good and receive good news of mighty reward.
