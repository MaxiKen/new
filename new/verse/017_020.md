## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:20

> **We provide both the former and the latter from the bounty of your Lord. And the bounty of your Lord can never be withheld.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**We Provide Both From Bounty**

Kullan numiddu hā'ulā'i wa-hā'ulā'i min ʿaṭā'i rabbika — each, all, We extend these and those from gift of your Lord. Kullan, each, all, everyone, both groups, from k-l-l, all, each. Numiddu from m-d-d, to extend, supply, provide, give مدد, to extend, to مدد, مدد, مدد, to مدد, to extend, to give abundantly, from madda, to extend, to مدد. Hā'ulā'i wa-hā'ulā'i, these and those, i.e., both groups: those desiring fleeting world and those desiring Hereafter, seekers of world and seekers of Hereafter, former and latter. Min ʿaṭā'i from ʿ-ṭ-w, gift, bounty, giving, from same root as ʿaṭā', gift, bounty, from aʿṭā, to give. Rabbika, your Lord, from r-b-b, Lord, master, sustainer, who provides. Al-Ṭabarī explains that each, both those who desire fleeting and those who desire Hereafter, We extend, provide, from gift of your Lord, meaning Allah provides for all, both believers and disbelievers, desirers of world and desirers of Hereafter, from His bounty in worldly life, worldly provision. Al-Qurṭubī says numiddu indicates continuous extension, provision, مدد, مدد, not one-time, and kullān indicates all are provided, no one excluded from worldly provision, both former and latter.

**Bounty Of Lord Never Withheld**

Wa-mā kāna ʿaṭā'u rabbika maḥẓūran — and bounty of your Lord is not withheld, not restricted, not forbidden, can never be withheld. Mā kāna, is not, was not, would never be. ʿAṭā'u, gift, bounty, from ʿ-ṭ-w, giving. Rabbika, your Lord. Maḥẓūran from ḥ-ẓ-r, to withhold, forbid, restrict, enclose, prevent, from same root as ḥaẓr, withholding, prohibition, and maḥẓūr, forbidden, withheld, restricted, from ḥaẓara, to forbid, enclose, prevent. Al-Ṭabarī says bounty of your Lord is not withheld, meaning His gift, provision, in world is not restricted from anyone, both groups receive, because worldly provision is general mercy, while Hereafter reward is specific for believers who strive and whose striving appreciated. Al-Rāzī says maḥẓūran means not forbidden, not prevented, not restricted, indicating that worldly bounty extends to all, even fleeting desirers, but Hereafter bounty only for Hereafter desirers who strive with faith, as distinction between general worldly gift and specific Hereafter reward.

**Why Both Provided From Bounty**

Why both provided from bounty? To show justice and mercy and wisdom: worldly provision given to all, not withheld, but Hereafter reward only for those who desire Hereafter and strive accordingly with faith. The person who desires fleeting alone still provided in world from bounty, as verse 18 said We hasten for him in it what We will to whoever We will, but then Hell condemned rejected. The person who desires Hereafter also provided from bounty in world, as he needs provision to strive, to seek bounty, and then appreciated striving in Hereafter with mighty reward. The verse therefore clarifies that provision in world does not indicate Allah's pleasure or displeasure; both groups provided from bounty, but end differs drastically. Worldly provision is not sign of success alone.

**Connection To Seeking Bounty And Knowing Calculation**

Verse 12 said We made day bright so you may seek bounty of your Lord and know number of years and calculation, and explained everything in detail. Verse 20 says each We extend these and those from gift of your Lord, bounty never withheld. The connection is that seeking bounty is from gift of Lord, and gift not withheld, so both groups can seek bounty, but intention differs: fleeting desirer seeks bounty as ultimate goal, Hereafter desirer seeks bounty as means to strive for Hereafter. The sūrah's moral code about giving relatives due, not spending wastefully, not being tight-fisted nor openhanded, etc., is about using bounty properly, not withholding, not wasting, using gift to desire Hereafter.

**Link To Previous Chapter About Provision In Due Measure**

Previous chapter said if Allah were to extend provision for His servants they would transgress in earth but He sends down in due measure what He wills, He is All-Aware, All-Seeing of His servants, and He sends water, subjects night and day, etc. This verse says each We extend these and those from gift of your Lord, bounty never withheld, indicating that extension of provision is from gift, measured as He wills, not withheld arbitrarily, but given to all as test. The provision is from gift, not earned solely, and bounty not withheld means no one prevented from receiving worldly provision if Allah wills, but Hereafter provision withheld from those who desire fleeting alone.

**Linguistic Note On Numiddu And Mahzur**

Kull from k-l-l, each, all. Numiddu from m-d-d, to extend, supply, give مدد, مدد, from madda, to extend, مدد, مدد, مدد, مدد, to مدد. Hā'ulā' from h-'-l-ā, these, those. ʿAṭā' from ʿ-ṭ-w, gift, giving, from aʿṭā, to give. Rabb from r-b-b, Lord. Mā kāna from k-w-n, to be. Maḥẓūr from ḥ-ẓ-r, to withhold, forbid, from ḥaẓara, to forbid, enclose. The verse's structure — each We extend these and those from gift of your Lord, and gift of your Lord is not withheld — with provision for both groups and statement that gift not restricted, shows general mercy in worldly provision and distinguishes from specific reward in Hereafter, which is withheld from fleeting desirers who get Hell condemned rejected.

**How This Verse Teaches Gratitude And Proper Use Of Bounty**

This verse teaches gratitude for bounty that is never withheld and proper use: since both groups provided from gift, use bounty to desire Hereafter and strive accordingly with faith, not only fleeting. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should be grateful that Lord's bounty not withheld, extends to all, both former and latter, and should use provision to strive for Hereafter, following most upright guidance, not to rebel like mutraf, elite, affluent who rebel when commanded. The practical lesson is to see worldly provision as gift from Lord, not as sign of success alone, and to use it to seek Hereafter, so that striving appreciated, not condemned rejected, and record laid open shows gratitude and good use.

**A Lesson On Bounty Never Withheld**

Wa-mā kāna ʿaṭā'u rabbika maḥẓūran — bounty of your Lord can never be withheld, is never withheld — is reassurance and warning: reassurance that provision not restricted, Allah provides for all, both former and latter, worldly gift extends, and warning that provision in world does not mean safety in Hereafter; both provided, but ends differ: fleeting desirer gets hastened provision but then Hell condemned rejected, Hereafter desirer gets provision in world and appreciated striving in Hereafter with mighty reward. The believer who reflects will use bounty never withheld to strive for Hereafter, seeking bounty of Lord with gratitude, knowing number of years and calculation, following most upright guidance, honoring parents, giving relatives due, etc., so that gift used well and leads to appreciated striving and mighty reward.
