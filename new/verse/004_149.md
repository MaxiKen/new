## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:149

> Whether you reveal or conceal a good or pardon an evil—surely Allah is Ever-Pardoning, Most Capable.

### Expanded Commentary

**Goodness may be public or hidden**

Whether a person reveals or conceals a good deed, Allah knows it. Public good can encourage others, support accountability, and mobilize resources; hidden good protects sincerity and the beneficiary’s dignity. The verse does not impose one form on every act.

**Pardoning an evil**

Pardoning wrong can mean refusing retaliation, accepting an apology, or leaving a matter to God after pursuing a legitimate remedy. It does not require a victim to remain in danger or to restore trust without evidence of change. Forgiveness is a virtue, not a command to enable abuse.

Allah is Ever-Pardoning and Capable. The believer imitates pardon while remembering that God alone has complete power to judge and restore. A person who cannot yet forgive may still refuse injustice and seek lawful protection; the verse invites generous strength, not emotional dishonesty.

**Pardon without denial**

A person can forgive inwardly while still requiring a boundary, repayment, or legal consequence. Concealing a good deed can protect sincerity, while revealing it can mobilize support. The verse gives room for both, provided the heart seeks Allah rather than applause or revenge.
