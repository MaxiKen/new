## Sūrah al-Isrāʾ 17:52

> **On the Day He will call you, you will ˹instantly˺ respond by praising Him, thinking you had remained ˹in the world˺ only for a little while.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**On Day He Will Call You, You Will Respond Praising Him**

Yawma yadʿūkum fa-tastajībūna bi-ḥamdihi — Day He will call you, then you will respond with His praise, praising Him. Yawma from y-w-m, Day, Day of Resurrection, Day He will call. Yadʿūkum from d-ʿ-w, to call, summon, as yadʿu human prays, calls. Fa-tastajībūna from j-w-b, to respond, answer, from istajāba, to respond, answer, as istajāba response. Bi-ḥamdihi from ḥ-m-d, with His praise, praising Him, from ḥamd, praise, as yusabbiḥu bi-ḥamdihi glorifies with His praise, all things glorify with praise. Al-Ṭabarī explains that on Day He will call you, summon you, for resurrection, reckoning, you will instantly respond, answer, by praising Him, with His praise, meaning you will respond praising Him, acknowledging, as all will respond, even disbelievers, praising, as Day call, as previous verse perhaps soon, resurrection near. Al-Qurṭubī says yawma yadʿūkum indicates Day He will call you for resurrection, you will respond with praise, praising Him, as all respond, as creation.

**Thinking You Remained Only Little While**

Wa-taẓunnūna in labithtum illā qalīlan — and you will think you remained not except little, only little while. Wa-taẓunnūna from ẓ-n-n, to think, assume, from ẓanna, to think. In labithtum from l-b-th, to remain, stay, tarry, from labitha, to remain, stay. Illā qalīlan, except little, only little while, from q-l-l, little, small, few, from qalīl, little. Al-Ṭabarī explains that you will think, assume, you remained in world, in graves, only little while, short time, as time passes quickly, as previous chapter said when Hour comes criminals swear they remained not except hour, etc., and as Qur'an says they will think they remained only little while, as worldly life short compared to Hereafter, as Hereafter far greater in rank and favour, as verse 21 says. Al-Rāzī says taẓunnūna in labithtum illā qalīlan indicates that on Day call, you will think worldly life, grave stay, was only little while, short, as time perception changes, as Hereafter long, worldly short.

**Why Day Call And Respond Praising And Little While**

Why Day call, respond praising, thinking remained only little while? Because resurrection perhaps soon, near, as previous verse said perhaps soon, and Day He will call you, you will respond praising Him, thinking remained only little while, showing nearness, shortness of worldly life, grave stay, compared to Hereafter, which is far greater, eternal, as verse 21 Hereafter far greater in rank and favour. The response with praise, bi-ḥamdihi, indicates that even disbelievers will praise Him on Day, acknowledge, as all will respond, as all things glorify Him with praise, but you cannot comprehend their glorification, and on Day you will respond with praise, as Day call.

**Connection To Bones And Ashes And Stones Iron And Whatever Harder And First Creation And Perhaps Soon**

Verses 49-51 bones and ashes new creation mocking, stones or iron, whatever harder, who will bring back, One Who created first time, shake heads when, perhaps soon. Verse 52 Day He will call you respond praising Him thinking remained only little while, showing resurrection soon, Day call, response with praise, short stay, as proof that resurrection near, not far, as perhaps soon, and that worldly life short, little while, compared to Hereafter far greater, so prepare, believe, not mock.

**Link To Command At Hand And Hour And Little While**

Previous chapter opened with command at hand, do not hasten it, and said criminals swear they remained not except hour, etc. This verse says Day He will call you respond praising Him thinking remained only little while, similar theme: worldly life, grave stay, short, little while, Hour near, command at hand, perhaps soon, so do not hasten, do not mock, believe, prepare.

**Linguistic Note On Yad'ukum And Tastajibuna And Hamd And Labithtum And Qalilan**

Yawm from y-w-m, Day. Yadʿūkum from d-ʿ-w, to call, summon. Tastajībūna from j-w-b, to respond, answer, from istajāba. Ḥamd from ḥ-m-d, praise. Taẓunnūna from ẓ-n-n, to think, assume. Labithtum from l-b-th, to remain, stay, tarry, from labitha. Qalīl from q-l-l, little, small, few. The verse's structure — Day He will call you then you will respond with His praise and you will think you remained not except little — with Day call, response with praise, thinking remained only little, shows resurrection Day, response praising, short stay perception, nearness, shortness of worldly life compared to Hereafter.

**How This Verse Teaches Resurrection Day Call And Short Stay**

This verse teaches resurrection Day call and short stay perception: On Day He will call you, summon you for resurrection, you will instantly respond, answer, by praising Him, with His praise, thinking, assuming you remained in world, graves, only little while, short time, as worldly life short, grave stay short, compared to Hereafter far greater, eternal, as Hereafter far greater in rank and favour. Al-Qurṭubī says believer should believe Day He will call, you will respond praising Him, and that worldly life short, little while, so prepare for Hereafter, believe, follow most upright guidance, wisdom, leading to mighty reward, appreciated striving, far greater rank and favour, while disbelievers mock, say bones and ashes new creation, stones, iron, whatever harder, who will bring back, shake heads when, but perhaps soon, Day call, etc., and will think remained only little while, but painful punishment prepared.

**A Reflection On Responding With Praise And Little While**

Bi-ḥamdihi — with His praise, praising Him — indicates that on Day call, you will respond praising Him, acknowledging, glorifying, as all things glorify Him with praise, seven heavens, earth, all those in them glorify Him, not a thing except glorifies with praise, but you cannot comprehend their glorification, He is Forbearing Forgiving, and on Day you will respond with praise, even disbelievers will praise, acknowledge, as Day call, as all respond. In labithtum illā qalīlan — you remained not except little, only little while — indicates shortness of worldly life, grave stay, compared to Hereafter, which is far greater, eternal, so worldly life little, short, not to be sole goal, fleeting, ʿājilah, hasty, immediate, but Hereafter, ākhirah, lasting, far greater in rank and favour, so desire Hereafter, strive accordingly, be believer, appreciated striving, mighty reward.
