## UNVERIFIED items (16)

All are points where the tafsīr tradition supplies detail the Qur'an does not, and where I could not confirm a chain of transmission:

1. 27:3 — saying attributed to al-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī, no isnād checked.
2. 27:18 — the name given to the ant in some tafsīr works.
3. 27:20 — reports of the hoopoe's specific duties (water-detection) in Sulaymān's service.
4. 27:23 — the name Bilqīs and her genealogy (later Islamic historical tradition).
5. 27:27 — ḥadīth on God's love of excuses, cited by al-Qurṭubī; collection unconfirmed.
6. 27:35 — detailed descriptions of the queen's gift.
7. 27:40 — the names Āṣif ibn Barkhiyā and Khiḍr for the man with knowledge of the Book.
8. 27:44 — stories of the queen's jinn ancestry, her legs, and a marriage to Sulaymān (rejected as *isrāʾīliyyāt*).
9. 27:49 — the three-day interval between the hamstringing of the she-camel and the conspiracy.
10. 27:51 — accounts of how the nine conspirators died.
11. 27:53 — survivors settling in Palestine or Ḥaḍramawt; the figure of four thousand; an etymology.
12. 27:72 — identification of the "near portion" with the Battle of Badr (al-Rāzī and others).
13. 27:82 — physical descriptions of the beast (al-Rāzī, Ibn Kathīr, ath-Thaʿlabī, al-Qurṭubī).
14. 27:87 — identification of those exempted from the terror (martyrs / named angels / prophets and saints).
15. 27:88 — the anecdote about Junayd al-Baghdādī reciting this verse.
16. 27:89 — al-Rāzī's inference that believers' punishment is not eternal.
