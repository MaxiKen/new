## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:85

> **Whosoever intercedes for a good cause shall receive a share of it; and whosoever intercedes for an evil cause shall share in its burden; and God is Sustainer of all things.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Man Yashfaʿ Shafāʿatan Ḥasanatan: The Intercessor's Share-Law**

The battle-and-governance pericope suddenly opens into a general social law — one of the ummah's most recited formulas of communal ethics: *man yashfaʿu shafāʿatan ḥasanatan yakun lahu naṣībun minhā* — "whoever intercedes a good intercession shall have for himself a share of it": *yashfaʿu* — from sh-f-ʿ — to pair, double, yoke-to (its root-image being *joining another's plea to one's own* — advocacy as partnership: the intercessor adding his weight onto the case-cass onto another's case); *shafāʿatan ḥasanatan* — a good advocacy — named with the Book's standard beauty-adjective; and the promise: *naṣībun minhā* — a share *from it* (the inheritance-word again — v. 7's far naṣīb familiar from the sūrah's own estate-chapters — now turned moral: advocacy itself earns a *bequest* from the cause advanced) — al-al-Qurṭubī's register the doctors' elaboration at once: the intercessor's share is not cut *from* the principal's reward but granted fresh from God in *parallel* (the hadith-capsule lodging it:

> *"Whoever guides to good has the like of the reward of its doer, without that diminishing from the doer's own reward aught; and whoever guides to evil has the like of the burden of its doer, without that diminishing from his burden aught."* (Muslim 2674 — Kitāb al-ʿIlm; with Tirmidhī's cousins 2670 & 2671)

— reward flowing *twice*, without siphoning: the divine economy no zero-sum ledger).

**Wa Man Yashfaʿ Shafāʿatan Sayyiʾatan Yakun Lahu Kiflun Minhā: The Counter-Share**

Then the mirror — and its vocabulary shifts from naṣīb to something heavier: *wa man yashfaʿu shafāʿatan sayyiʾatan yakun lahu kiflun minhā* — "and whoever intercedes an evil intercession shall have for himself a *kifl* of it": *sayyiʾah* — evil — and *kifl* — portion, burden-lot — from k-f-l — to be answerable-for, to carry liability (the commentators noting the deliberate word-swap: for good, a *naṣīb* — free gift-share; for evil, a *kifl* — weight of responsibility, kafālah-cognate — the man legally morally bonded to what he boosted: the guarantor-syntax of sin) — and al-Rāzī's note seizes the moral architecture: advocacy is *agency-multiplied* — the advocator who stands behind a deed shares in every downstream consequence the deed begets (cf. the hadith Max the celebrated Muslim 1017 — *"whoever makes a good precedent in Islam has its reward and the reward of those who work by it after him... and whoever makes an evil precedent has its burden and the burden of those who work by it after him"*) — the battlefield-face of the doctrine at hand in the asbāb-roll (al-Ṭabarī's and al-Rāzī's collations): those who *promoted* the marching — equipping contingents, arguing men forward, loaning mounts to the fighters — being *shufaʿāʾ ḥasanah-*intercessors, whose share of each detachment's wages was certified by heaven; and from the other bench — those whispering-out the marching those who dissuaded Madinese from answering the Prophet's muster-call (vv. 72, 83's tarriers and rumor-mon the fear-mongers) — being the *kifl*-intercessors of evil, bearing their cut of every missed obligation their discouragement spawned — with the classical application-rules drawn from the verse standing to this day in qānūn in Islamic legal-ethical lore: aid a plaintiff's just case — earn a share of the judgment's good; defend the wrong man knowing his wrong — bond yourself to his to his entire liability.

**Wa Kāna Allāhu ʿalā Kulli Shayʾin Muqītā: Muqīt — Nourisher-Watcher Over All**

The seal names its rarest attribute-token of the sūrah: *wa kāna Allāhu ʿalā kulli shayʾin muqītan* — "God is Sustainer of all things": *muqītab muqīt* — from a-w-t from q-w-t — provision of food-strength — the commentators' glà the commentators' array of glosses fixing its triplet: (1) *Nourisher* — He who metes every being's sustenance-exact, down to the down to the grain beneath the stone (11:6's "no moving creature is there upon the earth but that God provides its sustenance"); (2) *Powerful/Capable* — muqtadir — over all things; (3) *Witness-Preserver* — ḥafīẓ — watching everything for its accounting — the word's four occurrences across the Book sealing its rare-stra its select deployment (here; 33:58 cf. its three cousins: count: 4:85 plus its resisters standing main suf: its companions across the Revelation being likewise divine-self-testimonies of providential trusteeship) — and the reason the seal belongs exactly to v. 85 is disclosed by the tradition at once: *the keeper of the share-and-burden ledgers must be the One who supplies all things* — for only the universal Nourisher can grant rewards without robbing the original earners (the naṣīb is fresh mint after fresh mint — sent down like rain — and the kifl is weighed out with the same precision) — so the morality-r so the ethical doctrine inherits its ground: be an intercessor — in the family, in the war-camp, before the king, between quarreling spouses, at every desk of every administration — and only ever measure *whose case what you push upon whom*: shafāʿah is a currency God mints twice — and the good advocate joins the ranks of the few of whom the Prophet reported his glad tidings about reported across the Sahīh his standing hope-proclamation (Muslim 2636's register of the celebrated parallel: *"the best charity? no — *the best of people are those most beneficial to people"*) — with God's own muqīt-attributes standing over their ledgers forever.
