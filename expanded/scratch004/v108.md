## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:108

> **They seek to hide themselves from men, but they cannot hide from God. He is with them when they conspire by night with words displeasing to Him, and God encompasses whatsoever they do.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Yastakhfūna mina al-Nāsi wa Lā Yastakhfūna mina Allāh: The Two Audiences of Shame**

The court-block's camera now moves from the plea-chamber to the thief's real operating room — the night-parlor: *yastakhfūna mina al-nāsi wa lā yastakhfūna mina Allāh* — "they conceal from men — and they do not conceal from God": *yastakhfūna* — they seek-hiding — from kh-f-y — concealment (its standing Book-roll grading the sinner's pathetic double-standing: shame *calibrated exactly to the wrong audience* — the sinner's whole caution-energy poured into deflecting human gaze while the One before Whom it must all be disclosed stands ignored — the commentators' standing formulation being this verse's own ratio the standing formulation: al-khafiyy cf. 13:10's — "equal are you in secrecy — he who conceals his speech and he who proclaims it, and he who hides by night and he who goes abroad by day"; 10:61's "not an atom's weight escapes your Lord"; 20:7's "He knows the secret and the more hidden"; and the sūrah's own paired piece at v. 81 — "they hide within their souls what they do not disclose to thee" — the paired spy-exposure of the whole pericope now completing itself across the two peric the two passages) — and the operational detail follows: the Prophet's the clan-plot itself running by night: evidence-smuggling plans, planted stolen armor, the protected innocence the framed alibi — the standing asbāb recalled here again as illustration of the single principle: every night-parlor operates beneath a lamp it cannot see.

**Wa Huwa Maʿahum Idh Yubayyitūna Mā Lā Yarḍā mina al-Qawl: The Third in Their Chamber**

Then the sūrah's most terrible of consolations — God's own attendance at their conspiracy: *wa huwa maʿahum idh yubayyitūna mā lā yarḍā mina al-qawl* — "He is *with* them when they night-plot with speech that displeases Him": *maʿahum* — with them (maʿiyyah — accompaniment — the theologians' standing care: the He-is-with-them the pronoun triggered the standing commentary-protocol on the verse (al-Rāzī, al-al-Qurṭubī, Ibn Taymiyyah): *with* here means *encompassing them in knowledge, hearing, sight, and recording* — not in approbation or spatial lest men read spatial-presence lest anyone ever imagine bodily presence — cf. the paired party the twin-text's own guard: 58:7 — "He is the fourth of three, and the sixth of five — with them wheresoever they may be" — always in knowledge; and the ummah's soul-care the soul-doctors deriving from their own nuance: God's *maʿiyyah* speaks double-tongue — comfort-thread comfort to the lonely believer ("fear not — He is with us") — woe to the conspirator ("He is the third listener of their whisper")) — and the deed described: *yubayyitūna* — night-scheming (from b-y-t by night — the same participle as v. 81's *yubayyitūna* — the sūrah's deliberate echo across its two dark-chambers: the hypocrite cabal slandering the Prophet's obedience — v. 81; the Ṭuʿmah-clan staging the planted evidence — v. 108 — God's authorial scheme returning the identical verb to brand *night-labor itself* as the standing engine of public injustice: false verdicts are *conceived by night*, in curtains behind curtains, long before they are pronounced under noon) — *mā lā yarḍā mina al-qawl* — *that of speech which displeases Him* — the phrasing's own finesse its standing academic note: not *displeasing-to-God* as mere synonym of *sinful* but framed around *qawl* — speech: the whole crime-engine runs through said-words — doctored testimony, rehearsed accusation, alibi-scripts (the standing modern echoing the standing lesson of the jurisprudes ever since: the conspiracy of a false case is a *literary* factory — its product being polished speeches designed to move courts — and heaven's note is simply that the critic above every other critic already gave His review) — and the seal lands with the impassable name: *wa kāna Allāhu bimā yaʿmalūna muḥīṭan* — "God is encompassing whatsoever they do": *muḥīṭ* — from ḥ-w-ṭ — to surround, encompass — the Book's standing ominous attribute (3:120's "God is encompassing of what they do"; 4:126's "God is encompassing of all things"; 8:47's; 11:92's; 41:54's; 85:20's "and God is, behind them, encompassing" — the classical roll of its uses being uniformly shadowed: the word visits only scenes of defiant wrongdoing of confident wrongdoing — as if the culprit had built his fortress of private space and heaven simply replies: *muḥīṭ* — fortresses themselves lie within His ring) — the verse thereby sealing the entire courtroom-block with its own epistemology-of-justice: no chamber of man is truly private — no plot-script unread; no staged no planted no planted costume-piece resting quietly where its planter laid it: the Encompassing One sees each coil before the court of morning even sits.
