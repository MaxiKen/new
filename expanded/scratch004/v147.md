## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:147

> **What would God do with your punishment, if you give thanks and believe? And God is ever Thankful, Knowing.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Mā Yafʿalu Allāhu bi-ʿAdhābikum: Punishment as Divine Non-Interest**

The dossier of doom ends in a question of pure mercy — one of the Book's most tender confrontations of human math with divine purpose: *mā yafʿalu Allāhu bi-ʿadhābikum in shakartum wa āmantum* — "what would God do with your punishment, if you give thanks and believe?": the question-form here not interrogative but *expulsive* — a standing rhetorical figure of removal: punishment imagined as a commodity God has no *use* for — the commentators (al-Ṭabarī; al-Rāzī records the standing formulation: God has no need of punishing you; your punishment gives Him nothing; your pleasure and gratitude give you everything) — the doctrine sealed by the standing Name-pair of v. 131's file (*ghanī ḥamīd* — Self-Sufficient, Praiseworthy): the Self-Sufficient gains nothing from torturing His creatures, and therefore punishment enters the divine economy only as justice's response to refusal — never as divine appetite. Its standing Book-kin gathers the same expulsive-shape: its register of divine disinterest in the sinner's ruin: 39:7 — "if you disbelieve — lo, God is free of need of you — though He is not pleased with disbelief for His servants — but if you give thanks, He is pleased with it for you" — the near-twin of our verse's logic (His pleasure registered upon gratitude, His displeasure upon refusal — need never entering the account); 14:7’s standing covenant ("if you give thanks I shall surely increase you"); 4:131-132 above ("unto God belongs whatsoever is in the heavens..."); and the standing qudsī-complement in Muslim's register 2577 (the long hadith of divine absolutes: "righteousness does not increase My kingdom, nor wickedness decrease it"). The question thus legislates a theology of divine freedom: punishment is never God's *project*; it is only ever His *correspondence* to human refusal — correspondence that evaporates the moment its addressee corrects his own address.

**In Shakartum wa Āmantum: Gratitude and Belief as the Standing Antidote-Pair**

The condition is two-stemmed and beautiful in its ordering: *in shakartum wa āmantum* — "if you give thanks and believe": *shakara* — to give thanks — placed *before* īmān — a syntactic surprise the commentators savor (al-Zamakhsharī's standing note exploring the inversion): gratitude as the very *door* of belief — the thankful heart already bowing toward its Benefactor before its creed completes; and in immediate context the precise cure-codeword for the dossier's patients: the hypocrite's standing disease had been riyāʾ (v. 142 — performance for observers) — the religion of audience-seeking — and *shukr* is its exact pharmacological opposite: religion of Benefactor-facing — thanks directed not at the witnesses but at the Source. The tradition's standing gratitude-file assembles beside the verse: 14:7 — "your Lord proclaimed: if you give thanks, I shall surely increase you; but if you disbelieve — verily My punishment is severe"; 31:12 — Luqmān's standing "whoever gives thanks gives thanks for his own soul"; 34:13 — "work, O house of David, in thanks — and few of My servants are the thankful"; 39:7 above; 54:35; and the Prophet's standing embodiment — ʿĀʾishah's celebrated night-report (Bukhārī's standing register 4836; Muslim 2820): he prayed until his feet split — "shall I not be a thankful servant?" — gratitude named not as the minimum of religion but as its summit-occupation.

**Wa Kāna Allāhu Shākiran ʿAlīman: The Thankful God — the Semantic Miracle of the Pair**

Then the verse performs its standing linguistic wonder — the Name the commentators treat as among the Book's most astonishing revelations of the divine character: *wa kāna Allāhu shākiran ʿalīman* — "and God is ever Thankful, Knowing": *shākir* — the Thankful One — God named by the very predicate the verse asks of His creatures: *shakartum* — *you* thank — answered by *shākir* — *He* thanks — the semantic correspondence the commentators (the standing note from the earliest language-commentaries forward) call the verse's built-in music: the same root sounded from both parties within a few words — human gratitude echoing upward, divine Gratitude answering downward — an audible reconciliation after the dossier's nine verses of descending darkness. The commentators unfold *al-Shākir* with its standing doctrine: God's "thanks" are His *acceptance and recompense* — His counting little obedience as much, His praising the servant for what He Himself granted ("He thanks them for what they do — and increases them of His bounty" — 35:30; its standing register: 2:158 — "whoever does good of his own accord — verily God is Thankful, Knowing"; 9:121 — "nor do they spend any outlay... but it is recorded to them, that God may repay them the best of what they did"; 64:17 — "if you lend God a goodly loan — He shall double it for you and forgive you — and God is Thankful, Forbearing"; 42:23). And *ʿalīm* — Knowing — closing the audit: thankfulness is known even when unwitnessed (its standing complement: gratitude-hidden recorded as surely as gratitude-proclaimed — the riyāʾ-poisoned dossier thereby answered in full: the only Spectator who matters keeps the only record that counts). The hypocrisy-dossier (vv. 137–147) thus ends not in the basement of v. 145 but in this sweet expulsion-question — *what would God do with your punishment?* — the divine answer being: nothing at all, if only gratitude and belief make punishment obsolete — and the Name of the Thankful One standing over the closed file as its seal: the God who thanks the thankful, and knows.
