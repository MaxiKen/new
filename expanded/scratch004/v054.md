## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:54

> **Or do they envy men on account of what God has given them of His Bounty? We gave the House of Abraham the Book and Wisdom, and We granted them a mighty sovereignty.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Am Yaḥsudūna al-Nāsa: Envy Named, at Last, as the Engine**

The pericope's *am*-series penetrates deeper still — past purchase (v. 44), past tongue-work (v. 46), past lying (v. 50), past the throne-verdicts (v. 51) — into the motivating chamber itself: *am yaḥsudūna al-nāsa ʿalā mā ātāhumu Allāhu min faḍlih* — "or do they envy the people for what God has granted them of His bounty?": *yaḥsudūna*, from *ḥ-s-d* — to burn at another's blessing — and *al-nās*, *the people* — either the Prophet himself (al-Ṭabarī's concrete: the Book and the emigration-crowns given Muhammad) or the whole household of Islam (al-Qurṭubī's width: those who came into bounty with him) — the charge-sheet's engine thereby being confessed by heaven's own diagnostician: all the procedure of vv. 44–53 — error-buyings, mock-words, self-purity, endorsed idols — are *symptoms*; the fever beneath is ḥasad — the interior verdict that God's division was *unfair* (the Book's own disclosure of envy across its passages: 2:109 — "many of the People of the Book wish to restore you to unbelief out of envy from within themselves, though the truth has become manifest to them"; 3:19's community-splits born of envy *after knowledge came*; and 2:90's perfect mapping of the crime — *"disbelieving in what God sent down, grudging that God sends of His bounty upon whom He wills of His servants"* — for envy is, precisely, *quarrel with a gift-Giver over whom He chooses*) — and with the naming the pericope's psychology completes itself: hatred of *His choice* — the primal sin twice-cited by the commentators on this verse: Iblīs over Adam (the heavenly first — 7:11–13 — "what prevented you...? I am better than he" — the *comparison* being envy's whole syllogism) and Cain over Abel (the earthly first — 5:27–31) — the two standing stations of ḥasad between which the portion-bearers' posture was placed by the doctors: heaven-born, earth-learned.

**The Prophet's Envy-Medicine Beside the Verse**

The sūrah's diagnosis comes accompanied already by the Prophet's two pharmacist's jars (the traditions the commentators, al-Qurṭubī above all, lodge immediately beneath this clause):

> *"Beware of envy, for envy devours good deeds as fire devours wood — or he said: grass."* (Abū Dāwūd 4903 — narrated by Abū Hurayrah)

— the fire-metaphor measuring exactly what v. 54 displays: their rage *ate their own Book-wealth* while leaving its object untouched (for God's bounty upon Muhammad and his community only grew — He appointed as 4:54's own tail discloses) — and the subtler second jar:

> *"I have not seen an oppressor more like the oppressed than the envier: constant burning, shallow breathing, grief-cur sighs folded into the chest."* (the sages' citation through al-Qurṭubī's commentary register and the adab-collections)

— the envier being himself both ẓālim and maẓlūm — the violence consuming its owner — the Ḥasan al-Baṣrī-line summary afterwards ubiquitous in the piety-books: *"I have not seen an oppressor more like the oppressed than the envier"* — the same sentence in its shorter authentic roll-call — so that the sūrah's reader learns double-protection: *against envying men* (the audit of pleasure of pleasure at blessing-distribution) and *against being burned by envy-engines around him* — the Book's own proha its own shield-sūrah 113 already in his mouth every dawn: *"from the evil of an envier when he envies."*

**Āta- We Gave the House of Abraham: Precedence as the Envy-Cure**

The argument's answer arrives as a devastating precedent: *fa-qad ātaynā āla Ibrāhīma al-kitāba wa al-ḥikmata wa ātaynāhum mulkan ʿaẓīmā* — "We gave Abraham's House the Book and the Wisdom, and granted them a tremendous sovereignty": the syntax weights its own anaphora — *ātaynā*... *wa ātaynāhum* — We gave... and We gave them — heaven *reciting its record* against the grudgers: (1) *the Book* — the scrolls of Abraham (87:19 — "the scrolls of Abraham and Moses") and the great dispensations of the house thereafter (Torah, Psalms, Gospel in their line); (2) *the Wisdom (al-ḥikmah)* — inspiration never book-bound: judgment's light behind the written word (the pair kitāb/ḥikmah becoming the Book's own repeated formula for prophet-treas for prophets' equipment: 2:231, 3:48, 4:113 below for Muhammad himself, 62:2); and (3) *a mighty mulk* — the kingdom-line of David and Solomon (vast estate and temple — the wealth-kings whose empires their own people had gloried in) — the whole clause disclosing heaven's logic: *your objection to bounty given Quresh Quraysh has no legs* — God's freedom in distribution is Abraham-anchored, David-anchored, Solomon-anchored — one unbroken record of *outsize gift* — so to burn now over a prophet raised from Ishmael's line (who stands inside Abraham's house! — 2:125–129, 14:37's own landing of the Sacred House's prayer): is either to disown the family-file or to pretend the Father saw wrongly — al-Zamakhsharī's razor adding the worldly counterstroke for the envious of the Prophet's many marriages (33:50's exemption): the clause's own House-argument rebuts even that, since David and Solomon of their *own* house had wives beyond counting. The Shiʿi tradition (citing Jaʿfar al-Ṣādiq through al-Ṭabrisī's roll) extends the clause's "men" to the Prophet's *family* — imāmah granted as bounty — the Sunni which the broadest reading already holds inside itself: *bounty-envy in every generation indicts the same ancient disease* — refusing what God gives whom He wills — the medicine forever the same: *count not another's gifts; audit your own ingratitude* — for the House of Abraham's file shows: when God appoints, He appoints on a scale that outlasts the sul the sul outlasts the sun of every envier.
