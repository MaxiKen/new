**Wa La-ʾUḍillannahum: The Astray-Program First**

The accursed one's vow now unfolds into its celebrated four-chamber program — the longest single self-exposure of Satan in the Book: *wa la-ʾuḍillannahum* — "and I shall surely lead them astray": the first limb being his standing mission-verb (ḍ-l-l standing ḍalāl already sealed v. 116's own astray-verdict — the Book deliberately reusing the Book's own echo: astray-work is his standing plank — cf. 4:60's "Satan desires to lead them astray far off"; 15:39's vow 15:39; 7:16's watch- his garden-page his standing scheme from the first from the garden: 2:36's and 7:20's standing satanic induced-f induced-fall) — and *wa la-ʾumanniyannahum* — "and I shall surely stir desires in them": *tamnī* — the amānī machine from m-n-y — wish, longing, vain-hope (the root the Book imposes upon wish-projection — the commentators' standing taxonomy of his wish-work in two aisles: (1) worldly wishes — long life, endless postponement of repentance, imagined safety, the whole "sawfa" — "soon/later" economy of the of the put-off soul; (2) otherworldly wishes — undeserved forgiveness, automatic paradise, salvation without deeds — al-Rāzī's standing diagnosis: Satan flips fires *false promissory notes* on both banks — the world and the hereafter — "be at ease ya his whispered pledge: *you are of the elect, God will forgive, there is much time*;" with its standing Book-aud audit compiling 2:111's its own its own standing target at the Book's own the standing exposure at 2:111 & 111c the Book's own parallel from its standing own at the general exposure of amānī-check at 2:111-112 — "they say: none shall enter paradise but Jews and Christians — those are their vain hopes — amāniyyuhum"; and its standing sideways beside its favorite return at 3:24 — "that is because they said: the fire shall not touch us but for days numbered — their forged lies deceived them in their religion")

**Fa-La-ʾĀmurannahum fa-La-Yubattikunna Ādāna al-Anʿām: The Ear-Slitting, and the Alteration-Vow**

Then the two concrete command-limbs the tradition never tires of unpacking: *fa-la-ʾāmurannahum fa-la-yubattikunna ādāna al-anʿām* — "I shall command them — and they will slit the ears of cattle": *tabtīk* — cutting-though from b-t-k — cleaving off (the standing ritual of the idolaters: the *baḥīrah* — the ear-slit she-camel released onto the pasture-law the old pagan law: when a she-camel bore five offspring with the fifth a male, her ear was slit and she was consecrated-to-the-idol — now untouchable for burden nor for burden or milk (al-Ṭabarī's standing definition;
