## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:145

> **Verily the hypocrites shall be in the lowest depth of the Fire, and thou shalt never find for them a helper —**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Inna al-Munāfiqīna fī al-Darki al-Asfali min al-Nār: The Basement of the Basement**

The dossier now delivers its sentence of location — among the most fearful half-dozen words in the entire Book: *inna al-munāfiqīna fī al-darki al-asfali min al-nār* — "verily the hypocrites shall be in the lowest depth of the Fire": *dark* — a depth, a level-bottom — a word whose morphology (its standing lexical note: from d-r-k — to reach the bottom, to overtake) already carries descent; and *al-asfal* — the superlative of *sāfil* — low — *the lowest* — grammar announcing that the Fire itself is terraced, and that beneath its levels for the merely disbelieving yawns another level — the *sub-basement* reserved for the false. The commentators from al-Ṭabarī forward read the comparative precisely: the hypocrites outrank in punishment those whose creed they privately shared — the open idolater of Mecca graded *above* the secret traitor of Madīnah — and the standing reasons gathered across the literature (al-Bayḍāwī, al-Rāzī, al-Zamakhsharī): (1) hypocrisy compounds unbelief with *lying* — the whole apparatus of fabricated testimony ("we bear witness that thou art indeed the Messenger of God — and God knows that thou art His Messenger — and God bears witness that the hypocrites are liars" — 63:1); (2) it adds *contempt* — belittling the religion it publicly wears; (3) it functions as *espionage* — the hypocrite's position inside the community converting his unbelief into active sabotage: spying, demoralizing, secrets traded to the enemy (v. 141's invoice-file above) — the internal enemy more destructive than ten external ones; and (4) — the sealing reason — it *mocks the remedial arrangement itself*: the door of repentance exists exactly where the hypocrite pretends to stand — inside the community, inside the prayer-line — so his refusal of it is more deliberate than any outsider's distance. Al-Rāzī's standing judgment summarizes the dossier's cumulative force: hypocrisy is a state more evil than disbelief.

**The Terraced Fire and the Standing Fear of the Companions**

The tradition's reception of this verse is itself part of its exegesis — for the verse's most documented effect was not upon the hypocrites but upon the *sincere*: Ibn Abī Mulaykah's standing report (its standing register in the tafsīr-collections and the Ṣaḥīḥ-tradition of al-Bukhārī's Book of Tafsīr): "I met thirty of the Companions of the Prophet — every one of them feared hypocrisy for himself — none of them said he had the faith of Gabriel and Michael." Ḥasan al-Baṣrī's standing formulation orders the psychology: "no one fears it but a believer, and no one feels secure from it but a hypocrite" — the mark of the disease being precisely the confidence that one does not have it. ʿUmar ibn al-Khaṭṭāb's standing scene with Ḥudhayfah ibn al-Yamān — the Prophet's confidant to whom the secret names of the hypocrites had been entrusted — remains the standing icon: the Caliph of Islam, at whose approach Satan was said to flee (Bukhārī's standing register 3683: "whenever you take a path, Satan takes a path other than yours"), asking privately: *am I, by God, among them?* — and Ḥudhayfah's "no" treasured by the tradition as reassurance — yet the asking itself installed as the standing spiritual discipline of the ummah: only those who audit their worship by the kusālā-rubric of v. 142 — lazy standing, little remembrance — keep the basement-door locked against themselves.

**Wa Lan Tajida Lahum Naṣīran: The Court Without Appeal or Counsel**

The verdict doubles itself with its standing annihilation-clause: *wa lan tajida lahum naṣīran* — "and thou shalt never find for them a helper": *lan* — the perpetual never — addressed again to the Prophet, the community's supreme advocate: the verse of v. 143's structure repeated from abyss-side: no way (v. 143) and now no helper (v. 145) — the hypocrite's file closing with the two absences of eschatological bankruptcy: no road and no intercessor. Its standing Book-register gathers the form: 4:52 above — "those are the ones whom God has cursed — and whomsoever God curses, thou shalt find no helper for him"; 3:56 — "as for those who disbelieve, I shall punish them with a terrible punishment in this world and the Hereafter — and they shall have no helpers"; 2:86, 2:270, 3:192, 9:74's own 33:17; the several standing the register of *lā naṣīr* as the Book's standing signature of total estrangement (3:22; 3:126; 5:72; 9:113; 29:22). And the commentators note the justice of the symmetries: the hypocrites had spent their careers amassing helpers — the clans of v. 139, the Meccan connections of v. 141 — as substitutes for the one Helper; the lowest depth is precisely the territory where substitute-helpers are administratively impossible: every alliance of the fence-sitters dissolving at the horizon where it was purchased to operate.

**The Exception Coming: Verse 146's Door in the Wall**

Yet the Book, which has now descended lower than any other verse of the Qur'ān reaches — *the lowest depth* — reserves for the very next verse its standing reversal: "save those who repent and make amends, and hold fast to God, and devote their religion entirely to God — those are with the believers, and God shall grant the believers a great reward." The commentators insist the juxtaposition is the dossier's whole pedagogy: the severity of v. 145 is not the abolition of hope but its *pricing* — the deep cell announced so that the exit-terms, when they arrive, shine with their true value: the same Book that assigns the hypocrite the absolute bottom assigns the repentant hypocrite — one verse later — *maʿa al-muʾminīn* — "with the believers" — the association he never truly occupied in life restored to him in the Hereafter through the medicine of v. 137's own diagnosis: while breath remains, the pendulum of v. 143's dhabdhabah can still be swung, not merely watched. The lowest depth then functions in the economy of Mercy as what the standing tradition calls a *burden of warning* — its standing register in the Prophet's counsels: the Fire described in detail not to extinguish hope but to make the exit-legible — 4:145 the pit, 4:146 the ladder, both verses one rope.
