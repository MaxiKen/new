## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:168

> **Verily those who disbelieve and do wrong — God was not one to forgive them, nor to guide them unto a path,**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Inna Alladhīna Kafarū wa Ẓalamū: Disbelief and Wrongdoing Composed**

The verdict-file continues with a second doubled charge — the composition shifting from v. 167's *disbelief + barring* to *disbelief + ẓulm*: *inna alladhīna kafarū wa ẓalamū* — "verily those who disbelieve and do wrong": *ẓalamū* — from ẓ-l-m — wrongdoing, injustice: the standing comprehensive of every thing put out of its place (its standing Book-defining stations: 31:13 — Luqmān's master-axiom — "verily associating partners with God is a tremendous wrong — la-ẓulmun ʿaẓīm"; 2:51; 2:59; 2:229's limits-file; 4:64 above in this sūrah — "they wronged their souls"; 4:110 — "whoever does evil or wrongs his own soul, then seeks God's pardon, shall find God Forgiving, Merciful"; 3:135 — "those who — when they commit an indecency or wrong their souls — remember God and seek forgiveness for their sins" — the double-composition standing across the Book: kufr as the root-wrong, the displacement of God Himself from His centrality; and every derivative wrong as the echoed displacement in creation) — the commentators noting why the sūrah chooses *ẓulm* here at the summit of its indictment: the entire chapter has been one sustained inventory of ẓulm and its remedies — the wronged orphan (4:2-10), the wronged wife (4:19-22; 4:127-130), the wronged slave (4:36), the wronged adversary-before-testimony (4:135), the wronged covenant (4:154-155), the wronged prophets (4:155), the wronged Mary (4:156), the wronged Jesus (4:157): the sūrah of wrong-and-justice thus sealing its indictment with the genus-word that comprehends them all.

**Lam Yakuni Allāhu Li-Yaghfira Lahum: The Non-Forgiveness of a State**

Then the rarest register of the Book again — already met at v. 137 in the waverers' file — *lam yakuni Allāhu li-yaghfira lahum* — "God was not one to forgive them": the durative-negative construction (*lam yakun* + imperfect) declaring a settled divine disposition *against* — not a snapshot verdict but the standing posture of unalloyed Justice toward unrepented confirmed disbelief-with-wrongdoing. The commentators gather the verse into the standing register of the Book's "no forgiveness" files and map the register's boundaries with standing care: (1) 4:48 and 4:116 — "verily God forgives not that partners be associated with Him — but He forgives what is less than that, for whom He wills" — the shirk-exception absolute upon dying-unrepentant; (2) 4:137 above — the progressive disbelievers-unto-death; (3) 9:79-80 — the rider to the mocking-file — "if Thou forgivest them seventy times, God will not forgive them — that because they disbelieved in God and His Messenger"; 63:5-6's twin; (4) 47:34 — "verily those who disbelieve and bar from God's way, then die disbelievers — God will not forgive them" — the standing twin of our verse's own clause with its death-qualifier explicit; and the standing majority-rule of al-Rāzī and al-Zamakhsharī across all five: **every** non-forgiveness verdict of the Book adjudicates a *state* — disbelief-and-wrongdoing confirmed, persisted in, sealed in death — never an announcement that the repentance-door is barred from a living person (4:146's own exception-architecture standing within this very chapter: "save those who repent and make amends" — even of the lowest-depth hypocrites; 3:89's standing counterpart to 3:90's non-acceptance: "save those who repent thereafter and make amends — for God is Forgiving, Merciful"; and 39:53's standing universal-promissory: "say: O My servants who have transgressed against their souls — despair not of God's mercy — verily God forgives all sins — verily He is the Forgiving, the Merciful") — the non-forgiveness of vv. 137, 168, and their Book-companions thus standing as the Book's pronouncement upon the *condition*, while the repentance-door stands as its standing counter-pronouncement upon every *person* still alive to use it.

**Wa Lā Li-Yahdiyahum Ṭarīqan: All Ways Withdrawn**

The penalty compounds: *wa lā li-yahdiyahum ṭarīqan* — "nor to guide them unto a path": the standing guidance-withdrawal completing the non-forgiveness: forgiveness is heavenward amnesty; guidance is earthward direction — both revoked for the state named: the disbelieving wrongdoer sealed in his dispensation wanders without a road *in either world*: no road in this life to felicity (the withdrawing of insight, the sealing-train of 4:155 above — "God has set a seal upon them for their disbelief") and no road in the next save one — which the next verse delivers as the Book's most chilling version of "guidance": *illā ṭarīqa jahannam* — 4:169 — the path to Hell — the only route still signposted for the sealed — the ḥujjah-removal of v. 165 ("that mankind might have no argument against God after the messengers") answering here at its severest pole: to those who had full direction-provision and disbelieved-and-wronged against it, direction stands permanently withheld; and the standing pastoral reading — the commentators indispensable balance — holds the two files together: for the persisting state: no forgiveness, no way; for the returning person: Forgiving, Merciful (4:110 once more — "whoever does evil or wrongs his own soul, then seeks God's pardon, shall find God Forgiving, Merciful") — the sūrah of justice keeping both ledgers open so that none presumes, and none despairs.
