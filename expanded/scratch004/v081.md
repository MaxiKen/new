## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:81

> **They profess obedience, but when they leave thee, a party of them conspire by night against what thou sayest. But God records what they conspire by night. So turn away from them and trust in God. God suffices as a Guardian.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Yaqūlūna Ṭāʿatan: Obedience as Public Vocabulary**

The pericope's double-portrait of the pretender-class completes its behavioral map with its most most day-night contrast clause: *wa yaqūlūna ṭāʿatan fa-idhā barazū min ʿindika bayyata ṭāʾifatun minhum ghayra alladhī taqūl* — "and they say: obedience! — yet when they depart from thee, a party of them devises by night other than what thou sayest": *ṭāʿatan* — obedience — as a verbal deposit (the noun itself their whole profession — the mask's stand the face-value offered: *they say "obedience"* — the word enough at the door) — but *fa-idhā barazū min ʿindika* — when they emerge from thy presence — baraza, to come out into the open, depart out — the presence-field of the Prophet being the very environment that manufactured the mask (in his company every claimant spoke decorously) — and *bayyata ṭāʾifatun minhum* — a party of them *nightwork* — bayyata from b-y-t: the night-spent labor of secret schemes — its three classical calibrations collated at al-Ṭabarī and al-Rāzī's bench: (1) *scheming-nights* — plot-construction after dark (the majority), contrary to the words they'd spoken by day — the renome with the sub-reading (2) *reneging* — simply taking back at night what they vowed by day — al-Ṭabarī's lean-reading; and (3) *weighing contemplation* — al-Rāzī's softer philology: pondering deeply whether obedience benefits them — the counter in all three the same diagnosis: *agreement in his presence, private court elsewhere* — the classic two-chamber life of the nominal.

**Wa Allāhu Yaktubu Mā Yubayyitūna: The Night-Ledger**

Heaven's answer upon their night-floor is the verse's most terrifying altitude: *wa Allāhu yaktubu mā yubayyitūn* — "and God writes down what they contrive by night": *yaktubu* — present-continuous writing — the scribe-verb already deployed at 4:33's own knot at 2:210 at the Book's standing ledger-doctrine: al-Zamakhsharī's elegantness al-Zamakhsharī's gloss fixing its double-valence: it is written either upon the *deed-scroll* that shall be unrolled at the Reckoning — or already *into this very Qur'an* (their schemes now immortalized as verse — read against them until the Last Day) — either way the counter-pl the sealing of the dark as utterly public property in the higher register: night's rooms being the places where men believe speech evaporates; heaven's announcement being the Book's perpetual reversal of the dark means of the dark means of the dark's anonymity (5:71 cf. 21:47's scales; 10:61's "not an atom's weight escapes Him"; 20:7's "He knows the secret and what is more concealed"; and 36:65's own testimony-of-hands-and-feet already cited at v. 42) — with the Prophet's own lucid terror lucid guarantee (Muslim 2998's the famous an-Nawawī the celebrated qudsī register of Muslim 2577 — among the forty hadith — "God has prescribed excellence in all things..." — better sideboard rather the exact the directly-SHG the directly-fitted maxim: nothing is hidden from Him — and 4:81's own short-grammar standing as the pillar of God-watching) — the believer's whole ethics drawn from this one clause: *what we plot in the dark is already written in the Light* — and He who writes is not a spy (human espionage being forbidden by his own Law — 49:12’s ban) but the Owner of the dark itself.

**Fa-Aʿriḍ ʿAnhum wa Tawakkal ʿalā Allāh: The Twin Pastoral Directives**

Then the prophetic deportment for dealing with such: *fa-aʿriḍ ʿanhum wa tawakkal ʿalā Allāh* — "so turn away from them, and trust in God": turn away — the same aʿriḍ of v. 63's admonition-triage — meaning (al-Ṭabarī's summary): leave them to their scheming without escalating to accusation-drama — no vindictive expos no public showdown — the Prophet relieved of the burden of hunting hypocrites because heaven has taken over surveillance (his assignment being *outward conduct*, as the established sunnah of dealing at the outward level alone is based in is preserved most famously in ʿUmar ibn al-Khaṭṭāb's question at Ḥudhayfah's at the funeral of ʿAbdullāh ibn Ubayy — Bukhārī 4670 's record of ʿUmar's objection — the Prophet answering ʿUmar’s objection with peaceful finality, but rather prayed over him despite the record of his insults — "God offered me a choice": praying for them as far as the limit heaven set 9:80's ceiling of seventy-times) — yet ordered no exposure-hunt) — and *tawakkal ʿalā Allāh* — trust in God — the tawakkul-coupling returning once more (v. 71's tether-the-camel duplex already established): the Prophet being told in one breath: *do not police the dark; guard instead your inward reliance* — for policing hearts would have made the Prophet a moral police-state-builder — tawakkul instead moves all files to heaven's jurisdiction — the the with the second kafā-clause with the sūrah's reunion of its ruling sufficiency-signature: *wa kafā bi-Allāhi wakīlan* — "God suffices as a Guardian": *wakīl* — the deputy-charged, the attorney-of-record, the estate-manager (the name of agency-running 39:62's "God is wakīl over all things"; 4:132's ahead cf. 4:132 and 171 and 4:81's own sister at 33:3 and 48 — "trust in God; God suffices as wakīl") — the whole pericope having now taught the believing polity its complete code against the hidden: daylight-trust (accept their ṭāʿatan), night-surrender (leave their bayyata to the writing King), and sufficiency-rest (your wakīl never sleeps) — with this clarity: the same three-step engine running every later heart under trial — accept conduct; reference the dark; rely.
