## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:165

> **Messengers as bearers of glad tidings and as warners — that mankind might have no argument against God after the messengers. And God is ever Mighty, Wise.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Rusulan Mubashshirīna wa Mundhirīna: The Two Wings of Every Mission**

The register of revelation now names the *purpose* inscribed on every messenger's commission — a standing double-title that belongs to the Book's standing characterization of prophethood itself: *rusulan mubashshirīna wa mundhirīna* — "messengers as bearers of glad tidings and as warners": *mubashshir* — from b-sh-r — the glad-word already met in its ironically inverted station at v. 138 above ("give glad tidings to the hypocrites of a painful punishment") — here restored to its standing positive office: the announcement of God's mercy, of forgiveness, of the Gardens below which rivers flow; and *mundhir* — from n-dh-r — to warn: the announcement of accountability, of the Fire its fuel is men and stones — the two titles fused as one compound office (its standing register across the Book: 2:213 — "God sent the prophets as bearers of glad tidings and as warners"; 5:19 — "O People of the Book — Our Messenger has come to you after an interval of the messengers, lest you say: no bearer of glad tidings or warner has come to us"; 6:48 — "We send the messengers only as bearers of glad tidings and as warners — whoever believes and makes amends, no fear upon them nor do they grieve"; 11:2; 17:105 — "We have sent thee with the truth, as a bearer of glad tidings and a warner"; 18:56 — "We send the messengers only as bearers of glad tidings and as warners — and those who disbelieve contend with falsehood, that they may refute the truth thereby"; 25:56; 33:45 — "O Prophet — We have sent thee as a witness, and a bearer of glad tidings, and a warner"; 34:28 — "We have not sent thee save as a bearer of glad tidings and a warner to all mankind"; 48:8) — the commentators marking the pairing's standing balance: never gladness without warning (which would produce presumption) nor warning without gladness (which would produce despair) — the prophetic voice holding both because God's own revelation holds both (the standing Qur'ānic self-description: a Book of promise and threat fused — 39:16-20's standing parallel-verses of fire beneath and chambers above).

**Li-Allā Yakūna li-l-Nāsi ʿAlā Allāhi Ḥujjatun: The Removal of the Argument**

Then the verse delivers its standing jurisprudence of responsibility — one of the Book's most consequential purpose-clauses: *li-allā yakūna li-l-nāsi ʿalā Allāhi ḥujjatun baʿda al-rusul* — "that mankind might have no argument against God after the messengers": *ḥujjah* — from ḥ-j-j — to argue with proof, to overcome by evidence: an argument that counts, a legally binding demonstration (its standing register: 2:150 — "that people may have no argument against you"; 6:83 — "that is Our argument which We gave to Abraham against his people"; 6:149 — "unto God belongs the conclusive argument — al-ḥujjat al-bālighah"; 28:75; 40:4 — "none disputes concerning God's signs save those who disbelieve") — and the clause's declared purpose: the sending of messengers extinguishes the defense: after the glad tidings were announced and the warnings sounded, no defendant can stand at the Judgment and plead "I was never told": the standing anticipation-file of the Book making the clause's target everywhere explicit — 20:134 — "and had We destroyed them with a punishment before him, they would have said: our Lord — why didst Thou not send us a messenger, that we might have followed Thy signs before we were humbled and disgraced?"; 5:19 — "lest you say: no bearer of glad tidings or warner has come to us"; 28:47 — the standing companion (the same construction: "lest they say, when a calamity befalls them for what their own hands sent before: our Lord — why didst Thou not send us a messenger"); 16:35 — the standing shirkers' own spoiled argument ("had God willed we would not have worshipped beside Him aught") — answered in the register of 14:10-11; 67:8-9 — the people of the Fire confessing "if we had listened or had reason, we would not have been among the fellows of the Blaze" — every future objection preempted in advance by the very office of the messenger: *baʿda al-rusul* — after the messengers — the ḥujjah extinguished at the level of claim. And the commentators (al-Ṭabarī's chains; al-Rāzī's scholastic register; Ibn Taymiyyah's standing formulation in the fatawa-file) draw the juridical consequence standing across Muslim theology: no one is seized for punishment upon a message that never reached him — the standing rule that God's justice binds the accounting to the arrival of the warner ("We never punish until We send a messenger" — 17:15; "nor were We the punishers until We had raised up a messenger" — its own standing companion-clause at 28:59; 26:208-209 — "We destroyed no town but it had its warners — a reminder") — the principle known in the standing creed-literature as the doctrine that responsibility requires the ḥujjah's establishment — and its widest standing application: the inter-messenger intervals, the unreached peoples, the modern mind far from the signal — all covered by the principle that *ḥujjah* requires reliable delivery — the standing mercy-architecture inside the standing justice-architecture.

**Wa Kāna Allāhu ʿAzīzan Ḥakīman: Might and Wisdom Sealing the Office**

The verse seals with the standing Name-pair already met at v. 158 — and the commentators mark the pair's deliberate recurrence here: *wa kāna Allāhu ʿazīzan ḥakīman* — "and God is ever Mighty, Wise": *ʿazīz* — the Mighty — sealing the *authority* of the sending: the messengers arrive with a writ that heaven enforces — no community that outflanks a messenger outflanks the Sender (the standing register of the Name at passages of divine action: 2:209; 3:4; 5:95; 8:49; 9:40; 22:40; and the several standing stations) — every plot against the envoys scattered by the attribute (the crucifixion-boast of v. 157 scattering before it two verses above); and *ḥakīm* — the Wise — sealing the *design* of the sending: the staggered succession from Noah to Muḥammad, the matched tongues of 14:4, the gladness-and-warning dosage, the very selectivity of the recounted and unrecounted (v. 164) — all Wisdom’s arrangements: nothing of prophecy’s history is accidental, unmeasured, or makeshift. With v. 166 next — "but God Himself bears witness to what He sent down to thee — sending it with His knowledge — and the angels bear witness — and God suffices as a Witness" — the chapter’s certificate-architecture completes: the chain delivered (163), its horizon confessed (164), its purpose defined (165), and its witness posted (166) — the People of the Book addressed with the total standing evidence, so that the clause of v. 165 stands sealed against the very community to whom the sūrah’s final movement speaks: after *these* messengers, in *this* register, with *this* witness — "mankind has no argument against God": the Book’s own legal metaphor closing not in threat but in standing due process: everything was told, everything was warned, everything was witnessed — and upon that completed record the Judgment will rest.
