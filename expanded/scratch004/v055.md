## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:55

> **Among them are those who believe in him, and among them are those who turn away from him. And Hell suffices as a blazing flame!**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Fa-Minhum Man Yuʾminu Bih: The House Divides Itself**

The pericope closes its judgment-arc with an unsparing division-table: *fa-minhum man yuʾminu bih wa minhum man yaṣuddu ʿanh* — "among them are those who believe in him, and among them are those who turn away from him": the two-fold *minhum* — some of them... and some of them — heaven itself performing the census of a community split by the Prophet's arrival (the two pronoun-investigations the commentators run: *bih* — "in him" — either in the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and his message (the majority, al-Rāzī-logic al-Ṭabarī and al-Qurṭubī's roll: him = the one provisioned with bounty in v. 54) — with the wider reading taking it as *in it* — the Qur'an (al-Qurṭubī's note) — or even *in Abraham*, the House of v. 54 turned itself into the sift of the city — al-Zamakhsharī's alternate) — and *yaṣuddu* — *ṣadd ʿan* — to turn away from, to bar oneself (and others) from — the same ṣ-d-d root of the path-blocker's stubbornness that runs through the Book's portraits of obstinacy (2:217's hindering from God's way; 3:99, 6:126 7:45, 8:47, 34:32 — barring others also from the path they have refused) — the two camps therefore being: the portion-bearers who confirmed and the portion-bearers who entrenched. The historical census the tradition invariably consults: the first column contains the converts of Madinah — ʿAbdullāh ibn Salām and his circle (the tradition's exemplar of the scholar who crossed, Tirmidhī 3723's heaven-roll registering him among the ten assured) — and with them the second-class the second column: the Kaʿb-Huyayy line of v. 51 and the entrenched of the three Book-tribes — the verse thereby *explaining* what the previous verses had threatened: the ultimatum of v. 47 (blotting of faces) never descended en masse because the *first column came over* — repentance and conversion diverting the threat, in line with the tradition's global law (8:33 — "God would not punish them while you are among them; nor while they seek forgiveness" — and the standing prophetic dictum that general chastisement lifts where repentance spreads among a people).

**Wa Kafā bi-Jahannama Saʿīrā: The Sufficiency of What Remains**

For the second column the clause is terrible in its economy: *wa kafā bi-jahannama saʿīrā* — "and Hell suffices as a blazing flame": *kafā* — the sufficiency-verb already met twice in the pericope as comfort (*God suffices as Helper*, v. 45; *the sin suffices as evidence*, v. 50) — now deployed as terror: Hell *is enough* — no further sentence-items need stacking; *saʿīr* — from *s-ʿ-r*, to kindle, stoke — the active blazing Furnace (used of Hell in precisely this sufficing-mold at seventeen at its many stations: 25:55 cf. 25:11 the word runs 4:10 earlier in our own sūrah — *they shall enter a blazing flame* — 4:30, 17:8's *saʿīrā* for "We have prepared for the wrongdoers", 48:13's ident 76:4, and beyond) — and what the commentators make of the clause's calm definiteness is its very horror: not threat-crescendo but *closure* — the turners-away's file stamped with a line-item that stands alone: al-Ṭabarī's record of its function — the postponed count those who refused to confirm are *held to the Hereafter's sentence-book*, their worldly doom (v. 47's) deferred but never dropped — the kafā-construction declaring the storage-receipt complete: *no further punishment scheduled — because Hell suffices.*

**The Column-Question for Every Subsequent Reader**

The pericope's long address to the Book-holders therefore ends where it began (v. 47's summons *āminū bi-mā nazzalnā* — believe in what We sent down): the choice modeled — and the choice remains open — as the tradition consistently reads the whole trial-sequence: *portion-bearer* is not a fixed caste-title but a standing option the world offers every day (the envelope of lineage helps nothing; the record of confirmation alone counts — the very doctrine 49:13 places at its summit: *"the most noble of you before God is the most God-wary of you"*) — for every reader inherits both columns' possibilities: God's bounty given — and then the pivot: *confirm it or fortify against Him* — with the two *minhum* columns running open until the meeting, and the saʿīr-sufficiency standby waiting upon the turners: the Scripture having meanwhile already disposed the escape-clause at every door: repentance reroutes a man from the second column *before* the account is locked — even from the very center of obstinacy. Ibn ʿAbbās's The old exegetical note preserves the fiber of it in the unexpected conversion-tales of the scene (men like Mukhayrīq — the scholar of Banū al-Naḍīr who bequeathed his estate to the Prophet and died fighting with Muslims at Uḥud — a single crossing Column recorded in the biographies) — the whole pericope's code as delivered through v. 55: *the columns are open; the record lies with Him; repent before the receipt is drawn* — for the Furnace alone suffices to finish whatever column a man settles in.
