## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:65

> **But no, by thy Lord, they will not believe until they have made thee the judge between them in their disputes, and find no resistance in their souls to what thou hast decreed, and surrender with full submission.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Fa-Lā wa Rabbika Lā Yuʾminūna: The Oath Denying the Claim**

At the threshold of the verse heaven does what it does what it does in rare and heart-stopping solemnity — an oath by *the Prophet's own Lord*, sworn upon a negation of claims: *fa-lā wa rabbika lā yuʾminūna* — "nay, by thy Lord — they do not believe!": *fa-lā wa rabbika* — nay — by thy Lord! — the oath-formula addressing *Muhammad* in God's own mouth — an honor-grammar without peer (al-Rāzī's note trembling at it: God swearing *by* Himself but invoking the *kinship-of-lordship* — "thy Lord" — coupling His name to His Prophet's name in a single possessive phrase — the commentators never tired of noting the honor embedded in the form: sworn-truth addressed to no one else in quite this directed diction); and the negative absolute *lā yuʾminūna* — *they do not believe* — delivering the sharpest possible creed-vet creed-verdict upon the tribunal-bypassers of vv. 60–63: professed, pulpit-sitting, congregation-standing Muslims whose *īmān* heaven here flatly refuses until three conditions complete. The verse thereby builds for Islam its its belief*-conditions hierarchy: verbal confession is insufficient without this tripartite act — the tradition citing at its head the Prophet's own operational triad upon exactly this standard (al-Bukhārī 15; Muslim 43 — from Anas: *"None of you believes until I am dearer to him than his father, his child, and all mankind"* — love as the believer's true warrant, and 4:65 giving its *legal substrate*: acceptance of the Prophet's arbitration).

**Ḥattā Yuḥakkimūka fīmā Shajara Baynahum: The First Condition — Referral**

Condition one: *ḥatta yuḥakkimūka fīmā shajara baynahum* — "until they make thee judge in what branches out between them": *yuḥakkimū* — installing him *ḥakam* — judgment-maker; *shajara* — from the same sh-j-r of branches and trees — the dispute being *what grows entangled* (the beautiful root-image the philologists cleanse prize: disagreement as branches *overlapping into one another* — the case knotted — made for a judge to trim) — and the asbāb-occasion the tradition hands over with the verse's name written upon a water-course (al-Ṭabarī, al-Wāḥidī, and Ibn Kathīr's register): al-Zubayr ibn al-ʿAwwām and an Anṣārī neighbor of Banū Umayyah's palm-water line the two quarreling over the dividing of an irrigation rill from the shared highland flow; the Prophet ruled: *"irrigate first, O Zubayr, then send the water to your neighbor"* — a rule favoring the cousin — and the Anṣārī, stung, said: *"Because he is your aunt's son?"* — the side-remark heaven answered with our oath-verse — the Prophet's face darkening so the watery worldly so the public remembered the day as one of Islam's creed-crises: even a good, wronged-wrongless even a lawful cause is lost by grudging against the Messenger's gavel (the school-exegetes taking the occasion as secondary-probation secondary but illustrative: the verse's creed-logic stands universal — thus al-Ṭabarī's superscription of it over all rulings-refusals anywhere).

**Thumma Lā Yajidū fī Anfusihim Ḥarajan Mimmā Qaḍayta: The Second Condition — Interior Clearance**

Condition two — the hardest ever set for any court-order: *thumma lā yajidū fī anfusihim ḥarajan mimmā qaḍayta* — "then they find in their souls no tightness at what thou hast decreed": *ḥaraj* — constriction — the same word of breast-anguish the Book deploys against every inward resistance (6:125 — "He makes his breast tight (ḥarajan ḍayyiqan) as though climbing into the sky"; 33:36's exact sister — "it is not for a believing man or woman — when God and His Messenger have decided a matter — to have any choice in their affair") — the condition carrying belief *past compliance into interiority*: the disputant must receive the ruling *without chest-squeezing* — the tradition's saints commenting with memorable directness: outward bowing is servitude forced; the believer's distinct sign is *broad-chestedness under decree* (the hadith-testimony of its rarity and glory: Bukhārī 21 — *"the whole ummah will enter the Garden save those who refuse — What is refusal, Messenger of God Whoever obeys me will enter the Garden, and whoever refuses me has refused"*) — while al-Rāzī's theology raises the clause into the definition-of-faith debates: *ruling-acceptance without soul-tightness is the Qur'an's own operationalization of īmān* — head, heart, and chest all voting one way — the comparison with 33:36 sealing the sum total in every Muslim jurist's ethical canon thereafter: *no belief whatever until God's and His Messenger's decrees beat upon empty upon open rooms within.*

**Wa Yusallimū Taslīmān: The Third Condition — Total Handover**

Condition three — the cognate-accusative of completion: *wa yusallimū taslīman* — "and they surrender with full surrender": *yusallimū* — from the s-l-m of Islam itself — hand-over, consign; *taslīman* — the verbal noun — absolutely, unreservedly: the tree the triad then standing as Islam's hierarchical its creed-architecture before the ancient before every dispute-machine of history: referral (the case *goes* to the Prophet), interior release (the chest *opens* beneath the verdict), full consignment (the self *ignores executes* hand-over) — with the sufi-grammar finding all three stages telescoped forever into the single word *islām* — submission as end-station of arbitration (the early saying-books preserve ʿAlī's distilled gloss: *submission is Islam's *the nub the core the soul of Islam is þed the delivering-up of the outcome to the Prophet's decrees*) — and the pastoral-moral yield of 4:65 has ever since rung in the mufti's, husband's, and qāḍī's ears alike: *any* grudge against any established judgment of the Messenger's Sunnah is nothing less than a creed-issue — the believer's daily practice meaning being the folding of his preferences beneath the delivered rule — chest at ease, face at peace, step in obedience — until *taslīm* becomes instinct, and the proof of the profession stands not upon his statements but upon his comportment beneath the verdict: *by thy Lord — they believe not, until.*
