## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:114

> **There is no good in most of their secret converse, save for him who enjoins charity or kindness or reconciliation between men. And whosoever does that, seeking God's Good Pleasure, We shall grant him a great reward.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Lā Khayra fī Kathīrin min Najwāhum: The Moral Audit of Whisper-Traffic**

Into the wake of the night-plot exposure (v. 108's bayyata, the planted armor, the lobbying clan) the sūrah writes its standing law of whispered words: *lā khayra fī kathīrin min najwāhum* — "no good in most of their secret converse": *najwā* — confidential intimate private counsel — from n-j-w — low-voiced talk (the Book's standing audit-word for the sealed conversation — its immediate family crowding at once: 58:7 — "hast thou not considered that God knows all in the heavens and the earth there is no secret converse of three, but He is their Fourth;
