## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:115

> **But whosoever opposes the Messenger, after guidance has been made clear to him, and follows a way other than that of the believers, We shall leave him on [the path] he has taken, and We shall cause him to burn in Hell — what an evil journey's end!**

The Ṭuʿmah-file closes with its standing long-view lesson — the verse that turned a single clan-lobby into a statute for all centuries: *wa man yushāqiqi al-rasūla min baʿdi mā tabayyana lahu al-hudā* — "whosoever opposes the Messenger after the guidance has been made clear to him": *yushāqiq* — from sh-q-q — splitting asunder (the standing split-root: the verb pictures a man *cleaving away* from the fabric — shearing off from the compact thing to which he belonged — the comment-doctors unwrapping its internal axis its standing opposition-anatomy: shiqāq being not mere disagreement — it is *separation-with-hostility* — siding relocating one's whole self toward the opposite toward the opposing camp — the standing Quranic usage of this of the sh-q-q family everywhere being schism-level: 2:137's "if they believe in the like of what you believe, they are guided — and if they turn away, they are in schism"; 22:53's "that He may make what Satan casts a trial for those in whose hearts is disease — and whose hearts are hard — and indeed the wrongdoers are in far-off schism — shiqāqin baʿīd"; its its standing Book-kin: 4:115 here and 8:13's — "that is because they made schism against God and His Messenger" — the standing penalty-clause with the assembled usage setting the level of the charge: not casual dispute — *breaking ranks with the Prophet-as-locus-of-truth*) — *min baʿdi mā tabayyana lahu al-hudā* — after the clarification — tabayyun again (v. 94's tabayyanū-c bayyana-family once more: the deed set after the clarity-flash after the clarity after the truth had been lith-the after the truth had downloaded — the Prophet's teaching reaching ears open after the man *knew* — v. after the man knew) — and the second limb: *wa yattabiʿ ghayra sabīli al-muʾminīna* — "and follows a way other than the way of the believers": the Book's standing marker of blessed *consensus-way* — the famous ijmāʿ-text its primary usage in legal-theory in legal-theory al-Shāfiʿī's al-Shāfiʿī's own use of the verse the famous thesis of the schools: alongside the Messenger's way, God canonizes the *way of the believers* as a path one may not leave without punishment — the standing jurispr the standing juristic development of it into the doctrine of *ijmāʿ* — communal consensus — the ummah's collective the ummah held incapable of agreeing upon error (paired with the standing hadith: "my community will not come together upon misguidance" — Tirmidhī 2167 — and its standing companion: "the Hand of God is with the congregation" — Tirmidhī 2166) — the verse thereby jointly uplifting jointly upholding two non-negotiable rails: the Messenger's teaching, and the believers' corporate way — with the permission the absolution the standing modern and classical map of failure drawn beneath them: the man who invents a third line — neither the Prophet's nor the community's — and takes it.
