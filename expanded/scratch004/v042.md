## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:42

> **On that Day those who disbelieved and disobeyed the Messenger will wish that they were level with the earth, and they will conceal no account from God.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Yawma'idhin: The Named Day the Summons Descends**

The sequel-verse inherits its stage directly from v. 41 — *yawma'idhin*, on that Day — the Day made familiar beyond any other in all the Book (some ninety repetitions of the idiom across the Qur'an make *yawma'idhin* the reader's reflex-image of the Reckoning): the Day the prophet-witnesses are brought — and the actors now come into their sentence-frame: *alladhīna kafarū wa ʿaṣaw al-rasūl* — "those who disbelieved and disobeyed the Messenger": the pairing deliberate — kufr and messenger-disobedience — the two sides of the one sunken coin upon every community of history, and especially the two grand corruption-forms the sūrah has prosecuted in its recent lines: first the plain deniers (the pagans and their confederates), then the message-studiers who *obeyed not* though they heard (the Madinan malcontents around this very sūrah: the prayer-mockers of v. 46, the hedge-believers of vv. 60–63, the pious-self-flatterers of vv. 49–52 ahead). The Prophet's office glows in the very crime-list: disobeying *the Messenger* — not merely God — is arraigned as cosmic breach — *ʿiṣyān al-rasūl* — the same grammar as 3:32 and 4:80 ("whoever obeys the Messenger has obeyed God") and later 4:115's doom for path-splitters ("whoso opposes the Messenger after guidance is clear...") — so that the Day’s two indictments define themselves upon one man: *believe God; obey His Messenger* — upon which double-test all histories will be drawn forward for sentencing.

**Tusawwā Bihimu al-Arḍ: Leveling with the Dust — Three Registers**

Their wish is quoted in the strange grammar of the active earth: *yawma'idhin yawaddu alladhīna kafarū wa ʿaṣaw al-rasūla an tusawwā bihimu al-arḍ* — "they shall wish that the earth be leveled *with* them": *sawwā*, to make equal, level — the earth itself the subject, leveling *them* into itself — and the commentators developed the verse's three exegesis-registers whose conjunction is the tradition's reading: (1) leveling = *uck * self-erasure: to be so un-noticeable as to escape summons — to disappear into the level ground of no-account (al-Ṭabarī's plaintrack); (2) a wish for the earth to *open beneath* and swallow — the ground splitting under them before the words and judgments pass (the same earth that once swallowed Qārūn at 28:81 serving as their dream-exit); and (3) the sister-clause of 78:40 which turns the wish into its naked core: *"Fair Would that I were dust!"* — non-existence wished over presence-at-the-bar — the Judgment-psychology universalized by al-Rāzī's terse note: it is the wish of *annihilation*, the reversal of every sāʻ of every clinging-to-life the sinner practiced in the world: *he whose whole creed was life-no life at any price will price nonbeing above it on the Day.* A the well-attested backdrop echoes from the Prophet's own portraiture of that assembly (Bukhārī's Book of Tawḥīd scenes — the people gathered for accounting in *sweat per their deeds* on the standing-ground) — the terror such that obliteration becomes a prayer: and it tells the reader steep tells him directly — *accounted-wrong is infinitely worse than nullity; therefore make your accounts soft by repentance now*, for the dead man of dust has no such standing.

**Wa Lā Yaktumūna Allāha Ḥadīthan: The Impossibility of Editing**

The last clause contains the Day's second psychology: *wa lā yaktumūna Allāha ḥadīthan* — "and they shall not conceal from God a single tale": *ḥadīth* here a piece of the story — an account, a speech-item — and the tradition paints the scene's exact mechanics: at the summons they will at first *try* — like the generous-liars of the lower day they were — the frame of its preface being 6:23–24 (their self-vindicating oaths "by God our Lord we were not idolaters," immediately stamped *they lied against themselves*) and 16:111 (each soul arguing its case) — until the system of the Day itself revokes their tongue's franchise: the Book sealing a whole array of parallel declarations — hand and foot testifying (36:65: the Seal upon mouths while hands speak and feet bear witness); skin and hearing and eyes as witnesses (41:20–22: "their hearing, eyes, and skins bear witness against them... *their skins* — Why do you witness against us God made us speak who makes all things speak"); earth herself narrating (99:4–5: "that Day she shall tell her tidings, for her Lord has inspired her") — so that *no deed ever really stays hidden even in the world* (the Field the register which 43:80 also names: "your emissaries Our emissaries are at their side recording"); concealment has only ever been a *delay* — the world being God's own archive already taped full by what men thought was solitary night. The verse ends the double-ill of disbelief-and-disobedience in a surgical prognosis: on the Day eve ry on that Day *dodge-spaces close and tongue-leases terminate* — so the sūrah's whole ethic gains its eschatological teeth — the orphan-guardian, the husband, the almsgiver, the oath-keeper: every silent office will *speak* — and the only privacy left is repentance practiced while the ink can still be washed clean, for on that Day *they will conceal from God not a single word.*
