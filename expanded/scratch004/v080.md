## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:80

> **Whosoever obeys the Messenger obeys God, and as for those who turn away — We have not sent thee as their keeper.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Man Yuṭiʿi al-Rasūla Fa-Qad Aṭāʿa Allāh: The Equation of Obedience**

The pericope's obedience-thread — running from v. 59's triple-command through v. 64's messengers-sent-for-obedience and v. 65's creed-oath — now condenses into its most quoted formula: *man yuṭiʿi al-rasūla fa-qad aṭāʿa Allāh* — "whoever obeys the Messenger has obeyed God": *man*, whosoever — the universal conditional (no stipulation of rank, time, nation); *yuṭiʿ*, obeys — and the heavenly equation attached with *fa-qad* — thereby truly (the completion-particle of the Book's rock-bottom statements grammatically putting in the pluperfect what a believer must accept as already accomplished: obedience to the Messenger *counts as* fulfilled obedience to God — thereby declaratively sealing the two channels into one: no bypass-of-the-Prophet by direct-God piety exists in the religion of Islam — the direct road *is* through his Sunnah) — the doctors then mapping the equation's bearings into the standing corpus of law: al-Shāfiʿī's own perpetual his famous syllogistics at the Risālah's chapterheads: (1) obedience of God is enacted through obedience of His Messenger — for He made His Messenger’s word the criterion of the deed (alongside the Book itself pairing the orders in the same breath everywhere: 3:32, 3:132, 4:59, 5:92, 24:54, 47:33, 64:12 — the colloca the collocation itself so persistent it becomes grammar); (2) consequently the Sunnah is revelation-government in operation — "though it is not recited" in liturgy (the well-known prophetic utterance attributed: *"I have been given the Book and with it its like"* — Abū Dāwūd 4604 — though stands under tech its isnād weighed by the critics); (3) and therefore whoever constructs Islam *without* the Prophet's prescriptions stands outside the equation exactly as the ṭāghūt-petitioners did (v. 60's precedent-case bearing the verse's edge forever) — with the famously terse summary of ʿAbdullāh ibn of Abū Hurayrah's straightforwardly the direct tradition that al-Bukhārī preserved it in his Ṣaḥīḥ (al-Bukhārī 7280; Muslim 1835 — from Abū Hurayrah):

> *"Whoever obeys me has obeyed God; and whoever disobeys me has disobeyed God. And whoever obeys the commander I appoint has obeyed me; and whoever disobeys the commander I appoint has disobeyed me."*

**Wa Man Tawallā: The Turning-Away and Its UnalarmedUnsent Unherded Judgment**

The verse's second half then does something stranger — it *lets go*: *wa man tawallā fa-mā arsalnāka ʿalayhim ḥafīẓan* — "and whoever turns away — We have not sent thee over them as a keeper": *tawallā* — from w-l-y — to turn away, turn the back — the pivot-verb of the Book's freedom-clauses (the very verb heaven keeps placing beside every commission delivered to prophets: 10:108 — "whoso is guided, is guided only for himself; and whoso strays, strays only against himself"; 17:54 — "We have not sent thee over them as a warden"; 42:48 — "We have not sent thee as a keeper over them"; 39:41 — the same release-clause) — and *ḥafīẓ* — keeper, guardian-over — from h-f-ẓ — the watchman-post the Prophet is *released* from: he was not sent as a warden who can *force* compliance nor as a policeman accountable for every defection — the theological toll the theology here being Islam's great boundary-drawing between *responsibility-of-delivery* and *responsibility-of-result*: the Messenger accountable for conveying; each hearer accountable for his own turn (the pastoral the pastoral fruit the commentators harvest from it immediately – the spiritual shepherd's duty-billet the teacher, preacher, parent at the limit of effort may lay the burden down at heaven's door — *you were no ḥafīẓ over them* — which is neither indifference (the Prophet wept over the turning-away — 35:8's "let not thy soul waste away in sighing for them"; 18:6's "perhaps thou wouldst torment thyself to death over their traces, if they believe not") nor reverse-blame — anguish without self-destruction being the prophetic-and-parental permanent tarīq permanent road).

**The Equation as Pastoral Binding for Every Minister and Son and Every Soul*The pericope's logic hereby completes its entire circuit: vv. 59–65 set up the holy channels — God, Messenger, authorities, referral; vv. 66–70 pledged the payments; vv. 71–76 commanded the ranks; vv. 77–78 diagnosed the postponement-heart; v. 79 re-aligned providence's ascriptions — and v. 80 now closes the *whole* by locking the obedience-channel into One — with the mercy-side in the one hand competence-*clarified*: whoever obeys the Messenger has thereby fulfilled the whole covenant — no auxiliary courts to placate, no second heavens to apply to — and in the other heart-*set-free*: the Messenger may sleep — he was not commissioned as anyone's keeper — each turning-back standing under its own accounting — so that the believer in every age inherits the verse twice: as the law he lives by (obedience to the Sunnah = obedience to God-full-stop — with the Prophet's own taẓkīm-clause with his death-bed admonishing no — the straighterness the straight safety-line of his magnum his Farewell words: *"I have left among you two things; you will never go astray so long as you hold to them: the Book of God and the Sunnah of His Messenger"” — Mālik's Muwaṭṭaʾ registering its famous first-prong form with Bukhārī's own with the standing prayers accepted mainstream wording) — and as the balm he hands his own soul: where preaching failed, where sons wandered, where congregations nodded off — *fa-mā arsalnāka ʿalayhim ḥafīẓan* — no ḥafīẓ was ever sent; the greatest Messenger carried that same wound; the result-ledger belongs to heaven; the duty-ledger to us; and God's enough God's witnessing over the whole is the only keeper mankind ever needed — fa-inna Allāha kāna ʿalā kulli shayʾin shahīd *wa kafā bi-Allāhi shahīdan*, witnessing still.
