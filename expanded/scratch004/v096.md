## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:96

> **Degrees conferred by Him, and forgiveness and mercy. Surely God is Forgiving, Merciful.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Darajātin Minhu: The Plural Above the Singular**

A terse seven-syllable verse — the sūrah in its most economical gear — yet the commentators unfold from it an entire ladder of rank: *darajātin minhu* — "degrees from Him": the plural *darajāt* deliberately stepping beyond v. 95's single *darajah* (the stepped-jurisprudence the classical line savors: if the world enjoys one badge-of-favor — victory and ghanīmah — the next world discloses *many graded rungs*: al-Rāzī standing here with the two interpretations the tafsīr-houses pass through — either (1) the mujāhid is favored by *one* degree in this world (his spoils-wage) but by *many* in the Hereafter, or (2) by one degree over the legitimate excused over the legitimately excused sitter — and by layered degrees over the sitter without excuse — and a third reading mounted as Ibn ʿAjībah's register keeps it: the degrees counting also the soul's own station-scales — rising nearness-to-God gained through the very acts of striving) — and the standing hadith seals the arithmetic (Ibn Kathīr's citation — and across the Sahīh al-Bukhārī 2790 Bukhārī 2790 confirm the exact wording: *"in Paradise are a hundred degrees that God has prepared for those who strive in His path — what is between the two degrees is as what is between the heaven and the earth"* — the Paradise-pagan the mujāhid's vertical estate carved out by heaven's own surveyor — one stair of it spanning creation-dimensions).

**Wa Maghfiratan wa Raḥmatan: The Two Soft Companions of the Striver**

Then the provision of the death-threshold with its twin attendants: *wa maghfiratan wa raḥmatan* — "and forgiveness and mercy": the standing two-guardian procession of the Book's eschatological doors (forgiveness erasing the trail behind; mercy flooding the road ahead — cf. 3:157's "and if you are slain in the way of God or die, surely forgiveness from God and mercy are better than whatever they amass"; 3:158's "and if you die or are slain, it is unto God that you shall be gathered"; and the sūrah's own earlier sūrah's own paired recycle at 4:100's *wa ghafūran raḥīmā* ahead) — al-Rāzī's annotation picking up its placement: the striver lives with fear chained to his back (no man walks to battle spiritually spotless — intention warps, taunts slip, spoils glitter) — God's *maghfirah* answers exactly the striver's hidden deformity: the cover-over of his partial failures; and *raḥmah* pays him beyond all desert beyond desert: degrees traded by grace, not invoice — hence the Prophet's own standing report upon the striver's two doors: (Bukhārī the sahīh phrase: "he who fights so that God's word may be supreme — that is God's path" — Bukhārī 2810 above — matched now by Tirmidhī 1637's list of the martyr's six privileges — "forgiven at the first blood-drop; shown his seat in Paradise..." — the maghfirah arriving *at the wound's first instant*, not at Judgment's end).

**Inna Allāha Kāna Ghafūran Raḥīmā: The Double Forgiving-Merciful Seal**

The verse closes in the Book's most beloved attribute-couplet: *inna Allāha kāna ghafūran raḥīman* — "God is ever Forgiving, Merciful": Ghafūr — repeated-Forgiver (gh-f-r — cover-conceal-root — the verb of the helmet and the shield of the covering that takes the blame-wound off sight) — and Raḥīm — ever-Merciful (the sūrah's own recurrent seal — vv. 16, 23, 24, 25, 26, 29... its cadence stitching every legal chapter to its maternal underside) — the commentators' note upon its siting upon its placement just *after* the two-step of degrees-and-wages: lest any fighter misconstrue the Garden as a renting as a paid estate he earned — God signs the roll with the forgiveness-couplet: *all merit you collect stands first under My covering, then under My awarding: your darajāt are Minhu — from Him* — with the sūrah's war-wages chapter thereby ending on the doctrine every spiritual tradition must labor to keep alive: wages real — and mercy prior — the deeds of the striver *counted* because God first *forgave the warping within them* — after which the very next verse scrolls the warning back: to the souls who won't move, won't rise, won't strive nor even migrate — the angels of death asking them, at the door, "in what state were you?" — degrees all being behind them, nothing ahead.
