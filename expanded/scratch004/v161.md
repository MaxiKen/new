## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:161

> **And for their taking usury, though they had been forbidden it, and for their consuming men's wealth falsely; and We have prepared for the disbelievers among them a painful punishment.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Wa Akhdhihimu al-Ribā: Usury in the Covenant-Community's File**

The indictment hands down its economic counts: *wa akhdhihimu al-ribā wa qad nuhū ʿanhu* — "and for their taking usury, though they had been forbidden it": *ribā* — from r-b-w — increase, growth — the standing name of the usurious increment (its standing full dossier in the Book: 2:275 — "those who consume usury shall not rise save as one rises whom Satan has confounded by his touch — that because they said: commerce is but like usury — yet God has permitted commerce and forbidden usury"; 2:276 — "God blots out usury and augments charities"; 2:278-279 — "O you who believe — fear God and relinquish what remains of usury, if you are believers — but if you do not, then be informed of war from God and His Messenger"; 3:130 — "O you who believe — consume not usury, doubled and many-fold"; 30:39 — the ribā-zakāt antithesis: "what you give in usury, that it may increase upon people's wealth, increases not with God — but what you give in alms, desiring the Face of God — those are the doublers") — the verse's quiet historical precision noted by the commentators: *wa qad nuhū ʿanhu* — "though they had been forbidden it": the prohibition standing in *their own* covenant-code — the prior revelation's standing registers known to the addressed community — so that the count is not *failure to know* but defiance of known law: the covenant-community's own ribā-ban acknowledged, then bypassed by stratagem — the standing report-material in the tafsīr-file describing the evasion-mechanics (sale-constructs designed as loans in costume, the standing ḥiyal-techniques by which increase upon loans was dressed as commerce) — the same engineering of forms the Sabbath-file (4:154; 2:65's standing parallel; 7:163's net-casting on Friday-eve) already registered as the community's standing temptation: law-gaming named among the covenant-counts.

**Wa Aklihim Amwāla al-Nāsi bi-al-Bāṭil: Wealth-Devouring as Established Convict-File**

Then the second economic count — *wa aklihim amwāla al-nāsi bi-al-bāṭil* — "and for their consuming men's wealth falsely": the *akl-bāṭil* formula standing as one of the sūrah's own load-bearing phrases (4:29 — "consume not your wealth among yourselves falsely — except it be commerce by mutual consent"; 4:30's standing consequence-verse; 4:10's own standing archetype — "those who consume the orphans' property unjustly consume but fire into their bellies") — its extension into the covenant-file here registering further specific practices in the comment-canon's standing reports: religious fees for altering judgment; gifts to authorities that functioned as judgments-for-hire; the standing neighbor-charge of 9:34's file ("many of the rabbis and monks consume men's wealth falsely and bar from God's way"); 2:188's standing general prohibition ("consume not your wealth among yourselves falsely, nor proffer it to judges, that you may consume a portion of people's wealth sinfully while you know"); 5:62-63's standing note ("many of them hastening into sin and transgression and their consuming the unlawful — evil is what they used to do"). The commentators mark the phrase's standing anatomy: *bi-al-bāṭil* — "falsely" — the wealth-transfer morally possible (donations! fees! bargains!) yet legally void because its basis is null — and the consuming named *akl* — eating — the Book's standing metaphor of ingestion (as at 4:10): property-wrongdoing digested into the body of the perpetrator — the standing eschatological gloss of 4:10 already installed: such eating is fire-ingestion by stages.

**The Commerce-Question the Jurists Filed Under This Count**

The legal tradition's standing practical deliberation attaches at this verse — and al-Qurṭubī's register preserves its resolution: some early legists, reading this indictment of wealth-practices, deliberated whether *commerce with* the addressed community stood lawful at all; the standing answer derived from two fixed sources: the Book itself — 5:5 — "this day the good things are made lawful to you — and the food of those who were given the Book is lawful for you — and your food is lawful for them" — inter-community food-exchange explicitly sanctified; and the Prophet's standing practice — his own transactions — buying, pledging his armor in pledge (the standing armor-ransom-file: the Prophet's coat of mail mortgaged to a Jewish merchant for provisions, its register in Bukhārī's collections — Bukhārī 2916 and its standing parallels) — establishing the ummah's standing precedent: indictment of corrupt *practices* never annulling the lawfulness of ordinary *intercourse* — a distinction the Jurists enshrined as standing principle: accusation of a party's corrupt modes does not render the party untouchable; only the mode is judged.

**Wa Aʿtadnā li-l-Kāfirīna Minhum ʿAdhāban Alīman: The Prepared Punishment — with Its Standing Exemption**

The sentence seals its consequence with the standing preparation-formula — twice now in the chapter's register (v. 151's *muhīn*; here *alīm*): *wa aʿtadnā li-l-kāfirīna minhum ʿadhāban alīman* — "and We have prepared for the disbelievers among them a painful punishment": *aʿtadnā* — held ready (its standing register: 4:18; 4:37; 4:93; 4:102; 4:151; 5:33; 9:63; 17:10 and the several standing stations) — the punishment pre-fabricated; and the word the commentators seize as the verse's own open window: *minhum* — "*among* them" — the partitive: not the whole community uniformly but *the disbelievers among them* — the standing exemption-architecture of this sūrah's People-of-the-Book-file (v. 155's *illā qalīlan* window; v. 159's universal-belief horizon; and arriving immediately at v. 162: "but those firmly rooted in knowledge among them — and the believers — believing in what was sent down to thee and in what was sent down before thee... unto them We shall grant a great reward") — the file's final division thereby installed: this chapter never indicts a genealogy; it indicts *convictions and practices* — and in the very verses of sentencing it posts the exit-staircase to the rooted-in-knowledge and the believing: punishment prepared for "the disbelievers among them," reward granted to those of the same community who believe the whole revelation — the standing Qur'ānic jurisprudence that has run through the sūrah of the Watcher from its first verse: no party saved or damned by badge, but only by *taqwā and what it does with the covenant it received* — the ṭayyibāt-withdrawal in history and the prepared fire beyond it both pointed, at their standing targets, at conduct — while the door to *ajran kabīran* (a great reward) opens in the very next verse to any of them — and of any community — who will believe and do.
