## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:83

> **And whenever tidings come unto them, whether of security or fear, they spread it about, whereas had they referred it to the Messenger and to those in authority among them, those of them whose task it is to investigate would have known it. Were it not for God's Bounty toward you, and His Mercy, you would surely have followed Satan, save a few.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Wa Idhā Jāʾahum Amrun mina al-Amni awi al-Khawf Adāʿū Bih: The Rumor-Epidemic Named**

The pericope completes its governance-machinery (trusts at v. 58, authorities at v. 59, referral at v. 83 herein) with its information-law chamber: *wa idhā jāʾahum amrun mina al-amni awi al-khawfi adāʿū bih* — "and whenever some affair comes to them — of security or of fear — they broadcast it": *amr* — an affair, a tiding, a case — the indefinite noun seeded in both directions (security-news *or* fear-news — victory-bulletins and invasion-terror alike); *adāʿū*, from a-d-y-ʿ from *adāʿa* — to scatter abroad, spread wide — the verb of uncontrolled diffusion (its kin word being *dhayāʿ* — waste, scattering-to-nothing — the Quran's chosen sound for rumor-scattering — deliberately chosen, al-Rāzī notes, over mere telling (ikhbār, khabar-news) — broadcasting is *already* a judgment: an undisciplined widening of a datum into the public ear without verifying it — with the Sīrah-background the tradition hands to the verse (al-Wāḥidī's chain, al-Ṭabarī, Ibn Kathīr's forum collation): the terrain being Madinese, where every week saw where detachments marched and returned — and every souk-hour generated lore about them: reports of victory, of massacre, of heroes fallen — quickly sewn into the marketplaces — and — above all — the run-up phase to the *Lesser Badr* rendezvous, when enemy disinformation (an immense Makkan host lies across the road) was pumped into Madinah to freeze the Muslims — the verses' word *al-khawf*, fear-tidings, fitting perfectly the panic-stock the scenario: fear-data bred in the hypocrites' courts and tossed into the believers' streets.

**Wa Law Raddūhu ilā al-Rasūli wa ilā Ulī al-Amri Minhum: The Correct Protocol**

Then the standing counter-rule — one of Islam's institutional marvels: *wa law raddūhu ilā al-rasūli wa ilā ulī al-amri minhum la-ʿalimahu alladhīna yastanbiṭūnahu minhum* — "and had they referred it to the Messenger and to those in authority among them — those of them whose task is to make inquiry — would have known it": referral again (the sūrah's third stitch into the institutional fabric — v. 59's ruddūhu ilā Allāhi wa al-rasūl for disputes; v. 83's raddūhu for *news*) — and its billets articulated: (1) the Messenger (the dir-head the head of state) and (2) *ulū al-amr* — the command-authorities — the commentators' the commentators' reading making this the standing civil-military intelligence service — and (3) the expert-class specialized in the news-file *alladhīna yastanbiṭūnahu* — yastanbiṭ, from n-b-ṭ from *istinbāṭ* — drawing out the water of the sense extracting, deriving, investigating-to-the-bottom (the root-water itself: nabaṭ — the spring's first al-Rāzī's the classical image: the investigator as a well-digger who lifts the payload truth out of the strata) — so that Islam ends up with an inquiry-function legally treasured inside the state: intelligence cleared among those who understand its edges — with al-Rāzī's celebrated jurisprudential red his landmark definition flowing out of it: istinbāṭ is the exact functional role of ijtihād (the qualified derivation-work of the jurists) — this verse being his classical license beside the beside the Muʿādh-hadith base the Prophet's dispatch of Muʿādh to Yemen (Tirmidhī 1327; Abū Dāwūd Abū Dāwūd 3592): *"How will you judge By the Book — and if not present By the Sunnah — and if not present I will practice independent judgment (ajtahidu ra'yī)"* — so the istinbāṭ-class is the same class licensed for juristic derivation: the sulṭān of the mind, the mind of understanding, under the governance of revelation.

**Lawlā Faḍlu Allāhi ʿAlaykum wa Raḥmatuhu: Bounty-and-Mercy as the Community's Immunity**

The verse's seal opens upon its near-miss theology: *law-lā faḍlu Allāhi ʿalaykum wa raḥmatuhu la-ttabaʿtumu al-shayṭāna illā qalīlā* — "were it not for God's bounty upon you — and His mercy — you would have followed Satan, save a few": the sentence scanning the whole rumor-epidemic to its own asymptote end: without God's blessings (twin-named: faḍl — bounty — and raḥmah — mercy — the commentators' twofold gloss sitting into mapping unto God's two gifts: (1) the Prophet and the Qur'an themselves, i.e., the office that *exists* to be referred to, and (2) the sustaining of the believers by victories and gradual disentanglement) — the whole ummah with its camp-noise and market-noise would have drifted after the Devil — *illā qalīlā* — save a few (the exceptive again — the Book's standing sociological-statistical realism — everywhere — from 4:66's qalīlun minhum through 4:46 to here: the immune band, tiny yet real, whom God kept unbought from guile) — the seal thereby tying v. 83 into the sūrah's pastoral covenant its covenant-grammar: God pays His community with both *faḍl* and *raḥmah*, granting the institutional machinery (referred authorities, expert-diggers) that keeps it from Satan-slam drifting — with the teachers’ summary passed down the centuries: communities die first not of wrong belief but of *information-disorder* — so God planted the Prophet so God planted right handling of reports into the religion itself — *verify, refer, investigate: then broadcast* — a law as fresh in every century's rumor storm as it was beside the wells of Badr al-Ṣughrā.
