## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:175

> **Then as for those who believe in God and hold fast to Him — He shall cause them to enter into a mercy from Him, and bounty, and shall guide them unto Himself upon a straight path.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Fa-Ammā Alladhīna Āmanū bi-llāhi wa Iʿtaṣamū Bih: The Two Grips of Salvation**

The closing apportionment-verse of the chapter's doctrinal movement names the saved by their two grips: *fa-ammā alladhīna āmanū bi-llāhi wa iʿtaṣamū bihi* — "then as for those who believe in God and hold fast to Him": the *ammā... fa-* classification standing at the exact place where the chapter began its promise-register (4:57's standing pair; 4:122's counter-file; 4:152's archipelago-keepers; 4:162's rāsikhūn; 4:173's paid-in-full) — and the verb of the verb of safety proprietary to the chapter's own dossier: *iʿtaṣamū* — from ʿ-ṣ-m — to hold fast, to cling for protection — already sounded in the chapter's own standing exception-architecture: 4:146's repentance-file — "save those who repent and make amends and *hold fast to God* and make their religion sincere for Him" — the hypocrisy-dossier having named holding-fast as one of the cure-quadrants, now the closing movement names it as the believer's standing posture tout court: between 4:146 (the repentant hypocrite holding fast) and 4:175 (the believer holding fast), the chapter has woven its standing cord: the ʿ-iṣām-root as the sum of attachment in the Book's own lexicon — 3:101 — "whoever holds fast to God has been guided to a straight path" (the standing exact-parallel of our verse's own closing image); 3:103 — "hold fast, all of you together, to the cord of God, and do not separate" — the celebrated rope-image; 22:78 — "hold fast to God — He is your Protector — an excellent Protector and an excellent Helper"); the commentators (al-Ṭabarī's chains; Ibn Kathīr's standing gloss) glossing the object of the gripping variously-comprehensively: God Himself, and instrumentally His rope — the Qur'ān — and His covenant — the standing readings converging: to hold fast to God is to hold fast to what He extended — the cord from heaven's hand.

**Fā-Sa-Yudkhiluhum fī Raḥmatin Minhu wa Faḍl: Mercy First, Bounty Beyond**

The promise runs in two arrivals: *fa-sa-yudkhiluhum fī raḥmatin minhu wa faḍlin* — "He shall cause them to enter into a mercy from Him, and bounty": *yudkhiluhum* — the induction-verb — the standing ushering-language of the Book's hospitality-register (its own standing stations within the chapter's salvation-file: 4:13 — "He shall cause them to enter Gardens beneath which rivers flow"; 4:31 — "He shall cause you to enter with a noble entrance"; 4:57; 4:122 — the ushered-guest imagery of the reward-files; and the standing eschatological Book-register: 5:84-85; 14:23; 48:5; 58:22; 76:31 — "He admits whom He wills into His mercy") — entry *into* mercy as though mercy were a precinct, a garden-house with doors — and the commentators marking the double-object's standing distinction (the standing gloss across the comment-canon): *raḥmah* — mercy — the pardoning-covering benefit, the wage of the broken and the returned; *faḍl* — bounty — the overflow beyond desert, the extra that justice cannot be coaxed into and only generosity bestows (its standing pairing-file: 3:152; 3:174 — "they returned with bounty from God and grace — harm touched them not"; 9:74 — "verily God shall give them of His bounty — the Messenger and the believers — indeed God is Vast in bounty"; 30:45 — "that He may requite those who believe and do righteous deeds of His bounty"; 35:30; 42:26; 57:29) — the two arrivals thus matching the believer's two needs: *mercy* answering the past (its debts and limps), *bounty* answering the future (its fears of insufficiency)

**Wa Yahdīhim Ilayhi Ṣirāṭan Mustaqīman: Guided *Unto Him* — The Sūrah's Last Compass**

Then the verse's famous directional clause — one of the Book's most exalted statements of guidance: *wa yahdīhim ilayhi ṣirāṭan mustaqīman* — "and shall guide them unto Himself upon a straight path": *yaṣirāṭ mustaqīm* — the standing straight-path (its register: 1:6 — the Fātiḥah's standing daily petition — "guide us upon the straight path"; 2:213; 3:51 — "verily God is my Lord and your Lord — so worship Him — this is a straight path"; 4:68's own standing performance-file — "We would have guided them to a straight path"; 6:153 — "this is My path, straight"; 10:25; 11:56; 15:41; 19:36; 22:54; 36:61; 42:52; 43:43; 43:61; 46:30; 48:2's 48:20) — and the preposition the commentators fix as the verse's glory: *ilayhi* — "unto HIM" — the path's destination not a place called Paradise but a Person — the standing Book-formulation here and at 42:52-53 — its own matchless doublet ("thus did We reveal to thee a Spirit from Our command... verily thou guidest unto a straight path — the path of God — unto whom belongs whatsoever is in the heavens and the earth — lo — unto God all matters come home"); al-Rāzī's standing note gathering the tradition's remark: many verses pledge guidance to the path; this verse pledges guidance *to Him upon it* — the road straight precisely because it terminates in God — the standing mystics' reading (Ibn ʿAjībah's inward file; al-Qushayrī's standing register) completing the chapter's entire ethical-doctrinal transit: from 4:1's taqwā (fear of the Lord who watches) through the family-law and the justice-summit and the hypocrisy-dossier and Christological summit, the sūrah ends its doctrinal body with the believer ushered into mercy-and-bounty *and walking straight-toward-God*: the God of 4:1's watching (raqīb) now the God of 4:175's destination (ilayhi) — the Watcher at the opening become the Terminus at the closing — the whole chapter compassed between the Lord observed-from and the Lord journeyed-to. Thereby the closing doctrinal body of the chapter completes its last formula: believe and hold fast — be entered into mercy and bounty — and be guided *to Him*, straight — v. 176 alone remaining: the kalālah-ruling — law once more as the body of the chapter closes upon the earth it so perfectly compasses between them — God the End of the soul's straight path, and the Splitter of the estate between sisters and brothers.
