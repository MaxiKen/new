## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:47

> **O you unto whom the Book has been given! Believe in what We have sent down, confirming that which is with you, before We blot out faces and turn them backwards, or curse them as We cursed those [who broke] the Sabbath. And the Command of God shall be fulfilled.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Yā Ayyuhā Alladhīna Ūtū al-Kitāb: A Direct Summons Across the Scripture-Border**

After the triple-fence of smell after the indictments of vv. 44–46, heaven turns and speaks to the charged themselves: *yā ayyuhā alladhīna ūtū al-kitāba āminū bi-mā nazzalnā muṣaddiqan li-mā maʿakum* — "O you who have been given the Book! Believe in what We have sent down — confirming what is with you": a direct vocativo a direct vocative, rare for this address-class in the Book (its notable sisters at 2:41 — "believe in what I sent down confirming what is with you, and be not the first disbeliever in it" — and 5:68 — "O People of the Book! You are upon nothing until you uphold the Torah and the Gospel and what was sent down to you"): the summons always carries the same predicate — the Qur'an presented *not as a rival* to the inherited books but as their *muṣaddiq*: the confirmer — the traditions emphasizing the rhetorical tenderness of it (al-Ṭabarī and al-Zamakhsharī's noting how God does not say "accept what destroys your portion" but "what *confirms* it" — the argument taken from within their own cargo: the Torah predicted itself pointed forward, the convict and the Prophet's cameos in it — their own scholars having narrated which lines marked the Arabian prophet). The offer-terms, therefore, are not surrender-of-heritage but fulfillment-of-heritage — with the complementary with the complementary clause of 2:91 sealing the psychology of refusal: *"when it was said to them: believe in what God sent down, they say: we believe in what was sent down to us — disbelieving in what is after it, though it is the Truth confirming what they have."*

**Min Qabli an Naṭmisa Wujūhan Fa-Naruddahā ʿalā Adbārihā: The Erasure of Faces**

Then the ultimatum-side, a terror-form almost without parallel: *min qabli an naṭmisa wujūhan fa-naruddahā ʿalā adbārihā* — "before We blot out faces and turn them backwards": *ṭ-m-s*, the wiping-out that leaves no trace (~ the effacing root of ṭams as the Book uses it for blinding-untrace, 77:8's stars effaced; 36:66's "had We willed We would have blotted out their eyes so they raced toward the path") — and the commentators spread its registers into the two grand classical ones: (1) *spiritual-effacement* — faces = the honor-distinguishing features of the soul; features erased and *turned backwards* = discernment inverted — guidance-theorems denied them, all bearings reversed — the inward punishment of rejecting known truth being precisely the loss of moral front/rear (al-Rāzī's elegant reading: they who walk backward from confirmation find the future itself switched with the past); and (2) *corporal transformation* — al-Ṭabarī's chains relaying the report-grade of literal prophecy: celestial wrath blotting out eyes, features, and heads twisted to face the back — and al-Qurṭubī preserving both with the sober rider that the *first case* — the moral erasure — is the base meaning, the second left to His plan being what God may effect on whom He wills (cf. his parallel at 36:66). Either way the clause is a warning of *form-loss* aimed at scholars: those trained to dignity-of-word stand warned that knowledge refused becomes a *turning around of their own image* — the most devastating de-resolution of the fall from the writing-desk: *facing backward — featureless — in public*.

**Aw Nalʿanahum Ka-mā Laʿannā Aṣḥāba al-Sabt: The Sabbath-Precedent**

The second limb of the ultimatum cites their own ancestor-court-file: *or We curse them as We cursed the Sabbath-people* — the community God already banished from mercy for covenant-sabotage: the Sea-towners of 2:65 ("Be you apes, despised") and its full narrative at 7:163–166 (the fish tempted into the unlawful net on their consecrated rest-day — stratagem treated as covenant-fraud) and 4:154's mention nigher nearer ("We took their covenant... and said to them: transgress not the Sabbath") — the prosecutors thereby press pressed to face their own family precedent: God has expelled Book-holders from mercy before — upon Sabbath-law partly hidden, partly twisted, partly evaded — the same three vices charged in vv. 44–46 immediately above — so the logic closes in: *you know what came of those before you; the only survivor of your people is repentance.* The classes of curse for the present offenders run in al-Ṭabarī's and Ibn Kathīr's record along the same twin-tracks as the faces-threat — exclusion-from-mercy here or chastisement hereafter — with its history the historical memory of the commentators (al-Zamakhsharī's register and Ibn Kathīr's chronology) preserving the *outwake the outcome*-report at Madinah: some of the learned of the Book did accept (the ʿAbdullāh ibn Salām-line of the ten heaven-dwellers' assurance — Bukhārī the Tradition naming him therein, Tirmidhī 3723 census; and the conversions that then followed) — and those conversions, God willing in the Mercy's own timing, stayed the harsher external forms of the threatened wrath from descending upon them en bloc: repentance-interceding-for-a-city, the ancient law by which One by which *some* have always saved *many* from wrath's edge.

**Wa Kāna Amru Allāhi Mafʿūlā: The Decree That Cannot Be Filed Away**

The seal is one of the Book's severity-formats: *wa kāna amru Allāhi mafʿūlā* — "and the command of God is ever accomplished": *amr* — command-affair-decree — and *mafʿūl* — carried wholly through — the passive-participle itself a grammatical prophecy: what God ordains is already in the case of *done* (its parallel seals: 4:44's sequence 4:44's own preceding alarm-lantern in 3:25 and 33:37's "the command of God is ever a decree pre-ordained"; 65:3's "God will bring His command to pass" — the format always chosen when heaven certifies an ultimatum) — and al-Rāzī's note discloses the clause's double-valence: it is *assurance* to the Muslims (the Prophet's vindication *is decreed* — do not bustle) and *terror* to waverers of the Book-intervals (every clause of the two warnings above — the effacement, the curse — is *already administratively sealed*; only repentance re-opens the file). The pericope of the portion-trials then closes its first movement — charge-sheet (44–46), summons with ultimatum (47) — upon a line of sonorous finality: God's decree completes — the only unfixed variable in the whole drama being, still, the reader's response: believe in what came confirming what you have — or the registering hand shall stamp it — *mafʿūlā* — done.
