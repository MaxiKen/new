## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:61

> **And when it is said unto them, "Come to what God has sent down and to the Messenger," thou seest the hypocrites turn away from thee with aversion.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Wa Idhā Qīla Lahum Taʿālaw: The Simple Invitation**

The diagnostic of the pretender-class proceeds to its simplest test — the open invitation: *wa idhā qīla lahum taʿālaw ilā mā anzala Allāhu wa ilā al-rasūl* — "and when it is said unto them: Come — to what God has sent down, and to the Messenger!": *taʿālaw* — the summoning imperative, from *ʿalā* — to rise to — *come up unto* — the verb carrying itself the dignity of the destination (the invitations of the Book are always an ascent — to prayer, to the war, to God) — and its contents states the twofold referral in shortened form: God's sending-down (the text) and His Messenger (the living instance and judge) — the protocol of v. 59's *ruddūhu ilā Allāhi wa-l-rasūl* here field-tested: nothing more complicated is asked of the claimants than to walk up and stand before the revealed ordinance and its bearer. Al-Qurṭubī's jurisprudence finds in the clause itself the ummah's permanent court-summons value — the simple call being all that is ever due as the measure: the Prophet needed no procedural no procedural bodywork — a *come to God and the Messenger* sufficed to flush every counterfeit: and the lawyers noted for all later history the value of refusing the *simple* call: one may wrangle over complicated bills, but to be called *to the Book and the man* — and to turn — is a deed that no apologetics can re-texture: *evasion itself arraigns*.

**Raʾayta al-Munāfiqīna: The Witnessing-Seal of Hypocrisy**

The scene's proof arrives as sight endorsed to the Prophet: *raʾayta al-munāfiqīna yaṣuddūna ʿanka ṣudūdan* — "thou seest the hypocrites turning away from thee — a turning": three chairs in one clause: (1) *raʾayta* — *thou sawest* — hypocrisy passing from suspicion into witnessed conduct (its definition passing thereby from interior-state to track-record: the Prophet is not authorized is authorized to *see* it by what his eyes verify — the classical scholars' standing caution: only conduct condemns, no heart-guesswork admits an accusation to stand — with the hadith-sieve of ʿUmar's famous quandary: only God knows hearts, but *behavior is ours to judge*); (2) *al-munāfiqīna* — the hypocrites — the noun now named openly, extracted from n-f-q — the gerbil's the rodent's two-doored burrow (nāfiqāʾ: the tunnel with two exits — the desert-weasel that keeps an alternate door — the perfect organic emblem of the double-exit religion) — with the whole Surah al-Munāfiqūn (63) ahead as their full portrait-book; (3) *yaṣuddūna ʿanka ṣudūdan* — turning away from thee *a turning*: the cognate-accusative amplifying into spectacle — their turn is noted their turn is a full, flagrant, total turning (the verbal-noun doubling in Arabic for stamped excellence-of-act: the turning is *thorough*: no half-shoulder, no excused absence: the whole back). Ibn Kathīr's register at the clause records the two practical lessons the salaf drew: (a) that fidelity is *measured by response to the summons*, not by presence at sermons, so the believer's daily audit is likewise *the speed of his coming*; and (b) that the Prophet (and by extension every daʿwah-voice) was told to *watch conduct* — not to blast hearts with verdicts — pastoral method guarded by heaven itself — the cosmos-eyes the eye of the Prophet acting as the ummah's own audit-lens, the seen avoided, the unseen left to God.

**The Two Invitations and One Response**

The pericope thus builds its double-portrait of the trial that never fails (v. 60: they *desire* ṭāghūt-arbitration; v. 61: they *turn from* God-and-Messenger's invitation): the same two verbs pulled into exact opposition: *yurīdūna* — they wish — versus *yaṣuddūna* — they turn — willing and walking agreeing with each other against their claim (the claim of *īmān*) — and the sages' summary their summary-dictum that became classic precisely from this pair: *whoever claims discipleship yet shuns the *taʿālaw* of God and His Messenger tests as a munāfiq by the very test his own feet administer* — while the in the corresponding teaching on the *Messenger's* side completing the symmetry just below (4:64's *"We sent no messenger save to be obeyed by God's leave"* — the office's warrant making the community's walk the measure) — so that vv. 60–61 are the sūrah's mirrored doors: hypocrite-behavior on one side, prophet-authority on the other, and between them the standing referral — *to God and the Messenger* — upon which the next verse puts the hypocrites' Last-Day oath-theater (v. 62), upon which the verse after that dispatches the pastoral triage (v. 63): turn aside, admonish, speak the word that pierces their souls — *for the Prophet is judge of conduct, God of hearts, and the sign stands static at the gate: come up — to the sending-down and to the Messenger.*
