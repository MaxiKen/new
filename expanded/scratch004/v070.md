## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:70

> **Such is the Bounty from God, and God suffices as Knower.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Dhālika al-Faḍlu mina Allāh: The Inventory Sealed as Grace**

The apodosis-block completes with the ledger-signature of the whole: *dhālika al-faḍlu mina Allāh* — "that is the Bounty from God": *dhālika* — the demonstrative sweeping over the whole threefold payload (v. 67's mighty reward from His Presence; v. 68's guidance upon the road; v. 69's companionship with the blessed — each item now stamped under a single title: *faḍl*, bounty — surplus-grace) — and the root f-ḍ-l already etched in the reader's memory at this point of the sūrah from its many stations (v. 32's "ask God of His bounty" — for wage-claiming instead of envy; v. 37's misers concealing God's bounty; v. 54's bounty-envy of the portion-bearers — so that the word arrives here carrying its full pericope-history: *everything humans fight over — bread, gold, office, prophecy — hangs in this one vault, and here God opens it not by merit but by grace*). The theological sharpness of the seal is the whole Sunni grace-doctrine in miniature — al-Rāzī's comment standing: the triple payment outruns any arithmetic of deed-value — no obedience weighed in scales could *earn* prophet-companionship or Presence-proximity — hence the item's label: it is *bounty*, not compensation; payment received according to service, but measured by the Giver's generosity — a doubl the same twofold accountancy the Book carves upon its wage-verses everywhere (24:38's reward "and more of His bounty" — *min faḍlih* appended beyond the reckoning; 4:173's own parallel ahead: "He will pay their wages in full — and more of His bounty"; 62:4's "the bounty is in the hand of God") — works measured exactly, bounty lavished boundlessly, so the believer receives justice *plus* overflow, and never one without the other.

**Wa Kafā bi-Allāhi ʿAlīmā: The Sufficiency-Knower Behind the Payments**

Then the verse's closing attribute-signature — *wa kafā bi-Allāhi ʿalīmā* — "and God suffices as Knower": *kafā* — the sufficiency-verb fourth-time-deployed in the pericope (God suffices as Helper — v. 45; the manifest sin suffices as proof — v. 50; Hell suffices as blazing at v. 55 — each sufficiency fitted to its own chamber of assurances) — and here its object is *ʿalīmā* — the Quality-Noun of knowledge absolutized (God as the Knower-Par-Excellence: the verdict certified from omniscience) — its function in the clause being three-deep a triple-barreled warrant, as the commentators array it: (1) *knowledge of who obeys* — the eligibility-verifier of v. 69's maʿa-promise (no false enrollment in the cohort, no lost enrollment either — the sufficiency of the files its office being heaven's perfect HR-record: the obedient all counted); (2) *knowledge as the reward's own witness* — the bounty being indelibly registered (no amnesia-heaven — the promise cannot spoil in record); and (3) *knowledge replacing every human audit* — for men could never measure which obedience was real (v. 60's professed believers among them, recall) — so God's sufficiency as Knower seals over inter seals over man's file-insufficiencies: *His knowing is enough.* The established tradition’s note upon the attribute is hereby again with the whole pericope's: the hypocrite-bloc feared riyāʾ-audits and lost files while the obedient-bloc feared lost wages — and the same attribute waters both anxieties flat: *He who knows cannot be files fooled by the first, nor forgetful of the second.*

**The Trust-Block's Apodosis Completed — and the Hinge Toward the Battle-Calls**

With v. 70 the authority-architecture of vv. 58–70 has run its full circle: duties and trusts (58–59), the tribunal-refusers exposed and admonished (60–65), and the counterfactual payments pledged upon obedience (66–70) — bounty and knowledge sealing it on both ends: grace paying; omniscience certifying — and the believer left where every covenant-exchange leaves him: *with a standing contract*: obedience exchanged for the trinity — Presence-reward, path-guidance, blessed-companionship — God inscribed as the Bountiful-Granter and the Never-Forgetting-Knower upon the document's closing line — with the sūrah's very next verse's picking up next verse (71: "O you who believe! Take your precautions...") now swinging the polity from covenant-comforts into campaign-discipline: the ummah that knows its recompense must also keep its guard — the grace-doctrine never licensing carelessness — for as the pericope has taught from its first clause: trust *carries responsibility* (amānah must be delivered) — and responsibility in Madinah now requires shields: *take your precautions — God watching over all.*
