## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:166

> **But God Himself bears witness to what He has sent down to thee — He sent it down with His knowledge — and the angels bear witness. And God suffices as a Witness.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Lākini Allāhu Yashhadu bi-Mā Anzala Ilayka: The Witness Above Every Demand**

The chapter of certificates reaches its crown: the demanders of v. 153 had asked for a book descending from heaven with visible spectacle — and the Book answers not with spectacle but with *testimony*: *lākini Allāhu yashhadu bi-mā anzala ilayk* — "but God Himself bears witness to what He has sent down to thee": *yashhadu* — from sh-h-d — witnessing, attestation at first hand: the courtroom-word the sūrah has made its own (4:33 — "God is ever Witness over all things"; 4:79 — "God suffices as a Witness"; 4:135's standing witnesses-for-God; 4:159's Jesus-as-witness) — and the Witness here is God Himself: the highest attestation impossible to appeal above — every demand for *further* evidence thereby disclosed not as evidentiary rigor but as refusal (its standing counter-file already installed at 6:111 — "even if We sent down the angels to them, and the dead spoke to them, and We gathered all things before them — they would not believe — unless God wills") — the Book's standing doctrine that evidence complete is evidence refused only by hearts.

**Anzalahu bi-ʿIlmih: Sent Down *With His Knowledge* — The Qualification of the Transmission**

The witness is immediately glossed with a specification the commentators measure with care: *anzalahu bi-ʿilmih* — "He sent it down with His knowledge": the preposition *bi-* carrying its standing double-facing (the standing readings in the comment-canon): (1) *with* His knowledge — the whole revelation wrapped in divine knowing: nothing in it escapes or exceeds the Sender's awareness — its content coextensive with omniscience, no clause accidental, no word unweighed; and (2) by/through His knowledge as standing guarantee: the Book's standing declarations that revelation proceeds from the knowing-source — 12:102's standing tail-note ("that is of the tidings of the Unseen We reveal to thee"); its register: 18:109's exhaustless sea-image ("if the sea were ink for the words of my Lord, the sea would be exhausted before the words of my Lord were exhausted"); 31:27's standing all-the-trees-as-pens file; and the standing creed-principle: God's Speech is His standing attribute — the Qur'ān not created information about Him but His own kalām proceeding from His ʿilm — the verse thereby sealing the revelational chain of vv. 163–165 (waḥy from Noah to Muḥammad, an argument-voiding office) with its standing metaphysical ground: what moves down the chain is Knowledge itself made recitation.

**Wa al-Malāʾikatu Yashhadūn: The Angels in the Witness-Stand**

The attestation spreads to its second register: *wa al-malāʾikatu yashhadūn* — "and the angels bear witness": the standing angelic corroboration of revelation's authenticity (its sister-station at 3:18 — "God bears witness that there is no god but He — and the angels, and those possessed of knowledge — upholding justice") — the commentators glossing the angels' standing stake in the file: Gabriel the conveyor (2:97 — "whoever is an enemy to Gabriel — for he it is who brought it down upon thy heart by God's leave"; 26:193-194 — "the Trustworthy Spirit brought it down upon thy heart"); the standing general angelic witnessing of the descent — the Qur'ān itself descending amid their ranks (97:4 — "the angels and the Spirit descend therein by their Lord's leave, with every command") and being recited among them in the heavens (the standing report-literature of the celestial mosque — al-Bayt al-Maʿmūr of the night-journey report in Bukhārī's standing register 3207 — where the heavenly recitation-assembly meets the earthly); their testimony thereby the testimony of the entire transmission-personnel: the divine Author witnesses; the courier-host witnesses; and between them the demand for a *spectacle* is answered by the standing tribunal of the actual delivery.

**Wa Kafā Bi-llāhi Shahīdan: The Sufficiency-Formula at Its Highest Post**

Then the formula the sūrah has already planted at v. 79 and v. 132, now in its witness-station: *wa kafā bi-llāhi shahīdan* — "and God suffices as a Witness": the standing sufficiency-architecture — *kafā bi-llāhi...* — whose sūrah-register runs: 4:45 (suffices as Ally and Helper), 4:79 — "trust in God; God suffices as a Guardian"; 4:81 (Guardian); 4:132 (Guardian); and its Book-wide standing stations: 3:173 — the ḥasbunā-cry; 13:43 — "say: God suffices as a Witness between me and you" (the standing parallel-answer to the very context of ours: the Prophet instructed to answer the sign-demanders with this sufficiency-formula); 29:52 — "say: God suffices as a Witness between me and you — He knows whatever is in the heavens and the earth"; 46:8 — "say: God suffices as a Witness between me and you"; 48:28 — "God suffices as a Witness" sealing the Messenger's own mission-file; and 10:29 — "God suffices as a Witness between us and you." The commentators mark the fitting closure: the verse answers the v. 153-demand with the standing tribunal already sitting — God witness, angels co-signing, and the sufficiency-ruling delivered: *no further discovery procedure will be ordered* — the evidence having been, from the beginning, complete: the Prophet need not subpoena heaven when heaven has already filed its testimony. And the verse's own standing echo — 3:18's triple-witness (God, angels, possessors of knowledge attesting the One God) — links this testimony to the testimony of tawḥīd itself: the Book and the Oneness it teaches arriving under the same signatures — 4:166 the certificate of the text, 3:18 the certificate of the doctrine — both sealed: kafā bi-llāh.
