## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:111

> **And whosoever commits a sin, commits it only against his own soul, and God is Knowing, Wise.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Wa Man Yaksib Ithman fa-Innamā Yaksibuhu ʿalā Nafsihi: The Recoil-Law**

The court-block lays down, at its doctrinal close, the single sentence that sums its whole ontology: *wa man yaksib ithman fa-innamā yaksibuhu ʿalā nafsihi* — "whosoever earns a sin — earns it only against his own soul": *yaksib* — earns, acquires — the commerce-verb the Book applies to deeds (with the standing equivalence: every act a purchase; every ledger-opening a customer accounted — good or evil contracted-in contracted-in by its owner by its owner forever) — *ithm* — sin — from ā-th-m — the standing guilt-word — and the verse's hammer-clause — *fa-innamā yaksibuhu ʿalā nafsihi* — the store the same verb repeating with the same sin as its object, now pointed squarely at *his own soul* (the comment-canon savoring its doubled gent its doubled form: the sin enters the world's stage aimed outward — at property, at brother, at victim; by the time the account closes, the store the gilt staking the sin returns home — lodged not against its intended target but against its engineer — Ṭuʿmah's armor-job Ṭuʿmah's false frame its standing illustration direct: the armor stolen rests eventually in legitimate hands or punishment; the *earning* stays not is stored stays charged on the engineer remains charged upon the engineer of the frame) — with the corollary indicated by the attribute-pair — *wa kāna Allāhu ʿalīman ḥakīmā* — "God is Knowing, Wise": Knowing — the ledger-keeper of every earned account; Wise — the Measurer apportioning every sin's return-home with precision (v. 106's own two-attribute architecture again — sovereignty-by-knowledge plus sentence-by-wisdom — the block's final seals summing the court-chapter in the same twin-name brand with which it opened).

**Knowing, Wise: The Seals of the Reflex-Law**

Then the two names upon its closing — a seal the sūrah has now stamped repeatedly (v. 24; 26; 92; 104; 111): Knowing — the always-aware witness of the sin's whole passage (no guilt unwitnessed from its night-conception to its day-manifestation); Wise — the legi the legislator of corresponding returns the legislator whose matching of moral act to moral consequence is never caprice but built-in calibration — and with this seal the whole Ṭuʿmah-file ends upon its own theological syllogism: the innocent target was never really looted of innocence (his trial purified him); the thief never really kept his loot (his earning weighed him down); the clan never really rescued its own of it their own clansman (their pleading only loaned him toward greater account toward heavier owed sums) — and the Judge Himself, watching all, was never in doubt — with the tradition's own long-standing sum-up of the episode writing itself since the earliest tafsīr (Ibn Kathīr's prayer its own standing rec the standing lesson-list): (1) no aid for treachery; (2) no plea against evidence; (3) no night-panel sealed against the Encompassing; (4) no sinner but sins at his own chest; and (5) no closure of repentance while breath remains — the sūrah's courtroom-block now sealed complete, the bench washed, ledgers balanced, and heaven's own verdict written over the whole affair: *whosoever earns a sin — earns it only against his own soul*.
