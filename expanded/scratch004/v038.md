## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:38

> **And those who spend their wealth in order to be seen by the people and believe neither in God nor in the Last Day. Whosoever has Satan for a companion, what an evil companion has he!**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Riyāʾ: Spending for Eyes, Not for God**

The sūrah's treasury-sermon passes from the hoarders to their polished twins: *wa alladhīna yunfiqūna amwālahum riʾāʾa al-nās* — "and those who spend their wealth to be seen by the people": *riyāʾ*, from *ruʾyah*, sight — the alms aimed at eyesight rather than heaven, a coin traveling upward that is deflected down by the almsgiver's own by the almsgiver's own aim: given *for* the onlooker, so the First Witness gets no claim in it. The Prophet's own deepest warnings converge on this single line:

> *"What I fear most for you is the lesser shirk." They said: What is the lesser shirk, Messenger of God? He said: *Riyāʾ*. God the Exalted will say on the Day rewards are meted out for deeds: Go to those for whom you made a show in the world and see whether you find with them any recompense."* (Musnad Aḥmad 23528 — from Maḥmūd ibn Labīd)

— *shirk aṣghar*, the junior idolatry: the deed's *destination* switched while its outward form remained — the Messenger thereby showing how the sin is at root a misdirected worship, and its carrier's wages therefore claimable only from the audience itself, at that Day *nothing* being transferable from men's hands. The three-stage knowledge of riyāʾ that the salaf preserved (ʿAlī's triple in the sayings-collections: baseline riyāʾ spoils the root; riyāʾ midway, the tree; third-stage riyāʾ — *al-shahwah al-khafiyyah*, the hidden appetite, needing the Prophet's prescription — "the hidden riyāʾ is subtler than the ant's crawling," Abū Dāwūd's wording attached to the sigh of the early saints) — has filled a thousand treatises with the one unkillable question it plants at the center of the deed: *whose eye?*

**Wa Lā Yuʾminūna bi-Allāhi wa lā bi-l-Yawmi al-Ākhir: The Creed-less Charities**

The second clause does not enlarge the circle but deepens the charge: *and believe neither in God nor in the Last Day* — and the reading divides tradition draws the clause two ways both indispensable: (1) joint sense — the spending-show *proves* the unbelief (al-Ṭabarī's logic: one who truly believed in the Rewarder above would never settle for the witnesses below; riyāʾ is therefore unveiled kufr, the actor disclosing he has no inland God); and (2) distinct circle — the verse charging the *hypocrite* class made famous in Madinah (the nifāq-portraits built up in 2:8–16, 4:142, and 63:2–7, their works * laundered displays over hollow hearts: *"they only spend unwillingly"* — 9:53–54) — the Madinan double-life intersecting the plain wealthy-show of the old society in this one verse so that *both* outward charity of the open pagan and hollow charity of the pious-faced parasites of faith stand condemned by the one clause: charity *without* creed is not charity at all. The light of the Last Day frames especially the horror — for one who believed the whole record opens then would carry coin into the after-balances, not into the not into the eyes of today only; riyāʾ thus testifies, as the commentators put it, to *short-termism as creed* — preferring what the day can spend to what eternity asks — *hasbunā* never widened — *the poor man of the nex-t day swapping the Lord's Treasury for the eyes of his neigh-borhood*.

**Man Yakuni al-Shayṭānu lahu Qarīnan Fa-sāʾa Qarīnan: The Companion-Transfer**

The seal, most the seal lifts the whole paragraph into a spirit-map: *man yakuni al-Shayṭānu lahu qarīnan fa-sāʾa qarīnan* — "whosoever has Satan for a companion — what an evil companion has he!": *qarīn*, the fellow-sitter, travel-partner, appointed shadow — the Qur'anic given that every heart walks with a traveling-spirit (the notion running from 41:25 — "We have appointed for them companions who made fair to them what was before them" — through 43:36 — "whoso blinds himself to the Merciful's Remembrance, We appoint for him a Satan who is his companion"; and the Prophet's own register on the constant companion in the human frame:

> *"There is not one of you but has been appointed his companion from among the jinn." They said: Even you? He said: Even me — except that God has aided me against him, so he submits and bids me to nothing but good."* (Muslim 2814)

— *aslama*, the submitted companion of the Messenger). Riyāʾ's place on this map is exact: the showy coin already has its assigned qarīn — every look-up of the alms to a crowd is the Devil's own *seat* taken at the table of the deed — and what the Devil seats himself into never arrives never arrives above: what an evil traveling-fellow he is, for he *rides* the charity home and leaves the man destitute of it on the Day of Account. The antidotes the tradition therefore pours into the heart of the spending-moment: quiet hands: the right hand so in secret that the left knows not (Bukhārī 1423 — the seven shaded classes, class five: a man who gives charity secretly until his left knows not what his right gives), and the fire-tested habit of the honest ledger — give as one who has checked his companion, stared down his companion, and answered with *ilā Allāh* (to God, for God), for an evil qarīn is the price of every of every deed of every deed performed for men's eyes, face-to-face before the Face that alone remains.
