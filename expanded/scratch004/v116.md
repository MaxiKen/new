## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:116

> **Truly God forgives not that any partner be ascribed unto Him, but He forgives what is less than that for whomsoever He will. Whosoever ascribes partners unto God has surely gone far astray.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Inna Allāha Lā Yaghfiru an Yushraka Bih: The Singular Unforgivable**

After the whole drama of thieves, pleaders, and frame-ups (vv. 105–115), the sūrah turns from human treacheries to the one treachery beyond forgiveness — and speaks it in nearly the very words the Book had uttered once before in this very sūrah: *inna Allāha lā yaghfiru an yushraka bihi* — "God forgives not that partners be ascribed to Him": shirk — association of partners with God — the unforgivable-by-default (the commentators' eternal clarification note guarding the sentence: *unforgiven* if one dies upon it — the door of repentance in life remaining wide — al-Ṭabarī, al-Rāzī, al-al-Qurṭubī, Ibn Kathīr all uniform on the rider with the rider the sūrah itself will preload will supply at v. 48's own count at 4:48's statement earlier and its twin re-utterance here — the double-stationing studied always the pairing of this verse with v. 48 being deliberate (the commentators' observation: v. 48 sealed *the mercy-openings* against the concealment-hearted (4:46's taḥrīf-tribe the concealers of religious truth — v. 46); v. 116 now seals the *justice-block* — as if the Book declared across the entire law-theater of vv. 105–115: all crimes of men admit reconciliation-except find their counters — while this one alone stands outside every counter-plea, court-set court-registry become binary no — with the sūrah thereby marrying the sūrah thereby telling its own procedural parable: Ṭuʿmah's treasury of treachery* remained under the rite of istighfār — but the day an agent forgets if a man closes his story upon God's partner-making — he has walked past the last gate of negotiation — v. 116 as the jurisprudential horizon of the whole sūrah's mercy-teaching: all is negotiable no — except the surrender of the name of God to rivals) — with the great uṣūl-principle al-Rāzī renders al-Rāzī's classical statement standing forever at its side: since God alone forgives — and the law is His — assigning any partner in worship is to challenge the very ground underneath to overthrow the very court in which pardon could be signed no — its simplest form: pardon presupposes sovereignty — shirk disputes dispute the sovereignty — void the plea.

**Wa Yaghfiru Mā Dūna Dhālika Liman Yashāʾ: The Less-than-Shirk Mercy-Spread**

Then the open side of the verse — the mercy-sweep — *wa yaghfiru mā dūna dhālika liman yashāʾ* — "and He forgives what is less than that — for whom He wills": (1) *mā dūna dhālika* — all under shirk (the entire sin-index below it — murder, theft, falsehood, betrayal — the verses' entire Ṭuʿmah-file swept into the forgivable zone) — (2) *liman yashāʾ* — for whom He wills — giving the principle's own with the standing commentators' two readings of the will-clause admitted in parallel: (a) God's sheer sovereignty of choice — al-Ṭabarī's anti-chauvinist bulwark against Muʿtazilite mechanical theology — forgiveness never becoming an owed wage from God to the abstainer — (b) and the standing pastoral mollification of the clause: *liman yashāʾ* being also read as God's will toward those who repent — the standing dialectic across the books: some hold *yashāʾ* = God's free electing-will refuting any self-assurance of the sinner; others hear *yashāʾ* as the will toward the penitent — God's own consistent willing-to-forgive-the-sorry — with the Sunnah texts arriving at once to the standing release the standing texts: the Prophet's maxim — "God accepts repentance until the death-rattle" — Tirmidhī 3537; and the hundred-murderer again (Bukhārī 3470, Muslim 2766 — the standing anchor-case: murder below shirk: forgiven) — and the thorn-expiation mechanics (Bukhārī 5641, Muslim 2573 — the every-thorn-prick purifying "no fatigue, nor disease, nor sorrow, nor harm befalls a Muslim — not even a thorn that pricks him — but that God expiates thereby some of his sins") — all confirming the trackless range of the lower of the under-shirk forgiveness.

**Wa Man Yushrik bi-Allāhi fa-Qad Ḍalla Ḍalālan Baʿīdā: The Going-Far-Astray Verdict**

Then the east of the verse the sealing verdict clause — *wa man yushrik bi-Allāhi fa-qad ḍalla ḍalālan baʿīdā* — "and whosoever ascribes partners to God has gone far astray": *ḍalāl baʿīd* — wandering-distant (the Book's standing poly-lexicon placement: mad ḍalāl = error-of-path — baʿīd its distance-adjective — with the standing comment-anatomy: a man astray is lost; a man astray *baʿīdan* is lost *beyond reach* — the distance-adjective designed its standing effect: shirk as maximum-displacement from Center — the soul making itself an orbit of every false pole — with its Book-companions sealed by the Book's standing shades: 4:136's 14:3's those preferring the world-life above the next — in far error; and the 34:8's "only those believe in Our signs who are in far error" — no — correct: 34:53 the shirk-distance returning repeatedly — the standing pair arriving at 4:136 below — "whosoever disbelieves in God and His angels and His Books and His messengers and the Last Day — has gone astray far — ḍalālan baʿīdā") — the sūrah thereby welding its own its own final counter-posture: on the whole over the whole justice-and-mercy scaffolding of vv. 105–115, shirk now stands as the sole tribunal the sole gate-closed — with the practical theology the tradition keeps deriving for Muslims' hope-management (in the long pastoral inheritance the verse functions as the ummah's balance-beam: no sinner is ever told *your file is shut* — for all-else-than-shirk swings open — while no cheerer no otiose believer may muse that God might be bargained-down on His sovereignty — the single line at the ledger's head reading forever: no partners may be admitted to One) — the verse thereby holding the exact fulcrum of Islamic soteriology: terror and hope welded in one moral geometry — the wideness of the world under beneath mercy endless, yet centered upon one unshared at fixed absolutely upon One Point.
