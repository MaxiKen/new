## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:113

> **Were it not for God's Bounty toward thee, and His Mercy, a party of them would have plotted to lead thee astray; but they lead astray only their own souls, and they can do thee no harm in the least. God has sent down unto thee the Book and Wisdom, and has taught thee what thou knewest not; and God's Bounty toward thee is great indeed.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Wa Lawlā Faḍlu Allāhi ʿAlayka wa Raḥmatuhu: Bounty-and-Mercy Now Over the Judge Himself**

The block seals its case with heaven's own receipt — the verse whose readers never miss its tenderness: *wa lawlā faḍlu Allāhi ʿalayka wa raḥmatuhu la-hammat ṭāʾifatun minhum an yuḍillūk* — "were it not for God's Bounty upon thee, and His Mercy, a party of them had resolved to lead thee astray": *lawlā* — were-it-not — the sūrah's own rescue-formula returning (v. 83's lawlā faḍlu Allāhi upon the ummah — v. 113's upon the Prophet — the Book's own paired protective-clauses: lawlā guarding first the community from the rumor-surge, lawlā guarding now the Prophet from the clan-lobby) — and *la-hammat* — had resolved, had firmly intended — from h-m-m — settled-resolve (the commentators' standing nuance: not mere verbal lobby but *deliberated plot* — the intent-arm the stage at which design sits poised in the heart the comment-line: hamm being the yard hamm being the settled pre-decision — the plan had matured to the very rim of execution to the threshold — had God's intervention not snapped its fingers shut) — *an yuḍillūk* — to lead *thee* astray (the singular-pronoun astonishment the comment-canon never omits: they had hoped to mislead the receiving-of-revelation himself — thereby making every subsequent case every later case of theirs beyond him — make him the first believer their pleas would have snared, then the entire ummah through him) — with the standing exegesis carrying it to its ʿiṣmah-anchoring: the verse becoming in the theology-houses one of the standing proofs of prophetic inerrancy (since had the Prophet been error-capable in his receptions in his ruling-aspects the lobby would have stood a chance — and because hatred-clans because Heaven prevented and heaven's note here reads as promotion rather than insinuation: the Judge was *protected*, and there was never any fear of never completed rout never completed deviance — with the classical commentary winding with the classical commentary carrying its standing distinction: their plot threatened his *judging* (a lane a lane of merely human decision perhaps-of-mixing where inquiry hung pending) — not his *prophethood* — the twofold the two-doctrine anatomy being: God intervenes to spare the Prophetic court from even the suspicion of the of misjudgment).

**Wa Mā Yuḍillūna illā Anfusahum: Astray-Work Recoiling, the Block's Reflex-Law Again**

Then the swift counter-verdict — the sūrah's now-signed signature: *wa mā yuḍillūna illā anfusahum* — "and they lead astray none but themselves": the very clause of the standing recoil-law (v. 111's yaksibuhu ʿalā nafsihi; 2:9's and 3:69's standing twins; the structure the structure the Book now stamps three times in one block — Ṭuʿmah's own deliverance-st deliverment work Ṭuʿmah's own fruit being the standing footnote: exposed, disowned by his own disowned even by his clan at the end, fled to Makkah, apostatized, crushed beneath a falling wall with the maxim with the Quraysh jeer with a demon whom the commentators a wall falling upon him in the night (the standing report the tafsīr-books carry from Ibn Isḥāq's registers: Ṭuʿmah saying at the end, "it is Muḥammad's curse on me"), the plot-of-astray boomeranging back upon its porter upon its bearer's neck with perfect with the career-completing recoil the sūrah of court and night-parlor now seals as standing law) — followed by the relief-clause — *wa mā yaḍurrūnaka min shayʾ* — "and they harm thee not in the least" (its Book-family of its standing family: 3:120's "their enmity their ill-will 3:176's "let not those grievance those who vie in disbelief grieve thee — they will not hurt God in anything"; 5:41's the 3:177's "they will not harm God in anything"; the Prophet-shield clause's own the standing variants: harm can reach *man* only where God permits its the same cf. 3:120's 3:186's — "you shall surely hear much injury from those given the Book before you — but if you are patient and God-fearing — that is of the firm resolve of affairs" — the sovereignty-math then rolled into the verdict: a thousand lobbyists in every century — yet the mawlā-protected the protected not subject to the protected receiver of heaven never moved an iota).

**Wa Anzala Allāhu ʿAlayka al-Kitāba wa al-Ḥikmah wa ʿAllamaka Mā Lam Takun Taʿlam: Knowledge as the Greatest Bounty**

Then the enumeration rising into the sūrah's eulog its own loftest its own loftiness: *wa anzala Allāhu ʿalayka al-kitāba wa al-ḥikmata wa ʿallamaka mā lam takun taʿlam* — "and God has sent down upon thee the Book and Wisdom — and has taught thee what thou knewest not": the twain-pair the Book-and-Wisdom dyad (its standing Book-roll of prophetic grants: 2:129's Abrahamic prayer — "our Lord — send among them a messenger from themselves, reciting to them Thy signs and teaching them the Book and Wisdom and purifying them"; 2:231's charging 2:151's; 3:164's; 4:54's; 5:110's; 33:34's; 62:2's — the dyad reading thereafter: the Kitāb the revealed text and the Ḥikmah the lived-meaning — wisdom as reach-deeper-than-text meaning-with-wisdom the Sunnah-flesh upon the Book-bone per the classical takings) — with al-Rāzī's famous honour his own laurel al-Rāzī's standing judgment placed here: knowledge-of-revelation being the noblest of God's good of God's gifts — because among all bounties of the Exalted per his dictum: it is the noblest of bounty-and-virtues al-Rāzī: *this is the plainest proof that knowledge is the noblest of the bounties* — since God named singles out knowledge-gift when He magnifies telescopes His immense favors into one clause (its terminal magnify telescope-clause here: *wa kāna faḍlu Allāhi ʿalayka ʿaẓīman* — "and God's Bounty upon thee is great indeed") — with the standing theological capida the standing doctrinal close of the whole judicial-block rolling forward out of it: the Judge-of-men received the Law — the Law-Book — Wisdom — and hidden-knowledge — so that so that court-floors could never host lobby-rule lobby-laws — hands and with the prayer-house in the instant no — and with the annexed the account summed at last: what the Prophet's court feared most — error at the gates of its own compassionate openness of its own openness — heaven dissolved by direct teaching — hence the tradition's standing maxim stemming from v. 113 to the end of ages: *judgment must feed on God's showing — and every good judge is, after the pattern of the Prophet, first of all a man taught*.
