## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:67

> **Then We would surely have granted them, from Our Presence, a great reward,**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Wa Idhan la-Ātaynāhum min Ladunnā Ajran ʿAẓīmā: The Crossways of the Conditional**

The tiniest verse of the pericope — one dependent clause — yet it hangs upon the whole balance of vv. 66–68 as the first of the three then-payments: *wa idhan la-ātaynāhum min ladunnā ajran ʿaẓīmā* — "then would We surely have granted them, from Our Presence, a mighty reward": *wa idhan* — *then, at that* — the apodosis-marker of the entire conditional structure launched in v. 66 ("had they done what they were admonished... then We *would surely have*..."): grammar itself thus carrying the theology — the *la*-of-certainty (*la-ātaynāhum* — We surely would have given) answering the *law* of hypothetical obedience — the mutual-conditions heaven's fidelity being thereby prototyped into its syntax: *if the stride is taken by man, the response is signed by God* — never vice-versa (the sunnah-order everywhere in the Book: 5:9's "God promised"; 24:55's succession-promise; and the classical ḥadīth qudsī which the tradition placed at the pericope's threshold: Bukhārī 7405 & Muslim 2675 — *"I am as My servant thinks of Me; when he remembers Me I am with him... and if he draws near to Me a span I draw near to him a cubit, and if he draws near a cubit I draw near him an arm's length, and if he comes to Me walking I come to him running"* — hurrying down the conditional's own rush into mercy).

**Min Ladunnā: The Ladunnī Signature of the Reward**

Within the clause sits the preposition the tradition has always marked with a bright pin: *min ladunnā* — *from Our own Presence*: the l-d-n adjacency-word — *from beside Us, from Our immediate nearness* — which the Book's readers of reward-theology met already at v. 40 of this very sūrah (the *ajran ʿaẓīman min ladunhu* of its pristine multiplytude its clause there: a good deed multiplied, *then* a great reward *from His Presence*) — al-Rāzī's celebrated distinction at that locus returning here to anchor the whole pericope: there are *two reward-classes* — (1) the *multiplied* payback of deeds themselves, counted, measured, apportioned (the ledger-of-returns, *yuḍāʿifhā*), and (2) the *ladunnī* endowment — the bounty that streams directly from His nearness — which the gnostic commentators associate the commentators associated with goods unshaped by instrumentation: the pleasure of His countenance, the direct rainwater of vision-love-knowledge — al-Rāzī's own phrasing his own image at 4:40's commentary: the first reward for the limbs for limbs' work; the second for *the soul sanctified by light* — bodily happiness versus spiritual happiness — the very categories rekindled now at v. 67: had the waverers obeyed, their *wage* would not be disbursed merely from an account-office but from *ladunnā* — from God's immediate side — the preposition carrying what no arithmetic can: *closeness with the Recompenser built into the recompense*. The Book's other deployments widen the mint: 17:80's "grant me from Your Presence an authority that helps"; 18:10's youths in the cave — "grant us from Your Presence a mercy"; 3:8's — "our Lord, let not our hearts swerve... and grant us from Your Presence a mercy — Thou art the Bestower" — and the treasury the piety-books' summary Al-Ghazālī's register the standard devotion-key of them all: *everything from ladunnā arrives unmediated — such is the promise stood behind the obedience of the small* (the very class contemplated here — the few of v. 66's *illā qalīlun*).

**Ajran ʿAẓīmā — Mighty, Not Measured**

The size-word *ʿaẓīm* — great, mighty — carries into the tiny verse the pericope's entire grandeur of scale: *ajr* — reward-payment — qualified by neither boundary nor breakdown (the commentators' observation at its similar stations: when heaven wishes to *dwarf* a reward it details it; when it wishes to *exalt* it, it writes *ʿaẓīm* and leaves it open — cf. 48:10 — the Hudaybiyyah pledgees promised "a mighty reward"; 33:29 upon the Prophet's wives — "a mighty reward prepared"; and throughout the trading-verses at 4:95, 133, 162, 173 and on) — so that the wavering audience learns the scale of what their one shortcoming of obedience forfeits: *a reward classified as mighty, drawn from the immediate Presence, sealed with heaven's own oath-particle *la-* — the conditional tragedy thus standing in full: all this *would have been* — and the small few who did answer receiving exactly it — though the tradition always keeps the door-half-open where God leaves it: *would have been* is not *never shall be* — any late-claimer may still convert his file into the three then-payments by completing v. 65's triple submission) — and the verse after (v. 68) pours the second payment beside it, the third following at v. 69's companionships-roll: the trinity of *reward from His Presence, guidance on the Path, and the company of the blessed* — a majestic three-line bill for one act of obedience that the reluctant so nearly missed.
