## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:48

> **Truly God forgives not that any partner be ascribed unto Him, but He forgives what is less than that for whomsoever He will. And whosoever ascribes partners unto God has surely fabricated a tremendous sin.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Lā Yaghfiru an Yushraka Bih: The One Non-Negotiable Clause**

The sūrah's confrontation-block issues its mountain-verdict — one of the four one of the Book's most-cited creedal clauses, twinned almost identically at 4:116 later in this very sūrah: *inna Allāha lā yaghfiru an yushraka bih* — "God does not forgive that He be partnered": *shirk* — ascribing a share, a co-claimant — thus disclosed as the sole darkness outside the tribunal of pardon. The syntax itself presses the doctrine: *lā yaghfiru* — a standing truth in the imperfect — not a threatened one-off act; and *an yushraka bih* in the nominalized infinitive — the fact-of-partnering itself is what's beyond amnesty *as such* — the commentators (al-Ṭabarī's chains, Ibn Kathīr's now-standard handling) immediately posting the verse's own controlling condition: forgiveness shuts *upon shirk as it is met at death* — unrepented, roomed-into-the-hereafter shirk — for the Book itself abounds with the paired door of repair: shirk *repented of* converts to the forgiven-set (25:68–70 — idolaters, murderers, adulterers... "except whoever repents and believes and works righteousness — those God will exchange their evil deeds for good ones"; and 39:53's capstone psalm of hope: *"Say: O My servants who have transgressed against themselves — despair not of God's mercy! God forgives all sins. Indeed He is the Forgiving, the Merciful"* — the exegetical towers agreeing the clause settles *the whole* sweep, shirk included, when repentance intervenes) — Ibn ʿAbbās's unmistakable summary ruling standing in the books: the negation here is of forgiveness *without* turning back — *not of revolution not of return itself.*

**Wa Yaghfiru Mā Dūna Dhālika Li-man Yashāʾ: Mercy Under Sovereign Choice**

Then the great open hand: *wa yaghfiru mā dūna dhālika li-man yashāʾ* — "and He forgives what is less than that for whom He wills": *mā dūna* — what lies short of it — every sin's rest, from a false oath to the mountain-crimes named in this very sūrah (parent-disobedience, murder, adultery, orphan-gold, ribā) — placed under the Lord's choice for the *unrepenting* (*li-man yashāʾ* — for whom He wills: neither cheap automatic parole nor despair-guillotine; the major sinner lives between two unknowables: he cannot presume, nor may he despair — precisely the soteriology the Ahl al-Sunnah approved embraced against both the Muʿtazilī middle-mania (the grave-sinner as neither believer nor unbeliever, permanently in the Fire save by personal repentance) and the Khārijite excommunication-doctrine (sin = unbelief, exit complete) — al-Rāzī's pages summarizing the school-war with our verse as the fortress of God's *mashīʾah*: will, not mechanism — al-Ṭabarī lodging, beside it, the far-reaching believer-generated the far-reaching witness of the Messenger:

> *"God will bring out of the Fire whoever has in his heart the weight of a mustard-seed of faith one atom faith of an atom's weight, at the intercession of those who intercede..."* (the intercession-register of al-Bukhārī 44 among the among the bases of the shafāʿ corpus, with Muslim 193's register of the final egress — "until not one remains in the Fire who has said lā ilāha illā Allāh with a barley-grain of good in his heart")

— the mā-dūna mercy thus covering, *by God's own announcement*, even the vastest ledger of sins so long as the Unshared Name was never divorced.

**Iftara Ithman ʿAẓīmā: Fabrication as the Essential Analytic of Shirk**

The seal specifies the sin's genus with a verb of lying: *wa man yushrik bi-Allāhi fa-qad iftarā ithman ʿaẓīmal* — "whoever ascribes partners to God has fabricated a tremendous sin": *iftarā*, from *f-r-y* — to invent, contrive, cut cut novelFalse *to forge* — the Book's chosen analysis-word for shirk everywhere shirk's interior is exposed (6:21, 24, 137, 144; 7:37, 89 and scores more: the idols called *ifk*, fict the rapt and their cult summarized at 29:17 as *"you worship apart from God nothing but idols and create a falsehood"*): the idolater not merely confused but a *counterfeiter* — issuing currency of worship against a Treasury he never saw (the god-share he assigned no Owner ever authorized) — hence the coined pairing *ithm* (sin-in-general) super and upon it *ʿaẓīm*, tremendous — its scale adjective chosen above all the Book's sin-gradations for shirk's uniqueness (31:13 — Luqmān's own epitaph-grade advice: *"associate not, for shirk is a tremendous wrong"* — ẓulmun ʿaẓīm). The hadith-canon adds its weaponized its dreadful daily light in the Prophet's own interrogation-sequence — the sin admitting no comparison: when asked *which sin is greatest*, he answered (al-Bukhārī 4477 and 6819; Muslim 86 — the register of ʿAbdullāh ibn Masʿūd):

> *"That you set up a rival unto God while He created you. — Then what That you kill your child fearing he will eat with you. — Then what That you commit adultery with your neighbor's wife."*

— shirk first, *before blood* — because in heaven's logic all other sins wrong *creatures* while this one wrongs *the Giver Himself*: a fraud perpetrated upon the Throne.

**The Asbab-Treasure of the Verse and Its Meeting-at-the-Judgment Ruling**

The commentators' chains (al-Wāḥidī, Ibn Kathīr, al-Ṭabarī's variorum) remember the verse's descent-crossing with the famous question of the Qurayshi delegation — or more precisely the Meccan supplicant Waḥshī (the slayer of Ḥamzah, after accepting manumission as price) and the idolaters' negotiators asking the Prophet: how can men who partnered gods and shed blood *ever* be restored the answer coming back precisely through the twin-verses 25:68–70 (repentance-exchange) and our 4:48's principle: *shirk unpardoned — everything else's file belongs to God's discretion* — and indeed Waḥshī's own later Islam (Muslim lit al-Bukhārī 4072 remembering his conversion, the Prophet's human pang — "hide yourself from me" — but his *acceptance*) stands as the tradition's living marginalia under the verse: *the door to the second clause is never blocked by men; the block only seals the man who meets God still renting His Holiness out.* The sūrah thereby advertises advances from its jurisprudence-of-treasures and households to the tribunal's highest rule: distribute with justice; obey with heart; but keep the Throne singular — for *partnership* is not one mistake among mistakes; it is the demolition of the account-book itself; while every other fault, however towering, yet sits *under* the second half of this verse like a crowd beneath an unrolled canopy: *whatever is less — He forgives, for whom He wills.*
