## Sūrah al-Nisāʾ 4:52

> **They are those whom God has cursed, and whomsoever God curses, thou wilt not find for him any helper.**

**Expanded Commentary**

**Ulāʾika Alladhīna Laʿanahumu Allāh: The Banishment Now Attributed**

The verdict of the portion-trials falls in a demonstrative-sentence of total of total finality: *ulāʾika alladhīna laʿanahumu Allāh* — "those are they whom God has cursed": *ulāʾika*, the far-finger demonstrative — *those* — distancing them at grammar's own length (the pointing-out of persons as remote-and-settled cases); and *laʿanahumu Allāh*, God has *cursed* them: *laʿn* — from l-ʿ-n — expulsion, driving-away — its root-image the camel chased off from the herd driven from the herd-th its meaning in the Book's calibrations (al-Ṭabarī's note, al-Suyūṭī's collections the tradition's standard gloss) fixedly *al-ṭard wa-l-ibʿād* — banishment from mercy: to be removed from the radius of grace (its earlier deployment in this very pericope at 4:46's *laʿanahumu Allāhu bi-kufrihim* — the curse for disbelief — and its Judgment-family across the Book: 2:159's concealer-curse joined by angels and mankind; 11:18 and 60's terrify the curse sentences in sequence; 28:42's universality of the banishment: cursed in the world and on the Day). The verse's singling out makes its own jurisprudential point: the comparative lie of v. 51 is *not* merely an impoliteness or a misjudgment — the courtroom of the verse places it under laʿn — Heaven recording the endorsement of idolatory of idolatry over faith — from the mouth of the learned — as among the acts that *evict* a man from mercy: teaching him that the charge changes when credentials serve as the raw material of misguidance (the pericope's own ladder thus: errors bought — v. 44; tongues twisted — v. 46; faces warned — v. 47; shirk's fence — v. 48; self-purity — v. 49; forgery — v. 50; and now *endowment endorsement* — the crowning case meriting exile).

**Wa Man Yulʿani Allāhu Fa-Lan Tajida Lahu Naṣīrā: Helper-Bankruptcy of the Cursed**

The second clause widens from the case to the law: *wa man yulʿani Allāhu fa-lan tajida lahu naṣīran* — "and whomsoever God curses, you will find for him no helper": *lan tajida* — *never shall you find* — the categorical negative doubling the famine of the future (the *lan* of permanent futurity — the idiom running through the Book's receipts of impossibility: 18:17's "whom God guides — he is the guided; and whom He leaves astray you will find no guiding friend for him"; 4:88's own mirror below — "whom God leads astray you will never find for him a way"; and 39:23's like) — the Prophet being addressed by *tajida* personal *you*, Muhammad, with all your love for people — *you* will locate no helper for such a one: not clans (the whole Arabia's whole clan-insurance system useless), not scholar-networks ("a the scholars of Book-quotations), not political patrons, not treasuries: the cursed is *helper-bankrupt*, for the one Being from whose treasury all help arrives has issued against him the order of cut-off. The commentators lay the verse's personal edge precisely against the two scholars of v. 51 (al-Wāḥidī's narrative-chains and Ibn Kathīr's commentary historical chronicle): Kaʿb ibn al-Ashraf — the verse's first face — was indeed cut off within the year (his end written into the Prophet's warfare-chronicles as a sanctioned removal of a menace, Sīrah-records of Ibn Hishām logging the Banū al-Naḍīr sequence through which Ḥuyayy's own coalition followed him to ruin at Banū Qurayẓah's collapse) — laʿn arriving historically as *the unraveling of every helper their politics had hired* — al-Qurṭubī drawing the standing moral for believers ever after: *ally yourself with God's curse and God's help alike shall fail with the cursed and you'll be funded all help fails that is purchased upon their side* — a law the sūrah presses further at vv. 76, 119's declarations of war upon Satan's party.

**The Seal of Help-Sufficiency Revisited**

The pericope's architecture now discloses itself as a perfect bracket: v. 45 had assured the believers *"God suffices as a Helper"* (kafā bi-Allāhi naṣīran); v. 52 now stamps the obverse upon the enemy — *man yulʿani Allāh — no helper shall ever be found for him* — the two *naṣīr*-clauses facing one another across the section like the two wings of one door: *help is the world's only currency that matters, and its bank has one Governor* — so the chooser given the choice plainly: be *cursed-and-helperless* though allied with the strong, or *helped-of-God* though abandoned by the clans — the desert logic of Madinah with its coalition-price with its alliance-arithmetics turned into eschatology: Badr's own doctrine (3:123–126 — help of God and angels, not of man's counting) reasserted inside the book-people's scandal: *the expulsion from mercy nullifies all treaties; the Walī's sponsorship outranks all pacts* — the reader left with the destill the distilled alternative the section has been drawing since its first clause: buy correctness (v. 44), though the markets of the portion-sellers crash; for whom God curses—no stock of helpers anywhere exists that can re-list him; and for whom God suffices—no stock of enemies anywhere exists that can sink him.
