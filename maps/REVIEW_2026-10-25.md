# Independent review — 2026-10-25

> **Status since this review.** It examined the 27-layer, 14,723-feature build of
> 2026-10-25 and its numbers are that build's, kept as the record of what was found
> then. The file has since grown to **31 layers / 60,555 features + 9 rasters**, and
> four further audit passes (2026-10-27) re-measured everything in it: line lengths
> are now geodesic rather than measured on the equal-area grid, ring orientation is
> normalised, `km_in_nigeria` and the water-body areas were recomputed from their
> geometries, and the empty-string/NULL convention, keys and descriptions were
> regularised. Where a figure here disagrees with `LAYERS.md` or `README.md`, those
> two and `layer_audit.json` are current; this page is dated. Its findings stand —
> every one was reproduced from the raw files, and every one listed in §6 was fixed.

Scope: the two NGSA PDFs in the repo root, `maps/Nigeria_Geology_Minerals_4326.gpkg`, its
renders, the build scripts, and every claim in `README.md`, `accuracy_audit.md`,
`mineral_audit.md`, `minerals_named.md`, `metadata.json`, `crs_qa_report.json`,
`layer_audit.json`.

Method: the GeoPackage was read with GeoPandas; both PDFs were re-rendered from scratch
(PyMuPDF) and the shipped coordinates were projected back onto the printed pixels using the
affines documented in the repo. No file in `maps/` was modified by this review.

## 3. Real data issues (not just stale prose)

**3.1 The withdrawn layer is still the one on the map.** `render_combined.py` plots
`mineral_localities_icons` (line ~29) — i.e. the ~10 km-noise positions — on the printed
map, and its caption asserts "842/999 on a printed mineral symbol (84 %)". The README
withdraws that number; the deliverable PNG still publishes it. `minerals_named` (the valid
positions) is shipped but not drawn.

**3.2 "Use `mineral_symbols.mineral` for counts" is a trap as written.** That column
contains **56 rows literally named `not a mineral symbol: raster sliver`**, plus 15
"fragment of a symbol", 8 "half of a symbol", 2 "merged pair". Any `value_counts()` on it
reports a phantom commodity of 56 and inflates real ones. Counts need
`ident_mth`/`mineral` filtered first (1,553 rows survive the sliver filter).

**3.3 Five units have no era, so the map's era legend under-sums.**

| code | unit | area km² | why blank |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Gdss` | Sandstone, siltstone and clay | **4,193.7** | printed label only |
| `s` | unnamed, printed 's' | 670.9 | printed label only |
| `P` | Pegmatite | 311.7 | legend row, but era/period/eon/epoch stored as **empty strings** and age null |
| `Gmsh` | Shale with limestone intercalation | 114.8 | printed label only |
| `BCT` | unnamed, printed 'BCT' | 4.5 | printed label only |

The "GEOLOGICAL UNITS BY ERA" legend on the PNG covers 896,190 km² of 907,157 km² (plus
5,671 km² of water) and says nothing about the missing 1.2 %. `Gdss` at 4,194 km² is a
bigger unit than several that are in the legend.

Related: missing values are encoded two ways — `NULL` for 4 of these rows and `''` for
`P` — so `era IS NULL` misses `P`.

**3.4 Case-insensitive collisions in the geology `code` key.** `M`/`m` (Migmatite vs a
printed label `m`) and `Qs`/`qs` both exist. Any join or filter that lowercases codes (a
natural thing to do for a code column) silently merges two different units.

**3.5 The 97.5 % "ink agreement" is a per-polygon test, not an area-weighted one.** I
re-rendered the 2011 sheet at 75 dpi and sampled 2,257 random points *inside* the shipped
polygons, using the repo's own affine (which is right: agreement peaks at a −1, −1 px
offset):

* 66.8 % of samples show the unit's own printed colour;
* 8.6 % show another unit's legend colour, 3.2 % are line/text, 21.4 % are other printed
  colours (grey hatch, overlay marks);
* of the non-matching samples, 50 % are within 0.7 km (1 px) of the unit's own edge and
  73 % within 2 km — i.e. mostly sampling/rounding at contacts;
* ~16 % of all samples are more than 2 km inside a unit and still not its colour.

That is consistent with a faithful layer plus heavy printed overlays, but it does **not**
reproduce 97.5 %, and the README states it as if it were an independent verification of the
whole layer. Worth restating as "1,426 of 1,462 source polygons agree on a single sample
each" and keeping the point-sample number alongside.

## 4. Recommended fixes, in order

1. Replace the PNG's mineral points with `minerals_named` (or drop them), and remove
   "842/999 · 84 %" from the caption in `render_combined.py`.
2. Add a withdrawal banner at the top of `accuracy_audit.md` §1/§3b pointing to
   `mineral_audit.md`; add `pos_sigma_m`/`pos_confidence` back into
   `mineral_localities_icons` or correct §4.
3. Fix the README rows in §2 above — items 1, 2, 4, 5, 6 are one-line edits; 8 (the
   `Ts`/`BCT` story) needs a sentence rewritten so it matches `geology_final_report.json`
   (`units_dropped_no_area` should list `Ts`).
4. Decide what `Gdss`, `Gmsh`, `P`, `s`, `BCT` are, or mark them explicitly as
   "no stratigraphic attribution" in the PNG legend and in `metadata.json`, and encode
   missing as `NULL` everywhere (`P`'s empty strings).
5. Regenerate `crs_qa_report.json` and `metadata.json` from the current build
   (`area_check`, `minerals_registration`).
6. Document the sliver rows in `mineral_symbols` next to the "use this for counts"
   recommendation.

## 5. Not verified here

The build scripts were syntax-checked but not executed end-to-end: `build_combined.py`
writes the deliverable in place, and a full rebuild was out of scope for a read-only
review. `fetch_source.py` was executed and verified. Host-rock agreement (93.5 %),
the ±5 km inter-sheet offsets, and the ink-order resolution of specific overlaps were not
re-measured; the checks that were run did not contradict them.
